### 38. Do not hand money or gift cards to the people handling your case or enforcing the law: bribery gets you sentenced too, and bribing supervisory, law-enforcement, or judicial personnel is punished more heavily
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; it is simply not doing it
- In plain terms: "spend a little money to smooth things over" is a separate crime in the Criminal Law, and the one caught is not only the person taking the money. The crime of offering bribes is defined as giving property to state functionaries to obtain improper benefits; Criminal Law Amendment XII, in force from March 1, 2024, reworked the sentencing into three tiers: up to 3 years plus a fine; 3 to 10 years where the bribery to obtain improper benefits is serious in circumstances or causes major losses to state interests; over 10 years or life imprisonment where especially serious, plus a fine or confiscation of property. It also lists seven aggravating circumstances, two of which aim straight at this kind of scene: "bribing supervisory, administrative law-enforcement, or judicial personnel", and bribing in fields such as education, healthcare, social security, food and drugs, and work safety while carrying out unlawful or criminal activity. The law leaves two openings: giving property because you were extorted, without obtaining an improper benefit, is not bribery; and voluntarily confessing the bribery before being prosecuted can bring a lighter or mitigated punishment, with a mitigated or exempted punishment where the crime is minor and you played a key role in cracking a major case.
- Benefit: Criminal Law article 389: "giving property to a state functionary to obtain improper benefits is the crime of offering bribes"; "in economic exchanges, giving property to a state functionary in violation of state regulations, where the amount is relatively large, or giving kickbacks or handling fees to a state functionary under various names in violation of state regulations, is punished as offering bribes"; "giving property to a state functionary because of extortion, without obtaining an improper benefit, is not bribery". Criminal Law article 390 (as amended by Criminal Law Amendment XII, in force from March 1, 2024): "whoever commits the crime of offering bribes is sentenced to up to three years of fixed-term imprisonment or criminal detention, plus a fine; where the bribery to obtain improper benefits is serious in circumstances, or causes major losses to state interests, 3 to 10 years of fixed-term imprisonment plus a fine; where the circumstances are especially serious, or the losses to state interests especially major, 10 years or more of fixed-term imprisonment or life imprisonment, plus a fine or confiscation of property"; the seven aggravating circumstances are "(1) bribing repeatedly or bribing multiple people; (2) a state functionary offering bribes; (3) offering bribes in national key projects or major projects; (4) offering bribes to obtain promotion or adjustment of position or rank; (5) bribing supervisory, administrative law-enforcement, or judicial personnel; (6) offering bribes in fields such as the eco-environment, finance, work safety, food and drugs, disaster prevention and relief, social security, education, and healthcare while carrying out unlawful or criminal activity; (7) using proceeds of unlawful activity to offer bribes"; final paragraph: "where the bribe-giver voluntarily confesses the bribery before being prosecuted, a lighter or mitigated punishment may be given; among these, where the crime is minor, or where they played a key role in breaking open or solving a major case, or performed a major meritorious service, a mitigated or exempted punishment may be given" (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会. 中华人民共和国刑法（经刑法修正案十一修正的整合文本）第三百八十九条. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808181796a636a0179822a19640c92>；全国人大常委会 (2023). 中华人民共和国刑法修正案（十二）（第五项，修改刑法第三百九十条；自 2024 年 3 月 1 日起施行）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8081818c3ce31f018cb6a6bc412f55>
- Notes: the beneficiary is yourself. What this item aims at is the thought that "a little bribery can buy exemption from punishment": the person opposite you has colleagues, case ledgers, surveillance, and inspection bodies; what you hand over is evidence against him when he is investigated, and evidence of your own crime — and this category of bribery is squarely in the expressly aggravated tier. If the other side really does demand favors, the correct move is the channels from the previous item — keep the case-acceptance receipt and paperwork, and report to the inspection body or the discipline inspection and supervisory organs; the exemption condition in Criminal Law article 389(3) is "extorted and without obtaining an improper benefit" — once you use it to get something you could not have gotten otherwise, that condition no longer holds. Cash gifts in hospitals fall under a different set of rules; see item 12 of section 24

Номер, сохранённый только в телефоне, — всё равно что не сохранённый: телефон пропал — пропали оба.
- Эффект: глобальная горячая линия МИД КНР по чрезвычайным ситуациям консульской защиты и обслуживания работает круглосуточно: внутри страны — 12308, из-за рубежа — +86-10-12308 или +86-10-65612308. С момента запуска в 2014 году линией было принято в общей сложности более 3 млн входящих звонков; за то же время МИД КНР и заграничными учреждениями было разобрано более 500 тыс. дел консульской защиты разного рода
