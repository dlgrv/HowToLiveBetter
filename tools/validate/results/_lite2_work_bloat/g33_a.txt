### 3. Do follow-up tests at the intervals your doctor gives, and record every result in the same notebook
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: a few dozen to a few hundred yuan per visit
- In plain terms: what matters in a chronic disease is a trend line, not any single reading. Keep the date, the values, and the drugs and doses you were on at the time in one place, and you won't have to redo the tests — and pay for them again — when you switch hospitals.
- Benefit: the value in chronic disease lies not in any single reading but in the trend. The same record also avoids duplicate tests and duplicate spending when changing hospitals
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 作者经验，无直接文献
- Notes: the record should include at least the date, the values, and the drugs and doses you were taking at the time. Photographing lab reports on your phone works too; the key is keeping everything in one place
