# 24. Seeking medical care: How to save money and avoid unnecessary hassle

This section focuses on the practical steps involved in getting medical treatment — which hospitals to visit first, how to transfer between levels of care, and where you can cut down on expenses. It also explains how to wait in emergency queues, what evaluations and documents are required after serious injuries, and what benefits you may be eligible for (see Section 7). Long‑term management of chronic diseases is covered in Section 16, while immediate actions at the scene of an emergency are described in Section 13.

The content is largely based on the “Several Measures on Accelerating the Development of a Hierarchical Medical System” issued by the General Office of the State Council in April 2026. This system directs patients to hospitals according to the severity of their condition. The document explicitly links using primary‑level facilities with lower medical costs. However, implementation varies by region, so the national rules outlined here should be cross‑checked with local policies issued by your social insurance and health authorities. Your “coordinated region” is simply the area where you’re enrolled in medical insurance — usually a city.
### 1. For a common illness, start at the community clinic, go up by stepwise referral, and let the inpatient deductible carry forward
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. You do have to make a trip to the community clinic first. The hard part is holding back from booking a specialist directly.
- In plain terms: Go to the community clinic first. If the clinic then refers you to a large hospital for a stay, the stretch you pay at the start carries forward, and you do not pay that stretch a second time. How the arithmetic works is set locally. In Sichuan you only make up the difference between the hospitals' deductibles. If a large hospital transfers you back to the community clinic to continue the stay, the same illness is not calculated again during that period.
- Benefit: For an insured patient referred up step by step from primary care, the inpatient deductible at the higher hospital can be calculated continuously. The deductible is the stretch you pay yourself at the start of each hospital stay. Yibao (basic medical insurance) starts reimbursing only after that stretch is paid. The State Council document does not say how continuous calculation works in detail. Localities set that. Sichuan's method is to charge only the difference between the hospitals' deductibles. The county traditional Chinese medicine hospital has a deductible of 600 yuan. Transfer to the county people's hospital (deductible 800 yuan), and you only need to pay another 200 yuan. For an inpatient transferred down from a higher hospital back to primary care, no separate deductible is set again for the same illness in the same treatment period.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「统筹地区内经基层医疗卫生机构逐级转诊的参保患者，在上级医院的住院起付线可连续计算；由上级医院下转至基层医疗卫生机构的住院患者，同一疾病周期内不再另设住院起付线。」；四川省医疗保障局 (2025). 关于省第十四届人大第四次会议第14041026号建议办理答复的函. <https://ylbzj.sc.gov.cn/scsybj/nc010414/2025/3/19/fea6f0a5f8ba416abf7ed17541afdc7a.shtml>：「仪陇县人民医院起付线是800元，仪陇县中医院起付线是600元，仪陇县中医院转仪陇县人民医院起付线只需要补200元差额即可。」
- Notes: Referral rules are set separately by each province, each city, and each medical consortium. Ask the community clinic before you go. For a chronic illness that is stable, do not go back to a large hospital just to refill medicine, because ordinary outpatient visits of this kind at large hospitals will be cut back over time. A community clinic can prescribe 12 weeks of medicine at a time. See Section 16, item 5 (a community clinic can prescribe up to 12 weeks of medicine at a time).

### 2. In the same pooling area (where you are enrolled), a lower hospital level reimburses a higher share, a gap of about 10 percentage points
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is holding back from running to a large hospital.
- In plain terms: The document requires that, for the same inpatient bill, a lower hospital level reimburses a higher share. Localities have to open a gap between levels, about 10 percentage points for each step up. On a bill of 10000 yuan, if the share paid back drops from 80% to 70%, what you pay yourself goes from 2000 yuan to 3000 yuan. Newly added yibao (basic medical insurance) funds also tilt toward primary care.
- Benefit: The document requires localities to open a gap in inpatient reimbursement across hospital levels, in principle about 10 percentage points at each step. On that gap, each step up to a higher hospital means you pay about 10% more of the reimbursable bill yourself. Take 10000 yuan of reimbursable inpatient charges. If the reimbursement share drops from 80% to 70%, what you pay yourself goes from 2000 yuan to 3000 yuan. The extra 1000 yuan is a tenth of the total bill, but what you pay yourself is half again what you used to pay. Actual shares differ by place, and the deductible and the cap also move the gap. This is only an illustration using the document's 10 percentage points. Newly added yibao funds for the year also tilt toward primary care.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「因地制宜适当拉开参保人员在不同等级医疗卫生机构的住院报销水平，原则上统筹地区内医疗卫生机构住院报销比例逐级拉开10个百分点左右的差距。」「具备条件的地方，可按分级诊疗导向酌情拉开参保人员在不同等级医疗卫生机构的门诊报销水平。」
- Notes: For the same inpatient bill, the difference you pay yourself between primary care and a tertiary hospital can be a few thousand yuan. An illness that can be finished at primary care should not be taken up the ladder. That does not mean every illness has to be seen at the community clinic.

### 3. To reach a large hospital, go through a primary-care referral or the hospital's referral center, and do not go to a scalper
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. There is an extra referral step to complete.
- In plain terms: The lead hospital in a medical consortium has to hold back a share of appointment slots and beds for people referred up from primary care. The hospital also has to set up a referral center, or name a fixed department to handle referrals, with full coverage by 2027. Failing to get a slot does not mean there is no way in. You do not need to go to a scalper.
- Benefit: The lead hospital of a tight medical consortium must reserve a share of appointment slots and beds for primary care, and must take in, without delay, patients referred up from primary care. A tight medical consortium is a group of hospitals and community health institutions bound together to work as a set. A referral up from the community clinic therefore has its own slots and beds, and does not have to compete with people grabbing slots in the lobby. Medical institutions must set up a referral center or name a fixed department, with full coverage by 2027.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「牵头医院要为基层医疗卫生机构预留一定比例的号源和床位，并及时接诊经基层转诊患者。」「医疗机构要强化转诊服务统一管理，设立转诊中心或指定固定部门承担患者转诊服务工作，到2027年实现全覆盖。」
- Notes: The reserved slots are kept for referred patients. If you cannot grab a slot, you can still go through referral. If you cannot find the referral center, call the hospital's service line and ask.

### 4. Before going out of province for care, ask locally first. In principle, whether it is necessary is assessed by an associate chief physician or above
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Ask once more before you set out.
- In plain terms: Whether you need to go elsewhere for care is, in principle, assessed by a doctor at associate chief physician rank or above, at a secondary or tertiary hospital. How much temporary care away from home is reimbursed differs from care at a hospital of the same level where you are enrolled. The local rules say by how much. Asking locally before you set out is better than arriving elsewhere and only then finding it will not be reimbursed.
- Benefit: Whether cross-pool and cross-province care elsewhere is necessary is, in principle, assessed by staff at associate chief physician rank or above at a secondary or tertiary hospital. Crossing pooling areas means leaving the place where you are enrolled in yibao (basic medical insurance). Whether it is necessary to go elsewhere is, in principle, something a doctor of sufficient rank has to say. The reimbursement level for people who temporarily seek care out of province keeps a reasonable gap from institutions of the same level where they are enrolled. For the same illness seen elsewhere, the money that comes back is a step lower than at home.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「原则上由二、三级医院副主任医师及以上职称人员评估患者跨统筹地区、跨省异地就医的必要性。」「进一步强化异地就医直接结算管理服务，跨省临时外出就医人员报销水平与参保地同级别医疗机构报销水平保持合理差异。」
- Notes: Temporary care away from home and long-term residence elsewhere are different filings, and the reimbursement level is not the same. How to file, and which illnesses can be settled directly, is in Section 16. Do not rush off elsewhere and only then think about reimbursement.

### 5. After every visit, keep your own copy of the records, the test reports, and the images
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. A hospital may charge a materials fee for copies, as the rules allow, and the fee schedule has to be public. The hard part is remembering to copy a set after every visit.
- In plain terms: Looking up and copying your own records is a right the law gives you. Outpatient records, the inpatient record, lab slips, images, surgery and anesthesia records, pathology material, and the bill can all be copied. The hospital has to name a department or a person to take the request, and it may charge only the materials fee. Changing hospitals, having another doctor look again, claiming on commercial insurance, and a real dispute all rest on this stack of papers.
- Benefit: The rules list looking up and copying the medical record as a patient's right. The scope covers the outpatient record, the inpatient record, lab slips, medical images, surgery and anesthesia records, pathology material, medical charges, and the rest of the file. Changing hospitals, having a second doctor look again, claiming on commercial insurance, and a real dispute all rest on these papers.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2018). 医疗纠纷预防和处理条例（国务院令第 701 号）第十六条：「患者有权查阅、复制其门诊病历、住院志、体温单、医嘱单、化验单（检验报告）、医学影像检查资料、特殊检查同意书、手术同意书、手术及麻醉记录、病理资料、护理记录、医疗费用以及国务院卫生主管部门规定的其他属于病历的全部资料。」「患者要求复制病历资料的，医疗机构应当提供复制服务，并在复制的病历资料上加盖证明印记……可以收取工本费，收费标准应当公开。」「患者死亡的，其近亲属可以依照本条例的规定，查阅、复制病历资料。」第十五条「任何单位和个人不得篡改、伪造、隐匿、毁灭或者抢夺病历资料。」<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>；国家卫生计生委、国家中医药管理局 (2013). 医疗机构病历管理规定（2013 年版）：医疗机构「应当指定部门或者专（兼）职人员负责受理复制病历资料的申请」，复制在申请人在场的情况下进行并加盖证明印记. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2600084.htm>
- Notes: Copying is handled by the department the hospital names. You do not have to ask a doctor for a favor. For the list of what to copy, read out Article 16 of the Regulations on the Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes. First come the outpatient record, the inpatient record, the temperature chart, the order sheet, lab slips, and images. Then come the surgery and anesthesia records, pathology material, nursing notes, and the medical bill. After you have them, file them in time order. What matters most in the folder is the discharge summary, the operative note, the pathology report, and the key images. Write each round of chronic-illness readings in the same notebook. See Section 16.

### 6. If you have doubts about treatment, request to seal the medical records on the spot; both parties must be present, a list must be drawn up, and each side gets a copy
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: No cost at all. Simply ask the hospital to seal the records; both you and the medical staff must be present to complete the paperwork. The real challenge is mustering the courage to say this out loud in front of the doctors.

- In plain terms: If you’re unsure about any aspect of your treatment, you have the right to request that your medical records be sealed. Both you and the medical staff must be present for this process, which involves creating a detailed list, signing and stamping it, and each side receiving a copy. If the records aren’t fully completed yet, only the finished portions should be sealed first; the rest can be sealed later. Should you suspect that an intravenous infusion, blood transfusion, injection, or medication caused a problem, the actual fluid bag, medicine vial, and infusion set must also be sealed together with the records.

- Benefit: Regulations clearly state that “whenever a medical dispute arises and it is necessary to seal or unseal medical records, this must be done while both the patient and medical staff are present.” The sealed documents may be either originals or copies, which the hospital then stores. If the records are still being written, only the completed sections should be sealed first; the remainder can be sealed later. The hospital must produce a sealing list, signed or stamped by both parties, with each side keeping a copy. In cases where an adverse effect is suspected to stem from an IV, blood transfusion, injection, or medication, both parties must jointly seal and later unseal any related physical items. If testing is required, both parties must jointly appoint a qualified laboratory; should they fail to agree, the local health authority at the county level where the hospital is located will make the appointment. Should a transfusion-related problem be suspected and blood samples need sealing, the hospital must notify the blood bank to send representatives to the scene. Additionally, there is a strict rule: “No organization or individual may alter, forge, conceal, destroy, or seize medical records.” Once a dispute is resolved or three years pass without any further claims, the hospital may unseal the documents on its own initiative (nationwide, effective 1 October 2018).

- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2018). 医疗纠纷预防和处理条例（国务院令第 701 号，第十五、二十四、二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>

- Notes: Sealing records and making copies are two distinct actions. Copying records is something you should do proactively, as explained in item 5 (always keep your own copies of medical records and imaging). Sealing, on the other hand, serves to preserve evidence at the moment a dispute arises, preventing any later alterations or additions. If you suspect that an IV, blood transfusion, injection, or medication caused a problem, you must seal not only the records but also the fluid bag, medicine vial, and infusion set; otherwise they might be removed or discarded. Hospitals are required to prominently display information about dispute resolution procedures, contact details, and to maintain a dedicated complaints department. In case of any issue, approach that department rather than confronting the attending physician directly.

### 7. For a severe injury or illness, go straight to the emergency triage desk, and do not queue at the registration window
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. Walk a few more steps inside the door to find the triage desk. Say the most dangerous symptom in a single sentence.
- In plain terms: The emergency department does not go by who arrived earlier. It sorts people into Level I through Level IV by how serious the condition is. The place that sets the level is the triage desk at the emergency entrance, not the registration window. When you arrive, report the most dangerous thing: heavy bleeding, not being able to get air, not being fully conscious, chest pain like a heart problem, or pain you cannot stand.
- Benefit: The National Health Commission's 2024 edition of the emergency-medicine quality-control indicators says emergency triage must "sort patients into levels by how critical the condition is." "Level I is a critical emergency and needs treatment at once." "Level II is a severe emergency, and assessment and treatment often go on together." "Level III is an urgent case and needs to be seen within a short time." "Level IV is a non-urgent or less urgent case and is seen in order in the ordinary treatment area." Triage means that, on entering the emergency department, someone first rates how serious it is and sets the order of care. Beijing's implementation standard gives the response required at each level. Level 1: "assess and treat at once, and move the patient into the resuscitation room or the rescue room." Level 2: "monitor vital signs at once, start treatment within 10 minutes, and move the patient into the rescue room." Level 3: "treat ahead of level 4 patients, and have the patient wait in the ordinary treatment area; if the wait is longer than 30 minutes, assess again." Level 4: "seen in order, and the wait is long unless the condition changes; if the wait is longer than 4 hours, another assessment is allowed." In the Beijing standard, the manual rating indicators for level 1 include "acute disturbance of consciousness." Those for level 2 include "active or severe blood loss," "severe difficulty breathing," "chest pain that looks like a heart cause," and "severe pain from any cause (7 to 10 points)." Triage must "keep to the rule that critical and severe cases are seen first," and the entrance of the emergency department should have a triage area in a prominent place (the level standard is the national rule, and the response times are the Beijing standard).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2024). 关于印发急诊医学等 6 个专业医疗质量控制指标（2024 年版）的通知（国卫办医政函〔2024〕150 号）附件 1，指标四「急诊分级分诊执行率」. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202405/content_6951260.htm>；北京市卫生健康委员会 (2019). 北京市加强急诊预检分诊分级工作方案及附件《北京市医院急诊预检分诊分级标准（试行）》：「坚持急危重症优先就诊原则。按照患者病情的严重程度，遵循从重到轻、从病情迅速变化到相对稳定的原则，合理安排患者就诊顺序。」「在急诊科入口的显著位置，设置急诊预检分诊区域」「院前医疗急救医生应按照相关标准对患者进行初步分级，对1、2级患者需及时通知所送医院急诊科做好接诊准备」. <http://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk_20040/ylws/201912/t20191216_1242338.html>
- Notes: Sorting into levels is the same across the country. How soon each level is handled is set by each province and city. This Beijing text is a version that can be checked word for word. The rating looks at heart rate, blood pressure, blood oxygen, and symptoms. Pain is among the items. Pain at 7 to 10 points can be rated level 2. The hard indicators for level 1 include the following. A heart rate above 180 beats a minute, or below 40 beats a minute. A systolic pressure below 70 mmHg. Systolic pressure is the higher reading in a blood-pressure pair. Blood oxygen below 80%. Shock, a definite myocardial infarction, or acute disturbance of consciousness. If you think triage set you too low, add the most dangerous symptom and say it clearly. If the condition changes while you wait, go back to the triage desk and get rated again at once. When it is truly critical, call 120 (see Section 13). Beijing's plan requires the pre-hospital emergency doctor to give a first rating by the standard, and for levels 1 and 2 to notify the emergency department of the receiving hospital in time so it can prepare to take the patient.

### 8. With no money, no papers, and no way to say who you are, emergency care still has to come first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money.
- In plain terms: The state has a disease emergency-assistance fund that pays emergency fees for both of these groups: people whose identity cannot be established, and people who cannot pay. A hospital must not refuse or delay the rescue, and an emergency center must not keep the ambulance in because of money. So do not stay home and endure it because you have no money, and do not skip calling an ambulance for fear of the bill. This money covers only the emergency. The hospital stay after that still goes through yibao (basic medical insurance) and medical assistance.
- Benefit: The people the disease emergency-assistance system covers are "patients who suffer an acute, severe, or critical injury or illness inside China, who need emergency care, but whose identity is unclear or who cannot pay the corresponding fees." The fund pays both kinds of fees: "emergency fees incurred by a patient whose identity cannot be established," and "emergency fees left unpaid by a patient whose identity is clear but who cannot pay." The document also says "medical institutions of every kind, and their staff, must treat a patient with an acute, severe, or critical injury in time and effectively, and must not refuse, shift, or delay treatment for any reason." Pre-hospital emergency care has a rule too. The national pre-hospital medical emergency number is "120." An emergency center (station) "must not refuse, shift, or delay pre-hospital medical emergency service because of dispatch," and also "must not refuse or delay pre-hospital medical emergency service because of fees." Transport follows "the principles of nearness, urgency, meeting the professional need, and taking the patient's wishes into account" (nationwide, since 2013).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院办公厅 (2013). 关于建立疾病应急救助制度的指导意见（国办发〔2013〕15 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2013-03/01/content_6069.htm>；国家卫生和计划生育委员会 (2014). 院前医疗急救管理办法（委令第 3 号，第十三、二十二、二十三、二十五条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2580977.htm>
- Notes: The fund pays only the "emergency fee" stretch. Treatment comes first, and the institution applies to the fund afterward. You do not collect the money at a window. If you have yibao, you still use yibao first, as usual. The application steps and the caps differ by province. In the transport principles, "nearness and urgency" come before "the patient's wishes," so the ambulance will not necessarily take you to the hospital you name. When the case is critical, the nearest place that can handle it is the rule. If what you still pay yourself afterward is still too heavy, use medical assistance. See Section 7, item 10 (medical assistance).

### 9. A disability assessment waits until treatment has ended, or until the clinical result is stable
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: The assessment fee runs from a few hundred yuan to 2000 yuan. The side that applies usually advances it. You wait until treatment has ended, or until the clinical result is stable. How long that takes depends on the injury. The doctor decides. The hard part is wanting the rating fixed before the injury has properly healed, and holding that back.
- In plain terms: How much is paid depends mainly on which grade is given, and the grade depends on how much function is still lost after treatment. The standard says the assessment is done after treatment has ended, or after the clinical result is stable. Going for a rating before the bone has healed does not meet that requirement. An ordinary fracture that has healed and does not affect function generally does not get a grade.
- Benefit: The assessment principle in the Classification of the Degree of Disability Caused by Human Injury is that it "shall take the outcome or the end result of treatment of the injury as the basis, and shall objectively rate the loss of tissue or organ and/or the degree of functional impairment." The standard says the timing "shall be after treatment of the primary injury, and of complications that are truly linked to it, has ended, or after the clinical result is stable." The standard "divides the degree of disability caused by human injury into 10 grades, from the first grade (a human disability rate of 100%) to the tenth grade (a human disability rate of 10%), with each grade 10% apart in disability rate." The basis of judgment is "destruction of the structure of human tissues and organs, functional impairment, and how far the person depends on medical care and nursing, with due regard to the effect of the disability on social contact and on psychological factors." Clauses in the tenth grade that relate to fractures include the following. The first is "a compression fracture of a single vertebral body (compression reaching 1/3) or a comminuted fracture." The second is "fractures, or a comminuted fracture, at more than a single site on the pelvis, healed with deformity." The third is "loss of 25% or more of function in any large joint of the limbs (excluding the ankle)." Disability of the same site and the same nature must not be counted twice. Where there is disability at more than a single site, each grade should be written separately (nationwide, in force from January (1) 1, 2017).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部 (2016). 关于发布《人体损伤致残程度分级》的公告（2016 年 4 月 18 日发布，2017 年 1 月 1 日施行），标准第 4.1、4.2、4.3、4.4、4.5、5.10.6、6.2 条. <https://www.moj.gov.cn/pub/sfbgw/zwxxgk/fdzdgknr/fdzdgknrtzwj/201908/P020210316701681025917.pdf>
- Notes: The rating systems do not substitute for each other. Do not mix them up. For an assault, a traffic accident, or other personal injury, the judicial assessment uses the Classification of the Degree of Disability Caused by Human Injury, and it is used to calculate disability compensation (see Section 9). A work injury goes through a work-capacity assessment, which uses another set of disability grades and governs work-injury benefits (see Section 19). To receive the policy benefits for disabled people, you apply separately for a disability certificate (see item 10, the disability certificate). Before the assessment, have the records, the operative notes, and the follow-up images ready (see item 5, keep your own copy of the records and images). The standard does not say whether an early assessment comes out low or high. That is inferred from the principle "take the outcome or the end result of treatment of the injury as the basis." An early assessment rates how things look then. Function that has not yet recovered may later get better, or it may get worse. When this injury is mixed with an old problem that was already there, the opinion also has to say how large a share of the disability result this injury accounts for. That line directly affects how much is paid.

### 10. After treatment, functional impairments may indeed remain; apply for a disability certificate at the county-level disability association in your hukou area

### 11. Thank the doctors who saved you — send thank-you letters, banners, or satisfaction ratings instead of cash gifts. The rules prohibit money, not gratitude

- In plain terms: Paying money in advance will not buy you a better place in line. Appointment slots, beds, scarce drugs, and the scheduling of surgery must not be traded for a favor. - Benefit: Article 7 of the “Nine Guidelines on Ethical Conduct for Medical Staff” (No. 41, 2021) states: “Maintain orderly medical practices and ensure fairness. Likewise, attending paid meals or entertainment arranged by patients is prohibited. Hospitals must enforce these rules, conduct inspections, and penalize violations. Satisfaction ratings now form part of performance evaluations for all tertiary public hospitals under national policy. According to official documents, hospital satisfaction comprises both patient and staff satisfaction, measured via surveys of outpatients, inpatients, and medical staff. A unified national platform collects these results, which are then factored into hospital performance reviews. Hospitals must also handle patient complaints per regulations, establishing dedicated offices to receive, investigate, and respond to concerns regarding medical services, safety, and quality. The basis is the State Council General Office opinion, document (2019) 4: the performance index system has 4 groups of indicators. By the end of March (3) 2019, every tertiary public hospital was to be on the national satisfaction-survey platform. Complaints are governed by the medical-institution complaint measures, Order No. 3, effective 2019.

- Notes: A report to the yibao department is about the hospital misusing the yibao fund. What the regulations write is an order to correct, a return of yibao fund money, and a fine. They do not write that the extra you paid is returned to you. Getting back the extra you paid yourself depends mainly on the hospital correcting the charge, so go to the hospital's price-complaint window first, and keep the bill and the record of what was said. Suing in court over a few hundred yuan, with the ordinary procedure of a first-instance trial starting at 6 months and the lawyer's fee paid by you, is usually not worth it. A report has to give a concrete lead: which line on the bill, which day, and how many times it was charged. If you only want to ask about reimbursement policy, call the yibao inquiry line. That is not a report. An item you cannot read is worth asking the nurses' station or the admissions office about. It is not necessarily an improper charge. A drug yibao will not reimburse is not necessarily a drug you should not use. Ask how much it costs per day, and whether there is a reimbursable substitute, before you sign. At discharge, ask for a full bill and keep it with the records. See item 5 of this section (keep your own copy of the records and images). The benefit magnitude is set at medium, by judgment: no official figure was found for how much a single patient overpays. Under this book's thresholds, a few hundred to a few thousand yuan counts as medium. The beneficiary is mainly you and your family. Checking the bill for an elder in hospital matters especially.
