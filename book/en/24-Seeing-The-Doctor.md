# 24. Seeing a doctor: how to spend less and take fewer detours

Scope: money and time. This section is about how the process of seeking care itself works — where to go first, how referrals work, where the savings are, how to queue in the emergency department, and which assessments and certificates to obtain after treatment for a serious injury. For what benefits you can claim, see Section 7; for long-term management of chronic illness, see Section 16; for what to do first at the scene of an emergency, see Section 13.

This section is based mainly on the measures issued by the General Office of the State Council in April 2026 on accelerating the building of a tiered diagnosis and treatment system. That document ties "starting at the primary (community) level" directly to "spending less", but implementation timelines differ by locality. The main text sets out national-level rules; for the specifics of the pooling region (统筹地区 — the locality where your medical insurance is pooled and paid out) where you live, follow the guidance of the local medical-insurance and health authorities.
### 1. Start common ailments at the community clinic, get referred up through primary care tier by tier, and the inpatient deductible carries over

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; the price is one extra trip to the community clinic first, and accepting that you will not book a specialist slot directly
- In plain terms: when you are admitted upward to a higher-level hospital through step-by-step referral from primary care, the deductible you must cover out of pocket for each hospital stay counts only once. If you walk straight into a tertiary hospital on your own and later transfer back to a community facility to recover, you may end up paying it twice. And when a hospital refers you back down to primary care to continue the same stay, no new deductible is set within the same illness episode.
- Benefit: for insured patients referred up through primary care, the inpatient deductible at the higher-level hospital can be counted consecutively; for patients referred back down from a higher-level hospital to primary care, no additional deductible is set within the same disease cycle
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「统筹地区内经基层医疗卫生机构逐级转诊的参保患者，在上级医院的住院起付线可连续计算；由上级医院下转至基层医疗卫生机构的住院患者，同一疾病周期内不再另设住院起付线。」
- Notes: the deductible is the portion you pay yourself each admission. Going straight to a tertiary hospital on your own and then transferring back down to primary care for recovery can mean paying it twice; going through the referral route means it is counted once. Referral rules are set separately by each province, city and medical consortium, so ask the community clinic before you go.

### 2. Within the same pooling region, the lower the hospital tier, the higher the reimbursement rate — a gap of about 10 percentage points

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: for the same hospital bill, the lower the hospital's tier, the more gets reimbursed. Localities are widening the gap in the direction of roughly ten percentage points per tier, and newly added medical-insurance funds are also tilted toward primary care. If you take something a community clinic could handle to a big hospital instead, the difference you pay yourself can run to several thousand yuan.
- Benefit: the document requires localities to widen the gap in inpatient reimbursement levels across tiers of medical institutions, in principle by about 10 percentage points per tier; annual increases in medical-insurance funds are also tilted toward primary care
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「因地制宜适当拉开参保人员在不同等级医疗卫生机构的住院报销水平，原则上统筹地区内医疗卫生机构住院报销比例逐级拉开10个百分点左右的差距。」「具备条件的地方，可按分级诊疗导向酌情拉开参保人员在不同等级医疗卫生机构的门诊报销水平。」
- Notes: for the same hospital bill, the difference in what you pay out of pocket at a primary facility versus a tertiary hospital can be several thousand yuan. This does not say every illness should be treated at the community level — it says do not go up-tier for what can be handled at the primary level.

### 3. To get into a big hospital, use primary-care referral or the hospital's referral center — never ticket scalpers

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one extra referral procedure
- In plain terms: big hospitals have to set aside a share of appointment slots and beds for people referred up from primary care, and must set up a referral center or a designated department to handle referrals, with full coverage by 2027. Failing to snag a booking does not mean there is no route. A scalper's slot is not just expensive — it can also land you in a department that does not match your condition.
- Benefit: lead hospitals of close-knit medical consortia must reserve a certain proportion of appointment slots and beds for primary care and promptly receive patients referred up from primary level; medical institutions must set up a referral center or designate a fixed department, with full coverage by 2027
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「牵头医院要为基层医疗卫生机构预留一定比例的号源和床位，并及时接诊经基层转诊患者。」「医疗机构要强化转诊服务统一管理，设立转诊中心或指定固定部门承担患者转诊服务工作，到2027年实现全覆盖。」
- Notes: reserved slots are a dedicated channel for referred patients — not winning one in the open booking rush does not mean there is no route. The scalper route is expensive and can drop you into the wrong department. If you cannot find the referral center, call the hospital's service line and ask.

### 4. Before seeking care across provinces, ask once at home first — the necessity is in principle assessed by a doctor of associate-chief-physician rank or above

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one extra question asked
- In plain terms: whether you should travel elsewhere for treatment is in principle assessed by a doctor of associate chief physician (副主任医师 — the second-highest full-time physician rank in Chinese hospitals) title or above at a secondary or tertiary hospital. And for care sought while temporarily away, the reimbursement level runs a notch below what the same-tier hospital in your insurance enrollment area would pay. Asking at home before you set off beats discovering after arrival that it will not be reimbursed.
- Benefit: the necessity of cross-region or cross-province medical care is in principle assessed by personnel with the title of associate chief physician or above at secondary or tertiary hospitals; the reimbursement level for people seeking care temporarily outside the province is kept at a reasonable difference from same-tier institutions in the enrollment area
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「原则上由二、三级医院副主任医师及以上职称人员评估患者跨统筹地区、跨省异地就医的必要性。」「进一步强化异地就医直接结算管理服务，跨省临时外出就医人员报销水平与参保地同级别医疗机构报销水平保持合理差异。」
- Notes: "temporary out-of-town care" and "long-term residence elsewhere" are two different filings (备案 — registration with your insurance authority before seeking care outside your home region), with different reimbursement levels. How to file, and which categories of illness can be settled directly, are in Section 16. Do not wait until you are already out of town to think about reimbursement.

### 5. Stop using tertiary hospitals as prescription counters — bring stable chronic conditions back to primary care

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; requires dropping the "only big hospitals can be trusted" habit
- In plain terms: tertiary hospitals are being steered toward acute, critical and complex cases; routine follow-up visits for common ailments and outpatient care for diagnosed, stable chronic conditions will be phased down, while specialist teams' routine outpatient services shift toward extending into community settings. If you treat a big hospital as your prescription counter, booking a slot will get harder and harder.
- Benefit: tertiary hospitals are to focus on acute, critical, and difficult and complex diseases, gradually reducing routine outpatient services such as follow-up visits for common ailments and chronic diseases that are clearly diagnosed and stable; higher-level hospitals will extend specialist teams' routine outpatient services down to the primary level
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「三级医院要聚焦急危重症和疑难复杂疾病，加强转诊会诊和住院服务，逐步酌减常见病复诊和诊断明确、病情稳定的慢性病等普通门诊。」「紧密型医联体内上级医院要在基层医疗卫生机构开设高血压、糖尿病、慢性阻塞性肺疾病等常见病、慢性病门诊，提供诊疗和健康管理服务，并将专家团队普通门诊向基层医疗卫生机构延伸。」
- Notes: this is a policy direction, not advice: routine outpatient slots at big hospitals will keep getting harder to book. For stable chronic conditions, primary care can prescribe up to 12 weeks of medication at a time — see Section 16.

### 6. After every visit, keep your own copy of the medical record, test reports and imaging

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; the hospital may charge a copying cost fee as permitted, and the fee schedule must be public
- In plain terms: reviewing and copying your own medical record is a right granted by law, covering outpatient records, inpatient notes, lab slips, imaging, surgery and anesthesia records, pathology material and itemized bills; the hospital has a dedicated window to handle requests, may only charge a copying cost fee, and the standard must be published. Changing hospitals, seeking a second opinion, claiming on commercial insurance, or actually ending up in a dispute — all of these rest on this stack of paper.
- Benefit: regulations explicitly list reviewing and copying medical records as a patient's right, with scope covering outpatient records, inpatient notes, lab reports, medical imaging, surgery and anesthesia records, pathology material, medical expenses and all other records; changing hospitals, getting a second opinion, claiming on commercial insurance, or handling a dispute all rely on these
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2018). 医疗纠纷预防和处理条例（国务院令第 701 号）第十六条：「患者有权查阅、复制其门诊病历、住院志、体温单、医嘱单、化验单（检验报告）、医学影像检查资料、特殊检查同意书、手术同意书、手术及麻醉记录、病理资料、护理记录、医疗费用以及国务院卫生主管部门规定的其他属于病历的全部资料。」「患者要求复制病历资料的，医疗机构应当提供复制服务，并在复制的病历资料上加盖证明印记……可以收取工本费，收费标准应当公开。」「患者死亡的，其近亲属可以依照本条例的规定，查阅、复制病历资料。」第十五条「任何单位和个人不得篡改、伪造、隐匿、毁灭或者抢夺病历资料。」<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>；国家卫生计生委、国家中医药管理局 (2013). 医疗机构病历管理规定（2013 年版）：医疗机构「应当指定部门或者专（兼）职人员负责受理复制病历资料的申请」，复制在申请人在场的情况下进行并加盖证明印记. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2600084.htm>
- Notes: copying is handled at a dedicated window — you do not need to plead with a doctor. For the list of items to copy, just read out Article 16 of the medical-record regulation. Keep one folder in chronological order; the discharge summary, surgery record, pathology report and key imaging matter most. Log each round of chronic-disease test values in the same notebook — see Section 16.

### 7. If you have doubts about your treatment, demand the record be sealed on the spot — both parties present, an itemized list drawn up, one copy each

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; submit the sealing request to the medical institution and wait for both parties to complete the formalities together
- In plain terms: if you have doubts about your treatment, you can demand that the medical record be sealed: done with both parties present, with an itemized list drawn up, signatures or stamps applied, and one copy held by each side; if the record is not yet finished, the completed part is sealed first. If you suspect a problem with an infusion, blood transfusion, injection or medication, that bag of fluid, that vial and the infusion set must be sealed together as physical evidence. Once sealed, no one can add to or alter it afterward.
- Benefit: the regulation states that "where a medical dispute requires sealing or unsealing of medical record materials, it shall be carried out in the presence of both the medical and patient sides"; what is sealed may be the original or copies, kept by the medical institution; where a record is not yet complete and needs sealing, the completed portion is sealed first, and the rest sealed after completion; the medical institution must draw up a sealing list for the sealed record, signed or stamped by both sides, one copy each. Where infusion, blood transfusion, injection, medication or the like is suspected of causing adverse consequences, both sides shall jointly seal and unseal the physical items on site; where testing is needed, both sides shall jointly entrust a qualified testing institution, and if they cannot agree, the health authority of the county-level people's government where the medical institution is located designates one; where a blood transfusion is suspected and blood must be sealed, the medical institution shall notify the blood station to send personnel on site. One more hard constraint: "No organization or individual may tamper with, forge, conceal, destroy or seize medical record materials." After sealing, once the dispute is resolved, or if 3 years pass with no further request to resolve it, the medical institution may unseal it on its own (nationwide, effective October 1, 2018)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2018). 医疗纠纷预防和处理条例（国务院令第 701 号，第十五、二十四、二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>
- Notes: sealing and copying are two different things: copying is what you do routinely (see item 6), sealing is what fixes the evidence in place when a dispute arises, guarding against after-the-fact additions and alterations. If infusion, transfusion or medication is suspected, seal that bag of fluid, that vial and the infusion set along with the record — do not let anyone take them away and throw them out. Medical institutions must publish the channels, procedures and contact points for resolving medical disputes in a prominent place and set up a unified complaint-handling department — go to that department first rather than arguing with the doctor on duty.

### 8. For an urgent or critical injury or illness, head straight to the emergency triage desk — do not queue at the registration window

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; walk a few extra steps to the triage desk after entering and state the most dangerous symptom in one sentence
- In plain terms: the emergency department does not work first-come-first-served; it sorts patients into four levels by severity. Level 1, the critically dying, go straight into the resuscitation or emergency room; level 2, critical, must be treated within 10 minutes; level 3, urgent, are seen ahead of level 4 and re-assessed if waiting more than 30 minutes; level 4, non-urgent, wait in order and get re-assessed only after 4 hours of waiting. The place your level is assigned is the pre-hospital triage desk at the emergency entrance, not the registration window — skip triage and you have no level in the system at all, and however badly you are hurt you will only inch along with the queue. When you arrive, report the most dangerous item first: heavy bleeding, gasping for breath, altered consciousness, something impaled in the body, crushing chest pain. Conversely, if you only need a prescription, stitches removed or a look at an old complaint, you will be classed level 4, and the emergency department will just mean a longer wait.
- Benefit: the National Health Commission's 2024 emergency-medicine quality-control indicators stipulate that emergency pre-hospital triage "classifies patients into four levels by severity of condition: Level I, critically urgent patients who require immediate treatment; Level II, acute and severe patients, for whom assessment and treatment usually proceed simultaneously; Level III, acute patients who need diagnosis and treatment within a short time; Level IV, non-urgent or sub-acute patients, who are seen in order in the general care area". Beijing's implementing standard sets the response requirements for each level: level 1, "assess and treat immediately, place the patient in the resuscitation or emergency room"; level 2, "monitor vital signs immediately, treat within 10 minutes, place the patient in the emergency room"; level 3, "treat ahead of level 4 patients, have the patient wait in the general care area; if waiting exceeds 30 minutes, re-assess"; level 4, "be seen in order, with long waits unless the condition changes; if waiting exceeds 4 hours, re-assessment may be performed"; triage must "adhere to the principle that acute, critical and severe cases are seen first", and a pre-hospital triage area must be set up in a prominent position at the emergency department entrance (the four-level standard is national; the response times are Beijing's standard)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2024). 关于印发急诊医学等 6 个专业医疗质量控制指标（2024 年版）的通知（国卫办医政函〔2024〕150 号）附件 1，指标四「急诊分级分诊执行率」. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202405/content_6951260.htm>；北京市卫生健康委员会 (2019). 北京市加强急诊预检分诊分级工作方案及附件《北京市医院急诊预检分诊分级标准（试行）》：「坚持急危重症优先就诊原则。按照患者病情的严重程度，遵循从重到轻、从病情迅速变化到相对稳定的原则，合理安排患者就诊顺序。」「在急诊科入口的显著位置，设置急诊预检分诊区域」. <http://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk_20040/ylws/201912/t20191216_1242338.html>
- Notes: four-level triage is the national standard, but the response time at each level is set by each province and city — the Beijing document is a version that can be checked word for word. Classification looks at vital signs and symptoms, not how much pain you feel you are in: hard indicators for level 1 include heart rate above 180 beats/min or below 40 beats/min, systolic blood pressure below 70 mmHg, blood oxygen below 80%, shock, confirmed myocardial infarction, and acute altered consciousness. If you think your triage level was set too low, add the most dangerous symptom in plain words; if your condition changes while waiting, return to the triage desk for re-assessment immediately. When things are truly critical, calling 120 is faster than getting there yourself — the pre-hospital physician applies the same standard for an initial classification and alerts the hospital in advance (see Section 13).

### 9. With no money, no documents, or unable to say who you are, the emergency department must still treat you first

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: the state runs a disease emergency relief fund that specifically pays the emergency-care costs of two kinds of people: those whose identity cannot be established, and those whose identity is clear but who cannot pay. Medical institutions must not refuse, pass off or delay treatment for any reason, and emergency centers must not refuse or delay dispatching an ambulance over cost. So do not tough it out at home just because you have no money on you, and do not hold back from calling an ambulance for fear of an unpaid bill. This money covers only the emergency segment; hospitalization that follows still goes through medical insurance and medical assistance.
- Benefit: the beneficiaries of the disease emergency relief system are "patients within China who suffer an acute, severe or critical illness or injury requiring emergency care but whose identity is unclear or who are unable to pay the corresponding costs"; the fund pays "emergency costs incurred for patients whose identity cannot be established" and "emergency costs unpaid by patients whose identity is clear but who are unable to pay"; "medical institutions of all levels and types and their staff must promptly and effectively treat patients with acute, severe or critical conditions, and must not refuse, pass off or delay treatment for any reason". On the pre-hospital side: the national pre-hospital medical emergency call number is "120"; emergency centers (stations) "must not refuse, pass off or delay pre-hospital medical emergency services on grounds of command or dispatch", nor "refuse or delay pre-hospital medical emergency services over cost issues"; transport follows "the principles of nearest facility, most urgent need, satisfying professional requirements, while taking the patient's wishes into account" (nationwide, from 2013)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2013). 关于建立疾病应急救助制度的指导意见（国办发〔2013〕15 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2013-03/01/content_6069.htm>；国家卫生和计划生育委员会 (2014). 院前医疗急救管理办法（委令第 3 号，第十三、二十二、二十三、二十五条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2580977.htm>
- Notes: the fund covers only the "emergency costs" segment, and treatment comes first — the medical institution applies to the fund afterward; you do not collect money at a window. Those with medical insurance still go through insurance first as usual, and each province's application procedure and limits differ. In the transport principles, "nearest and most urgent" rank ahead of "patient's wishes", so the ambulance will not necessarily take you to the hospital you name — when critical, the nearest one that can handle the case prevails. If the out-of-pocket burden afterward is still too heavy, turn to medical assistance (see Section 7).

### 10. Get the disability assessment after treatment has concluded — assessed too early, the grade comes out lower

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: an assessment fee from a few hundred to 2,000 yuan, generally advanced by the applicant; requires waiting until treatment has concluded or the clinical outcome is stable — usually three to six months after the injury or longer
- In plain terms: how much compensation you get depends mainly on the grade assessed, and the grade depends on how much functional impairment remains once treatment is done. The standard states the assessment must be done after treatment has concluded or the clinical outcome is stable; if you go get assessed while the bone has not yet healed or the plate has not yet been removed, what gets assessed is how you look at that moment — you are effectively pushing your own grade down. Grade 10 corresponds to a disability rate of 10%, each level up adds 10%, and grade 1 is 100%. For a fracture to reach grade 10 it must fall under a specific clause — for example one vertebral body compressed by one third, fractures in two or more places in the pelvis healing with deformity, or loss of 25% or more of function in a major limb joint. An ordinary fracture that heals without affecting function does not qualify.
- Benefit: the grading of disability caused by human body injury specifies that the timing of assessment "shall be after treatment of the primary injury and its clearly related complications has concluded or the clinical treatment outcome has stabilized"; "this standard divides the degree of disability from human body injury into 10 grades, from Grade 1 (disability rate 100%) to Grade 10 (disability rate 10%), with each grade differing by 10% in disability rate"; the basis of judgment is "destruction of the structure of human tissues and organs, functional impairment, and the degree of dependence on medical care and nursing, with due consideration of the social-interaction and psychological effects caused by the disability"; the grade-10 clauses related to fractures include "a compression fracture of one vertebral body (compression reaching 1/3) or a comminuted fracture", "fractures in two or more sites of the pelvis or comminuted pelvic fractures, healing with deformity", and "loss of 25% or more of function in any major joint of the limbs (excluding the ankle)"; disability of the same site and nature must not be counted twice, and where two or more disabling injuries exist, the grade for each shall be stated separately (nationwide, effective January 1, 2017)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部 (2016). 关于发布《人体损伤致残程度分级》的公告（2016 年 4 月 18 日发布，2017 年 1 月 1 日施行），标准第 4.2、4.4、4.5、5.10.6、6.2 条. <https://www.moj.gov.cn/pub/sfbgw/zwxxgk/fdzdgknr/fdzdgknrtzwj/201908/P020210316701681025917.pdf>
- Notes: the three disability-rating systems are not interchangeable — do not mix them up: injuries from assault, traffic accidents and other personal injury go through judicial appraisal under the grading of disability caused by human body injury, used to calculate disability compensation (see Section 9); work injuries go through labor-capacity assessment, which uses a different set of ten disability grades and governs work-injury benefits (see Section 19); to enjoy policy benefits for persons with disabilities you must separately obtain a disabled-persons certificate (see item 11). Before the assessment, have the medical record, surgery record and follow-up imaging ready (see item 6); missing imaging is the most common cause of a lower grade. Where an injury coexists with a pre-existing condition, the appraisal opinion must also state how much the injury contributed to the disability outcome — this entry directly affects the compensation ratio.

### 11. If treatment leaves a lasting functional impairment, apply for a disability certificate at the county-level disabled persons' federation where your household is registered
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: the certificate itself carries no fee; the assessment fee and photo fee are in principle paid by the applicant, with waivers available for those in difficulty; bring your ID card, household register, and 3 two-inch photos with a white background to the county-level disabled persons' federation at your registered household; after a passing assessment, the result is posted publicly in the village (community) for 5 working days, and the federation finishes its review within 10 working days
- In plain terms: the disability certificate is the gateway to disability benefits, not a proof of injury. It covers seven categories — vision, hearing, speech, physical, intellectual, mental, and multiple — each graded 1 to 4, assessed against the national standard. The process is application, acceptance, assessment at a designated institution, public posting, review and approval, then issuing the certificate; it is valid for ten years and renewed free at expiry. If you don't qualify, you don't qualify — this is not something an acquaintance can fix. If the condition later changes you can apply for reassessment; if you disagree with the conclusion, you can apply to the city-level federation for re-assessment within ten working days.
- Benefit: the disability certificate "is the lawful credential for determining that a person is a person with a disability and their disability category and grade, and an important basis for persons with disabilities to enjoy the preferential policies of the state and local governments according to law"; the assessment standard is the national standard《Classification and Grading of Disabilities of Persons with Disabilities》(GB/T 26341-2010); application is voluntary and managed locally, and all seven categories — vision, hearing, speech, physical, intellectual, mental, and multiple — may apply, with grades 1 through 4; the county-level federation handles applications, acceptance, and issuance, based on the assessment conclusion of a hospital or professional institution designated by the province; conclusions meeting the standard are posted publicly in the village (community) for five working days, and the county-level federation "completes the review within ten working days"; "no fee is charged for issuing the disability certificate," with assessment and photo costs "in principle borne by the applicant personally; localities with the means may subsidize them from local finances, and fee reductions or waivers should be coordinated with relevant departments for applicants in special difficulty"; valid for ten years and renewed free at expiry; those whose disability category or grade changes may apply for reassessment; those who dispute the assessment conclusion may apply to the prefecture-level federation for re-assessment within ten working days, and remaining disputes are decided finally by the provincial disability-assessment expert committee (nationwide, effective January 1, 2018)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 中国残疾人联合会、国家卫生和计划生育委员会 (2017). 中华人民共和国残疾人证管理办法（2017 年 5 月 27 日发布，2018 年 1 月 1 日施行；第二、三、五、七、九、十七、十九、二十二、二十五条）. <https://www.bdpf.org.cn/cms68/web1459/subject/n1/n1459/n1508/n1509/n1512/c68465/content.html>
- Notes: do the arithmetic before deciding whether to apply: the certificate itself pays no money directly; what it unlocks are the two subsidies for persons with disabilities (a living subsidy for disabled people in minimum-livelihood-guarantee households, and a care subsidy for grade 1–2 people needing long-term care — see section 7, item 9), plus local benefits for rehabilitation, assistive devices, taxes, employment, and transport, which vary widely by province — check the local federation's website before deciding whether to make the trip. It, the forensic impairment grading, and the work-injury labor-capacity assessment are three separate systems that do not substitute for one another (see item 10). The certificate is for the holder's use only and may not be lent out; it is cancelled if the disability no longer meets the standard, and a cancelled holder may not reapply within one year. Multiple disabilities are graded by the most severe category.

### 12. Thank the doctors who saved you through thank-you letters, commemorative banners, and satisfaction ratings — not red envelopes: the code bans money and gifts, not gratitude
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; writing a letter or filling in a satisfaction survey takes about ten-plus minutes
- In plain terms: these are two different things — don't mix them. One is paying money beforehand to buy "priority," which cannot be bought: item 7 of the nine-point code strictly forbids using appointment slots, beds, scarce drugs and supplies, or the scheduling of tests and surgery to take benefits — the very priority you want to buy is itself a forbidden trade. The other is wanting to thank the people who saved your life afterward, which deserves thanks, but the form has to change: item 8 strictly forbids soliciting or accepting gifts, cash, stored-value cards, securities, or other valuables from patients and their relatives and friends — meaning that when you push cigarettes or liquor over, you put the other person in the position of violating the rules; pushing it back is not ingratitude, it is protecting their own job. Three ways of thanking that genuinely reach them: ① fill in the satisfaction survey seriously on the National Health Commission's satisfaction platform — satisfaction ratings are one of the four dimensions of the performance evaluation of tertiary public hospitals, made up of outpatient, inpatient, and staff satisfaction, and the results count toward the performance evaluation; ② write a thank-you letter naming the person and the date and operation, and hand it to the hospital's complaint-management department (secondary and above medical institutions must have a doctor-patient relations office or a designated department to uniformly handle opinions, suggestions, and complaints), or give a commemorative banner — a silk banner with words of thanks embroidered on it, a customary way in China to thank a doctor or hospital publicly, hung in the department; ③ follow up as instructed and report your recovery honestly — for the doctors who treated you, your living well is the final outcome of that operation.
- Benefit:《Nine-Point Code of Clean Practice for Staff of Medical Institutions》(Guo Wei Yi Fa [2021] No. 41), item 7: "Safeguard the order of diagnosis and treatment; do not undermine fairness in seeking care. Uphold the principle of equality and jointly build a fair care-seeking environment. Strictly forbidden to use medical resources such as appointment slots, beds, and scarce drugs and supplies, or the arrangement of tests, surgery, and other diagnostic and treatment services, to take benefits for oneself at the expense of the public"; item 8: "Jointly build harmonious relations; do not accept 'red envelopes' from patients. Abide by medical ethics and strict self-discipline. Strictly forbidden to solicit or accept gifts, cash gifts, stored-value cards, marketable securities, equity, other financial products, or other valuables from patients and their relatives and friends; strictly forbidden to attend banquets or travel, fitness, entertainment, or other activities arranged, organized, or paid for by them"; the document also requires medical institutions to fulfill their primary responsibility, carry out supervision and inspection, and "resolutely investigate and punish violations."《Opinions of the General Office of the State Council on Strengthening the Performance Evaluation of Tertiary Public Hospitals》(Guo Ban Fa [2019] No. 4): "the performance-evaluation indicator system for tertiary public hospitals consists of indicators in 4 areas: medical quality, operational efficiency, sustainable development, and satisfaction evaluation"; "hospital satisfaction consists of two parts, patient satisfaction and medical staff satisfaction... through satisfaction evaluations of outpatients, inpatients, and medical staff, it measures patients' sense of gain and staff motivation"; "the state establishes a satisfaction management system for public hospitals... by the end of March 2019, all tertiary public hospitals nationwide were to be included in the National Health Commission's satisfaction survey platform. Localities shall apply the platform and incorporate the survey results into the performance evaluation of tertiary public hospitals."《Measures for the Administration of Complaints in Medical Institutions》(Order No. 3 of the National Health Commission, effective 2019), Article 2: complaint management is the activity in which patients "raise opinions, suggestions, or complaint requests" concerning medical service conduct, medical management, medical quality and safety, and the like, and medical institutions investigate, handle, and give feedback; "secondary and above medical institutions shall set up a doctor-patient relations office or designate a department (hereinafter collectively the complaint-management department) to uniformly undertake complaint management," and the duties of that department include "uniformly accepting complaints; investigating and verifying complaint matters; proposing handling opinions; and promptly answering patients," and medical institutions "shall encourage staff to proactively collect patients' opinions and suggestions on medical services, medical quality and safety, and other matters" (nationwide)

- Notes: the beneficiary is yourself, and also the person who saved you. This item does not argue "express nothing at all" — wanting to thank someone after an all-night resuscitation is human nature; only the form needs to change: red envelopes, cigarettes, and liquor shift the risk onto the other person, while thank-you letters, commemorative banners, and satisfaction ratings are the route the system itself recognizes. If you already gave something and it was pushed back, don't take it as thinking the amount too small or putting on airs — that is a move they are required to make. Also keep two things apart: giving a doctor a red envelope falls under professional discipline and hospital handling, whereas giving money or goods to case-handling or law-enforcement personnel is the crime of bribery under criminal law — not remotely the same order of magnitude; for the latter see section 8, item 38. Hospitals generally accept thank-you letters and banners, but "it goes into the personal and department record" is practical experience, not a written rule; what has a written basis is that satisfaction-survey results are incorporated into the performance evaluation of tertiary public hospitals.
