# 10. Are Dating and Marriage Worth It

This section breaks the question "is marriage worth it" into several accounts that can be tallied separately: money, time, health, legal risk, and relationship quality. Each account gives only numbers that can be found in official statistics or meta-analyses, makes no moral judgment, and draws no conclusion for the reader; health figures use a mortality-risk measure, time figures use a daily-minutes measure, and legal figures use a statutory-text measure, and the three are not converted into one another. For the method of splitting the accounts, a fill-in checklist, and common misconceptions, see [docs/结婚划不划算.md](../docs/结婚划不划算.md).
### 1. Meet more people instead of fixating on one: two people's compatibility cannot be predicted before they meet
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; spend the time widening the circle of people you meet, not brooding over the same person
- In plain terms: two speed-dating studies had people fill out over 100 items of partner-selection questionnaires before meeting, and the results could only predict "how easily this person tends to like others in general" and "how popular this person tends to be in general" — but not "whether these two specific people hit it off." Even a person's stated ideal type (valuing looks versus valuing financial prospects) did not predict their in-the-moment interest in a particular person. Compatibility is only known after meeting, so spending time meeting several people beats betting everything on one.
- Benefit: in the two speed-dating studies, participants filled out more than 100 self-report scales related to partner selection beforehand; random forest models could predict "how easily a person likes others overall" (4%–18% of variance) and "how popular a person is overall" (7%–27%), but for "whether these two specific people hit it off," no combination of pre-event traits and preferences predicted it. An earlier speed-dating experiment by the same researchers also found that participants' pre-event stated ideal-partner preferences (valuing looks versus valuing financial prospects) did not predict their in-the-moment interest in a specific person
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Joel S, Eastwick PW, Finkel EJ (2017). Is romantic desire predictable? Machine learning applied to initial romantic attraction. Psychological Science, 28(10), 1478-1489. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797617714580>；Eastwick PW, Finkel EJ (2008). Sex differences in mate preferences revisited: do people know what they initially desire in a romantic partner? Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 94(2), 245-264. <https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.94.2.245>
- Notes: these two studies say "it cannot be computed before meeting," not "no effort helps." Since compatibility is only known after meeting, spending the effort on meeting several people and actually meeting each one beats staking all your time on one person. Both used Western college-student samples; China's dating market is structured differently, so discount the generalization

### 2. Stop after the other person clearly refuses; continued pestering is a public-order offense, not "sincerity"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; hold back from messaging again and from waiting for them again
- In plain terms: after a clear refusal, sending more messages or going to wait for them becomes a public-order offense as of January 2026: up to 5 days of detention or a fine of up to 1,000 yuan, or 5 to 10 days of detention in more serious cases. The police can also order you not to contact the person for a period of time, and violating that no-contact order means another 5 to 10 days of detention. Such a record also affects civil-service exams, public-institution hiring, political vetting, and professional licensing in some occupations.
- Benefit: the public security administration punishments law, in force from January 1, 2026, lists pestering and stalking as a separate offense: "repeatedly sending obscene, insulting, or threatening messages, or using harassment, pestering, stalking, and similar methods to interfere with another person's normal life" carries up to 5 days of detention or a fine of up to 1,000 yuan, and 5 to 10 days of detention in more serious cases, which may be combined with a fine of up to 1,000 yuan; and this provision adds one more layer — with the approval of the head of the public security organ, the offender may be ordered not to contact the victim for a specified period, and violating the no-contact order carries another 5 to 10 days of detention
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025 修订). 治安管理处罚法（第五十条第一款第五项、第二款）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>（最高人民检察院转载）
- Notes: "pursuing someone to the end will move them" has no research support, but detention and no-contact orders are written in black and white. The record left behind also affects civil-service exams, public-institution hiring, political vetting, and professional licensing in some occupations. The line between pursuing someone and harassing them is not set by your motives; it is set by whether the other person has clearly refused

### 3. Judge interest by behavior, not by "signals": who initiates, who plans, who follows up
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; shift attention from reading faces and tones to recording facts
- In plain terms: 52 pairs of stranger men and women chatted for about ten minutes and then judged whether the other was flirting; the result was that "the other person is not flirting" was judged fairly accurately, while "the other person is flirting" was mostly missed; switching to 261 people judging one-minute videos gave the same result. So do not infer intentions from expressions and tone — watch observable behavior: who initiates, who arranges the next meeting, and whether the other person follows up when you do not push things forward.
- Benefit: two studies measured how accurately people recognize flirting: 52 pairs of previously unacquainted heterosexuals talked one-on-one for 10 to 12 minutes and each reported whether they had been flirting and whether they thought the other had been; the result was "interactions where no flirting occurred were judged more accurately, and interactions where flirting occurred were judged worse"; the second study used 26 one-minute video clips judged by 261 observers, with the same conclusion — people can fairly reliably see "the other person is not flirting" but have great difficulty seeing "the other person is flirting"
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Hall JA, Xing C, Brooks S (2015). Accurately detecting flirting: error management theory, the traditional sexual script, and flirting base rate. Communication Research, 42(7), 939-958. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0093650214534972>
- Notes: contested. Other research argues that men do not overestimate women's sexual intent; rather, women underreport it in self-reports, and which side's judgments are more accurate remains unsettled (Perilloux C, Kurzban R (2015). Do men overperceive women's sexual interest? Psychological Science, 26(1), 70-77. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797614555727>). But both camps agree on one thing: inferring the other person's mind from subtle cues like expressions and tone has very low accuracy. Observable behavior is far more reliable — whether they initiate, whether they arrange the next meeting, whether they follow up even when you do not push

### 4. Judge a relationship mainly by how you feel in it yourself, not by the other person's attributes
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; replace the partner checklist with observation of how the relationship actually goes
- In plain terms: in an analysis of 43 paired tracking datasets from 29 labs, what best indicated whether a relationship was good was all the participants' own feelings — perceived commitment from the partner, appreciation, sexual satisfaction, perceiving the partner as satisfied, how much conflict there was — not the partner's attributes; these self-reported items predicted two to four times better than partner-reported ones. But the same study also found that whether the relationship would get better or worse afterward was essentially incalculable with any self-report questionnaire.
- Benefit: a machine-learning analysis of 43 longitudinal paired datasets from 29 labs: the strongest predictors of relationship quality all come from the participant's own feelings about the relationship — perceived partner commitment, appreciation, sexual satisfaction, perceived partner satisfaction, conflict; at the individual level the strongest are life satisfaction, negative affect, depression, avoidant attachment, and anxious attachment. Relationship variables explained at most 45% of variance at baseline and at most 18% at study end. And "self-reported variables" predicted two to four times better than "partner-reported variables"; after adding individual differences and partner reports, they added no predictive power over the self-reported relationship variables alone
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Joel S, Eastwick PW, Allison CJ, et al. (2020). Machine learning uncovers the most robust self-report predictors of relationship quality across 43 longitudinal couples studies. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 117(32), 19061-19071. <https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1917036117>
- Notes: the same study also found that "change" in relationship quality (whether it got better or worse over the study period) was essentially unpredictable from any combination of self-report variables. So the usable part of this item is: for judging whether the current relationship is good, asking your own feelings is more accurate than checking the other person's attribute list; but do not expect to predict how it will turn out. This says the same thing as the earlier item "don't ask only whether emotional value exists"; this one specifies which items they are

### 5. To build closeness, take turns answering a set of questions from light to deep; skip the lines
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; 45 minutes
- In plain terms: pair up two strangers and have them take turns answering three sets of progressively deeper questions, 36 in all; after 45 minutes their felt closeness was clearly higher than a control group that made small talk for the same amount of time, and even higher than those students' average rating of their closest real-life relationship. What works is taking turns on both sides and going one level deeper with real honesty each round — not canned lines. What it measured was only closeness over a short period, not whether it can grow into a long-term relationship.
- Benefit: pairing two unacquainted people and having them take turns answering three sets of progressively deeper questions (36 in total, from "who would you most want to have dinner with" to "when did you last cry in front of another person"); after 45 minutes, closeness was significantly higher than a control group that made small talk for the same length of time, and also higher than the college students in that study's average rating of their own closest real-life relationship
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Aron A, Melinat E, Aron EN, Vallone RD, Bator RJ (1997). The experimental generation of interpersonal closeness: a procedure and some preliminary findings. Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin, 23(4), 363-377. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167297234003>
- Notes: a single experiment with a college-student sample, measuring short-term closeness, not whether a long-term relationship can develop. The usable part is its mechanism: closeness comes from gradually escalating two-way self-disclosure, not from lines, routines, or one-sided performance. So the effective move in conversation is "I share something true, and ask a question of matching depth," with both sides taking turns

### 6. Long distance is no worse than living in the same city; the real hurdle is the first three months after moving in together
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: travel money and time; low frequency of meeting
- In plain terms: long-distance relationships are actually steadier while apart, because people meet less and imagine more; the real hurdle comes after moving in together. In the tracking data, about half of long-distance couples made it to the same-city stage, and the other half ended while still apart; of the half that reunited, one third broke up within 3 months. The longer the gap between visits while apart, and the rosier the imagining, the shakier things get after reunion. What to prepare for is those months after reunion: less autonomy, more knowledge of each other, more conflict and jealousy.
- Benefit: two studies give a consistent picture: long-distance relationships are actually more stable than same-city ones while the partners are apart, because idealization (idealized distortion, romantic love, relationship memories, perceived agreement) is significantly higher in long-distance relationships, and idealization correlates with low meeting frequency; but this stability holds only while the two are still apart — once they become same-city, long-distance couples are more likely to break up. The study tracking the move from long-distance to same-city gives these proportions: about half of long-distance couples reached the point of moving in together, the other half ended while apart; and of the half that reunited, one third broke up within 3 months of reunion. The longer the gaps between meetings while apart and the stronger the idealization, the less stable the relationship after reunion
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Stafford L, Merolla AJ, Castle JD (2006). When long-distance dating partners become geographically close. Journal of Social and Personal Relationships, 23(6), 901-919. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0265407506070472>；Stafford L, Merolla AJ (2007). Idealization, reunions, and stability in long-distance dating relationships. Journal of Social and Personal Relationships, 24(1), 37-54. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0265407507072578>
- Notes: the samples are American college students' dating relationships, not marriages, and the era is earlier, so do not apply the absolute proportions directly. Another study with 717 long-distance and 425 same-city participants found the two types of relationship differed very little in relationship quality; what predicted relationship quality was personal and relationship characteristics rather than distance itself (Dargie E, et al. (2015). Journal of Sex & Marital Therapy, 41(2), 181-202. <https://doi.org/10.1080/0092623X.2013.864367>). The usable conclusion: in deciding whether to accept long distance, do not use "long distance always falls apart" as a reason; what to prepare in advance is the months after reunion — less autonomy, more mutual knowledge, more conflict and jealousy

### 7. Check the registration data before listening to elders: what the marriage and divorce rates actually measure
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; read the marriage-registration section of the Ministry of Civil Affairs annual bulletin once, a few minutes
- In plain terms: in 2024 there were 6.106 million marriage registrations nationwide, 20% fewer than the year before, and 3.513 million divorce proceedings. The "divorce rate over half" circulating online comes from dividing these two numbers (about 57.5%); the numerator and denominator are not the same group of people at all, the bulletin contains no such indicator, and it cannot be read as "half of marriages will end in divorce." The bulletin's marriage rate of 4.3‰ and divorce rate of 2.5‰ take the whole population as the denominator, not the married population.
- Benefit: in 2024, 6.106 million marriage registrations were processed nationwide under law, down 20.5% from the previous year, with a marriage rate of 4.3‰; 3.513 million divorce proceedings were processed under law (2.622 million registered divorces with civil affairs departments, 0.891 million divorces by court judgment and mediation), with a divorce rate of 2.5‰; the bulletin notes that the marriage (divorce) rate = number of marriages (divorces) in the year / average total population in the year × 1000‰, a "crude rate" whose denominator is the whole population, not the married population
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 民政部 (2025). 2024 年民政事业发展统计公报（五（二）1 婚姻登记服务、注释 5）. <https://www.mca.gov.cn/n1288/n1294/n1554/c1662004999980006190/content.html>（PDF：<https://www.mca.gov.cn/gdnps/n2445/n2451/n2458/n2681/c1662004999980006189/attr/400985.pdf>）
- Notes: the common online claim of a "divorce rate above 50%" divides the year's divorce count by the year's marriage count (351.3 / 610.6 ≈ 57.5%); this is the "divorce-to-marriage ratio," its numerator and denominator are not the same group of people, the bulletin contains no such indicator, and it cannot be read as "half of marriages will end in divorce"; "the probability that a given marriage eventually ends in divorce" requires cohort data, and no official source text was found in this section

### 8. Count the health benefits, but discount them as observational data
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; knowing that these numbers are correlations of "married people have lower mortality," not causation that "marriage will make you live longer"
- In plain terms: married people were about 12% less likely to die in the same period than unmarried people; those who never married were about 24% more likely than married people; the gap shrinks with age, and men are affected somewhat more. But this is correlation, not causation: people who start out healthier, wealthier, and more socially connected are both more likely to marry and more likely to live long; the more rigorous the study, the smaller the computed gap; and the numbers come almost entirely from Western samples, so for China they serve only as an order-of-magnitude reference.
- Benefit: a meta-analysis in older adults (53 comparisons, over 250,000 people) found married relative to non-married all-cause mortality RR 0.88 (95% CI 0.85–0.91), widowed 1.11, divorced or separated 1.16, never-married 1.11, no difference by sex, most conservative estimate RR 0.94; a meta-analysis of never-married relative to married (95 publications, over 500 million people) found HR 1.24 (1.19–1.30), with the effect shrinking as age rises; a meta-analysis broken down by sex (21 studies, over 7.89 million people) found being non-married associated with all-cause, cancer, and cardiovascular death in both sexes, with stronger associations for cardiovascular and all-cause death in men, and unmarried men had 31% more stroke deaths and 9% more all-cause deaths than unmarried women
- Evidence grade: A (contested)
- Sources: Manzoli L, et al. (2007). Marital status and mortality in the elderly: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Soc Sci Med 64:77–94. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.socscimed.2006.08.031>；Roelfs DJ, et al. (2011). The rising relative risk of mortality for singles: meta-analysis and meta-regression. Am J Epidemiol 174(4):379–389. <https://doi.org/10.1093/aje/kwr111>；Wang Y, et al. (2020). Sex differences in the association between marital status and the risk of cardiovascular, cancer, and all-cause mortality: a systematic review and meta-analysis of 7,881,040 individuals. Glob Health Res Policy 5:4. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s41256-020-00133-8>
- Notes: the dispute concerns selection effects: people who are healthier, higher-income, and more socially connected are both more likely to marry and more likely to live long; all three meta-analyses are observational studies, the authors themselves state publication bias or methodological limitations, and Roelfs also found the effect shrinking as study quality rises; these numbers come mainly from Western samples and serve only as an order-of-magnitude reference for Chinese populations

### 9. Tally time as "unpaid labor"; settle the division of work before getting the certificate
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; spend an hour or two before marriage turning housework, caregiving, and whose career yields into an explicit division of labor, then follow it
- In plain terms: in the national time use survey, unpaid work such as housework and caring for family members averages 1 hour 52 minutes a day for men and 3 hours 29 minutes for women — a gap of an hour and a half; participation rates also differ by 16 percentage points. The survey does not break results down by marital status, so there is no official number for "how much more housework marriage adds"; treat this gender gap only as an upper-bound reference. The division of labor can absolutely be negotiated into something different before you get the certificate.
- Benefit: the 2024 national time use survey: unpaid labor (housework, accompanying and caring for family members, etc.) averaged 2 hours 45 minutes per day among participants, 1 hour 52 minutes for men and 3 hours 29 minutes for women, with participation rates of 67.5% for men and 83.9% for women; residents averaged 1 hour 17 minutes of housework per day (1 hour 59 minutes among participants, 28 minutes less than in 2018), and 30 minutes accompanying and caring for family members (1 hour 46 minutes among participants); in 2018, housework was 45 minutes for men and 2 hours 6 minutes for women, and childcare 17 minutes for men and 53 minutes for women
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家统计局 (2024). 第三次全国时间利用调查公报（第二号、第三号）. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202410/t20241031_1957216.html>、<https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202410/t20241031_1957215.html>；国家统计局 (2019). 2018 年全国时间利用调查公报. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202302/t20230203_1900224.html>
- Notes: both surveys break results down only by sex, urban/rural residence, and age, not by marital status, so there is no official number for "how much more housework marriage adds"; the gender gap serves only as an upper-bound reference; the figures are national averages, and an individual couple's division can absolutely be negotiated into something different — that is exactly the value of negotiating

### 10. In the money account, first read the law's default rules, then decide whether a written agreement is needed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: from 0 yuan; read through the Civil Code's marital and family property provisions once, and if needed sign a written property agreement (the law requires only written form; notarization is not a prerequisite)
- In plain terms: without a written agreement, the law defaults to joint ownership of wages and bonuses earned during marriage, business and investment income, intellectual-property income, and inherited or gifted property; only premarital property, personal-injury compensation, property that a will or gift contract explicitly gives to one side, and one spouse's personal daily-use items stay individual. Both parties can agree in writing that everything is separate or partly shared, and a signed agreement is legally binding — notarization is not a prerequisite. The spouse who puts in more on childcare, caring for the elderly, and so on can claim compensation at divorce.
- Benefit: the default rule is that wages, bonuses, and labor remuneration earned during marriage, income from production, business operation, and investment, intellectual-property income, and inherited or gifted property belong to both spouses jointly (Article 1062); premarital property, personal-injury compensation, property that a will or gift contract explicitly assigns to one side only, and one spouse's personal daily-use items are individual property (Article 1063); the two parties may agree in writing that premarital and marital property belongs to each separately, jointly, or partly separately and partly jointly, and the agreement is legally binding on both (Article 1065); the party bearing more of the duties of raising children, caring for the elderly, or assisting the other's work has the right to claim compensation at divorce (Article 1088)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人民代表大会 (2020). 中华人民共和国民法典（2021 年 1 月 1 日起施行，第一千零六十二、一千零六十三、一千零六十五、一千零八十八条）. 国家法律法规数据库 <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?title=%E4%B8%AD%E5%8D%8E%E4%BA%BA%E6%B0%91%E5%85%B1%E5%92%8C%E5%9B%BD%E6%B0%91%E6%B3%95%E5%85%B8&id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>；条文全文见最高人民法院公报转载 <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/7f184078694d811fb3314f6af9accf.html>
- Notes: bride price, weddings, and the marital home have no official statistics, so this item gives no numbers; compensation under Article 1088 is set by agreement between the parties, or by the court if agreement fails — there is no statutory formula; a property agreement cuts both ways, useful for the higher-earning side and for the side bearing more unpaid labor alike

### 11. When parents fund a home purchase, write down at the time of the transfer whether it is a loan or a gift
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; on the day of the transfer write a one-page statement saying whether the money is gifted to or lent to whom, signed by the funding parents and the child (if written as a loan, have both spouses sign), and put the same sentence in the transfer note
- In plain terms: when parents fund a home purchase before marriage, it defaults to belonging to their own child alone; money contributed during marriage, absent an agreement, is treated as joint property. From February 2025, when one spouse's parents fund the full price during the marriage and the gift contract does not state it is only for their own child, a court at divorce can award the house to the funding side's child, then decide whether to compensate the other side based on the shared life and whether children were born, any fault in the divorce, each side's contribution to the family, and the house's current value. If it is meant as a loan, put it in writing that day and have both sides sign.
- Benefit: where parents fund a home purchase for the couple before marriage, it is recognized as a gift to their own child alone, unless the parents explicitly stated it was a gift to both; where the contribution is made during marriage, an agreement governs, and absent an agreement or where the agreement is unclear, it is handled under the principle of marital joint property. From February 1, 2025, where one spouse's parents fund the full purchase price during the marriage and the gift contract does not state it is gifted only to their own child, the court may at the division of property award the house to the funding parent's child's side, and then, taking into account the circumstances of the shared life and any children born together, fault in the divorce, the size of each side's contribution to the family, and the market price of the house at divorce, determine whether and how much to compensate the other side; where one side's parents contributed part, or both sides' parents contributed, the ruling assigns the house to one of them with reasonable compensation, based on the source and proportion of the contributions (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 最高人民法院 (2020). 关于适用《民法典》婚姻家庭编的解释（一）（法释〔2020〕22 号，第二十九条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282071.html>；最高人民法院 (2025). 关于适用《民法典》婚姻家庭编的解释（二）（法释〔2025〕1 号，第八条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/452771.html>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一千零六十二条第一款第四项）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: this item cuts both ways. Funding parents no longer need to count on a backdated IOU at divorce to protect the contribution — after Interpretation (II) took effect, the source of the funds is itself a basis for division, and whose name the house is registered in no longer decides ownership; the side receiving the funds need not wait until divorce to learn what the money counts as. If it is meant as a loan, run it truly as a loan: an agreement to lend, repayment terms, a transfer note, repayment records when needed — missing any one of these has to be explained in court

### 12. Money borrowed in bulk by your spouse, without your signature or ratification, does not automatically become your debt
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; do not sign "co-borrower" or "guarantor" on your spouse's IOUs or guarantee documents, and do not write things like "we will repay this money" in chats, which could be treated as later ratification
- In plain terms: money your spouse borrowed behind your back, as long as it clearly exceeds the household's daily expenses and you neither signed nor ratified it, does not automatically become your debt — the creditor must prove the money went to the shared life or the shared business. But money for a house, if it really became a home you live in together or jointly own, can often be proven to that level. If at divorce the other side's parents suddenly produce an IOU signed only by the spouse, first contest whether the loan actually happened: with only a transfer receipt, the side claiming the loan still has to keep producing evidence.
- Benefit: debts incurred by both spouses' joint signature or by one spouse's later ratification — joint expressions of intent — and debts one spouse incurs in their own name during the marriage for the family's daily-life needs are joint marital debts; a debt one spouse incurs in their own name exceeding the family's daily-life needs is not a joint marital debt, unless the creditor can prove the debt was used for the spouses' shared life, joint production and business operation, or was based on both parties' joint expression of intent. House-purchase money clearly exceeds a family's daily-life needs, and the burden of proof lies with the side asserting the claim; but conversely, if that money really became a house the couple live in together or jointly own, the creditor can often prove the "used for the spouses' shared life" level — so what really works is the step in item 11 (nationwide, from 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一千零六十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2020 年第二次修正). 关于审理民间借贷案件适用法律若干问题的规定（第十五、十六条）. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/94b6623974526df7d2430a3c73f050.html>（最高人民法院公报）
- Notes: when at the property division the other side's parents suddenly produce a loan agreement signed only by the spouse, contest it on two levels: first whether the loan relationship exists — where a party argues the loan never actually took place and can give a reasonable explanation, the court is to judge comprehensively based on the loan amount, the delivery of funds, the parties' financial capacity, transaction methods and customs, changes in property, and witness testimony; with only a transfer receipt, the side claiming the loan still bears the burden of proving the loan relationship exists. Only after it is established does the question of joint debt arise. For the general rules on IOUs and guarantees, see section 8, item 16

### 13. Getting the certificate takes only an ID card plus one signed declaration — no household register, no fee, issued on the spot
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; both parties appear in person, usually done in a few dozen minutes
- In plain terms: from May 2025 getting the certificate takes only two things — your own ID card, plus a signed declaration that "I have no existing spouse and am not related to the other party by direct bloodline or within three generations of collateral bloodline." You need not bring the household register, and you need not return to either party's place of household registration. The registration itself costs nothing, the certificate is issued on the spot if you qualify, and it usually takes a few dozen minutes. If asked for a "production fee" or "expediting fee," refuse outright and file a complaint.
- Benefit: the new marriage registration regulation, in force from May 10, 2025, requires mainland residents marrying to produce only two things: "(1) the person's resident identity card; (2) a signed declaration that the person has no existing spouse and is not related to the other party by direct bloodline or within three generations of collateral bloodline"; the household register is no longer a required document; the registration authority is "the civil affairs department of the county-level people's government, or a township (town) people's government designated by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or directly administered municipality under the principle of convenience"; the old regulation's requirement of "going to the place of habitual household registration of one of the parties" has been removed; "marriage registration authorities shall not charge fees for marriage registration"; and "where a party meets the conditions for marriage, registration shall be granted on the spot and a marriage certificate issued"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2025). 婚姻登记条例（国令第 804 号，第二、六、八、十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202504/content_7017752.htm>
- Notes: the four circumstances in which registration is refused are: below the legal marriage age, not fully voluntary on both sides, one or both parties already having a spouse, or being related by direct bloodline or within three generations of collateral bloodline. The only charges possible are peripheral services such as photographs and photocopying; the registration itself costs nothing — on encountering a "production fee" or "expediting fee," refuse outright and file a complaint. The 30-day cooling-off period for divorce registration is covered in the item on exit costs in this section

### 14. Have one premarital health check before the wedding; major illness must be disclosed truthfully before registration
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: free or reduced in most areas for people of appropriate age, or a few hundred yuan self-paid; half a day
- In plain terms: if you have a major illness and do not tell the other person truthfully before registration, they can ask a court to annul the marriage, and the annulment must be requested within one year of the day they found out. The premarital check itself has not been a prerequisite for the certificate since 2003; it is voluntary, most areas waive or reduce the fee for people of appropriate age, and self-paid it is only a few hundred yuan and takes half a day.
- Benefit: the legal consequence of concealment is explicit: the new marriage registration regulation states that "where one party has a major illness, that party shall truthfully inform the other party before marriage registration; if not truthfully informed, the other party may petition the people's court to annul the marriage in accordance with Article 1053 of the Civil Code." The state treats the premarital check as the first gate of birth-defect prevention, with the current targets that "the premarital medical examination rate and the coverage of pre-pregnancy eugenic health checks among target populations remain above 70% and 80% respectively," and requires "promoting locating premarital medical examination facilities near marriage registration venues for people's convenience" and rolling out "one-stop" services combining marriage registration, premarital medical examination, and fertility guidance
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 国务院 (2025). 婚姻登记条例（国令第 804 号，第十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202504/content_7017752.htm>；国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2023). 出生缺陷防治能力提升计划（2023—2027 年）（国卫办妇幼发〔2023〕9 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202308/content_6900320.htm>
- Notes: the premarital check has not been a prerequisite for registration since 2003 and is voluntary, but the right to annul for "failure to truthfully disclose a major illness" is a real remedy. The annulment must be petitioned in court within one year of the day of finding out. Whether it is free and which items are checked are set locally; ask the local maternal and child health institution or the one-stop window at the marriage registration office. ### 15. Ask of emotional value not just "is there any" but "how good is the relationship"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; observe relationship quality before marriage through several conflicts and a few months of being together, rather than only checking an attribute list
- In plain terms: a synthesis of 126 studies covering over 72,000 people shows that the higher the marriage quality, the better the health, but the association is weak — about the same magnitude as the effect of health behaviors like diet on health — and basically no difference was found between men and women. The usable part: judge whether the relationship is good through several conflicts and a few months together, not by checking a list of attributes. As for "whether a bad marriage is worse than not marrying," there is no directly comparative data; for now it is only an inference.
- Benefit: a meta-analysis of 126 studies and over 72,000 people shows that higher marriage quality goes with better health, with effect sizes r between 0.07 and 0.21, including mortality risk r = 0.11 and cardiovascular reactivity during conflict r = −0.13, magnitudes comparable to the effect of health behaviors such as diet on health; studies that explicitly tested for sex differences basically found none between men and women; a review in the cardiovascular field notes that marital dissatisfaction and marital quality have a significant influence on cardiovascular risk
- Evidence grade: B (contested)
- Sources: Robles TF, et al. (2014). Marital quality and health: a meta-analytic review. Psychol Bull 140(1):140–187. <https://doi.org/10.1037/a0031859>；Dhindsa DS, et al. (2020). Marital status and outcomes in patients with cardiovascular disease. Trends Cardiovasc Med 30(4):215–220. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcm.2019.05.012>
- Notes: the dispute concerns small effect sizes, publication bias in some outcomes, and mostly cross-sectional designs that cannot establish causation — Robles's original paper lists these limitations itself; this section found no meta-analysis directly comparing the mortality of "low-quality marriages" with "never married," so the statement "a bad marriage is worse than not marrying" currently counts as an inference, not a numerical conclusion

### 16. Tally the exit costs: registered divorce has a 30-day cooling-off period, contested divorce has statutory conditions
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; read once before marriage about "how long an exit would take and what procedure it follows"
- In plain terms: registered divorce is not done once signed: within 30 days after filing the application either party can withdraw it, and after the period expires both must appear in person within another 30 days to collect the divorce certificate; failing to go counts as withdrawal. If the other side does not agree, the only route is to sue; the court mediates first, and only if mediation fails does it grant the divorce where there is bigamy or cohabitation with another person, domestic violence or abuse or abandonment, gambling or drug addiction unchanged despite repeated education, or two full years of separation; if the court refuses the divorce and separation then lasts another full year, a second suit shall be granted.
- Benefit: registered divorce requires a written divorce agreement signed by both and an application in person at the marriage registration authority (Article 1076); within 30 days of the authority's receipt of the application either party may withdraw it, and within 30 days after the period expires both must apply in person for the divorce certificate — failure to apply counts as withdrawal (Article 1077); when one side does not agree, litigation follows and the court shall first mediate; where mediation fails, divorce shall be granted for bigamy or cohabitation with another person, domestic violence or maltreatment or abandonment, gambling or drug use unchanged despite repeated education, or two full years of separation due to breakdown of affection, and where a divorce is denied and separation again lasts one full year, a subsequent suit shall be granted (Article 1079); of the 3.513 million divorces nationwide in 2024, 0.891 million went through court judgment or mediation
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人民代表大会 (2020). 中华人民共和国民法典（第一千零七十六、一千零七十七、一千零七十九条）. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/7f184078694d811fb3314f6af9accf.html>；民政部 (2020). 关于贯彻落实《中华人民共和国民法典》中有关婚姻登记规定的通知（民发〔2020〕116 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2020-12/04/content_5567010.htm>；民政部 (2025). 2024 年民政事业发展统计公报. 同第 1 条
- Notes: the cooling-off period applies only to registered divorce; contested divorce has no cooling-off period but takes longer; litigation fees and attorney fees vary by locality, so this item gives no numbers; high exit costs are not a reason not to marry, but you should know them before marrying

### 17. Keep a separate account for "marrying for the elders"; do not mix it with your own
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; write two columns on paper: which benefits of this marriage are yours (the ones computable from the earlier items in this section), and which are the elders' (peace of mind, face, the matchmake-urging stopping)
- In plain terms: the elders' share of the benefits (peace of mind, face, no more urging) is real, but it does not automatically become your health benefit or relationship quality, while the time account, the money account, and the exit costs — all three are borne by you alone. Two columns on paper, with which benefits go to you and which to the elders written apart, makes whether this account is worth it visible at a glance.
- Benefit: the elders' benefits are real, but they do not automatically convert into health benefits (item 8) or relationship quality (item 15), while the time account (item 9), the money account (items 10 to 12), and the exit costs (item 16) are all borne by the parties themselves; once the accounts are kept separately, whether "marrying-as-a-task" is good value can be seen at a glance, with no need for anyone else to judge for you
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 作者经验，方法类建议；数据部分见本节前面各条
- Notes: this item draws no conclusion for the reader; the elders' account may also be counted as a heavy one; the fill-in checklist is at [docs/结婚划不划算.md](../docs/结婚划不划算.md)
