# 32. Studying abroad: status, work, insurance, and recognition back home

This section counts money and personal liberty. It does not discuss whether you should go abroad, and it does not compare schools. Not knowing the following will cost you: how a visa status stays unbroken, where the cap on work hours is, which insurance and fees you must pay, and whether the diploma will be recognized after you return.

It covers the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia, with the figures listed country by country. The sources are official pages from each country's immigration authority and China's Ministry of Education. **Study-abroad rules in these countries change often, more often than regulations in China. Every figure in this section is current as of September (9) 2026. Before you leave, and before each renewal, check again yourself using the links in the Sources line.**

How to find an embassy or consulate if something happens while you are abroad, what 12308 can cover, and how to buy overseas medical and evacuation insurance are in section 21. The account of studying versus working is in section 23. Stolen accounts are in section 14. Telecom fraud is in section 8. Those are not repeated here.
### 1. Before you pay tuition, check whether the school is on the certified-institution list at the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. Type the school name once into the "certified-institution lookup" at the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange (留学服务中心 — the Ministry of Education office that certifies overseas degrees). It takes a few minutes.
- In plain terms: If you need this diploma back in China for further study, a job, or an exam, you can apply to the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange for certification. It publishes a list of schools that passed certification in the past 5 years. Being on the list does not guarantee you will pass. Being off the list does not mean they will refuse to take the application. Check once before you pay tuition. If the school is not on the list, ask a further question.
- Benefit: Article 1 of the Measures for Certifying Academic Degrees and Diplomas from Outside China, issued by the Chinese (Ministry of Education) Service Center for Scholarly Exchange, states that certification is to "meet the applicant's needs for further study, employment, exams, and similar purposes." Article 2 states that certification is done "on the applicant's own application." The center has a certified-institution lookup. You search by school name. Its instructions say the list is the foreign awarding institutions that appeared in applications certified in the past 5 years, and that it is "for the reference of people who studied abroad only, and is not a guarantee or basis for any successful certification." The instructions also say the scope of applications they will accept "is not limited to the institutions this lookup shows," that each application is assessed on its own, and that whether the school is lawful and qualified to issue the credential is only part of the assessment. The list is updated once a year. The 2025 update removed 270 schools that had not had a certificate issued in the past 5 years. Separately, the Ministry of Education information site on supervision of cross-border education (教育涉外监管信息网) also publishes supervision information on Chinese-foreign cooperative programs and on schools run overseas.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:中国（教育部）留学服务中心. 认证院校查询（含使用须知）. <http://yxcx.cscse.edu.cn/>；中国（教育部）留学服务中心 (2023). 国（境）外学历学位认证办法（第一、二条）. <http://zwfw.cscse.edu.cn/zwfw/lxfwzxwsfwdt2020/xlxwrz32/409891/rzbf/2026042823373762003/index.html>；教育部教育涉外监管信息网. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/>
- Notes: An agent saying "recognized by the Ministry of Education" does not count. Go by what you find online yourself. The list changes. A school that is on it when you enroll may not still be on it when you graduate, so check again once a year while you are studying. Chinese-foreign cooperative programs have a separate certification and registration system. If you enrolled after 2008, you can look up your registration number on that supervision site with your name and national ID number.

### 2. Legal battle over new U.S. F-1 rules: “Stay until graduation” remains the rule for now
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No cost at all. Simply copy the program end date from your I-20 into your calendar and set a reminder 90 days in advance. The I-20 is the official document issued by your school confirming your eligibility to study in the U.S. Also, check updates from your school’s international student office each semester to see if there are any new developments in this case.
- In plain terms: The U.S. originally planned to change F-1 rules so that students could stay for a maximum of four years, with only 30 days allowed to leave after finishing their studies. However, just one day before those rules were set to take effect, a federal court issued a stay. As a result, the old rule still applies: students may remain in the country for the full length of their program, with a 60-day grace period after graduation to depart. This stay does not mean the rules have been repealed; the government can still appeal, so the regulations might change again at any time.
- Benefit: On July 17, 2026, the Department of Homeland Security announced a new rule scheduled to become effective on September 15, 2026. Under this proposal, F-1 students would no longer be allowed to stay “until graduation”; instead, they would be limited to a fixed maximum stay of four years, after which they would need to apply for an extension. Following completion of their studies or internship, they would have only 30 days to leave the country. On September 14, 2026, Judge Saylor of the U.S. District Court for the District of Massachusetts, citing Section 705 of the Administrative Procedure Act, issued a stay on the implementation of this rule nationwide. The court did not overturn the rule; the request to do so was denied, though the government retains the right to appeal later. The case remains ongoing. Thanks to this court order, holders of F, J, and I visas continue to be allowed to stay “until graduation,” with the same 60-day post-graduation departure window and no need to apply for an extension.
- Evidence grade: A

U.S. Department of Homeland Security, No. 1:26-cv-13799-FDS (D. Mass. Sept. 14, 2026)，法院案卷. <https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/74661796/presidents-alliance-on-higher-education-and-immigration-v-united-states/>；Yale Office of International Students & Scholars (2026-09-14). - Notes: This information is current as of September 25, 2026. The government may appeal to the First Circuit Court of Appeals, and DHS could revise and reissue the rules. Therefore, students planning to study for multiple years should not assume that the current rules will remain unchanged indefinitely. The next court hearing to review progress in the case is scheduled for October 2, 2026; after that date, it’s advisable to check your school’s international student office for any updates. Should these new rules eventually take effect, any extension request must be submitted before the expiration date; there will be no automatic renewal. Under the current rules, the departure deadline for students who decide to leave early differs from that for those who complete their program; in such cases, consult your school’s international student office for guidance. The same rule proposal also applies to J visas, though specific provisions differ from those for F-1 students; check details based on your own visa category.

### 3. The maximum allowed work hours are strict limits in the U.S., Canada, UK, and Australia — exceeding them violates visa terms
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: There’s no cost involved. You simply need to track your weekly hours yourself. The difficulty lies in employers often claiming “no one checks extra hours,” but you must never believe this.
- In plain terms: All four countries cap the hours a student visa lets you work. Going over the cap breaks the visa conditions. During term, the United States is 20 hours a week. The United Kingdom is 20 hours a week on a degree course and 10 hours below degree level. Canada is 24 hours a week. Australia is 48 hours every two weeks. If you work two jobs, add the hours together.
- Benefit: F-1 students in the U.S. may work up to 20 hours per week on campus during term time, with the same 20-hour weekly cap applying to approved off-campus jobs; full-time work is allowed during holidays. This is stipulated under 8 CFR 214.2(f)(9) of U.S. federal immigration regulations. UK students pursuing degree-level courses may work 20 hours weekly during term time, while those on non-degree programs are limited to 10 hours per week; no work is permitted for part-time students at all. Self-employment is also prohibited, meaning you cannot work for yourself, nor may you serve as a professional athlete or performer. These rules are outlined in the UK immigration rules for students, specifically Student ST26.1 and ST26.5. Eligible full-time students in Canada may work up to 24 hours per week off campus, per Article 186(v) of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations (IRPR). For Australian student visas, the limit is 48 hours every two weeks while classes are in session; this restriction does not apply to research-based master’s or PhD students, nor their dependents.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:8 CFR 214.2(f)(9). <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-214/section-214.2>；UK Home Office. Immigration Rules Appendix Student（ST26.1、ST26.5）. <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student>；IRCC. Work off campus as an international student. <https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-off-campus.html>；Australian Government Department of Home Affairs. Student visa (subclass 500). <https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500>
- Notes: In the U.S., any off-campus work must first receive official approval; you cannot begin working while your I-765 work permit application is still pending. Older Canadian work permits may list a 20-hour weekly cap, but if you still meet all eligibility criteria, current regulations permit up to 24 hours per week. The UK’s self-employment ban is broadly interpreted: taking private contracts, running an online store, or working as a freelancer all count as prohibited self-employment.

### 4. The root of the status is "enrolled full-time": a leave, a withdrawal, or a gap while you transfer all mean you cannot work
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No cost. Before you stop classes, take a leave, or change schools, go to the school's international student office and get a clear answer on your status.
- In plain terms: Whether you can work depends on whether you are in full-time study right now. The moment you take a leave, withdraw, get expelled, or are not in class during a transfer, the right to work is gone on the spot. If you keep working then, that is illegal work. A visa that has not expired does not mean you can still work.
- Benefit: Canada states in writing that you may not work off campus during an approved leave. If you are not studying during a transfer, you may not work off campus either. You can start working again only after you resume your studies. On-campus work in the United States is only for F-1 students whose status in SEVIS is Active. SEVIS is the U.S. record system for international students. Active means enrolled and valid. Those students must also keep a full-time course load. In the United Kingdom, permission to work is given by course type. A part-time course does not allow work.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:IRCC. Work off campus as an international student. <https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-off-campus.html>；U.S. Department of Homeland Security, Study in the States. Working in the United States. <https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/students/work/working-in-the-united-states>；U.S. ICE SEVP, SEVIS Help Hub. Terminate a Student. <https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/sevis-help-hub/student-records/completions-and-terminations/terminate-a-student>；UK Home Office. Immigration Rules Appendix Student（ST26.1）. <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student>
- Notes: If the course load is heavy and you want to drop classes, ask first how far you can drop before you no longer count as full-time. That fails sooner than the work rules. In the United States, if the school terminates your SEVIS record because you broke the status rules, there is no grace period. You either apply to have the status restored, or you leave at once. If an early withdrawal is approved, you have 15 days to leave. The rules in the other 3 countries differ. Check the immigration page for the country you are in. Do not wait for the school to send a formal letter before you act.

### 5. United States: after you move, you must report the new address within 10 days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No cost. Submit an address change online. It takes 10 minutes.
- In plain terms: If you change where you live in the United States, the law requires you to tell the immigration authority within 10 days. Not reporting it is not a small matter. First, it is itself against the law. Second, mail the immigration authority sends you may not arrive.
- Benefit: 8 CFR 265.1, in the U.S. federal immigration regulations, says that a foreign national who must register under the law has to report within 10 days after an address change. Report it to USCIS (U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services), in the way USCIS requires. Report both that the address changed and what the new address is.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:8 CFR 265.1. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-265/section-265.1>；USCIS. AR-11, Alien's Change of Address Card. <https://www.uscis.gov/ar-11>；UK Government. Report a change of circumstances if you have a visa or expired BRP. <https://www.gov.uk/change-circumstances-visa-brp>；Australian Government Department of Home Affairs. See your visa conditions（Student visa subclass 500）. <https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/already-have-a-visa/check-visa-details-and-conditions/see-your-visa-conditions?product=500>
- Notes: After you report to the immigration authority, the SEVIS record on the school side (your international-student status file) still has to be updated separately. If you hold a UK visa or a residence card, you report when your circumstances change, and you change the address in your UKVI account (UK Visas and Immigration). An Australian student visa carries condition 8533, named "tell the school your address." No matching rule was found for Canada this time. The deadlines and the methods differ by country. Follow the immigration page for the country you are in.

### 6. Before you go and while studying abroad, check the Ministry of Education’s study‑abroad warnings
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Nothing to pay. Check it once before departure and then once each semester.
- In plain terms: The Ministry of Education issues specific warnings for certain countries or regions when recent safety conditions or policies there appear unfavorable to Chinese students. These are official, publicly posted risk notices that you can verify in writing — far more reliable than what any agency might tell you. Checking them costs nothing.
- Benefit: The Ministry posts study‑abroad warnings on its Overseas Education Supervision website. In 2025, four such warnings were issued. Warning No. 1 warned that certain higher‑education bills in some U.S. states contained provisions hostile toward China (April 9, 2025). Warnings No. 2 and No. 3 both highlighted the high incidence of cases involving Chinese citizens in the Philippines and an overall poor study‑abroad environment there (July 18, 2025 and August 30, 2025). Warning No. 4 noted deteriorating safety and study conditions in Japan, advising prospective students to plan carefully before going (November 16, 2025).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:教育部教育涉外监管信息网. 预警信息. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/>；教育部发布 2025 年第 4 号留学预警. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/n2/2/2/2060.shtml>；第 1 号. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/n2/2/2/2001.shtml>
- Notes: These warnings are not outright bans; they simply provide an official basis for evaluating risk. ### 7. Australia: OSHC must cover the entire stay without any gaps; lack of proof may lead to denied entry
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Several thousand to tens of thousands of RMB. Purchase it once based on the length of your visa; if you’re studying for several years, buy coverage for that entire period.
- In plain terms: Australia wrote student health insurance into the visa conditions. It is not something you may or may not buy. The cover has to last the whole stay. When you renew and switch policies, there must be no gap in the middle. If you cannot show proof of insurance at entry, you can be refused entry on the spot. Family members who come to stay with you while you study need this insurance too.
- Benefit: According to Australian Department of Home Affairs regulations, student visa applicants and their dependents must obtain OSHC, which must remain in effect throughout their entire stay in Australia without any interruptions. The policy must be issued by an insurer approved by Australian authorities, except for applicants who qualify for exemptions. There must be no gap between the end date of one policy and the start date of a new one. Failure to provide proof of active coverage upon entry can result in denial of entry. For travelers arriving in Australia prior to their course start date, the policy start date must be set to the actual date of arrival.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:Australian Government Department of Home Affairs. Student visa (subclass 500). <https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500>
- Notes: If your school arranges the insurance for you, be sure to confirm the insurer’s name, the policy’s start and end dates, and the policy number — these details must be included in your visa application. Purchasing coverage for a shorter period than needed can cause issues when you apply to renew your visa; it’s better to buy coverage that extends well beyond your graduation date.

### 8. UK: Besides the visa fee, there is an annual healthcare surcharge — £776 per year for students, paid in full at application time
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: The visa fee is £558. There is also a healthcare surcharge of £776 per year, both of which must be paid in full at the time of application.
- In plain terms: The amount you pay to go to the UK isn’t limited to just the visa fee. You also have to prepay the healthcare surcharge based on the length of your visa, which is required to access the UK’s National Health Service. For a two‑year visa, that means paying £1,552 upfront. Add this to the visa fee, and you’ll need to budget for this total amount before you travel.
- Benefit: The fee for a UK student visa is £558, regardless of whether you apply from outside the UK or extend/change your visa status while already there. In addition, the healthcare surcharge must be paid to use the National Health Service; it is calculated per year of your visa and paid in full at application. For students and their dependents, the charge is £776 per year — so a two‑year visa requires a payment of £1,552. Other applicant categories pay £1,035 per year. If your visa lasts more than six months but less than a full year, the charge is still applied for a full year.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:UK Government. Student visa. <https://www.gov.uk/student-visa>；UK Government. Pay for UK healthcare as part of your immigration application. <https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application>
- Notes: Both the fee amounts and exchange rates may change; always refer to the two pages listed under Sources for the most current figures. Generally, students aged 18 or older enrolled in degree programmes can stay for up to five years, while those on non‑degree courses are limited to two years. Use these timeframes to estimate the total healthcare surcharge you’ll need to pay.

### 9. Allow time for certification before returning to China: CSCSE certification takes 10–20 working days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: The application and payment are both done online. Preparing documents and waiting for review also take about a month when counted in working days.
- In plain terms: To use a foreign qualification back in China, you first need the degree and diploma certified. The review alone takes 10 to 20 working days. Time to prepare the papers is on top of that. Autumn hiring, civil-service exam registration, and settling a hukou (户口 — local household registration) all have deadlines. Do not wait until you need it to start.
- Benefit: Certification of overseas degrees in China is handled via the online portal of the China Scholarship Council Education Service Center. The procedure involves four steps: registration and identity verification, online submission of documents, payment, and finally evaluation and review. The officially stated processing time is 10–20 working days, which excludes weekends and holidays and represents the duration needed for the Center’s review.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:中国（教育部）留学服务中心. 网上服务大厅·学历学位认证. <http://zwfw.cscse.edu.cn/>
- Notes: Required documents include your diploma, passport or travel permit, residence permit or visa stamp, a photo, and an authorization statement. Entry and exit records during your stay abroad are retrieved automatically by the system, so be sure to keep those visa pages intact. The Center also posts announcements regarding revoked certificates or temporary suspensions of applications from certain institutions; it’s advisable to check whether your school is affected before applying.

### 10. Before you enroll, search the school name once in the center's notices. Certification for a school under "enhanced review" takes at least 60 working days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. Before you enroll, search the school name once in the notices of the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange (留学服务中心 — the Ministry of Education office that certifies overseas degrees).
- In plain terms: The center names some foreign institutions for a separate, tighter review of their degree certification, and it also stops taking certification applications for particular schools. Searching the school name once before you enroll is much less trouble than appealing afterward.
- Benefit: The Chinese (Ministry of Education) Service Center for Scholarly Exchange keeps issuing notices that strengthen certification review for degrees from some foreign institutions (an extra check, done separately, on diplomas from those schools). The latest is No. 9 (2025-10-28), which names 4 schools. During enhanced review, certification applications for these schools take "in principle no fewer than 60 working days counted from the day the application is submitted, that day included." The basis is Article 28 of the Measures for Certifying Academic Degrees and Diplomas from Outside China: when certification data looks abnormal, when a lead about a problem comes in, and in similar cases, the center can start an enhanced review, require the applicant, the school, and others to help by providing materials, and extend the certification period at the same time. In May (5) 2025 the center also issued a notice of "enhanced certification review, again" for 3 schools. It has also issued notices that pause acceptance of certification applications for particular overseas institutions. The anti-fraud notice of August (8) 2025 states: "Any organization or person that claims 'guaranteed certification,' 'certification through inside channels,' 'pandemic backfill,' 'expedited certification,' or the like is making a false claim."
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:中国（教育部）留学服务中心. 关于对部分国外院校学历学位认证加强认证审查的公告（九）（2025-10-28）. <https://www.cscse.edu.cn/cscse/sy/tzgg/2025102809225023345/index.html>；中国（教育部）留学服务中心 (2025-08-29). 关于谨防借国（境）外学历学位认证实施诈骗的重要提示. <https://www.cscse.edu.cn/cscse/sy/tzgg/2025082913242989351/index.html>；中国（教育部）留学服务中心 (2023). 国（境）外学历学位认证办法（第二十八条）. <http://zwfw.cscse.edu.cn/zwfw/lxfwzxwsfwdt2020/xlxwrz32/409891/rzbf/2026042823373762003/index.html>；中国（教育部）留学服务中心. 通知公告. <https://www.cscse.edu.cn/cscse/sy/tzgg/index.html>
- Notes: "Enhanced review" means an extra check and a longer deadline. It does not mean the credential definitely cannot be certified. Anything that promises "guaranteed certification" or "a rush job through inside channels" is a false claim, according to the center's notice. If you pay someone across a border to write a thesis for you or to attend online classes for you, and you are caught, the credential can be voided, and the visa record can be affected as well.

### 11. United States: on post-graduation OPT, write down the days without work. The total cannot pass 90 days. With a STEM extension, the combined total cannot pass 150 days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No cost. Write down the start and end dates of every stretch without work. As soon as you find a job, report the employer information to the school. The hard part is that when the job search goes badly, the days keep climbing.
- In plain terms: OPT (Optional Practical Training) is the permission the United States gives F-1 students to do practical work after graduation. During this period your status rests on having work. If the days without work add up past 90, the status can be ended. With a STEM extension (science, technology, engineering, and mathematics), the combined total before and after cannot pass 150 days. If the school system has no employer on file, that also counts as not working.
- Benefit: 8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(ii)(E), in the U.S. federal immigration regulations, says that during post-completion OPT, status depends on whether you have work. The total days without work must not exceed 90. With a 24-month STEM extension, the total across the whole OPT period must not exceed 150 days, and the stretch before the extension counts. The official explanation from the Student and Exchange Visitor Program (SEVP) says: if the SEVIS record has no employer information, it counts as not working. When the total reaches 90 days, an SEVP official can manually terminate that student's status. SEVIS is the U.S. record system for international students. Application timing, from the page of USCIS (U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services): you can apply starting 90 days before graduation, and at the latest 60 days after graduation. After the school enters the recommendation in SEVIS, you must file within 30 days.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(ii)(E)、(f)(11)(i)(B). <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-214/section-214.2>；U.S. ICE SEVP, SEVIS Help Hub. F-1 Optional Practical Training (OPT). <https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/sevis-help-hub/student-records/fm-student-employment/f-1-optional-practical-training-opt>；USCIS. Optional Practical Training (OPT) for F-1 Students. <https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/students-and-exchange-visitors/optional-practical-training-opt-for-f-1-students>；DHS (2026). Optional Practical Training Fees（拟议规则，2026-10-08 公布）. <https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/08/2026-20660/optional-practical-training-fees>
- Notes: This item is current as of October (10) 14, 2026. The OPT rules are changing. There are 2 things to watch. First, the stayed new rule in item 2 (the lawsuit over the new F-1 rules) changed "apply within 60 days after graduation" to 30 days. The text the eCFR site shows now is the wording of the new rule, so the safest course is to file before you graduate. Second, on October (10) 8, 2026, the Department of Homeland Security proposed fees for OPT. The first OPT would cost 70000 US dollars, and each later OPT 30000 US dollars. This is only a proposal. Comments are collected until November (11) 9, 2026. It is not in effect. If you plan to stay and work in the United States after you finish, count this possible sum before you pay tuition, and check again before you file.

### 12. Canada: before you apply for a study permit, get the provincial attestation letter (PAL/TAL) through the school. Master's and doctoral students at public universities are exempt from 2026
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No extra cost. Apply to the school that admitted you. In most cases the school will issue it only after you accept the offer and pay part or all of the tuition.
- In plain terms: To study in Canada, most people must submit a provincial attestation letter together with the study-permit application. The letter shows you were given a study place in that province. You cannot file it later. If you apply again after a study permit is approved or refused, or if you transfer schools, you need a new letter. People doing a master's or a doctorate at a public university do not need it.
- Benefit: IRCC (Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada) says the attestation letter is issued by the province or territory where you will study. It shows you were allocated a study place there. Most study-permit applications must include it, and it must be filed with the application. You cannot add it later. People in kindergarten through grade 12 are exempt. From January (1) 1, 2026, people in master's or doctoral degree programs at public institutions are exempt as well. People studying in Quebec submit the acceptance certificate issued by Quebec (CAQ) instead. Exchange students who do not pay tuition, people renewing at the same school for the same level of study, and a few other special cases are also exempt. People who do not have to submit the letter must attach proof that they qualify for the exemption. The letter is generally valid only in the year it is issued. A letter issued in 2026 is valid through December (12) 31, 2026. It only needs to be valid when you apply for the study permit. It does not matter if it has expired by the time classes start. If you apply again after a study permit is approved or refused, you need a new letter. From January (1) 22, 2025, a transfer also requires a new letter.
