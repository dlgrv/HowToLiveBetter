# 32. Studying Abroad: Status, Part-Time Work, Insurance, and Credential Recognition at Home

The yardstick here is money and personal freedom. This section does not discuss whether to go abroad or compare the quality of schools; it covers only four things where not knowing costs you: what counts as keeping your visa status uninterrupted, where the red lines on working hours lie, which insurance and fees are mandatory, and whether your diploma can be recognized once you return to China.

It covers the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia, with figures listed country by country, sourced from the official pages of each country's immigration authorities and China's Ministry of Education. **Study-abroad policies in these countries change more often than Chinese regulations; the as-of date for every figure in this section is September 2026 — before departure and before every visa renewal, check the links in the sources column yourself.**

For how to reach an embassy or consulate if something happens to you abroad, what the 12308 hotline can handle, and how to buy overseas medical and evacuation insurance, see Section 21; the accounting of study versus work is in Section 23; stolen accounts and telecom scams are in Sections 14 and 8 — none of that is repeated here.
### 1. Before paying tuition, check whether the school is on the CSCSE list of accredited institutions
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 — one search of the school's name in the Chinese MOE's Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange (CSCSE) "accredited institutions" lookup takes a few minutes
- In plain terms: if you want to use this diploma back in China for civil-service exams, state-owned-enterprise jobs, household registration, or professional-title evaluations, it first has to pass the CSCSE's degree and diploma certification. If the school is outside the certifiable scope, what you bought with several hundred thousand in tuition and one or two years cannot be used in China. The check is free — check first, pay afterward.
- Benefit: the CSCSE runs an accredited-institutions lookup where you can search by country and school name whether degrees and diplomas issued by that school fall within the certifiable scope; the Ministry of Education's information website on cross-border educational supervision also publishes regulatory information on Sino-foreign cooperative programs and overseas schools
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 中国（教育部）留学服务中心. 认证院校查询. <http://yxcx.cscse.edu.cn/>；教育部教育涉外监管信息网. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/>
- Notes: an agent saying "the Ministry of Education recognizes it" counts for nothing — the lookup result is what counts. The list changes: being on it at enrollment does not mean being on it at graduation, so re-check it once a year in the middle. Sino-foreign cooperative programs have a separate registration system for certification; those who enrolled in 2008 or later can look up their registration number on the supervision website with their name and ID number.

### 2. From 2026-09-15 the US changed the rules: F-1 is no longer "as long as it takes to finish" — a maximum of four years, and 30 days to leave once you finish
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 — copy the program end date on your I-20 and the admission period on your I-94 into a calendar, with a reminder 90 days ahead of each
- In plain terms: F-1 used to be admitted on "duration of status" — however long the studies take. Now it comes with a fixed admission period, four years at most, and an extension has to be applied for separately when it expires. If you finish your program or stop studying early, you have 30 days to prepare to leave or change to another lawful status — not the 60 days that circulate in rumors. Past that date, you are out of status.
- Benefit: 8 CFR 214.2(f) provides that an F-1 student who has completed studies and been approved for practical training has 30 days from the program end date, the four-year maximum admission period, or the end date of OPT/STEM OPT work authorization to prepare for departure or apply for a change of status under law; a student who ends studies or training early must depart or seek another lawful status within 30 days of the end date. The fixed-period rule was published by the Department of Homeland Security on 2026-07-17 and took effect 2026-09-15
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 8 CFR 214.2(f)（现行版本）. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-214/section-214.2>；DHS (2026). Establishing a Fixed Time Period of Admission and an Extension of Stay Procedure for Nonimmigrant Academic Students, Exchange Visitors（2026-07-17 公布，2026-09-15 生效）. <https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/17/2026-14439/establishing-a-fixed-time-period-of-admission-and-an-extension-of-stay-procedure-for-nonimmigrant>
- Notes: this rule only took effect on 2026-09-15, and the many online and agent claims of a "60-day grace period" or "duration of status until graduation" are outdated. The extension must be applied for separately under (f)(7) before the period expires; it is not automatic. The J visa was changed by the same rule with different details — check the original text for your own visa class.

### 3. Work-hour caps are hard red lines in all four countries — one hour over is a breach of visa conditions
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 — record your hours weekly, and don't believe an employer who says "a few extra hours, nobody checks"
- In plain terms: all four countries cap the work hours allowed on a student visa. Going over is a breach of visa conditions and can mean visa cancellation and removal, with awkward questions at any future application. The cap is counted per week or per fortnight, and two jobs are added together.
- Benefit: in the US, F-1 on-campus work during the academic term may not exceed 20 hours per week, and approved off-campus work is likewise capped at 20 hours per week, with full time allowed during breaks (8 CFR 214.2(f)(9)); in the UK, a student visa for degree-level courses allows 20 hours per week during term, below-degree courses 10 hours, and part-time courses allow no work at all, with self-employment, professional sport, and entertainment work prohibited (Immigration Rules Appendix Student ST26.1, ST26.5); in Canada, eligible full-time students may work off campus up to 24 hours per week (IRPR section 186(v)); in Australia, work on a student visa during the course is capped at 48 hours per fortnight, with no such limit for research master's and doctoral students and their dependents
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 8 CFR 214.2(f)(9). <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-214/section-214.2>；UK Home Office. Immigration Rules Appendix Student（ST26.1、ST26.5）. <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student>；IRCC. Work off campus as an international student. <https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-off-campus.html>；Australian Government Department of Home Affairs. Student visa (subclass 500). <https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500>
- Notes: in the US, off-campus work must be approved first — you cannot start while the I-765 is still pending. In Canada, older permits printed with 20 hours per week actually allow up to 24 hours as long as the conditions are still met — the current rule governs. The UK's self-employment ban is broad: private jobs on the side, online shops, and freelance work all count.

### 4. The root of your status is "enrolled full time": during leave, withdrawal, or a transfer gap you cannot work at all
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 — before stopping classes, taking leave, or changing schools, confirm your status with the school's international student office
- In plain terms: the right to work hangs on "you are currently studying full time." The moment you take leave, withdraw, are expelled, or sit in a gap between schools without attending classes, the right to work vanishes on the spot, and continuing to work is illegal work. Many people assume that as long as the visa hasn't expired they are fine, but their status broke long before.
- Benefit: Canada states expressly that during an approved leave of absence, or when not studying during a transfer between schools, you may not work off campus, and work may resume only after you return to your studies; in the US, on-campus work is limited to F-1 students whose status in SEVIS is Active and who maintain a full course of study; in the UK, work permission is granted by course type, and part-time courses allow no work
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: IRCC. Work off campus as an international student. <https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-off-campus.html>；U.S. Department of Homeland Security, Study in the States. Working in the United States. <https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/students/work/working-in-the-united-states>；UK Home Office. Immigration Rules Appendix Student（ST26.1）. <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student>
- Notes: before cutting your course load because the pressure is high, first ask how far you can cut before you stop counting as full time — this breaks your status earlier than work does. If the school expels you or the program ends, your status usually breaks the same day; don't wait for the official letter before acting.

### 5. US: report a new address within 10 days of moving
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 — one online address change takes ten minutes
- In plain terms: if you change where you live in the US, the law requires you to notify immigration authorities within 10 days. Not reporting is not a small matter: it is a violation in itself, and mail from USCIS can go astray — much of it carries a reply deadline, and missing it counts as giving up.
- Benefit: 8 CFR 265.1 requires any alien subject to the registration duty to report the change of address and the new address to USCIS, in the manner it requires, within 10 days of the change
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 8 CFR 265.1. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-265/section-265.1>；USCIS. AR-11, Alien's Change of Address Card. <https://www.uscis.gov/ar-11>
- Notes: reporting to immigration and reporting to the school are two different things; the school's SEVIS record must be updated separately. The other three countries also require notification of an address change to immigration authorities or the school, with deadlines and methods differing by country — follow your own immigration authority's page.

### 6. Before departure and while studying, read the Ministry of Education's study-abroad alerts
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 — read once before departure, then once each semester
- In plain terms: the Ministry of Education issues dedicated alerts about a country or region when public security or policy there has recently turned unfavorable to Chinese students. This is an officially published, verifiable written risk notice — more reliable than what agents say, and reading it costs nothing.
- Benefit: the Ministry of Education publishes study-abroad alerts through its cross-border education supervision website. In 2025 it issued four: No. 1 noted that higher-education legislation passed in certain US states contained negative provisions concerning China (2025-04-09); Nos. 2 and 3 twice noted that cases against Chinese citizens were frequent in the Philippines and the study environment there was poor (2025-07-18, 2025-08-30); No. 4 noted Japan's public-security situation and poor study environment and advised careful planning before studying in Japan (2025-11-16)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 教育部教育涉外监管信息网. 预警信息. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/>；教育部发布 2025 年第 4 号留学预警. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/n2/2/2/2060.shtml>；第 1 号. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/n2/2/2/2001.shtml>
- Notes: an alert is not a ban and does not mean you cannot go; what it gives you is an official basis for doing a risk assessment. The alert list changes with circumstances, this section does not maintain it over time, and the website's current page governs. Personal safety abroad and consular protection are in Section 21.

### 7. Australia: OSHC must cover the whole stay without a gap, and you can be refused entry at the border if you can't show insurance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: buy it in one purchase for the length of the visa — however many years you study, that many years of cover; several thousand to over ten thousand yuan
- In plain terms: Australia writes student health insurance into the visa conditions; it is not optional. The cover must span the entire period of stay, with no gap between policies when you renew, and if you cannot show proof of insurance at the border you can be refused entry on the spot. Once you are there, any family member coming along on a dependent visa needs a separate policy too.
- Benefit: the Department of Home Affairs requires student visa applicants and their family members to hold, and maintain for the whole of their stay in Australia, Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) from an insurer recognized by Australia, unless an exemption applies; there must be no gap between the cover and that of the previous visa; if you cannot show you are insured when you enter, you can be refused entry; and if you arrive before the course starts, the policy's start date must be the actual date of arrival in Australia
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Australian Government Department of Home Affairs. Student visa (subclass 500). <https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500>
- Notes: if the school arranges it, ask for the insurer's name, the policy start and end dates, and the policy number — the visa application requires them. Buying too short a term causes trouble at renewal; better to buy through graduation plus a little extra.

### 8. UK: beyond the visa fee there is a healthcare surcharge charged per year — 776 pounds a year for students, paid in full when you apply
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: visa fee 558 pounds, plus the healthcare surcharge at 776 pounds per year, all paid at the time of application
- In plain terms: the money for the UK is not just the visa fee — you must also prepay the healthcare surcharge in one lump sum for the full length of the visa before you can use the National Health Service. A two-year visa means 1,552 pounds at once, and together with the visa fee that is roughly ten-odd thousand yuan at the current exchange rate before you even set out — don't leave it out of the budget.
- Benefit: the fee for a UK student visa is 558 pounds whether applied for from outside the UK or extended or switched from inside; the healthcare surcharge is 776 pounds per year for students and their dependents — for example 1,552 pounds on a 2-year visa — and 1,035 pounds per year for applicants in other categories; a visa longer than 6 months but less than 1 year is charged as a full year
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: UK Government. Student visa. <https://www.gov.uk/student-visa>；UK Government. Pay for UK healthcare as part of your immigration application. <https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application>
- Notes: both the amounts and the exchange rate change — work from the current figures on the two pages above. For a degree course, those aged 18 and over can usually stay up to 5 years and below-degree courses 2 years; estimate the surcharge from that length.

### 9. Leave time for certification before returning home: CSCSE certification takes 10 to 20 working days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: apply and pay online; counting document preparation plus review, it takes about a month in working days
- In plain terms: to use a foreign degree in China you must first have it certified for both diploma and degree. The review itself takes 10 to 20 working days, and longer if it lands in the job-hunting season or your documents are sent back. Autumn recruitment, civil-service registration, and household-registration applications all have deadlines — don't start the process only when you need the paper.
- Benefit: the online service hall of the CSCSE handles certification of foreign academic degrees and diplomas; the process is registering with real-name identity verification, submitting the application and documents online, paying online, then assessment and review, with a published processing time of 10 to 20 working days
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 中国（教育部）留学服务中心. 网上服务大厅·学历学位认证. <http://zwfw.cscse.edu.cn/>
- Notes: the documents include the diploma, passport or travel document, residence card or visa and entry endorsements, an ID photo, and an authorization statement; your entry-exit records for the period abroad are pulled by the system itself, so don't lose the visa pages from that period. Announcements such as invalidated certifications or suspension of certification for particular schools are also posted on the CSCSE website — check before applying whether your school is affected.

### 10. The list of schools under "enhanced review" for certification grows every year — the road of buying a diploma is closed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 — search the school's name once in the CSCSE announcements before you enroll
- In plain terms: the CSCSE names batches of foreign institutions whose degree certification is subject to separate enhanced review, and it has suspended certification applications for individual schools altogether. These are often exactly the schools advertised as "no language requirement, no need to leave the country, a master's in a few months." Search the school's name once before you enroll — far less trouble than appealing afterward.
- Benefit: the CSCSE continuously publishes announcements on enhanced certification review for the degrees and diplomas of selected foreign institutions, the latest being No. 9 (2025-10-28); it has also issued announcements suspending the acceptance of certification applications from individual overseas institutions, and a warning about fraud committed in the name of foreign degree certification
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 中国（教育部）留学服务中心. 关于对部分国外院校学历学位认证加强认证审查的公告（九）（2025-10-28）. <https://www.cscse.edu.cn/cscse/sy/tzgg/2025102809225023345/index.html>；中国（教育部）留学服务中心. <https://www.cscse.edu.cn/>
- Notes: "enhanced review" does not mean certification is impossible, but more documents will be demanded and it takes longer. Any agent promising "certification guaranteed" or "expedited through inside channels" is a scam — the CSCSE has issued a dedicated anti-fraud warning. Getting caught having someone ghostwrite papers or attend online classes for you across the border not only voids the credential but can also affect your visa record.
