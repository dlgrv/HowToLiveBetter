# 11. Red lines that programmers and tech people easily cross

The focus of this section is personal freedom and money: people who write code or do technical work often treat the things below as "just technical issues," when in fact they correspond to specific crimes under criminal law or specific damages under civil law. Each entry gives the exact article numbers and cases published on the official websites of the Supreme People's Procuratorate and the Supreme People's Court. Criminal law citations use the consolidated text "as amended by Criminal Law Amendment (XI)" as republished by the Beijing traffic management bureau. Entries are ordered from highest to lowest by "how easily tech people assume it is harmless × how severe the consequences are"; entry 1 is the general principle.
### 1. Before you act, ask three questions: whose interests does this hurt, how able is the other side to come after you, how much evidence have I left behind; if they come after you, get a criminal defense lawyer right away
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Before acting, swap the question "can it be done technically" for another one: will the vendor, the platform, or the company report it. The hard part is resisting the thought "this is too small for anyone to care" — server logs, payment records, and group chat logs all get pulled by default when a case is handled.
- In plain terms: Before you do anything, ask one question: will the vendor, the platform, or the company report it. Server logs, payment records, and group chat logs can all be pulled when a case is handled. If you actually get criminally detained, the decision on whether to arrest you comes within 37 days at most. You can hire a lawyer starting from the first interrogation, and these 37 days are when a criminal defense lawyer is most useful.
- Benefit: Any unit or individual who discovers facts of a crime has the right to report it, and the case-handling authorities must open a case when they believe the facts of a crime exist; after criminal detention the police must submit the arrest request within 3 days — that is, apply to the procuratorate for formal arrest; in special circumstances this can be extended by 1 to 4 days, and for things like committing crimes across places (moving from place to place and offending again and again) it can be extended to 30 days, after which the procuratorate takes another 7 days to decide — added up, the decision on whether to arrest comes within 37 days at most; you can retain a lawyer from the first interrogation or from the moment coercive measures are imposed (detention, arrest, that category), and while the case is still in the police investigation stage only a lawyer can be retained; when the lawyer requests a meeting, the detention center arranges it within 48 hours at the latest; if the possible sentence is something lighter than prison, such as public surveillance or criminal detention, or only supplementary penalties without a main sentence, release on bail pending trial is possible — that is, you pay a bond or find a guarantor and go home to wait out the case — and release on bail lasts at most 12 months (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 全国人大 (2018). 刑事诉讼法（2018 年修正，第三十四、三十九、六十七、七十九、九十一、一百一十、一百一十二条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml>
- Notes: These 37 days count from detention to the procuratorate decision on whether to approve the arrest — they are not the ceiling on how long you can be held; after approval there is still the custody period during investigation, and the clock keeps running. These 37 days are also when a lawyer who knows the system is most useful: meeting you, handing the procuratorate a written opinion that the arrest should not be approved, and applying for release on bail. Waiting until after prosecution to find a lawyer is too late. The rest of the entries in this section are all about the same thing: whether the other side has grounds to report.

### 2. Do not make or sell game cheats and assist tools, including AI assists that "only read the screen and never touch memory"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. The price is giving up income from this kind of gig and these "small tools." The hard part is that the money comes fast and you cannot see who would ever come after you.
- In plain terms: Selling it 20 times, or earning 5,000 yuan, is enough for "serious circumstances" — up to 3 years. Especially serious gets 3 to 7 years. One person made wallhack and aimbot cheats, sold over 80,000 yuan worth in 3 months and pocketed over 30,000 himself — 1 year, suspended for 1 year, plus a 5,000 yuan fine. Someone who got the server-side program and ran private servers, taking in over 20 million yuan in top-ups, got up to 4 years, with fines up to 10 million yuan.
- Benefit: Providing programs or tools specially built to intrude into or illegally control computer systems carries up to 3 years in serious circumstances, plus a fine or a fine alone — that is, a prison term with a fine at the same time, or only a fine; especially serious gets 3 to 7 years plus a fine; the threshold for "serious circumstances" is providing the tools 20 or more times, or illegal gains of 5,000 yuan or more — illegal gains means the money you made from it; deliberately getting around or destroying the technical protections a rights holder put in place can also amount to the crime of copyright infringement — up to 3 years, or 3 to 10 years in serious cases; official cases: Wang and others made and sold cheats that read and modified game memory to enable wallhacks and aimbots, selling over 80,000 yuan worth in 3 months and profiting over 30,000 — 1 year, suspended 1 year, plus a 5,000 yuan fine; suspended for 1 year means a 1-year sentence that is not carried out for now — stay out of trouble that year and you do not go to prison; Xiang and others sold the "TZ" series of cheats, with sales over 200,000 yuan — Xiang got 3 years suspended 3 years and a 20,000 yuan fine; Liu and others got a game server program, changed parameters, and ran private servers, taking in over 20 million yuan in top-ups — sentences of 4 years down to 2 years 6 months, and fines of 10 million down to 1.3 million yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第三款、第二百一十七条第六项）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2011). 关于办理危害计算机信息系统安全刑事案件应用法律若干问题的解释（第三条）. <https://ga.sz.gov.cn/ZWGK/ZCFG/ZCJD/content/post_1304363.html>（深圳市公安局转载）；最高人民检察院 (2026). 上海静安：严惩制售游戏外挂黑灰产共护清朗网络空间. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202608/t20260825_735480.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2021). 2020 年度检察机关保护知识产权典型案例（案例五）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/xwfbh/wsfbt/202104/t20210425_516525.shtml>
- Notes: The cheat convictions published so far are all the kind that read and write game memory or bypass anti-cheat. There is no official precedent yet for assists that "only take screenshots" or "only simulate key presses." But the ticket-grabbing case in entry 3 shows that never touching memory and only sending requests can still get you convicted of illegally obtaining data. Whether it is a crime at all, and which crime it counts as, is decided by the case-handling authorities based on the materials the vendor submits when reporting — not by the tool author. Selling it 20 times is enough for "serious circumstances"; that is a very small number.

### 3. Do not write or sell ticket-grabbing, flash-sale, order-brushing, or freebie-hunting scripts, even if they only "automate button presses"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free, and it takes no time.
- In plain terms: A script that never modifies memory, never cracks anything, and just sends requests faster than a human is still convicted of illegally obtaining computer system data. The threshold is earning 5,000 yuan or causing the other side 10,000 yuan in losses. One person used purchased 12306 accounts to grab tickets in bulk — booked over 200 tickets for others, earned over 20,000, and got 1 year 6 months suspended for 1 year 6 months plus a 5,000 yuan fine. Someone who sold ticket-grabbing software in an online store was separately found liable for unfair competition and ordered to pay 20,000 yuan.
- Benefit: Intruding into a computer system, or using other technical means to get the data inside, carries up to 3 years in serious circumstances plus a fine or a fine alone, and 3 to 7 years when especially serious; the threshold for "serious circumstances" is illegal gains of 5,000 yuan or more, or losses of 10,000 yuan or more caused; official cases: someone used a tool to bulk-import purchased 12306 accounts and fired off dense multithreaded ticket-purchase requests, booking over 200 train tickets for others and profiting over 20,000 yuan — the court convicted him of illegally obtaining computer system data, 1 year 6 months suspended for 1 year 6 months plus a 5,000 yuan fine; Zheng sold ticket-grabbing software targeting a ticketing app in an online store and was found to have committed unfair competition, paying 20,000 yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第二款）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2011). 关于办理危害计算机信息系统安全刑事案件应用法律若干问题的解释（第一条）. <https://ga.sz.gov.cn/ZWGK/ZCFG/ZCJD/content/post_1304363.html>；最高人民检察院 (2026). 上海静安：查办利用外挂软件批量代抢火车票牟利案. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/dfjcdt/202607/t20260707_731426.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2025). 2024 年人民法院知识产权典型案例（案例七，抢票软件不正当竞争案）. <https://enipc.court.gov.cn/zh-cn/news/view-4205.html>
- Notes: The script in the ticket case never modified memory and never cracked anything — it was just "faster than a human" — and was still convicted under Article 285 of the criminal law. Taking money to grab tickets for others and selling scripts both count as profiting. For using one yourself once, no official case was found.

### 4. Crawlers scrape only public pages that need no login; do not get around anti-scraping, do not touch personal information, and do not sell what you scrape
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. A crawler is just a program that flips through web pages on its own and grabs the data on them. Before scraping, glance at the robots file and the API authorization terms — the robots file is the file a website puts in its root directory listing which pages must not be scraped. Data that needs a login, a token (a string of temporary pass credentials), or signature cracking to get: do not scrape it. The hard part is that technically the data is one grab away, and holding back is entirely on your conscience.
- In plain terms: Data you can only get by logging in, holding a token, or cracking a signature counts as getting around protection — up to 3 years, or 3 to 7 years in serious cases. Scraped personal information makes it worse. 50 items of location, communications, credit, or property data already counts as serious circumstances. So do 500 items of accommodation, health, or transaction data, 5,000 items of anything else, or 5,000 yuan earned.
- Benefit: Getting around protection to take data from a system carries the same sentences as entry 3 (ticket-grabbing, flash-sale, and order-brushing scripts) — up to 3 years, or 3 to 7 years; illegally obtaining, selling, or providing citizens' personal information counts as "serious circumstances" at any one of these: 50 or more items of location, communications content, credit, or property information; 500 or more items of accommodation, call records, health, or transaction information; 5,000 or more items of other personal information; or illegal gains of 5,000 yuan or more — serious circumstances carry up to 3 years plus a fine or a fine alone, and 3 to 7 years when especially serious; official cases: software developer Li disguised a function that stole the phone photo album as a "facial attractiveness test" app, obtained 1,751 face photos, and also resold doxxing-database material — 3 years suspended 3 years; a doxxing database is a database that pools personal information leaked from all over; an internet company used crawlers to take restaurant and order data from a food-delivery platform, causing losses of over 40,000 yuan, and was prosecuted for illegally obtaining computer system data (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第二款、第二百五十三条之一）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2017). 关于办理侵犯公民个人信息刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（第三、四、五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/201705/t20170509_190088.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2022). 检察机关依法惩治侵犯公民个人信息犯罪典型案例（案例二）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/xwfbh/wsfbt/202212/t20221207_594915.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 检察机关依法惩治侵害企业数据安全犯罪 服务保障企业创新发展（案例一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202501/t20250124_680670.shtml>
- Notes: The 2017 judicial interpretation says that "giving legally collected information to others without the person's consent" also counts as "providing." Exporting a user table to an external partner inside a company falls under the same line. 5,000 items is about one small table.

### 5. Do not take development contracts for gambling, fraud, or pornography apps; do not set up payment channels or unblock accounts for "clients"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. The price is walking away from this kind of outsourcing, where the quotes are high and the requirements are impossible to pin down. The hard part is that the money offered is often a lot higher than a normal contract.
- In plain terms: Take one of these high-quote, vague-requirements contracts and you are counted as an accomplice of the gambling ring, under the crime of running a gambling venue: up to 5 years, or 5 to 10 years in serious circumstances. It is not the "just helping out, at most 3 years" tier. In the esports betting app case, the 28 people writing code in Hainan got 3 years 6 months down to 1 year for running a gambling venue. Someone who unblocked banned QQ accounts for a fraud ring got 1 year 6 months and a 20,000 yuan fine.
- Benefit: Knowingly providing technical support, payment settlement, or that kind of help to someone using the internet to commit crimes carries up to 3 years in serious circumstances plus a fine or a fine alone; when it also constitutes another crime, the heavier one applies; running a gambling venue carries up to 5 years plus a fine, or 5 to 10 years in serious circumstances; official cases: the Zhang Sheng group developed gambling games and then did "site-package export," collecting site-setup and reporting fees, with nearly 7 billion yuan involved — Zhang Sheng got 9 years 9 months and a 50 million yuan fine, and the 79 people responsible for outsourced development, Wang Qiang among them, got sentences from 6 months to 9 years 9 months; Chen Dong and others developed and ran an esports betting app with the technical team writing code in Hainan — 28 people got 3 years 6 months down to 1 year for running a gambling venue, plus fines; Zhang ran a studio unblocking banned QQ accounts for a fraud ring — 1 year 6 months and a 20,000 yuan fine (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十七条之二、第三百零三条第二款）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民检察院 (2023). 建赌博网站并出售，涉案近 70 亿元. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202307/t20230711_621248.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2022). 上海闵行区：办理一起新型开设网络赌场案. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202208/t20220805_569952.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 依法惩治帮助信息网络犯罪活动及相关犯罪典型案例（案例一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202507/t20250728_702377.shtml>
- Notes: "knowingly" does not require the other side to say out loud what it is for. Quotes clearly above market rate, features that are deposits and withdrawals plus payout odds, a client who refuses to say what the product is for — all of it gets used to infer that you knew. Technical staff who develop gambling apps are handled as accomplices to running a gambling venue, not the aiding-cybercrime tier capped at 3 years. Aiding-cybercrime is short for the crime of assisting information network criminal activities.

### 6. When leaving a job, hand over all accounts and permissions; do not delete the database, leave backdoors, or change passwords to lock the system, even if the company owes you money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. If the company owes you wages, get them back through labor arbitration (see entry 7) — do not take matters into your own hands. The hard part is keeping your hands off the system while you are angry.
- In plain terms: The company gave you the account and password — that does not mean you may keep using them after you leave. Logging in already counts as illegal control, up to 3 years. Deleting data so the system cannot run carries up to 5 years, and more than 5 years when the consequences are especially serious. One senior engineer who was fired logged into the company servers with his old admin account and deleted data and logs, causing losses of over 120,000 yuan — 3 years suspended 5 years, plus a 30,000 yuan fine.
- Benefit: Illegally controlling a computer system carries up to 3 years in serious circumstances plus a fine or a fine alone, and 3 to 7 years when especially serious; deleting or modifying system functions or data so the system cannot run normally carries up to 5 years when the consequences are serious, and more than 5 years when especially serious; official cases: senior IT engineer Lü was fired and then used his old admin account to log into the company shared server, changed the passwords, and deleted data and logs, causing losses of over 120,000 yuan — 3 years suspended 5 years, plus a 30,000 yuan fine; Bai, an employee of a software company, deliberately slipped faulty code into an upgrade program so customers could not log into their systems, causing losses of over 40,000 yuan, and was prosecuted for the crime of damaging computer information systems (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第二款、第二百八十六条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民检察院 (2024). 检察机关「依法惩治网络犯罪 助力网络空间综合治理」典型案例（案例六）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202402/t20240223_644505.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 检察机关依法惩治侵害企业数据安全犯罪 服务保障企业创新发展（案例七）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202501/t20250124_680670.shtml>
- Notes: Being given the account and password by the company does not mean you may keep using them after leaving. Logging in with the old account already counts as "illegal control." Losses are measured by the cost of restoring the data; a few tens of thousands of yuan is enough to open a case.

### 7. Do not take source code, client lists, or technical documents when you leave; do not upload them to a personal cloud drive or reuse them at your next employer
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Before leaving, wipe every company file off your personal computer and cloud drive. The hard part is that some files are lines you wrote yourself, and letting go hurts.
- In plain terms: Even code you wrote yourself belongs to the company as copyright — taking it is taking the company property. Client lists count as trade secrets too. One departing employee took security-inspection system source code and core algorithms to a new company: he got 5 years and a 500,000 yuan fine, and the new company was fined 4 million yuan. Someone else used a system vulnerability to download more than 33,000 design documents — 1 year 10 months and a 100,000 yuan fine. On top of the criminal case, the company can file a separate civil claim for damages.
- Benefit: Breaching confidentiality obligations by disclosing trade secrets or using them yourself carries up to 3 years in serious circumstances plus a fine or a fine alone, and 3 to 10 years plus a fine when especially serious; the anti-unfair-competition law also lists two things as infringement: using "electronic intrusion" — that is, technical means to break into systems — to obtain trade secrets, and instigating or helping others breach confidentiality obligations; official cases: departing employees including Sun Mouming took security-inspection system source code and core algorithms and used them in the new company products — Sun Mouming got 5 years and a 500,000 yuan fine, and the new company was fined 4 million yuan; Tian used a system vulnerability to download more than 33,000 design documents — 1 year 10 months and a 100,000 yuan fine; engineer Yang stored app module source code on his personal computer and in the cloud and built a similar app after changing jobs — prosecuted for the crime of infringing trade secrets (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百一十九条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 反不正当竞争法（2025 年修订，第十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699862.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 检察机关知识产权保护典型案例（案例七）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202504/t20250423_693691.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2020). 2019 年度检察机关保护知识产权典型案例（案例十六）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202004/t20200425_459623.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 检察机关依法惩治侵害企业数据安全犯罪 服务保障企业创新发展（案例五）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202501/t20250124_680670.shtml>
- Notes: Even "code you wrote yourself" belongs to the company as copyright — see entry 13 (code written with company resources belongs to the company). Taking it is taking the company property. Client lists are business information and trade secrets all the same. Besides the criminal case, the company will file a separate civil claim for damages.

### 8. Do not run your own programs on other people's computers, servers, or cameras; do not use company machines to mine crypto
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free, and it takes no time.
- In plain terms: Controlling other people's computers, servers, or cameras without permission counts as serious circumstances at 20 devices — up to 3 years, or 3 to 7 years when especially serious. Even short of a crime you get up to 5 days of detention, plus possibly a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 yuan. What weighs heavier on tech people is the knock-on consequence that follows: anyone who has served a criminal sentence can never again hold key positions in network security management or network operations.
- Benefit: Illegally controlling computer systems carries up to 3 years in serious circumstances plus a fine or a fine alone, and 3 to 7 years when especially serious; controlling 20 or more devices is "serious circumstances"; where it falls short of a crime, the police confiscate illegal gains and detain for up to 5 days, and may fine 50,000 to 500,000 yuan, or in heavier cases detain 5 to 15 days and fine 100,000 to 1 million yuan; after a public-security punishment you may not hold key network security management or network operation positions for 5 years, and after a criminal punishment, never; official cases: Li bought the IP addresses of network cameras and used software to control 105 of them to spy on private life — 3 years suspended 5 years, plus a 20,000 yuan fine; Wu and others implanted trojans into phone motherboards and controlled over 3.3 million phones to siphon verification codes — sentences of 2 years to 4 years 6 months (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第二款）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2011). 关于办理危害计算机信息系统安全刑事案件应用法律若干问题的解释（第一条）. <https://ga.sz.gov.cn/ZWGK/ZCFG/ZCJD/content/post_1304363.html>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 网络安全法（2025 年修正，2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第二十九、六十六条；2016 年文本为第二十七、六十三条，终身禁业为修正后的第六十六条第三款）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-12/29/c_1768735112911946.htm>；最高人民检察院 (2022). 山东滨州开发区：办理一起非法控制计算机信息系统案. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/dfjcdt/202205/t20220518_557113.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2021). 充分发挥检察职能 推进网络空间治理典型案例（案例六）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/xwfbh/wsfbh/202101/t20210125_507452.shtml>
- Notes: For mining on company servers, no case published by the Supreme People's Procuratorate or the Supreme People's Court was found; this entry is written from the statute on "controlling other people's systems without permission." The "lifetime ban from key positions" is a knock-on consequence the cybersecurity law attaches on top of fines and sentences — for tech people it weighs more than the fine.

### 9. Do not test other people's systems without written authorization; "good intentions" and "reporting it afterward" are not defenses
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Before joining a vendor's SRC or a crowd-testing program, first get a document that states the authorized scope, the target list, and the time window when testing is allowed. An SRC is a security emergency response center a vendor runs itself, for receiving vulnerabilities reported from outside. Stop as soon as you have verified the vulnerability really exists. The hard part is giving up that one "let me just quickly try it."
- In plain terms: Whether you get convicted depends on whether you had authorization and how much you took, not on what you meant to do. Hold 500 sets of ordinary account credentials, or earn 5,000 yuan, or cause the other side 10,000 yuan in losses, and up to 3 years is on the table. To prove one vulnerability, exporting a user table "while you are at it" often crosses the 500 line in a single go. If you already hold the account credentials and use them beyond the authorized scope, that act by itself counts as intrusion.
- Benefit: This entry covers computer systems outside state affairs, national defense, and cutting-edge science and technology — that is, the systems of ordinary companies and individuals; intruding into such a system, or using other technical means to take the data stored, processed, or transmitted inside it, carries up to 3 years of imprisonment or criminal detention in serious circumstances, plus a fine or a fine alone, and 3 to 7 years plus a fine when especially serious; "serious circumstances" has several thresholds: obtaining 10 or more sets of identity authentication information (account credentials and the like) for online financial services such as payment settlement, securities trading, or futures trading; obtaining 500 or more sets of other identity authentication information; illegally controlling 20 or more computer systems; illegal gains of 5,000 yuan or more, or economic losses of 10,000 yuan or more caused — five times any of those numbers is "especially serious circumstances"; official case: Procuratorate Case No. 36 established one sentence: "using account credentials to log into a computer system beyond the authorized scope is an act of intruding into a computer information system"; the meaning is that you already hold the account and password, but using them outside the authorized scope counts as intrusion in itself; in that case three people used accounts, passwords, and tokens from their work to log into the company internal systems from another location, downloaded data outside the scope of their jobs, and sold it online, with illegal gains of 37,000 yuan; the three got 4 years, 3 years 9 months, and 4 years respectively, each with a 40,000 yuan fine (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第一款、第二款）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2011). 关于办理危害计算机信息系统安全刑事案件应用法律若干问题的解释（第一条）. <https://ga.sz.gov.cn/ZWGK/ZCFG/ZCJD/content/post_1304363.html>（深圳市公安局转载）；最高人民检察院 (2017). 第九批指导性案例（检例第 36 号，卫梦龙、龚旭、薛东东非法获取计算机信息系统数据案）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/jczdal/201710/t20171017_202593.shtml>
- Notes: The guiding case behind Procuratorate Case No. 36 is itself a case of selling data for profit. It is cited only for that one sentence — logging in beyond the authorized scope counts as intrusion — not because well-meaning testing gets sentenced just as heavily. What you meant to do and whether you reported it afterward cannot wipe out the charge; they can only affect how heavy the sentence is and whether you get prosecuted. A vendor thanking you first and reporting you later are two things that do not contradict each other — thanks are not authorization. When this section was written, no good-faith-testing case that could be checked word for word was found on the Supreme People's Court or Supreme People's Procuratorate websites, so it is written from the statutes and the penalty standards only. If you want to test, go through the vendor's public SRC or an assessment with a written commission, with the scope, the targets, and the time window written into the document. Prove a vulnerability exists with the least evidence possible; do not pull data in bulk. Even the tier short of a crime still brings a public-security punishment and a ban from the profession — fine amounts and years are the same as entry 8 (do not run your own programs on other people's machines). For what to do after you find a vulnerability, see entry 10 (report vulnerabilities as required).

### 10. Report vulnerabilities the way the rules require; before the patch, do not publish details, do not publish exploit tools, do not hand them to anyone abroad
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Report the vulnerability to the provider of the product, or to an official platform. Until the vendor has fixed it, publish no details and no proof-of-concept code.
- In plain terms: The regulation on managing network product security vulnerabilities covers not only companies — individuals are in it too. Publishing a vulnerability has five hard lines, and the first two are: do not publish before the vendor has produced a fix, and do not publish details of vulnerabilities in systems that are in use. Also do not publish programs and tools made specifically to exploit vulnerabilities, and when you publish, say at the same time how to fix it. One more line is easy to miss: an undisclosed vulnerability must not be given to organizations or individuals abroad other than the product provider.
- Benefit: The regulation covers three kinds of parties: network product providers in China, network operators, and organizations or individuals engaged in finding, collecting, or publishing vulnerabilities; no organization or individual may use vulnerabilities to harm network security, and may not collect, sell, or publish vulnerability information unlawfully; publishing vulnerability information to the public must follow five rules: first, no publishing before the product provider has produced remediation measures; second, no publishing details of vulnerabilities in networks, information systems, and equipment that network operators have in use; third, no deliberately exaggerating the harm and risk, and no using vulnerability information for malicious hype or fraud; fourth, no publishing or supplying programs and tools made specifically to exploit vulnerabilities in ways that harm network security; fifth, remediation or protection measures must be published at the same time; in addition, undisclosed vulnerability information must not be provided to organizations or individuals abroad other than the product provider; the regulation encourages reporting vulnerabilities to four official platforms: the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology platform for sharing network security threats and vulnerability information; the vulnerability platform of the National Network and Information Security Information Notification Center; the vulnerability platform of the National Computer Network Emergency Response Technical Handling Coordination Center; and the vulnerability database of the China Information Technology Security Evaluation Center; collecting or publishing vulnerability information in violation of the rules is handled by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and the Ministry of Public Security each within its own remit; where the cybersecurity law applies, penalties follow that law; publishing system vulnerabilities and other network security information to the public must follow the relevant national rules — violations bring an order to correct, a warning, and a fine of 10,000 to 100,000 yuan; refusing to correct, or serious circumstances, brings a fine of 100,000 to 1 million yuan; the authorities may also order the relevant business suspended, a shutdown for rectification, or the closing of the website or app, and may revoke the relevant business permit or the business license; the directly responsible managers and other directly responsible personnel face fines of 10,000 to 100,000 yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 工业和信息化部、国家互联网信息办公室、公安部 (2021). 网络产品安全漏洞管理规定（工信部联网安〔2021〕66 号，第二、四、九、十、十四条，2021 年 9 月 1 日施行）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5641351.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 网络安全法（2025 年修正，2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第二十八、六十五条；2016 年文本为第二十六、六十二条，规定第十四条援引的是 2016 年文本的条号）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-12/29/c_1768735112911946.htm>
- Notes: The regulation names individuals explicitly among the parties it governs — "I am just a hobbyist" is not an exception. Submitting holes in Chinese systems to bug bounty platforms abroad falls squarely under the line that undisclosed vulnerabilities must not be given to parties abroad. This entry is about what to do once you have a vulnerability; whether you were allowed to test in the first place is entry 9 (do not test other people's systems without written authorization). Keep the two separate: a lawfully found vulnerability can still get you fined for the way you published it; a vulnerability found without authorization is exposed on both ends. Among the reporting channels, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology platform and CNCERT, the national computer network emergency response center, are the most used. The regulation does not separately address the case where you have notified the vendor and the vendor will not fix it; in practice you keep a record of the back-and-forth and report to the official platforms as well.

### 11. Do not sell circumvention tools or VPN accounts; do not set up such nodes for other people
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free, and it takes no time.
- In plain terms: Building your own channel to get out of the country, or using an international channel other than the state public channels, lets the police order you to stop connecting, issue a warning, fine up to 15,000 yuan, and confiscate the illegal gains. If you run a business on it, the case-handling authorities can treat it as the crime of illegal business operation — up to 5 years in serious circumstances. This entry is about selling and setting up nodes. For personal use, the punishment tops out at a warning plus a fine.
- Benefit: Direct international networking must use the international gateway channels provided by the state public telecom network; no unit or individual may build or use other channels on their own — that means you may not build your own line out, and you may not use one someone else built; violators are ordered by the police to stop connecting, given a warning, and may be fined up to 15,000 yuan and have illegal gains confiscated; if you run a business on it, the case-handling authorities can handle it as the crime of illegal business operation — up to 5 years in serious circumstances, plus a fine of 1 to 5 times the illegal gains or a fine alone, and more than 5 years when especially serious (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 国务院 (1997). 计算机信息网络国际联网管理暂行规定（第六、十四条）. <https://nyncw.cq.gov.cn/wsdw/zz/zhfb/wlyxxaq/202406/t20240619_13306645.html>（重庆市农业农村委员会转载）；全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百二十五条第四项）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>
- Notes: When this section was written, no circumvention precedent was found published on the Supreme People's Court or Supreme People's Procuratorate websites, and handling varies a lot from place to place — hence grade B. This entry covers only "selling and setting up." For personal use, the heaviest punishment is still the warning plus fine written in Article 14 of the provisional regulations.

### 12. If you signed a non-compete and the company stops paying the monthly compensation after you leave, demand payment in writing; after 3 full months of nothing you can terminate it; if your role never touched trade secrets, you can ask to have the clause declared void
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. When you leave, keep the original of the non-compete agreement, and keep evidence that the company is not paying month by month.
- In plain terms: A non-compete means that for a period after you leave you may not go work for a competitor. This one protects you. A non-compete lasts at most 2 years, and the company must pay compensation every month. Where no amount was agreed, it is 30% of your average wage over the 12 months before you left, and never below the local minimum wage. If the company goes 3 months without paying for its own reasons, you can ask to terminate it. Starting in 2025, if your role never exposed you to trade secrets, you can ask to have the clause declared void.
- Benefit: A non-compete may only bind senior executives, senior technical personnel, and others with confidentiality obligations, and lasts at most 2 years; the company must pay monthly economic compensation during that period; where no amount was agreed, it is 30% of the average wage over the 12 months before departure and not below the local minimum wage; if the company fails to pay for 3 months for its own reasons, the worker may ask to terminate the non-compete; if the company terminates it first, it owes an extra 3 months of compensation; starting in 2025, a worker who never learned of or came into contact with trade secrets in their role may ask to have the clause declared void, and any scope that goes beyond the secrets they actually knew is void; official case: a pharmaceutical company sued its chief technology officer Zheng for 7.1 million yuan in penalties — the court found that the new employer product was not a substitute for the original company's and that the non-compete scope should be limited to the secrets he actually knew, and dismissed the claim entirely (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2012). 劳动合同法（2012 年修正，第二十三、二十四条）. <https://yjglj.fushun.gov.cn/008/20260622/d846d3c2-df3d-4c4c-af16-898ede43e1ee.html>（抚顺市应急管理局转载）；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）（第三十六至四十条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/282121.html>；最高人民法院 (2025). 关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（二）（法释〔2025〕12 号，第十三至十五条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/472691.html>；最高人民法院 (2025). 劳动争议典型案例（案例四）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/472681.html>
- Notes: This one protects you. Look at it the other way: if the agreement is valid and the company did pay month by month, going to work for a competitor means paying the agreed penalties, and after paying you may still be required to sit out the rest of the non-compete period. A non-compete agreed while you are still employed counts even if the company pays nothing extra for it.

### 13. Code written on work time or with company resources belongs to the company; do your own open-source projects on your own time and your own equipment, and never mix in company code
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. When you join, read the intellectual-property clause in the labor contract carefully. The hard part is that personal projects and work often share one computer, and they have to be kept apart.
- In plain terms: A work-made work is something produced doing your job. Software is explicitly listed by law in the category where copyright belongs to the employer. If you mainly used company equipment, the intranet, or business data, and the company bears responsibility, the copyright on the code is the company's — same when the contract says so. You keep only the right to be named as author. Inventions made with company resources belong to the company as patents too. Something "written after hours" that used a company computer or company business data can equally be found to have mainly used the employer's material and technical conditions.
- Benefit: Works created to complete tasks assigned by the employer are work-made works — that is, things produced doing your job; in the ordinary case the copyright belongs to the author, the employer has the right to use them first within its business scope, and for 2 years the author may not license third parties to use them in the same way; but two categories are different: works such as engineering designs and software created mainly using the employer's material and technical conditions with the employer bearing responsibility, and works the contract assigns to the employer — for these two the author keeps only the right to be named; the employer's material and technical conditions means the company equipment, the intranet, the business data, that kind of thing; an invention completed in the course of your duties or mainly using the employer's material and technical conditions is a service invention, and both the right to apply for the patent and the patent itself belong to the employer (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 著作权法（2020 年修正，第十八条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308796.html>；全国人大常委会 (2020). 专利法（2020 年修正，第六条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308800.html>
- Notes: Software is written directly into the category where copyright belongs to the employer, so programmers almost never land in the tier where the copyright is the author's and the employer has two years of priority use. "Written after hours" can still be counted as mainly done with the employer's equipment and materials if it used a company computer, the intranet, or company business data. To keep a personal project clean, use your own machine, never touch company repositories, and do not overlap with the company business.

### 14. When you build a product on GPL or other open-source code, open-source it as the license requires or switch licenses; do not assume "nobody enforces this"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Before using someone's open-source code, glance at its license — the terms of use the author wrote out. The GPL family requires that products built on it also release their source, which clashes with selling closed-source; if it clashes, switch to another one. The hard part is giving up a library you have gotten comfortable with.
- In plain terms: For copyright infringement you pay for the rights holder's actual losses, or what you made. When neither can be pinned down, the reference is what a normal purchase of that license would cost. Deliberate infringement with serious circumstances can be multiplied 1 to 5 times. In one case the defendant hired the plaintiff's former employees to develop gateway software, and the code was substantially similar; the defendant argued that the plaintiff's software was itself based on GPLv2 and should have been open-sourced anyway — the Supreme People's Court held that these were two separate issues and still ordered the infringement stopped and 500,000 yuan paid.
- Benefit: Copyright infringement is compensated by the rights holder's actual losses, or by the infringer's gains; when neither can be calculated, the reference is what normally buying that license would cost; deliberate infringement with serious circumstances can draw 1 to 5 times the damages; where the public interest is harmed, illegal gains may also be confiscated and fines imposed; official case: case number (2021) Zui Gao Fa Zhi Min Zhong No. 51 — the defendant recruited the plaintiff's former employees and developed gateway software substantially similar to the plaintiff's; the defendant argued that the plaintiff's software was based on GPLv2 and should have been open-sourced anyway; the Supreme People's Court held that "whether the developer itself violated the GPLv2 license and whether it holds the copyright to the software are two independent issues," and ordered the infringement stopped and 500,000 yuan in damages (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 著作权法（2020 年修正，第五十三、五十四条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308796.html>；最高人民法院知识产权法庭 (2022). 涉「开源软件」著作权侵权案（（2021）最高法知民终 51 号）. <https://ipc.court.gov.cn/zh-cn/news/view-3042.html>
- Notes: The point of this case: "the other side used GPL code" is no shield for copying from them. The other way around — GPL authors suing commercial users who fail to open-source already has effective judgments in China, but the original text was not found on the Supreme People's Court website, so it is not cited here. Grade B, because whether open-source licenses are honored rests only on contracts and the general provisions of copyright law; there is no dedicated statute.

### 15. When you build an app, collect only the personal information the business needs; anyone under 14 needs guardian consent; write the privacy policy clearly
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Before launch, go through the list of permissions the app asks for against the privacy policy and drop every permission you can do without. The hard part is that the product side always wants to keep a bit more data "we might need later."
- In plain terms: Collect only the information the business truly needs, and for anyone under 14 get the guardian's consent. For violations: first an order to correct, a warning, and confiscation of illegal gains, and the app can be ordered to suspend or terminate service. Refusing to correct brings a fine of up to 1 million yuan, and the directly responsible people are fined 10,000 to 100,000 yuan. Serious circumstances bring a fine of up to 50 million yuan, or 5% of the previous year's revenue, with the responsible people fined 100,000 to 1 million yuan. The "directly responsible personnel" in the statute can include the tech and product people who actually did the work.
- Benefit: Processing personal information must have a clear and reasonable purpose, be directly related to that purpose, and take the approach that affects individual rights the least; processing requires the individual's consent, or must fit a situation the law allows; for anyone under 14 the consent of parents or a guardian is required; violations bring an order to correct, a warning, and confiscation of illegal gains from the regulator, and the violating app can be ordered to suspend or terminate service; refusing to correct brings a fine of up to 1 million yuan, and the directly responsible managers and other directly responsible personnel are fined 10,000 to 100,000 yuan; serious circumstances bring a fine of up to 50 million yuan, or up to 5% of the previous year's revenue, with the responsible people fined 100,000 to 1 million yuan; these people can also be barred for a period from serving as directors, supervisors, or senior executives of related companies; for selling or providing personal information as a crime, see entry 4 (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 个人信息保护法（第六、十三、三十一、六十六条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202108/t20210820_313088.html>
- Notes: In practice "directly responsible personnel" often lands on the people writing the code and building the product. The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology regularly publishes notices removing violating apps; which specific notice could not be verified for this section, so it is not cited.

### 16. File ICP registration before a website or app goes live, and keep logs for at least 6 months as the classified protection regime requires
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Registration is free and takes a few weeks through the cloud provider's process. Classified protection assessments are charged by level. Classified protection, often shortened to dengbao, is the set of security measures the state requires websites and systems to carry, graded by how important the system is; small sites generally do not need an assessment.
- In plain terms: Registration costs nothing and takes a few weeks through the cloud provider's process. On a server inside China, a domain without registration simply will not resolve. Unregistered sites first get an order to correct; refuse, and the site gets ordered shut. Not displaying the registration number on the homepage brings a fine of 5,000 to 50,000 yuan. Registering as non-commercial while actually charging is handled as operating without a license — confiscation of illegal gains plus a fine of 100,000 to 1 million yuan. Logs must be kept for a full 6 months; failing that brings an order to correct and a warning, and refusing to correct or causing consequences brings a fine of 10,000 to 100,000 yuan.
- Benefit: Commercial internet information services run on a license and non-commercial ones on registration; without the license or the registration you may not operate; unregistered sites are ordered to correct by the telecom authority, and refusal means an order to close the website; operating without a license brings confiscation of illegal gains plus a fine of 3 to 5 times the gains, or a fine of 100,000 to 1 million yuan; not displaying the registration number on the website homepage brings a fine of 5,000 to 50,000 yuan; network operators must set up security management systems under the classified protection regime, guard against attacks, keep network logs for no less than 6 months, and classify, back up, and encrypt data; not doing it brings an order to correct and a warning, and refusing to correct or causing consequences brings a fine of 10,000 to 100,000 yuan with the managers fined 5,000 to 50,000 yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2011). 互联网信息服务管理办法（2011 年修订，第四、十九、二十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1860864.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 网络安全法（2025 年修正，2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第二十三、六十一条；2016 年文本为第二十一、五十九条）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-12/29/c_1768735112911946.htm>
- Notes: On a server inside China, a domain without registration simply will not resolve. The two ways people actually step on the rake: registering as personal non-commercial while actually charging, and changing the domain or the entity without updating the registration. Keeping logs for 6 months is both a duty and self-protection — when something happens, they prove it was not you.

### 17. Before offering generative AI services to the public, run the required security assessment and file the algorithm registration, and label generated content
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free, but it goes through the cyberspace administration process and takes some time to complete. A personal small tool that is not open to the public never triggers any of this.
- In plain terms: Offering the public a generative AI service that can shape public opinion or mobilize people requires a security assessment under national rules and algorithm registration before launch. Change it or shut it down, and you also file the change or deregistration paperwork. Generated images and videos must carry labels. A small tool for your own use, not open to the public, never triggers any of this. The measures themselves contain no penalty clause — violations are handled under the cybersecurity law, the data security law, and the personal information protection law.
- Benefit: Providing generative artificial intelligence services with public-opinion attributes or social mobilization capacity requires running a security assessment under national rules, and filing the algorithm registration under the algorithm recommendation management rules, plus the follow-up change and deregistration procedures; public-opinion attributes or social mobilization capacity means the ability to shape what the public thinks, or to organize and mobilize people; the provider must label generated content such as images and videos; the measures took effect on August 15, 2023 (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 国家互联网信息办公室等七部门 (2023). 生成式人工智能服务管理暂行办法（第十二、十七条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891752.htm>
- Notes: The measures themselves do not say how much any of this costs in fines. When they are violated, the penalty is found back in the cybersecurity law, the data security law, and the personal information protection law — hence grade B. Civil cases where AI-generated content infringed someone's copyright or likeness were not verified against the Supreme People's Court website for this section, so they are not cited.
