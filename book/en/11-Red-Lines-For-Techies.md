# 11. Red lines that programmers and tech people easily cross

The focus of this section is personal freedom and money: people who write code or do technical work often treat the things below as "just technical issues," when in fact they correspond to specific crimes under criminal law or specific damages under civil law. Each entry gives the exact article numbers and cases published on the official websites of the Supreme People's Procuratorate and the Supreme People's Court. Criminal law citations use the consolidated text "as amended by Criminal Law Amendment (XI)" as republished by the Beijing traffic management bureau. Entries are ordered from highest to lowest by "how easily tech people assume it is harmless × how severe the consequences are"; entry 1 is the general principle.
### 1. Before acting, ask three questions: whose interests are harmed, how able is the other side to pursue it, how much evidence have I left; if pursued, get a criminal defense lawyer immediately

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; replace "can it be done technically" with "will the vendor, platform, or company report it to the police" — server logs, payment records, and group chat logs are all retrieved by default
- In plain terms: before acting, the thing to think about is not "can it be done technically" but "will the vendor, platform, or company report it to the police" — server logs, payment records, and group chat logs are all retrieved by default. If you really get criminally detained, the decision whether to arrest happens within 37 days at most; from the first interrogation you can retain a lawyer, and once a lawyer requests a meeting, the detention center must arrange it within 48 hours at the latest. These 37 days are the most useful window for finding a criminal defense lawyer; waiting until after prosecution is too late.
- Benefit: any individual or organization that discovers a crime has the right to report it, and the authorities must open a case if they find facts indicating a crime; after criminal detention the police submit the arrest request within 3 days, extendable by 1 to 4 days in special circumstances and up to 30 days for suspects who commit crimes across regions, and the procuratorate then takes another 7 days to decide — meaning the decision whether to arrest comes within 37 days at most; you can retain a lawyer from the first interrogation or from the moment coercive measures are imposed, and during the investigation stage only a lawyer may be retained; when the lawyer requests a meeting, the detention center arranges it within 48 hours at the latest; in situations such as possible sentences of public surveillance, criminal detention, or supplementary penalties applied independently, release on bail pending trial is possible, for at most 12 months (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 全国人大 (2018). 刑事诉讼法（2018 年修正，第三十四、三十九、六十七、七十九、九十一、一百一十、一百一十二条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml>
- Notes: the 37 days are the ceiling from detention to the arrest decision, not the ceiling on custody; after arrest the investigation custody period begins. The window where a lawyer "who knows the system" helps is mainly within these 37 days: the meetings, submitting non-arrest opinions to the procuratorate, and applying for release on bail. Waiting until after prosecution is too late. The other entries in this section are all about "whether the other side has grounds to report."

### 2. Do not make or sell game cheats and assist tools, including AI assists that "only read the screen and never touch memory"

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; give up this kind of gig and "small tool" income
- In plain terms: selling to 20 people or earning 5,000 yuan is enough for "serious circumstances," punishable by up to 3 years; especially serious cases get 3 to 7 years. The amounts are small. One person built cheats that read and wrote game memory to enable wallhacks and aimbots, sold over 80,000 yuan in 3 months and personally earned over 30,000 — sentenced to 1 year, suspended for 1 year, plus a 5,000 yuan fine. Someone who obtained the server-side program, changed parameters, and ran private servers took in over 20 million yuan in top-ups — sentenced to 4 years down to 2 years 6 months, with fines up to 10 million yuan.
- Benefit: providing programs or tools specifically designed to intrude into or illegally control computer systems, in serious circumstances, carries up to 3 years plus a fine (or a fine alone); especially serious circumstances carry 3 to 7 years plus a fine; the serious-circumstances threshold is providing tools to 20 or more people or illegal gains of 5,000 yuan or more; deliberately circumventing or destroying a rights holder's technical measures can also constitute the crime of copyright infringement, punishable by up to 3 years, or 3 to 10 years in serious cases; official cases: Wang and others made and sold cheats that read and modified game memory for wallhacks and aimbots, selling over 80,000 yuan in 3 months and profiting over 30,000 — 1 year, suspended 1 year, 5,000 yuan fine; Xiang and others sold the "TZ" series of cheats with sales of over 200,000 yuan, Xiang getting 3 years suspended 3 years and a 20,000 yuan fine; Liu and others obtained a game server program, changed parameters, and ran private servers, taking in over 20 million yuan in top-ups — sentences of 4 years to 2 years 6 months and fines of 10 million to 1.3 million yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第三款、第二百一十七条第六项）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2011). 关于办理危害计算机信息系统安全刑事案件应用法律若干问题的解释（第三条）. <https://ga.sz.gov.cn/ZWGK/ZCFG/ZCJD/content/post_1304363.html>（深圳市公安局转载）；最高人民检察院 (2026). 上海静安：严惩制售游戏外挂黑灰产共护清朗网络空间. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202608/t20260825_735480.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2021). 2020 年度检察机关保护知识产权典型案例（案例五）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/xwfbh/wsfbt/202104/t20210425_516525.shtml>
- Notes: the published cheat convictions all involve reading and writing memory or bypassing anti-cheat. There are no official precedents yet for assists that "only take screenshots or only simulate key presses," but the ticket-scalping case in entry 3 shows that never touching memory and only sending requests can still support a conviction for illegally obtaining data. How it is ultimately characterized is decided by the case-handling authorities based on the materials in the vendor's report, not by the author's own judgment. Selling to 20 people is enough for "serious circumstances" — the amounts are small.

### 3. Do not write or sell ticket-grabbing, flash-sale, order-brushing, or freebie-hunting scripts, even if they only "automate button presses"

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: a script that never touches memory and never cracks anything, that just sends requests faster than a human, is still convicted under the crime of illegally obtaining computer system data. The threshold is earning 5,000 yuan or causing 10,000 yuan in losses. One person bulk-imported purchased 12306 accounts and ran multithreaded ticket grabbing, booking over 200 tickets for others and earning over 20,000 yuan — 1 year 6 months, suspended 1 year 6 months, plus a 5,000 yuan fine. Someone who sold ticket-grabbing software through an online store was separately found liable for unfair competition and ordered to pay 20,000 yuan.
- Benefit: intruding into a computer system or using other technical means to obtain data stored in it, in serious circumstances, carries up to 3 years plus a fine (or a fine alone); especially serious circumstances carry 3 to 7 years; the serious-circumstances threshold is illegal gains of 5,000 yuan or more or losses of 10,000 yuan or more caused; official cases: a person used a tool to bulk-import purchased 12306 accounts and submitted dense multithreaded ticket-purchase requests, booking over 200 train tickets for others and profiting over 20,000 yuan — convicted of illegally obtaining computer system data, 1 year 6 months suspended 1 year 6 months and a 5,000 yuan fine; Zheng sold ticket-grabbing software targeting a ticketing app through an online store and was found to have committed unfair competition, paying 20,000 yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第二款）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2011). 关于办理危害计算机信息系统安全刑事案件应用法律若干问题的解释（第一条）. <https://ga.sz.gov.cn/ZWGK/ZCFG/ZCJD/content/post_1304363.html>；最高人民检察院 (2026). 上海静安：查办利用外挂软件批量代抢火车票牟利案. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/dfjcdt/202607/t20260707_731426.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2025). 2024 年人民法院知识产权典型案例（案例七，抢票软件不正当竞争案）. <https://enipc.court.gov.cn/zh-cn/news/view-4205.html>
- Notes: the script in the ticket-grabbing case never modified memory and never cracked anything — it was just "faster than a human" — and was still convicted under Article 285. Taking money to grab tickets on commission and selling scripts both count as profiting; there are no official cases for one-time personal use.

### 4. Crawlers scrape only public pages that need no login; do not bypass anti-scraping, do not touch personal data, and do not sell what is scraped

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; check robots and API authorization — data that needs a login, a token, or signature cracking to obtain does not get scraped
- In plain terms: data you can only get by logging in, holding a token, or cracking a signature counts as bypassing protection, punishable by up to 3 years, or 3 to 7 years in serious cases. Scraped personal information makes it worse: 50 items of location, communications, credit, or property data; 500 items of accommodation, health, or transaction data; 5,000 items of any other kind; or 5,000 yuan earned — any of these is "serious circumstances." One developer disguised an album-stealing function as a "facial attractiveness test" app, obtained 1,751 face photos and resold them — sentenced to 3 years, suspended for 3 years.
- Benefit: bypassing protection to obtain system data carries the same penalties as entry 3, up to 3 years or 3 to 7 years; illegally obtaining, selling, or providing citizens' personal information is "serious circumstances" at 50 or more items of location-movement, communications content, credit, or property information, 500 or more items of accommodation, call records, health, or transaction information, 5,000 or more items of other personal information, or illegal gains of 5,000 yuan or more — punishable by up to 3 years plus a fine (or a fine alone), 3 to 7 years when especially serious; official cases: software developer Li disguised a function that stole the phone's photo album as a "facial attractiveness test" app, obtained 1,751 face photos and resold doxxing-database material — 3 years suspended 3 years; an internet company used crawlers to take restaurant and order data from a food-delivery platform, causing losses of over 40,000 yuan, and was prosecuted for illegally obtaining computer system data (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第二款、第二百五十三条之一）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2017). 关于办理侵犯公民个人信息刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（第三、四、五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/201705/t20170509_190088.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2022). 检察机关依法惩治侵犯公民个人信息犯罪典型案例（案例二）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/xwfbh/wsfbt/202212/t20221207_594915.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 检察机关依法惩治侵害企业数据安全犯罪 服务保障企业创新发展（案例一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202501/t20250124_680670.shtml>
- Notes: the 2017 judicial interpretation also counts "providing legally collected information to others without consent" as providing — exporting a user table to an external partner inside a company falls under the same rule; 5,000 items is the size of one small table.

### 5. Do not take development contracts for gambling, fraud, or pornography apps; do not set up payment channels or unblock accounts for "clients"

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; walk away from this kind of high-quote, vague-requirements outsourcing
- In plain terms: these contracts come with high quotes and vague requirements, and the price is being treated as an accomplice of the gambling ring — not the 3-year ceiling of the aiding-cybercrime offense. A ring that built gambling games for "site-package export" was involved in nearly 7 billion yuan; the 79 people responsible for outsourced development got sentences from 6 months to 9 years 9 months. In the esports betting app case, the 28 people writing code in Hainan were convicted of operating a gambling venue with sentences of 3 years 6 months down to 1 year. Someone who unblocked banned QQ accounts for a fraud ring got 1 year 6 months and a 20,000 yuan fine.
- Benefit: knowingly providing technical support, payment settlement, or other help for another person's online crime, in serious circumstances, carries up to 3 years plus a fine (or a fine alone); when it also constitutes another crime, the heavier one applies; operating a gambling venue carries up to 5 years plus a fine, 5 to 10 years in serious circumstances; official cases: Zhang Sheng's ring developed gambling games and did "site-package export," collecting site-setup and reporting fees, with nearly 7 billion yuan involved — Zhang Sheng got 9 years 9 months and a 50 million yuan fine, and the 79 people responsible for outsourced development, including Wang Qiang, got sentences from 6 months to 9 years 9 months; Chen Dong and others developed and ran an esports betting app with the technical team writing code in Hainan — 28 people were convicted of operating a gambling venue with sentences from 3 years 6 months to 1 year plus fines; Zhang ran a studio unblocking banned QQ accounts for a fraud ring — 1 year 6 months and a 20,000 yuan fine (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十七条之二、第三百零三条第二款）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民检察院 (2023). 建赌博网站并出售，涉案近 70 亿元. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202307/t20230711_621248.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2022). 上海闵行区：办理一起新型开设网络赌场案. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202208/t20220805_569952.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 依法惩治帮助信息网络犯罪活动及相关犯罪典型案例（案例一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202507/t20250728_702377.shtml>
- Notes: "knowingly" does not require the other side to say so outright; quotes clearly above market rates, features that are deposits, withdrawals plus payout odds, and clients who refuse to say what the product is for are all used in practice to infer knowledge. Technical staff who develop gambling apps are treated as accomplices to operating a gambling venue, not under the 3-year ceiling of aiding-cybercrime.

### 6. When leaving a job, hand over all accounts and permissions; do not delete databases, leave backdoors, or change passwords to lock the system, even if the company owes you money

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; wage disputes go through labor arbitration (see section 7)
- In plain terms: the account and password were given by the company — that does not mean you may keep using them after leaving. Logging in already counts as illegal control, punishable by up to 3 years; deleting data so the system cannot run carries up to 5 years, and over 5 years when the consequences are especially serious. A senior engineer who was fired logged into the company's servers with his old admin account, changed passwords, and deleted data and logs, causing losses of over 120,000 yuan — sentenced to 3 years, suspended for 5 years, plus a 30,000 yuan fine. Losses are calculated by the cost of restoring the data; a few tens of thousands of yuan is enough to open a case.
- Benefit: illegally controlling a computer system, in serious circumstances, carries up to 3 years plus a fine (or a fine alone); especially serious circumstances carry 3 to 7 years; deleting or modifying system functions or data so the system cannot run normally, with serious consequences, carries up to 5 years, and 5 years or more when especially serious; official cases: senior IT engineer Lü was fired, then used his original admin account to log into the company's shared server, change passwords, and delete data and logs, causing losses of over 120,000 yuan — 3 years suspended 5 years and a 30,000 yuan fine; Bai, an employee of a software company, deliberately inserted faulty code in an upgrade program so customers could not log into their systems, causing losses of over 40,000 yuan, and was prosecuted for the crime of damaging computer information systems (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第二款、第二百八十六条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民检察院 (2024). 检察机关「依法惩治网络犯罪 助力网络空间综合治理」典型案例（案例六）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202402/t20240223_644505.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 检察机关依法惩治侵害企业数据安全犯罪 服务保障企业创新发展（案例七）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202501/t20250124_680670.shtml>
- Notes: being given the account and password by the company does not mean you may keep using it after leaving; logging in with the old account already counts as "illegal control." Losses are calculated by data-restoration costs; a few tens of thousands of yuan is enough to open a case.

### 7. Do not take source code, client lists, or technical documents when leaving a job; do not upload them to a personal cloud drive or reuse them at the next employer

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; before leaving, wipe all company materials from your personal computer and cloud drive
- In plain terms: even code you wrote yourself belongs to the company as copyright — taking it is taking the company's property, and client lists count as trade secrets too. One departing employee took security-inspection system source code and core algorithms to a new company: he was sentenced to 5 years and a 500,000 yuan fine, and the new company was fined 4 million yuan. Someone else exploited a system vulnerability to download over 33,000 design documents — 1 year 10 months and a 100,000 yuan fine. On top of the criminal case, the company usually files a separate civil claim for damages.
- Benefit: disclosing or using trade secrets in breach of confidentiality obligations, in serious circumstances, carries up to 3 years plus a fine (or a fine alone); especially serious circumstances carry 3 to 10 years plus a fine; the anti-unfair-competition law lists obtaining trade secrets through "electronic intrusion" and abetting or helping others breach confidentiality obligations as infringement; official cases: departing employees including Sun Mouming took security-inspection system source code and core algorithms for use in the new company's products — Sun Mouming got 5 years and a 500,000 yuan fine, and the new company was fined 4 million yuan; Tian exploited a system vulnerability to download over 33,000 design documents — 1 year 10 months and a 100,000 yuan fine; engineer Yang stored app module source code on his personal computer and the cloud and built a similar app after changing jobs — prosecuted for the crime of infringing trade secrets (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百一十九条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 反不正当竞争法（2025 年修订，第十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699862.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 检察机关知识产权保护典型案例（案例七）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202504/t20250423_693691.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2020). 2019 年度检察机关保护知识产权典型案例（案例十六）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202004/t20200425_459623.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 检察机关依法惩治侵害企业数据安全犯罪 服务保障企业创新发展（案例五）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202501/t20250124_680670.shtml>
- Notes: even "code you wrote yourself" belongs to the company as copyright (see entry 11); taking it is taking the company's property. Client lists are business information and also trade secrets. Besides the criminal case, the company will also file a civil claim for damages.

### 8. Do not run your own programs on other people's computers, servers, or cameras; do not mine crypto on company machines

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: controlling other people's computers, servers, or cameras without permission counts as "serious circumstances" at 20 devices — up to 3 years, and 3 to 7 years when especially serious. Even short of a crime, you get up to 5 days of detention plus a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 yuan, or 5 to 15 days plus 100,000 to 1 million yuan in more serious cases. What weighs more on tech people is the knock-on effect: after a public-security punishment you cannot hold key network security management or network operation positions for 5 years, and after a criminal sentence, for life.
- Benefit: illegally controlling a computer system, in serious circumstances, carries up to 3 years plus a fine (or a fine alone); especially serious circumstances carry 3 to 7 years; controlling 20 or more devices is "serious circumstances"; where it does not yet constitute a crime, the police confiscate illegal gains, detain for up to 5 days and may fine 50,000 to 500,000 yuan, or in more serious cases detain 5 to 15 days and may fine 100,000 to 1 million yuan; after a public-security punishment you may not work in network security management or key network operation positions for 5 years, and after a criminal punishment, never; official cases: Li bought camera IP lists and used software to control 105 surveillance cameras to peep on private life — 3 years suspended 5 years and a 20,000 yuan fine; Wu and others implanted trojans into phone motherboards to control over 3.3 million phones and siphon verification codes — sentences from 2 years to 4 years 6 months (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第二款）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2011). 关于办理危害计算机信息系统安全刑事案件应用法律若干问题的解释（第一条）. <https://ga.sz.gov.cn/ZWGK/ZCFG/ZCJD/content/post_1304363.html>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 网络安全法（2025 年修正，2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第二十九条；终身禁业条款 2016 年文本为第六十三条第二款，修正后法律责任章条号有调整，本次未逐条核实）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-12/29/c_1768735112911946.htm>；最高人民检察院 (2022). 山东滨州开发区：办理一起非法控制计算机信息系统案. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/dfjcdt/202205/t20220518_557113.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2021). 充分发挥检察职能 推进网络空间治理典型案例（案例六）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/xwfbh/wsfbh/202101/t20210125_507452.shtml>
- Notes: no case published by the Supreme People's Procuratorate or Supreme People's Court was found for mining on company servers; this entry is written from the statute on "controlling other people's systems without authorization." The "lifetime ban from key positions" is a knock-on consequence under the cybersecurity law — for tech people it weighs more than the fine.

### 9. Do not sell circumvention tools or VPN accounts; do not set up such nodes for others

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: building your own or using an international channel other than the state's public channels lets the police order you to stop connecting, issue a warning, and fine up to 15,000 yuan and confiscate illegal gains; if you run a business on it, the case-handling authorities can treat it as the crime of illegal business operation — up to 5 years in serious circumstances plus a fine of 1 to 5 times the illegal gains, and over 5 years when especially serious. This entry is about selling and setting up nodes; the punishment for personal use tops out at a warning plus a fine. Handling varies widely by region, and no published precedent was found on the official websites.
- Benefit: direct international networking must use the international gateway channels provided by the state's public telecom network; no individual or organization may build or use other channels — violators are ordered by the police to stop networking, given a warning, and may be fined up to 15,000 yuan and have illegal gains confiscated; running a business on this can be handled by case authorities as the crime of illegal business operation, with up to 5 years in serious circumstances plus a fine (or a fine alone) of 1 to 5 times the illegal gains, and over 5 years when especially serious (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 国务院 (1997). 计算机信息网络国际联网管理暂行规定（第六、十四条）. <https://nyncw.cq.gov.cn/wsdw/zz/zhfb/wlyxxaq/202406/t20240619_13306645.html>（重庆市农业农村委员会转载）；全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百二十五条第四项）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>
- Notes: at the time of writing, no published circumvention precedent was found on the Supreme People's Court or Supreme People's Procuratorate websites, and handling varies widely by region — hence grade B; the entry covers only "selling and setting up," and the punishment for personal use tops out at the warning and fine in Article 14 of the provisional regulations.

### 10. If you signed a non-compete and the company stops paying monthly compensation after you leave, demand payment in writing; after 3 full months unpaid you can terminate it; if your role never touched trade secrets, you can ask to have the clause declared void

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; keep the original agreement and evidence that the company did not pay
- In plain terms: this one protects you. Non-compete restrictions last at most 2 years, and the company must pay monthly compensation; where no amount is agreed, it is 30% of your average wage over the 12 months before departure, and never below the local minimum wage. If the company does not pay for 3 months for its own reasons, you can ask to terminate. Starting in 2025, if your role never exposed you to trade secrets, you can ask to have the clause declared void — a pharmaceutical company sued its chief technology officer for 7.1 million yuan in penalties, and the court held that the non-compete scope could only cover secrets he actually knew, and dismissed the claim entirely.
- Benefit: non-compete restrictions may only bind senior executives, senior technical personnel, and others with confidentiality obligations, last at most 2 years, and the company must pay monthly economic compensation during the period; where no compensation amount is agreed, it is 30% of the average wage over the 12 months before departure and not below the local minimum wage; if the company fails to pay for 3 months for its own reasons, the worker may request termination; if the company terminates early, it owes an extra 3 months of compensation; starting in 2025, a worker who never learned of or came into contact with trade secrets may request that the clause be declared void, and any scope beyond the secrets actually known is invalid; official case: a pharmaceutical company sued its chief technology officer Zheng for 7.1 million yuan in penalties — the court found that the new employer's product was not a substitute for the original company's and that the non-compete scope should be limited to secrets he actually knew, and dismissed the claim entirely (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2012). 劳动合同法（2012 年修正，第二十三、二十四条）. <https://yjglj.fushun.gov.cn/008/20260622/d846d3c2-df3d-4c4c-af16-898ede43e1ee.html>（抚顺市应急管理局转载）；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）（第三十六至四十条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/282121.html>；最高人民法院 (2025). 关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（二）（法释〔2025〕12 号，第十三至十五条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/472691.html>；最高人民法院 (2025). 劳动争议典型案例（案例四）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/472681.html>
- Notes: this is an entry about protecting yourself. The other way around: if the agreement is valid and the company paid monthly, joining a competitor means paying the agreed penalties, and after paying you may still be required to keep honoring the restriction; a non-compete agreement made during employment does not depend on compensation being paid.

### 11. Code written during work hours or with company resources belongs to the company; do personal open-source projects on your own time and equipment, and never mix in company code

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; read the intellectual-property clause in the labor contract when joining
- In plain terms: software is explicitly listed by law in the "copyright belongs to the employer" category: if you mainly used company equipment, the intranet, or business data and the company bears responsibility, or the contract says so, the code's copyright belongs to the company and you keep only attribution rights; for inventions made with company resources, the patents belong to the company too. "Written after hours" still counts as mainly using the employer's material and technical conditions if it was done on a company computer or with company business data.
- Benefit: works created to complete an employer's work tasks are works made in the course of employment — in the general case the copyright belongs to the author, the employer has the right to use them first within its business scope, and for 2 years the author may not license third parties to use them in the same manner; but engineering designs, software, and similar works created mainly using the employer's material and technical conditions with the employer bearing responsibility, or works the contract assigns to the employer, leave the author only attribution rights; inventions completed as part of one's duties or mainly using the employer's material and technical conditions are service inventions, and both the patent application right and the patent right belong to the employer (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 著作权法（2020 年修正，第十八条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308796.html>；全国人大常委会 (2020). 专利法（2020 年修正，第六条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308800.html>
- Notes: software is explicitly listed in the clause where copyright belongs to the employer — programmers have almost no room under the general rule; "written after hours" can still be found to mainly use the employer's material and technical conditions if it used a company computer, the intranet, or company business data. To keep a personal project clean, use your own machine, never touch company repositories, and do not overlap with company business.

### 12. When building products on GPL and other open-source code, open-source as the license requires or switch licenses; do not assume "nobody enforces it"

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; glance at the license when adding a dependency; if the GPL family conflicts with closed-source commercial use, switch
- In plain terms: copyright infringement damages are the rights holder's actual losses or your illegal gains; when neither can be calculated, licensing fees are the reference, and deliberate infringement with serious circumstances can be multiplied 1 to 5 times. In one case the defendant hired the plaintiff's former employees to develop gateway software, and the code was substantially similar; the defendant argued that the plaintiff's software was itself based on GPLv2 and should have been open-sourced anyway, but the Supreme People's Court held these were two independent issues and still ordered the infringement stopped and 500,000 yuan paid.
- Benefit: copyright infringement is compensated by the rights holder's actual losses or the infringer's illegal gains; when hard to calculate, licensing fees are the reference; deliberate infringement with serious circumstances can be awarded 1 to 5 times the damages, and where the public interest is harmed, illegal gains may also be confiscated and fines imposed; official case: (2021) Zui Gao Fa Zhi Min Zhong No. 51 — the gateway software the defendant developed by recruiting the plaintiff's former employees was substantially similar to the plaintiff's software; the defendant argued the plaintiff's software was based on GPLv2 and should have been open-sourced anyway, but the Supreme People's Court held that "whether the developer itself violated the GPLv2 license and whether it holds the software's copyright are two independent issues," and ordered the infringement stopped and 500,000 yuan in damages (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 著作权法（2020 年修正，第五十三、五十四条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308796.html>；最高人民法院知识产权法庭 (2022). 涉「开源软件」著作权侵权案（（2021）最高法知民终 51 号）. <https://ipc.court.gov.cn/zh-cn/news/view-3042.html>
- Notes: this case shows that "the other side used GPL code" is not a shield for copying from it; conversely, GPL authors suing commercial users who fail to open-source already have effective judgments in China — the original text was not found on the Supreme People's Court website, so it is not cited here. Grade B because license obligations rest on contracts and the general provisions of copyright law, with no dedicated statute.

### 13. Apps collect only the personal information the business needs; those under 14 require guardian consent; write the privacy policy clearly

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; before launch, reconcile the permission list against the privacy policy and drop any permission you can do without
- In plain terms: collect only what the business truly needs, and for anyone under 14 get guardian consent. Violations first bring an order to correct, a warning, and confiscation of illegal gains; the app can be ordered to suspend or terminate service, and refusal to correct brings a fine of up to 1 million yuan, with the directly responsible people fined 10,000 to 100,000 yuan; in serious cases the fine reaches 50 million yuan or 5% of the previous year's revenue, with the responsible people fined 100,000 to 1 million yuan. In practice, "directly responsible personnel" often lands on the people who write the code and build the product.
- Benefit: processing personal information must have a clear and reasonable purpose, be directly related to that purpose, and use the method with the least impact on individual rights; processing requires the individual's consent or another statutory basis, and for those under 14 the consent of parents or guardians is required; violations bring an order to correct, a warning, and confiscation of illegal gains from the regulator; non-compliant apps can be ordered to suspend or terminate service; refusal to correct brings a fine of up to 1 million yuan, and the directly responsible managers and other directly responsible personnel are fined 10,000 to 100,000 yuan; in serious cases fines reach 50 million yuan or up to 5% of the previous year's revenue, with responsible personnel fined 100,000 to 1 million yuan and possibly barred from serving as directors, supervisors, or senior executives of relevant companies for a set period; for selling or providing personal information as a crime, see entry 4 (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 个人信息保护法（第六、十三、三十一、六十六条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202108/t20210820_313088.html>
- Notes: in practice "directly responsible personnel" often falls on the people who write the code and build the product; the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology regularly announces removals of non-compliant apps — the specific notice numbers were not verified for this section, so they are not cited.

### 14. File ICP registration before a website or app goes live, and keep logs for at least 6 months as required by the classified protection regime

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: registration is free and takes a few weeks through the cloud provider's process; classified protection assessments are charged by level, and small sites generally do not need an assessment
- In plain terms: registration costs nothing and takes about a few weeks through the cloud provider's process; on a mainland-China server, an unregistered domain simply will not resolve. Unregistered sites are first ordered to correct, and refusal means an order to shut the site down; not displaying the registration number on the homepage brings a fine of 5,000 to 50,000 yuan; registering as non-commercial but actually charging is handled as operating without a license — confiscation of illegal gains plus a fine of 100,000 to 1 million yuan. Logs must be kept for a full 6 months; failing that brings an order to correct and a warning, and refusal to correct or resulting harm brings a fine of 10,000 to 100,000 yuan.
- Benefit: commercial internet information services operate under licensing and non-commercial ones under registration; neither may operate without a license or registration; unregistered sites are ordered to correct by the telecom authority, and refusal means an order to close the website; operating without a license brings confiscation of illegal gains plus a fine of 3 to 5 times the gains or 100,000 to 1 million yuan; not displaying the registration number on the website homepage brings a fine of 5,000 to 50,000 yuan; network operators must establish security management systems under the classified protection regime, guard against attacks, retain network logs for no less than 6 months, and classify, back up, and encrypt data — failure brings an order to correct and a warning, and refusal to correct or resulting harm brings a fine of 10,000 to 100,000 yuan with managers fined 5,000 to 50,000 yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2011). 互联网信息服务管理办法（2011 年修订，第四、十九、二十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1860864.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 网络安全法（2025 年修正，2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第二十三、六十一条；2016 年文本为第二十一、五十九条）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-12/29/c_1768735112911946.htm>
- Notes: on a mainland-China server an unregistered domain simply will not resolve; the real traps are "registered as personal non-commercial but actually charging" and "changed the domain or entity without updating the registration." Keeping logs for 6 months is both an obligation and self-protection — when something happens, they prove it was not you.

### 15. Before offering generative AI services to the public, complete the required security assessment and algorithm registration first, and label generated content

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; go through the cyberspace administration process — a personal small tool not opened to the public does not trigger this
- In plain terms: offering the public a generative AI service with public-opinion attributes or social mobilization capacity requires a security assessment under national rules and algorithm registration before launch, plus change or deregistration procedures when the service is modified or stopped, and generated images and videos must carry labels. A small tool for your own use, not open to the public, does not trigger any of this. The measures themselves contain no penalty clause — violations are handled under the cybersecurity law, the data security law, and the personal information protection law.
- Benefit: providing generative artificial intelligence services with public-opinion attributes or social mobilization capacity requires a security assessment under national rules, and fulfillment of algorithm registration and change and deregistration procedures under the algorithm recommendation management rules; providers must label generated content such as images and videos; the measures took effect on August 15, 2023 (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 国家互联网信息办公室等七部门 (2023). 生成式人工智能服务管理暂行办法（第十二、十七条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891752.htm>
- Notes: the measures refer violations to the cybersecurity law, the data security law, and the personal information protection law, with no standalone penalty clause — hence grade B; civil cases over AI-generated content infringing others' copyrights or likeness were not verified against the Supreme People's Court website for this section, so they are not cited.
