# 31. What Roads Are Open After Eighteen

The yardstick is money and time; the road of refusing military service also touches personal freedom. This section is a route map, not a manual for any single road: it first lays the legal thresholds of the various roads side by side, then takes each road in turn and sets out the things you only have use for once you know them — what serving as a conscript gets you, which doors into the state system exist besides the civil-service exam, whether a credential can still be made up later, and how those who never join a work unit handle social insurance.

Which road to take is your own affair; this section offers only two things: which document states each threshold, and, once you are on a road, which deadlines and eligibility rules lapse for good if missed. How to weigh studying against working is covered in Section 23; overtime pay, work injury, and severance once you are inside a work unit are in Section 19; opening your own shop or company is in Section 12; the safety net after losing a job is in Section 7. None of that is repeated here.
### 1. Check the thresholds first: the age and education requirements of the twelve roads are fixed in writing
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: Free. Spend ten minutes going through the list to see which ones you can reach.
- In plain terms: The age and education thresholds of these twelve roads are all written in plain text in official documents. To take a job you must be at least 16. Military service: men at 18 or above, and university graduates can have the age relaxed to 24. The civil-service exam: 18 to 35, with a junior-college degree or above. The self-taught exam and registering as an individual business look at neither education nor age.
- Benefit: Military service follows the military service law: "Male citizens who have reached the age of eighteen shall be conscripted for active service; those not conscripted in that year may still be conscripted for active service before reaching twenty-two. The conscription age may be relaxed to twenty-four for graduates of regular institutions of higher education and to twenty-six for postgraduates." The civil-service exam follows Article 18 of the Provisions on the Recruitment of Civil Servants. The application conditions include "aged eighteen or above and under thirty-five" and "education at the junior-college level or above". These two may be adjusted appropriately on approval by the civil-service competent department at the provincial level or above. Article 19 of those provisions lists five categories of people who may not apply, for example those who received criminal punishment for a crime, those dismissed from public office, and those listed as targets of joint disciplinary action for dishonesty. Firefighters follow the firefighter recruitment measures for the national comprehensive fire and rescue force. Article 5: "aged 18 or above and under 22" and "senior high school education or above". Article 6: "persons with a junior-college degree or above, and former service members of the PLA and the People's Armed Police... may have the age relaxed to 24". Military civilian personnel follow Article 9 of the regulations on civilian personnel of the Chinese People's Liberation Army; the basic conditions include "having reached the age of 18". Article 19 of those regulations states that the maximum age for first-time recruitment and hiring to lower-rank posts is 35. Lower-rank posts are the entry-level posts. Public institutions follow Article 8 of the regulations on personnel management of public institutions: "When a public institution newly employs staff, it shall conduct open recruitment open to society". Individual businesses follow the regulation on promoting the development of individual businesses: "citizens with the capacity to operate... who register as individual businesses in accordance with the law are governed by this regulation". The self-taught exam follows the interim regulations on self-taught higher-education examinations. Article 3: "Citizens of the People's Republic of China, without restriction by sex, age, ethnicity, race, or prior level of education, may take the self-taught higher-education examinations under these regulations". Article 20: "students enrolled in full-time schools of all types and at all levels may not register". Government-funded normal students follow Article 2 of the Measures for Government-Funded Education of Undergraduate-to-Master Normal Students at Normal Universities Directly under the Ministry of Education. Recruitment happens only at six ministry-affiliated normal universities: Beijing Normal University, East China Normal University, Northeast Normal University, Central China Normal University, Shaanxi Normal University, and Southwest University. The undergraduate and postgraduate stages are designed as one whole with staged assessment, meaning you go straight on to the postgraduate stage after finishing the undergraduate one. The central budget covers tuition and housing during the years at school, with a living stipend on top. Article 5 of those measures: "recruitment of government-funded normal students uses early-batch admission", that is, admission before the regular batch. Targeted medical students follow the notice on the employment placement and agreement performance management of rural order-based targeted tuition-free medical students. It says: "after admission and before receiving the admission letter, the student must sign an agreement with the training school and with the county-level health administration ... and human resources and social security department where the targeted employment is located". Working abroad follows the Regulation on the Administration of Overseas Labor Cooperation. Article 5: engaging in overseas labor cooperation requires business qualification approved by the commerce department. Article 7, paragraph 4: "without lawfully obtaining the overseas labor cooperation business qualification certificate and completing registration, one may not engage in overseas labor cooperation". That means a company without this qualification certificate is breaking the law simply by organizing people to work abroad. Article 8, paragraph 2: "no organization or individual may organize workers to go abroad for work under the name of business, tourism, study, or the like". Taking orders on platforms and flexible employment have no statutory education threshold; for enrolling in social insurance yourself and for occupational injury protection see entries 11 and 12 of this section. The compulsory education law "prohibits employers from hiring school-age children and adolescents who should be receiving compulsory education" (nationwide)

### 2. Register for military service before October 31 of the year you turn 18; a conscript's active-service term is two years
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: Free. Registration is filled in online once and takes a few minutes. If you really go and serve, the price is two years full-time, and during those two years you cannot do anything else alongside.
- In plain terms: Military-service registration is a legal duty, not signing up to enlist, and registering does not mean you have to go. A male who turns 18 that year must complete registration online before October 31. If you fail to register, the county will order you to correct it within a time limit, and if you still do not, you will be forced to perform your service duty and fined. If you really go and serve, a conscript's active-service term is two years.
- Benefit: Registration follows the military-service registration work rules. It says: "The military-service authorities of the people's governments of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, and municipal districts shall, before October 31 each year, organize initial military-service registration for male citizens within their jurisdiction who turn 18 before December 31 of that year." The same rules also say: "initial military-service registration is conducted mainly online, completed by the citizen independently through the military-service registration network platform." What happens if you do not register is in Article 57, paragraph 1, of the military-service law. That paragraph governs those who "refuse or evade military-service registration." The penalty is "an order from the county-level people's government to correct within a time limit; if the deadline passes without correction, the county-level people's government shall compel performance of the service duty and impose a fine." The military-service law also says "the term of active service for conscripts is two years." It also says "where the military needs it and the person volunteers, citizens who have reached seventeen but not eighteen may be conscripted for active service." Non-commissioned officers who "have served twelve years on active duty or meet other conditions set by the state shall be given jobs by the local people's government at or above the county level of the placement area." Non-commissioned officers who "have served thirty years or reached fifty-five years of age or meet other conditions set by the state receive retirement placement." Being given a job means the government provides one; retirement placement means leaving under retirement arrangements. The conscription work regulations say "priority is given to graduates of regular institutions of higher education and to the recruitment of personnel with special political, physical, or professional-skill requirements." Those regulations also say "citizens applying for service who are full-time current students or fresh graduates of regular institutions of higher education may answer the call either at their place of household registration before enrollment or where their school is located" (nationwide)

- Sources: 国务院办公厅、中央军委办公厅 (2025). 兵役登记工作规定（国办发〔2025〕41 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202512/content_7051363.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2021). 兵役法（2021 年修订，第二十、二十五、二十六、五十四、五十七条）. <http://www.mod.gov.cn/regulatory/2021-08/20/content_4892491.htm>（国防部刊登）；国务院、中央军委 (2023). 征兵工作条例（国务院、中央军委令第 759 号，第五条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2023/content_5753313.htm>
- Notes: Registration and actually being drafted are two different things, and the costs differ by a wide margin. The string of penalties within two years, including the exit ban, applies only to those who renege after being drafted; see entry 3 (refusing military service). Women are not required to do initial military-service registration and are conscripted according to the military's needs. The conditions for soldiers to sit the military-academy exam, and for soldiers with good records to become officers, are written in internal military regulations and cannot be found outside. The military-service law says only two things: active-duty officers are selected and recruited from among "active-duty soldiers with outstanding performance" and similar personnel, and military academies' student recruitment is not bound by the conscription age limits. This book gives no specific score lines or quotas.

### 3. Refusing military service after answering the call: for two years no leaving the country and no returning to school, and no way into the civil service or state-owned enterprises
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. The price is having thought through whether to go before you sign up.
- In plain terms: If you sign up and then refuse to go and serve, the consequences come as a chain. You cannot be hired as a civil servant, and state-owned enterprises and public institutions cannot recruit you either. For two years you may not leave the country, may not move up to further schooling, and may not return to your school to resume your studies. You are also placed on the list of seriously dishonest subjects in performing national-defense obligations. So "sign up first and back out later if it does not work out" is a very expensive idea.
- Benefit: Article 57, paragraph 1, item 2, of the military-service law covers "citizens drafted for service who refuse or evade conscription into active service." The penalty is likewise "an order from the county-level people's government to correct within a time limit; if the deadline passes without correction, the county-level people's government shall compel performance of the service duty and impose a fine." Paragraph 2 of the same article: "Whoever commits the act in item 2 of the preceding paragraph and refuses to correct it may not be hired as a civil servant or as staff managed with reference to the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China, may not be recruited or employed as staff of state-owned enterprises or public institutions, may not leave the country or resume or continue studies for two years, and is placed on the list of seriously dishonest subjects in performing national-defense obligations for joint disciplinary action." Joint disciplinary action means several departments take the same list and enforce the restrictions above together. Article 58 attaches the same set of penalties to people already enlisted. When a service member refuses to perform duties or deserts the force with the aim of evading military service, the consequences depend on the outcome of the handling. Those struck off the rolls, expelled from the military, or held criminally liable under law are punished under Article 57, paragraph 2; among them, those struck off the rolls are also fined (nationwide, effective October 1, 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 兵役法（2021 年修订，第五十七、五十八条）. <http://www.mod.gov.cn/regulatory/2021-08/20/content_4892491.htm>（国防部刊登）
- Notes: This entry counts personal freedom and employment eligibility, not money. Read precisely who gets penalized: this chain of punishments hits only two kinds of people. One kind is those "drafted for service who refuse or evade conscription into active service." The other is those who, after enlisting, are struck off the rolls, expelled from the military, or prosecuted criminally. Someone who merely has not done military-service registration is not among them; registration is in entry 2. The precondition for the penalty is "refusal to correct," determined and imposed by the county-level people's government. How much the fine is, each locality decides itself; this book does not state it. This entry does not push you to go or not go; it only says that from the moment you sign up, the decision can no longer be undone.

### 4. The two most tangible benefits of serving as a student: tuition made up at up to 20,000 yuan a year, student status kept for two years, and a dedicated plan for the postgraduate exam
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. After discharge you submit the documents and apply as your school requires, one or two trips in all.
- In plain terms: When current students, fresh graduates, and newly admitted students go and serve, the state pays the tuition for the years of service: at most 20,000 yuan per person per year for undergraduate and vocational students, and at most 25,000 yuan per person per year for postgraduates, so two years means at most 40,000 or 50,000. Your student status is kept for you, and within two years after discharge you may enroll or return to school. The postgraduate exam has a separate dedicated plan that recruits only retired university-student soldiers.
- Benefit: The 2024 notice from the Ministry of Finance and three other departments says that higher-education students who go and perform military service get three things: tuition compensation, repayment on their behalf of state student loans taken out for tuition, and tuition waiver. Repayment on your behalf means the state pays off that student loan for you. The standard for all three is "adjusted accordingly to no more than 20,000 yuan per undergraduate or vocational student per year and no more than 25,000 yuan per postgraduate per year". The new standard takes effect from the fall semester of 2024. How much is actually made up follows this sentence: "implemented at the higher of the tuition the student actually paid or the state student loan received (including principal and all interest accrued before repayment)". There is a further sentence: "any portion above the standard is not compensated, repaid, or waived". On the student-status side, see the veterans support law. It says: "A person admitted to a regular institution of higher education before enlisting, or studying at one, retains the admission qualification or student status during active service and may enroll or resume studies within two years of discharge, and may transfer to another major at the same school under relevant state provisions". The law also says: "institutions of higher education, under overall state arrangements, may recruit veterans through separately listed plans, separate admissions, and other means". For the postgraduate-exam road, see the Ministry of Education general office's 2026 notice. The plan is called the "retired university-student soldier plan". The notice says: "issued as a separately listed quota within the overall national postgraduate enrollment plan and used exclusively for this purpose, and may not be converted to ordinary plans or other special plans. Admitting institutions independently set... the exam score requirement (generally no higher than the requirement for ordinary-plan candidates in the corresponding discipline) ... where candidates are otherwise equal, those with longer service are admitted first". Separately listed and exclusively used means this portion of quotas is carved out on its own and can only be used to recruit retired university-student soldiers. In 2026 the plan is carried by 532 regular institutions of higher education (nationwide)

### 5. Only six situations lead to a government-arranged job after discharge; those on self-employment must report within 30 days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Take your discharge certificate and make one trip to the veterans affairs department of the placement area.
- In plain terms: "The army guarantees you a job" stopped being the general case long ago. Now only six situations lead to a government-arranged job, and a conscript who serves two years and leaves is in the vast majority of cases not among those six. The bracket where the government does not arrange work is called self-employment, and it has a hard deadline: you must report within 30 days of leaving active service. Passing 30 days without a justified reason counts as giving up your placement benefits.
- Benefit: Article 25 of the regulation on the placement of veterans lists six situations in which the placement-area people's government arranges work. The original text: "(1) NCOs who have served 12 years on active duty; (2) those who during active service were personally awarded a medal or an honorary title; (3) those who during active service were personally awarded a third-class combat merit or a second-class merit or above; (4) those who during active service personally received a first-class commendation; (5) those disabled in combat and assessed at disability grade 5 to 8; (6) children of martyrs." The regulation also says: "the people's government of the placement area shall complete the task of arranging jobs for discharged NCOs and conscripts within 6 months of accepting them". Employers "shall sign with them, as required, a medium- or long-term employment contract or labor contract of no less than 3 years. Among these, when an enterprise accepts a discharged NCO with 10 or more years of military service, it shall sign an open-ended labor contract". Open-ended means the contract carries no end date. While awaiting placement to a job, "a living allowance is paid month by month at the local monthly minimum wage standard". Where placement to a post is not made on time for reasons not your own, "living expenses are paid month by month at no less than 80 percent of the average wage of personnel of equal standing in the unit, until placement to a post". Situations in which placement by job arrangement is not allowed include expulsion from the Communist Party of China and having received criminal punishment. For the self-employment deadline, see Article 51: "Discharged NCOs and conscripts who are self-employed shall report to the competent veterans affairs department of the people's government of the placement area within 30 days from the date their discharge from active service is approved. Failure to report within the prescribed time for more than 30 days without justifiable reason is deemed a waiver of placement benefits." (nationwide, effective September 1, 2024)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院、中央军委 (2024). 退役军人安置条例（国务院、中央军委令第 787 号，2024 年 7 月 29 日公布、9 月 1 日施行；第二十五、三十一、三十二、三十五、五十一、七十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11526/202408/content_6969192.html>；全国人大常委会 (2020). 退役军人保障法（第十三、十四、十五条）. <http://www.mod.gov.cn/gfbw/fgwx/flfg/16048597.html>（国防部刊登）
- Notes: On the same day this regulation took effect, the 2011 regulation on the placement of retired soldiers (State Council and Central Military Commission Decree No. 608) was repealed. Large numbers of online guides still cite the old regulation, whose wording of the conditions has changed: the old version read "awarded a second-class merit or above in peacetime or a third-class merit or above in wartime". The 30-day deadline governs only the self-employment bracket; other placement methods require reporting "within the prescribed time". At reporting you are issued the veterans preferential-treatment card, and the later discharge allowance, employment and entrepreneurship support, and education subsidies all connect from there.

### 6. Three accounts after discharge: the one-time discharge allowance, military service counted as seniority, and a 20,000-yuan-per-year tax deduction for going it alone
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. At discharge the military transfers your social insurance records and money out for you. You then complete the transfer at the social insurance handling agency of the placement area, which is the window that handles social insurance.
- In plain terms: After discharge there are three accounts. The first is the one-time discharge allowance for self-employment, paid by years of active service, with an extra payment for those with merit citations. The second is that the years in the army do not count as a social insurance gap: military service counts as working years, and the contribution years are combined with those before enlistment and after discharge. The third is tax: if you open your own shop and do business, you can pay at most 20,000 yuan less in tax each year for 3 years.
- Benefit: For the first account, see the regulation on the placement of veterans. It says: "Discharged NCOs and conscripts who are self-employed are paid a one-time discharge allowance based on their years of active service". Who pays the money: "it is arranged as a special item by central finance, with the specific standard determined... and adjusted in due course by the state council veterans affairs department and the political work department of the Central Military Commission together with the state council finance department". The uplift percentages come in five tiers. A medal or honorary title adds 25 percent; a first-class combat merit or a first-class commendation adds 20 percent. A second-class combat merit, a first-class merit, or a second-class commendation adds 15 percent. A third-class combat merit or a second-class merit adds 10 percent, and a fourth-class combat merit or a third-class merit adds 5 percent. The regulation also says: "veterans who are not employed in time may claim unemployment insurance benefits under the law from the human resources and social security department of their place of household registration, with years of active service counted as years of insured contributions, except veterans placed by retirement or support". Counted as contribution years means that although you paid nothing for those years, they still count as paid. For the second account, see the veterans support law: "Years of active service of veterans count as working years and, after discharge, are accumulated together with years worked at their employer". Working years are the years you have been in work; the years of service and the years after discharge add on continuously. The law also says that years of active service and the pension, medical, and unemployment contribution years before enlistment and after discharge are "combined as provided by law", and that "the active-service experience of discharged NCOs and conscripts is deemed grassroots work experience". The military insurance law: "Years of a service member's active duty are deemed years of contribution to employee basic medical insurance and are combined with contribution years under employee basic medical insurance before enlistment and after discharge from active service". For the third account, see Announcement No. 14 (2023) of the Ministry of Finance, the State Taxation Administration, and the Ministry of Veterans Affairs. The announcement covers retired soldiers on self-employment running an individual business: "within 3 years (36 months, the same below) deduct in sequence, up to 20,000 yuan per household per year, the VAT, urban maintenance and construction tax, education surcharge, local education surcharge, and individual income tax actually payable for the year. The cap may be raised by up to 20 percent". Deduction means subtracting in order from the taxes of these kinds actually owed for that year, down to the cap; it is not money paid to you. For enterprises that hire them, "the fixed standard is 6,000 yuan per person per year, which may be raised by up to 50 percent". The implementation period runs from January 1, 2023 to December 31, 2027. Those who have not reached 3 years by expiry may continue to benefit until the term ends (nationwide)

### 14. Before you go work abroad, check whether the company holds an overseas labor cooperation license: it may not charge you a deposit
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Spend ten-plus minutes before departure checking the company's credentials, then read both contracts through.
- In plain terms: When it comes to working abroad, the law does not target you; it targets the company that organizes your trip. Only enterprises that have obtained the overseas labor cooperation business qualification certificate and completed registration may do this. If you hear "get a tourist visa first, then switch to a work visa," that sentence by itself shows the other side is breaking the law. The company also may not charge you a deposit under any name, or ask you to put up property as security.
- Benefit: Article 5 of the Regulation on the Administration of Overseas Labor Cooperation: engaging in overseas labor cooperation requires business qualification approved by the commerce department at the provincial level or the level of a city divided into districts. That qualification is the overseas labor cooperation business qualification certificate; without it no one may organize people to work abroad. Article 7: the list of qualification certificates is reported to the commerce department of the State Council and communicated to Chinese embassies and consulates abroad. This article also states that "without lawfully obtaining the overseas labor cooperation business qualification certificate and completing registration, one may not engage in overseas labor cooperation." Article 8: "no organization or individual may organize workers to go abroad for work under the name of business, tourism, study, or the like." Article 9: within 5 working days of registration the enterprise must deposit with a designated bank "an overseas labor cooperation risk disposal reserve of no less than 3 million yuan," and the list of deposits is published to society. Article 10: the reserve is used to pay service fees due for refund, labor remuneration, and legally owed compensation that the enterprise refuses or is unable to bear. It is also used for "costs needed when workers return home or receive emergency assistance because of a sudden event." Article 11: workers may not be organized to go abroad for work related to gambling or pornography. Article 12: those without arranged training may not be organized to go abroad. Article 13: personal accident insurance must be bought for workers covering their time working abroad. Articles 21 and 23: written contracts must be concluded separately with the foreign employer and with the workers; without them no one may be organized to go abroad. Article 25: "no deposit may be collected from workers under any name, nor may workers be asked to provide property as security." Article 26: within 10 working days of the contract's conclusion it is filed with the commerce department for the record. Article 29: where a worker's actual entitlements do not match the contract, the enterprise must assist with the claim; if due compensation is not received, the worker has the right to demand the enterprise bear liability for it. Article 31: where the security situation of the destination country or region cannot realistically protect personal safety, the enterprise may not organize workers to go there. Article 37: the commerce department maintains a bad-credit record and announcement system. Penalties follow in the later articles. Article 39: those without the qualification for overseas labor cooperation are investigated, punished, and shut down; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability is pursued. Article 40: organizing trips abroad under the name of business, tourism, or study, lending out one's qualification, or organizing work related to gambling or pornography leads to revocation of the business qualification certificate. Article 43: organizing trips abroad without a signed contract, or while concealing information or providing false information, draws a fine of 100,000 to 200,000 yuan. The main person in charge is also fined 20,000 to 50,000 yuan (nationwide)

- Sources: 国务院 (2012). 对外劳务合作管理条例（国务院令第 620 号，第五、七、八、九、十、十一、十二、十三、二十一、二十三、二十五、二十六、二十九、三十一、三十七、三十九、四十、四十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2012-06/11/content_1091.htm>
- Notes: This entry is about how the legitimate route works. The compliant sequence is this. The company first signs a written labor cooperation contract with the foreign employer, then signs a written service contract or employment contract with you. The contract must state the work content, term, pay, working hours, leave, social insurance, and personal safety risks. Within 10 working days after signing, the company files the contract with the commerce department for the record. The company must also arrange training for you in job skills, safety precautions, the local language, and local laws and customs. The company must buy personal accident insurance covering your work abroad. A company that will not show you the contract, asks for a deposit, or hurries you to leave first and complete paperwork later is exactly the kind this entry is meant to block. The overseas high-pay recruitment scam and the fraud-park side of it are in Section 21, entry 5; the safety advisories to check before departure and 12308 are in Section 21, not repeated here. The regulation sets no education or age threshold for the people going out to work; the threshold sits in the company's credentials and in whether the destination country grants a permit. The company can only sign contracts with enterprises or institutions approved by that country, not with private individuals abroad. The reserve fund is not money you can casually draw on; it is the backstop the competent authority taps when the company defaults. So if something really goes wrong, you still complain first to the commerce department; Article 20 gives you that right to complain. When more than 100 people are sent to the same country or region, accompanying management personnel must be arranged and reported to the embassy or consulate for the record. Projects with fewer people do not have that layer of protection, so keep your contract and transfer records yourself all the more carefully.

- Cost: Free. One annual reconciliation settlement each year, from March 1 to June 30 of the following year. When incoming transfers exceed the annual quota, you also need an employment contract and an income certificate ready. The hard part is remembering to file this tax yourself.
- In plain terms: Whether this money counts as domestic or foreign depends on where you do the work, not where the money is sent from. Working from inside China remotely for a foreign company is domestic-source income, and you pay personal income tax in China. The foreign company has no establishment in China, so nobody withholds it for you; you have to file this tax yourself. The money comes in under the "worker remuneration" channel.
- Benefit: Whether this money counts as domestic-source income is governed by Article 3 of the Implementation Regulations of the Individual Income Tax Law (State Council Decree No. 707, effective January 1, 2019). It says: "except as otherwise provided by the finance and tax authorities of the State Council, the following income counts as income derived from within China regardless of whether the place of payment is inside China: (1) income from providing services within China through employment, engagement, or performance of contracts." Article 6 of the same regulations defines two tax categories. One is wages and salaries: "wage and salary income means wages, salaries, bonuses, year-end raises, labor dividends, allowances, and subsidies received by an individual through employment or engagement, and other income connected with employment or engagement." The other is labor remuneration: "labor remuneration income means income an individual receives from performing services, including income from design, decoration, installation, drafting, laboratory testing, testing, medical care, legal work, accounting, consulting, lecturing, translation, manuscript review, calligraphy and painting, carving, film and video, sound recording, video recording, performance, acting, advertising, exhibitions, technical services, introduction services, brokerage services, agency services, and other services." Article 6 of the Individual Income Tax Law states how taxable income is computed: "for a resident individual's comprehensive income, the taxable income is the balance of each tax year's income after deducting the 60,000-yuan expense allowance, special deductions, special additional deductions, and other deductions determined by law." The same article also says "labor remuneration income, author's remuneration income, and royalty income count as income at the balance after deducting 20 percent of the income as expenses." That 20-percent deduction lists only these three items; wages and salaries are not among them. In other words, wages and salaries count toward income at full amount, so the two tax categories produce different tax. Article 9: "the person receiving the income is the taxpayer in individual income tax, and the unit or individual paying the income is the withholding agent." Article 10: "a taxpayer must file a tax return as required by law in any of the following circumstances: ... (2) taxable income is received with no withholding agent; ... (4) income from outside China is received." Article 11: "when a resident individual receives comprehensive income, individual income tax is computed on an annual basis ... where a reconciliation settlement is required, it must be completed between March 1 and June 30 of the year following the year the income was received." For bringing money in, see the Measures for the Administration of Individual Foreign Exchange (People's Bank of China Decree 〔2006〕 No. 3, effective February 1, 2007). Article 9: "individual settlement of foreign exchange and purchase of foreign exchange by individuals within China are managed under an annual quota. Within the annual quota, it is handled at a bank on the strength of the person's valid identity document; above the annual quota, under the current account it is handled at a bank on the strength of the valid identity document plus relevant supporting materials such as proof of the transaction amount." Article 7: "when handling individual foreign exchange business, banks and individuals must observe the relevant provisions of these measures, may not evade quota supervision through splitting or similar means, and may not use false commercial documents or vouchers to evade authenticity management." Article 2 of the Detailed Rules for Implementing the Measures for the Administration of Individual Foreign Exchange (Hui Fa 〔2007〕 No. 1): "individual settlement of foreign exchange and purchase of foreign exchange by individuals within China are managed under an annual quota. The annual quotas are the equivalent of 50,000 US dollars per person per year each." Article 10: "where an individual within China settles non-operating current-account foreign exchange above the annual quota, it is handled at a bank on the strength of the valid identity document and the following supporting materials: ... (7) worker remuneration: the employment contract and an income certificate" (nationwide)

First make one trip to the human resources and social security department for a qualification review, then complete the paperwork once each at the guarantee institution and the handling bank.
