# 31. What options are available after turning 18?

This section deals with money and time, while the option of avoiding military service also affects personal freedom. It serves as a roadmap of possible paths, not a step‑by‑step guide for any single one. First, we list the legal requirements for each path. Then we describe the details that become relevant only after you decide to pursue them: what benefits military service provides, other ways to enter the civil service besides the civil service exam, which college majors directly lead to government positions, how to later upgrade your education, what kind of employers to seek for overseas work, how to handle social insurance without a formal job, and whether there are government loans that cover part of the interest for those lacking startup capital.

Let us be clear: after turning 18 there are many more options than those covered here. We only include paths for which nationwide official documents specify eligibility criteria, requirements, and benefits. Paths such as joining state‑owned enterprises, working in factories, becoming a seafarer, pursuing freelance work, or continuing high school or university lack such uniform documentation. They are certainly possible, but without verifiable legal references we cannot describe their conditions in this book.

Choosing a path is entirely up to you. This section provides two things only: the official documents that set the eligibility rules, and the deadlines and qualifications that expire once you start down a particular route. How to weigh the decision between studying and working, and how to pick relevant skills, is explained in Section 23. Overtime pay, workplace injuries, and severance after joining a job are covered in Section 19. Starting or closing a business is discussed in Section 12. Studying abroad is the topic of Section 32, while safety nets for the unemployed are outlined in Section 7. None of those topics are repeated here.
### 1. Check the thresholds first: for most of the 12 paths, age and education are written into the documents

- Cost: No money. Take 10 minutes and check whether you can reach them.
- In plain terms: The thresholds of these 12 paths are, for the most part, written in the documents. Paid work requires age 16 or older. For the military, men must be 18 or older, and college graduates can be relaxed to 24. The civil service exam generally requires age 18 to 35 and a junior-college education or above. The self-study exam has no limit on age or prior education, but full-time students cannot register. A sole proprietorship only requires "the capacity to operate a business."
- Benefit: For the military, the Military Service Law says: "Male citizens who have reached 18 shall be called up for active service; those not called up that year may still be called up before they reach 22. The call-up age for graduates of ordinary higher-education institutions may be relaxed to 24, and for graduate students to 26." For the civil service, see Article 18 of the Regulations on the Recruitment of Civil Servants. The application conditions include "age 18 or older and under 35" and "a junior-college education or above." Both may be adjusted with approval from the civil service authority at provincial level or above. Article 19 lists 5 categories of people who may not apply. Examples are people who have received a criminal penalty for a crime, people expelled from public office, and people listed as targets of joint punishment for breach of trust. For firefighters, see the Measures for Recruiting Firefighters of the National Comprehensive Fire and Rescue Teams. Article 5: "age 18 or older and under 22" and "a high-school education or above." Article 6: "people with a junior-college education or above, and discharged soldiers of the People's Liberation Army and the People's Armed Police... the age may be relaxed to 24." For military civilian posts, see Article 9 of the Regulations on Civilian Personnel of the Chinese People's Liberation Army. The basic conditions include "having reached 18." Article 19 of those regulations says that the maximum age for a first recruitment into a lower-rank post is 35. A lower-rank post is an entry-level post. For public institutions, see Article 8 of the Regulations on Personnel Management of Public Institutions: "Newly hired staff of public institutions shall be openly recruited from society." For sole proprietors, see the Regulations on Promoting the Development of Individual Industrial and Commercial Households: "Citizens with the capacity to operate a business... who register according to law as individual industrial and commercial households are covered by these regulations." For the self-study exam, see the Interim Regulations on Higher Education Self-Study Examinations. Article 3: "Citizens of the People's Republic of China may take the higher education self-study examination under these regulations, without restriction of sex, age, ethnicity, race, or level of education already received." Article 20: "Current students of full-time schools of every type and level may not register." For government-funded teacher-training students, see Article 2 of the Implementation Measures for Government-Funded Education of Teacher-Training Students on a Bachelor's-to-Master's Track at Normal Universities Directly under the Ministry of Education. Recruitment is only at 6 normal universities under the ministry: Beijing Normal University, East China Normal University, Northeast Normal University, Central China Normal University, Shaanxi Normal University, and Southwest University. The bachelor's and graduate stages are designed as one track and assessed in stages. After the bachelor's, you continue into the graduate program. Tuition and housing during school are borne by central finance, and there is also a living stipend. Article 5: "Recruitment of government-funded teacher-training students uses early-batch admission," meaning they are admitted before the regular batch. For targeted medical students, see the Notice on Doing Well the Employment Placement and Contract Performance of Rural Order-Based Tuition-Free Medical Students. It says: "After admission, and before receiving the admission notice, an agreement must be signed with the training school and with the county health department... and the human resources and social security department of the place of directed employment." For working abroad, see the Regulations on the Administration of Foreign Labor Cooperation. Article 5: engaging in foreign labor cooperation requires approval from the commerce authority and an operating qualification. Article 7, paragraph 4: "Without obtaining the foreign labor cooperation qualification certificate according to law and completing registration, one may not engage in foreign labor cooperation." A company without this certificate that organizes people to work abroad is itself breaking the law. Article 8, paragraph 2: "No unit or individual may organize workers to work abroad in the name of business, tourism, study, or the like." Platform orders and flexible employment have no statutory education threshold. The basis for insuring yourself and for occupational injury coverage is items 11 and 12 of this section. For paid work, the Compulsory Education Law says: "Employers are prohibited from hiring school-age children and adolescents who should be receiving compulsory education." Article 2 of the Provisions on Prohibiting the Use of Child Labor states that no one under 16 may be hired. The penalties are in section 23, item 1 (under 16).

### 2. In the year you turn 18, military service registration has to be done before October (10) 31; conscript active duty is 2 years
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. Registration is filled in online once, and it takes a few minutes. Actually going means 2 years full time, and during those 2 years you cannot do other work at the same time.
- In plain terms: Military service registration is a legal duty. It is not an application to enlist, and registering does not mean you have to go. Men who turn 18 that year must finish registering online before October (10) 31. If you do not register, the county orders you to correct it by a deadline. If you still do not, it forces you to perform the military service duty and fines you. If you do enlist, conscript active duty is 2 years.
- Benefit: Registration is in the Provisions on Military Service Registration. They say: "The military service organ of the people's government of a county, autonomous county, city without districts, or municipal district shall, before October (10) 31 each year, organize initial military service registration for male citizens in its area who reach 18 before December (12) 31 of that year." The same provisions also say: "Initial military service registration is mainly done online, completed by the citizen on the military service registration network platform." What happens if you do not register is in Article 57, paragraph 1, of the Military Service Law. That paragraph covers "refusing or evading military service registration." The penalty is: "the county people's government orders correction by a deadline; if it is not corrected in time, the county people's government forces performance of the military service duty and imposes a fine." Article 15 of the Military Service Law says the people subject to initial military service registration are "male citizens who reach 18 before December (12) 31 each year." Article 20, paragraph 2, says: "According to the needs of the armed forces, female citizens may be called up for active service under the preceding paragraph." Article 15 of the Provisions on Military Service Registration says: "According to the needs of the armed forces... female citizens who meet the conditions may be organized for reserve registration." The Military Service Law also says: "The term of active service for a conscript is 2 years." "According to the needs of the armed forces and the person's own wish, citizens who have reached 17 but are not yet 18 may be called up for active service." A non-commissioned officer "who has served 12 years, or who meets other conditions set by the state, is assigned a job by the local people's government at county level or above in the place of placement." A non-commissioned officer "who has served 30 years, or who has reached 55, or who meets other conditions set by the state, is placed in retirement." Assigning a job means the government gives you a job. Retirement placement means you step down under retirement rules. The Regulations on Conscription say: "Priority goes to ensuring the call-up of graduates of ordinary higher-education institutions and of recruits with special requirements for political quality, physical condition, or professional skill." Those regulations also say: "Where the person to be called up is a full-time student or a graduating student of an ordinary higher-education institution, the call-up may be at the place of household registration before enrollment, or at the place of the school" (nationwide).

- Notes: Registration and call-up are different things, and the price differs by a long way. The string of penalties that includes a ban on leaving the country for 2 years punishes only people who back out after being called up. See item 3 (refusing military service). The people the law names for initial military service registration are male only. Women are called up according to the needs of the armed forces, and they may also be registered for the reserve according to those needs. Admission of soldiers to military academies, and turning a soldier with a strong record into an officer, are written in internal military rules and cannot be looked up from outside. The Military Service Law says only 2 things: active-duty officers are selected from "active-duty soldiers with an excellent record" and similar personnel, and military academies recruiting cadets are not limited by the call-up age. This book does not give score lines or quotas.

### 3. Refusing military service after enlistment: no travel abroad, no further education, and barred from civil service and state-owned enterprises for two years
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->

- Cost: There is no monetary cost. The price is having to decide well in advance whether you want to serve or not.
- In plain terms: If you sign up and then refuse to serve, and still do not correct it after being ordered to, the consequences come as a set. You cannot be hired as a civil servant, and a state-owned enterprise or a public institution cannot hire you either. For 2 years you may not leave the country, start further study, or return to school, and you are put on the list of subjects in serious breach of the duty to fulfill national defense obligations. So "sign up first and pull out if it does not work" is an expensive idea.
- Benefit: Article 57, Paragraph 1, Item 2 of the Military Service Law explicitly states that “any conscript who refuses or evades enlistment shall be subject to penalties.” These penalties include a requirement to correct the behavior within a set timeframe; if they still refuse, the local county-level government may compel them to serve and impose a fine. Paragraph 2 adds that such individuals must also be barred from civil service roles, from employment in state-owned enterprises and public institutions, and from any travel abroad or further education for two years. They are placed on a special list of those who have failed to meet defense obligations, leading to joint enforcement actions by various agencies. Article 58 extends these same penalties to active-duty personnel who deliberately evade service or desert. Those who are discharged, expelled from the military, or face criminal prosecution are subject to the same restrictions outlined in Paragraph 2 of Article 57, including a nationwide fine effective as of October 1, 2021.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2021). 兵役法（2021 年修订，第五十七、五十八条）. <http://www.mod.gov.cn/regulatory/2021-08/20/content_4892491.htm>（国防部刊登）
- Notes: This rule concerns personal freedom and employment eligibility rather than financial penalties. Only two categories of people are subject to these measures: those who refuse or evade enlistment after being called up, and those who are discharged, expelled, or criminally prosecuted after already joining the armed forces. Individuals who simply fail to register for conscription are not included; registration details are covered in Item 2. The penalties apply only when the person refuses to comply after being instructed to do so, as determined by the local county-level government. The exact amount of any fine is set locally and is not specified in this guide. This information is provided solely to illustrate the irreversible nature of the decision once enlistment is initiated; it does not serve as encouragement or discouragement regarding military service.

### 4. The 2 concrete benefits for college students who enlist: tuition covered at up to 20000 yuan a year, student status kept until 2 years after discharge, and a dedicated postgraduate plan
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. After discharge, submit the materials the school asks for. It takes 1 or 2 trips.
- In plain terms: Current students, graduating students, and newly admitted students who enlist have the tuition for their years in school paid by the state. For junior-college and undergraduate students the cap is 20000 yuan a year, and for graduate students 25000 yuan a year. If the tuition was already lower, only the amount actually paid is covered. Student status is kept during service, and you can return to school within 2 years after discharge. Postgraduate admissions have a separate plan that recruits only discharged college-student soldiers.
- Benefit: A 2024 notice from 4 departments, including the Ministry of Finance, says college students who serve get 3 benefits: tuition compensation, state repayment of the national student loan used for tuition, and a tuition waiver. Repayment means the state pays off that student loan for you. The standard "is adjusted accordingly to no more than 20000 yuan per person per year for junior-college and undergraduate students, and no more than 25000 yuan per person per year for graduate students." The new standard applies from the autumn semester of 2024. How much is paid follows this sentence: "Apply the higher of the tuition the student actually paid and the national student loan obtained (including the principal and all interest accrued before it is fully repaid)." The next sentence is: "The part above the standard is not compensated, repaid, or waived." So the money each year is the higher of the actual tuition and the student loan, then the lower of that amount and the cap. Tuition of 5000 yuan a year is compensated at 5000 yuan. The number of years follows the years of study, not the years of service. The 2019 measures on student financial aid funds say: "Where the prescribed length of study was already completed before enlistment, that length is the period of tuition compensation or of state repayment of the national student loan; where the remaining prescribed length is completed after returning to school following discharge, that length is the period of the tuition waiver." A graduating student who enlists is compensated for the tuition of the whole program. Student status is in the Law on the Protection of Veterans. It says: "Where a service member was already admitted to an ordinary higher-education institution before enlistment, or is a student already studying at an ordinary higher-education institution, the admission qualification or student status is kept during active service. Within 2 years after discharge, enrollment or return to school is allowed, and the student may transfer to another major at the same school under the relevant state rules." The law also says: "Higher-education institutions may, under the state's overall arrangement, recruit veterans through separately listed plans, separate admissions, and similar methods." Postgraduate study is in a 2026 notice from the General Office of the Ministry of Education. The plan is called «退役大学生士兵计划» (the plan for discharged college-student soldiers). The notice says: "It is issued separately within the total national master's enrollment plan and is used only for this purpose. It may not be shifted to the regular plan or to another special plan. Admitting institutions set... the preliminary-exam score requirement themselves (generally not higher than the preliminary-exam score requirement for regular-plan candidates in the same discipline at that institution)... Under otherwise equal conditions, candidates who served longer are admitted first." Separately listed and reserved means these places are set aside and can be used only to recruit discharged college-student soldiers. In 2026, 532 ordinary higher-education institutions carry this plan (nationwide).

### 5. Only six scenarios allow the government to assign jobs after discharge; those opting for self-employment must register within 30 days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost involved. Simply bring your discharge certificate and visit the local Civil Affairs Bureau for veterans.
- In plain terms: The notion that “the military guarantees job placement” no longer applies broadly. Currently, only six specific scenarios qualify for government job assignments. Most conscripts who serve two years do not fall into these categories. Those not eligible for government placement are considered self-employed, and they must register within 30 days of discharge. Failing to do so without valid reasons means they forfeit any entitlement to government assistance.
- Benefit: Article 25 of the Regulations on Placement of Veterans specifies six circumstances under which local governments must assign jobs. These include: (1) non-commissioned officers with at least 12 years of service; (2) personnel awarded medals or honorary titles during service; (3) those receiving third-class combat merits or higher; (4) individuals receiving first-class commendations; (5) those disabled at levels 5–8 due to combat; and (6) children of martyrs. The same regulations state that local governments must complete job placement within six months of receiving discharged personnel. Employers must sign medium- or long-term contracts lasting at least three years; for non-commissioned officers with ten-plus years of service, employers must offer open-ended contracts. During the placement period, veterans receive monthly living allowances equal to the local minimum wage. If placement fails due to reasons unrelated to the veteran, they receive 80% of the average wage of comparable workers until employment is secured. Exceptions include individuals expelled from the Communist Party of China or convicted of crimes. Article 51 clarifies that self-employed veterans must register within 30 days of discharge; otherwise, they lose eligibility for government support. (Nationwide, effective 1 September 2024.)

The 30-day registration rule applies solely to self-employed veterans; others must register “within the prescribed timeframe.” Upon registration, veterans receive a preferential treatment certificate, which serves as a prerequisite for subsequent benefits such as pensions, employment support, and educational grants.

### 6. The 3 accounts after discharge: a lump-sum discharge payment, service years counted as work seniority, and 20000 yuan a year off the tax if you work for yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. At discharge the military transfers out your social insurance (社保) relationship and the money. You then continue it at the social insurance agency in the place of placement (the window that handles social insurance).
- In plain terms: After discharge there are 3 accounts. The first is a lump-sum discharge payment for people who take the self-employment path. It is paid by years of active service, and it is increased if you were decorated. The second is service years counted as work seniority, with contribution years combined with the years before enlistment and after discharge. The third is tax: if you open a shop and run a business yourself, for 3 years you pay less tax each year within a cap of 20000 yuan, and the cap can be raised by at most 20%.

### 8. Teachers in the Special Post Program who complete three years of service, pass evaluations, and wish to stay should be promptly added to the official teaching staff

- Notes: The national-exam guide does not say whether signing a labor contract or paying social insurance counts as "being placed in a work unit." If you are not sure, call the consulting phone of the recruiting organ printed on the guide. Question 21 of the guide tells candidates to go straight to the recruiting organ. What is often said online, that paying social insurance means you no longer count as a fresh graduate, is the rule of some exams, not a rule for the whole country. The Fujian and Chongqing notices cited above are only examples. Provinces may change them every year. Follow the announcement of the year you take the exam. People in a grassroots service project such as «三支一扶» can also apply for fresh-graduate posts within 2 years after the term ends. See item 7 of this section (grassroots service projects). The same application guide also states that this exam does not designate a prep book, and does not run or entrust any institution or person to run a prep class. Article 4 of the Regulations on the Recruitment of Civil Servants states that civil servants are recruited by "open examination, strict assessment, equal competition, and admission of the better candidates." So if someone takes money and promises an "internal slot" or "a guaranteed staff post," treat it as a scam and report it. See section 8, item 3 (hard rules on fraud). The national-exam wording here follows the 2027 announcement as of October (10) 2026. In later years, also read that year's announcement.
