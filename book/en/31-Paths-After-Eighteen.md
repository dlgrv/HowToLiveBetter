# 31. What Roads Are Open After Eighteen

The yardstick is money and time; the road of refusing military service also touches personal freedom. This section is a route map, not a manual for any single road: it first lays the legal thresholds of the various roads side by side, then takes each road in turn and sets out the things you only have use for once you know them — what serving as a conscript gets you, which doors into the state system exist besides the civil-service exam, whether a credential can still be made up later, and how those who never join a work unit handle social insurance.

Which road to take is your own affair; this section offers only two things: which document states each threshold, and, once you are on a road, which deadlines and eligibility rules lapse for good if missed. How to weigh studying against working is covered in Section 23; overtime pay, work injury, and severance once you are inside a work unit are in Section 19; opening your own shop or company is in Section 12; the safety net after losing a job is in Section 7. None of that is repeated here.
### 1. Check the thresholds first: the age and education requirements of the eight roads are fixed in writing
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; ten minutes to go through the list and see which roads you can reach.
- In plain terms: You must be at least 16 to take a job. Military service: men enter the conscription pool at 18 and can still be conscripted up to 22; the ceiling is relaxed to 24 for university graduates and 26 for postgraduates. Civil-service exam: ages 18 to 35, junior college or above; anyone punished for a crime, dismissed from public office, or listed for joint disciplinary action as a dishonest party may not apply. Firefighters: 18 to 22, high school or above, relaxed to 24 for those with a junior-college degree or higher and for veterans. Military civilian personnel: at least 18; for ordinary-work and lower-rank posts the maximum age at first hiring is 35. Public institutions: new hires must in principle come through open, society-wide recruitment. Individual businesses (getihu): the only requirement is being "a citizen with the capacity to operate"; education is not asked about. The self-taught higher-education exam: no restriction by sex, age, ethnicity, race, or prior education, but students enrolled full-time in school may not register.
- Benefit: The military-service law: "Male citizens who have reached the age of eighteen shall be conscripted for active service; those not conscripted in that year may still be conscripted for active service before reaching twenty-two. The conscription age may be relaxed to twenty-four for graduates of regular institutions of higher education and to twenty-six for postgraduates." The civil-service recruitment rules, Article 18, sets the application conditions including "aged eighteen or above and under thirty-five" and "education at the junior-college level or above", with adjustments allowed on approval by civil-service authorities at the provincial level or above; Article 19 lists five categories of people who may not apply. The firefighter recruitment measures for the national comprehensive fire and rescue force, Article 5: "aged 18 or above and under 22" and "senior high school education or above"; Article 6: "persons with a junior-college degree or above, and former service members of the PLA and the People's Armed Police... may have the age relaxed to 24". The regulations on civilian personnel of the Chinese People's Liberation Army, Article 9, lists among the basic conditions "having reached the age of 18"; Article 19 sets 35 as the maximum age for first-time recruitment to lower-rank posts. The regulations on personnel management of public institutions, Article 8: "When a public institution newly employs staff, it shall conduct open recruitment open to society". The regulation on promoting the development of individual businesses: "citizens with the capacity to operate... who register as individual businesses in accordance with the law are governed by this regulation". The interim regulations on self-taught higher-education examinations, Article 3: "Citizens of the People's Republic of China, without restriction by sex, age, ethnicity, race, or prior level of education, may take the self-taught higher-education examinations under these regulations"; Article 20: "Students enrolled in full-time schools of all types and levels may not register". The compulsory-education law: "Employers are prohibited from hiring school-age children or adolescents who are subject to compulsory education" (nationwide).

### 2. Register for military service before October 31 of the year you turn 18; a conscript's active-service term is two years
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; online registration takes a few minutes, but actually serving costs two years full-time.
- In plain terms: Military-service registration is a legal duty, not signing up to enlist. Males who turn 18 by December 31 of that year must complete initial registration by October 31 of that year, organized by the county-level military-service authority — it is done online, which you complete yourself on the platform. If you fail to register, the county-level government orders correction within a time limit; if the deadline passes without correction, you are forced to perform your service duty and fined. If you do go, a conscript's active-service term is exactly two years, and if you volunteer you can enlist from age 17. To keep serving after the two years, you transfer to the non-commissioned corps; an NCO needs 12 years of service to qualify for a state-arranged job, and only after 30 years of service or at age 55 does one get retirement placement. Conscription gives priority to university graduates; current students and fresh graduates may register either at their household-registration place or where their school is.
- Benefit: The military-service registration work rules: "The military-service authorities of the people's governments of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, and municipal districts shall, before October 31 each year, organize initial military-service registration for male citizens within their jurisdiction who turn 18 before December 31 of that year"; "initial military-service registration is conducted mainly online, completed by the citizen independently through the military-service registration network platform". Military-service law, Article 57, paragraph 1: those who "refuse or evade military-service registration" face "an order from the county-level people's government to correct within a time limit; if the deadline passes without correction, the county-level people's government shall compel performance of the service duty and impose a fine"; "the term of active service for conscripts is two years"; "where the military needs it and the person volunteers, citizens who have reached seventeen but not eighteen may be conscripted for active service"; non-commissioned officers who "have served twelve years on active duty or meet other conditions set by the state shall be given jobs by the local people's government at or above the county level of the placement area", and those who "have served thirty years or reached fifty-five years of age or meet other conditions set by the state receive retirement placement". The conscription work regulations: "Priority is given to graduates of regular institutions of higher education and to the recruitment of personnel with special political, physical, or professional-skill requirements"; "citizens applying for service who are full-time current students or fresh graduates of regular institutions of higher education may answer the call either at their place of household registration before enrollment or where their school is located" (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅、中央军委办公厅 (2025). 兵役登记工作规定（国办发〔2025〕41 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202512/content_7051363.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2021). 兵役法（2021 年修订，第二十、二十五、二十六、五十四、五十七条）. <http://www.mod.gov.cn/regulatory/2021-08/20/content_4892491.htm>（国防部刊登）；国务院、中央军委 (2023). 征兵工作条例（国务院、中央军委令第 759 号，第五条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2023/content_5753313.htm>
- Notes: Registration and actually being drafted are two different things, with a big gap in cost. The string of penalties within two years — including the exit ban — applies only to those who renege after being drafted; see item 3. Female citizens are not in the mandatory initial-registration scope and are conscripted according to the military's needs. The conditions for soldiers to sit the military-academy exam and for outstanding soldiers to be commissioned as officers are written in internal military regulations, not findable through public channels; the military-service law only says active-duty officers are selected from "active-duty soldiers with outstanding performance" and similar personnel, and that military academies' student recruitment is not bound by the conscription age limits — this book gives no specific score lines or quotas.

### 3. Refusing military service after being drafted: for two years no leaving the country, no resuming studies, and no entry into the civil service or state enterprises
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; the price is deciding clearly whether to go before you sign up.
- In plain terms: If, after being drafted, you refuse or dodge active service, the military-service law attaches a chain of joint disciplinary measures: you may not be hired as a civil servant or as personnel managed under the civil-service law, may not be recruited or employed by state-owned enterprises or public institutions, may not leave the country or resume or continue your studies for two years, and go onto the list of seriously dishonest subjects in performing national-defense obligations. This one provision shuts almost every road into the state system at once, and also shuts off going abroad and going back to school. So "sign up first, and back out later if it doesn't work out" is a very expensive idea.
- Benefit: Military-service law, Article 57, paragraph 1, item 2, covers "citizens drafted for service who refuse or evade conscription into active service": "the county-level people's government shall order correction within a time limit; if the deadline passes without correction, the county-level people's government shall compel performance of the service duty and impose a fine". Paragraph 2: "Whoever commits the act in item 2 of the preceding paragraph and refuses to correct it may not be hired as a civil servant or as staff managed with reference to the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China, may not be recruited or employed as staff of state-owned enterprises or public institutions, may not leave the country or resume or continue studies for two years, and is placed on the list of seriously dishonest subjects in performing national-defense obligations for joint disciplinary action." Article 58 attaches the same set of penalties to those already enlisted: a service member who refuses to perform duties or deserts the force with the aim of evading military service, and is struck off the rolls, expelled from the military, or held criminally liable, is punished under Article 57, paragraph 2, with a fine added in the case of being struck off (nationwide, effective October 1, 2021).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 兵役法（2021 年修订，第五十七、五十八条）. <http://www.mod.gov.cn/regulatory/2021-08/20/content_4892491.htm>（国防部刊登）
- Notes: The yardstick here is personal freedom and employment eligibility, not money. Read the scope precisely: joint disciplinary action applies only to two cases — "a drafted citizen refusing or evading conscription into active service" and, after enlistment, being struck off, expelled from the military, or prosecuted criminally; merely failing to complete military-service registration is not among them (see item 2). The trigger is "refusal to correct"; the determination and penalty are made by the county-level people's government, the fine amount is set locally, and this book does not state it. This item neither urges you to go nor not to go; it says the decision stops being reversible from the moment you sign up.

### 4. The two most tangible benefits for students who serve: tuition subsidies up to 20,000 yuan per year, plus two years of preserved enrollment and a dedicated track for postgraduate exams
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; after discharge, submit the paperwork as your school requires — one or two trips.
- In plain terms: For current students, fresh graduates, and newly admitted students who answer the call, tuition during service is compensated by the state; any state student loan taken out for tuition is repaid on your behalf; and after you resume or begin your studies, tuition is waived outright — the standard is up to 20,000 yuan per person per year for undergraduate and vocational students, up to 25,000 for postgraduates, so two years is up to 40,000 or 50,000. These are ceilings, computed on whichever is higher between the tuition you actually paid and the loan you actually received; anything above is not covered. The 8,000-yuan and 16,000-yuan figures still circulating online are old standards. On the enrollment side: your admission qualification or student status is preserved during service, you may enroll or return within two years of discharge, and you may transfer to another major at the same school under the rules. The postgraduate exam has a separate dedicated track — in 2026 carried by 532 universities, with each admitting institution setting its own exam score requirement, generally no higher than its ordinary-plan line.
- Benefit: The 2024 notice from the Ministry of Finance and three other departments: the tuition-compensation standard for students in higher education who perform military service, the repayment of state student loans taken out for tuition, and the tuition-waiver standard are "adjusted accordingly to no more than 20,000 yuan per undergraduate or vocational student per year and no more than 25,000 yuan per postgraduate per year", effective from the fall semester of 2024; the compensation is computed "on the higher of the tuition the student actually paid or the state student loan received (including principal and all interest accrued before repayment)", and "any portion above the standard is not compensated, repaid, or waived". The veterans support law: "A person admitted to a regular institution of higher education before enlisting, or studying at one, retains the admission qualification or student status during active service and may enroll or resume studies within two years of discharge, and may transfer to another major at the same school under relevant state provisions"; "institutions of higher education, under overall state arrangements, may recruit veterans through separately listed plans, separate admissions, and other means". The 2026 notice of the Ministry of Education's general office: the "retired university-soldier plan" is "issued as a separately listed quota within the overall national postgraduate enrollment plan and used exclusively for this purpose, and may not be converted to ordinary plans or other special plans. Admitting institutions independently set... the exam score requirement (generally no higher than the requirement for ordinary-plan candidates in the corresponding discipline) ... where candidates are otherwise equal, those with longer service are admitted first"; in 2026 carried by 532 regular institutions of higher education (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 财政部、教育部、中国人民银行、金融监管总局 (2024). 关于调整完善国家助学贷款有关政策的通知（财教〔2024〕188 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202411/content_6984812.htm>；财政部等五部门 (2019). 学生资助资金管理办法（财科教〔2019〕19 号，第五条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2019/content_5421553.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2020). 退役军人保障法（第三十四、三十五条）. <http://www.mod.gov.cn/gfbw/fgwx/flfg/16048597.html>（国防部刊登）；教育部办公厅 (2025). 关于做好 2026 年退役大学生士兵专项硕士研究生招生计划招生工作的通知（教学厅〔2025〕8 号）. <http://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A15/moe_778/s3261/202512/t20251211_1423174.html>

### 5. Only six categories of veterans get a government-arranged job; those on self-employment must report within 30 days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one trip to the veterans affairs department of the placement area with your discharge papers.
- In plain terms: "The army guarantees you a job" has long ceased to be the general rule. The current regulation lists only six situations in which the government arranges a job: NCOs with 12 years of active service; those personally awarded a medal or an honorary title; those personally awarded a third-class combat merit or a second-class merit or above; those receiving a first-class commendation; those disabled in combat and rated grade 5 to 8; and children of martyrs. A conscript serves two years and is discharged, so the vast majority fall into the self-employment bracket. Those who qualify for placement must have it carried out by the placement-area government within 6 months of acceptance; the employer signs a contract of no less than 3 years, and an open-ended labor contract for anyone with 10 or more years of military service; while awaiting placement a living allowance is paid at the local monthly minimum wage. The self-employment bracket carries a hard deadline: you must report within 30 days from the date your discharge is approved; if you exceed 30 days without justifiable reason, you are deemed to have given up your placement benefits — resting at home for a while first could rest away the whole package.
- Benefit: The regulation on the placement of veterans, Article 25, lists the six situations in which the placement-area people's government arranges work: "(1) NCOs who have served 12 years on active duty; (2) those who during active service were personally awarded a medal or an honorary title; (3) those who during active service were personally awarded a third-class combat merit or a second-class merit or above; (4) those who during active service personally received a first-class commendation; (5) those disabled in combat and assessed at disability grade 5 to 8; (6) children of martyrs"; "the people's government of the placement area shall complete the task of arranging jobs for discharged NCOs and conscripts within 6 months of accepting them"; employers "shall sign with them, as required, a medium- or long-term employment contract or labor contract of no less than 3 years. Among these, when an enterprise accepts a discharged NCO with 10 or more years of military service, it shall sign an open-ended labor contract"; while awaiting job placement a "living allowance is paid month by month at the local monthly minimum wage standard", and where placement to a post is not made on time for reasons not attributable to the person, "living expenses are paid month by month at no less than 80 percent of the average wage of personnel of equal standing in the unit until placement to a post"; situations in which placement by job arrangement is not allowed include expulsion from the Communist Party of China and having received criminal punishment. Article 51: "Discharged NCOs and conscripts who are self-employed shall report to the competent veterans affairs department of the people's government of the placement area within 30 days from the date their discharge from active service is approved. Failure to report within the prescribed time for more than 30 days without justifiable reason is deemed a waiver of placement benefits." (nationwide, effective September 1, 2024)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院、中央军委 (2024). 退役军人安置条例（国务院、中央军委令第 787 号，2024 年 7 月 29 日公布、9 月 1 日施行；第二十五、三十一、三十二、三十五、五十一、七十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11526/202408/content_6969192.html>；全国人大常委会 (2020). 退役军人保障法（第十三、十四、十五条）. <http://www.mod.gov.cn/gfbw/fgwx/flfg/16048597.html>（国防部刊登）
- Notes: On the same day this regulation took effect, the 2011 regulation on the placement of retired soldiers (State Council and Central Military Commission Decree No. 608) was repealed; many online guides still cite the old one, and the conditions have been reworded (the old version read "awarded a second-class merit or above in peacetime or a third-class merit or above in wartime"). The 30-day deadline governs only the self-employment bracket; other placement methods require reporting "within the prescribed time". At reporting you are issued the veterans preferential-treatment card, from which the later discharge allowance, employment and entrepreneurship support, and education subsidies all connect.

### 6. Three accounts after discharge: the one-time discharge allowance, military service counted as seniority, and a 20,000-yuan-per-year tax deduction for going it alone
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; at discharge the military transfers your insurance records and funds, and you complete the transfer at the social-insurance agency of the placement area.
- In plain terms: The first account is the one-time discharge allowance for the self-employed, centrally financed and paid by years of active service — 25 percent extra for a medal or honorary title, then 20, 15, 10, and 5 percent down the scale; the regulation itself does not state amounts, leaving them to the veterans affairs department together with the finance ministry, so for the exact figure you can only ask your local veterans affairs bureau. The second account is that these two years are not a social-insurance gap: military service counts as working years, and the contribution years for pension, medical, and unemployment insurance are combined with those before enlistment and after discharge; the medical portion counts as deemed contribution years, and if you have not found work right away you can still claim unemployment insurance benefits. The third account is the tax on going it alone: for 3 years from the month you register as an individual business, taxes such as VAT are deducted in sequence up to 20,000 yuan per household per year, with each province allowed to raise the cap by up to 20 percent; if an enterprise hires you and signs a contract of 1 year or more while paying social insurance as required, it deducts a fixed 6,000 yuan per person per year for 3 years, with provinces allowed up to 50 percent — the policy runs until December 31, 2027.
- Benefit: The regulation on the placement of veterans: "Discharged NCOs and conscripts who are self-employed are paid a one-time discharge allowance based on their years of active service", "arranged as a special item by central finance, with the specific standard determined... and adjusted in due course by the state council veterans affairs department and the political work department of the Central Military Commission together with the state council finance department"; the uplift percentages are 25 percent for a medal or honorary title, 20 percent for a first-class combat merit or first-class commendation, 15 percent for a second-class combat merit, first-class merit, or second-class commendation, 10 percent for a third-class combat merit or second-class merit, and 5 percent for a fourth-class combat merit or third-class merit; "veterans who are not employed in time may claim unemployment insurance benefits under the law from the human resources and social security department of their place of household registration, with years of active service counted as years of insured contributions, except veterans placed by retirement or support". The veterans support law: "Years of active service of veterans count as working years and, after discharge, are accumulated together with years worked at their employer"; years of active service are "combined as provided by law" with the pension, medical, and unemployment contribution years before enlistment and after discharge; "the active-service experience of discharged NCOs and conscripts is deemed grassroots work experience". The military insurance law: "Years of a service member's active duty are deemed years of contribution to employee basic medical insurance and are combined with contribution years under employee basic medical insurance before enlistment and after discharge from active service". Announcement No. 14 (2023) of the Ministry of Finance and two other departments: self-employed retired soldiers operating an individual business "within 3 years (36 months, the same below) deduct in sequence, up to 20,000 yuan per household per year, the VAT, urban maintenance and construction tax, education surcharge, local education surcharge, and individual income tax actually payable for the year. The cap may be raised by up to 20 percent"; for enterprises that hire them "the fixed standard is 6,000 yuan per person per year, which may be raised by up to 50 percent", running from January 1, 2023 to December 31, 2027, and those who have not reached 3 years by expiry may continue to benefit until the term ends (nationwide).

- Notes: TODO (to verify: the current specific amount of the one-time discharge allowance) — the regulation makes clear the amount is set separately by the competent department, and the original text of the current standard was not obtained through public channels this round, so no figure is given here, and do not trust the specific amounts in online guides. While a conscript serves, the family separately receives a family preferential-payment; the military-service law only says "the standard is set by the local people's government with a fixed subsidy from central finance" — ask the county-level people's government of the place that approved the enlistment. - 收益：公费师范生看《教育部直属师范大学本研衔接师范生公费教育实施办法》（2024 年，2018 年办法同时废止）。第二条：由中央财政承担学生在校期间学费、住宿费并给予生活费补助。第三条：重点为中西部地区省会城市之外的地（市、州、盟）及以下培养。它还写着「不得定向到直辖市、计划单列市或省会城市主城区任教（五个自治区、陆地边境省份、海南省、贵州省、青海省除外）」。第九条：毕业后一般回生源所在省份定向地（市、州、盟）中小学任教，「承诺从事中小学教育工作 6 年以上」。到城镇学校工作的，「应到农村义务教育学校任教服务至少 1 年」。第十三条：没履约或者履约期限不够的，按协议承担责任。后果之一是「违约行为纳入信用记录，并及时与全国信用信息共享平台共享，违约相关材料归入本人人事档案」。另一句是「履约任务完成前不得到定向地（市、州、盟）之外从教，不得被招录（聘用）为其他公务人员。完成履约任务后即修复相关信用」。也就是协议约定的服务期没干满之前，别的公职岗位报不了，干满了信用记录才恢复。第六条：录取后经考察不适合从教的，入学 1 年内退还已享受的公费教育费用，并调整到非师范专业。第十四条：没在规定学习年限内取得教育硕士学历学位证书和教师资格证书的，按违约处理，退费并缴纳违约金。第二十一条：各地在现有事业编制总量内，解决公费师范生到中小学任教所需编制。农村订单定向免费医学生看另一份通知。录取后、获得入学通知书前，须与培养学校和定向就业所在地县级卫生健康、人社部门签署协议。协议里要「承诺毕业后到定向农村基层医疗卫生机构服务 6 年」。履约就是按协议去指定的地方干满。履约报到就业后，须参加 3 年全科专业住院医师规范化培训，简称规培。规培就是毕业后在医院按统一标准接着练本事的阶段。培训期间原人事、工资关系不变。违约记录归入个人人事档案，并作为医疗卫生机构等行政事业单位公开招录（招聘）、住院医师规范化培训招收、研究生招生录取的诚信评价依据。恶意违约的人，通过「信用中国」网站全国公示，并实行失信联合惩戒。违约的培训年限不计入服务期。没按协议退还费用和违约金的，由县级卫生健康行政部门通过法律途径追缴（全国）
- 证据等级：A
- 备注：2024 年那份办法把公费师范生改成了「本研衔接」，也就是本科接着读教育硕士。2018 年的老办法已经废止，网上还在传的「本科毕业直接任教」是旧说法。办法发布前已经签了协议的，按原协议走。「有编制」不等于「进去就不用管」：编制要在现有总量里腾，具体岗位由地市组织用人学校专门招聘，你和学校互相挑。定向医学生服务期满想走是可以的，文件写明「服务期满，对自主择业的应予同意」。已经违约的也有把违约记录消掉的办法，代价是按原协议、或者改到省内其他农村基层机构再干满至少 6 年，已交的费用和违约金不退。医学生还要算上 3 年规培的时间，整个周期比师范长。各省还有自己的公费师范生和定向医学生计划，条件由省里定，填志愿前看当年本省的招生文件。

### 14. 想出国打工，先查这家公司有没有对外劳务合作经营资格：它不得向你收押金
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。出发前花十几分钟核一下公司资质，再把两份合同看完。
- 说人话：出国打工这件事，法律管的不是你，是组织你出去的那家公司。只有拿到对外劳务合作经营资格证书、又办了登记的企业才能干这个。听到「先办旅游签过去再转工签」，这句话本身就说明对方在违法。公司也不得以任何名目向你收押金，或者要你拿财产做担保。
- 收益：《对外劳务合作管理条例》第五条：从事对外劳务合作应当经省级或者设区的市级商务主管部门批准取得经营资格。这个经营资格就是那张对外劳务合作经营资格证书，没有它就不能组织人出国干活。第七条：经营资格证书名单报国务院商务主管部门，并通报中国驻外使馆、领馆。这一条还写着「未依法取得对外劳务合作经营资格证书并办理登记，不得从事对外劳务合作」。第八条：「任何单位和个人不得以商务、旅游、留学等名义组织劳务人员赴国外工作」。第九条：企业应当自登记之日起 5 个工作日内在指定银行缴存「不低于 300 万元人民币的对外劳务合作风险处置备用金」，缴存名单向社会公布。第十条：备用金用于支付企业拒绝承担或者无力承担的应退服务费、劳动报酬和依法赔偿的损失。它还用于「因发生突发事件，劳务人员回国或者接受紧急救助所需费用」。第十一条：不得组织劳务人员赴国外从事与赌博、色情活动相关的工作。第十二条：没安排培训的不得组织出国。第十三条：应当为劳务人员购买在国外工作期间的人身意外伤害保险。第二十一、二十三条：须与国外雇主、与劳务人员分别订立书面合同，没订立的不得组织出国。第二十五条：「不得以任何名目向劳务人员收取押金或者要求劳务人员提供财产担保」。第二十六条：合同订立之日起 10 个工作日内报商务主管部门备案。第二十九条：劳务人员实际权益与合同不符的，企业应当协助索赔；没得到应有赔偿的，有权要求企业承担赔偿责任。第三十一条：有关国家或者地区安全状况难以保障人身安全的，企业不得组织劳务人员前往。第三十七条：商务主管部门建立不良信用记录和公告制度。罚则在后面几条。第三十九条：没资格从事对外劳务合作的，查处取缔，构成犯罪的追究刑事责任。第四十条：以商务、旅游、留学名义组织出国、出借资格、组织从事赌博色情相关工作的，吊销经营资格证书。第四十三条：没签合同、或者隐瞒信息、提供虚假信息组织出国的，罚 10 万元以上 20 万元以下。还要对主要负责人罚 2 万元以上 5 万元以下（全国）
- 证据等级：A
- 备注：这条讲的是正规路子怎么走。合规的流程是这样。公司先和国外雇主签书面劳务合作合同，再和你签书面服务合同或者劳动合同。合同要写明工作内容、期限、报酬、工时、休假、社保和人身安全风险。签完 10 个工作日内，公司要报商务主管部门备案。公司还必须安排你接受技能、安全防范、外语和当地法律风俗的培训。公司必须给你买境外工作期间的人身意外伤害保险。对方不让看合同、要押金、催你先走后补手续，就是这条要拦的那种公司。境外高薪招聘骗局、电诈园区那一面在第 21 节第 5 条，出发前要查的安全提醒和 12308 在第 21 节，不重复。条例没给出去干活的人设学历或年龄门槛，门槛在公司的资质上，也在干活所在国给不给许可上。公司只能跟那个国家批准过的企业或机构签合同，不能跟国外的个人签。备用金不是你能随便取的钱，是公司赖账时由主管部门动用的兜底。所以真出事还得先向商务主管部门投诉，第二十条给了你这个投诉权。往同一个国家或地区派超过 100 人的，要安排随行的管理人员并报使领馆备案。人少的项目没有这层保障，更要自己留好合同和转账记录。

- Sources: 国务院 (2012). 对外劳务合作管理条例（国务院令第 620 号，第五、七、八、九、十、十一、十二、十三、二十一、二十三、二十五、二十六、二十九、三十一、三十七、三十九、四十、四十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2012-06/11/content_1091.htm>

### 15. 在家给境外公司远程干活，这笔钱在税法上算境内所得：个税自己报，收汇走「职工报酬」
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。每年办一次汇算清缴，时间是次年 3 月 1 日到 6 月 30 日。收汇超过年度总额的时候，还要准备一份雇佣合同和收入证明。难在这笔税得你自己记着去报。
- 说人话：这笔钱算境内还是境外，看的是你在哪儿干活，不是钱从哪儿打进来。人在国内远程给境外公司干活，算境内所得，要在境内交个税。境外公司在中国没有机构，没人替你代扣，这笔税得你自己去报。钱汇进来走「职工报酬」这条路。
- 收益：这笔钱算不算境内所得，看《个人所得税法实施条例》（国务院令第 707 号，2019 年 1 月 1 日起施行）第三条。它写着：「除国务院财政、税务主管部门另有规定外，下列所得，不论支付地点是否在中国境内，均为来源于中国境内的所得：（一）因任职、受雇、履约等在中国境内提供劳务取得的所得」。同一部条例第六条给两个税目下了定义。一个是工资薪金：「工资、薪金所得，是指个人因任职或者受雇取得的工资、薪金、奖金、年终加薪、劳动分红、津贴、补贴以及与任职或者受雇有关的其他所得」。另一个是劳务报酬：「劳务报酬所得，是指个人从事劳务取得的所得，包括从事设计、装潢、安装、制图、化验、测试、医疗、法律、会计、咨询、讲学、翻译、审稿、书画、雕刻、影视、录音、录像、演出、表演、广告、展览、技术服务、介绍服务、经纪服务、代办服务以及其他劳务取得的所得」。《个人所得税法》第六条写怎么算应纳税所得额：「居民个人的综合所得，以每一纳税年度的收入额减除费用六万元以及专项扣除、专项附加扣除和依法确定的其他扣除后的余额，为应纳税所得额」。同一条还写着「劳务报酬所得、稿酬所得、特许权使用费所得以收入减除百分之二十的费用后的余额为收入额」。减 20% 这一句只列了这三项，工资薪金不在其内。也就是说，工资薪金按全额计入收入额，两个税目算出来的税不同。第九条：「个人所得税以所得人为纳税人，以支付所得的单位或者个人为扣缴义务人」。第十条：「有下列情形之一的，纳税人应当依法办理纳税申报：……（二）取得应税所得没有扣缴义务人；……（四）取得境外所得」。第十一条：「居民个人取得综合所得，按年计算个人所得税……需要办理汇算清缴的，应当在取得所得的次年三月一日至六月三十日内办理汇算清缴」。钱汇进来看《个人外汇管理办法》（中国人民银行令〔2006〕第 3 号，2007 年 2 月 1 日起施行）。第九条：「对个人结汇和境内个人购汇实行年度总额管理。年度总额内的，凭本人有效身份证件在银行办理；超过年度总额的，经常项目项下凭本人有效身份证件和有交易额的相关证明等材料在银行办理」。第七条：「银行和个人在办理个人外汇业务时，应当遵守本办法的相关规定，不得以分拆等方式逃避限额监管，也不得使用虚假商业单据或者凭证逃避真实性管理」。《个人外汇管理办法实施细则》（汇发〔2007〕1 号）第二条：「对个人结汇和境内个人购汇实行年度总额管理。年度总额分别为每人每年等值 5 万美元」。第十条：「境内个人经常项目项下非经营性结汇超过年度总额的，凭本人有效身份证件及以下证明材料在银行办理：……（七）职工报酬：雇佣合同及收入证明」（全国）

### 16. 不进单位自己干，起步钱先看创业担保贷款：个人最高 30 万、财政贴一半利息
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：咨询不花钱。先跑一次人社部门做资格审核，再到担保机构和经办银行各办一次手续。经办银行就是放这笔贷款的那家银行。难在借来的钱终究要还。
- 说人话：如果一定要借钱起步，先看这笔有财政贴息的。个人最高能借 30 万元，最长 3 年，利息由财政替你出一半。有个硬条件常被忽略：申请时你和配偶名下不能有别的贷款，只有助学贷款、脱贫人口小额信贷、房贷、车贷和 5 万元以下的小额消费贷不算在内。
- 收益：个人申请条件写在《普惠金融发展专项资金管理办法》（财金〔2023〕75 号）第六条。第一个条件是要属于重点就业群体。文件列的是城镇登记失业人员、就业困难人员（含残疾人）、退役军人、刑满释放人员。还有高校毕业生（含大学生村官和留学回国学生）、化解过剩产能企业职工和失业人员。还有返乡创业农民工、网络商户、脱贫人口、农村自主创业农民等。第二个条件是名下不能有别的贷款：「除助学贷款、脱贫人口小额信贷、住房贷款、购车贷款、5 万元以下小额消费贷款（含信用卡消费）以外，申请人提交创业担保贷款申请时，本人及其配偶无其他贷款」。第九条：「对符合条件的创业担保贷款，财政部门给予贷款实际利率 50% 的财政贴息。对展期、逾期的创业担保贷款，财政部门原则上不予贴息」。贴息就是利息由财政替你出一半。展期是到期后再往后延，逾期是过期没还，这两种情况财政不再替你出。第十条：个人申请的 10 万元以下创业担保贷款原则上取消反担保，也就是不必再另外找人、或者拿财产给担保机构做担保。第十一条：纳入奖补支持范围的个人创业担保贷款「额度不超过 30 万元，贷款期限不超过 3 年」。合伙创业可适当提高，最高不超过个人额度上限之和的 110%。奖补支持的贷款利率上限分三档：东部地区为 LPR+50BPs，中西部地区 LPR+150BPs，脱贫地区 LPR+250BPs。LPR 是银行贷款市场报价利率，BPs 是利率的计量单位，加得越多利息越高。「还款积极、带动就业能力强、创业项目好」的，到期后可继续申请，累计次数不超过 3 次。第八条：申请人先向当地人力资源社会保障部门申请资格审核，再向担保机构和经办银行提交申请。第十二条：经省级人民政府同意，各地可扩大支持范围、提高额度和贴息比例（全国）
- 证据等级：A
- 备注：财政替你出一半利息，不改变「借的钱要还」这件事。本书在第 12 节第 1 条的立场没变：创业只拿亏得起的钱，不动家底，不给公司贷款签个人担保，配偶更不要跟着签。这条只是说，如果你已经决定要借，这种政策贷款是利息最低的那一档，别去碰消费贷和民间借贷。额度和适用范围各省可以往上调，实际能批多少，以当地人社部门当年的说法为准。2020 年疫情期间那份「个人最高 20 万」的通知是临时文件，现在看这份办法。开店是办个体户还是开公司、许可证、报税和退场，见第 12 节。失业期间的培训补贴、社保补贴和就业见习，见第 7 节第 13 条（职业培训补贴）。
