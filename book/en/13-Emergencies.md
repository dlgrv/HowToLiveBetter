# 13. Emergencies: What to Do First

This section answers only one thing: in a sudden emergency, what to do first, what to do next, and what not to do. For first-aid and wilderness items the yardstick is specific causes of death and survival rates; for threats and scams the yardstick is money and personal freedom, and the two kinds of numbers are never converted into each other. Items are ranked from high to low by "how common it is × the life-or-death difference between doing it right and doing it wrong." Under the book-wide beneficiary yardstick, the through-line of the first-aid items is yourself, your spouse, and your immediate family — 79.2% of cardiac arrests happen at home, so the people you are most likely to save are your own household; friends and colleagues come next; for strangers the benefit is not zero, but it ranks lowest and also carries the risk of being extorted through false accusation or getting entangled in a legal case, so those items put self-protection steps and liability disclaimers up front.

> Translator's note: In mainland China, 120 = ambulance, 119 = fire, 110 = police, 122 = traffic accidents. In the US call 911; in the UK 999. All numbers in the items below are Chinese.
### 1. If someone collapses and is not breathing, start forceful chest compressions at once, have a bystander call 120 and fetch an AED
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; a Red Cross or hospital CPR course takes half a day to a day, and one training lasts for years; you can do it even without training: stack both hands over the midpoint of the line between the nipples, push down hard about 2 times per second, do not stop
- In plain terms: The person you are most likely to press is your own family — of 38,227 cases, 79.2% of cardiac arrests happened at home. When someone does compressions, the share of patients surviving to hospital discharge rises from 3.9% to 16.1%, a more-than-fourfold difference. If someone has collapsed, won't respond, and isn't breathing, just start pressing: midpoint between the nipples, both hands stacked, press hard, 2 times a second, without stopping. Have a second person call 120 and grab the AED while your hands keep going.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis of 79 studies covering 142,740 people: overall survival to hospital discharge after out-of-hospital cardiac arrest is 7.6%; with bystander CPR, survival rises from 3.9% to 16.1%, yet 53% of arrests are witnessed and only 32% get bystander compressions. China's BASIC-OHCA registry recorded 38,227 emergency-treated cases, of which 30,282 (79.2%) were cardiac arrests at home, with a bystander compression rate of 20.3% and survival to discharge of 1.2%. Start compressions first, then have a second person call and fetch the AED; once the machine is on, follow its voice prompts
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Sasson C 等 (2010). Predictors of survival from out-of-hospital cardiac arrest: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Circulation: Cardiovascular Quality and Outcomes. <https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCOUTCOMES.109.889576> ; Zheng J 等 (2023). Incidence, process of care, and outcomes of out-of-hospital cardiac arrest in China: a prospective study of the BASIC-OHCA registry. The Lancet Public Health. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2468-2667(23)00173-1> ; 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一百八十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: Do not first pinch the philtrum, do not first look for family members, do not first film a video; do not hold back out of fear of breaking ribs. Under Article 184 of China's Civil Code, a person who voluntarily provides emergency assistance and causes harm to the person being assisted bears no civil liability.

### 2. When an elderly person or anyone has fallen, first crouch down, call out to them, and call 120; do not rush to lift them up; for a stranger, walking away is legal, and if you stop, do not start moving the person
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; crouching down to ask a question and making a call takes a few minutes; for a stranger, also factor in that calling 120 leaves your number and may get you a call-back or one round of questioning; if a family member really does target you, litigation runs from several months upward, and attorney fees are not covered by "the losing party pays"
- In plain terms: The through-line of this item is your own elderly parents and the people around you. First check breathing; if there is none, go straight to the chest compressions in item 1. Even with breathing, do not pick the person up: falls are the number-one cause of injury death in people over 65, and the person may have fallen because of a stroke or fainting that came first. Crouch down, call out, and ask whether they remember how they fell — if they cannot recall, it may be syncope or a cerebrovascular event; then ask about severe headache, a drooping mouth, slurred speech, or weakness in the limbs — with any of those, lifting is even more wrong, because forcing them up can worsen brain hemorrhage or brain ischemia; with back or lower-back pain, incontinence of urine or stool, or anything that looks like a fracture, never move the person without professional knowledge. In all of these cases, call 120 where you are. If they try to get up themselves, support them as they rise slowly into a sitting position, watch for a while to confirm they are fine, and only then leave. Fear of being extorted is two separate questions: if you injure them in the course of helping, China's Civil Code states explicitly that the rescuer bears no civil liability — in a Supreme People's Court model case, a rescuer performing CPR cracked the recipient's ribs, was sued for over 9,000 yuan, and the court ruled no compensation was owed. Conversely, if they claim you hit them, the rule is that whoever makes a claim must produce the evidence, and the party that cannot produce evidence bears the adverse consequences.
- Benefit: The health ministry's technical guideline on fall intervention for the elderly: "Falls are the leading cause of injury death among people over 65 in China," and "When you find an elderly person who has fallen, do not rush to lift them up; handle the situation according to the circumstances." If consciousness is impaired, call emergency services immediately; with vomiting, turn the head to one side and clear the mouth and nose; if breathing and heartbeat have stopped, start chest compressions at once; "If moving is necessary, it must be steady, with the person kept as flat as possible." If the person is conscious, first ask whether they remember the fall; "If they cannot recall, it may be syncope or a cerebrovascular accident"; with severe headache, a drooping mouth corner, slurred speech, or weak limbs, "immediately lifting the elderly person may worsen brain hemorrhage or brain ischemia and aggravate the condition"; with signs of fracture, lower-back pain, or incontinence, "if you lack the relevant professional knowledge, do not move them casually" — in all these cases call emergency services immediately; if the person tries to stand up on their own, assist them to rise slowly, "have them rest sitting or lying down and observe, and leave only after confirming nothing is wrong." Article 90 of the Supreme People's Court's Interpretation on the Application of China's Civil Procedure Law: "A party must provide evidence to prove the facts on which its claims are based," and "if before judgment a party fails to provide evidence, or the evidence is insufficient to prove its factual claim, the party bearing the burden of proof bears the adverse consequences." Article 1198 of China's Civil Code: "Where operators or managers of business premises or public places such as hotels, shopping malls, banks, stations, airports, stadiums, and entertainment venues, or organizers of mass activities, fail to fulfill their duty of safety protection and cause harm to others, they bear tort liability." Article 184 of China's Civil Code: "Where a person voluntarily provides emergency assistance and causes harm to the person being assisted, the rescuer bears no civil liability." In a Supreme People's Court model case, a pharmacy operator performed CPR on a person in cardiac arrest, fracturing their ribs and leading to 18 days in hospital; the recipient claimed over 9,000 yuan in damages, and the court ruled that the rescuer bore no civil liability
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 卫生部 (2011). 老年人跌倒干预技术指南（「老年人跌倒后的处理」）. 全文 PDF 见广东省疾病预防控制中心 <http://cdcp.gd.gov.cn/gcdcdfiles/editor/uploadfile/20111027094832117.pdf>，现场处理部分全文见贵南县人民政府转载 <https://www.guinan.gov.cn/lnb/zc1__zwgk/zc/aztfl/qt/content_27609778>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一百八十三、一百八十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2023). 人民法院抓实公正与效率践行社会主义核心价值观典型案例（案例十三，齐某某诉孙某某健康权纠纷案，辽宁省康平县人民法院）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/408162.html>；最高人民法院 (2022 年第二次修正，2022 年 4 月 10 日施行). 关于适用《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的解释（第九十条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/353651.html>；民法典第一千一百九十八条同上最高检转载全文；全国人大常委会 (2023 年修正，2024 年 1 月 1 日施行). 民事诉讼法（第一百五十二、一百六十四条）. 上海市发展和改革委员会转载全文 <https://fgw.sh.gov.cn/ys-syjf-zc-2.4.1-h5/20240408/073febdb2b04464390546232d4089f51.html>；国务院 (2006). 诉讼费用交纳办法（国务院令第 481 号，第六、二十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/ziliao/flfg/2006-12/29/content_483682.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，2026 年 1 月 1 日起施行，第二十六条第一项、第五十条第五项）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>
- Notes: The reason for grade B is that the guideline gives handling rules but no effect size for "how many fewer deaths if followed." Article 184 exempts harm caused in the course of the rescue, on the premises of voluntariness and emergency; it does not cover moves like "deciding on your own to stuff the person into your car and drive them to a hospital" — waiting for 120 is the safer course. After calling 120, do not hang up; do what the dispatcher says. Under the beneficiary yardstick, this set of judgments is first of all for your own elderly relatives and acquaintances; for someone you do not know on the street, walking away is lawful and the least costly — you have no duty to rescue, and stopping means paying in time, a phone number on record, being tracked down by family, being listed as a defendant, escalation to your workplace, and being filmed and spread online, costed layer by layer in [should you stop when a stranger collapses](../../docs/en/遇到陌生人出事该不该停.md). If you really do decide to stop, the only move with no second-guessing is **do not start moving the person** — the reason is medical, not legal: forcing them up can worsen brain hemorrhage or spinal injury. One exception to remember: if you are the operator or manager of the premises or the organizer of the activity, walking away is no longer free — Article 1198 of China's Civil Code imposes on you a duty of safety protection. For fall prevention itself see Section 1, item 13; for delayed symptoms after an elderly person hits their head see item 9 of this section; for money after being injured while rescuing see item 40 of this section; for preserving evidence and reporting cyberbullying see Section 8, item 14.

### 3. Sudden facial droop, weakness in one arm, or slurred speech: call 120 at once; do not wait and do not drive there yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; remember three actions: have them smile, raise both arms level, and say a complete sentence
- In plain terms: Thrombolysis within 3 hours gives roughly 1.75 times the chance of recovering to near-independent daily living compared with no thrombolysis; dragged out to 3 to 4.5 hours it falls to 1.26 times; beyond 4.5 hours no difference can be shown. Drooping mouth, one arm that won't lift, speech that won't come out — call 120 immediately, note the exact time symptoms started, don't give water or medicine, and don't drive there yourself.
- Benefit: An individual-patient-data meta-analysis of 16 intravenous thrombolysis randomized trials with 6,756 patients: thrombolysis with alteplase within 3 hours of onset gives an odds ratio of 1.75 (95% CI 1.35–2.27) for a good outcome (modified Rankin score 0–1); at 3 to 4.5 hours, 1.26 (1.05–1.51); beyond 4.5 hours, 1.15 (0.95–1.40), no longer significant
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Emberson J, Lees KR, Lyden P, et al. (2014). Effect of treatment delay, age, and stroke severity on the effects of intravenous thrombolysis with alteplase for acute ischaemic stroke: a meta-analysis of individual patient data from randomised trials. Lancet, 384(9958), 1929-1935. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(14)60584-5>
- Notes: The earlier it is given, the more effective it is; beyond the 4.5-hour window there is essentially no thrombolysis benefit left. Call 120 rather than driving yourself because the ambulance will take the patient directly to a hospital with a stroke center and notify them in advance. Note the exact time symptoms began — doctors use it to decide whether thrombolysis is possible. Give no water or medicine, and do not wait to "rest a bit and see."

### 4. Sudden violent dizziness with unsteady standing, double vision, one eye going dark, or a finger that cannot touch its own nose tip: also treat it as a stroke and call 120
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; add two checks to the three actions in the previous item: have them stand straight, close their eyes, and see whether they sway, and ask whether their vision is doubled or has a missing patch; if you suspect it yourself, extend a finger and touch your nose tip, then do it again with your eyes closed
- In plain terms: Of 736 strokes, 14.1% began with neither a drooping mouth nor a paralyzed hand — checking only those three signs misses them; once "unsteady standing, double vision, or a missing patch in the visual field" is added too, the miss rate drops to 4.4%. Sudden spinning dizziness with an inability to stand, double vision, or a finger that can't find its own nose tip — call 120 all the same, and note the time all the same.
- Benefit: A 2014 review of 736 acute ischemic strokes at the University of Kentucky Stroke Center in the US: 14.1% of patients had none of the three FAST symptoms at onset (facial droop, arm weakness, slurred speech); adding "unsteady gait, weak legs, and visual symptoms" to the screen reduced the proportion missed from 14.1% to 4.4%
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Aroor S, Singh R, Goldstein LB (2017). BE-FAST (Balance, Eyes, Face, Arm, Speech, Time): Reducing the Proportion of Strokes Missed Using the FAST Mnemonic. Stroke, 48(2), 479-481. <https://doi.org/10.1161/STROKEAHA.116.015169>
- Notes: Infarcts in the cerebellum and brainstem (posterior circulation) often cause neither facial droop nor limb paralysis, showing only as vertigo, a veering gait, double vision, or choking on swallowing, and are most easily delayed by being mistaken for cervical spondylosis, low blood sugar, or benign paroxysmal positional vertigo. Inaccurate nose-tip pointing and inability to stand with eyes closed are signs of cerebellar involvement, but no single action can rule stroke out in the other direction: if it is sudden in onset and does not ease off, call 120 and note the exact time of onset. The study is a single-center retrospective analysis, and its authors state themselves that prospective validation is needed

### 5. One eye suddenly going dark like a curtain pulled down — even if it clears on its own within minutes, go to the emergency department the same day as a stroke
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; it takes half a day to a day for emergency evaluation and vascular workup
- In plain terms: One eye suddenly going dark like a curtain coming down means a vessel at the back of the eye is blocked, and the longer the blockage lasts, the less retina can be saved; it is also a form of cerebral ischemia, and the next few days are exactly the high-risk window for a stroke. When similar patients were switched to same-day evaluation and same-day treatment, the share having another stroke within 90 days fell from 10.3% to 2.1%. Go to an emergency department with a stroke center the same day; do not wait until the next day to book ophthalmology.
- Benefit: The American Heart Association's 2021 scientific statement on central retinal artery occlusion: "Acute central retinal artery occlusion is an emergency"; care systems should recognize and triage it as an emergency, and the longer the ischemia, the less retinal tissue can still be salvaged; the Oxford EXPRESS study: after patients with transient ischemic attack and minor stroke were switched to same-day evaluation and same-day treatment, the proportion of recurrent stroke within 90 days fell from 10.3% (32/310) to 2.1% (6/281)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Mac Grory B, Schrag M, Biousse V, et al. (2021). Management of Central Retinal Artery Occlusion: A Scientific Statement From the American Heart Association. Stroke, 52(6), e282-e294. <https://doi.org/10.1161/STR.0000000000000366> ; Rothwell PM, Giles MF, Chandratheva A, et al. (2007). Effect of urgent treatment of transient ischaemic attack and minor stroke on early recurrent stroke (EXPRESS study): a prospective population-based sequential comparison. Lancet, 370(9596), 1432-1442. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(07)61448-2>
- Notes: It is graded B because EXPRESS was not a randomized trial but a sequential before-and-after comparison in the same population, so the effect size contains some time-trend component. A few minutes of darkness that then clears by itself is the easiest thing to wave off as "tiredness" or "low blood sugar"; it is a form of transient ischemic attack, and the next few days are exactly the high-risk window for stroke. Single-eye vision loss with no pain, no redness, and no tearing deserves particular seriousness: do not wait for a next-day ophthalmology clinic appointment, go straight to the emergency department of a hospital with a stroke center.

### 6. Chest pain, tightness, or pressure lasting more than 15 minutes without easing: call 120; do not tough it out and do not drive yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: Chest pain, tightness, or pressure that lasts more than 15 minutes without easing — call 120. Among patients who get balloon dilation within 55 minutes of arriving at the hospital, 2.8% die within 30 days; beyond 55 minutes, 9.7% — every stretch of delay kills off another patch of heart muscle. Do not drive yourself: losing consciousness on the road is a second accident.
- Benefit: An analysis of ST-elevation myocardial infarction patients from Israel's national acute coronary syndrome survey: those with door-to-balloon time ≤55 minutes had 30-day mortality of 5/178 (2.8%); those with >55 minutes, 15/155 (9.7%)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Karkabi B, Meir G, Zafrir B, et al. (2021). Door-to-balloon time and mortality in patients with ST-elevation myocardial infarction. European Heart Journal - Quality of Care and Clinical Outcomes. <https://doi.org/10.1093/ehjqcco/qcaa037>
- Notes: The study compares in-hospital delay after arrival, not time from symptom onset to arrival, but the direction is the same: every stretch of delay is charged against dead heart muscle. The pain may present as upper-abdominal pain, back pain, jaw pain, or left-arm numbness; people with diabetes and the elderly may feel no pain at all, only shortness of breath. Do not drive yourself — losing consciousness on the road is a second accident

### 7. Sudden tearing agony migrating from the chest to the back and down into the waist: call 120 and say clearly that "the pain is moving"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: A tearing pain that runs from the chest to the back and down into the waist — when you call 120, say straight out "it feels like tearing, and the pain is traveling downward." Without surgery, on drugs alone, the death rate for this disease is 58%, and its management is the opposite of a heart attack's — only when the doctor thinks of it will they order a contrast CT instead of giving you anticoagulation and thrombolysis. If the blood pressure differs a lot between the two arms, or the pulse on one side cannot be felt, say that too.
- Benefit: The International Registry of Acute Aortic Dissection (IRAD), 464 cases at 12 centers: sudden severe sharp pain is the most common presenting complaint; overall in-hospital mortality is 27.4%, and for type A dissection treated without surgery, with medication alone, mortality is 58%
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Hagan PG, Nienaber CA, Isselbacher EM, et al. (2000). The International Registry of Acute Aortic Dissection (IRAD): new insights into an old disease. JAMA, 283(7), 897-903. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.283.7.897>
- Notes: Dissection is managed in exactly the opposite way from myocardial infarction — a heart attack gets anticoagulation and thrombolysis, but giving those in dissection amounts to bleeding out through the tear, so at the hospital proactively say "tearing pain, traveling downward" so the doctor thinks of ordering a contrast CT. A large blood-pressure gap between the arms or an absent pulse on one side is a clue. People with poorly controlled hypertension, Marfan syndrome, or a family history of aortic aneurysm or dissection are at high risk. Likewise, do not drive yourself and do not endure it until the next day

### 8. A sudden "worst headache of your life" that peaks within an hour: go straight to the emergency department for a head CT
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; an emergency head CT costs a few hundred yuan
- In plain terms: What matters is not how much it hurts but how fast it gets there: among 2,131 people with a severe headache peaking within an hour, 6.2% had a subarachnoid hemorrhage. Being over 40, neck pain or stiffness, someone seeing you pass out, onset during exertion, an instant exploding pain, or a neck that won't bend forward — hit any one of these and go to the emergency department for a head CT. Almost everyone who gets checked turns out fine; miss one rebleed and it is death. Do not swallow a painkiller and go back to sleep.
- Benefit: A prospective cohort of 2,131 cases of "severe headache reaching maximum intensity within one hour" across 10 Canadian emergency departments: 132 cases (6.2%) were subarachnoid hemorrhage. The Ottawa SAH rule (age 40 or over, neck pain or stiffness, witnessed loss of consciousness, onset during exertion, thunderclap onset, or limited neck flexion on examination; a positive on any one of the six calls for imaging) had 100% sensitivity for detecting hemorrhage (95% CI 97.2%–100.0%) and 15.3% specificity
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Perry JJ, Stiell IG, Sivilotti MLA, et al. (2013). Clinical decision rules to rule out subarachnoid hemorrhage for acute headache. JAMA, 310(12), 1248-1255. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2013.278018>
- Notes: The key is not "how painful" but "how fast it peaks": a headache that explodes within seconds to a minute is the danger signal; a migraine that builds gradually is not. With vomiting, a stiff neck, light sensitivity, or brief fainting, take it even more seriously. Specificity is only 15.3%, which means the great majority of people checked under this rule turn out fine, but missing one rebleed is death, so the check is worth it. Do not suppress it with a painkiller and go to sleep

### 9. If an elderly person becomes unsteady, dull, sleepy, or weak on one side two or three weeks to a few months after a head knock, get a head CT
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A head CT costs two to three hundred yuan
- In plain terms: After an elderly person knocks their head, blood usually does not pool until the third week; at the moment of impact there can be nothing at all, and the person may not even remember the knock. This condition has a postoperative recurrence rate of up to 33% and a fatality rate of up to 32%, and it is most easily delayed by being written off as dementia or the aftermath of a previous stroke. If an elderly relative suddenly becomes confused, walks as if floating, is always sleepy, or has one weak hand, first work backward and ask whether they fell or hit their head in the past two or three months, then get the CT.
- Benefit: The Chinese expert consensus on drug treatment of chronic subdural hematoma from the Neurosurgery Branch of the Chinese Medical Association and others: chronic subdural hematoma is a chronic space-occupying collection of blood between the arachnoid and the dura mater, "usually forming in the third week after head injury," mostly in the elderly, with postoperative recurrence up to 33%, fatality up to 32%, and an overall good-outcome rate of 24% in those over 90; a systematic review: global incidence is 3.39 to 39.1 per 100,000 person-years, rising with age and up to threefold in those over 80; the most common causes are trauma and falls, followed by long-term heavy alcohol use, anticoagulation, and assault; a geriatric review: clinical presentation "typically includes confusion, cognitive decline, gait disturbance, or somnolence and may resemble other geriatric syndromes"
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Zhang J 等 (2021). Expert consensus on drug treatment of chronic subdural hematoma. Chinese Neurosurgical Journal, 7(1), 47. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s41016-021-00263-z> ; Dziho A 等 (2025). Global prevalence and incidence of chronic subdural hematoma: A systematic review. Brain and Spine, 5, 105893. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bas.2025.105893> ; Mathon B, Shotar E (2026). Diagnostic and therapeutic management of chronic subdural hematoma in elderly patients. Gériatrie et Psychologie Neuropsychiatrie du Vieillissement, 24(1), 30-41. <https://doi.org/10.1684/pnv.2026.1274>
- Notes: At the moment of impact there can be nothing at all, and the elderly person may not even recall the knock. So if at home you find "suddenly confused lately, walking unsteadily, always sleepy, one weak hand," first work backward and ask about falls or head knocks in the past two or three months, then get a CT — do not jump straight to "dementia" or "leftover from that last stroke"; that is the only fork that causes delay. Elderly people on aspirin, clopidogrel, warfarin, or a novel oral anticoagulant are at higher risk, as are heavy long-term drinkers. For fall prevention see Section 1, item 13.

### 10. One leg suddenly swollen, tight, and tender to press: seek care promptly; if sudden breathlessness or chest pain is added on top, call 120 at once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A lower-limb venous ultrasound costs one to two hundred yuan
- In plain terms: One leg suddenly swollen, a full size bigger than the other, and hurting when pressed — go get a leg venous ultrasound promptly. If sudden breathlessness, chest pain on deep breaths, a racing heart, or lightheadedness appears on top, the clot may already have traveled to the lungs: call 120 directly — 17.4% of people diagnosed with pulmonary embolism die within 3 months. Sitting still for more than 4 hours roughly doubles the risk, but the absolute probability is still low.
- Benefit: The International Cooperative Pulmonary Embolism Registry (ICOPER), 2,454 confirmed acute pulmonary embolism cases from 52 hospitals in 7 countries: 3-month all-cause mortality of 17.4% (426/2454); the World Health Organization's WRIGHT project: journeys of 4 hours or more raise the risk of venous thromboembolism to "about double," but absolute risk remains low, at "about one in six thousand" for more than 4 hours of immobility
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Goldhaber SZ, Visani L, De Rosa M (1999). Acute pulmonary embolism: clinical outcomes in the International Cooperative Pulmonary Embolism Registry (ICOPER). Lancet, 353(9162), 1386-1389. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(98)07534-5> ; WHO (2007). Study results released on travel and blood clots. <https://www.who.int/news/item/29-06-2007-study-results-released-on-travel-and-blood-clots>
- Notes: Both legs swelling together is mostly a heart, liver, kidney, or venous-insufficiency problem; one leg suddenly swollen and a size bigger than the other is the typical picture of deep vein thrombosis. High-risk scenarios are long car or plane journeys with immobility, bed rest after surgery or fracture, a cast, pregnancy and the postpartum period, cancer, and oral contraceptives. When a clot breaks off and blocks a pulmonary artery, that is pulmonary embolism: sudden shortness of breath, chest pain worse on deep breaths, racing heart, lightheadedness, coughing up blood — at that stage call 120 directly. The 17.4% is the mortality among diagnosed pulmonary embolism cases, not "a swollen leg carries that much risk"; do not scare yourself with this number.

### 11. For major bleeding, first press the wound hard with your hand; if a limb cannot be controlled, apply a tourniquet — and call 120 at the same time
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->

### 13. After a burn or scald, immediately rinse under cool running water for 20 minutes; do not apply toothpaste or soy sauce

### 15. When someone convulses and collapses: clear the surroundings, put them on their side, time it with a watch, and put nothing into their mouth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: Move hard objects out of the way, lay them on their side with the mouth pointing down, and time the seizure. Do not hold them down, do not pry the mouth open, do not stuff anything in — that only damages teeth and the jaw. Call an ambulance if it lasts over 5 minutes, if it stops and quickly recurs, if they cannot be woken or breathe with difficulty afterward, if it happens in water, or if it is the first-ever seizure. Give water only after they are fully alert.
- Benefit: Avoids tooth and jaw injuries from prying the mouth open or inserting hard objects, and fractures from forcibly holding the person down; a seizure lasting over 5 minutes is the red line that requires an ambulance
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 美国疾病控制与预防中心. 癫痫发作急救：「Don't hold the person down or stop their movements」「Don't put anything in their mouth. This can hurt their teeth or jaw」「Don't offer water or food until they are fully alert」「Time the seizure to track if it lasts more than 5 minutes. - Notes: The same page lists further situations requiring an immediate ambulance call: another seizure soon after one ends, inability to wake the person or breathing difficulty afterward, injury sustained during the seizure, a seizure in water, and a first-ever seizure. After it stops, lay them on their side with the mouth down, and give water only once fully alert. ### 16. If a person with diabetes suddenly trembles, sweats cold, and slurs speech: give 15 grams of sugar first and retest after 15 minutes

### 19. After swallowing a cleaner, pesticide, or medicine by mistake, do not induce vomiting — take the bottle and seek care immediately; for splashes on eyes or skin, flush with plenty of clean water for 15 minutes
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; store cleaners, disinfectants, pesticides, and medicines on high shelves or in locked cabinets out of children's reach, and never decant them into beverage bottles
- In plain terms: After swallowing a cleaner, pesticide, or medicine, do not induce vomiting — corrosive liquid coming back up burns the esophagus a second time. First drink a suitable amount of milk or egg white, take the bottle and its instructions, and get to a hospital immediately; doctors need them to identify the ingredient. For splashes on skin or eyes, flush with plenty of clean water for a full 15 minutes; for eyes, hold the lids open while flushing and do not rub. Mixing disinfectant with toilet-bowl cleaner produces chlorine gas, which can cause an incident on the spot.
- Benefit: The handling guidance from China CDC's National Institute of Occupational Health and Poison Control — ingestion: immediately drink a suitable amount of milk or egg white, "remember, do not induce vomiting," and seek medical care promptly while carrying the packaging or instructions, "so that the doctor can quickly identify the ingredient and formulate a treatment plan"; inhalation: immediately move to a well-ventilated area, loosen the collar to keep the airway clear, and seek medical care immediately with severe coughing, rapid breathing, or breathing difficulty; skin contact: immediately flush the affected area with plenty of clean water for at least 15 minutes, keeping the runoff away from the eyes and other intact skin; eye contact: immediately flush the eyes with plenty of clean water or saline for at least 15 minutes, holding the eyelids open so the water fully contacts the eyeball and inner lids, "never rub the eyes," and go straight to ophthalmology afterward. One combination that goes wrong on the spot: disinfectant used together with an acid-containing toilet-bowl cleaner produces chlorine gas, which irritates the airways, triggers severe coughing and shortness of breath, and can even be life-threatening
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 中国疾病预防控制中心职业卫生与中毒控制所 (2025). 暑假安全提示：警惕强酸强碱清洁剂. <https://niohp.chinacdc.cn/kpdw/202507/t20250722_308659.htm>
- Notes: The reason for grade B is that the official guidance gives handling procedures and identification methods but no effect size for "how much harm is reduced by following it." The logic against vomiting: corrosive liquid coming back up burns the esophagus a second time. If you are not sure what was swallowed, take the bottle and instructions, along with any vomit, to the hospital. Prevention is far cheaper than emergency care: store by category, never mix with food and drinks, tighten caps and discard empty bottles after use, and never pour leftovers into a water bottle; wear rubber gloves and goggles when using, open windows for ventilation, and never mix different product types. ### 31. In an earthquake, first know what kind of building you are in: in a code-compliant building, take cover where you are; in an old building that can collapse with the door only a few steps away, get out
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; know in advance how many steps to your door, which route to take, and which table is sturdy

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: An extra day or two for acclimatization; preventive medication is a prescription drug — ask a doctor before departure
- In plain terms: What counts is how high you sleep, not how high you went during the day: sleeping above 2,450 meters carries altitude illness risk. Above 3,000 meters, raise your sleeping elevation by no more than 500 meters a night, and add an extra acclimatization day for every 1,000 meters gained. Headache plus nausea, a staggering walk, altered consciousness, or breathlessness even at rest — go down. Descent comes before oxygen and medication; continuing to climb while breathing oxygen is the wrong move.
- Benefit: Sleeping above 2,450 meters already carries altitude illness risk; high-altitude cerebral edema and pulmonary edema kill if descent is delayed

- Sources: 美国疾病控制与预防中心. 黄皮书·高海拔旅行与高原病：「Any unacclimatized traveler proceeding to a sleeping altitude of ≥2,450 m (≥8,000 ft)—and sometimes lower—is at risk for altitude illness」；上升速度「500 m (1,650 ft) per night in sleeping altitude once above 3,000 m (9,800 ft)」，每上升 1000 米加一天适应日；乙酰唑胺预防「125 mg twice a day; 250 mg twice a day if >100 kg body weight」；「If symptoms become worse despite rest or treatment at the same elevation」必须下撤. <https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/environmental-hazards-risks/high-altitude-travel-and-altitude-illness.html>；荒野医学会 (2024). 急性高原病预防、诊断与治疗临床实践指南（2024 更新）. - Notes: What counts is "how high you sleep," not how high you went during the day. ### 36. Boil all wilderness water before drinking; a rolling boil for one minute is enough — no extra boiling for altitude
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: The weight of a stove and fuel
- In plain terms: Every microorganism that can sicken you via drinking water dies within seconds at boiling temperature; the extra minute is only a margin for "misjudging the boil," with no added boiling for high altitude. If you cannot boil, use a filter or purification tablets — each misses some pathogens the other covers, so boiling is still first choice. Crystal-clear stream water and snowmelt can carry parasites just the same.
- Benefit: All waterborne pathogens are inactivated within seconds at boiling temperature; "water boils inadequately at high altitude" is a common misconception
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 美国疾病控制与预防中心. 黄皮书·旅行者的水消毒：「All organisms that may cause illness from drinking water are killed within seconds at boiling temperature」「CDC recommends that travelers boil water for a full minute to account for user variability in identifying boiling points and to add a margin of safety」「Although the boiling point for water decreases with increasing elevation, at common travel elevations the temperature needed to achieve boiling is still well above the temperature required to inactivate enteric pathogens」. <https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/preparing-international-travelers/water-disinfection-for-travelers.html>
- Notes: The same page also notes that a true boil is not strictly necessary: holding water at 60°C for 30 minutes works just as well; boiling is simply the visible endpoint that needs no thermometer. If you cannot boil, use a filter or chemical tablets — each has pathogens the other misses — so boil when you can. Clear-looking streams and snowmelt can still carry parasites; do not judge by appearance.

### 37. If a stranger demands money in remote open country: hand it over, do not fight, note their features, and report to the police once clear
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: The cash on you; swallowing your anger
- In plain terms: Hand the money over, do not fight, do not talk back. For a few hundred yuan the other party is risking robbery charges: starting at 3 to 10 years, and with home invasion, use of a gun, or serious injury or death, 10 years up to death. Once they take the money and turn to leave, chasing them to strike is no longer self-defense. The moment you are clear, write down their appearance, accent, plate number, and direction of flight, and call 110. Do not follow them anywhere secluded "to get money," do not get in their car, and do not hand over your phone password.
- Benefit: Life is dearer than money, and for a few hundred yuan the other party is risking a serious crime: Article 263 of China's Criminal Law — whoever robs public or private property by violence, coercion, or other means is sentenced to 3 to 10 years of fixed-term imprisonment plus a fine, and where a gun is used, a home is invaded, or serious injury or death results, 10 years or more up to death; Article 20 on justifiable defense protects only "stopping an unlawful infringement in progress" — once they take the money and turn away, chasing them down to strike is no longer defense. As soon as you are clear, note appearance, accent, plate, and direction, and call 110
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020 年修正). 刑法（第二十条、第二百六十三条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>（北京市公安交管局转载整合文本）
- Notes: The reason for grade C is that there is no direct literature for "complying rather than resisting is safer"; the statutes only state the other party's risk and your boundaries. Do not follow them anywhere secluded "to withdraw money," do not hand over your phone password, do not get in their car; this item is not a lesson in fighting, nor in not reporting to the police.

### 38. When you come upon a group fight: withdraw and leave; do not step in to break it up, do not gawk, do not pick up weapons off the ground; if you report it, move to a safe distance and call 110
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; hold down two urges — stepping in to pull people apart and standing around to watch; after reporting you may be re-contacted for one statement, costing at most half a day
- In plain terms: The person who steps in to break up a fight is often the last one counted into it — once you lay hands on anyone, it changes from peacemaking to joining the brawl. Joining an affray carries up to 3 years; with large numbers and scale, on a public road, or with weapons, 3 to 10 years, and if it causes serious injury or death, it is charged as intentional injury or intentional homicide; at the public-order level, group brawling carries up to 15 days of detention plus a 2,000-yuan fine. Standing around watching is not free either — the more people, the more chaotic the scene, and the easier to be hurt by mistake. The move is to withdraw and leave; if you are willing to report, get to a safe distance and call 110, stating the location, how many people, whether anyone has a knife or club, and whether anyone is lying motionless, and film a clip from far off. If you see someone being surrounded and beaten one-sidedly, you are indeed entitled to stop it — justifiable defense protects others as well as yourself — but going in bare-handed most likely just adds another casualty; shouting, filming, and calling are more effective.
- Benefit: Article 292 of China's Criminal Law: "For affray, ringleaders and other active participants are sentenced to up to three years of fixed-term imprisonment, criminal detention, or public surveillance"; for repeated affray, large numbers and scale with vile social impact, serious disruption of public order in public places or on major thoroughfares, or carrying weapons, "sentence of three to ten years of fixed-term imprisonment"; where affray "causes serious injury or death," conviction and punishment follow the crimes of intentional injury or intentional homicide. The Public Security Administration Punishment Law: assault carries 5 to 10 days of detention plus a fine of 500 to 1,000 yuan; group brawling as picking quarrels and provoking trouble carries up to 15 days of detention plus a 2,000-yuan fine. The Guiding Opinion on the Lawful Application of Justifiable Defense, Article 5: an unlawful infringement "includes both infringements directed at oneself and those endangering the state or the public interest or directed at others"; Article 9: where one party strikes first with clearly excessive means, or strikes first and continues the attack while the other party is trying hard to avoid the conflict, the party striking back should generally be found to be acting in defense
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百三十二、二百三十四、二百九十二、三百零五、三百一十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第三十、五十一条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部 (2020). 关于依法适用正当防卫制度的指导意见（法发〔2020〕31 号，第 5、9 条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/251611.html>；全国人大 (2018). 刑事诉讼法（2018 年修订，第六十二、一百一十、一百一十一、一百九十三条）. 宁夏回族自治区人民检察院转载全文 <https://www.nx.jcy.gov.cn/zwsy/qwfb/202011/t20201119_608817.html>
- Notes: The reason for grade B is that the provisions on consequences are hard law, but there is no official original text for the step "withdraw and report, do not step in to break it up" — it is inferred from the statutes and the standards for finding mutual combat. "I was just breaking it up" is no talisman; the finding turns on whether you used force and whom you hit. Chasing and striking after the other party is down or has fled is no longer defense. What is guaranteed profit in this item is "keep your hands off, do not gawk"; the benefit of the 110 call falls mainly on the person on the ground — the lowest tier of beneficiary — and whether to spend that time is your own call. To weigh it you need to know: the duty to testify already exists (Article 62 of the Criminal Procedure Law: everyone who knows the circumstances of a case has a duty to testify), and not making the call does not make it go away; with serious injury or death, police tracing surveillance will find those present anyway, and onlookers are the easiest to register; for a reporting person unwilling to disclose their name, the handling organ "shall keep their identity secret" (Article 111), and once you have stated the location, the number of people, and whether anyone is armed, you may hang up without staying on the scene. Reaching admonishment or detention of ten days or less requires refusing to testify after the court notifies you to appear (Article 193), which passersby almost never reach. Not reporting is itself not unlawful: the "right and duty" wording of Article 110 of the Criminal Procedure Law carries no sanction, and the Criminal Law has no offense punishing an ordinary person's failure to report what they know — walking away is a lawful option; after walking away, only two red lines remain — later hiding the offender or giving false proof to shelter them (Article 310, up to 3 years, 3 to 10 years in serious cases), and deliberately giving false testimony when called to testify (Article 305, up to 3 years, 3 to 7 years in serious cases). Staying silent and telling the truth are both fine; making things up is what brings trouble. For being drawn into a conflict yourself see Section 8, item 9; for a stranger demanding money see item 37 of this section; for money after being injured while rescuing see item 40 of this section.

### 39. Possible HIV exposure: get post-exposure prophylaxis within 72 hours — the earlier the better
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A 28-day course of medication costs over a thousand yuan, out of pocket in most regions; and a hospital run is needed that very night
- In plain terms: If you suspect you have been infected with HIV, 72 hours is the hard line, with the best result from starting within 2 hours, and the drugs must be taken for a full 28 days. Past 72 hours this road is gone, so do not wait until the next day to decide.
- Benefit: The Beijing municipal government's HIV-prevention information page: post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP) should begin "preventive medication as soon as possible, ideally within 2 hours, within 24 hours at best, and no later than 72 hours," and be "taken continuously for 28 days." ### 40. If you are injured or out of pocket from rescuing someone: first pursue the wrongdoer and basic medical insurance, then apply for confirmation of brave-and-meritorious conduct
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- In plain terms: Getting hurt or out of pocket rescuing someone does not mean absorbing the loss alone. For injuries from brave-and-meritorious conduct, treatment comes before payment; where there is a wrongdoer, they pay the medical costs, and if they fled or cannot pay, basic medical insurance covers it. For losses incurred protecting someone else, first seek compensation from the infringer; with no infringer, or if they fled or cannot pay, the person you saved "shall" give appropriate compensation — that is legal wording, not a matter of their conscience. ### 41. If a knife, rebar, or glass is embedded in the body: press around the object to stop the bleeding — do not pull it out
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; hold back the instinct to "pull it out first," and stay with the person until the ambulance comes
- In plain terms: The embedded object is also plugging the vessels it cut, so pulling it out often means major bleeding on the spot, and it makes it harder for doctors to judge how deep the damage goes. The approach: call 120, press on the wound around the object with hands or dressings to stop the bleeding, pad the object so it does not shift, and keep the person from moving. If the object pulses with the heartbeat, stabilize it loosely — do not press it down or straighten it forcibly. With organs protruding from an abdominal wound, do not push them back in; cover them with a clean, moist dressing. For a chest wound that is bubbling or hissing, do not seal it with plastic sheeting or the like — sealing it can trap air in the chest cavity and collapse the lung.
- Benefit: The International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies' 2025 International First Aid Guidelines: "First aid providers should apply pressure around an embedded object (e.g. a knife) to stabilize the object. Avoid removing the object."; "Do not remove any impaled objects from an abdominal or chest wound, as this may cause serious bleeding and make it more difficult for a medical professional to determine the severity of the wound."; "Impaled objects in the body should be stabilized, and if the object is pulsating, it should be allowed to do so while loosely stabilized."; "First aid providers should not push internal organs back into the body."; open chest-wall wounds not actively bleeding "may be left open, without the application of a dressing, because a sealed wound may allow air to build up in the chest, which may lead to the lung partially or fully collapsing (pneumothorax)"; all penetrating chest and abdominal wounds warrant an immediate call for emergency services
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies (2025). International First Aid, Resuscitation and Education Guidelines 2025，第 256–257、267–268 页. <https://www.ifrc.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/IFRC%20International%20First%20Aid,%20Resuscitation%20and%20Education%20Guidelines%202025.pdf>
- Notes: It is graded B because this is the guideline's good-practice recommendation without randomized trials — "pull out" versus "leave in" cannot ethically be randomized. The only exception is an object blocking the airway so the person cannot breathe. At the hospital, go straight to the triage desk at the emergency entrance and report "penetrating injury" — that is the tier that goes straight to the resuscitation room; do not queue at the registration window (see Section 24, item 8); if truly impaled, call 120 and the ambulance will notify the hospital ahead of arrival. ### 42. If you suspect a fracture, stop moving that body part; support it still, ice for under 20 minutes, and do not straighten it yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; find a stiff board, a rolled magazine, or clothing to use as support, and place a cloth between the cold pack and skin
- In plain terms: Treat any limb injury as a fracture first — whether they can stand or move rules nothing out, and only an X-ray can settle it. Keep the injured part motionless, cradle it with your hand or support it in its original position with a sling or stiff board, and ice it early — no more than 20 minutes at a time, with a cloth between pack and skin. In the city, if the ambulance can arrive, do not straighten anything yourself; straightening is considered only in the field, when rescue cannot be relied on, when the limb below the break is cold and pale, and when the rescuer is trained. With a femur fracture, the bleeding from the bone itself alone can be fatal — with severe pain, severe swelling, an obviously misaligned limb, or signs of shock, call 120 directly. If broken bone has pierced the skin, stop the bleeding and cover with a clean dressing first; do not push the bone back in.
- Benefit: The International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies' 2025 International First Aid Guidelines: "Any injury to an extremity should be approached as a potential bone fracture." "The only accurate way to diagnose a fracture is with an X-ray. When in doubt, assume it is a fracture."; the purpose of immobilization is "to minimize any movement and pain"; cooling "for up to 20 consecutive minutes, as this may decrease the pain. Cooling for longer than this may damage the skin"; "If a long bone is injured, such as the femur, the bleeding from the bone itself can be life-threatening"; call emergency services with marked pain or swelling, signs of shock, a femur fracture, or an abnormally positioned limb; on-site realignment of an angulated fracture is considered only for a "remote environment (or one with limited resources)" where the limb below the angulated fracture is cool or pale or the first aid provider is unable to splint for movement, and the rescuer is trained; for open fractures the priorities are stopping bleeding and preventing infection
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies (2025). International First Aid, Resuscitation and Education Guidelines 2025，第 301–302 页. <https://www.ifrc.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/IFRC%20International%20First%20Aid,%20Resuscitation%20and%20Education%20Guidelines%202025.pdf>
- Notes: It is graded B because these are good-practice recommendations: prehospital traction splinting of femur fractures is supported by only one retrospective cohort (less transfused blood within 24 hours and shorter hospital stays, with differences in mortality and complications not reaching significance, very low certainty of evidence), and for realign-first versus immobilize-in-place no evidence was found at all. Three common misconceptions to shed: standing and walking does not rule out a fracture; use cold only, never heat, and never alternate hot and cold; a sprain will not "work itself out with movement" — rest it first. For the moving prohibitions after an elderly person falls with a suspected fracture see item 2; for one leg swelling after a cast or bed rest, beware thrombosis — see item 10; for whether to seek a disability assessment or a disability certificate afterward see Section 24, items 10 and 11.
