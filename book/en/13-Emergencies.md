# 13. Emergencies: what to do first

This section answers only one thing: when something happens, what to do first, what to do next, and what not to do. The first-aid and wilderness entries count deaths from specific causes and survival rates. The scam and blackmail entries count money and personal freedom. The two sets of numbers are not interchangeable. Entries are ordered by "how common this is × how many lives the difference between doing it right and doing it wrong costs," from high to low. These entries are primarily for yourself, your spouse, and your immediate family. 79.2% of cardiac arrests happen at home; the person you are most likely to save is someone in your household. Friends and colleagues come second. For strangers there is still some benefit, but it is the lowest tier, and it carries the risk of being scammed or being dragged into a court case. That is why these entries put self-protection steps and liability disclaimers up front.

This section only covers actions to take within minutes. Recognizing a scam, or being blackmailed for money, is handled on a timescale of days and is not covered here. The hard anti-fraud rules and the seven most common scams are in Section 8, item 3 (hard anti-fraud rules). Being blackmailed with private photos or a recording of a video chat with nudity is covered in Section 8, item 32 (when the other side has leverage and demands money). If the money has already been transferred, call 110 or 96110 immediately and request a payment freeze; see Section 8, item 2 (payment freeze).
### 1. If someone collapses and is not breathing, press hard on their chest immediately, have someone else call 120 and find an AED
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. A Red Cross or hospital CPR course takes half a day to one day; one training is enough for years. You can do it even without training: stack your hands at the midpoint of the line between the nipples and press down hard about 2 times per second, without stopping.
- In plain terms: The person you are most likely to press is your own family member: 79.2% of cardiac arrests happen at home. With someone doing compressions, the share of people leaving hospital alive rises from 3.9% to 16.1%. If the person does not respond and is not breathing, just press. The position is the midpoint of the line between the nipples; stack your hands and press hard, 2 times per second, without stopping. Have a second person call 120 and get an AED while your hands keep going.
- Benefit: 79 studies with 142,740 people pooled together (meta-analysis): of people whose heart stops outside the hospital (out-of-hospital cardiac arrest), only 7.6% in total leave hospital alive. When bystanders perform CPR, the share leaving hospital alive rises from 3.9% to 16.1%. In 53% of cases someone witnessed the collapse, but only 32% actually received bystander compressions. China's BASIC-OHCA registry recorded 38,227 emergency-care cases; 30,282 of them (79.2%) had their cardiac arrest at home. In this group, bystander compression rate was 20.3% and 1.2% left hospital alive. The order is: press first, then have a second person call and fetch the AED. Once the machine is on, follow its voice prompts.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Sasson C 等 (2010). Predictors of survival from out-of-hospital cardiac arrest: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Circulation: Cardiovascular Quality and Outcomes. <https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCOUTCOMES.109.889576> ; Zheng J 等 (2023). Incidence, process of care, and outcomes of out-of-hospital cardiac arrest in China: a prospective study of the BASIC-OHCA registry. The Lancet Public Health. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2468-2667(23)00173-1> ; 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一百八十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: Do not start by pinching the philtrum (人中), do not start by looking for relatives, do not start by filming. Do not hold back out of fear of breaking ribs. Article 184 of the Civil Code states clearly: if you voluntarily give emergency aid and injure the person being helped, you bear no civil liability.

### 2. An elderly person falls, someone collapses: first crouch down, call out to them, and call 120; do not rush to lift them up; for a stranger, walking away is legal, and if you stop, do not move the person
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. Crouching down to ask a question and making a call takes a few minutes. The hard part is holding back the impulse to reach out and help them up. For a stranger there is an extra ledger: calling 120 leaves your phone number, you may get called back, and you may be asked to give a statement once. If the family really comes after you, a lawsuit starts at several months. And even if you win in the end, the lawyer's fees come out of your own pocket.
- In plain terms: The main line of this entry is your own elderly relatives and people close to you. First check breathing; if there is none, do chest compressions per item 1 (press hard on the chest). Even with breathing, do not scoop the person up: they may have had a stroke or fainted first, and the fall came after; force-lifting makes it worse. Crouch down, call to them, ask whether they remember how they fell. Lower back pain, or anything that looks like a fracture: never move them; call 120 where you are.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Health's Technical Guideline on Fall Intervention for the Elderly states two sentences. One: "Falls are the leading cause of injury-related death among people over 65 in our country." The other: "When you find an elderly person who has fallen, do not rush to lift them up; handle it according to the situation." If consciousness is unclear, call the emergency number immediately. If there is vomiting, turn the head to one side and clear the mouth and nose. If breathing and heartbeat have stopped, start chest compressions immediately. "If the person must be moved, keep it steady and flat on the back as much as possible." If they are conscious, first ask whether they remember the fall itself; "if they cannot recall it, it may have been syncope or a cerebrovascular accident." Severe headache, a drooping corner of the mouth, slurred speech, or weakness in the arms or legs: "immediately lifting the elderly person may worsen cerebral hemorrhage or cerebral ischemia and aggravate the condition." Signs of a fracture, lower back pain, or incontinence: "without relevant professional knowledge, do not move them casually." In all of these cases, call the emergency number immediately. If the elderly person wants to get up by themselves, you may help them rise slowly; "have them sit or lie down to rest and observe, and only leave once you are sure they are fine." On "fear of being extorted," the legal side is this. Article 90 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China has two sentences. First: "A party must provide evidence to prove the facts on which their claims are based..." Second: "If, before judgment is rendered, a party fails to provide evidence, or the evidence is insufficient to prove their factual claim, that party bears the adverse consequences of the burden of proof." Article 1198 of the Civil Code reads as follows: "Where operators or managers of business premises such as hotels, shopping malls, banks, stations, airports, or sports venues, or organizers of mass activities, fail to fulfill their duty of safety protection and cause harm to others, they shall bear tort liability." Article 184 of the Civil Code: "Where harm to the person assisted results from voluntarily performed emergency aid, the person rendering aid bears no civil liability." The Supreme People's Court published a typical case: a pharmacy operator performed CPR on a person in cardiac arrest and broke their ribs. The person helped was hospitalized for 18 days and claimed a bit over 9,000 yuan. The court ruled that the rescuer bore no civil liability.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 卫生部 (2011). 老年人跌倒干预技术指南（「老年人跌倒后的处理」）. 全文 PDF 见广东省疾病预防控制中心 <http://cdcp.gd.gov.cn/gcdcdfiles/editor/uploadfile/20111027094832117.pdf>，现场处理部分全文见贵南县人民政府转载 <https://www.guinan.gov.cn/lnb/zc1__zwgk/zc/aztfl/qt/content_27609778>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一百八十三、一百八十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2023). 人民法院抓实公正与效率践行社会主义核心价值观典型案例（案例十三，齐某某诉孙某某健康权纠纷案，辽宁省康平县人民法院）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/408162.html>；最高人民法院 (2022 年第二次修正，2022 年 4 月 10 日施行). 关于适用《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的解释（第九十条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/353651.html>；民法典第一千一百九十八条同上最高检转载全文；全国人大常委会 (2023 年修正，2024 年 1 月 1 日施行). 民事诉讼法（第一百五十二、一百六十四条）. 上海市发展和改革委员会转载全文 <https://fgw.sh.gov.cn/ys-syjf-zc-2.4.1-h5/20240408/073febdb2b04464390546232d4089f51.html>；国务院 (2006). 诉讼费用交纳办法（国务院令第 481 号，第六、二十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/ziliao/flfg/2006-12/29/content_483682.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，2026 年 1 月 1 日起施行，第二十六条第一项、第五十条第五项）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>
- Notes: It is graded B because the guideline only says how to handle it and has no numbers on "how many fewer deaths if you follow it." Article 184 exempts harm done in the process of rescuing, on the condition that you act voluntarily and the situation is an emergency. It does not cover things like "on your own initiative stuffing the person into your private car and driving them to a hospital"; waiting for 120 is the safer course. After calling 120, do not hang up; do what the dispatcher on the line says. This set of judgments is first of all for your own elderly relatives and acquaintances. For a stranger on the street, walking away is not illegal and is the simplest option: the law does not require you to rescue. If you stop, the bill gets counted layer by layer: time, leaving your number, being tracked down by the family, being sued, trouble reaching your workplace, being filmed and the footage spread online. See [docs/遇到陌生人出事该不该停.md](../../docs/遇到陌生人出事该不该停.md) for details. If you truly decide to stop, the one action with no second answer is **do not move the person**. The reason is medical, not legal: force-lifting can worsen cerebral hemorrhage or a spinal injury. One exception: if you run the place, manage it, or organized the activity, walking away is no longer free. Article 1198 of the Civil Code's duty of safety protection says you carry a share of responsibility for the safety of people on the premises. For fall prevention itself, see Section 1, item 13 (training balance and leg strength). For delayed symptoms after an elderly person hits their head, see item 10 in this section. For money after you are injured while rescuing someone, see item 39 in this section. For documenting online harassment and reporting it to the police, see Section 8, item 16.

### 3. Sudden crooked mouth, weakness in one arm, slurred speech: call 120 immediately; do not wait and do not drive there yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. Three actions you learn after seeing them once: have them smile, hold both arms out level, say one complete sentence.
- In plain terms: Crooked mouth, one arm that will not lift, speech that will not come out clearly: call 120 immediately. If the clot-dissolving drug is given within 3 hours of onset, the chance of recovering to roughly self-sufficient daily life is about 1.75 times that of not giving the drug. Dragged out to 3 to 4.5 hours, only 1.26 times remains. Past 4.5 hours the difference can no longer be seen. Write down the exact time symptoms began. Do not feed water or medicine, and do not drive there yourself.
- Benefit: Pooling the individual data of 16 intravenous thrombolysis randomized trials and 6,756 patients (meta-analysis). Intravenous thrombolysis means giving a drug to open the blocked vessel. With alteplase within 3 hours of onset, the odds ratio for recovering to roughly self-sufficient daily life is 1.75. "Roughly self-sufficient daily life" means a modified Rankin score of 0–1. The confidence range of this odds ratio is 95% CI 1.35–2.27. In other words, this outcome is about 1.75 times as likely as without thrombolysis. At 3 to 4.5 hours it is 1.26 (1.05–1.51). Past 4.5 hours it is 1.15 (0.95–1.40), statistically indistinguishable.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Emberson J, Lees KR, Lyden P, et al. (2014). Effect of treatment delay, age, and stroke severity on the effects of intravenous thrombolysis with alteplase for acute ischaemic stroke: a meta-analysis of individual patient data from randomised trials. Lancet, 384(9958), 1929-1935. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(14)60584-5>
- Notes: Thrombolysis means opening the blocked vessel with a drug; the earlier, the more effective, and past 4.5 hours it is essentially useless. Call 120 rather than driving yourself because the ambulance goes straight to a hospital with a stroke center and calls ahead so they can prepare. Note the exact time symptoms began; the doctor needs it to decide whether thrombolysis is possible. No water, no medicine. Do not wait to "rest a bit and see."

### 4. Sudden spinning vertigo with unsteadiness, double vision, one eye going dark, or a finger that cannot find its own nose tip: also call 120 as a stroke
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. Add two more checks to the three actions "face crooked, raise arms, speak" (item 3, sudden crooked mouth and slurred speech). One: have him stand straight with eyes closed and see whether he sways; then ask whether he sees double or a piece missing from his vision. The other: if you suspect it in yourself, extend a finger to touch your nose tip, then do it again with eyes closed.
- In plain terms: Of 736 strokes, 14.1% at onset had neither a crooked mouth nor a paralyzed arm. Relying only on the three checks "face crooked, raise arms, speak" misses them. Add "unsteady standing, double vision or a missing piece of vision" and the missed share drops to 4.4%. Sudden spinning vertigo that keeps you standing, seeing double, a finger that cannot find the nose tip: call 120 just the same, and note the time just the same.
- Benefit: In 2014 the University of Kentucky Stroke Center in the US reviewed the records of 736 cases of acute ischemic stroke (a stroke caused by a blocked vessel). 14.1% of those patients had none of the three FAST symptoms at onset, that is, crooked face, weak arm, slurred speech. Adding "unsteady walking, weak legs, visual symptoms" to the screening cut the missed share from 14.1% to 4.4%.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Aroor S, Singh R, Goldstein LB (2017). BE-FAST (Balance, Eyes, Face, Arm, Speech, Time): Reducing the Proportion of Strokes Missed Using the FAST Mnemonic. Stroke, 48(2), 479-481. <https://doi.org/10.1161/STROKEAHA.116.015169>
- Notes: Infarcts of the cerebellum and brainstem (posterior circulation infarct) — a blocked vessel in that region — often show neither a crooked mouth nor a weak arm. There is only spinning vertigo, veering while walking, diplopia (seeing double), choking on water or food; it is most often delayed as a misdiagnosis of cervical spondylosis, low blood sugar, or BPPV. Inaccurate nose-pointing and inability to stand with eyes closed are signs of cerebellar involvement. But no single test rules a stroke out in reverse: if it came on suddenly and is not easing, call 120, and note the exact time of onset. That study reviewed the records of only one hospital; the authors themselves state further validation is needed.

### 5. One eye suddenly goes dark like a curtain pulled down: even if it clears by itself within minutes, go to the emergency room that same day as a stroke
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. Emergency evaluation and vessel imaging take up half a day to one day.
- In plain terms: One eye suddenly going dark like a curtain pulled down means a vessel at the back of the eye is blocked. The longer the blockage, the less retina can be saved. It is also a form of brain ischemia, and the next few days are the high-risk window for a stroke. When such patients were switched to same-day evaluation and same-day start of treatment, the share having another stroke within 90 days fell from 10.3% to 2.1%. Go to the emergency room of a hospital with a stroke center that same day; do not wait until the next day to book ophthalmology.
- Benefit: The American Heart Association's 2021 scientific statement on central retinal artery occlusion (blockage of the main vessel at the back of the eye) states: "Acute central retinal artery occlusion is an emergency." Care systems should identify it as an emergency and triage it by urgency. The longer the blood supply is cut off, the less retina can still be saved. The Oxford EXPRESS study looked at two groups of patients: brain ischemia whose symptoms resolve on their own, and minor stroke. Brain ischemia whose symptoms resolve on their own is medically called a transient ischemic attack. After this group was switched to same-day evaluation and same-day start of treatment, the share with recurrent stroke came down. Within 90 days it fell from 10.3% (32/310) to 2.1% (6/281).
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Mac Grory B, Schrag M, Biousse V, et al. (2021). Management of Central Retinal Artery Occlusion: A Scientific Statement From the American Heart Association. Stroke, 52(6), e282-e294. <https://doi.org/10.1161/STR.0000000000000366> ; Rothwell PM, Giles MF, Chandratheva A, et al. (2007). Effect of urgent treatment of transient ischaemic attack and minor stroke on early recurrent stroke (EXPRESS study): a prospective population-based sequential comparison. Lancet, 370(9596), 1432-1442. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(07)61448-2>
- Notes: Graded B because EXPRESS was not a randomized trial. It compared the same population before and after the practice change, and other conditions between the two periods may also have changed; that part cannot be separated out. A brief blackout of vision that recovers in a few minutes is most easily waved off as "tiredness" or "low blood sugar." It is a form of transient ischemic attack, and the next few days are the high-risk window for a stroke. Loss of vision in one eye without pain, without redness, without tearing deserves particular seriousness. Do not wait for next day's ophthalmology clinic; go straight to the emergency room of a hospital with a stroke center.

### 6. One eye swollen, aching, and red, with a rainbow ring around lights, plus headache, nausea, and the urge to vomit: go to ophthalmic emergency the same day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. Ophthalmic emergency measurement of eye pressure and examination of the anterior chamber take up half a day.
- In plain terms: This is an acute glaucoma attack. The fluid inside the eye cannot drain out, and pressure shoots up all at once. The longer you delay, the more the optic nerve is crushed, and what is crushed does not come back. Patients during the Wuhan lockdown delayed on average 241 hours before treatment, and 21.87% went blind. The same period the next year, without lockdown, average delay was 121 hours and 7.84% went blind.
- Benefit: An eye hospital in Wuhan compared two periods of patients with acute primary angle-closure glaucoma. In the 76 lockdown days of 2020 there were 54 people with 64 eyes; in the same period of 2021, 46 people with 51 eyes; the two groups were similar in demographics. From symptom onset to treatment, the lockdown group was 241.84±211.95 hours versus 121.53±96.12 hours in the control group (P=0.001). Eye pressure at presentation was 52.63±12.45 mmHg versus 45.16±9.79 mmHg (P=0.001). The share blinded was 21.87% versus 7.84%. Glaucomatous optic neuropathy was found in 20/64 eyes (31.25%) versus 7/51 eyes (13.73%) (P=0.03). A hospital in South Korea reviewed 50 eyes that had one attack and later had the lens removed. A year later, 25 (50%) had some degree of visual field defect. The longer the time from symptom onset to lowering the eye pressure, the greater the chance of a residual defect (P=0.005). Another prospective study had 43 people with shallow anterior chambers lie prone in a dark room for 1 hour. Eye pressure rose markedly in both eyes (P<0.01). The usually dominant eye rose by a median of 3.60 mmHg, the other by 2.70 mmHg (P<0.05).
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Zhou L, Wu S, Wang Y, Bao X, Peng T, Luo W, Ortega-Usobiaga J (2022). Clinical presentation of acute primary angle closure during the COVID-19 epidemic lockdown. Frontiers in Medicine, 9, 1078237. <https://doi.org/10.3389/fmed.2022.1078237> ; Sung MS, Kim HJ, Park SW (2023). Predictors of long-term visual field outcome after an episode of acute primary angle closure. Clinical & Experimental Ophthalmology, 51(4), 291-299. <https://doi.org/10.1111/ceo.14206> ; Wang J, Wang J, Ng TK, Huang C (2025). Asymmetric intraocular pressure changes in dominant and contralateral eyes: the dark room prone provocative test. Seminars in Ophthalmology, 40(4), 325-331. <https://doi.org/10.1080/08820538.2024.2443972>
- Notes: Two reasons for the B grade. First, the Wuhan comparison was done by digging through old records; the lockdown itself brought other differences that cannot be separated from the "delay" factor. Second, the Korean group was only 50 eyes and also retrospective. This entry does not say that looking at screens causes glaucoma. In the dark the pupil dilates; long hours with the head lowered or lying prone push the lens forward; the two stacked together can trigger it in people whose anterior chamber is already shallow. So the truly high-risk are people over 50, farsighted, with a family member who had angle-closure glaucoma. Young people gaming all night basically do not go down this path. The most common misjudgment during an attack is to treat it as gastroenteritis, migraine, or high blood pressure, because the headache and vomiting are more conspicuous than the eye symptoms. Painless, non-red one-eye blindness is a different matter; see item 5 (one eye suddenly goes dark). For whether blue-light-blocking glasses work, see Section 6, item 16 (blue-light-blocking glasses).

### 7. Chest pain like a heavy weight, tightness, or pressure that does not ease after more than 15 minutes: call 120; do not tough it out and do not drive yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free.
- In plain terms: Chest pain like a heavy weight, tightness, or pressure that does not ease after more than 15 minutes: call 120. If the vessel is opened within 55 minutes of reaching the hospital, 2.8% die within 30 days. Past 55 minutes, 9.7%. Every segment of delay necroses another patch of heart muscle. Do not drive yourself; losing consciousness on the road is a second accident.
- Benefit: Israel conducted a nationwide survey of acute coronary syndrome. The analysis covered patients with ST-segment elevation myocardial infarction, a class of heart attack. It measured the interval from arriving at the hospital to opening the blocked vessel with a balloon (balloon dilation). With an interval of ≤55 minutes, 5/178 (2.8%) died within 30 days. With an interval of >55 minutes, 15/155 (9.7%) died within 30 days.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Karkabi B, Meir G, Zafrir B, et al. (2021). Door-to-balloon time and mortality in patients with ST-elevation myocardial infarction. European Heart Journal - Quality of Care and Clinical Outcomes. <https://doi.org/10.1093/ehjqcco/qcaa037>
- Notes: The study measured the delay after reaching the hospital, not the segment from onset to arrival. But the logic is the same: every moment of delay necroses another patch of heart muscle. The pain may sit in the upper abdomen, the back, or the jaw, or be numbness in the left arm. Diabetics and the elderly may feel no pain, only shortness of breath. Do not drive yourself; losing consciousness on the road is a second accident.

### 8. Sudden tearing agony moving from the chest down through the back toward the waist: call 120 and say clearly "the pain is moving"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free.
- In plain terms: Tearing pain moving from the chest down through the back toward the waist: when you call 120, say directly "it feels like something tearing, the pain is moving downward." The death rate of this disease without surgery, on medication alone, is 58%. Its treatment is the opposite of a heart attack: only if the doctor thinks of it will they order a contrast CT, instead of giving you anticoagulants and clot-dissolving drugs. Say as well if the blood pressure differs a lot between the two arms or the pulse on one side cannot be felt.
- Benefit: The International Registry of Acute Aortic Dissection (IRAD) collected 464 cases from 12 centers. Aortic dissection is the disease with that tearing chest-and-back agony. Among the symptoms patients first reported, the most common was sudden onset of severe sharp pain. Overall in-hospital mortality was 27.4%. For type A dissection without surgery, on medication only, mortality was 58%.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Hagan PG, Nienaber CA, Isselbacher EM, et al. (2000). The International Registry of Acute Aortic Dissection (IRAD): new insights into an old disease. JAMA, 283(7), 897-903. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.283.7.897>
- Notes: The treatment of dissection is exactly opposite to that of a heart attack. A heart attack gets anticoagulation and thrombolysis — drugs that keep blood from clotting; used on a dissection, they are the equivalent of letting blood out through the tear. So at the hospital, proactively say "tearing pain, moving downward," so the doctor thinks of ordering a contrast CT. A large blood pressure difference between the two arms, or a pulse that cannot be felt on one side, are clues. Uncontrolled blood pressure, Marfan syndrome, or a family history of aortic aneurysm or dissection raise the risk. Likewise do not drive yourself, and do not endure it until the next day.

### 9. A sudden "worst headache of your life" that reaches peak pain within an hour: go to the emergency room immediately for a head CT
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: The emergency visit itself costs little. A head CT in the ER costs a few hundred yuan.
- In plain terms: The key is not how much it hurts, but how fast it reaches the worst. Among 2,131 people with a severe headache peaking within an hour, 6.2% had a burst vessel on the surface of the brain. Over 40, neck pain or stiffness, someone saw you pass out, onset during exertion, an instant explosion of pain, or a stiff neck on bending forward: hit any one of these and go to the ER for a head CT. Most people who go and get checked turn out fine; missing one rebleed is death.
- Benefit: Ten emergency departments in Canada ran a prospective cohort study — criteria fixed in advance, then continuous tracking — on 2,131 cases of "severe headache reaching maximal pain within one hour." Of these, 132 (6.2%) were subarachnoid hemorrhage, that is, a burst vessel on the surface of the brain. The Ottawa SAH rule has six items: age 40 or above; neck pain or stiffness; a witnessed loss of consciousness; onset during exertion; thunderclap onset, all at once; limited neck flexion on the doctor's examination. Any one item means imaging. This rule picked out every single bleeder, sensitivity 100%. The confidence range of that number is 95% CI 97.2%–100.0%. The price: among people without a bleed, only 15.3% were ruled out by it (specificity 15.3%).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Perry JJ, Stiell IG, Sivilotti MLA, et al. (2013). Clinical decision rules to rule out subarachnoid hemorrhage for acute headache. JAMA, 310(12), 1248-1255. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2013.278018>
- Notes: The key is not "how much it hurts" but "how fast it reaches the worst." A headache that explodes within seconds to a minute is the danger sign; a migraine that builds slowly is not. With vomiting, a stiff neck, light sensitivity, or brief fainting, take it even more seriously. Specificity of only 15.3% means most people who get checked under this rule turn out fine. But missing one rebleed is death, so the check is worth it. Do not swallow a painkiller and go back to sleep.

### 10. Two to three weeks to months after an elderly person bumps their head, they become unsteady, slow, sleepy, or weak on one side: get a head CT
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One head CT costs two to three hundred yuan.
- In plain terms: After an elderly person bumps their head, the blood usually pools only by the third week. At the moment of impact there can be nothing at all wrong, and the person themselves cannot recall the bump. After surgery this disease can recur at up to 33%, and mortality can reach 32%. When an old person suddenly turns confused, walks as if floating, keeps wanting to sleep, or has one weak hand, first work backward and ask whether they fell or hit their head in these past two or three months, then go get a CT.

In professional terms, a chronic space-occupying collection formed between the arachnoid and the dura mater. Among patients over 90, overall only 24% recover well. Systematic review: worldwide there are 3.39 to 39.1 new cases per 100,000 people per year, rising with age, up to threefold past 80. The most common causes are trauma and falls, followed by long-term heavy drinking, use of blood-thinning drugs (anticoagulants), and assault. A geriatrics review: the clinical presentation "typically includes confusion, cognitive decline, gait disturbance, or drowsiness, and may resemble other geriatric syndromes."
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Zhang J 等 (2021). Expert consensus on drug treatment of chronic subdural hematoma. Chinese Neurosurgical Journal, 7(1), 47. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s41016-021-00263-z> ; Dziho A 等 (2025). - Notes: At the moment of impact there can be nothing at all wrong, and the old person may not even remember the bump. So if the family notices "suddenly confused lately, floating gait, always sleepy, one hand weak," first ask backward whether there was a fall or a head knock in the past two or three months, then get the CT. Do not file it directly under "senile dementia" or "sequelae of the last stroke"; that is the one fork that causes delay. Elderly people on aspirin, clopidogrel, warfarin, or a newer oral anticoagulant are at higher risk, as are long-term heavy drinkers. For fall prevention, see Section 1, item 13 (training balance and leg strength).

### 11. One leg suddenly swells, feels tight, hurts when pressed: see a doctor promptly; add sudden breathlessness or chest pain, and call 120 immediately

- Cost: One ultrasound of the leg vessels (lower-limb venous ultrasound) costs one to two hundred yuan.
- In plain terms: One leg suddenly swollen, a size thicker than the other, painful when pressed: get a leg venous ultrasound promptly. Add sudden breathlessness, chest pain on deep breaths, racing heart, or dizziness, and the clot may already have traveled to the lungs: call 120 directly. 17.4% of people with confirmed pulmonary embolism die within 3 months. Sitting over 4 hours roughly doubles the risk, but spread over any one person it stays low.
- Benefit: The International Cooperative Pulmonary Embolism Registry (ICOPER) collected 2,454 confirmed cases of acute pulmonary embolism from 52 hospitals in 7 countries. Pulmonary embolism is a clot blocking the pulmonary artery. All-cause mortality at 3 months in this group was 17.4% (426/2454). The World Health Organization WRIGHT project: travel of more than 4 hours continuously "approximately doubles" the risk of venous thromboembolism — a clot forming in a vein and traveling to the lungs. But the probability per person remains low: after over 4 hours of motionless sitting, "about one in six thousand."
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Goldhaber SZ, Visani L, De Rosa M (1999). Acute pulmonary embolism: clinical outcomes in the International Cooperative Pulmonary Embolism Registry (ICOPER). Lancet, 353(9162), 1386-1389. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(98)07534-5> ; WHO (2007). - Notes: Both legs swelling together is mostly a heart, liver, kidney, or venous insufficiency problem. One leg suddenly swollen, a size thicker than the other, is the typical picture of deep vein thrombosis. High-risk situations: long car or plane rides, bed rest after surgery or a fracture, a cast, around pregnancy and childbirth, cancer, oral contraceptives. A clot breaking off and blocking the pulmonary artery is a pulmonary embolism: sudden breathlessness, chest pain worse on a deep breath, racing heart, dizziness, coughing up blood (hemoptysis). At that stage call 120 directly. The 17.4% is the death rate among confirmed pulmonary embolism cases, not "a swollen leg carries that much risk" — do not frighten yourself with that number.

### 12. For major bleeding, first press the wound hard with your hand; if a limb cannot be controlled, apply a tourniquet; and call 120 at the same time
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A windlass tourniquet costs a few dozen to over a hundred yuan; keep one in the car and in the first-aid kit. Learning once "place above the wound, twist until bleeding stops, note the time" takes a few minutes.
- In plain terms: The order is: first press hard with your hand or clothing; if a limb cannot be controlled, apply a tourniquet; then call 120, and once it is on, do not loosen it. In battlefield data, those who got a tourniquet before shock set in survived 90%; after shock, only 10%; of the 5 who needed one and did not get it, all died. Place it above the wound, twist until the bleeding stops, and write down the time. Do not substitute shoelaces or string. Neck and torso: compression only, never a tourniquet.

A Baghdad battlefield hospital's 232 casualties: those tourniqueted before shock appeared survived 90%; after shock, only 10%. All 5 casualties who needed a tourniquet and did not get one died; those who got one survived 87%. ### 13. Bitten or scratched by a dog or cat: first alternate rinsing with soapy water and running water for 15 minutes, and get the vaccine the same day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Rinsing is free. A full rabies vaccine course costs a few hundred yuan. Category III exposure also requires rabies passive immunization — ready-made antibodies injected directly to cover you until your own develop — a few hundred to over a thousand yuan. The most expensive thing is delay.
- In plain terms: Once rabies breaks out, the death rate is nearly 100%; rinsing plus the vaccine is the only way. First alternate rinsing with soapy water and running water for 15 minutes, then get the vaccine the same day. A bite through the skin, or a broken-skin spot being licked, is category III exposure and requires passive immunization as well. "It's a family pet, it's been vaccinated" does not change the treatment; the level is set by the wound, not by the dog's pedigree.
- Benefit: Original text of the 2023 standard from the National Disease Control and Prevention Administration and the National Health Commission: "Rabies case fatality is almost 100%." The treatment: "thoroughly rinse all bite and scratch wounds for about 15 minutes, alternating soapy water (or another weakly alkaline cleanser) with running water under some pressure." A light bite on uncovered skin, or a scratch without bleeding, is category II exposure: vaccine required. A penetrating bite, or broken skin being licked, is category III exposure: "the wound should be treated and rabies passive immunization and rabies vaccine administered." WHO: about 59,000 people die of rabies worldwide each year, 99% transmitted by dogs.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家疾控局综合司、国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2023). 关于印发狂犬病暴露预防处置工作规范（2023 年版）的通知. <https://www.ndcpa.gov.cn/jbkzzx/c100014/common/content/content_1706569159854649344.html> ; WHO. 狂犬病 实况报道. <https://www.who.int/zh/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/rabies>
- Notes: Do not skip treatment because "it's a family pet, it's been vaccinated." The standard grades the wound itself (exposure level), not the dog's background. Do not bandage the wound yourself. ### 14. After a burn, immediately rinse with cool running water for 20 minutes; do not apply toothpaste or soy sauce
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. Takes 20 minutes.
- In plain terms: Rinsing with cool running water for a full 20 minutes within 3 hours of the injury cuts the later chance of needing a skin graft to roughly 60% of not rinsing. Cool water, not ice water; icing worsens the injury. Do not tear clothing stuck to the wound; after rinsing, cover with a clean cloth and go to the hospital. No toothpaste, soy sauce, sesame oil, or flour. If you only remember later, a late rinse within 3 hours still helps.
- Benefit: Australia tracked 2,495 children with burns. For children rinsed with cool running water for 20 minutes within 3 hours of injury, the odds ratio for later needing a skin graft was 0.6. The confidence range of that number is 95% CI 0.4–0.8. That is, roughly 60% of those not rinsed this way. Pooling 7 studies gave the same finding: "20 minutes of cool running water within three hours of injury significantly reduced the likelihood of needing skin grafting and surgical wound treatment."
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Griffin BR, Frear CC, Babl F, Oakley E, Kimble RM (2020). - Notes: Cool water, not ice water; icing worsens the injury. If clothing is stuck to the wound, do not force it off; after rinsing, cover with a clean cloth and go to the hospital. ### 15. Sudden full-body rash with breathlessness or dizziness: treat as anaphylactic shock, call 120 immediately and say so clearly
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. For people with a known severe allergy, keeping an epinephrine auto-injector costs money, and it is a prescription drug.
- In plain terms: Sudden rash over the whole body plus breathlessness or dizziness: handle it as anaphylactic shock. Call 120 immediately and say straight out "suspected anaphylactic shock" so the ambulance brings epinephrine. Epinephrine is the first-choice drug; antihistamines and steroids act slowly and cannot fill that role. If it has happened once, ask a doctor to prescribe an auto-injector; inject into the outer mid-thigh, through clothing if needed.
- Benefit: Epinephrine is the first-line drug for grade II and above anaphylactic shock, and the guidelines list it as a strong recommendation. Antihistamines and steroids act slowly and cannot fill its role.
- Evidence grade: B (strong guideline recommendation, but the underlying evidence is low-grade)
- Sources: 中国医药教育协会等 (2022). 过敏性休克急救临床实践指南（2020）. ### 16. Someone convulsing on the ground: clear the area around them, turn them on their side, watch the clock — and put nothing in their mouth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free.
- In plain terms: Move hard objects away from around them, turn them on their side with the mouth down, and time the seizure. Do not pin them down, do not pry the mouth open, do not put anything in the mouth — that only injures teeth and jaw. Call an ambulance if it lasts over 5 minutes, if another seizure comes quickly after one ends, if they will not wake or have trouble breathing after it, if it happened in water, or if it is the first-ever seizure. Give water only after they are fully awake.
- Benefit: Avoids tooth and jaw injuries from prying the mouth open and stuffing in hard objects. Avoids fractures from forcibly pinning the person down. Over 5 minutes without stopping is the red line that requires calling an ambulance.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 美国疾病控制与预防中心. 癫痫发作急救：「Don't hold the person down or stop their movements」「Don't put anything in their mouth. - Notes: The same page lists further situations requiring an immediate ambulance call: another seizure soon after one ends; not waking or difficulty breathing after the seizure; injury during the seizure; a seizure in water; the first-ever seizure. ### 17. A diabetic suddenly trembling, sweating cold, slurring speech: give 15 g of sugar first, retest after 15 minutes

### 20. Swallowed cleaner, pesticide, or medicine: do not induce vomiting first; bring the bottle and seek care immediately; splash to eye or skin — flush with large amounts of water for 15 minutes
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. Store cleaners, disinfectants, pesticides, and medicines on high shelves children cannot reach, or in a locked cabinet. Never decant them into drink bottles.
- In plain terms: If cleaner, pesticide, or medicine is swallowed, do not induce vomiting — a corrosive liquid coming back up burns the esophagus a second time. First drink a suitable amount of milk or egg white, bring the bottle and instructions, and go to the hospital immediately. If it splashed onto skin or into eyes, flush with large amounts of water for a full 15 minutes; for eyes, hold the eyelids open while rinsing and do not rub. Mixing disinfectant with toilet-bowl cleaner produces chlorine gas.
- Benefit: The China CDC's National Institute for Occupational Health and Poison Control gives guidance in four cases. Swallowed (ingestion): immediately drink a suitable amount of milk or egg white; "remember, do not induce vomiting." Seek care as soon as possible, bringing the packaging or instructions, "so the doctor can quickly identify the ingredient and work out a treatment plan." Breathed in (inhalation): immediately move to a well-ventilated area and loosen the collar to keep the airway clear; with severe coughing, rapid breathing, or breathing difficulty, seek care immediately. Skin contact: immediately rinse the contact area with large amounts of water for at least 15 minutes, taking care not to let the rinse splash into the eyes or onto other intact skin. Eye contact: immediately rinse the eyes with large amounts of water or saline for at least 15 minutes. Hold the eyelids open while rinsing so the water fully reaches the eyeball and the inside of the lids; "never rub the eyes." Go straight to ophthalmology after rinsing. Recognize high-risk products by acidity (pH). pH<3: toilet-bowl cleaners, descalers, floor cleaners, stainless-steel polishes. Some heavy-duty drain openers also contain dilute sulfuric acid. pH>11: drain openers, heavy-duty kitchen degreasers, dishwasher powder, oxygen bleach ("explosion salt"), stain-removing powder. One combination that injures on the spot: disinfectant used together with an acid toilet cleaner releases chlorine gas, which irritates the airways, causes severe coughing and rapid breathing, and can even be life-threatening.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 中国疾病预防控制中心职业卫生与中毒控制所 (2025). 暑假安全提示：警惕强酸强碱清洁剂. <https://niohp.chinacdc.cn/kpdw/202507/t20250722_308659.htm>
- Notes: Graded B because the official guidance explains only how to handle and how to recognize, without numbers on "how much harm following it prevents." Why no vomiting: a corrosive liquid coming back up burns the esophagus (the tube from throat to stomach) a second time. If you are not sure what was swallowed, bring the bottle and instructions, along with any vomit, to the hospital. Prevention is far cheaper than first aid: store by category, never mixed with food or drinks, tighten caps and discard empty bottles after use, and never pour any of it into a water bottle. Wear rubber gloves and goggles when using them, open windows for ventilation, and never mix different types. Carbon monoxide: see item 19; burns: see item 14.

### 21. Acid, alkali, or other chemicals splashed on the body: immediately strip contaminated clothing and flush with large amounts of running water; hold eyelids open for eyes; rinse the full time before leaving
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. On your first day on the job, note where the eyewash stations and safety showers are; do not go looking when something happens.
- In plain terms: Three steps. Step one: immediately take off the soaked clothing, shoes, and socks; wherever the fabric held the chemical against the skin is hurt worst. Step two: flush with large amounts of running water, at least 15 minutes for both skin and eyes; the Hunan Provincial Emergency Management Department gives 20 to 30 minutes — go by the longer one. Step three: for eyes, hold the eyelids open while rinsing, and go straight to ophthalmology afterward. Use room-temperature water, never hot.
- Benefit: Three steps on site. Step one: immediately remove contaminated clothing, shoes, and socks; the poison keeps absorbing through the skin, and the parts held by fabric are hurt worst. Step two: flush with large amounts of running water. The floor given by the China CDC's National Institute for Occupational Health and Poison Control is "at least 15 minutes" each for skin and eyes. ### 22. In extreme heat: dizziness, nausea, no sweating, or altered consciousness — move to shade at once, strip clothing, douse with water; if not fully conscious, give no water and call 120

### 23. A heat-illness person who is fully conscious should sip cool lightly salted water or a sports drink little and often; no gulping, no alcohol or sugary drinks
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->

### 25. Seeing someone drowning: first shout for people and call 110/120, extend a pole, throw flotation — do not go into the water yourself

### 30. In an earthquake, first look at what building you are in: in a building built to seismic code, take shelter where you are; in an old building that may collapse, go out if the door is a few steps away
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. Look in advance at how many steps your front door takes, which route, and which table is sturdy.
- In plain terms: First look at what building you are in. In buildings built to code after 2001, where getting out means stairs or a lobby, shelter in place: protect head and neck under a sturdy table and hold the table leg. In single-story houses, old brick buildings, and self-built houses with no seismic design, if the door is a few steps away, go out. In no case jump from a building, take the elevator, or go down stairs while the shaking is on.
- Benefit: The China Earthquake Administration's educational film "An earthquake hits: shelter or run?" gives the rule "shelter nearby during the shaking, evacuate quickly after," and states its limit explicitly: "this refers to being inside a building that meets seismic design requirements." To shelter: "protect the head and neck with your hands or something soft" "and hold the table leg firmly"; without a table, "crouch quickly against a load-bearing wall" "avoiding exterior walls, windows, and balconies." ### 33. Going to altitude: ascend slowly by sleep altitude (how high you sleep that night); descend when symptoms worsen; do not push through
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: An extra one to two days for acclimatization. Preventive drugs are prescription drugs; ask a doctor before departure. The hard part is leaving those days open even on a tight itinerary.
- In plain terms: What counts is how high you sleep, not how high you got during the day. Sleeping above 2,450 meters carries altitude illness risk. Past 3,000 meters, raise the night's sleeping altitude by only 500 meters at a time, and add a spare acclimatization day for every 1,000 meters gained. Headache plus nausea, a staggering gait, changes in consciousness, or breathlessness at rest: go down. Descending comes before oxygen and medication.
- Benefit: Sleeping above 2,450 meters carries altitude illness risk. High-altitude cerebral edema and pulmonary edema — fluid in the brain and lungs — kill if you do not descend to lower ground in time.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 美国疾病控制与预防中心. 黄皮书·高海拔旅行与高原病：「Any unacclimatized traveler proceeding to a sleeping altitude of ≥2,450 m (≥8,000 ft)—and sometimes lower—is at risk for altitude illness」；上升速度「500 m (1,650 ft) per night in sleeping altitude once above 3,000 m (9,800 ft)」，每上升 1000 米加一天适应日；乙酰唑胺预防「125 mg twice a day; 250 mg twice a day if >100 kg body weight」；「If symptoms become worse despite rest or treatment at the same elevation」必须下撤. <https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/environmental-hazards-risks/high-altitude-travel-and-altitude-illness.html>；荒野医学会 (2024). 急性高原病预防、诊断与治疗临床实践指南（2024 更新）. Wilderness & Environmental Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wem.2023.05.013>
- Notes: What counts is "how high you sleep," not how high you got during the day. Headache plus nausea, a staggering gait, changes in consciousness, or breathlessness at rest are all signals to descend. Descending takes priority over oxygen and medication; continuing upward while breathing oxygen is the wrong move.

### 34. A tick embedded in the skin: pull it out steadily with fine-tipped tweezers flush to the skin; no oil, no burning
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A pair of fine-tipped tweezers.
- In plain terms: Grip the tick's head with fine-tipped tweezers right against the skin and pull steadily outward. Do not coat it with oil, do not burn it, do not twist it out — that makes it squeeze pathogen-laden fluids into the skin, and the mouthparts easily stay in the flesh. Note the date after removal. If fever, rash, or fatigue shows up in the following weeks, tell the doctor proactively that you were bitten by a tick.
- Benefit: Oiling, burning, and twisting irritate the tick, making it squeeze pathogen-laden fluids into the skin. These methods also tend to leave the embedded mouthparts (the hypostome) inside.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 美国疾病控制与预防中心. 被蜱叮咬之后：「Grasp the tick as close to the skin's surface as possible using clean fine-tipped tweezers」「Pull tick away from the skin with steady, even pressure」「Do not use petroleum jelly, heat, nail polish, or other substances to try and make the tick detach from the skin. This may agitate the tick and force infected fluid from the tick into the skin」. <https://www.cdc.gov/ticks/after-a-tick-bite/index.html>
- Notes: Write down the date after removal. If fever, rash, or fatigue appears in the following weeks, tell the doctor you were bitten by a tick. If the doctor does not ask and you do not say, it is easily treated as a cold. ### 35. Any water in the wild: boil before drinking; a rolling boil for one minute is enough, no extra boiling for high altitude
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Carrying the extra weight of a stove and fuel.
- In plain terms: Every microorganism that can make you sick through drinking water dies within seconds at boiling temperature. One more minute of rolling boil is only margin for a misjudged eye, not something to extend because of altitude. If you cannot boil, use a filter or purification tablets; each leaves some pathogens uncovered, so boil when you can. Clear-looking streams and snowmelt can carry parasites just the same.
- Benefit: Every microorganism that can cause disease through drinking water is killed (inactivated) within seconds at boiling temperature. "Water at high altitude does not boil hot enough" is a common misconception.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 美国疾病控制与预防中心. 黄皮书·旅行者的水消毒：「All organisms that may cause illness from drinking water are killed within seconds at boiling temperature」「CDC recommends that travelers boil water for a full minute to account for user variability in identifying boiling points and to add a margin of safety」「Although the boiling point for water decreases with increasing elevation, at common travel elevations the temperature needed to achieve boiling is still well above the temperature required to inactivate enteric pathogens」. <https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/preparing-international-travelers/water-disinfection-for-travelers.html>
- Notes: The same page also notes you need not truly bring it to a boil: holding 60°C for 30 minutes is just as effective; boiling is simply the endpoint you can see without a thermometer. If you cannot boil, use a filter or chemical tablets; the two each leave some pathogens uncovered, so boil when you can. Clear-looking streams and snowmelt can still carry parasites; do not judge by appearance.

### 36. A stranger demands money in the middle of nowhere: hand it over, do not fight, note the features, and call the police once you are safe
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: The cash on you. The hard part is swallowing the anger.
- In plain terms: Give the money, do not fight, do not talk back. For a few hundred yuan the other side is risking robbery: starting at 3 to 10 years, and over 10 years up to the death penalty for home invasion, use of a gun, or causing serious injury or death. Once he takes the money and turns to go, if you chase and strike, it no longer counts as lawful self-defense. Once you are safe, immediately note appearance, accent, plate number, and direction of flight, and call 110.
- Benefit: Life is dearer than money. For a few hundred yuan, the other side is risking a felony. Article 263 of the Criminal Law sets it out: robbing public or private property by violence, coercion, or other means carries 3 to 10 years of fixed-term imprisonment plus a fine. With a gun, home invasion, or causing serious injury or death, the sentence is over 10 years up to the death penalty. Lawful self-defense under Article 20 protects only "stopping an unlawful attack in progress." If he took the money and turned to leave, chasing him to strike is no longer defense. Once safe, immediately note appearance, accent, plate number, and direction of flight, and call 110.
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020 年修正). 刑法（第二十条、第二百六十三条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>（北京市公安交管局转载整合文本）
- Notes: The C grade is because "complying rather than resisting is safer" has no direct literature; the statutes only describe his risk and your boundary. Do not follow to a secluded spot "to withdraw money." Do not hand over phone passwords. Do not get into his car. This entry is not a lesson in fighting, nor in skipping the police. It covers being cornered face to face with your physical safety threatened, so hand over the money and get away first. Online blackmail with private photos or sextortion recordings is the reverse: not a cent, ever; see Section 8, item 32 (when the other side holds leverage and demands money).

### 37. Stumbling on a group fight: back off and leave; do not break it up, do not rubberneck, do not pick up anything off the ground; to report, call 110 from a safe distance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is resisting two impulses: stepping in to pull someone out, and standing around to watch. After reporting you may be called back once to give a statement, costing at most half a day.
- In plain terms: The moment you lay hands, your role changes from peacemaker to participant. Joining a group brawl carries up to 3 years; with large numbers, on the road, or with weapons, 3 to 10 years. Watching is also a bad deal: the more people, the more chaotic the scene, and stray blows land easily. The action is to back off and leave. If you are willing to report, withdraw to a safe distance and call 110, stating clearly the location, how many people, whether knives or clubs are involved, and whether anyone is lying motionless.
- Benefit: Article 292 of the Criminal Law: "For assembling a crowd to brawl, ringleaders and other active participants are sentenced to up to three years of fixed-term imprisonment, criminal detention, or public surveillance." For repeat brawls, large numbers and scale with vile social impact, serious disorder in public places or on key traffic arteries, or possession of weapons, "the sentence is three to ten years of fixed-term imprisonment." "Where crowd brawling causes serious injury or death," conviction and punishment follow the crimes of intentional injury and intentional homicide. The Public Security Administration Punishments Law also has penalties: assault means 5 to 10 days of detention plus a fine of 500 to 1,000 yuan. Group fighting is handled as picking quarrels and provoking trouble, with up to 15 days of detention plus a fine of 2,000 yuan. The Guiding Opinions on the Lawful Application of the Lawful Self-Defense System, Article 5: an unlawful attack "covers attacks against oneself as well as those endangering the state or public interests or directed at others." Article 9 of the same document: where one side strikes first with clearly excessive means, or strikes first and keeps attacking while the other party is trying hard to avoid conflict, the counterattacking side should generally be recognized as acting in defense.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百三十二、二百三十四、二百九十二、三百零五、三百一十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第三十、五十一条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部 (2020). 关于依法适用正当防卫制度的指导意见（法发〔2020〕31 号，第 5、9 条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/251611.html>；全国人大 (2018). 刑事诉讼法（2018 年修订，第六十二、一百一十、一百一十一、一百九十三条）. 宁夏回族自治区人民检察院转载全文 <https://www.nx.jcy.gov.cn/zwsy/qwfb/202011/t20201119_608817.html>
- Notes: Graded B because the consequences half has hard statutory text, while "back off, report, do not step in to break it up" has no official text; it is inferred from the statutes and from how "mutual brawling" is determined. "I was only breaking it up" is no talisman; the determination looks at whether you laid hands and on whom. If the other side is already down or has run, chasing to strike is no longer defense. If you see someone being beaten unilaterally by a crowd, you can indeed legally intervene: lawful self-defense protects not only yourself but others. But going in bare-handed usually just adds one more casualty. Shouting for people, filming from a distance, and calling the police work better. The sure win in this entry is "no hands, no rubbernecking." The benefit of the 110 call falls mainly on the person on the ground; it is the lowest-benefit tier in this book, and whether to spend that time is your own trade-off. To weigh it, know a few things. The duty to testify already exists: Article 62 of the Criminal Procedure Law states that everyone who knows about a case has a duty to testify, and it does not disappear because you skip the call. With serious injury or a death, police pulling camera footage will still find who was present; standing around watching is what gets you registered most easily. A reporting person who does not want their name public: the handling organ "shall keep it confidential" (Article 111). State the location, the number of people, and whether weapons are involved, then you may hang up; you need not stay on the spot. Reaching an admonishment or detention under 10 days requires the court to summon you to testify and your refusing without a legitimate reason (Article 193) — a bystander almost never reaches that step. Not calling the police is not itself illegal: the "rights and obligations" phrase in Article 110 of the Criminal Procedure Law carries no penalty, and the Criminal Law has no offense of a private person failing to report knowledge; walking away is a lawful option. After walking away, there are only two red lines. One: afterward hiding the person or giving false testimony to cover for them (Article 310 of the Criminal Law, up to 3 years, or 3 to 10 years if serious). Two: deliberately giving false testimony when asked to testify (Article 305, up to 3 years, or 3 to 7 years if serious). Silence and truthful statements are both fine; making things up is what carries consequences. Being dragged into a conflict yourself: see Section 8, item 10. A stranger demanding money: see item 36 in this section. Money after you are injured while rescuing: see item 39 in this section.

### 38. Possible HIV exposure: get post-exposure prophylaxis within 72 hours; the earlier the better
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A 28-day course of medication costs over a thousand yuan, out of pocket in most areas. You have to make the hospital trip that same night. The hard part is admitting on the spot that you may have been infected and leaving the house immediately.
- In plain terms: If you suspect HIV infection, 72 hours is the hard line, and ideally the drugs are taken within 2 hours. The course runs 28 days without a break. Past 72 hours this road is gone, so do not wait until tomorrow to decide.
- Benefit: The Beijing municipal government's HIV prevention science column gives the standard: prevention medication after exposure, called post-exposure prophylaxis, abbreviated PEP, should be taken "as soon as possible, ideally within 2 hours, no later than 24 hours, and never beyond 72 hours," and "taken continuously for 28 days." Starting after 72 hours is no longer recommended. It is a hard deadline: hesitating overnight and leaving on the spot lead to completely different outcomes.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 北京市人民政府. 事前、事后分不清？六问六答全面了解艾滋病病毒暴露前/暴露后预防. <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/fuwu/bmfw/jhsyfwzdzx/sjazbr/fakp/202311/t20231110_3299397.html>；中国疾病预防控制中心性病艾滋病预防控制中心 (2020). 艾滋病病毒暴露后预防技术指南（试用）. <https://www.chinacdc.cn/jkyj/crb2/yl/azb/jswj_azb/202410/t20241027_302131.html>
- Notes: Where to get it: the HIV-designated medical institution appointed by the local CDC, usually the infectious disease department (STD/AIDS clinic). Many cities now run 24-hour clinics; Chongqing's published figure is 29 clinics across 26 districts and counties, 14 of them around the clock. Before going, call the local CDC to ask which one has the drug; do not burn time in the emergency department. What counts as "exposure": unprotected sex, a broken condom, a needlestick, blood on a mucous membrane — whether to medicate is the doctor's call. The full 28 days must be taken; a few missed doses noticeably degrade the effect. Follow-up testing per medical advice at weeks 4 to 6 and at 3 months. This is a remedy, not a substitute; everyday prevention and testing are in Section 1, items 30 and 31 (condoms every time; if a high-risk act happened, go get tested).

### 39. Injured or out of pocket from rescuing someone: first go after the wrongdoer and basic medical insurance, then apply for confirmation as an act of courageous assistance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Applying means submitting materials, waiting for confirmation, several trips, weeks to months.
- In plain terms: Being hurt while rescuing someone does not mean eating the loss alone. If you are injured in a courageous act, treatment comes first and billing after. If there is a wrongdoer, the wrongdoer pays the medical bills; if they fled or cannot pay, it goes through basic medical insurance. Where there is no tortfeasor, the person you saved "shall" give appropriate compensation — the legal word, not a matter of their conscience. At the same time, apply for confirmation as an act of courageous assistance; if you were injured, benefits are settled as work-injury insurance.
- Benefit: Article 183 of the Civil Code: "Where a person suffers harm to themselves in protecting another's civil rights and interests, the tortfeasor bears civil liability, and the beneficiary may give appropriate compensation. Where there is no tortfeasor, the tortfeasor fled, or the tortfeasor cannot bear civil liability, and the injured person requests compensation, the beneficiary shall give appropriate compensation." Guobanfa [2012] No. 39 covers what happens to people injured in courageous acts. Medical institutions must open a green channel, "adhering to the principle of 'treatment first, payment after.'" The original text on medical costs: "Medical expenses arising from emergency treatment shall be borne by the perpetrator or responsible party where one exists; where there is none, or the perpetrator or responsible party has fled or cannot pay, they are resolved through the basic medical security system as stipulated." For deaths: "where the conditions for martyr status are met, be posthumously recognized as a martyr according to law"; "where it falls under circumstances regarded as a work injury, enjoy the one-time work-death benefit and a special survivor's subsidy equal to 40 months of the person's own wages." For disability: "where the conditions for work-injury insurance benefits are met, implement the corresponding benefits under the Regulations on Work-Related Injury Insurance; where not met, the civil affairs department assesses the disability grade and implements the corresponding benefits." On employment, "give priority in employment assistance and key support," with public-welfare posts "giving priority to eligible people honored for courageous acts." On housing, "give priority inclusion in the housing security system, with priority allocation of government-subsidized housing for rent or sale, or issuance of housing rental subsidies."

Generally you apply to the public security organ where it happened or to the courageous-assistance body (foundation). Confirmation depends on evidence: the police report receipt, scene camera footage, witnesses' names and phone numbers, hospital records — keep them from the start. ### 40. A knife, rebar, or glass embedded in the body: press around the object to stop the bleeding; do not pull it out
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is suppressing the "pull it out first" instinct and staying with the person while waiting for the ambulance.
- In plain terms: Pulling it out can cause massive bleeding, and it makes it harder for the doctor to judge how deep the damage goes. The method: call 120, press with hands or dressings around the object to stop the bleeding, and pad the object steady so it cannot wobble. For an abdominal wound with organs protruding, do not push them back in; cover with a clean, moist dressing. For a chest wound that bubbles, do not seal it with plastic sheeting; sealing it can crush the lung flat.
- Benefit: The International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies' 2025 International First Aid Guidelines: "First aiders should press around an embedded object (such as a knife) and stabilize it; do not remove it." "Do not remove any embedded object from an abdominal or chest wound; removing it may cause massive bleeding and make it harder for the doctor to judge how bad the injury is." "An embedded object should be stabilized; if it moves with the pulse, let it move, stabilizing it loosely." "First aiders should not push protruding organs back into the body." An open chest-wall wound that is not welling blood outward "can be left uncovered and undressed, because sealing the wound may trap air in the chest and make the lung partially or completely collapse (pneumothorax)." All penetrating chest and abdominal wounds call for emergency services immediately.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies (2025). International First Aid, Resuscitation and Education Guidelines 2025，第 256–257、267–268 页. <https://www.ifrc.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/IFRC%20International%20First%20Aid,%20Resuscitation%20and%20Education%20Guidelines%202025.pdf>
- Notes: Graded B because it is accepted practice in guidelines without randomized trials — you cannot split the injured into "pull" and "do not pull" groups to test it. The only exception is an object blocking the airway so the person cannot breathe. At the hospital, go straight to the triage desk at the ER entrance and say "penetrating injury." That is the tier that goes into the resuscitation room immediately; do not queue at the registration window (see Section 24, item 8). If it truly went through, call 120; the ambulance will notify the hospital ahead to prepare. How to press major bleeding and apply a tourniquet: see item 12.

### 41. If a fracture is suspected, stop moving that body part; support it still, ice for no more than 20 minutes, and do not straighten it yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. Find a rigid board, a rolled magazine, or clothing to use as support. Ice through a layer of cloth.
- In plain terms: Treat any injured hand or foot as a fracture first; being able to stand or move rules nothing out, and only an X-ray can settle it. Keep the injured part still, support it with your hand, or brace it in its original position with a triangular bandage or rigid board. Ice early, no more than 20 minutes at a time. Do not straighten it yourself. If broken bone has pierced the skin, stop the bleeding first and cover with a clean dressing; do not press the bone back in.
- Benefit: The International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies issued the 2025 International First Aid Guidelines. It states that "any injury to a hand or foot should first be treated as a possible fracture." It also states that "the only accurate way to confirm a fracture is an X-ray. If in doubt, treat it as a fracture." Immobilization aims to "minimize movement and pain." Icing: "for a maximum of 20 continuous minutes at a time, which relieves pain; colder for longer than this may damage the skin." "Injuries to long bones such as the femur can be fatal from the blood lost by the bone alone." Call emergency services when pain or swelling is marked, when there are signs of shock, for a femoral (thigh bone) fracture, or when the limb is visibly out of position. For a fracture bent at an angle, whether to straighten it on the spot (reduction) is considered only if two conditions hold together. First: "a remote location (or limited conditions), and the limb below the angle is cold or pale, or the rescuer cannot immobilize it well enough for transport." Second: the rescuer is trained. For open fractures (bone piercing the skin), the priorities are stopping bleeding and preventing infection.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies (2025). International First Aid, Resuscitation and Education Guidelines 2025，第 301–302 页. <https://www.ifrc.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/IFRC%20International%20First%20Aid,%20Resuscitation%20and%20Education%20Guidelines%202025.pdf>
- Notes: Graded B because these are accepted practices. Pre-hospital traction splinting for femoral fractures rests on a single records-review study; its result was less blood transfused within 24 hours and shorter hospital stays, with differences in deaths and complications too small to call. That study's evidence is of very low credibility. As for reducing first (setting the bone back) versus immobilizing as found, no evidence was found at all. Three common misconceptions to drop: being able to stand and walk does not mean no fracture; use cold only, not heat, and no alternating hot and cold; a sprain does not get better by "walking it off" — rest first. For moving prohibitions when an elderly faller may have a fracture, see item 2. One leg swelling after a cast or bed rest — watch for a clot, see item 11. After treatment, whether to seek a disability assessment or a disability certificate: see Section 24, items 10 and 11.
