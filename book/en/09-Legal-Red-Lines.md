# 9. Legal red lines that ordinary people easily trip over

This chapter's yardstick is personal freedom and money: the things below are ones ordinary people often assume are "no big deal," yet they can actually lead to detention, fines, or a prison sentence. Each entry gives the specific numbers from the statute and cases published through official channels. The Public Security Administration Punishments Law (治安管理处罚法) is cited from the 2025 revision (in force from January 1, 2026; article numbers differ from the old law); the Criminal Law is cited from the consolidated text "as amended by Criminal Law Amendment XI" republished by the Beijing Municipal Public Security Bureau's traffic administration department, with the corresponding amendment noted. Entries are ordered from highest to lowest by "how easy to trip over unknowingly × how severe the consequences."
### 1. Do not forward disaster, epidemic, or police-incident messages of unknown authenticity in group chats; do not photoshop or use AI to fake scene images
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; before forwarding, glance at whether it is an official announcement; if unsure, don't repost, don't alter images, don't add "rumor has it"
- In plain terms: forwarding a disaster, epidemic, or police-incident message you haven't verified yourself can get you detained 5 to 10 days and fined 1,000 yuan even if you didn't make it up; if it disrupts social order you get under 3 years, and 3 to 7 years if the consequences are serious. Photoshopped and AI-generated fake scene images count the same as making it up yourself.
- Benefit: deliberately spreading rumors — falsely reporting danger, epidemic, disaster, or police incidents to disrupt public order — means 5 to 10 days of detention plus a fine of up to 1,000 yuan, or under 5 days of detention or a fine of up to 1,000 yuan for lesser cases; fabricating false danger, epidemic, disaster, or police-incident information and spreading it online, or knowingly spreading it while aware it is false, in a way that seriously disrupts social order, means under 3 years, or 3 to 7 years for serious consequences (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第二十九条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2015). 刑法修正案（九）（第三十二条，即刑法第二百九十一条之一第二款）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180205_364562.shtml>；全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百九十一条之一）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>（北京市公安交通管理局转载）
- Notes: "knowingly forwarding something false" and "making it up yourself" fall under the same provision — forwarding is not an exemption; photoshopped and AI-generated fake scene images likewise count as fabrication. The Ministry of Public Security published several batches of typical online-rumor cases involving disasters and dangerous situations in 2025; its website could not be opened while this section was written, so no specific case is cited.

### 2. Do not post or forward images, memes, or comments insulting heroes and martyrs; do not joke about the national flag or anthem
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; content involving heroes and martyrs, the national flag, the national emblem, or the national anthem — no commenting, no altering images, no forwarding
- In plain terms: joking about heroes and martyrs, the national flag, or the national anthem means 5 to 10 days of detention or a 1,000–3,000 yuan fine in light cases, 10 to 15 days of detention in more serious cases, and under 3 years where a crime is formed. One person posted two messages denigrating Yuan Longping in a 499-member village group chat and got 6 months of controlled surveillance; another used a Weibo account with over 2.5 million followers to post 2 items distorting the deeds of border troops and got 8 months, plus a public apology in national media.
- Benefit: insulting, defaming, or otherwise infringing the name, likeness, reputation, or honor of heroes and martyrs to the detriment of the public interest, or desecrating or denying the deeds and spirit of heroes and martyrs, or making and spreading statements, images, or audio-video material glorifying wars of aggression in a way that disrupts public order — 5 to 10 days of detention or a fine of 1,000 to 3,000 yuan, or 10 to 15 days of detention plus a fine of up to 5,000 yuan in more serious cases; where the circumstances are serious it constitutes a crime, punishable by under 3 years; publicly insulting the national flag or national emblem, or altering the lyrics or score of the national anthem, or playing or singing it in a distorted or demeaning way in serious cases — under 3 years; official cases: Xiao posted 2 messages denigrating and insulting Yuan Longping in a 499-member village WeChat group and was sentenced to 6 months of controlled surveillance; Qiu used a Weibo account with over 2.5 million followers to post 2 items distorting the deeds of troops defending the border and was sentenced to 8 months with a public apology in national media (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第三十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2018). 英雄烈士保护法（第二十二、二十六条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/gyssshmhsh/201912/t20191202_440079.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2020). 刑法修正案（十一）（第三十五条，即刑法第二百九十九条之一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/202012/t20201227_503682.shtml>；全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百九十九条、第二百九十九条之一）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院 (2022). 涉英烈权益保护十大典型案例（案例二、案例三）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/382301.html>
- Notes: this provision of the public security law was only added in the 2025 revision; before that only the criminal law and civil public-interest litigation applied. A WeChat group of a few hundred people counts as a public place in the courts' eyes.

### 3. Close foreign websites once you've finished reading; do not screenshot, forward, or move content into group chats, and do not chime in below in comment sections
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; once the page is closed, the matter is closed — no archiving, no forwarding, no moving to domestic platforms, no long-term posting of political content on foreign platforms under an account that can be tied to your identity, and no accepting honoraria or funding from foreign institutions, organizations, or individuals
- In plain terms: the law punishes spreading outward, not reading — with one exception: books, audio-video material, and items promoting terrorism or extremism, if knowingly kept in your possession with serious circumstances, are enough to convict on possession alone, under 3 years. For everything else: screenshots, forwarding, moving into groups, and echoing it in comment sections all count as dissemination. At the level of inciting subversion or inciting secession, it's under 5 years, 5 years or more for ringleaders or major offenses, plus 1 to 5 years of deprivation of political rights as an accessory penalty and possible confiscation of property; providing state secrets or intelligence to foreign institutions starts at 5 to 10 years. Forwarding it earns you nothing in return.
- Benefit: inciting subversion of state power or overthrow of the socialist system through rumor-mongering, defamation, or other means — under 5 years, 5 years or more for ringleaders or those whose crimes are grave (statutory maximum of 15 years); inciting secession or undermining national unity is the same tier; collaborating with foreign institutions, organizations, or individuals on either offense brings heavier punishment; where domestic or foreign institutions, organizations, or individuals fund others to commit them, directly responsible persons get under 5 years, 5 years or more in serious cases; stealing, spying out, buying, or illegally supplying state secrets or intelligence for foreign institutions, organizations, or personnel — 5 to 10 years, 10 years or more or life imprisonment in especially serious cases, under 5 years in lesser cases; crimes endangering national security always carry 1 to 5 years of deprivation of political rights as an accessory penalty, and confiscation of property may be added; at the administrative level, no individual or organization may use the internet to endanger national security, honor, or interests, to incite subversion of state power or overthrow of the socialist system, or to incite secession or undermine national unity, and publishing or transmitting such information is punished under relevant laws and administrative regulations (nationwide); one category exists without any dissemination: knowingly and illegally possessing books, audio-video material, or other items promoting terrorism or extremism, where circumstances are serious — up to 3 years of fixed-term imprisonment, criminal detention, or controlled surveillance, with a fine imposed concurrently or separately (Criminal Law Article 120-6, added by Criminal Law Amendment IX)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第四十五、五十五、五十六、一百零三、一百零五、一百零六、一百零七、一百一十一、一百一十三条，这几条未经修正案修改）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 网络安全法（2025 年修正，2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第十三条第二款、第七十一条第一项）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-12/29/c_1768735112911946.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2015). 刑法修正案（九）（新增第一百二十条之六）. 国家信访局转载全文 <https://www.gjxfj.gov.cn/gjxfj/fgwj/flfg/webinfo/2017/03/1601761496614423.htm>
- Notes: the provisions cited here punish "inciting," "disseminating," and "supplying," not "reading," but don't extend that to all content: terrorism and extremism materials are an explicit exception — "saving a copy" alone can sustain a conviction (Article 120-6), so "no archiving" is not a prudence tip for this category but a hard line. Once you've seen it, stop there: no screenshots or archives, no forwarding, no comments, no pulling others in to repost. Forwarding and commenting both count as dissemination — "I only shared it" is not a defense, and content from foreign platforms may be an aggravating factor; if you can't tell whether something is true, even less should you forward it, same as item 1 of this chapter. Punishment for circumvention tools themselves is covered in Section 11, item 9. Do the math: forwarding this kind of content earns no substantive benefit in return, and the cost is prison time plus deprivation of political rights plus confiscation. Publicly checkable similar cases (a blogger who long posted political commentary on foreign platforms was sentenced to 7 years in 2023) appear only in foreign media and documents released by family; this book could not obtain a verdict verifiable through official channels, so this entry is written from the statutes alone, with no case attached.

### 4. Keep adult videos to yourself; don't post them to group chats, don't sell "resources," don't create groups
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; no forwarding, no saving into group files, no serving as owner or admin of such a group
- In plain terms: watching alone is not in the punishment provisions; posting once to a group is dissemination to the whole group: 10 to 15 days of detention and a fine of up to 5,000 yuan, under 2 years if serious. Making money selling "resources" means under 3 years, 3 to 10 years if serious, and 10 years or more up to life if especially serious. Creating a dissemination group with 30 or more members — the founder, the admins, and the main posters all count under the crime of disseminating obscene materials.
- Benefit: using information networks to disseminate obscene information — 10 to 15 days of detention plus a fine of up to 5,000 yuan, or under 5 days of detention or a 1,000–3,000 yuan fine in lesser cases, with heavier punishment where the content involves minors; disseminating obscene materials with serious circumstances — under 2 years; selling or disseminating for profit — under 3 years plus a fine, 3 to 10 years if serious, 10 years or more or life if especially serious; creating a group used mainly for disseminating obscene electronic information, with 30 or more members or causing serious consequences — the founder, administrators, and main disseminators are convicted of the crime of disseminating obscene materials (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第八十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第三百六十三、三百六十四条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2010). 关于办理利用互联网、移动通讯终端、声讯台制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（二）（第三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201002/t20100204_25001.shtml>
- Notes: both laws punish "dissemination"; private viewing is not in the provisions. Posting once to a group is dissemination to the whole group. The quantitative thresholds for "serious circumstances" are set by judicial interpretation; this section did not verify the specific figures.

### 5. Refuse any "part-time job" that has you receiving money, withdrawing cash, or transferring funds with your own cards, no matter how good the fee
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; pass up jobs of the "a few hundred a day, bring your bank card" kind
- In plain terms: this kind of part-time work earns a few hundred yuan; the price is a criminal record. One person brought their ID card and 1 bank card to a designated spot to help with transfers and withdrawals, earned just over 500 yuan — the procuratorate declined to prosecute, yet the police still detained them 10 days and fined 1,000 yuan; another person handed over their own and other people's bank cards and phone cards to a gang for receiving payments and cashing out, and got 2 years 3 months and a 22,000 yuan fine.
- Benefit: transferring, purchasing, or selling proceeds of crime on behalf of another, or otherwise concealing or disguising them while knowing their origin — under 3 years plus a fine imposed concurrently or separately; official cases: Wang, following instructions, brought an ID card and 1 bank card to a designated location to assist with transfers and withdrawals, gained just over 500 yuan, the procuratorate decided not to prosecute, but the police detained him 10 days and fined 1,000 yuan under the Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law; Man handed over his own and others' bank cards and phone cards to a gang for receiving payments, transfers, and cash withdrawals, and was sentenced to 2 years 3 months plus a 22,000 yuan fine (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第三百一十二条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民检察院 (2023). 检察机关依法惩治电信网络诈骗及其关联犯罪典型案例（案例九）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202311/t20231130_635188.shtml>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2025). 依法惩治掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益犯罪典型案例（案例五）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202508/t20250825_704514.shtml>
- Notes: lending out cards and "paofen" (跑分 — laundering money through one's own payment accounts) are covered in Section 8, item 7; this entry is about physically doing the cash withdrawals and transfers yourself; "I didn't know where the money came from" is treated in practice as knowledge presumed from pay that is conspicuously too high, requests to show your face to verify, transactions that defy common sense, and the like. The lightest outcome is still 10 days of detention plus a criminal record.

### 6. Refuse any "packaged documentation" loan where you're offered a cut of the loan amount — decline every one
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; say no, don't lend out your ID card or your credit record, don't sign a loan contract you haven't read, don't attend a bank face-to-face signing on someone else's behalf
- In plain terms: helping someone "package documentation" for a loan in exchange for a cut is handled as fraud. A 126-member gang dressed up unemployed people as "premium clients" and defrauded more than 30 million yuan from over 80 banks; the frontmen got 30 percent of the loan as their cut, and of the 80 already sentenced, 76 got 1 year 4 months to 6 years 6 months. "I intended to repay it" was not accepted: these people were found to have no job and no assets and could never have repaid. Whichever name the loan is in, that person owns the debt and the credit record.
- Benefit: obtaining loans, acceptance of bills, letters of credit, guarantees, or the like from banks or other financial institutions by deception — under 3 years plus a fine imposed concurrently or separately where major losses are caused to the bank or financial institution, or 3 to 7 years plus a fine where especially major losses or other especially serious circumstances exist; the threshold for filing and prosecution is direct economic loss of 500,000 yuan or more; where there was never any intent to repay and loans are obtained through fabricated reasons for introducing funds or projects, false economic contracts, false supporting documents, or false property certificates, it is the crime of loan fraud — a case is filed from 50,000 yuan, under 5 years plus a fine of 20,000 to 200,000 yuan, 5 to 10 years for huge amounts or other serious circumstances, 10 years or more up to life for especially huge amounts; forging, altering, or buying and selling official documents, certificates, or seals of state organs — under 3 years, or 3 to 10 years in serious cases; official case: a 126-member gang across 16 provinces and municipalities dressed up unemployed people as "premium clients" and defrauded over 30 million yuan from more than 80 bank branches; the "blank-slate" (白户 — people with no credit history) frontmen got 30 percent of the loan amount as their cut; the procuratorate prosecuted 105 "blank slates," and of the 80 sentenced at first instance by 2026, 76 were given 1 year 4 months to 6 years 6 months plus fines for loan fraud, while 4 others who cashed out for the gang were convicted of both loan fraud and money laundering with combined punishment, getting 2 years 4 months to 7 years 6 months (nationwide; the crime of fraudulently obtaining loans per the text in force from March 2021 under Criminal Law Amendment XI)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 刑法修正案（十一）（第十一条，修改刑法第一百七十五条之一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202012/t20201227_503700.shtml>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第八十八、一百九十三、二百八十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；最高人民检察院、公安部 (2022). 关于公安机关管辖的刑事案件立案追诉标准的规定（二）（第二十二、四十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/xwfbh/wsfbt/202204/t20220429_555906.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2026). 骗贷流水线背后藏洗钱黑手（检察日报）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202604/t20260428_726494.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2012). 出境入境管理法（第十二条）. <https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741547/c1013311/content.html>（国家移民管理局）；全国人大常委会 (2023 修正). 民事诉讼法（第二百六十六条）. <https://amr.guizhou.gov.cn/zwgk/xxgkml/jcxxgk/zcfg/fl/202401/t20240129_83645867.html>（贵州省市场监管局转载）
- Notes: "I meant to repay it at the time" was not accepted in that case: the procuratorate established that these people had no jobs, no fixed assets, and no ability to repay at all, and the cuts had all been spent, from which it inferred intent to unlawfully take possession; withdrawing the money for the gang may additionally constitute money laundering. Whoever's name the loan is in and whoever signed the contract is the borrower — even if no crime is formed, the debt and the credit record are theirs. Taking the money and fleeing abroad does not end the matter: once investigation is filed, evading investigation or prosecution is not subject to any time limit; those sentenced with punishment not yet fully served, criminal suspects, and defendants may not leave the country; where an undecided civil case exists the court may decide to bar exit, and where an enforcement subject fails to comply the court may take or have relevant units assist in taking exit-restriction measures. Cash withdrawals and transfers for others are covered in item 4 of this chapter; how to discover loans taken out in your name is in Section 8, item 8.

### 7. Don't withdraw money from a bank card you found; contact the owner or hand a found phone or wallet to the police instead of pocketing it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one phone call, or a trip to the police station
- In plain terms: keeping found property for yourself, refusing to hand it over when the amount is relatively large, means under 2 years, or 2 to 5 years for huge amounts. Withdrawing money from a found card at an ATM is worse — it counts as fraudulently using another person's credit card: under 5 years plus a fine of 20,000 to 200,000 yuan for relatively large amounts, 5 to 10 years plus a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 yuan for huge amounts; and this kind is filed by the police directly, with no need for the owner to sue.
- Benefit: lost property found must be returned to the rights holder, with timely notice to collect it or delivery to public security and other departments; converting another person's forgotten property to one's own and refusing to surrender it when the amount is relatively large constitutes the crime of embezzlement — under 2 years, or 2 to 5 years for huge amounts; using another person's found credit card at an ATM counts as "fraudulently using another person's credit card," and where a crime is formed it is handled as credit card fraud — under 5 years plus a fine of 20,000 to 200,000 yuan for relatively large amounts, or 5 to 10 years plus a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 yuan for huge amounts or other serious circumstances (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第三百一十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第一百九十六、二百七十条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民检察院 (2008). 关于拾得他人信用卡并在自动柜员机（ATM 机）上使用的行为如何定性问题的批复. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/flfg/sfjs/201208/t20120830_47871.shtml>
- Notes: embezzlement is "handled only upon complaint" — the owner must sue in court themselves — but withdrawing money with a found card is a publicly prosecuted case that the police file directly; the specific sums for "relatively large amounts" are set by judicial interpretation, which this section did not verify. After found property is handed in, the owner pays storage costs when collecting it (Civil Code Article 317).

### 8. Claim discounts under your real identity; don't buy virtual numbers to mass-register alt accounts, don't exploit loopholes to claim repeatedly
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; pass up the work in "coupon-hunting task groups," leave platform loopholes alone once discovered, and don't treat discounts as income that can be replicated at scale
- In plain terms: joining a promotion normally and claiming one coupon on your own account is outside the punishment provisions; mass-registering fake users to claim more, or ordering from yourself and taking the orders yourself, is handled as fraud — 3,000 yuan obtained is enough for under 3 years, 30,000 yuan and up gets 3 to 10 years, and 500,000 yuan or more starts at 10 years, so costing a merchant four or five hundred thousand lands right in the 3-to-10-year tier. Delivery riders used virtual numbers at 0.1 yuan apiece to register as new users, claimed the "15 off 29" coupons to place orders and then resold the goods for cash, rinsing just over 90,000 yuan; 12 people in the case got 3 years 6 months down to 3 months of criminal detention. "Their system had a loophole" doesn't help: one person exploited the gap between refunds and shipping to walk away with 224 anime figures and 249,000 yuan — 3 years, suspended 4, repaying the proceeds plus the fine came to 330,000. And many participants don't make it legally safe: all 163 people prosecuted in the ride-hailing order-brushing case were sentenced, and the 34 minor participants not prosecuted were still transferred for administrative punishment.
- Benefit: defrauding public or private property in relatively large amounts — under 3 years plus a fine imposed concurrently or separately, 3 to 10 years plus a fine for huge amounts or other serious circumstances, 10 years or more or life plus a fine or confiscation of property for especially huge amounts or other especially serious circumstances; relatively large, huge, and especially huge mean 3,000 yuan to over 10,000 yuan, 30,000 yuan to over 100,000 yuan, and over 500,000 yuan respectively, with each province setting its local standard within these ranges; teaching the method to others or selling "tutorials" — under 5 years for the crime of imparting criminal methods, 5 to 10 years if serious, 10 years or more or life if especially serious; the upstream and downstream of buying and selling phone numbers and verification codes — under 3 years for the crime of infringing citizens' personal information, 3 to 7 years if especially serious; using bulk accounts, automatic network-address switching systems, and code-receiving platforms that bulk-collect SMS verification codes is itself prohibited under Article 14 of the Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law, and illegally buying, selling, renting, or lending phone cards and internet accounts, or providing real-name verification assistance, is listed in Article 31 — violating the former means confiscation of illegal gains plus a fine of 1 to 10 times the amount, a fine of up to 500,000 yuan where there are no illegal gains or they are under 50,000 yuan, and detention of up to 15 days in serious cases; violating the latter carries the same 1-to-10-times fine, up to 200,000 yuan where there are no illegal gains or they are under 20,000 yuan, and detention of up to 15 days in serious cases; participating in telecom and online fraud without constituting a crime — 10 to 15 days of detention, confiscation of illegal gains plus a fine of 1 to 10 times, or a fine of up to 100,000 yuan where there are no illegal gains or they are under 10,000 yuan; official cases: delivery rider Liu bought virtual phone numbers at 0.1 yuan apiece to register as new users on a platform, claimed the "15 off 29" newcomer coupons to place orders and then resold the goods to small shops for cash, and also used virtual numbers to invite each other for reward money of 2 to 20 yuan; he and fellow rider Tan defrauded just over 90,000 yuan and just over 70,000 yuan respectively; the Shanghai Yangpu District Procuratorate prosecuted 12 people for fraud and the court sentenced them to 3 years 6 months down to 3 months of criminal detention; Wang exploited the gap between refunds and the recovery of virtual lottery coins at an online store — 1,156 refunds, 224 figures taken, over 249,000 yuan, cashed out on second-hand platforms — and was sentenced to 3 years, suspended 4, plus a 30,000 yuan fine for fraud (during trial he repaid the proceeds and prepaid the fine, 330,000 yuan in total); a ride-hailing platform's new-user coupons and driver subsidies were defrauded through fake rides of "ordering a car for yourself and accepting the order yourself," spanning 19 provinces and more than 70 cities and over 2 million yuan; the Chaoyang Longcheng District Procuratorate in Liaoning prosecuted 163 people, all convicted; principals Huang and Cai each got 3 years 6 months with combined punishment for fraud, infringing citizens' personal information, and imparting criminal methods, and 34 accessories with minor circumstances were given relative non-prosecution yet still transferred to the police for administrative punishment (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A

- Benefit: intercourse with a girl under 14 is treated as rape and punished more heavily, with a base sentence of 3 to 10 years; the Supreme People's Court's position: where violence, coercion, or other compulsory means are used, rape is convicted regardless of whether the girl's age was known; where no compulsory means are used, for girls under 12 "reason to know" is always deemed established, and for those 12 to 14, where physical development, speech, manner, dress, or the like would reveal she might be a girl under 14 and sexual assault is still committed, "knowledge" is deemed established, with the "genuinely did not know" exception applied strictly (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百三十六条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院 (2015). 关于「与十四周岁以下幼女发生性关系是否要明知才构成犯罪」的答复. <https://www.court.gov.cn/hudong/xiangqing/13363.html>

### 19. Before you hit someone, do the math: detention, compensation, prison time
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; hold out for those few seconds
- In plain terms: the ledger for throwing one punch: 5 to 10 days of detention plus a 500–1,000 yuan fine; group assaults, hitting the elderly, pregnant women, or children, or hitting several people at once, means 10 to 15 days of detention plus a 1,000–2,000 yuan fine; minor injury makes it a criminal case — under 3 years, and 3 to 10 years for serious injury. The money is counted separately: one person broke three of the other's ribs and paid 113,000 yuan in a settlement to escape prosecution; another broke the other's radius, paid nothing, and got 10 months.
- Benefit: beating another person or intentionally causing injury — 5 to 10 days of detention plus a fine of 500 to 1,000 yuan; group assault, beating disabled persons, pregnant women, the elderly, or children, repeated beatings, or beating several people at once — 10 to 15 days of detention plus a fine of 1,000 to 2,000 yuan; minor injury constitutes the crime of intentional injury — up to 3 years of fixed-term imprisonment, criminal detention, or controlled surveillance, and 3 to 10 years for serious injury; on the civil side, medical expenses, lost wages, nursing costs, transport costs, hospital meal allowances, and nutrition costs must be paid, and for disability the disability compensation is calculated as the previous year's per-capita disposable income of urban residents in the court's locality multiplied by 20 years (minus 1 year per year of age over 60, and 5 years for those over 75); in 2022 procuratorates nationwide accepted over 70,000 minor-injury cases, mostly arising from marriage, family, or neighborhood disputes or chance events; Supreme People's Procuratorate typical cases: Lu broke three of the other's ribs (serious injury grade II minor) and only got non-prosecution after paying 113,000 yuan to reach a settlement; Shi broke the other's radius (serious injury grade II minor), paid nothing, and was sentenced to 10 months of fixed-term imprisonment (nationwide; the Public Security Administration Punishments Law in force from January 1, 2026)

### 20. If you can't raise a child after birth, only one legal way out exists: civil-affairs registration; taking money to hand over the child can be sentenced as child trafficking, and abandoning the child is the crime of abandonment
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; file an adoption registration with a county-level or higher civil-affairs department, submit materials, and wait for review and the adoption assessment
- In plain terms: there is a way out if you can't raise the child, but only one legal one: adoption registration through the civil-affairs department, with the adoptive relationship established only from the day of registration; those who may place a child for adoption are the guardian of an orphan, child-welfare institutions, and "birth parents with special difficulties who cannot raise the child." Handing a child over privately, especially for money, can turn "placing for adoption" directly into the crime of child trafficking — starting at 5 years or more, up to the death penalty in especially serious cases. The official line is "whether there was a purpose of unlawful gain": selling the child right after birth, "giving" the child to someone for money while knowing the recipient has no intent to raise the child or not caring at all whether they can, and taking huge sums clearly not "nutrition fees" or "thank-you fees" — all count as selling one's own child. Conversely, genuinely placing a child privately under hardship and accepting only small nutrition or thank-you fees counts as private folk adoption and is not handled as trafficking. Abandoning the child is the crime of abandonment, under 5 years; strangling or drowning the child is intentional homicide — it does not become lighter just because the child was newborn.
- Benefit: Article 240 of the Criminal Law: "whoever abducts and traffics women or children shall be sentenced to not less than 5 years but not more than 10 years of fixed-term imprisonment, and shall also be fined"; where aggravating circumstances exist, 10 years or more of fixed-term imprisonment or life imprisonment plus a fine or confiscation of property, and "where the circumstances are especially serious, the death penalty plus confiscation of property." Article 17 of the Opinion on Punishing the Crimes of Abducting and Trafficking Women and Children in Accordance with the Law: the key to distinguishing selling one's own child from private folk adoption "is whether the actor has the purpose of unlawful gain," and three situations may be found to be selling one's own child and punished as abducting and trafficking women or children — "treating childbearing as a means of unlawful gain and selling the child immediately after birth," "knowing the other party has no intent to raise the child, or without considering at all whether the other party intends to raise it, 'giving' the child to them in exchange for money," and "'giving' the child to another in exchange for huge sums clearly not 'nutrition fees' or 'thank-you fees'"; placing a child privately under life hardship without a purpose of unlawful gain, accepting only small nutrition or thank-you fees, is private folk adoption and is not punished as trafficking. Article 261 of the Criminal Law: "whoever has a duty to support the elderly, the young, the sick, or others without the ability to live independently and refuses to do so, where the circumstances are odious, shall be sentenced to not more than 5 years of fixed-term imprisonment, criminal detention, or controlled surveillance." Article 1094 of the Civil Code: those who may place a child for adoption are the guardian of an orphan, child-welfare institutions, and birth parents with special difficulties who cannot raise the child; Article 1105: "adoption shall be registered with the civil-affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level. The adoptive relationship is established from the date of registration"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百四十、二百六十一条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、司法部 (2010). 关于依法惩治拐卖妇女儿童犯罪的意见（法发〔2010〕7 号，第 17 条）. 最高人民法院官网 <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/977.html>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一千零九十四、一千一百零五条）. 盐城市人民政府转载收养编全文 <https://xxgk.yczf.gov.cn/xzf/ycmzj/fdzdgknr/fgwj_41995/202112/t20211203_1506640.shtml>
- Notes: this entry does not discuss whether to give birth; it only addresses the fork in the road after the child is born. The order-saving sequence: give birth in a hospital (prenatal checkups and in-hospital delivery are in Section 27), since the medical birth certificate is the prerequisite for all later paperwork; for adoption, go to a county-level or higher civil-affairs department first and never go through a middleman — once money changes hands, the nature may change; and thoughts of "harming yourself or the child" within one year after delivery are treated as an emergency — see Section 27, items 7 and 16, and Section 1, item 25.
