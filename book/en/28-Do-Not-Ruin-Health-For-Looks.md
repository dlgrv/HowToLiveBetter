# 28. Don't harm your body just to change its appearance

This section is about a few ways of changing how you look by doing something to the body: going hungry, taking drugs, getting injections, and having surgery. The decision is usually made by comparing yourself with other people. The cost falls entirely on you, and a fair part of it cannot be undone.

This section does not judge what anyone wants to look like. That is each person's own affair. For the same goal, you can look for a prescription, a qualified provider, and follow-up, or you can buy online, go to a beauty salon, and raise the dose yourself. This section only compares how far apart the risks of those 2 ways are. The account looks mainly at what actually happens to the body, such as blindness, a stay in hospital, and death. Those 2 items on cosmetic medicine also involve criminal liability for practicing medicine illegally.
### 1. Do not use extreme dieting, fasting, or making yourself vomit to control weight. If you want to lose weight, lose it on the exercise side
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The other way around, you also save what you would have spent on weight-loss products.
- In plain terms: People with anorexia nervosa (the illness of starving yourself past the point of health) are about 5.9 times as likely to die as people the same age. Follow 1000 of them for 1 year and 5.1 die, and about 1 in 5 of those deaths are suicide. People who diet are more likely to get an eating disorder. Students at 44 secondary schools were followed for 3 years, and girls who dieted severely were 18 times as likely to get ill as girls who did not diet. If you want to control your weight, exercise more. Do not eat so little that you are hungry.
- Benefit: 36 studies taken together. The people with anorexia nervosa had been followed for 166642 person-years in all (the years each person was followed, added up). Their standardized mortality ratio was 5.86, that is, the chance of death is about 5.9 times that of people the same age. There were 5.1 deaths per 1000 person-years, which means 5.1 deaths if 1000 people are followed for 1 year. About 1/5 of the people who died died by suicide. The standardized mortality ratio for bulimia was 1.93, and for other eating disorders it was 1.92, about 1.9 times people the same age in each case. Another study, in 44 secondary schools in Victoria, Australia, followed students aged 14 to 15 for 3 years, and the same students were checked across 6 rounds. New eating disorders in girls were 21.8 per 1000 person-years, that is, 21.8 new cases if 1000 girls are followed for 1 year. Girls who dieted severely had 18 times the risk of girls who did not diet. Girls who dieted moderately had 5 times the risk. After a further adjustment for past dieting and psychiatric symptoms, taking out the effect of having dieted before and of already having psychiatric symptoms, BMI (body mass index, weight divided by height squared), amount of exercise, and sex no longer predicted a new eating disorder. The authors' conclusion was that "among adolescents, controlling weight with exercise rather than restricting food seems to carry a lower risk of an eating disorder."
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:Arcelus J et al. (2011). Mortality rates in patients with anorexia nervosa and other eating disorders. A meta-analysis of 36 studies. Arch Gen Psychiatry. <https://doi.org/10.1001/archgenpsychiatry.2011.74>；Patton GC et al. (1999). Onset of adolescent eating disorders: population based cohort study over 3 years. BMJ. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.318.7186.765>
- Notes: "Severe dieting" here means keeping what you eat far below what the body needs for a long time. It does not mean skipping 1 meal or skipping a late-night snack. If making yourself vomit, guilt after a binge, or weighing yourself over and over and being unable to stop has already appeared, go to a psychiatry department or the clinical psychology department of a general hospital. This is an illness with a standard treatment. It is not a willpower problem. The link between BMI and mortality is in section 2, item 32. That item is about the long-term weight range, not about the method of losing weight.

### 2. Two things to check before getting injections, thread lifts, or surgery: does the facility’s license list “medical aesthetics,” and is the practitioner a licensed attending physician?
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->

- Cost: There is no cost involved. Checking these two documents takes only a few minutes. The clinic should display its “Medical Institution Practice License” in the lobby. A doctor’s qualifications can be verified via the health authority’s online registry of licensed physicians.

- In plain terms: A needle, a knife, or a medical device that breaks the skin or goes into the body counts as medical care. It can be done only where the license lists "medical aesthetics," and the person who does it must be a registered licensed physician. Beauty salons, studios, and hotel rooms are not allowed. A person with no physician's qualification who does it is practicing medicine illegally. If they seriously injure someone, the sentence is 3 to 10 years. If they cause death, the sentence is 10 years or more.

- Benefit: Article 2 of the “Administrative Measures for Medical Aesthetic Services” defines medical aesthetics as “the use of surgery, drugs, medical devices, and other invasive or traumatic medical techniques to repair and reshape a person’s appearance and body contours.” In simpler terms, any procedure that breaks the skin or introduces needles or devices into the body qualifies as medical treatment. Articles 8 and 24 stipulate that a facility must first obtain a “Medical Institution Practice License” and be approved for medical aesthetic services before conducting such procedures; if “medical aesthetics” is not listed on the license, the practice is prohibited. Article 11 requires attending physicians to hold valid licenses and be registered; those performing cosmetic surgery must have at least six years of relevant clinical experience, cosmetic dentistry five years, and cosmetic traditional Chinese medicine or dermatology three years each. Article 20 mandates that patients receive written explanations of indications, contraindications, risks, and precautions prior to consent. Article 336 of the Criminal Law criminalizes illegal medical practice — performing injections, surgery, or treatment without proper licensing. Penalties range from up to three years imprisonment for minor offenses to three to ten years for causing injury, and more than ten years for resulting in death.

- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:卫生部 (2002). 医疗美容服务管理办法（卫生部令第 19 号，第二条、第八条、第十一条、第十六条、第二十条、第二十四条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62198.htm>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第三百三十六条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>

- Notes: Article 16 of the same regulations further specifies that medical aesthetic procedures may only be conducted at licensed medical aesthetic facilities or at hospitals/clinics with designated medical aesthetic departments. Therefore, performing injections at a patient’s home or in a private studio already violates regulations. Article 20 also requires parental consent for minors undergoing such treatments. Routine beauty services such as facials, massages, or nail care fall outside this regulatory framework; however, any procedure involving needles or surgery remains classified as medical aesthetics.

### 3. Avoid facial fillers in the nasal bridge and entire nose, glabella, forehead, and nasolabial folds, and never seek unlicensed injections to save money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. Just choose a different injection site, or skip fillers altogether.
- In plain terms: Among people blinded by facial filler injections, more than half received them in the nose, followed by the area between the eyebrows (glabella), the forehead, and the smile lines (nasolabial folds), and over eight in ten received hyaluronic acid. Only about one in five recovered their vision completely, just over one in ten partially recovered, and nearly one in five also suffered brain damage. Because no emergency treatment reliably works to restore sight, the safest course is to avoid injections in these areas entirely.
- Benefit: Aggregating cases reported in global medical journals from January 2015 to September 2018 identified 48 new cases of partial or complete vision loss following filler injections. The most frequent complication site was the nose (56.3%), followed by the glabella (27.1%), forehead (18.8%), and nasolabial folds (smile lines) (14.6%). Hyaluronic acid was the filler used in 81.3% of cases. Skin changes occurred in 43.8%, and 18.8% experienced central nervous system complications (brain damage). Complete vision recovery was achieved in 10 cases (20.8%), partial recovery in 8 cases (16.7%), and no recovery in the remainder. The authors concluded that treatment protocols varied widely and no single therapy has been proven consistently effective.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:Beleznay K et al. (2019). Update on Avoiding and Treating Blindness From Fillers: A Recent Review of the World Literature. Aesthet Surg J. <https://doi.org/10.1093/asj/sjz053>
- Notes: These figures were compiled by counting journal-reported cases one by one. They capture only the number of people who suffered complications, not the total number of people who received injections. Consequently, it is impossible to calculate the exact risk of blindness per injection; the data only reveal which facial sites see the most complications and that reversing the damage afterward is rarely successful. This is also why the evidence is graded B. Standard practices at licensed medical facilities routinely mitigate these risks: mapping underlying blood vessels beforehand, using blunt-tip cannulas instead of sharp needles, injecting small volumes while slowly withdrawing the cannula, and keeping hyaluronidase ready on hand. Each safeguard requires a properly trained physician, which is why unauthorized channels should never be used. Before undergoing any procedure, check the clinic's license and the injector's qualifications as outlined in item 2 of this section.

### 4. Do not buy weight-loss pills, slimming coffees, diet candies, or enzyme plums (prunes claimed to be soaked in enzymes) promising "rapid weight loss"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. In fact, it saves you money on these products. The hard part is accepting that there is no such thing as "losing several pounds in a week."
- In plain terms: These products are frequently caught secretly spiked with a banned weight-loss drug (sibutramine), which was taken off the market in China in 2010. Among overweight people aged 55 and older with heart disease or diabetes who took it for over three years, the risk of a non-fatal heart attack was about 28% higher, and the risk of a stroke was about 36% higher. It only led to an extra 1.7 kg of weight loss. By the time spot tests catch it, you have already swallowed it.
- Benefit: The SCOUT trial enrolled 9,804 participants. All were aged 55 or older, overweight or obese, and had cardiovascular disease or type 2 diabetes. They were randomized into two groups, receiving either the drug or a placebo, with an average treatment duration of 3.4 years. The primary endpoint was a composite of prespecified adverse events: non-fatal myocardial infarction, non-fatal stroke, resuscitated cardiac arrest, and cardiovascular death. The incidence of these events was 11.4% vs 10.0%. Hazard ratio HR 1.16 (95% CI 1.03–1.31, P=0.02), indicating about a 16% higher risk of adverse events in those taking the drug. The true value roughly falls between 3% higher and 31% higher. Non-fatal myocardial infarction was 4.1% vs 3.2%, HR 1.28 (1.04–1.57), a risk roughly 28% higher. Non-fatal stroke was 2.6% vs 1.9%, HR 1.36 (1.04–1.77), a risk roughly 36% higher. There was no increase in cardiovascular death or all-cause mortality (all-cause meaning deaths from any cause combined). After randomization, the sibutramine group lost an average of 1.7 kg more weight. Based on a safety assessment, the former State Food and Drug Administration determined that "the risks of weight-loss treatment outweigh the benefits" and issued a notice in October 2010 ordering a halt to the production, sale, and use of sibutramine formulations and active pharmaceutical ingredients. Both sibutramine and phenolphthalein are banned from being added to food, including health foods.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:James WPT et al. (2010). Effect of sibutramine on cardiovascular outcomes in overweight and obese subjects. NEJM. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1003114>；天津市市场监督管理委员会. 警惕部分食品中违法添加「盐酸西布曲明」和「酚酞」. <https://scjg.tj.gov.cn/tjsscjdglwyh_52651/hdpt/cjwtyxfts/202007/t20200720_2972549.html>
- Notes: SCOUT enrolled a high-risk population with pre-existing heart disease or diabetes. This trial did not prove that healthy young people taking it would experience the same harm. However, precisely because it is impossible to distinguish who is at high risk, regulators simply withdrew it from the market. Another commonly adulterated ingredient is an old laxative (phenolphthalein); long-term excessive intake can disrupt electrolytes and trigger cardiac arrhythmias. How to tell: legitimate weight-loss medications are prescription drugs, with approval numbers verifiable in pharmacies and hospitals. Products marketed under the guise of "coffee," "candies," "plums," or "meal replacements" that promise losing several pounds a week should, by definition, have no pharmaceutical effect; if they do, it means something was added. The official text discontinuing sibutramine is Notice No. [2010] 432 issued by the former State Food and Drug Administration, published on the NMPA website. Cited here is the secondary relay page from the Tianjin market regulation authorities, where the phrases "risks outweigh benefits" and "halt production, sale, and use in October 2010" can be verified verbatim.

### 5. Do not use anabolic steroids ("muscle injections" or oral pills) to build muscle
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing, and saves you money on drugs. The hard part is accepting that muscle builds slowly.
- In plain terms: Men who test positive for doping at the gym face a noticeably higher risk of death than their peers and visit the hospital more than twice as often each year. Severe acne, enlarged male breasts, and erectile dysfunction each hit at least one out of every ten users. In China, these are strictly prescription drugs, so any channel selling them to you without a prescription is already breaking the law.
- Benefit: A Danish retrospective registry study matched each user with age-matched controls. The study included 545 men who tested positive for androgenic anabolic steroids during fitness center doping controls between 2006-01-03 and 2018-03-01, matched with 5,450 controls. The authors also re-evaluated the findings using another cohort of 644 men who refused testing and 6,440 matched controls. Results: The hazard ratio for all-cause mortality among users was HR 3.0 (95% CI 1.3–7.0), meaning the true value roughly falls between 1.3 and 7. The median annual number of hospital contacts or visits was 0.81 vs 0.36 (P < 0.0001). A median means half the people had more and half had fewer. This P-value indicates the difference is virtually impossible to be a coincidence. Acne (breakouts), gynecomastia (enlarged male breasts), and erectile dysfunction each affected over 10% of users—meaning at least one in ten users was affected—all significantly higher than in controls (P < 0.0001). The re-evaluated cohort yielded consistent results. Article 7 of China's Anti-Doping Regulations strictly regulates prohibited substances on the doping list; illegal production, sale, import, or export by any entity or individual is prohibited. The same regulations mandate that anabolic agents and peptide hormones are managed as prescription drugs, meaning they can only be purchased with a doctor's prescription ("anabolic preparations" is the statutory legal term for such substances).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:Horwitz H et al. (2019). Health consequences of androgenic anabolic steroid use. J Intern Med. <https://doi.org/10.1111/joim.12850>；国务院 (2004). 反兴奋剂条例（国务院令第 398 号，第七条、第九条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2004/content_63129.htm>
- Notes: Caveat: This was an observational study tracking groups over time, not a randomized controlled trial. Users might engage in other risky behaviors, so the 3.0 hazard ratio cannot be blamed solely on the drugs. The true figure could be as low as 1.3 or as high as 7.0, which is a wide range. However, visible side effects—where acne, male breast enlargement, and erectile dysfunction each affect over 10% of users—are much less susceptible to this type of confounding bias. Legally, these drugs are referred to as "anabolic preparations"; whenever you see that term, it means these substances. Ordinary protein powder and creatine are not covered by this rule. The real danger is products claiming rapid muscle growth that secretly add prohibited ingredients. The way to assess them is the same as with weight-loss products (Item 4, do not buy weight-loss pills promising rapid slimming): legitimate medications are prescription-only and have verifiable official approval numbers. Anything sold under the label of "mass gainer" or "nutritional supplement" that promises results within weeks and actually works has almost certainly been adulterated.

### 6. If you take a weight-loss drug, or a weight-loss injection such as semaglutide, get the prescription at a hospital and raise the dose and come back for checks as the doctor arranges. Do not buy from an online shop that ships without a prescription, and do not get injected at a beauty salon
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 1 registration visit, plus the trip there and back. Tens of yuan, and half a day. If you are getting the weight-loss injections, you also go back for checks at the times the doctor sets. The money for the drug is separate. After you stop the injections the weight mostly comes back, so count this money as long-term use.
- In plain terms: A prescription drug has to be sold against a prescription. Selling it online also takes a real prescription and a real name. An online shop that ships without a consultation, or a beauty salon that comes to your home to inject, is usually supplying drugs of unclear origin, and nobody is responsible for the dose or for who should not have it. 1 year after the weight-loss injections stop, about 2/3 of the weight lost comes back, so count it as long-term treatment.
- Benefit: The Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Drug Distribution and Use Quality say that a drug retailer must follow the system that sorts drugs into prescription drugs and non-prescription drugs. That system puts drugs in 2 kinds. One kind can be bought only with a doctor's prescription. The other can be bought by the person. The pharmacy must sell prescription drugs against a prescription, as the rules require, and keep the prescription for at least 5 years. When the pharmacist or another pharmacy technician is not on duty, a sign must say so, and prescription drugs must not be sold without their review. Prescription drugs must not be sold from open shelves, for a customer to take down. Article 9 of the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Online Drug Sales says that, when prescription drugs are sold to an individual online, the seller must make sure the prescription comes from a real and reliable source, and must use real-name rules. Article 10 requires a risk warning shown prominently under each drug's display page. Before the prescription has been reviewed, the package insert must not be shown, and no buying service may be offered. Before the prescription is reviewed, the page should not even give a button to buy. Article 8 says that drugs under special state control must not be sold online. These include vaccines, blood products, narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, toxic drugs for medical use, radioactive drugs, and drug-class precursor chemicals. The National Health Commission's Guidelines for the Diagnosis and Treatment of Obesity (2024 edition) state that 5 drugs have been approved by the National Medical Products Administration for weight loss in adult primary obesity. The 5 are orlistat, liraglutide, beinaglutide, semaglutide, and tirzepatide. The same guidelines count a BMI (body mass index) from 24 up to but not including 28 as overweight, and 28 and above as obese. A drug is considered when the person is overweight and also has a related disease, such as high blood sugar, hypertension (high blood pressure), abnormal blood lipids, or fatty liver, or is already obese. The premise is that changing how you live has not brought the weight down. The drug is added on top of that. The guidelines require a full assessment of the illness and the contraindications before the drug, and regular follow-up under a specialist physician while it is used. Semaglutide starts at 0.25 mg a week, with a maintenance dose of 2.4 mg or 1.7 mg. Tirzepatide starts at 2.5 mg a week, up to 15 mg. If the weight lost is under 5%, the guidelines suggest stopping the drug. What happens after stopping is in 3 trials. The STEP 1 extension followed 327 people. After 68 weeks on semaglutide they had lost 17.3% on average. 1 year after stopping, the weight had come back by 11.6 percentage points, and the net loss left was 5.6%. The authors' conclusion was that they had regained 2/3 of the weight previously lost, and that most cardiovascular and metabolic markers had also moved back near the start. STEP 4 took 803 people who had already injected for 20 weeks and lost 10.6% on average, and split them by drawing lots. Those who kept injecting lost another 7.9% over the next 48 weeks. Those switched to a placebo regained 6.9%. In SURMOUNT-4, 670 people first injected tirzepatide for 36 weeks and lost 20.9% on average. Those who kept injecting then lost another 5.5%. Those switched to a placebo regained 14.0%. In July (7) 2026 the Yinchuan market supervision administration issued a risk alert. It said that the internet-famous "weight-loss injections" and "slimming pens" on the market are mostly repackaged drugs of unknown source, smuggled drugs, or counterfeit drugs. Buying them online or through a purchasing agent and injecting them yourself can easily set off a severe allergy, acute pancreatitis, organ damage, and a wound infection. An injection of this cosmetic-medicine kind can be done only at an institution that has a medical-aesthetics qualification. An injection at the person's home, or a private injection at a beauty salon, is not compliant. See item 2 of this section (2 things to check before injections, thread lifts, or surgery).
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:国家市场监督管理总局 (2023). 药品经营和使用质量监督管理办法（总局令第 84 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10846/202311/content_6917322.html>；国家市场监督管理总局 (2022). 药品网络销售监督管理办法（总局令第 58 号，第八条至第十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2022/content_5717002.htm>；国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2024). 关于印发肥胖症诊疗指南（2024年版）的通知（附件第三部分「十、药物治疗」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202410/content_6981734.htm>；Wilding JPH et al. (2022). Weight regain and cardiometabolic effects after withdrawal of semaglutide: The STEP 1 trial extension. Diabetes Obes Metab. <https://doi.org/10.1111/dom.14725>；Rubino D et al. (2021). Effect of Continued Weekly Subcutaneous Semaglutide vs Placebo on Weight Loss Maintenance in Adults With Overweight or Obesity: The STEP 4 Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2021.3224>；Aronne LJ et al. (2024). Continued Treatment With Tirzepatide for Maintenance of Weight Reduction in Adults With Obesity: The SURMOUNT-4 Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2023.24945>；银川市市场监督管理局 (2026). 市场监管部门医美消费及「减肥神药」风险提示. <https://scjg.yinchuan.gov.cn/zxdt/tzgg/202607/t20260703_5281032.html>
- Notes: The articles can be checked word for word, and the figures for weight coming back after stopping come from trials that split people by drawing lots. But nobody has quantified "how many fewer events you get if you use a prescription instead of buying it yourself," so it is graded B as hard to quantify. The STEP 1 extension stopped not only the drug. The guidance on diet and exercise stopped with it, and only some of the people in the original trial were followed. The steroids and sex hormones in items 5 and 7 of this section follow the same rules. When weight really has to come off with a drug, the doctor first looks at BMI, at whether there are other diseases, and at whether there is a reason the drug cannot be used, and only then decides whether to take it and which one. That judgment cannot be bought.

### 7. Use sex-hormone drugs only when a doctor prescribes them and checks you on a schedule. Do not buy them online, and do not raise the dose yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Regular registration visits, plus blood tests. A few hundred yuan a year, and a few half-days.
- In plain terms: This does not discuss whether to use them. It only discusses how. Transgender women who use estrogen have clearly more blood clots in the vessels than people the same age, about 4 extra per 1000 people over 2 years, and about 17 extra over 8 years. This kind of risk has to be watched with regular follow-up. If you buy the drugs online and set the dose yourself, nobody is checking you.
- Benefit: The researchers followed a group in the electronic records of Kaiser Permanente, a health-care group. There were 2842 transgender women and 2118 transgender men. Average follow-up was 4.0 years and 3.6 years. Each person was matched, on birth year, race, the clinic, and the time of joining, with about 10 times as many cisgender controls, 48686 cisgender men and 48775 cisgender women in all. Transgender women had more venous thromboembolism (a blood clot blocking a vessel) than the controls. Compared with cisgender men, at 2 years it was 4.1 cases per 1000 people (95% CI 1.6-6.7), and at 8 years it was 16.7 cases (6.4-27.5). Counted out, that is 4.1 extra cases among 1000 people over 2 years, and 16.7 extra over 8 years. Compared with cisgender women, the 2 figures were 3.4 (1.1-5.6) and 13.7 (4.1-22.7). Ischemic stroke (the kind of stroke in which a vessel is blocked) and myocardial infarction were, overall, close across the groups. Among transgender women who started hormone treatment only during the follow-up, the differences in venous thrombosis and ischemic stroke were clearer. The evidence on transgender men is not enough for a conclusion. The authors' conclusion was that "these results may suggest a need for long-term vigilance about the vascular side effects of estrogen in transgender hormone treatment."
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:Getahun D et al. (2018). Cross-sex Hormones and Acute Cardiovascular Events in Transgender Persons: A Cohort Study. Ann Intern Med. <https://doi.org/10.7326/M17-2785>；国家市场监督管理总局 (2022). 药品网络销售监督管理办法（总局令第 58 号，第九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2022/content_5717002.htm>
- Notes: The researchers themselves wrote down the shortcoming of this study. They could not find out how many people had bought the drugs themselves outside the hospital system. So the line of whether a person was on the drug was never drawn cleanly. That, in turn, is why drug use should sit inside regular follow-up. This study only looked at whether the blood vessels had an event. It does not answer whether hormone treatment should be used, or what good it does. That is a different set of evidence. Every use of sex hormones is the same, including contraceptive pills, menopausal hormone treatment, and testosterone supplementation. If you buy it online yourself, adjust the dose from posts on the internet, and never check clots or liver and kidney markers, the risk sits in the stretch where nobody is watching, not in the reason for using the drug. The regular path in China is the endocrinology department, or the matching specialty, of a proper medical institution, for an assessment, a prescription, and follow-up.

### 8. If feeling that you look ugly has reached the point of checking the mirror over and over and thinking over and over about changing it, get a body-image assessment before you talk about surgery
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Register for 1 visit at a psychiatry department or a clinical psychology department. Tens of yuan. The hard part is making this trip before you book the surgery.
- In plain terms: About 15% of people seen in plastic surgery have body dysmorphic disorder, which means magnifying a flaw other people can barely see until it affects daily life. About 13% of people seen in dermatology have it. In both groups, more than 70% are women. Book 1 visit first, see whether this is your situation, and only then decide whether to have surgery.
- Benefit: 33 studies were found by the rules and then combined. Among people seen in plastic surgery, the rate of body dysmorphic disorder (magnifying a flaw other people can barely see until it affects daily life) was 15.04%, and the studies ranged from 2.21% to 56.67%. In headcount, about 15 of every 100 people who go for plastic surgery meet it, and study by study the count runs from 2 to 57. Their mean age was 34.54±12.41 years. The second figure is how far the ages spread up and down. 74.38% were women. Among people seen in dermatology the rate was 12.65%, and the studies ranged from 4.52% to 35.16%. Mean age was 27.79±9.03 years, and 76.09% were women. The authors suggest that plastic surgeons and dermatologists assess on their own initiative, pick out the high-risk people, and have several departments manage them together. Another study asked 200 patients with body dysmorphic disorder. 71.0% had sought non-psychiatric treatment such as dermatology or plastic surgery, and 64.0% had received it. The authors' conclusion was that this kind of treatment "rarely improves" body dysmorphic disorder, and looks ineffective for it.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:Ribeiro RVE (2017). Prevalence of Body Dysmorphic Disorder in Plastic Surgery and Dermatology Patients: A Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis. Aesthetic Plast Surg. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00266-017-0869-0>；Crerand CE, Phillips KA, Menard W, Fay C (2005). Nonpsychiatric medical treatment of body dysmorphic disorder. Psychosomatics, 46(6), 549-555. <https://doi.org/10.1176/appi.psy.46.6.549>；NHS. Body dysmorphic disorder (BDD). <https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/conditions/body-dysmorphia/>

### 9. To straighten teeth, have a doctor with orthodontic qualifications examine you in person, and go back on schedule. Do not buy aligners online and wear them at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs more than buying online, for the registration and the return visits, and every so often you have to go to the clinic. The hard part is that the course often lasts 1 or 2 years, and you have to keep the return visits.
- In plain terms: Clear aligners are a Class II (2) medical device in China. The instructions require that you wear them under the guidance of a doctor with orthodontic qualifications, and that you go back regularly. The US drug regulator says it has not approved aligners sold straight to a person without a dentist's prescription. With nobody examining you in person, the bite can go crooked, teeth can move to the wrong place, there can be pain, and teeth can even fall out.
- Benefit: The 2022 Guiding Principles for the Registration Review of Bracketless Aligners, from the Center for Medical Device Evaluation of the National Medical Products Administration, say that this kind of aligner has a "management category of Class II (2)." They require the instructions to state that "during treatment it must be worn under the guidance of a physician qualified in orthodontics," and also to state "the requirements for eating, brushing, and regular return visits." On the product name, they say it is "not recommended to contain empty, hypothetical conceptual names such as 'invisible'." The US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) says: "The FDA has not authorized clear aligners that are sold directly to consumers without a prescription and supervision by a dentist or orthodontist," meaning it has not approved a single clear aligner sold directly to a person without a dentist's prescription and supervision. The FDA also says: "In-person examination by a dentist or orthodontist can help lower the risks of uneven bite, unintended tooth movement, pain, and tooth loss," that is, an examination in person by a dentist or an orthodontist can lower the risk of an uneven bite, teeth moving where they should not, pain, and losing a tooth. After you start wearing them, only regular return visits in person can confirm that the teeth are moving safely as planned. Both of these are regulatory documents. Neither gives the proportion of people who come to harm.
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources:国家药品监督管理局医疗器械技术审评中心 (2022). 无托槽矫治器注册审查指导原则（2022 年第 41 号）. <https://www.cmde.org.cn/flfg/zdyz/zdyzwbk/20230103095116100.html>；国家卫生健康委办公厅等 (2023). 关于进一步推进口腔医疗服务和保障管理工作的通知（国卫办医政发〔2023〕11 号，第五部分第（二）项）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202309/content_6903786.htm>；U.S. Food and Drug Administration (2026). Clear Dental Aligners（页面 2026 年 9 月 9 日更新）. <https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/dental-devices/clear-dental-aligners>
- Notes: The benefit magnitude is set at small, because no proportion of harm was found, so the judgment can only rest on the consequences. No risk alert from China's drug regulator or health departments, aimed at buying aligners online and wearing them at home, was found, so this item relies mainly on the registration requirements and on what the FDA says. Orthodontics is a medical act. For tooth straightening done as cosmetic medicine, the institution's license has to list the matching subject, and the doctor who does it has to be qualified. How to check is in item 2 of this section (the institution's license, and the attending physician). In 2023, 4 departments including the National Health Commission issued a notice on dental care. Where there is no practice license or clinic filing certificate, or non-health technicians are hired to treat teeth, the notice requires that they "be severely punished according to the law and the rules." Orthodontics is not reimbursed by yibao (医保 — basic medical insurance). See section 24, item 12 (ask the package price before you get a tooth implant).

### 10. Before you buy a spot-fading whitening cream, take the «国妆特字» number on the package and check it on the National Medical Products Administration website. Do not use a spot-fading cream sold by a WeChat reseller, or one the beauty salon mixed itself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Checking a number takes a few minutes. The hard part is not buying the ones that acquaintances and WeChat resellers (微商 — people selling through private chats) push the hardest.
- In plain terms: A spot-fading whitening cream has to be registered with the National Medical Products Administration and get a «国妆特字» number before it can be sold. Spot checks have found spot-fading creams with mercury thousands of times over the limit. The package printed a «国妆特字» number, but the factory named on it had not made that batch. Mercury taken into the body from the face can harm the kidneys. Stopping the cream, plus treatment, gets most people better, but the treatment takes several months to 2 years.
- Benefit: Article 16 of the Regulations on the Supervision and Administration of Cosmetics lists spot-fading and whitening cosmetics as special cosmetics. Article 17 says that special cosmetics may be produced or imported only after registration with the drug supervision department of the State Council. Article 36 requires the label to carry the special-cosmetics registration certificate number. Article 20 requires the registration information to be made public, so the number can be looked up on the National Medical Products Administration website. Article 62 of the Measures for the Administration of Cosmetic Registration and Filing says that a domestic number is «国妆特字» plus the year and a serial number, and an imported number is «国妆特进字». National Medical Products Administration Announcement No. 14 of 2026 published 2 batches of spot-fading cream, in which mercury was found at 4938 mg/kg (milligrams per kilogram) and 9281 mg/kg. The limit listed in the annex to the announcement is not more than 1 mg/kg. These 2 batches were thousands of times over that limit. They were bought at 2 small cosmetics shops, in Sichuan and in Anhui. The package printed a «国妆特字» number. The factory named on it objected. After review, the Guangdong medical products administration found that this enterprise had not produced that batch. A review brought together 30 cases reported in journals, all of them nephrotic syndrome after using a mercury-containing whitening cream, which means a large amount of protein leaking into the urine. All 30 were Asian women, mostly aged 18 to 52. From starting use to falling ill was 1 to 60 months, and half were within 5 months. Most kidney biopsies were minimal change disease (67%) or membranous nephropathy (23%). These are 2 diseases of the glomeruli, the kidney's filters. Very few got better on their own within a month and a half after stopping. In the cases with a record, those who used a mercury-removing drug took 1 to 9 months for the urine protein to turn negative, with a median of 2 months. All 3 who did not use a mercury-removing drug took more than 12 months. A hospital in Beijing admitted 288 people with mercury poisoning from 2014 to 2019. 84 of them (29.27%) were related to cosmetics. The main source of poisoning among women was cosmetics. Of the 18 patients with nephrotic syndrome who were followed, 15 had urine protein and albumin back to normal after an average of 22.67 months. The mercury in these 18 people did not all come from cosmetics.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:国务院 (2020). 化妆品监督管理条例（国务院令第 727 号，第十六条、第十七条、第二十条、第三十六条、第四十二条、第六十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2020-06/29/content_5522593.htm>；国家市场监督管理总局 (2021). 化妆品注册备案管理办法（总局令第 35 号，第六十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-01/12/content_5579239.htm>；国家药品监督管理局 (2026). 国家药监局关于25批次化妆品检出禁用原料的通告（2026年第14号）及附件. <https://www.nmpa.gov.cn/xxgk/ggtg/hzhpggtg/hzhpchjgg/hzhpcjgjj/20260424173212101.html>；Chan TYK, Chan APL, Tang HL (2020). Nephrotic syndrome caused by exposures to skin-lightening cosmetic products containing inorganic mercury. Clin Toxicol. <https://doi.org/10.1080/15563650.2019.1639724>；Yawei S et al. (2021). Epidemiology, clinical presentation, treatment, and follow-up of chronic mercury poisoning in China: a retrospective analysis. BMC Pharmacol Toxicol. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s40360-021-00493-y>
- Notes: Those 2 batches show that a number can be misused too. When you check the number, see whether the product name, the factory, and the package match what comes up. It is better to buy from the brand's official shop or a regular store. Article 42 of the regulations says that a beauty salon using cosmetics on a customer must follow the rules for a cosmetic operator. Item 5 of Article 60 prohibits an operator from compounding cosmetics on their own. A "salon-line spot-fading cream" that the salon mixes itself is illegal. If you have already used a spot-fading cream of unknown origin for a long time, stop, take the product to a hospital, and check urine mercury and urine protein. In that review, 6 people had normal blood mercury, and all 24 whose urine mercury was checked were above the normal upper limit. The cases were reported case by case, so you cannot calculate what share of people who use a mercury cream will harm their kidneys, and the grade is B for that reason. The benefit magnitude is set at medium from the consequences: nephrotic syndrome can mostly be treated, but treatment takes several months to 2 years.
