# 16. How to Live After a Chronic Disease Diagnosis

Scope: all-cause mortality and money. The diagnosis is not the endpoint — how you manage the following decades is the real accounting.
### 1. Take your medication for the full prescribed course; do not stop just because you feel better
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan — it actually saves the money spent on complications
- In plain terms: people who take their medication as prescribed have roughly a little over half the probability of dying in the same period as people who take it on and off. Discount this number: people who diligently take a placebo also have lower mortality, which means a fair share of the benefit comes from "people willing to stick with medication were already more health-conscious." But the harm of stopping the drug itself is certain, especially for blood pressure medication, anticoagulants, and anti-rejection drugs.
- Benefit: meta-analysis of 21 studies with 46,847 people: compared with poor adherence, good adherence gave a death odds ratio of 0.56 (95% CI 0.50–0.63); good adherence to treatments with proven efficacy was 0.55 (0.49–0.62)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Simpson SH, Eurich DT, Majumdar SR, et al. (2006). A meta-analysis of the association between adherence to drug therapy and mortality. BMJ, 333(7557), 15. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.38875.675486.55>
- Notes: contested, and the single number in this book most in need of discounting: the same meta-analysis found that people who diligently take a placebo also have lower mortality (0.56, 0.43–0.74), meaning a substantial part of the benefit comes from the confounder that "people who can stick with medication already take better care of their health" — the authors call it the healthy-adherer effect. But the harm of stopping the drug itself is clear, especially for blood pressure medication, anticoagulants, and anti-rejection drugs

### 2. Get outpatient chronic/special disease certification first, then cross-region registration — and five conditions (hypertension, diabetes, radiotherapy/chemotherapy, dialysis, anti-rejection) can then be settled directly out of region
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; file the cross-region medical treatment registration in the medical insurance app in advance
- In plain terms: for five conditions — hypertension, diabetes, outpatient radiotherapy and chemotherapy for malignant tumors, dialysis for uremia, and anti-rejection treatment after organ transplant — outpatient visits in another region can be settled directly with medical insurance, without paying out of pocket first and then going back to your insurance-enrollment region for reimbursement. You must first get outpatient chronic/special disease certification in your enrollment region, then file the cross-region registration; only when both are done does it work.
- Benefit: the National Healthcare Security Administration's document states that it has "basically achieved county-level availability of cross-provincial direct settlement for outpatient chronic/special disease costs of 5 conditions: hypertension, diabetes, outpatient radiotherapy and chemotherapy for malignant tumors, dialysis for uremia, and post-transplant anti-rejection treatment," with 5 more conditions added on top of that
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家医保局办公室、财政部办公厅 (2024). 关于稳妥有序扩大跨省直接结算门诊慢特病病种范围的通知（医保办发〔2024〕19 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202409/content_6974467.htm>
- Notes: the prerequisite is to first complete outpatient chronic/special disease certification in your enrollment region and then the cross-region registration — both are required. For the 5 newly added conditions and the rollout progress in each locality, go by what local medical insurance authorities announce

### 3. Do follow-up tests at the intervals your doctor gives, and record every result in the same notebook
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: a few dozen to a few hundred yuan per visit
- In plain terms: what matters in a chronic disease is a trend line, not any single reading. Keep the date, the values, and the drugs and doses you were on at the time in one place, and you won't have to redo the tests — and pay for them again — when you switch hospitals.
- Benefit: the value in chronic disease lies not in any single reading but in the trend. The same record also avoids duplicate tests and duplicate spending when changing hospitals
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 作者经验，无直接文献
- Notes: the record should include at least the date, the values, and the drugs and doses you were taking at the time. Photographing lab reports on your phone works too; the key is keeping everything in one place

### 4. Do not stop formal treatment in order to try folk remedies or supplements
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan — it also saves the money spent on supplements
- In plain terms: the harm of stopping medication is certain, the benefit of folk remedies and supplements is not; that means trading a certain loss for an uncertain gain. If you want to try a supplement, tell your attending doctor first — some of them affect blood levels of anticoagulants and anti-rejection drugs.
- Benefit: the harm of stopping medication is certain, the benefit of folk remedies is not. Section 6 of this book lists a batch of commonly ineffective product categories
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 作者经验，无直接文献；相关证据见第 6 节
- Notes: if you want to try a supplement, tell your attending doctor; some interact with prescription drugs, for example by affecting blood concentrations of anticoagulants and anti-rejection drugs

### 5. For a stable chronic condition, a community hospital can prescribe up to 12 weeks of medication at once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan — first ask whether the community health center can issue long-term prescriptions
- In plain terms: if your condition is stable and the medication plan unchanged, a community health center can prescribe 12 weeks of medication at once, saving the half day each month spent traveling to a big hospital and queuing for registration. Within the same pooling region, the reimbursement rate for a hospital stay at a primary facility is usually about ten percentage points higher than at the hospital one level up.
- Benefit: eliminates the monthly trip to a large hospital to queue for prescriptions; within the same pooling region, the inpatient reimbursement rate at a primary facility is, in principle, about 10 percentage points higher than at the next level up
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「对于符合条件的慢性病患者，基层医疗卫生机构单次可开具不超过12周用药的长期处方。」；同文「原则上统筹地区内医疗卫生机构住院报销比例逐级拉开10个百分点左右的差距」，并要求上级医院在基层开设高血压、糖尿病、慢性阻塞性肺疾病等常见病慢性病门诊
- Notes: "meeting the criteria" is the doctor's call — usually a clear diagnosis and a stable medication plan. The drug is the same drug; what you save is the monthly half day and the registration fee. If a primary facility lacks a drug, ask about its out-of-stock registration and delivery service.

### 6. Before signing up with a family doctor at a community clinic, ask which services go through medical insurance and which you pay yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan — one extra question
- In plain terms: the basic service package in the contract goes through medical insurance as required, while the personalized service package is paid entirely out of your own pocket. When someone pitches you an "upgrade package," first ask whether it is a basic or a personalized package, whether it has been filed with the authorities, and how much it costs a year.
- Benefit: the basic service package is covered by medical insurance payment as required; a personalized service package must be filed with the county-level health department and is paid by the individual
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「加强基层门诊付费与签约服务政策联动，基本服务包按规定纳入医保支付；个性化服务包由签约基层医疗卫生机构按程序向县级卫生健康部门备案，费用由个人支付。」
- Notes: the value of signing up lies in having someone follow your condition over the long term, not in how many items are bundled. When pitched an "upgrade package," first ask whether it is basic or personalized, whether it has been filed, and the annual price.

### 7. Get one fundus exam when diagnosed with diabetes, then repeat at the intervals your doctor sets; check your feet once a year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: a few dozen to a few hundred yuan per dilated fundus exam; the foot exam is usually included in the outpatient visit
- In plain terms: diabetic retinopathy and diabetic foot give almost no warning before vision is gone or the foot starts to rot, and catching them early still leaves room to intervene. Get one dilated fundus exam at the time of a type 2 diabetes diagnosis; with type 1, start 5 years after onset. If one or two exams come back clean and blood sugar is at target, you can stretch to once every one or two years; feet are checked once a year.
- Benefit: diabetic retinopathy and diabetic foot are almost symptom-free before anything goes wrong; found early they can still be treated, but by the time you can't see or the foot is ulcerated it is too late
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 美国糖尿病学会 (2026). 糖尿病诊疗标准 2026·第 12 章 视网膜病变、神经病变与足部护理. Diabetes Care：「People with type 2 diabetes should have an initial dilated and comprehensive eye examination by an ophthalmologist or optometrist at the time of the diabetes diagnosis.」「Adults with type 1 diabetes should have an initial dilated and comprehensive eye examination by an ophthalmologist or optometrist 5 years after the onset of diabetes.」「If there is no evidence of retinopathy from one or more annual eye exams and glycemic indicators are within the goal range, then screening every 1–2 years may be considered.」「Perform a comprehensive foot evaluation at least annually to identify risk factors for ulcers and amputations.」<https://doi.org/10.2337/dc26-S012>
- Notes: screen at the moment of type 2 diagnosis, but only from 5 years after onset for type 1 — because type 2 has often been running high silently for years. If one or two exams are clean and blood sugar is at target, once every 1 to 2 years is fine. Anyone with reduced sensation should look at the soles of their feet daily; a mirror works.
