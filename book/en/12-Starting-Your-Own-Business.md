# 12. Starting a Business and Doing Deals: Don't Bet the Family Savings

This section covers money and legal liability: opening a shop or founding a company — at which step your personal property, family property, or your spouse's property gets dragged into liability, what the law says, and whether you can ring-fence yourself in advance. Life expectancy conversion is not used here. Items are ordered from high to low by "most likely to cost you everything, easiest to avoid": signature-type items come first, methods requiring active work come later, and experience-based items are graded C.
### 1. Start a business only with money you can afford to lose; never touch the family savings or borrow to open

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; before putting money in, set yourself a number — an amount that, even if lost entirely, would not affect your mortgage payments, your children, or medical care; anything above that number, don't invest; family members' money and borrowed money do not count toward that number
- In plain terms: debts of a sole proprietorship get paid out of your personal property, and if it's unclear whether the business is a household one, out of the whole family's property; if both spouses sign, or borrowed money goes into a business the two of you run together, both of your assets are on the hook. When a company can't pay its debts as they fall due, subscribed capital that hasn't been paid up yet can be demanded early. So set your investment ceiling by the rule "even a total loss still leaves the mortgage and the doctor's bills covered."
- Benefit: losing money isn't the only risk: debts of an individually-owned business are borne from personal property when run by an individual, and from family property when run by the household or when the split can't be determined; debts co-signed by both spouses or ratified afterwards are joint debts, and money borrowed in one spouse's name counts as joint debt too if the creditor can prove it went into the couple's joint production and business operations; when a company can't pay debts as they fall due, creditors can demand early payment of subscribed capital that hasn't come due yet (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第五十六、一千零六十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四十七、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Notes: official statistics on business survival rates and average company lifespans could not be verified this time on the National Bureau of Statistics or the State Administration for Market Regulation websites, so no figures are given — only the legal consequences; graded B. "Affordable to lose" means that a total loss would not force you to sell your home or borrow from relatives to plug the hole.

### 2. Don't sign personal guarantees on company loans; make sure your spouse never signs either

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; when a bank, supplier, or landlord demands a "joint and several personal guarantee from the shareholder" or a "spouse's signature," treat that page as a waiver of limited liability — don't sign if you can avoid it, and if you must sign, sign only a general (ordinary) guarantee with a stated cap on the guaranteed amount
- In plain terms: if you sign a "joint and several liability guarantee" and the company can't pay, the creditor can skip the company and come straight after you; "general guarantee" means the creditor must first sue the company and exhaust its assets before reaching you. If your spouse signs too, the two of you repay out of both of your assets together — sign that page, and the "limited" in "limited liability company" stops applying to you personally.
- Benefit: a guarantee contract means you pay when the debtor doesn't; with a "joint and several liability guarantee," the creditor can come after you without first pursuing the company; with a "general guarantee," the creditor must first sue or arbitrate and enforce against the company's assets, and only if that still leaves the debt unpaid does it reach you. Debts co-signed by both spouses or ratified afterwards are joint marital debts — once the spouse signs, both of your assets repay together (nationwide, since 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第六百八十一、六百八十七、六百八十八、一千零六十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: in small and micro business lending practice, banks almost always require a personal joint and several guarantee from shareholders. This item doesn't mean "don't take loans" — it means knowing what you're signing before you sign, and capping the guarantee within the "affordable to lose" number from item 1. For general rules on IOUs and guarantees, see section 8, item 16.

### 3. Pick the right legal form before opening: sole proprietorships and general partners pay to the bitter end; only limited companies are "limited"

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: registration is done on the local government services portal; the main cost is the time or bookkeeping agency fee for monthly accounts and tax filing once you have a limited company (varies by locality); a few hours one-time
- In plain terms: sole proprietors and general partners pay to the bitter end; shareholders of a limited company only lose their subscribed amount. But whatever you subscribe has to be actually paid in within 5 years, so writing 1,000,000 yuan of registered capital means a potential debt of 1,000,000 yuan; capital you pull back out must be returned, and if you mix the company account with your household one — or in a single-member company you can't show the two pools of money are separate — the "limited" part is void all the same.
- Benefit: debts of an individually-owned business are borne from personal or family property; in a general partnership, partners bear unlimited joint and several liability for partnership debts, and in a limited partnership only the limited partner is capped at their subscribed capital; shareholders of a limited liability company are liable to the company only up to their subscribed capital. But "limited" fails in three situations: subscribed capital must be paid in full within 5 years of the company's formation, and when the company can't pay debts as they fall due creditors can demand early payment; capital withdrawn after being paid in must be returned; and anyone who abuses the company's independent legal status to dodge debts — or, in a single-member company, a shareholder who cannot prove company property is separate from their own — bears joint and several liability for company debts (nationwide, since July 2024)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第五十六条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2006). 合伙企业法（2006 年修订，第二条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2006/content_413955.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四、二十三、四十七、五十三、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Notes: the premise of "limited" is keeping business and private money apart: company account and personal account not mixed, no paying household expenses out of company money, complete books kept. Don't inflate registered capital to look good — whatever you subscribe must actually be paid in within 5 years, and writing 1,000,000 yuan means a potential debt of 1,000,000 yuan.

### 4. Don't be a nominee shareholder; don't hold shares on someone else's behalf

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; refuse requests like "just lend me your ID to be listed as a shareholder" or "keep the shares under your name for now"
- In plain terms: once your name is on the register, you are the shareholder in the eyes of the law: your subscribed capital must be paid in full on schedule, and if you fail, you not only pay it up but compensate the company for its losses; if at the company's founding other shareholders hadn't paid theirs in, you are on the hook for their shortfall too. Someone else runs the business; the debts land under your name.
- Benefit: a shareholder on the register is a shareholder in law: subscribed capital must be paid in full on schedule per the articles of association, and failing that, besides paying up you compensate the company for its losses; where at founding some shareholder hadn't paid in, the other founding shareholders are jointly liable for the shortfall; when the company can't pay debts as they fall due, creditors can demand early payment of subscribed capital not yet due. The person actually running the business isn't on the register — the debts are still under your name (nationwide, since July 2024)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四十七、四十九、五十、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Notes: the validity of nominee-holding agreements between the registered (nominee) shareholder and the actual investor, and how liability is divided between them, are set out in the Supreme People's Court provisions on several issues concerning the application of the Company Law (III). On the risks of being a nominee legal representative, see section 8, item 26.

### 5. Before franchising, check MOFCOM's filing registry, get written disclosures, and write a "cooling-off period" into the contract

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; check the brand's registration in the MOFCOM commercial franchise information management system, and demand written information disclosure and the contract text before signing; a few hours in total
- In plain terms: by the rules, a franchisor must have 2 directly-operated stores running for over 1 year, and must file with the commerce authorities within 15 days of signing; not filing draws a fine of 10,000–50,000 yuan, and being late to file draws 50,000–100,000 yuan plus a public announcement. Before you sign, it must give you in writing 12 items, including franchise fee and deposit refund terms, how current stores are operating, audit summaries for the last two years, and litigation over the past five years. No filing on record, no disclosure documents, or a demand for payment before you can even see the contract — walk away.
- Benefit: the regulation requires a franchisor to have at least 2 directly-operated stores with more than 1 year of operation, and to file with the competent commerce department within 15 days of signing its first franchise contract; failing to file draws a fine of 10,000–50,000 yuan, and filing late draws 50,000–100,000 yuan plus a public announcement; before signing it must disclose in writing 12 items, including the types and amounts of franchise fees, conditions for refunding deposits, the number and operating status of existing franchise stores, summaries of audit reports for the last 2 years, and litigation and arbitration over the past 5 years; the contract must stipulate that the franchisee may unilaterally terminate within a certain period after signing; and if the franchisor conceals relevant information or provides false information, the franchisee may terminate the contract (nationwide, since May 2007)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2007). 商业特许经营管理条例（国务院令第 485 号，第七、八、十二、二十二、二十三、二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_4179.htm>；商务部 (2012). 商业特许经营信息披露管理办法（商务部令 2012 年第 2 号，第五、九条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2012/content_2177025.htm>；商务部. 商业特许经营信息管理系统. <https://txjy.syggs.mofcom.gov.cn/>
- Notes: no filing on record, refusal to provide disclosure documents, or pay-first-contract-later — walk away. The exact length of the "cooling-off period" is set by the contract; the regulation only says "a certain period," so insist the number of days be written in when signing. For the disclosed "average sales, gross profit, and net profit of existing franchise stores," press for the data source — the implementing measures require the franchisor to state it.

### 6. Settle the name, premises, business scope, and registered capital before registering; with complete documents you can get the license on the spot

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: enterprise registration fees and individual business registration fees were abolished from January 1, 2015, so registration itself costs nothing; doing it yourself on the local government services portal or at the service hall takes a few hours one-time. Seal engraving, bookkeeping agency, and address hosting are market-rate expenses, not administrative charges
- In plain terms: registration itself is free — the registration fee was abolished back in 2015; you submit exactly the five statutory items, and once they're complete the license is issued on the spot, or within 3 working days if not. You declare the name yourself and subscribe the registered capital yourself, so what someone charges you is mostly an errand-runner's fee; you also don't have to accept claims that "seals must be engraved at a designated shop."
- Benefit: the regulation fixes the documents to submit at five items: "(1) the application form; (2) documents on the applicant's qualifications and the natural person's identity proof; (3) documents relating to the domicile or main place of business; (4) the articles of association of a company, non-company enterprise legal person, or farmers' cooperative (union), or the partnership agreement of a partnership; (5) other documents required by laws, administrative regulations, and the State Council's market regulation department" — companies must additionally submit appointment documents and identity proofs for the legal representative, directors, supervisors, and senior management, while individual businesses need no articles; the name is "declared by the applicant independently according to law," registered capital "follows the subscription registration system," and the business scope is chosen from the classification standard published by the registration authority; applications that are complete and meet statutory form are "confirmed and registered on the spot," and those that cannot be handled on the spot are done "within 3 working days," extendable by another 3 working days in complicated cases; "the issue date on the business license is the establishment date of the market entity" (nationwide, since March 2022)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2021). 市场主体登记管理条例（国务院令第 746 号，第十、十三、十四、十六、十九、二十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5636139.htm> ; 市场监管总局 (2022). 市场主体登记管理条例实施细则（市场监管总局令第 52 号，第十二、二十五、二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-03/02/content_5676403.htm> ; 财政部、国家发展改革委 (2014). 关于取消、停征和免征一批行政事业性收费的通知（财税〔2014〕101 号，附件 1）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2014-12/23/content_5023513.htm>
- Notes: registration is free, so any charge is most likely a middleman's fee. Seal engraving prices are set by the market; the General Office of the State Council expressly forbids "designating seal-making units to make official seals," so you don't have to accept the "must engrave at the designated place" claim. The registered capital figure doesn't affect your storefront image — it only sets your liability ceiling and must be paid in within 5 years; see item 3 of this section. If the business scope includes licensed items, you cannot open until the permit comes through; see item 7 of this section.

### 7. In trades that require a license, don't open until the license comes through

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; before opening, find out whether your business items require a permit (restaurants and food sellers need a food business license; selling only prepackaged food has been switched to filing), and start operating only once it's issued
- In plain terms: open without the license and the light penalty is an order to shut down, confiscation of illegal gains, and a fine of up to 10,000 yuan; if you deal in goods under state monopoly or exclusive sale, or in securities, futures, insurance, or funds payment and settlement, serious cases become the crime of illegal business operation — starting at up to 5 years in prison with a fine of 1 to 5 times the illegal gains. Fit out the shop first and get the license later, or run a "soft opening" before the license arrives — that's the most common trip-up.
- Benefit: operating without a required permit is investigated and dealt with by the departments designated in laws and regulations; where the law sets no explicit penalty for operating without a license, the punishment is an order to stop, confiscation of illegal gains, and a fine of up to 10,000 yuan. Operating without a permit in goods subject to statutory monopoly, exclusive sale, or restricted trading, or running securities, futures, insurance, or funds payment and settlement business without approval, constitutes the crime of illegal business operation when serious: up to 5 years of fixed-term imprisonment or criminal detention plus a fine of 1 to 5 times the illegal gains, and over 5 years when especially serious (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2017). 无证无照经营查处办法（国务院令第 684 号，第五、六、十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-08/23/content_5219861.htm>；市场监管总局 (2023). 食品经营许可和备案管理办法（市场监管总局令第 78 号，第四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10606/202307/content_6894763.html>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>
- Notes: finance, medical, and education-training licensing conditions each have their own dedicated laws; this item verified only the general enforcement rules and the criminal law provisions. Bubble tea shops and snack bars count as food service and need a food business license; fit-out before the license and "trial operation" before it arrives are common pitfalls.

### 8. Filing obligations start the moment you get the license: file a zero return on schedule even with no income

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; file monthly or quarterly yourself on the electronic tax bureau, a few minutes each time; hiring a bookkeeper is a separate market-rate expense. If you employ people, you must also complete social insurance registration within 30 days of establishment
- In plain terms: once you have the license, filing obligations begin, and even if you haven't earned a cent you must file a zero return on schedule. Failing to file first draws a fine of up to 2,000 yuan, rising to 2,000–10,000 yuan in serious cases; if non-filing led to underpaid tax, you repay the tax plus a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% (5 in 10,000), and on top of that a fine of 0.5 to 5 times the underpaid amount. Go three straight months without filing any tax and the system automatically flags you as an abnormal-status taxpayer, suspends your invoices, and blocks you from the simplified deregistration route. If you employ people, social insurance registration must also be done within 30 days of establishment.
- Benefit: "a taxpayer with no tax payable within the tax period must still complete tax filing as required"; failing to file on time means "the tax authority orders correction within a time limit and may impose a fine of up to 2,000 yuan; in serious cases, a fine of 2,000 yuan up to 10,000 yuan"; where non-filing also caused underpaid tax, the tax and late-payment surcharge are recovered plus a fine of "no less than 50% and no more than 5 times" the underpaid amount, with the surcharge accruing daily at 5 in 10,000; "where no tax return has been filed for any tax type for three consecutive months, the tax administration system automatically designates the entity as an abnormal-status taxpayer and suspends the use of its invoice ledger and invoices," automatically lifted after supplementary filing and acceptance of penalties; an employer "must, within 30 days of its establishment... apply to the local social insurance agency for social insurance registration" (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2002). 税收征收管理法实施细则（国务院令第 362 号，第三十二、七十五条）. <https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100010/c5195082/content.html> ; 全国人大常委会 (2015). 税收征收管理法（2015 年修正，第二十五、三十二、六十二、六十四条）. <https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/c5195081/content.html> ; 国家税务总局 (2019). 关于税收征管若干事项的公告（国家税务总局公告 2019 年第 48 号，第三条）. <https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100012/c5195194/content.html> ; 全国人大常委会 (2018). 个人所得税法（2018 年修正，第十二条）. <https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/c5193028/content.html> ; 全国人大常委会 (2018). 社会保险法（2018 年修正，第五十七条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/zgrdw/npc/xinwen/2019-01/07/content_2070267.htm>
- Notes: after the "multi-license-in-one" reform there is no separate tax registration — log into the electronic tax bureau with the unified social credit code on your business license and complete registration information confirmation and tax-type designation. Individual businesses and sole proprietorship enterprises pay individual income tax on business income, prepaid "within 15 days after the end of each month or quarter," with annual reconciliation settled "by March 31 of the year following the year the income was earned." File even with no opening — a zero return is still a filing; ignore it for three months and you become an abnormal-status taxpayer, invoices are suspended, and deregistration cannot go through the simplified procedure; see item 19 of this section.

### 9. Issue invoices only for real transactions, and use the small-scale taxpayer's tax-free allowance in full

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; don't buy invoices, don't sell invoices, don't "run accounts and issue invoices" for others — invoice and pay tax on real business only
- In plain terms: small-scale taxpayers pay no VAT on monthly sales up to 100,000 yuan or quarterly sales up to 300,000 yuan, and over the same period the 3% levy rate is cut to 1% — this is a free allowance, take all of it. Conversely, "finding someone to issue invoices so you can offset costs" is false invoicing: issuing for others, having others issue to you, and brokering invoicing between others all count — it starts at up to 3 years in prison plus a fine of 20,000–200,000 yuan, and for huge amounts can reach 10 years or more, or life.
- Benefit: for small-scale taxpayers, "sales below the threshold are exempt from VAT"; from January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2027 the threshold is monthly sales of 100,000 yuan, quarterly sales of 300,000 yuan, and per-transaction (per-day) sales of 1,000 yuan; over the same period, except for selling or leasing real property and transferring land-use rights, the 3% levy rate is reduced to 1%; conversely, falsely issuing VAT special invoices or other deductible invoices (issuing for others, issuing for oneself, having others issue for oneself, and brokering such issuance all count) draws up to 3 years of fixed-term imprisonment or criminal detention plus a fine of 20,000–200,000 yuan, 3 to 10 years when the tax amount is large, and over 10 years or life imprisonment for huge amounts (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2024). 增值税法（2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第二十三条）. <https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/c5237365/content.html>；财政部、税务总局 (2026). 关于增值税法施行后增值税优惠政策衔接事项的公告（财政部 税务总局公告 2026 年第 10 号，第一、二、六条）. <https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c102416/c5247434/content.html>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百零五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>
- Notes: the VAT Law takes effect on January 1, 2026, and the earlier small-scale taxpayer relief notices (No. 19 of 2023 and No. 1 of 2023) cease to apply or are repealed with it — the monetary thresholds are unchanged but the legal basis has changed, and this item has been updated to the new document. The criminal law text online is the 1997 version; Article 205's penalty tiers were later adjusted by amendments, and this item states only the tiers that were verified and unchanged. What's exempt is VAT; income tax is calculated separately. "Finding someone to issue invoices to offset costs" is false invoicing, not tax saving.

### 10. Anyone claiming to be the tax bureau and pressing you to pay is a scammer; handle tax matters only via the electronic tax bureau and 12366

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; on getting a call, text, or friend request about "spot tax checks," "abnormal accounts," or "fines if you don't file," hang up first, then verify by calling back via 12366 or the competent tax authority's landline
- In plain terms: the tax authorities will never phone asking for your bank card password, payment password, or SMS verification codes, and will never give you a designated account to transfer your taxes into; genuine on-site audits come with an inspection notice and an inspector's credentials shown. Get a "pay up or be fined for not filing" call — hang up first, then call back on 12366 or the tax bureau's landline. Impersonating state functionaries starts at 10 to 15 days of detention. If money has already left your account, call 110 or 96110 immediately to freeze the payment.
- Benefit: the STA's warning, verbatim: "tax authorities will not demand bank card passwords, payment passwords, account balances, or SMS verification codes related to bank card payments via WeChat mini-programs, phone calls, or text messages," and "some criminals impersonate tax officials to approach taxpayers under the guise of 'assisted tax filing,' 'handling tax incentives,' or 'policy guidance'"; the verification channels are "the 12366 tax service hotline or the competent tax authority's landline"; a genuine audit "requires presenting the inspection notice and inspection credentials"; "tax payments can only be made through the electronic tax bureau or at the tax service hall, and the tax authorities will never provide a designated account"; tax-related professional service agencies that "extort taxpayers or withholding agents in the name of tax authorities or tax personnel" are fined according to the circumstances; impersonating a state functionary to commit fraud draws 10 to 15 days of detention and may add a fine of up to 1,000 yuan, and where an act constitutes both fraud and the crime of impersonating a state functionary, "the offender is convicted and punished under the provision carrying the heavier penalty" (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家税务总局 (2025). 税务部门提醒广大纳税人——增强防范意识 警惕互联网涉税诈骗. <https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/chinatax/n810219/n810724/c5243181/content.html> ; 国家税务总局扬州市税务局 (2023). 扬州防范涉税诈骗宣传篇. <https://jiangsu.chinatax.gov.cn/art/2023/8/28/art_23038_432322.html> ; 国家税务总局 (2025). 涉税专业服务管理办法（试行）（国家税务总局令第 58 号，第三十二条第七项、第三十三条）. <https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100011/c5239044/content.html> ; 全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第六十二条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml> ; 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部 (2016). 关于办理电信网络诈骗等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的意见（法发〔2016〕32 号）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201612/t20161221_176278.shtml>
- Notes: these scams target newly registered individual businesses and small companies, with scripts like "our system shows you haven't filed; handle it today or be fined," then steering you to their own official account or mini-program to charge a few hundred yuan for a filing that is free and takes you minutes to do yourself. Using a market-rate bookkeeping agency isn't illegal, but it is not a government body and will never chase you in the tax bureau's name. If you have already paid or transferred money, call 110 or 96110 immediately to freeze the payment; see section 8, item 2.

### 11. Take deposits labeled "dingjin" not "yudingjin," state the penalty amount in figures, and vet credit terms as if lending money

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; use the characters "定金" in the contract, keep the amount within 20% of the contract value, collect payment before shipping, and check the counterparty before granting credit terms
- In plain terms: only the exact word "dingjin" (定金 — statutory deposit) triggers the double rule: if you breach, you forfeit it; if the other side breaches, they owe you double; anything above 20% of the contract value has no deposit effect. "Yudingjin" (订金 — advance payment) is usually treated as just a prepayment and protects nothing. State the penalty amount explicitly — courts can adjust penalties that are wildly too high or too low. Granting credit terms means lending the other side your money interest-free, and if they go under, it becomes a bad debt.
- Benefit: a deposit contract takes effect upon actual delivery; if the paying party breaches, the deposit is forfeited, and if the receiving party breaches, it must return double; the portion exceeding 20% of the main contract's value has no deposit effect; where the agreed penalty is excessively higher or lower than the loss, the court may adjust it at a party's request; where both a penalty and a deposit are agreed, you must choose one upon breach (nationwide, since 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第五百八十五、五百八十六、五百八十七、五百八十八条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: "yudingjin" (订金) has no deposit rules in the Civil Code and is usually treated as a mere prepayment — this is the prevailing practical view; this section did not verify case law. Credit terms are an interest-free loan of your own money, and a bankrupt counterparty means a bad debt; before granting credit, check the counterparty's record and litigation history on the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.

### 12. Sign written contracts within the first month of employment; complete social insurance registration within 30 days

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: the employer's share of social insurance contributions is a long-term expense (rates vary by locality); signing the contract itself costs 0 yuan — use the contract template from the local human resources and social security department
- In plain terms: if a month of employment passes without a written contract, you owe double wages every month starting from the second month, all the way up to the one-year mark. Social insurance must be registered within 30 days of hiring; failing to register draws a fine of 1 to 3 times the contributions owed plus 500–3,000 yuan on the person in charge, and unpaid contributions accrue a daily late surcharge of 0.05% (5 in 10,000). In law there is no such thing as "the employee voluntarily waives social insurance."
- Benefit: where employment lasts over one month but under one year without a written contract, double wages are owed monthly; social insurance registration for employees must be completed within 30 days of the start of employment, and failing that means an order to correct, then a fine of 1 to 3 times the social insurance contributions payable once the deadline passes, plus 500–3,000 yuan for the directly responsible person; contributions not paid in full and on time accrue a daily late surcharge of 5 in 10,000 from the date of arrears, and continued non-payment adds a fine of 1 to 3 times the unpaid amount; the employer must declare on its own behalf and pay in full and on time, with deferral or reduction only for statutory causes such as force majeure — there is no "employee voluntary waiver" option (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 劳动合同法（第十、八十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2010). 社会保险法（2018 年修正，第五十八、六十、八十四、八十六条）. <https://www.mohrss.gov.cn/xxgk2020/fdzdgknr/zcfg/fl/202011/t20201102_394629.html>
- Notes: double wages run from the second month up to the one-year mark, so delay only gets more expensive. Once an employee without a contract or social insurance suffers a workplace injury or files for labor arbitration, back contributions plus fines plus compensation usually far exceed what was saved.

### 13. Pay wages per the contract and the law; put raises, loans, and bonuses in writing

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; write labor remuneration clearly into the contract, and sign paper for every raise, bonus, advance, or loan — no verbal promises, and never pay company wages out of your own pocket
- In plain terms: wages must be paid in full and on time per the contract and the law, and if you fall behind, employees can go straight to court for a payment order. Raises, bonuses, and advances agreed verbally leave no evidence — when a real dispute comes, the side without proof loses. If you lend an employee money privately, write a written IOU stating the amount, interest, term, and repayment method; run private money through company accounts and a company loss becomes your own bad debt.
- Benefit: mandatory clauses of a labor contract include labor remuneration and social insurance; the employer must pay wages in full and on time per the contract and state rules, and for unpaid wages employees can apply directly to the court for a payment order. When a boss lends an employee money privately, it is a loan contract, which must be in writing unless otherwise agreed between natural persons, stating the amount, interest rate, term, and repayment method; bonuses promised verbally have no written evidence, and in a dispute the side without proof loses (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 劳动合同法（第十七、三十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第四百六十九、六百六十八条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: this is the neutral version of the reader's advice "don't over-sympathize when managing a company": it's not about refusing to help people, but about helping through the system. Once an out-of-system favor becomes routine, cutting it off turns into a dispute; when private loans and company accounts mix, a company loss lands as your own bad debt. The statutory parts are verified; the advice itself is experience, hence grade C.

### 14. Sell before you build: use pre-sales and small orders to verify demand before investing in production

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: tens to a few hundred yuan for a sample or a landing page, plus finding a few dozen real customers to prepay or place small orders; a few weeks slower than starting production outright
- In plain terms: following 116 Italian startups for about a year showed that founders trained to "state hypotheses first, then test them strictly against market feedback" performed better and were more willing to pivot in time — without giving up any earlier. It comes down to one line: if nobody will prepay and nobody will place a small order, don't believe "once it's built, buyers will come."
- Benefit: a randomized controlled trial tracking 116 Italian startups for about a year: founders trained to make decisions by "stating hypotheses first, then testing them rigorously with market feedback" achieved better performance and were more likely to pivot to alternatives in time, with no higher early abandonment rate; the authors' interpretation is that this approach reduces the chance of continuing to pour money into false-positive projects (Italy, published 2020)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Camuffo A, Cordova A, Gambardella A, Spina C (2020). A Scientific Approach to Entrepreneurial Decision Making: Evidence from a Randomized Control Trial. Management Science 66(2):564-586. <https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2018.3249>
- Notes: a single study, small sample, conducted in Italy, hence grade B; the citation and abstract were cross-checked against Crossref and Semantic Scholar, and the full-text effect sizes were not verified for this section. In practice: if nobody will prepay and nobody will place a small order, don't believe "build it and they will buy."

### 15. Run the prototype through a mass-production checklist before discussing production

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: a few hundred yuan for a small pilot run, plus a few hours to work out the items below: yield (how many of 100 units are sellable), supplier minimum order quantities and payment terms, how much cash inventory ties up, packaging and logistics, certifications and permits, trademarks
- In plain terms: the difference between one sample and a thousand units is where the money gets stuck: yield sets the true cost, minimum orders and payment terms set how much you must front, and inventory sets when the money comes back. On top of that, products on the compulsory certification catalogue cannot leave the factory or be sold without certification and the certification mark — selling them un-certified brings penalties under the rules.
- Benefit: the difference between one sample and a thousand units is where the money sits: yield determines the real cost, minimum order quantities and payment terms determine how much cash must be fronted, and inventory determines when the money comes back; products listed in the compulsory product certification catalogue may only leave the factory and be sold after certification and marking with the certification mark, and selling without certification is penalized under the certification and accreditation regulation; restaurants and food sellers need a food business license (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 质检总局 (2009). 强制性产品认证管理规定（质检总局令第 117 号，第二、四十九条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2010/content_1533513.htm>；市场监管总局 (2023). 食品经营许可和备案管理办法（市场监管总局令第 78 号，第四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10606/202307/content_6894763.html>
- Notes: the checklist itself is an experienced practice; the statutory parts were verified against original texts. Before production, search the CNIPA trademark search system for similar registrations and then file your trademark application; the red lines on using others' trademarks and designs are in items 16 and 17. For the compulsory certification catalogue, defer to the latest publication by the State Administration for Market Regulation.

### 16. Keep invoices and supplier details for every batch of stock, and refuse goods priced far below market: when an employee stocks counterfeits, it's the boss who gets sentenced

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; for every batch keep the purchase contract, invoices, payment records, and the supplier's business license and authorization documents; set an internal rule that whoever stocks the goods signs for them, and goods priced obviously below market are never taken in
- In plain terms: selling counterfeits reaches the criminal threshold at 30,000 yuan in illegal gains or 50,000 yuan in sales, drawing up to 3 years plus a fine, and 3 to 10 years at ten times that standard — and goods sitting unsold in the warehouse count too. "I didn't know they were fake" is not yours to decide: buying at prices obviously below market without a legitimate reason, authorization documents that are forged or altered, or moving and destroying goods and books after an inspection — all are treated directly as knowledge. Keep the purchase contracts, invoices, payment records, and supplier details, or the escape route of "lawfully acquired and able to name the supplier" is gone.
- Benefit: selling goods one knowingly bears a counterfeited registered trademark reaches the criminal threshold at 30,000 yuan or more in illegal gains or 50,000 yuan or more in sales, drawing up to 3 years of imprisonment plus a fine or a fine alone; at ten times that standard, 3 to 10 years plus a fine; unsold goods count too, and goods whose value reaches three times the sales threshold are likewise criminal. "Knowledge" doesn't require your confession: the 2025 SPC-SPP judicial interpretation lists five situations directly deemed as knowledge (except where evidence proves genuine ignorance) — knowing the registered trademark on the goods was altered, swapped, or covered; forging or altering the trademark owner's authorization documents or knowing they were forged or altered; having received criminal or administrative penalties for selling counterfeits and then selling the same kind again; buying or selling at prices obviously below market without legitimate reason; or after discovery by administrative or judicial authorities, transferring or destroying infringing goods, accounting vouchers, and other evidence, or providing false proof. When a company commits this offense, "the entity is fined, and the directly responsible managers and other directly responsible personnel" are punished under the same provision — so if an employee stocked the goods, it's the decision-maker who is fined and sentenced. Conversely, the Trademark Law leaves one way out: for selling goods without knowing they infringe a registered trademark, "whoever can prove the goods were lawfully obtained and can identify the supplier bears no liability to compensate" — that defense consists entirely of invoices and supplier details; without them it can't be used (nationwide, judicial interpretation in force from April 26, 2025)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 刑法修正案（十一）（第十七、二十四项，即刑法第二百一十四、二百二十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/202012/t20201227_503682.shtml>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2025). 关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2025〕5 号，2025 年 4 月 26 日起施行）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202504/t20250424_693977.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2019). 商标法（2019 年修正，第五十七、六十四条；2026 年 6 月 26 日修订后为第七十二、七十八条，自 2027 年 1 月 1 日起施行）. <https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2019/7/30/art_95_28179.html>、<https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2026/6/26/art_95_206942.html>
- Notes: cosmetics, liquor, mother-and-baby products, digital goods, and clothing are the high-incidence categories, with the common feature of huge price spreads for the same item — claims like "channel goods," "overruns," or "duty-free overseas" are no substitute for paperwork. There is also an administrative layer: where infringement is established, the infringing goods and tools are confiscated, and with illegal business revenue of 50,000 yuan or more a fine of up to 5 times may be imposed on top. If stock already taken in turns out wrong, immediately stop selling and seal it, contact the brand owner or the market regulation authority — don't keep selling, and above all don't destroy the goods or the books: destroying evidence is precisely the fifth situation presuming knowledge. On using others' designs, see the next item.

### 17. Every image on products, packaging, tags, and ads must be self-made or licensed; changing the color or adding an icon doesn't count as "changed"

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: licensed stock images start at tens of yuan per image, original commissioned design runs a few hundred to a few thousand yuan; keep an authorization certificate and purchase record for every image used on a product
- In plain terms: recoloring someone's image, swapping the background, or adding your own icon is still using their work without permission. Copying someone else's artwork for profit hits the criminal threshold at 30,000 yuan in illegal gains, 50,000 yuan in illegal business revenue, or 500 copies printed — that's the crime of copyright infringement, up to 3 years, and 3 to 10 years at ten times the standard; merely selling infringing copies printed by someone else draws up to 5 years for selling infringing copies at 50,000 yuan in illegal gains or 100,000 yuan in sales. On the trademark side, "similarity likely to cause confusion" is itself written into the infringement situations.
- Benefit: the Copyright Law lists "using a work through adaptation, translation, annotation, or other means without the copyright holder's permission" alongside direct copying as infringement, so changing colors, swapping backgrounds, or adding your own icon to the original image is still unauthorized use of someone's work; the criminal threshold here: copying and distributing artistic works for profit without the copyright holder's permission constitutes the crime of copyright infringement at 30,000 yuan or more in illegal gains, or 50,000 yuan or more in illegal business revenue, or 500 copies (sheets) or more in total, drawing up to 3 years plus a fine or a fine alone, and 3 to 10 years at ten times the standard; merely selling infringing copies printed by someone else draws up to 5 years for selling infringing copies at 50,000 yuan or more in illegal gains or 100,000 yuan or more in sales. The same logic on the trademark side: using a mark similar to a registered trademark on the same goods, or an identical or similar mark on similar goods, is within infringement scope where "confusion is likely" — "similarity" is an infringement situation written explicitly in the statute, and tweaking your way out of it doesn't create a safe zone (nationwide, judicial interpretation in force from April 26, 2025)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 著作权法（2020 年修正，第五十二条第六项、第五十三条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308796.html>；全国人大常委会 (2020). 刑法修正案（十一）（第二十、二十一项，即刑法第二百一十七、二百一十八条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/202012/t20201227_503682.shtml>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2025). 关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2025〕5 号）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202504/t20250424_693977.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2019). 商标法（2019 年修正，第五十七条第二项；2026 年修订后为第七十二条第二项）. <https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2019/7/30/art_95_28179.html>
- Notes: three common traps: cartoon characters and celebrity photos found online printed on clothes, mugs, and phone cases; fonts — most commercial fonts require separate licensing, and a free preview is not free commercial use; unlicensed assets slipped into design drafts made for clients, where liability still falls on the seller. To keep it simple, use stock libraries with commercial licenses and save the license pages, or have the designer state the asset sources and license scope in the contract and accept responsibility for problems. Beyond criminal liability there are civil damages and administrative fines; where the public interest is harmed, illegal gains may be confiscated and a fine of 1 to 5 times the illegal business revenue imposed. For checking trademarks before production, see item 15; if your own work is used by others, the same rules apply to enforce your rights.

### 18. Follow procedures within your post; before helping beyond them, think through who bears the liability

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; handle job duties by the rules, the doctor's orders, and the process, and don't make decisions for others beyond your own authority; before helping, first ask "if something goes wrong, who is responsible"
- In plain terms: on-the-job matters go by procedure and by doctor's orders — step beyond your authority to decide for someone else, and when things go wrong the liability lands on you. Conversely, the law does not require you to stand by while someone dies: voluntary emergency rescue that harms the person being helped carries no civil liability for the rescuer.
- Benefit: taking nurses as an example, the statutory boundaries of the role are: notify the physician immediately upon finding a patient in critical condition, and in an emergency, perform necessary emergency care first to save a critically ill patient; where a doctor's order violates laws or regulations or technical standards for diagnosis and treatment, raise it with the physician and, when necessary, report to the head of the department. The other side must also be stated: the Civil Code provides that a rescuer who voluntarily performs emergency aid bears no civil liability for harm to the person being helped — the law protects good-faith rescue, not "help someone and you pay" (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 国务院 (2008). 护士条例（国务院令第 517 号，第十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6169.htm>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一百八十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: this is the neutral version of the reader's advice "don't meddle in other people's business": within your post, follow procedure; emergency rescue is legally protected; for everything else, first establish whose authority and whose responsibility it is, and don't substitute private goodwill for decisions the system should make. ### 19. When the business fails, exit by the book: use simplified deregistration if you can, file for bankruptcy when insolvent, and never just walk away

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: simplified deregistration requires a 20-day public notice on the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System and can be done yourself; businesses with debts must first liquidate, and bankruptcy means applying to the court, taking months
- In plain terms: with no debts or all debts cleared, no unpaid wages, taxes, or social insurance, and a written commitment from all investors, you can deregister via simplified procedure once the 20-day public notice passes without objection — individual businesses need no public notice and can deregister directly after 10 days without objection. When liabilities exceed assets and due debts can't be paid, apply to the court for bankruptcy. The cost of walking away: missing annual reports puts you on the abnormal-operations list, three years there puts you on the serious-violations list, the legal representative cannot serve as another company's legal representative for 3 years — and once on the abnormal-operations list, even simplified deregistration is blocked.
- Benefit: where there are no claims or debts or they are fully settled, no unpaid wages, social insurance, or taxes, and all investors give written commitment, simplified deregistration is available after a 20-day public notice without objection; individual businesses need no public notice and are deregistered directly if the relevant departments raise no objection within 10 days; a company unable to pay due debts and insolvent may apply to the court for reorganization, composition, or bankruptcy liquidation, and where a dissolved company has not been liquidated and is insolvent, those bearing liquidation responsibility should apply for bankruptcy liquidation. Walking away: failing to publish the annual report on time means listing on the abnormal-operations list; three years without fulfilling the disclosure obligation means listing on the serious-violations enterprise list; the legal representative or person in charge may not serve as legal representative or person in charge of another enterprise for 3 years; entities on the abnormal-operations list cannot use simplified deregistration (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
