# 12. Starting a business: don't bet the family savings

This section counts only money and legal liability—not life expectancy. When you open a shop or register a company, which steps can drag down your own money, family money, and your spouse's money with the business? What does the law require, and what can you separate in advance? Items that most often wipe out savings but are easiest to avoid come first. Signature-heavy items sit up front; do-it-yourself steps come later. Items backed only by experience, with no literature, are grade C.

Items are grouped by theme, with item numbers in parentheses.

**Before opening, don't bet the nest egg**: Only put in money you can afford to lose (item 1); don't sign personal guarantees on company loans (item 2); pick the right entity before you open (item 3); don't be a nominal shareholder (item 4); check franchise filing before joining (item 5); ask the right questions before paying someone to run your online store (item 24).

**Registration and licenses**: Fix name, premises, business scope, and registered capital before registration (item 6); don't open until licenses are in hand (item 7).

**Food**: See which regulatory tier you fall into (item 8); prepackaged food labels (item 9); ordinary food must not claim to treat disease (item 10); criminal lines for food (item 11).

**Tax, annual reports, and invoices**: File on time even with zero income (item 12); issue invoices only for real transactions (item 13); anyone posing as the tax bureau demanding payment is a scam (item 14); file last year's annual report online in the first half of each year (item 25).

**Contracts and hiring**: When taking money, write deposit (定金), not booking-fee wording (订金) (item 15); sign employment contracts within the first month (item 16); pay wages per contract and law (item 17); follow procedures on the job (item 22).

**Products and sourcing**: Sell first, then make (item 18); run samples through a mass-production checklist (item 19); keep purchase receipts and upstream supplier details (item 20); use your own designs or buy a license (item 21).

**Exit**: If you lose money, exit by the book (item 23).
### 1. Only put in money you can afford to lose; don't touch family savings or borrow to open
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Before you start, set a cap: you could lose every yuan of it and still pay the mortgage, support the kids, and see a doctor. Don't invest above that cap. Family money and borrowed money don't count toward the cap. The hard part is when the business looks like it's picking up and you want to pour in more.
- In plain terms: Debts of a sole proprietorship come out of assets in your name. If it's unclear whether the whole family ran the business, creditors can reach family property. A company doesn't fully shield you either: when the company can't pay, what you promised as subscribed registered capital but haven't paid yet can be called in early. Size your stake by «I could lose it all and still pay the mortgage and medical bills».
- Benefit: Losing money isn't the only risk. For sole proprietorships, if you ran the business alone, debts are paid from personal property; if the family ran it together or ownership is unclear, from family property. Debts both spouses signed count as joint marital debt; later assent by one spouse counts too. A loan in one spouse's name still counts as joint debt if the creditor shows it went into the couple's shared business. For companies: when the company can't pay a due debt, creditors may demand early payment of subscribed registered capital not yet due (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第五十六、一千零六十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四十七、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Notes: «Affordable to lose» means losing it all wouldn't force you to sell the home or borrow from relatives. This round found no verifiable official statistics on survival rates or average lifespan on the National Bureau of Statistics or State Administration for Market Regulation sites, so the text gives legal consequences only, not headcount figures—grade B.

### 2. Don't sign personal guarantees on company loans; spouses should stay off the signature line
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. When a bank, supplier, or landlord wants a «personal joint-and-several guarantee» or a spouse's signature, pause. That page is you giving up «company debt stays with the company» (limited liability). Don't sign if you can avoid it. If you must, choose «general guarantee» only and cap how much you guarantee. The hard part is «no signature, no loan»—you have to hold the line.
- In plain terms: «Joint-and-several guarantee» means if the company doesn't pay, the creditor can skip the company and come straight to you. «General guarantee» means sue or arbitrate against the company first, enforce on company assets, and only then collect any shortfall from you. If your spouse signs too, both households' assets are in play and the company's «limited» liability stops protecting you personally.
- Benefit: Guarantee wording means «if the debtor doesn't pay, you pay». With «joint-and-several guarantee», creditors need not pursue the company first—they can demand payment from you directly. With «general guarantee», they must sue or arbitrate against the company and enforce on its assets first; only the remainder can be collected from you. Debts both spouses guaranteed count as joint marital debt; later assent counts too. A spouse's signature puts both sides' family assets on the hook (nationwide, from 2021).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第六百八十一、六百八十七、六百八十八、一千零六十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: Banks lending to small and micro enterprises almost always require shareholder personal joint-and-several guarantees. Loans are still available; know what that page costs before you sign, and keep guaranteed amounts within the «affordable to lose» cap in item 1. General rules for IOUs and guarantees: section 8, item 18.

### 3. Pick the right entity before opening: sole props and general partners pay to the end; only LLCs are «limited»
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Register on the local government services portal— a few hours once. After you form a limited liability company, monthly bookkeeping and tax filing are ongoing: your time if you do it yourself, or paid bookkeeping, with prices varying by region.
- In plain terms: Sole proprietors and general partners in ordinary partnerships owe without limit—pay what you owe until it's cleared. LLC shareholders owe only up to what they subscribed at registration. But subscribed capital must be paid in within 5 years: write 1 million, you may owe up to 1 million. Mix company money with household money and «limited» can fail.
- Benefit: Sole proprietorship debts are paid from personal or family property. General partners in an ordinary partnership bear unlimited joint and several liability—pay in full, and creditors may demand the entire debt from one partner alone. In a limited partnership, limited partners are liable only up to subscribed capital. LLC shareholders are liable only up to subscribed capital. «Limited» fails in three situations: subscribed capital must be fully paid within 5 years of formation, and if the company can't pay due debts, creditors may demand early payment; capital withdrawn after contribution must be returned; using the company as a shell to dodge debt—including a one-shareholder company that can't show separate company and personal funds—makes you personally liable for company debts (nationwide, from July 2024).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第五十六条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2006). 合伙企业法（2006 年修订，第二条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2006/content_413955.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四、二十三、四十七、五十三、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Notes: «Limited» requires clean separation: don't mix company and personal accounts, don't pay household bills from the company, keep complete books. Don't inflate registered capital for show—whatever you subscribe must be paid within 5 years. 1 million subscribed means up to 1 million of potential debt.

### 4. Don't be a nominal shareholder or hold shares for someone else
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. When someone asks to «borrow your ID for a shareholder slot» or «hold shares in your name for now», refuse.
- In plain terms: If your name is on the register, you're a shareholder in law. Pay subscribed capital on time; miss the deadline and you owe make-up plus company losses. If another founder didn't really pay in, you may share liability for the gap. Someone else runs the business; the debt sits on your name.
- Benefit: A registered shareholder is a legal shareholder. Pay subscribed capital in full by the deadline in the articles; otherwise pay the shortfall and compensate company losses. If a founder didn't contribute at formation, other founders at formation share joint liability for that gap. When the company can't pay due debts, creditors may demand early payment of capital not yet due. The operator isn't on the register; the obligation is still yours (nationwide, from July 2024).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四十七、四十九、五十、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>；最高人民法院 (2020 修正). 关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》若干问题的规定（三）（法释〔2011〕3 号，2014 年法释〔2014〕2 号、2020 年法释〔2020〕18 号两次修正，第二十四、二十五条）：「如无法律规定的无效情形，人民法院应当认定该合同有效」「实际出资人未经公司其他股东半数以上同意，请求公司变更股东……人民法院不予支持」「名义股东处分股权造成实际出资人损失，实际出资人请求名义股东承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持」. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808181799def980179ac07a9ca117c>（国家法律法规数据库）；最高人民法院 (2025). 关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》若干问题的解释（征求意见稿）第九十条. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/477881.html>
- Notes: A nominee agreement between nominal and actual investor is valid unless a statutory invalidity applies. The actual investor can't get their name on the register without consent from over half of the other shareholders. If the nominal holder sells or pledges shares, courts apply good-faith acquisition rules: a buyer who didn't know, paid fairly, and registered may keep the shares; the investor can only sue the nominal holder. A draft judicial interpretation was released September 2025; when finalized it replaces the current text—for now the 2020 amended version remains in force as of September 2026. Risks of being a nominal legal representative: section 8, item 28.

### 5. Before franchising, check MOFCOM filing, get written disclosure, and put a cooling-off period in the contract
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. On the Ministry of Commerce «Commercial Franchise Information Management System» site, check whether the brand is filed. Before signing, require written disclosure of everything they must tell you, together with the contract text. A few hours.
- In plain terms: By rule the franchisor needs at least 2 company-owned stores open 1 year, and must file with commerce authorities within 15 days after signing. Before signing it must give you 12 topics in writing—fees, when deposits return, how existing stores perform, and more. No filing, no disclosure, or pay-before-you-see-contract: walk away.
- Benefit: The franchisor must have at least 2 directly operated stores running 1 year or more. Within 15 days of the first franchise contract it must file with the commerce department; no filing: fine 10,000–50,000 yuan; late filing: 50,000–100,000 yuan plus public notice. Before signing it must deliver written disclosure on 12 items—including fee types and amounts, deposit refund conditions, franchisee count and performance, audited report summaries for the last 2 years, litigation and arbitration in the last 5 years. The contract must state that the franchisee may unilaterally terminate within a period after signing. Concealment or false disclosure lets the franchisee cancel the contract (nationwide, from May 2007).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2007). 商业特许经营管理条例（国务院令第 485 号，第七、八、十二、二十二、二十三、二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_4179.htm>；商务部 (2012). 商业特许经营信息披露管理办法（商务部令 2012 年第 2 号，第五、九条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2012/content_2177025.htm>；商务部. 商业特许经营信息管理系统. <https://txjy.syggs.mofcom.gov.cn/>
- Notes: No filing, no disclosure file, or pay-before-reading contract: leave. «Cooling-off» is the post-signing window for unilateral exit; the regulation only says «a certain period», so get the day count written in. On disclosed average sales, gross, or net profit, ask how the brand sourced the numbers—the rules require disclosure of sources. Schemes that skip «franchise» and only charge «store operations» or «opening training» fees: item 24.

### 6. Before registration, fix name, premises, scope, and registered capital; with complete papers you can get the license on the spot
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Registration costs nothing. Enterprise and individual-business registration fees were abolished from 1 January 2015. Do it yourself on the local government services site or at the service hall— a few hours. Seal carving, paid bookkeeping, and a registered address (address hosting) are market services, not government fees.
- In plain terms: Registration is free; the registration fee has not been charged since 2015. Only five material types: application, identity or eligibility proof, premises proof, articles or partnership agreement (not for sole props), plus anything else the rules name. Complete and correct papers: license on the spot; otherwise within 3 working days. Money taken is usually agency fees.
- Benefit: The regulation lists exactly five items. «(1) Application form». «(2) Applicant qualification documents and natural-person ID». «(3) Domicile or main business premises documents». «(4) Articles for companies, non-company enterprise legal persons, farmer specialized cooperatives (unions), or partnership agreement». «(5) Other materials required by laws, administrative regulations, and the State Council market supervision department». In plain language: one application; proof of who you are or that you're eligible; proof you may use the shop or office; articles or partnership agreement; plus anything else required. Companies also submit appointment documents and ID for the legal representative, directors, supervisors, and senior managers. Sole props don't file articles. The name is «declared by the applicant according to law». Registered capital uses «subscribed registration»—state the amount promised, no payment at the counter. Business scope is chosen from the authority's published categories. Complete and correctly formatted materials are «confirmed and registered on the spot». If not finished on the spot, «within 3 working days», with up to 3 more for complex cases. «The business license issue date is the establishment date»—that printed date is when the entity is born (nationwide, from March 2022).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2021). 市场主体登记管理条例（国务院令第 746 号，第十、十三、十四、十六、十九、二十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5636139.htm> ; 市场监管总局 (2022). 市场主体登记管理条例实施细则（市场监管总局令第 52 号，第十二、二十五、二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-03/02/content_5676403.htm> ; 财政部、国家发展改革委 (2014). 关于取消、停征和免征一批行政事业性收费的通知（财税〔2014〕101 号，附件 1）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2014-12/23/content_5023513.htm>
- Notes: Registration itself is free; charges are usually agency fees. Seal prices are market-set. The General Office of the State Council forbids mandating a designated seal maker—you need not accept «must use the designated shop». Registered capital doesn't buy prestige; it caps liability and must be paid within 5 years—see item 3. If scope includes licensed activities, don't open until licensed—see item 7.

### 7. Licensed trades: don't open until the license is issued
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Before opening, check whether your line needs a license. Catering and selling food need a food business license. Selling only others' prepackaged food needs no license—file for record instead. Open only after the license or filing is done.
- In plain terms: Opening without a license: at minimum cease operations, confiscate illegal gains, fine up to 10,000 yuan. For state-monopoly goods or securities, futures, insurance, or payment-settlement businesses without approval, serious cases can be illegal business operation—prison from up to 5 years. Renovate first, «soft open» before the license—the common misstep.
- Benefit: Operating without authorization is handled by the department the law designates. No license where the law doesn't spell out a penalty: stop, confiscate gains, fine up to 10,000 yuan. Heavier track: monopoly or restricted goods without permission, or securities, futures, insurance, or payment settlement without approval—serious cases are illegal business operation, up to 5 years' imprisonment or short-term detention plus a fine of 1–5 times illegal gains; especially serious, above 5 years (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2017). 无证无照经营查处办法（国务院令第 684 号，第五、六、十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-08/23/content_5219861.htm>；市场监管总局 (2023). 食品经营许可和备案管理办法（市场监管总局令第 78 号，第四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10606/202307/content_6894763.html>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>
- Notes: Finance, medical, and training each have their own licensing laws; this item only checked generic enforcement and criminal articles. Milk tea and snack shops are catering—food business license required. Renovate-first, trial-before-license is a common trap. Food tiers and whether fresh meat and vegetables need a license: item 8.

### 8. For food, know your tier: production and catering need a license; prepackaged-only sales need filing; fresh meat and vegetables need no license
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: The license itself is free. Premises and equipment must pass standards; site inspection plus processing take weeks. Meat sellers must keep quarantine certificates and meat quality inspection certificates for each batch.
- In plain terms: Make and sell yourself—snack bar, braised goods, baking, homemade pickles—all need a license. Sell only others' prepackaged food: no license; file with the county-level or higher market supervision department. Fresh meat and vegetables need no license. Without a license, minimum fine 50,000–100,000 yuan—often above a small shop's stake.
- Benefit: Food Safety Law: food businesses need a license first—produce, sell, or cater. «However, selling edible agricultural products and selling prepackaged food only do not require a license. Selling prepackaged food only shall be filed with the local county-level or above food safety supervision department.» Edible agricultural products are unprocessed meat, vegetables, etc. Prepackaged food is made and sold in packaging by others. «Specific rules for small food workshops and food vendors are set by provinces.» Operating without a license: confiscate illegal gains, food, tools, equipment, and raw materials. If value of goods (货值金额) is under 10,000 yuan, additionally fine 50,000–100,000 yuan; if 10,000 yuan or more, fine 10–20 times value. Pig Slaughter Regulations: pigs only at designated slaughter plants with official quarantine. «Except personal self-slaughter for consumption in rural areas, no unit or individual may slaughter pigs without designation.» «Pork sold or used in catering and centralized meals must come from designated plants with quarantine and meat quality inspection passed.» Private slaughter: shut down, confiscate pigs, tools, gains; fines same as unlicensed food operation. Animal Epidemic Prevention Law: sold or shipped animal products must carry quarantine certificates and marks; missing them: order correction, fine up to one times value of compliant goods. Judicial interpretation: food from small workshops or vendors below safety standards—buyers may claim tenfold punitive damages; courts should uphold (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2025 年第三次修正). 食品安全法（第三十五、三十六、一百二十二条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=7b5a76d0461745a08d3f964916b87ef3>；国务院 (2021). 生猪屠宰管理条例（国务院令第 742 号，第二、二十三、三十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-07/22/content_5626534.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2021 修订). 动物防疫法（第五十一、一百条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8080817703add2017737395a973e31>；最高人民法院 (2024). 关于审理食品药品惩罚性赔偿纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2024〕9 号，第四条）. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/bf531a70471f10d377788f28d3d8f8.html>
- Notes: Thresholds for small workshops, small catering, and street vendors are provincial—no numbers here. On your provincial market supervision site search «small food workshop small catering vendor regulations». Online homemade food still needs licenses where required. Also see item 6 for entity and license, item 7 for other licensed trades, section 5 item 31 for tenfold food damages.

### 9. Bag it and sell it—that's prepackaged food: production date, shelf life, and ingredient list are all mandatory on the label
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Labels cost cents to dimes each. Every batch needs correct dates and ingredients—some time.
- In plain terms: Food put in packaging before sale is prepackaged food; the package must have a label with nine required items including production date and shelf life. Bulk food still needs name, date, and seller name and address on the container. Missing required label items: if value under 10,000 yuan, fine 5,000–50,000 yuan; if 10,000 yuan or more, fine 5–10 times value.
- Benefit: Food Safety Law: prepackaged food must have a label with name, spec, net content, production date, ingredients (配料表), producer name, address, contact, shelf life, product standard code, storage conditions, additive generic names under national standards, production license number, plus anything else law or standards require. Bulk food sold by weight needs on container or outer pack: food name, production date or batch, shelf life, operator name, address, contact. Prepackaged food without a label or with noncompliant label/instructions: confiscate gains and food. Value under 10,000 yuan: additionally fine 5,000–50,000 yuan; 10,000 yuan or more: 5–10 times value. Serious cases: stop production until license revoked. Minor label defects that don't affect safety or mislead consumers: correct first; if refused, fine up to 2,000 yuan. GB 7718-2025 Prepackaged Food Labeling General Rules published 16 March 2025, effective 16 March 2027, two-year transition—requires a dedicated area showing production date and expiry, high-contrast legible dates in year-month-day order (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2025 年第三次修正). 食品安全法（第六十七、六十八、一百二十五条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=7b5a76d0461745a08d3f964916b87ef3>；国家卫生健康委员会、国家市场监督管理总局 (2025). 关于发布《食品安全国家标准 预包装食品标签通则》（GB 7718-2025）等 50 项食品安全国家标准和 9 项修改单的公告（2025 年第 2 号）. <https://jkw.lasa.gov.cn/wswyh/wjzl/202504/a90766908ef24869a74f7373bf42463f.shtml>（拉萨市卫生健康委员会转载公告全文）；最高人民法院 (2024). 关于审理食品药品惩罚性赔偿纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2024〕9 号，第六、八条）. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/bf531a70471f10d377788f28d3d8f8.html>（标签瑕疵与必标事项缺失的分档）；北京市市场监督管理局 (2026). 找不到生产日期？食品标签要「变脸」！新规这些重点必看. <https://scjgj.beijing.gov.cn/zwxx/scjgdt/202605/t20260507_4639154.html>（实施日期、过渡期与日期展示新要求）
- Notes: Two defect tiers drive fines and damages. Minor: typos, font issues—no safety impact, no misleading—correct only; if refused, fine up to 2,000 yuan; no tenfold from buyers. Missing mandatory items like production date or shelf life are not minor: fines as above; buyers may also claim tenfold on purchase price, minimum 1,000 yuan if tenfold is lower (section 5 item 31). GB 7718-2025 date and display requirements checked via announcement and official interpretation; exact format follows the standard text.

### 10. Ordinary food must not claim to treat disease—labels, instructions, ads, and live-stream scripts count
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Review ad copy, product pages, and live-stream scripts. The hard part is not saying «lower the three highs» or «treat constipation».
- In plain terms: Labels, instructions, and ads for ordinary food must not claim disease prevention or treatment. Except medical, drug, and device ads, no ad may claim treatment or use medical-sounding words. Penalty: 1–3 times ad spend; if spend unknown, 100,000–200,000 yuan. Serious cases can revoke the business license.
- Benefit: Food Safety Law: «Labels and instructions for food and food additives must not contain false content and must not involve disease prevention or treatment functions. Producers and operators are responsible for label and instruction content.» «Food advertising shall be true and lawful, must not contain false content or involve disease prevention or treatment; food operators are responsible for ad truth and legality.» Packaging, instructions, and ads can't claim prevention or cure; sellers answer for what they write. False or misleading food ads are penalized under the Advertising Law: «Except medical, drug, and medical device advertising, no other advertising may involve disease treatment functions, and must not use medical terms or terms easily confused with drugs or devices.» Violations: stop the ad; advertiser must clarify and remove impact in the scope published; fine 1–3 times ad fees; if fees unknown or clearly low, 100,000–200,000 yuan; serious: 3–5 times fees or 200,000–1,000,000 yuan, and may revoke the license (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A

### 13. Issue invoices only for real transactions; small-scale taxpayers should use the full VAT exemption allowance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->

- In plain terms: If you are a small-scale taxpayer on the simplified VAT regime, and monthly sales stay under 100,000 yuan or quarterly sales under 300,000 yuan, you owe no VAT. - Benefit: Small-scale taxpayers on the simplified regime are covered by the rule that (销售额未达到起征点的，免征增值税) (sales below the threshold are exempt from VAT). ### 15. Write «定金» not «订金» in contracts, state penalty amounts in figures, and treat payment terms like lending
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: No cost. Use the two characters «定金» in the contract; the amount must not exceed 20% of the contract total. Collect payment before delivery. Before agreeing to deliver first and pay later (credit terms), check the counterparty first.

- In plain terms: Only «定金» triggers the double rule: if you breach, you forfeit it; if they breach, they return double. Amounts above 20% of the contract total do not count as deposit. «订金» has no such rule in law. Credit terms are interest-free use of your money; if they fail, it is a bad debt.

- Benefit: A deposit is formed «自实际交付时成立» (when money is actually delivered). If the paying party breaches, the deposit is forfeited. If the receiving party breaches, they return the original sum plus an equal amount (double return). The portion above 20% of the main contract amount is not treated as deposit. If agreed liquidated damages are far above or below actual loss, a court may adjust up or down on request. If both liquidated damages and a deposit clause appear, the injured party may claim only one, not both (nationwide, from 2021).

- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第五百八十五、五百八十六、五百八十七、五百八十八条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>

- Notes: «订金» under the Civil Code lacks the deposit rules and is usually treated as advance payment—common practice; this section did not verify a specific ruling. Credit terms are lending your funds at zero interest; default means a bad debt. Before granting terms, search the counterparty on the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System for litigation history.

### 16. Sign a written labor contract within the first month; register for social insurance within 30 days

### 18. Sell first, then produce: use pre-sales and small orders to prove demand before you invest in production
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: Tens to hundreds of yuan for a sample or a landing page, then reach dozens of real customers for prepayment or small orders. That is slower by weeks than making stock first. The hard part is not producing before anyone pays.

- In plain terms: One study followed 116 new Italian firms for about a year: founders who set a hypothesis and tested it against market feedback ran better businesses, pivoted sooner when needed, and did not quit earlier. If nobody prepays or places a small order, do not trust «做出来自然有人买» (make it and buyers will appear).

- Benefit: A randomized controlled trial tracked 116 Italian startups for about one year (participants split at random; one group got training, outcomes compared). Founders trained to decide by (先提假设、再用市场反馈严格检验) (hypothesis first, then strict market feedback tests) performed better, were more likely to pivot in time, and did not drop out more often. The authors argue this cuts spending on false-positive projects that look viable but are not (Italy, published 2020).

### 20. Keep invoices and supplier records for every purchase batch; skip goods priced far below market—if staff buy counterfeits, the owner can still be convicted
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->

- Cost: No cost. For each batch keep purchase contract, invoice, payment record, supplier business license, and authorization. Rule internally: whoever buys signs; never accept prices clearly below market. The hard part is passing up cheap stock that looks profitable.

- In plain terms: Selling counterfeits: illegal gains of 30,000 yuan or sales of 50,000 yuan meets the criminal threshold—up to three years. Keeping contract, invoice, payment trail, and supplier identity is the path to the no-damages defense.

- Benefit: Knowingly selling goods bearing counterfeit registered trademarks: illegal gains ≥30,000 yuan or sales ≥50,000 yuan triggers up to three years plus fines (or fines alone). Trademark law offers an out: selling without knowing infringement, (能证明该商品是自己合法取得并说明提供者的，不承担赔偿责任) (lawful acquisition with provider identified)—no compensation; without invoices and supplier info the defense fails (nationwide; interpretation effective 26 April 2025).

- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2020). 刑法修正案（十一）（第十七、二十四项，即刑法第二百一十四、二百二十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/202012/t20201227_503682.shtml>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2025). 关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2025〕5 号，2025 年 4 月 26 日起施行）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202504/t20250424_693977.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2019). 商标法（2019 年修正，第五十七、六十四条；2026 年 6 月 26 日修订后为第七十二、七十八条，自 2027 年 1 月 1 日起施行）. <https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2019/7/30/art_95_28179.html>、<https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2026/6/26/art_95_206942.html>

- Notes: Cosmetics, liquor, baby goods, electronics, and apparel see many cases—large price spreads on the same SKU. «渠道货», «尾单», «海外免税» do not replace paperwork. Beyond criminal liability, administrative fines: infringing goods and tools confiscated; illegal turnover above 50,000 yuan can draw a fine up to five times turnover. If stock is bad, stop selling, seal it, contact brand or market regulators—do not keep selling or destroy goods and books; destruction fits presumption (5). Artwork rules: Section 12, item 21. One scam: free pens or small goods «consignment», later a buyer and evidence, then infringement claims—free goods lack contract, payment, and real provider; all three conditions for lawful acquisition fail. Do not list door-to-door free stock with only a WeChat contact. Real consignment needs a written agreement, ID and business license copies, and who owns goods and bears infringement risk.

### 21. Art on products, packaging, tags, and ads must be yours or licensed—recoloring or adding icons is not «different enough»
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->

- Cost: Licensed stock art from tens of yuan per image; original design, hundreds to thousands. Keep license proof and purchase record for every image used on goods. The hard part is not grabbing random web images.

- In plain terms: Changing colors, background, or slapping on your logo on someone else’s work is still unlicensed use. Reproducing others’ art for profit—illegal gains ≥30,000 yuan, turnover ≥50,000 yuan, or ≥500 copies—can be copyright infringement, up to three years. On trademarks, «容易导致混淆的» (likely to cause confusion) is infringement by itself.

- Benefit: Copyright law treats (未经著作权人许可，以改编、翻译、注释等方式使用作品) (adaptation, translation, annotation without permission) like copying—recolor, new background, or added icons still count. Criminal thresholds for reproducing and selling art without permission: illegal gains ≥30,000 yuan, illegal business amount ≥50,000 yuan, or ≥500 copies (sheets)—copyright infringement, up to three years plus fines (or fines alone); ten times those levels, three to ten years. Selling others’ infringing copies: illegal gains ≥50,000 yuan or sales ≥100,000 yuan—crime of selling infringing copies, up to five years. Trademarks: same goods with a similar mark, or similar goods with same or similar marks, «容易导致混淆的» is listed infringement; «近似» is explicit—small edits still infringe (nationwide; interpretation effective 26 April 2025).

- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2020). 著作权法（2020 年修正，第五十二条第六项、第五十三条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308796.html>；全国人大常委会 (2020). 刑法修正案（十一）（第二十、二十一项，即刑法第二百一十七、二百一十八条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/202012/t20201227_503682.shtml>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2025). 关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2025〕5 号）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202504/t20250424_693977.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2019). 商标法（2019 年修正，第五十七条第二项；2026 年修订后为第七十二条第二项）. <https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2019/7/30/art_95_28179.html>

- Notes: Three common slips: (1) web cartoon or celebrity images on apparel, mugs, cases; (2) fonts—commercial fonts usually need separate license, preview ≠ commercial use; (3) client designs with unlicensed assets—the seller still liable. Easiest path: commercial stock with saved license pages, or a design contract stating asset source, scope, and designer liability. Beyond criminal exposure, civil damages and admin fines—public interest harm can mean confiscation and fines of one to five times illegal turnover. Pre-production trademark check: Section 12, item 19. If others use your art, same rules apply. Posting others’ comics or shows online for ads or membership fees: Section 9, item 24.

### 22. Work within posted procedures; before off-book favors, decide who bears liability
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->

- Cost: No cost. Follow rules, medical orders, and process at work. Do not decide outside your authority for others. Before helping, ask who is responsible if something goes wrong. The hard part is refusing on-the-spot «通融一下» (just bend the rules).

- In plain terms: Nurses, for example: regulations require immediate physician notice when a patient is critically ill, and raising non-compliant orders with the prescriber. Other jobs: know where procedures draw the «must report» line. The law does not require standing by: voluntary emergency aid that injures the person helped does not create compensation liability.

- Benefit: Nurse regulations set duty boundaries: critical illness → notify physician at once; emergencies allow necessary rescue before physician arrival; illegal or non-standard orders → raise with prescriber, report to department head if needed. Civil Code: voluntary emergency assistance that harms the rescued party—«不承担民事责任» (no civil liability) (nationwide).

- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:国务院 (2008). 护士条例（国务院令第 517 号，第十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6169.htm>；国务院 (2020). 护士条例（2020 年修订，第十七条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808081777d07c50177b8ed29bf3950>（国家法律法规数据库）；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一百八十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>

- Notes: Inside your role, follow procedure; emergency rescue is legally protected. Otherwise separate authority from responsibility—do not replace institutional decisions with personal favors. Nurse Regulations 2020 revision (State Council Order 726) changed registration only; Article 17 unchanged. Physician Law clauses on clinical standards were not opened from official text this round, so not quoted.

### 23. When you are losing, exit by the book: simplified deregistration if you qualify, bankruptcy if insolvent—do not walk away
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: Simplified deregistration you can do yourself: post on the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System for 20 days. If debts remain, liquidate—collect receivables, pay creditors. Bankruptcy needs a court filing and months. The hard part is facing losses instead of stalling.

- In plain terms: No debts, no unpaid wages, tax, or social insurance: all investors sign a commitment, 20-day public notice with no objection → simplified deregistration. Sole proprietors skip the notice; no objection within 10 days → deregister. Cannot pay debts → apply for bankruptcy. Ignore filings for two years and become unreachable at the registered address → license revoked; responsible legal representative barred from that role for three years.

- Benefit: No debts owed or owed to you, or all settled; no unpaid wages, social insurance, or tax; all investors’ written commitment—then 20-day notice with no objection allows simplified deregistration. Sole proprietors: no notice; no objection within 10 days → direct deregistration. Company cannot pay due debts and liabilities exceed assets («资不抵债»)—may apply to court for reorganization, composition, or bankruptcy liquidation. Dissolved but not liquidated with insolvency—the liquidator must apply for bankruptcy liquidation. Neglect: late annual report → abnormal operation listing. Two consecutive years no report, still on the list unchanged, unreachable at registered address → business license revoked. Person responsible as legal representative of a revoked entity: barred from legal representative and director, supervisor, or senior manager roles for three years from revocation. Already on the abnormal list → no simplified deregistration (nationwide).

- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2021). 市场主体登记管理条例（国务院令第 746 号，第三十一、三十二、三十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-08/24/content_5632964.htm>；市场监管总局等六部门 (2025). 企业注销指引（2025 年修订）（2025 年第 52 号公告）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202512/content_7053238.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2006). 企业破产法（第二、七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2006/content_413952.htm>；国务院 (2024 修订). 企业信息公示暂行条例（国务院令第 777 号修订，第十八条）：「企业因连续2年未按规定报送年度报告被列入经营异常名录未改正，且通过登记的住所或者经营场所无法取得联系的，由县级以上市场监督管理部门吊销营业执照」. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8081819c46fcc5019cd18fc6e9557d>（国家法律法规数据库）；国务院 (2024). 关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定（国务院令第 777 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11246/202403/content_6941844.html>；市场主体登记管理条例（第十二条第四项）与公司法（2023 年修订，第一百七十八条第一款第四项）：担任因违法被吊销营业执照的企业的法定代表人并负有个人责任的，自被吊销营业执照之日起未逾 3 年不得担任法定代表人和董事、监事、高级管理人员. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-08/24/content_5632964.htm>；<https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>

- Notes: Enterprise Information Publicity Interim Regulations revised by State Council Order 777, effective 1 May 2024, unchanged since. Old Article 17 (three years abnormal → serious illegal list) is gone; the serious untrustworthy list now targets serious false annual reports. Many online posts still cite the old text—do not copy. When and how to file annual reports: Section 12, item 25. Individual bankruptcy pilots exist in some regions only—not verified here, not covered.

### 24. Before paying for store agency, shop-opening training, or «无货源» (no-inventory) schemes, ask what extra fees follow and where sales numbers come from
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: No cost before paying. Get three things first: written contract, the company’s full legal name, and refund terms. Pay only to the company on the contract, never a personal account. The hard part is not paying when shown «学员月入过万» (trainees earn tens of thousands a month) screenshots.

- In plain terms: Scams take a small fee first, then «升级套餐», advance goods payment, or «解冻金» (unlock fees) again and again. Backend orders and income may be faked; withdrawal fails when you try. Fake-order boosting is illegal; agencies caught face penalties.

- Benefit: Guangdong Higher People’s Court ruled on an agency fraud: Jiang, Xu, and 55 others ran companies in Guangzhou, Nanning, and Hangzhou. The court found they «明知根本不具备电商代运营的能力和技术条件» (knew they lacked e-commerce agency capability) yet sold or upgraded «服务套餐» to 7,711 victims for more than 52.994 million yuan. Two leaders received 14 years and 13 years 6 months for fraud; the scheme was «严丝合缝», «多名被害人一再受骗» (tight design; many victims paid repeatedly). Jiangsu Public Security Bureau in 2025 described a typical «无货源» cross-border fraud in four steps: social posts «零基础», «无需囤货»; «运营指导» adds you and pushes a custom app; first order appears, you prepay «成本价» while «货款冻结»; fake volume, then «账户异常» or «系统维护» and more unlock fees. One victim paid over 900,000 yuan; the site closed in three days. Separate case: agency ran fake group buys for ranking—100,000 yuan admin fine, 50,000 yuan platform deposit forfeited; Supreme People’s Court 2026 typical case: (虚假交易、刷单炒信本为法律明令禁止的行为). SAMR 2025: (一键搬家」「一键代发) software copying other platforms’ listings—530,000 yuan fine; warning against «搬家盗店» and «无货源经营». Civil Code: contract signed under fraud may be revoked within one year of discovery; after revocation, money taken must be returned (nationwide).

- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:广东省高级人民法院 (2023). 广东法院打击治理电信网络诈骗犯罪典型案例（案例三：蒋某、徐某等 55 人犯罪集团诈骗案）. <https://www.gdcourts.gov.cn/gsxx/quanweifabu/anlihuicui/content/post_1388464.html>；江苏省公安厅 (2025). 防范提示：无货源跨境电商，当心有诈！. <https://gat.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2025/2/13/art_89956_11491354.html>；最高人民法院 (2026). 最高人民法院发布依法规范平台经营、保护消费者合法权益典型案例（案例三：某代运营商诉某平台服务合同纠纷案）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/507691.html>；市场监管总局 (2025). 市场监管总局公布五起网络不正当竞争典型案例（案例一）. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/xw/zj/art/2025/art_0d043840ec9445a689496bcf30c94820.html>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一百四十八、一百五十二、一百五十七条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>

- Notes: Pause on «零基础», «无需囤货», (低投入，高回报), «保证盈利», or prepaying goods into their platform. Before paying, check license and penalties on the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System. Contract must state service scope and refund if targets miss. Keep chat, contract, and transfer records. If you paid, call 110 or 96110 for stop payment—Section 8, item 2 (stop payment after fraud). If they did some work but poorly, likely a contract dispute—negotiate, complain, or sue. Revocation needs litigation; ordinary first instance from six months, lawyer fees on you, defendant may be gone. Franchise-style pitches: Section 12, item 5 (check MOFCOM franchise filing first). Primary beneficiary: yourself.

### 25. Companies and sole proprietors must file the prior year’s annual report online 1 January–30 June each year—even with no business; filing is free
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->

- Cost: No cost. Log into the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System once a year; sole proprietors may file on paper. Reports go to market regulation; they are separate from monthly or quarterly tax filing. Paying tax does not substitute for the annual report. Easy to forget because it is once a year.

- In plain terms: Each year in the first half you file last year’s report online. Missing it lands you on the abnormal operation list; after catching up you can apply to leave. Filing is free. Calls claiming you missed the report and must pay via a mini-program or official account are scams—courts have sentenced over ten years in some cases. Check the publicity system yourself first.

- Benefit: Companies must (每年1月1日至6月30日，通过国家企业信用信息公示系统向市场监督管理部门报送上一年度年度报告，并向社会公示). Late filing → abnormal operation list and administrative penalties. The list is public—procurement and bidding often restrict listed firms. Sole proprietors file the same window, online or on paper; opened this year → report from next year. Sole proprietors may choose whether report contents are public, but failure still lists abnormality. Listed for missing reports: after filing missing years, apply to the registration authority to delist. Company: two years no report, still abnormal and unreachable at registered address → license revoked. Company reports are public; sole proprietors who hide content still show «已经报送年度报告» on the system—status is visible online. SAMR warned of scams «冒充市场监管人员», claiming overdue listing, then third-party mini-programs charging «人工申报材料费». «年报不收费»; use official channels only. Courts have convicted fraud—e.g. Anhui Jingde: agency hid identity, 98–398 yuan fees via mini-program, 10,099 sole proprietors, 3.19 million yuan, main defendants 10 years 6 months to 10 years 8 months; Jiaxing Nanhu: fine print said filing free but callers hid free online option and invented large fines—9 defendants 8 months to 4 years (nationwide).

- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2024 修订). 企业信息公示暂行条例（国务院令第 777 号修订，第八、十八、十九条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8081819c46fcc5019cd18fc6e9557d>（国家法律法规数据库）；市场监管总局 (2025). 关于修改《个体工商户年度报告办法》等四部规章的决定（市场监管总局令第 108 号，附重新公布的个体工商户年度报告办法第三、五、八、十、十三、十六条，企业经营异常名录管理办法第六、十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12546/202602/content_7057451.html>；市场监管总局 (2023). @所有经营主体，这些都是假的（中国政府网部门动态转载，原发市场监管总局「市说新语」微信公众号）. <https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202311/content_6917465.htm>；市场监管总局 (2024). 市场监管总局发布提示：年报不收费，切勿受骗上当（滨州市市场监督管理局转载）. <http://amr.binzhou.gov.cn/art/2024/12/16/art_164869_10306521.html>；安徽省旌德县人民法院 (2025). 冒充市场监管人员以年审申报收费诈骗个体工商户案（无锡市市场监督管理局转载）. <https://scjgj.wuxi.gov.cn/doc/2025/03/20/4524579.shtml>；嘉兴市南湖区人民法院 (2024). 企业年报要费用？莫入「年报诈骗」陷阱. <http://nhcourt.gov.cn/art/2024/7/23/art_1217290_58718476.html>

- Notes: On a «you missed the annual report» call, hang up, log into the publicity system, or call your registration authority. Do not follow SMS links or pay on stranger mini-programs or official accounts. Same playbook as fake tax bureau calls—Section 12, item 14. If you transferred money, 110 or 96110 immediately. Individual business and enterprise annual report rules were amended twice in 2025; current version effective 25 December 2025. Annual report and tax filing are separate; zero tax filing: Section 12, item 12. After revocation, responsible legal representative barred three years—Section 12, item 23. If you are done operating, deregister under item 23; do not use non-filing as a stall. Primary beneficiary: yourself.
