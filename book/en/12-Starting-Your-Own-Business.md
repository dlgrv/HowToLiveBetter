# 12. Starting a business: don't bet the family savings

This section counts only money and legal liability—not life expectancy. When you open a shop or form a company, which steps can wipe out your own money, family money, and your spouse's money together? What does the law require, and can you wall them off in advance? Items written from experience only, with no literature behind them, are grade C.

Items in this section are grouped by theme below; numbers in parentheses are item numbers.

**Before opening, don't bet the nest egg**: Only put in money you can afford to lose (item 1); don't sign personal guarantees on company loans (item 2); pick the right entity before you open (item 3); don't be a nominal shareholder (item 4); check franchise filing before joining (item 5); ask the right questions before paying someone to run your online store (item 24); when raising money for a business, only approach relatives, friends, and employees (item 26); put a partnership shop in a written agreement (item 28).

**Registration and licenses**: Fix name, premises, business scope, and registered capital before registration (item 6); don't open until licenses are in hand (item 7); before leasing a shopfront, check whether that unit can host your trade (item 27).

**Food**: See which regulatory tier you fall into (item 8); prepackaged food labels (item 9); ordinary food must not claim to treat disease (item 10); criminal lines for food (item 11).

**Tax, annual reports, and invoices**: File on time even with zero income (item 12); issue invoices only for real transactions (item 13); anyone posing as the tax bureau demanding payment is a scam (item 14); file last year's annual report online in the first half of each year (item 25).

**Contracts and hiring**: When taking money, write deposit (定金), not booking-fee wording (订金) (item 15); sign employment contracts within the first month (item 16); pay wages per contract and law (item 17); follow procedures on the job (item 22).

**Products and sourcing**: Sell first, then make (item 18); run samples through a mass-production checklist (item 19); keep purchase receipts and upstream supplier details (item 20); use your own designs or buy a license (item 21).

**Exit**: If you lose money, exit by the book (item 23).
### 1. Only put in money you can afford to lose; don't touch family savings or borrow to open
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Before you start, set a cap: you could lose every yuan of it and still pay the mortgage, support the kids, and see a doctor. Don't invest above that cap. Family money and borrowed money don't count toward the cap. The hard part is when the business looks like it's picking up and you want to pour in more.
- In plain terms: Debts of a sole proprietorship come out of assets in your name. If it's unclear whether the whole family ran the business, creditors can reach family property. A company doesn't fully shield you either: when the company can't pay, what you promised as subscribed capital but haven't paid yet can be called in early. Size your stake by «I could lose it all and still pay the mortgage and medical bills».
- Benefit: Losing money isn't the only risk. For a sole proprietorship, if you ran the business alone, debts are paid from property in your name (personal property). If the family ran it together or who ran it is unclear, debts are paid from family property. Debts both spouses signed count as joint marital debt; later assent by one spouse counts too. A loan in one spouse's name still counts as joint debt if the creditor shows it went into the couple's shared business. For companies there is another rule: subscribed capital is what a shareholder promised at registration. When the company can't pay a due debt, creditors may demand early payment of the portion not yet due (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第五十六、一千零六十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四十七、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Notes: «Affordable to lose» means losing it all wouldn't force you to sell the home or borrow from relatives to plug the hole. This round found no verifiable official statistics on how many firms survive or how long they last on the National Bureau of Statistics or State Administration for Market Regulation sites, so the text gives legal consequences only, not headcount figures—grade B.

### 2. Don't sign personal guarantees on company loans; spouses should stay off the signature line
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. When a bank, supplier, or landlord wants a «shareholder personal joint-and-several guarantee» or a spouse's signature, pause. That page is you giving up «company debt stays with the company» (limited liability). Don't sign if you can avoid it. If you must, choose «general guarantee» only and write a clear cap on how much you guarantee. The hard part is «no signature, no loan»—you have to hold the line.
- In plain terms: «Joint-and-several guarantee» means if the company doesn't pay, the creditor can skip the company and come straight to you. «General guarantee» means sue or arbitrate against the company first, sell company assets, and only then collect any shortfall from you. If your spouse signs too, both households' assets are in play and the company's «limited» liability stops protecting you personally.
- Benefit: Guarantee wording means «if the debtor doesn't pay, you pay». With «joint-and-several guarantee», creditors need not pursue the company first—they can demand payment from you directly. With «general guarantee», they must sue or arbitrate against the company and have the court enforce on its assets first; only the remainder can be collected from you. Debts both spouses signed count as joint marital debt; later assent counts too. A spouse's signature puts both sides' family assets on the hook (nationwide, from 2021).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第六百八十一、六百八十七、六百八十八、一千零六十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: The author's experience is that banks lending to small and micro enterprises often require shareholder personal joint-and-several guarantees; this book found no official statistics. Loans are still available; know what that page costs before you sign, and keep guaranteed amounts within the «affordable to lose» cap in item 1. General rules for IOUs and guarantees: section 8, item 18.

### 3. Pick the right entity before opening: sole props and general partners pay to the end; only LLCs are «limited»
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Register on the local government services portal—a few hours once. After you form a limited liability company, monthly bookkeeping and tax filing are ongoing: your time if you do it yourself, or paid bookkeeping, with prices varying by region.
- In plain terms: Sole proprietors and general partners owe without limit—pay what you owe until it's cleared. LLC shareholders owe only up to what they subscribed at registration. But whatever you subscribe must be paid in within 5 years: write 1000000, you may owe up to 1000000. Mix company books with household books or use the company to dodge debt, and «limited» can fail. With only one shareholder, you must prove the books are not mixed.
- Benefit: Sole proprietorship debts are paid from personal property or from family property. General partners in an ordinary partnership bear unlimited joint and several liability—pay in full until cleared, and a creditor may demand the entire debt from one partner alone. Limited partnerships are different: limited partners are liable only up to subscribed capital. LLC shareholders are also liable only up to subscribed capital. «Limited» fails in three situations. First, subscribed capital must be fully paid within 5 years of formation, and when the company can't pay due debts, creditors may demand early payment. Second, capital withdrawn after contribution must be returned. Third, using the company as a shell to dodge debt and seriously harming creditors makes you personally jointly liable for company debts. One-shareholder companies are stricter: that shareholder must prove company funds and personal funds are separate; if not, personal liability follows. Courts mainly look at whether money and books are separate. Shareholders casually spending company money without books, or mixing company and personal accounts, count as mixing (nationwide, from July 2024).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第五十六条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2006). 合伙企业法（2006 年修订，第二条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2006/content_413955.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四、二十三、四十七、五十三、五十四条；第二十三条第三款：「只有一个股东的公司，股东不能证明公司财产独立于股东自己的财产的，应当对公司债务承担连带责任。」）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>；最高人民法院 (2019). 全国法院民商事审判工作会议纪要（第 10 点「人格混同」）：「最主要的表现是公司的财产与股东的财产是否混同且无法区分」「公司账簿与股东账簿不分，致使公司财产与股东财产无法区分的」. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/199691.html>
- Notes: Keep company and personal accounts separate; don't take customer payments on a personal card; don't pay household bills from the company; keep complete books. Don't inflate registered capital for show.

### 4. Don't be a nominal shareholder or hold shares for someone else
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. When someone asks to «borrow your ID for a shareholder slot» or «hold shares in your name for now», refuse.
- In plain terms: If your name is on the register, you're a shareholder in law. Pay subscribed capital on time; miss the deadline and you owe make-up plus company losses. If another founder didn't really pay in at formation, you share liability for the gap. Someone else runs the business; the debt sits on your name.
- Benefit: A registered shareholder is a legal shareholder. Pay subscribed capital in full by the deadline in the articles; otherwise pay the shortfall and compensate company losses. If a founder didn't contribute at formation, other founders at formation share joint liability for that gap. When the company can't pay due debts, creditors may demand early payment of capital not yet due. The operator isn't on the register; the obligation is still yours (nationwide, from July 2024).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四十七、四十九、五十、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>；最高人民法院 (2020 修正). 关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》若干问题的规定（三）（法释〔2011〕3 号，2014 年法释〔2014〕2 号、2020 年法释〔2020〕18 号两次修正，第二十四、二十五条）：「如无法律规定的无效情形，人民法院应当认定该合同有效」「实际出资人未经公司其他股东半数以上同意，请求公司变更股东……人民法院不予支持」「名义股东处分股权造成实际出资人损失，实际出资人请求名义股东承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持」. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808181799def980179ac07a9ca117c>（国家法律法规数据库）；最高人民法院 (2025). 关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》若干问题的解释（征求意见稿）第九十条. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/477881.html>
- Notes: A nominee agreement between the nominal holder and the actual investor is valid unless a statutory invalidity applies. The actual investor can't get their name on the register without consent from over half of the other shareholders. If the nominal holder sells or pledges shares, courts apply Civil Code good-faith acquisition rules: a buyer who didn't know, paid a fair price, and registered may keep the shares; the investor can only sue the nominal holder. A draft new interpretation was released in September 2025; when finalized and in force it replaces the current text. As of September 2026, the 2020 amended version remains in force—this item follows it. Risks of being a nominal legal representative: section 8, item 28.

### 5. Before franchising, check MOFCOM filing, get written disclosure, and put a cooling-off period in the contract
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. On the Ministry of Commerce «Commercial Franchise Information Management System» site, check whether the brand is filed. Before signing, require written disclosure of everything they must tell you, together with the contract text. A few hours.
- In plain terms: By rule the franchisor needs at least 2 company-owned stores open 1 year, and must file with commerce authorities within 15 days after signing. Before signing it must give you 12 topics in writing—fees, when deposits return, how existing stores perform, and more. No filing, no disclosure, or pay-before-you-see-contract: walk away.
- Benefit: Regulations require the franchisor to have at least 2 directly operated stores running 1 year or more. Within 15 days of the first franchise contract it must file with the commerce department; no filing: fine 10000–50000 yuan; still unfiled after the deadline: 50000–100000 yuan plus public notice. Before signing it must deliver written disclosure on 12 items—including fee types and amounts, deposit refund conditions, franchisee count and how stores are doing, audited report summaries for the last 2 years, and litigation and arbitration in the last 5 years. The contract must also state that the franchisee may unilaterally terminate within a period after signing. Concealment or false disclosure lets the franchisee cancel the contract (nationwide, from May 2007).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2007). 商业特许经营管理条例（国务院令第 485 号，第七、八、十二、二十二、二十三、二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_4179.htm>；商务部 (2012). 商业特许经营信息披露管理办法（商务部令 2012 年第 2 号，第五、九条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2012/content_2177025.htm>；商务部. 商业特许经营信息管理系统. <https://txjy.syggs.mofcom.gov.cn/>
- Notes: No filing, no disclosure file, or pay-before-reading contract: leave. «Cooling-off» is the post-signing window for unilateral exit; the regulation only says «a certain period», so get the day count written in. On disclosed average sales, gross, or net profit for existing franchisees, ask how the brand sourced the numbers—the rules require disclosure of sources. Schemes that skip «franchise» and only charge «store operations» or «opening training» fees: item 24.

### 6. Before registration, fix name, premises, scope, and registered capital; with complete papers you can get the license on the spot
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Registration itself costs nothing. Enterprise and sole-proprietorship registration fees have not been collected since 1 January 2015. Do it yourself on the local government services site or at the service hall—a few hours once. Seal carving, paid bookkeeping, and a registered address (address hosting) are market services, not government fees.
- In plain terms: Registration is free; the registration fee has not been charged since 2015—if someone charges you, it is not the registration fee. Only five material types are required: application, identity or eligibility proof, premises documents, articles or partnership agreement, plus anything else the rules name. Complete papers: license on the spot; if not on the spot, within 3 working days; complex cases get 3 more working days.
- Benefit: The regulation fixes the materials as five items. «(1) Application form». «(2) Applicant qualification documents and natural-person identity proof». «(3) Domicile or main business premises documents». «(4) Articles for companies, non-company enterprise legal persons, farmer specialized cooperatives (unions), or partnership agreement». «(5) Other materials required by laws, administrative regulations, and the State Council market supervision department». In plain language: one application; proof of who you are or that you're eligible; proof you may use the shop or office; articles or partnership agreement; plus anything else named. Companies also submit appointment documents and ID for the legal representative, directors, supervisors, and senior managers. Sole props don't file articles. The name is «declared by the applicant according to law»—you choose and report it. Registered capital uses «subscribed registration»—state how much you promise; you need not pay at the counter. Business scope is chosen from the authority's published categories. Complete and correctly formatted materials are «confirmed and registered on the spot». If not finished on the spot, «within 3 working days», with up to 3 more for complex cases. «The business license issue date is the market entity's establishment date»—the printed date is when the company or shop is born (nationwide, from March 2022).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2021). 市场主体登记管理条例（国务院令第 746 号，第十、十三、十四、十六、十九、二十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5636139.htm> ; 市场监管总局 (2022). 市场主体登记管理条例实施细则（市场监管总局令第 52 号，第十二、二十五、二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-03/02/content_5676403.htm> ; 财政部、国家发展改革委 (2014). 关于取消、停征和免征一批行政事业性收费的通知（财税〔2014〕101 号，附件 1）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2014-12/23/content_5023513.htm> ; 国务院办公厅 (2018). 关于进一步压缩企业开办时间的意见（国办发〔2018〕32 号，第二部分第三项）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-05/17/content_5291643.htm>
- Notes: Registration itself is free. Charges are for agency help, seal carving, and similar market services. Seal prices are market-set. A 2018 General Office of the State Council document says applicants choose the seal maker themselves and forbids designating one—so you need not accept «must use the designated shop». How much registered capital you write does not dress up the storefront; it only caps your liability, and it must be paid within 5 years—see item 3. If scope includes licensed activities, don't open until licensed—see item 7.

### 7. Licensed trades: don't open until the license is issued
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Before opening, check whether your line needs a license. Catering and selling food need a food business license. Selling only others' prepackaged food needs no license—file for record instead. Open only after the license or filing is done.
- In plain terms: Opening without a license is handled by the department that governs that license under its rules. With no business license and no specific penalty rule: ordered to stop, illegal gains confiscated, plus a fine of up to 10000 yuan. Selling monopoly or restricted goods, or doing securities, futures, insurance, or payment settlement—serious cases are illegal business operation; the lowest tier is up to 5 years.
- Benefit: Operating without authorization is handled by the department the law designates. No license where the law doesn't spell out a penalty: stop, confiscate gains, fine up to 10,000 yuan. Heavier track: monopoly or restricted goods without permission, or securities, futures, insurance, or payment settlement without approval—serious cases are illegal business operation, up to 5 years' imprisonment or short-term detention plus a fine of 1–5 times illegal gains; especially serious, above 5 years (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2017). 无证无照经营查处办法（国务院令第 684 号，第五、六、十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-08/23/content_5219861.htm>；市场监管总局 (2023). 食品经营许可和备案管理办法（市场监管总局令第 78 号，第四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10606/202307/content_6894763.html>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>
- Notes: Finance, medical, and training each have their own licensing laws; this item only checked generic enforcement and criminal articles. Milk tea and snack shops are catering—food business license required. Renovate-first, trial-before-license is a common trap. Food tiers and whether fresh meat and vegetables need a license: item 8.

### 8. For food, know your tier: production and catering need a license; prepackaged-only sales need filing; fresh meat and vegetables need no license
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: The license itself is free. Premises and equipment must meet standards; site inspection plus processing take weeks. Meat sellers must also keep quarantine certificates and meat quality inspection certificates for each batch.
- In plain terms: Make and sell yourself—snack shop, braised goods, baking, homemade pickles—all need a license; small workshops and street vendors follow provincial rules. Sell only others' fully packaged food: no license; file with the county-level or higher market supervision department. Fresh meat, vegetables, and the like need no license. Operating without a license: even if goods value is under 10000, fine 50000–100000 yuan.
- Benefit: Food Safety Law: food businesses need a license first—produce food, sell food, or do catering, all need a license by law. «However, selling edible agricultural products and selling prepackaged food only do not require a license. Selling prepackaged food only shall be filed with the local county-level or above people's government food safety supervision department.» Edible agricultural products are unprocessed fresh meat, vegetables, and the like. Prepackaged food is made by others and sold in packaging. «Specific management rules for small food production workshops and food vendors are set by provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities»—those two tiers follow provincial rules. Operating food without a license: confiscate illegal gains, food, tools, equipment, and raw materials. If goods value (sale-price total for the batch) is under 10000 yuan, additionally fine 50000–100000 yuan; if 10000 yuan or more, fine 10–20 times goods value. Pig Slaughter Management Regulations: pigs only at state-designated slaughter plants with official centralized quarantine. «Except personal self-slaughter for own consumption in rural areas, no unit or individual may engage in pig slaughter without designation.» «Pig products sold or used by sellers, meat processors, catering operators, and centralized meal units must come from designated slaughter plants with quarantine and meat quality inspection passed.» So pork you sell or use must come from designated plants, with both quarantine and quality inspection passed. Private slaughter outside designated plants: ordered shut down; pigs, slaughter tools and equipment, and illegal gains confiscated; fines match the unlicensed-food tier above. Animal Epidemic Prevention Law: sold or shipped animal products must carry quarantine certificates and marks with the goods; missing them: order correction, then fine up to one times the value of comparable compliant goods. Judicial interpretation covers punitive damages—extra pay beyond a refund. Food made or sold by small workshops or vendors that fails food safety standards: buyers claiming tenfold damages should be upheld by courts (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2025 年第三次修正). 食品安全法（第三十五、三十六、一百二十二条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=7b5a76d0461745a08d3f964916b87ef3>；国务院 (2021). 生猪屠宰管理条例（国务院令第 742 号，第二、二十三、三十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-07/22/content_5626534.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2021 修订). 动物防疫法（第五十一、一百条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8080817703add2017737395a973e31>；最高人民法院 (2024). 关于审理食品药品惩罚性赔偿纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2024〕9 号，第四条）. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/bf531a70471f10d377788f28d3d8f8.html>
- Notes: Thresholds for small workshops, small catering, and food vendors are set by provinces—no specific numbers here. On your provincial market supervision site search «食品小作坊小餐饮小摊贩管理条例» for the local version. Selling homemade food online does not skip a license where one is required. Entity and license setup: item 6. Whether other trades need a license: item 7. How much buyers can claim: section 5, item 31 (tenfold food damages).

### 9. Bagged for sale is prepackaged food: production date, shelf life, and ingredient list on the label—none may be missing
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Printing labels costs a few fen to a few jiao each. Filling dates and ingredients correctly for every batch takes some time.
- In plain terms: Food put in packaging and sold is prepackaged food in law; the package must have a label, and nine items are mandatory—including production date and shelf life. Bulk food must still show name, dates, and the seller's name and address. Missing required marks: goods value under 10000 → fine 5000–50000; at or above 10000 → fine 5–10 times goods value.
- Benefit: Food Safety Law: prepackaged food (food sold in packaging) must have a label. The label must show name, specification, net content, production date, composition or ingredient list, producer's name, address, and contact, and shelf life. Also product standard code, storage conditions, the national-standard generic names of food additives used, and production license number. Anything else required by laws, regulations, or food safety standards must also appear. Bulk food is unpackaged and weighed to order. Selling bulk food: containers and outer packaging must show name, production date or batch number, and shelf life, plus the operator's name, address, and contact. Producing or selling prepackaged food without a label, or with a label or instructions that don't meet this law: confiscate illegal gains and the food. If goods value is under 10000 yuan, additionally fine 5000–50000 yuan; if 10000 yuan or more, fine 5–10 times goods value. Serious cases: ordered to stop production or business, up to license revocation. Labels or instructions with only minor defects that don't affect food safety or mislead consumers: ordered to correct first; fine up to 2000 yuan only if they refuse. The new Prepackaged Food Labeling General Standard is numbered GB 7718-2025, issued 16 March 2025. It takes effect 16 March 2027, with a two-year transition. It requires an independent area on the package showing both production date and shelf-life end date together. Dates must use high-contrast, easy-to-read type and color, in year-month-day order (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A

### 15. When you take money, write "earnest deposit", not "booking payment". Write the penalty as a number, and review a payment term as if you were lending

### 18. Sell first, then produce: use pre-sales and small orders to prove demand before you invest in production
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Tens to hundreds of yuan for a sample or a landing page, then reach dozens of real customers for prepayment or small orders. That is slower by weeks than making stock first. The hard part is not producing before anyone pays.
- In plain terms: An Italian study found that founders who set a hypothesis and tested it against market feedback ran better businesses. They were also more likely to pivot in time, and did not quit earlier. So if nobody prepays or places a small order, don't trust «make it and buyers will appear».
- Benefit: A randomized controlled trial tracked 116 Italian startups for about one year. A randomized controlled trial randomly splits people into two groups, trains only one, then compares outcomes. Founders trained to decide by «hypothesis first, then strict market-feedback tests» performed better. They were also more likely to pivot to other plans in time, and early dropout was not higher. Projects that look viable but can't work—the authors call them false-positive projects. The authors' explanation is that this approach cuts the chance of pouring more money into such projects (Italy, published 2020).

### 20. Keep invoices and supplier records for every purchase batch; skip goods priced far below market—if staff buy counterfeits for the company, the owner can still be the one convicted
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. For each batch keep purchase contract, invoice, payment record, and the upstream party's business license and authorization. Set an internal rule: whoever buys signs; never accept prices clearly below market. The hard part is passing up cheap stock that looks profitable.
- In plain terms: Selling counterfeits: illegal gains of 30000 yuan, or sales of 50000 yuan, meets the criminal threshold—up to 3 years. Unsold warehouse stock counts if goods value reaches three times the sales threshold. «I didn't know it was fake» is not yours alone to decide: buying far below market without a good explanation supports knowledge. Keeping purchase contract, invoice, payment trail, and upstream identity is the path to the no-damages defense.
- Benefit: Knowingly selling goods bearing counterfeit registered trademarks: illegal gains of 30000 yuan meets the criminal threshold; sales of 50000 yuan likewise. Up to 3 years plus a fine, or a fine alone. At ten times those levels: 3–10 years plus fine. Unsold stock still counts: goods value at three times the sales threshold or more is also a crime. «Knowing» need not be admitted. A 2025 SPC and SPP interpretation lists five situations where knowledge is presumed unless you prove otherwise: (1) knowing the registered trademark on the goods was altered, swapped, or covered; (2) forging or altering the trademark owner's authorization, or knowing it was forged or altered; (3) prior criminal or administrative penalty for selling counterfeits, then selling the same kind again; (4) no reasonable grounds, buying or selling far below market; (5) after discovery by administrative or judicial authorities, moving or destroying infringing goods and accounting records, or providing false proof. When a company commits this offense, «the unit is fined, and the directly responsible managers and other directly responsible persons» are punished under the same article—so even if staff sourced the goods, the person who approved still faces judgment. Trademark law leaves an out: selling without knowing the goods infringe another's registered trademark, «if you can prove lawful acquisition and identify the provider, you bear no compensation liability». Whether that defense works depends entirely on invoices and upstream identity; without them you can't use it (nationwide; interpretation effective 26 April 2025).

### 21. Art on products, packaging, tags, and ads must be yours or licensed—recoloring or adding icons is not «changed enough»
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Licensed stock art from tens of yuan per image; original design, hundreds to thousands of yuan. Keep license proof and purchase record for every image used on goods. The hard part is not grabbing random web images.
- In plain terms: Changing colors, swapping the background, or slapping on your own icon on someone else's work is still unlicensed use. Reproducing others' fine-art works for profit—illegal gains of 30000, turnover of 50000, or 500 copies—can be copyright infringement, up to 3 years. On trademarks, «likely to cause confusion» is infringement by itself.
- Benefit: Copyright law lists «using a work by adaptation, translation, annotation, or similar means without the copyright owner's permission» alongside direct copying as infringement. So recoloring, changing the background, or adding your own icon is still unlicensed use of someone else's work. The criminal threshold: for profit, without the copyright owner's permission, you reproduce and distribute fine-art works. Three numbers trip the offense: illegal gains of 30000 yuan; illegal business amount of 50000 yuan; or 500 copies (sheets) or more. Hit any one and it is copyright infringement—up to 3 years plus fine, or fine alone. At ten times those levels: 3–10 years. Only selling others' infringing copies: illegal gains of 50000 yuan or more, or sales of 100000 yuan or more—crime of selling infringing copies, up to 5 years. Trademarks likewise: two «likely to cause confusion» situations are infringement by themselves—using a similar mark on the same goods, or the same or a similar mark on similar goods. «Similar» is an express statutory infringement form. Editing someone else's trademark and reusing it still infringes if confusion is likely (nationwide; interpretation effective 26 April 2025).

If you don't plan to continue, deregister under that item; don't stall by skipping the annual report. Primary beneficiary: yourself.

### 26. When raising money for a business, only talk face to face with relatives, friends, and employees—don't solicit publicly on Moments or in groups, and don't let relatives recruit further for you
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Borrowing or bringing in shareholders: only approach relatives, friends, and employees you know, one by one, face to face. No Moments posts, no group posts, no flyers, no pitch meetings. If a relative says «I'll ask others for you», stop that on the spot. The hard part is when cash is short and «pull in a few more people» looks like the fastest money.
- In plain terms: Borrowing from relatives, friends, and employees, or taking them as co-investors, is not itself a crime. The crime is public promotion, taking money from strangers, and promising principal-and-interest repayment or returns. Taking in 1000000 yuan, or money from 150 people, meets the criminal threshold—starts at up to 3 years. Money used mainly in normal production, and fully repaid before indictment, may avoid criminal punishment.
- Benefit: Criminal Law Article 176 covers «illegally absorbing public deposits or absorbing them in a disguised form, disrupting financial order». Up to 3 years' imprisonment or short-term detention plus a fine, or a fine alone. Huge amounts or other serious circumstances: 3–10 years. Especially huge amounts or other especially serious circumstances: 10 years or more. An SPC judicial interpretation says all four conditions must be met: (1) no lawful permit from the relevant department, or taking money under a shell of lawful business; (2) public promotion through the internet, media, pitch meetings, flyers, phone messages, or similar channels; (3) promising to repay principal and interest or give returns within a period in money, goods, equity, or similar form; (4) taking money from unspecified members of the public. The same article continues: «Absorbing funds from specific persons among relatives, friends, or inside a unit without public promotion is not illegal absorption or disguised absorption of public deposits.» But a joint SPC–SPP–MPS opinion carves out two exceptions that still count as taking from the public: (1) when taking from relatives, friends, or employees, you know they are collecting from strangers for you and let it happen; (2) you first hire outsiders as employees in order to take money from them. Criminal lines trip on any one of three: 1000000 yuan or more absorbed; 150 people or more; or direct losses of 500000 yuan or more. 5000000 yuan or more, 500 people or more, or losses of 2500000 yuan or more move into the 3–10 year tier. Amounts are counted on the full sum taken in. If the money was used mainly in normal production and operations, and the full amount taken is repaid before indictment, criminal punishment may be waived. Significantly slight harm may not be treated as a crime. Below the criminal line there is still administrative penalty: the local lead department for illegal fundraising fines 20% to 1 times the amount raised. For unit fundraising, the legal representative and directly responsible persons «are given a warning and fined 500000 yuan or more and up to 5000000 yuan» (nationwide; interpretation effective 1 March 2022).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2020). 刑法修正案（十一）（第十二项，即刑法第一百七十六条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/202012/t20201227_503682.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2022 修正). 关于审理非法集资刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2022〕5 号修正，第一、三、四、六条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/346901.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部 (2014). 关于办理非法集资刑事案件适用法律若干问题的意见（第二、三点）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201404/t20140401_70180.shtml>；国务院 (2021). 防范和处置非法集资条例（国务院令第 737 号，第二、十九、二十五、三十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-02/10/content_5586632.htm>
- Notes: Once labeled illegal fundraising, relatives who lent you money also lose. Article 25 of the regulation says losses from participating in illegal fundraising are borne by the people who put the money in. Article 19 also names a common business form: taking money while selling goods, providing services, or investing in projects by promising money, equity, or goods returns. Store prepaid cards or membership cards that also promise rebates or dividends, sold to strangers, fall under that article. When cash is short, safer paths are bank loans, or converting money to equity written into a partnership agreement—see item 28. How lenders protect themselves: section 5, item 6 («high return», «principal guaranteed», «sure win»). How older people protect themselves: section 17, item 5 (don't touch «invest for retirement»). Primary beneficiary: yourself; next, relatives who lent to you.

### 27. Before leasing a shopfront, check whether that unit can host your trade: converting a residence to a shop needs unanimous consent from owners in the same building; new oil-smoke restaurants are barred in residential buildings
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Before signing a lease, paying deposit, or renovating, spend a few hours on three checks. First, the use on the property certificate—residential or commercial. Second, whether the building is pure residential, mixed-use, or a standalone shop unit, and whether people live upstairs. Third, for catering: whether there is a dedicated smoke flue, then ask local market supervision whether a food business license can be issued. The hard part is verifying yourself when the landlord or agent says «no problem—the last tenant ran one».
- In plain terms: Converting a residence into a shop needs unanimous consent from the other owners in the same building—one household against is enough to block it. Neighbors asking for restoration and damages will be upheld. Residential buildings and mixed-use buildings without a dedicated smoke flue may not open new oil-smoke restaurants; if found, you are ordered to correct first. Refuse and the shop is closed, with a fine of 10000–100000 yuan—renovation money and deposit may be lost with it.
- Benefit: Civil Code Article 279: «An owner who changes a residence into business premises shall, besides complying with laws, regulations, and the management covenant, obtain unanimous consent from interested owners.» An SPC judicial interpretation is more specific. Other owners in the same building all count as «interested owners». Owners in other buildings in the same estate count only if they prove impact on their property value or living quality. Changing use without their unanimous consent: requests to stop, restore, or compensate for losses shall be upheld. Arguing «a majority of owners agreed» is not upheld. The Ecological Environment Code took effect 15 August 2026; the Air Pollution Prevention and Control Law was repealed at the same time. Code Article 250 bans new construction, renovation, or expansion of catering projects that produce oil smoke, odors, or exhaust in three places: (1) residential buildings; (2) mixed-use buildings without a dedicated smoke flue; (3) commercial floors in mixed-use buildings adjacent to residential floors. Violations: ordered to correct first; refuse → ordered closed, plus a fine of 10000–100000 yuan. The code also requires local governments to flag catering siting bans at registration. Applying for a food business license: premises must match the food types and volumes handled, and keep required distance from toxic or hazardous sites and other pollution sources. Conversely, if the premises themselves fail mandatory use conditions in laws or administrative regulations, you may terminate the lease—provided the inability to use is not your fault (nationwide).

Ordinary first-instance procedure starts at 6 months; lawyer fees are on you—so writing «if a license can't be obtained or the shop is ordered closed, the lease ends and deposit is returned» into the lease is cheaper. Which licenses you need: items 7 and 8. Primary beneficiary: yourself.

### 28. When several people open a shop together, put capital, profit splits, who decides, who draws wages, and how to exit into a written agreement before opening
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Sit down once or twice with the partners, write about ten items clearly, and each sign. Partnership-agreement templates are online; fill the ten items listed in the Partnership Enterprise Law one by one. The hard part is acquaintances feeling that «talking money hurts the relationship» and not wanting to start.
- In plain terms: Without an agreement, the law sets default rules for you. Decisions need unanimous consent; profits follow each person's actual capital paid in; the person who watches the shop every day draws no wage by default. With no partnership term agreed, anyone may exit after reasonable advance notice to the others. Changing those defaults only works if written into the agreement in advance.
- Benefit: Partnership Enterprise Law Article 4: the partnership agreement is made in writing by unanimous negotiation of all partners. Article 18 lists ten items the agreement should cover—including capital contribution form, amount, and payment deadline; profit distribution and loss sharing; who executes partnership affairs; admission and withdrawal; dispute resolution; dissolution and liquidation; and breach liability. Partners who don't form a partnership enterprise and only work together under a Civil Code partnership contract follow that contract; where the contract is silent, Civil Code defaults apply. Partnership decisions, unless the contract says otherwise, need unanimous partner consent. A partner who executes partnership affairs may not claim pay for that alone, unless the contract says otherwise. Profits and losses follow the contract. If silent: negotiate first; if negotiation fails, by actual capital ratios; if ratios can't be figured, equal shares. Partners bear joint liability for partnership debts; anyone who pays more may seek contribution from the others. With no partnership term agreed, it is an open-ended partnership—a partner may terminate at any time with reasonable advance notice to the others. For a partnership enterprise with no term agreed, a partner may withdraw with 30 days' notice to the others. The agreement also may not assign all profits to only some partners, or dump all losses on only some (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2006). 合伙企业法（2006 年修订，第四、十八、三十三、四十六条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf63dce034d>（国家法律法规数据库）；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第九百六十七、九百七十、九百七十一、九百七十二、九百七十三、九百七十六条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: The most common fight is how capital and labor split. One puts in money and does nothing; one puts in no money and watches the shop every day—under defaults the shop-watcher gets a smaller share and no wage. So write the labor partner's wage or equity clearly. The agreement only governs partners among themselves. Outward debts: general partners still pay to the end—see item 3. Red lines for pulling in more investors: item 26. Statutory parts were verified at source; «a written agreement reduces fights» is experience without statistics, hence grade C. Beneficiaries: you and your partners.
