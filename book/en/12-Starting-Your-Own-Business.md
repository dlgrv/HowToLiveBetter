# 12. Starting a Business and Doing Deals: Don't Bet the Family Savings

This section covers money and legal liability: opening a shop or founding a company — at which step your personal property, family property, or your spouse's property gets dragged into liability, what the law says, and whether you can ring-fence yourself in advance. Life expectancy conversion is not used here. Items are ordered from high to low by "most likely to cost you everything, easiest to avoid": signature-type items come first, methods requiring active work come later, and experience-based items are graded C.
### 1. Start a business only with money you can afford to lose: don't touch the family savings, don't borrow to open
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Before you start, set yourself a number: even if this money is lost in full, it does not affect paying the mortgage, raising the children, or medical care. Do not invest beyond that number. Money from family members and borrowed money do not count toward that number. The hard part is holding back from putting in more once the business seems to be picking up.
- In plain terms: Money owed by a sole proprietor is repaid with the money and property in your own name. If it cannot be told whether the whole family runs the business, the family's property is used to repay it. Forming a company is not a shield either: when the company cannot repay its debts, the money you originally promised to contribute and that has not fallen due yet can be demanded from you early. So decide how much to put in by the rule: even if it is all lost, the mortgage and medical care are still covered.
- Benefit: Losing is not just the money gone. Debts of an individually-owned business: if the business is run by you alone, it is repaid with the money and property in your own name (borne with personal property). If the whole family runs it, or it cannot be told who runs it, it is repaid with the family's money and property (borne with family property). A debt signed by both spouses counts as a joint debt the two repay together. If one spouse agrees to it afterwards, that counts too. Money borrowed in one spouse's name: as long as the creditor can prove it was used in the business the couple runs together, the two still repay it together. On the company side there is also this: when the company cannot repay debts that have fallen due, the shareholder's subscribed capital contribution — the money promised in the registered capital whose payment deadline has not arrived — can be demanded early by the creditor (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第五十六、一千零六十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四十七、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Notes: Can afford to lose means: even if this money is lost in full, you do not have to sell your home and do not have to borrow from relatives to fill the hole. How many businesses survive and how many years they last on average — this kind of official statistic could not be found as a verifiable original on the websites of the National Bureau of Statistics and the State Administration for Market Regulation this time. So this entry writes no numbers, only the legal consequences, and is graded B.

### 2. Don't sign a personal guarantee on a company loan, and your spouse should never sign one either
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. When a bank, supplier, or landlord asks you to sign a shareholder personal joint-and-several guarantee or a spouse signature, stop first. Signing that page means you give up, with your own hand, the protection that a company's debts do not reach you personally (giving up limited liability). Do not sign if you can avoid it. If you really must sign, sign only a general guarantee, and write clearly the maximum amount you guarantee. The hard part is that the other side often says no signature, no loan; you have to hold your ground.
- In plain terms: If what you sign is a joint-and-several liability guarantee and the company cannot pay, the creditor can bypass the company and come straight for you. Only a general guarantee means the creditor sues the company first, and you are reached only for whatever is still short after the company's assets are sold. If your spouse signs too, the two of you sink the family property of both together, and the limited in a limited liability company stops counting for you personally.
- Benefit: The sentence on the guarantee means: when the debtor does not pay, you pay. If it is written as a joint-and-several liability guarantee, the creditor does not have to go after the company first and can come straight to your door for the money. If it is written as a general guarantee, the creditor must first sue the company or go to arbitration, then ask the court to force a sale of the company's assets against the debt. Only whatever is still short after the sale reaches you. A debt signed by both spouses counts as a joint debt the two repay together. If one spouse agrees to it afterwards, that counts too. Once the spouse has signed, the family property of both is sunk together (nationwide, since 2021).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第六百八十一、六百八十七、六百八十八、一千零六十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: Banks making loans to small and micro enterprises almost always require shareholders to sign a personal joint-and-several guarantee. So this entry is not telling you never to take a loan; it tells you to work out what that page signs away before you sign. Then press the guaranteed amount down inside the can-afford-to-lose number from item 1 (start a business only with money you can afford to lose). For the general rules on IOUs and guarantees, see section 8, item 18.

### 3. Pick the right legal form before opening: sole proprietorships and partners pay to the bitter end, only a limited company is limited
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Registration is done online on the local government services portal and takes a few hours at a time. After you open a limited company there is monthly bookkeeping and tax filing, a long-term expense: doing it yourself costs time, hiring a bookkeeping agency costs money, and prices differ by place.
- In plain terms: Sole proprietors and general partners pay to the bitter end: owe how much, repay how much. The shareholder of a limited company only loses up to the amount promised at registration. But whatever you promise, it must actually be paid in within 5 years: writing 1 million yuan means potentially owing 1 million yuan. And if the company account and the household account are used mixed together, the limited also stops working.
- Benefit: Debts of an individually-owned business are repaid with the person's own money and property, or with the family's money and property. Partners in a general partnership bear unlimited joint and several liability for the partnership's debts. That means owe how much, repay how much, and unpaid is not finished. The creditor can also seize on just one of the partners and demand the whole amount from them. A limited partnership is different: the limited partners in it only lose up to the money they promised to contribute (capped at subscribed capital contribution). Shareholders of a limited liability company also only lose up to the money they promised to contribute (capped at the subscribed capital contribution amount). But the limited fails in three situations. First, the promised capital must actually be paid in full within 5 years of the company's formation, and when the company cannot repay debts that have fallen due, the creditor can demand early payment. Second, money that was paid in and then pulled back out (withdrawn capital) must be returned. Third, using the company as a shell to hide from debts. A company with only one shareholder that cannot prove the company's money and its own money are kept apart also falls under this third case. Under these last two, you personally repay the company's debts together with it (bear joint and several liability) (nationwide, since July 2024).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第五十六条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2006). 合伙企业法（2006 年修订，第二条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2006/content_413955.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四、二十三、四十七、五十三、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Notes: The premise of limited is keeping company and private money apart: company account and personal account not used mixed together, household expenses not paid out of the company's money, complete books kept. Do not write a big number in the registered capital just to look good. Whatever you subscribe, that much must actually be paid in within 5 years. Writing 1 million yuan means a potential debt of 1 million yuan.

### 4. Don't be a shareholder in name only, don't hold shares on someone else's behalf
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. When someone comes with lend me your ID card to list you as a shareholder or leave the shares under your name for now, refuse across the board.
- In plain terms: Once your name is on the register, you are the shareholder in law. The money you promised to contribute at registration must be paid in full on schedule, and if you cannot pay it, besides making up the shortfall you also compensate the company for its losses. If at the company's founding the other shareholders never actually paid their money in, you repay the shortfall however large it is. Someone else runs the business; the debts are recorded under your name.
- Benefit: A shareholder on the register is a shareholder in law. Within the deadline set in the company's articles of association, you must pay in every last bit of the money you promised to contribute (pay subscribed capital in full). If it is not paid on schedule, besides making up the shortfall you also compensate the company for its losses. Where at the company's founding some shareholder did not actually pay money in, the other founding shareholders repay together inside that gap (bear joint and several liability). When the company cannot repay debts that have fallen due, the creditor can demand early payment of the money whose deadline has not arrived. The person actually doing the business is not on the register, but the debts are recorded under your name (nationwide, since July 2024).
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四十七、四十九、五十、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Notes: Between the shareholder who is listed in name for someone else and the person who actually puts up the money, whether the holding-on-behalf agreement counts and who bears which responsibility are set out in the Supreme People's Court provisions on several issues concerning the application of the Company Law (III). ### 5. Before joining a franchise, check MOFCOM's filing first, get the written disclosures, and write the cooling-off period into the contract
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Go to the Ministry of Commerce's commercial franchise operation information management system website and check whether the brand owner has filed. Then require them, before you sign, to give you the matters they must disclose as written materials, together with the contract text. A few hours is enough to do all this.
- In plain terms: By the rules, the brand owner must first have opened 2 directly-operated stores and run them for a full 1 year, and within 15 days after signing it must file with the commerce authorities. Before signing, it must give you in writing, as 12 items, the things it has to disclose: the kinds of franchise fees and how much each costs, when the deposit is refunded, whether the existing stores actually make money, and so on. If you cannot find a filing, they will not hand over the materials, or you must pay first before you can see the contract, walk away on the spot.
- Benefit: The regulation requires the brand owner (called the franchisor in the regulation) to have at least 2 directly-operated stores it opened and runs itself, and to have kept them open for more than 1 year. Counting from the day the first franchise contract is signed, it must file with the competent commerce department within 15 days. Not filing draws a fine of 10,000 to 50,000 yuan. Not filing even after the deadline draws a fine of 50,000 to 100,000 yuan and a public announcement. Before signing, it must give you the matters to disclose as written materials, 12 items in all. They include: which kinds of franchise fees are charged and how much each costs. Under what conditions the deposit is refunded. How many franchise stores there are now and how their business is doing. Summaries of audited reports for the last 2 years. Which lawsuits and arbitrations there have been over the past 5 years. The contract must also state that within a period of time after signing, the franchisee can terminate on its own side whenever it chooses (unilateral termination). If the brand owner conceals things or gives false information, the franchisee can terminate the contract (nationwide, since May 2007).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2007). 商业特许经营管理条例（国务院令第 485 号，第七、八、十二、二十二、二十三、二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_4179.htm>；商务部 (2012). 商业特许经营信息披露管理办法（商务部令 2012 年第 2 号，第五、九条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2012/content_2177025.htm>；商务部. 商业特许经营信息管理系统. <https://txjy.syggs.mofcom.gov.cn/>
- Notes: If you cannot find a filing, they refuse to give the disclosure documents, or you must pay first before seeing the contract, walk away. The cooling-off period is the days after signing when you can still terminate on your own side whenever you choose. The number of days is set by the contract; the regulation only says a certain period, so require the number of days to be written in when you sign. For the disclosed average sales, gross profit, and net profit of existing franchise stores, press for where the numbers come from; the implementing measures require the brand owner to state the source.

### 6. Settle the name, business premises, business scope, and registered capital before registering; with the documents complete you can get the license on the spot
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Registration itself costs nothing. The registration fee for enterprises and individually-owned businesses has not been collected since January 1, 2015. Do it yourself on the local government services portal, or go to the government service hall; it takes a few hours at a time. Engraving the company seal, hiring a bookkeeping agency, renting a registered address (address hosting) are services bought on the market, not fees collected by the government.
- In plain terms: Registration itself costs nothing; the registration fee stopped being collected in 2015. The documents to hand in are only five items: the application form, identity or qualification proof, premises documents, the articles of association or partnership agreement (an individually-owned business does not need to hand in one), plus whatever laws and regulations name separately. With the documents complete the license is issued on the spot, and if it cannot be issued on the spot it is done within 3 working days. The money you were charged is most likely an agent's errand fee.
- Benefit: The regulation fixes the documents to hand in as five items. (1) The application form. (2) The applicant's qualification documents and the natural person's identity proof. (3) Documents relating to the domicile or the main place of business. (4) The articles of association of a company, a non-company enterprise legal person, or a farmers' cooperative (union), or the partnership agreement of a partnership. (5) Other documents required by laws, administrative regulations, and the market regulation department of the State Council. In plain words: one application form. One document proving who you are, or that you are qualified to do this. One document proving the shop or office is yours to use. The company's articles of association or the partnership agreement. Plus whatever laws and regulations name separately. A company also hands in the appointment documents and identity proofs of the legal representative, directors, supervisors, and senior managers. An individually-owned business does not need to hand in articles of association. The name is declared independently by the applicant according to law, meaning you choose it yourself and file it yourself. The registered capital follows the subscription registration system, meaning you first write down how much you promise to contribute and do not have to pay it on the spot. The business scope is picked from the classification published by the registration authority. If the documents are complete and the format correct, it is confirmed and registered on the spot. What cannot be finished on the spot is done within 3 working days, with 3 more working days added for complicated cases. The issue date on the business license is the establishment date of the market entity, meaning the day printed on the license is the birthday of the company or the shop (nationwide, since March 2022).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2021). 市场主体登记管理条例（国务院令第 746 号，第十、十三、十四、十六、十九、二十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5636139.htm> ; 市场监管总局 (2022). 市场主体登记管理条例实施细则（市场监管总局令第 52 号，第十二、二十五、二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-03/02/content_5676403.htm> ; 财政部、国家发展改革委 (2014). 关于取消、停征和免征一批行政事业性收费的通知（财税〔2014〕101 号，附件 1）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2014-12/23/content_5023513.htm>
- Notes: Registration itself collects no fee; the money you are charged is most likely the agent's errand fee. The price of seal engraving is set by the market. The General Office of the State Council expressly forbids designating seal-making units to make official seals, so if someone says you must engrave the seal at a designated place, you do not have to accept it. How much you write in the registered capital does not affect your storefront; it only sets the ceiling of your liability, and it must be paid in full within 5 years; see item 3 of this section (pick the right legal form before opening). If the business scope includes licensed items, you cannot open until the permit is issued; see item 7 of this section (trades that need a license).

### 7. In trades that need a license, do not open until the license comes through
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Before opening, first find out whether the business you want to run needs a license. Running a restaurant or selling food requires a food operation license. Selling only prepackaged food made by others needs no license; it is changed to filing. Open for business only after the license is issued.
- In plain terms: Opening without the license brings, at the light end, an order to shut down, confiscation of the money earned, and a fine of up to 10,000 yuan. If what you sell is goods under exclusive state operation, or you run securities, futures, insurance, or funds payment and settlement, serious cases constitute the crime of illegal business operation, starting at a sentence of up to 5 years. Fitting out the shop before applying for the license, or holding a trial opening before the license comes through, is the most common step on the rake.
- Benefit: Those who open for business without obtaining the license are investigated and dealt with by the department designated in laws and regulations. Operating without a license, where the law does not say how to punish it, brings an order to stop, confiscation of the money earned (illegal gains), and a fine of up to 10,000 yuan. There is a heavier tier. Dealing without permission in goods that laws and regulations place under exclusive state operation, exclusive sale, or restricted trading. Running securities, futures, insurance, or funds payment and settlement without approval. In these two situations, serious cases constitute the crime of illegal business operation. The sentence is fixed-term imprisonment of up to 5 years or criminal detention (short-term custody), with a fine of 1 to 5 times the money earned. Especially serious cases are sentenced to more than 5 years (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2017). 无证无照经营查处办法（国务院令第 684 号，第五、六、十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-08/23/content_5219861.htm>；市场监管总局 (2023). 食品经营许可和备案管理办法（市场监管总局令第 78 号，第四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10606/202307/content_6894763.html>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>
- Notes: Licensing conditions for finance, medical care, and education-training each have their own dedicated laws; this entry verified only the general enforcement rules and the criminal law clauses. Bubble tea shops and snack bars count as food service and need a food operation license. Fitting out first and licensing later, or trial operation before the license comes through, is a common pitfall. For which tiers the food trade splits into and whether selling fresh meat and vegetables needs a license, see item 8 (check which tier of the food trade you fall into first).

### 8. Working with food, first see which tier you fall into: making it and catering need a license, selling only prepackaged food changes to filing, selling fresh meat and vegetables needs no license
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: The license itself costs nothing. But the premises and facilities must meet the standard, and the on-site inspection plus getting the license takes a few weeks. Those selling meat must also keep the quarantine certificate and the meat quality inspection certificate for every batch.
- In plain terms: Making things yourself to sell — snack shops, braised food, baking, homemade pickles — all requires a license. Selling only prepackaged food made by others needs no license; filing with the market regulation department at or above the county level is enough. Selling fresh meat, vegetables, and the like needs no license. Doing it without a license draws a fine of at least 50,000 to 100,000 yuan, more than the startup capital of most small businesses.
- Benefit: The Food Safety Law: to go into the food business you must first obtain a license. Producing food yourself, selling food, and running catering all require a license according to law. But those selling edible agricultural products and selling only prepackaged food need not obtain a license. Those selling only prepackaged food must file with the food safety supervision department of the local people's government at or above the county level. Edible agricultural products are unprocessed things like fresh meat and vegetables. Prepackaged food is food made by others and sold together with the packaging. The specific management measures for small food production and processing workshops and food vendors are formulated by provinces, autonomous regions, and directly-administered municipalities, meaning these two categories are governed by each province's own rules. Making a food business without a license brings confiscation of all the money earned, the food, and the tools, equipment, and raw materials. Where the total value of the goods at sale price (value of goods) is under 10,000 yuan, an additional fine of 50,000 to 100,000 yuan is imposed. Where the value of goods is 10,000 yuan or more, the fine is 10 to 20 times the value of the goods. The Pig Slaughter Regulation: pigs may only be slaughtered at designated slaughterhouses, with quarantine concentrated in official hands. Except for individuals in rural areas slaughtering for their own consumption, which is not subject to designated slaughter, no unit or individual may engage in pig slaughtering without being designated. Pig products sold by units and individuals engaged in selling pig products or producing and processing meat food, and pig products produced and operated by catering providers and collective dining units, must be pig products from designated pig slaughterhouses that have passed quarantine and meat quality inspection. That means the pork you sell and the pork you use must all come from designated slaughterhouses, and both quarantine and meat quality inspection must be passed. Slaughtering pigs privately outside designated slaughterhouses brings an order to close, confiscation of the pigs, the slaughtering tools and equipment, and the money earned, with the fine the same as the tier above for unlicensed food business. The Animal Epidemic Prevention Law: animal products sold and transported must travel with the goods carrying quarantine certificates and quarantine marks. Not carrying them brings an order to correct, plus a fine of up to one time the value of similar compliant goods. The judicial interpretation: where food made or sold by small workshops and food vendors does not meet food safety standards, buyers demanding tenfold compensation (punitive damages, the extra sum paid on top of the refund) shall be supported by the courts (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025 年第三次修正). 食品安全法（第三十五、三十六、一百二十二条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=7b5a76d0461745a08d3f964916b87ef3>；国务院 (2021). 生猪屠宰管理条例（国务院令第 742 号，第二、二十三、三十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-07/22/content_5626534.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2021 修订). 动物防疫法（第五十一、一百条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8080817703add2017737395a973e31>；最高人民法院 (2024). 关于审理食品药品惩罚性赔偿纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2024〕9 号，第四条）. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/bf531a70471f10d377788f28d3d8f8.html>
- Notes: Thresholds for small workshops, small caterers, and food vendors are set by each province; this entry writes no specific numbers. Go to the website of your province's market regulation bureau, search for the regulation on small food workshops, small caterers, and food vendors, and read the local version. Selling homemade food online does not save you a license just because it is online; whatever needs a license still needs it. Beyond licensing there are the general rules of this section: legal form and license, see item 6 (settle the name, premises, business scope, and registered capital before registering); whether other trades need a license, see item 7 (trades that need a license). What buyers can claim on their side, see section 5, item 31 (tenfold compensation for food).

### 9. Bag it up and sell it, and it is prepackaged food: the label must not miss the production date, shelf life, or ingredients list
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Printing labels costs a few cents to a few tenths of a yuan per sheet. Filling in the dates and ingredients correctly for every batch takes some time.
- In plain terms: Food put into packaging and then sold is called prepackaged food in law, and the packaging must carry a label, with nine items that must be stated, production date and shelf life among them. Bulk food must also be labeled with the name, the dates, and the seller's name and address. Missing what should be stated draws a fine of 5,000 to 50,000 yuan where the value of goods is under 10,000, and 5 to 10 times the value where it reaches 10,000.
- Benefit: The Food Safety Law: prepackaged food, meaning food put into packaging and then sold, must have a label on the packaging. The label must state these contents: name, specifications, net content, production date. The ingredients or the list of ingredients. The producer's name, address, and contact details. Shelf life. The product standard code. Storage conditions. The generic names in the national standard of the food additives used. The production license number. And whatever laws, regulations, or food safety standards require to be stated in addition. Bulk food is the kind not put into bags, weighed as needed. Selling bulk food also requires stating several things on the container and outer packaging. First, the name of the food. Second, the production date or production batch number. Third, the shelf life. Fourth, the name, address, contact details, and similar contents of the producer or seller. Prepackaged food made or sold without a label, or with a label or instructions not conforming to this law, brings confiscation of the money earned together with the food. Where the total value of the goods at sale price (value of goods) is under 10,000 yuan, an additional fine of 5,000 to 50,000 yuan is imposed. Where the value of goods is 10,000 yuan or more, the fine is 5 to 10 times the value of the goods. Serious cases bring an order to stop production or business, up to revocation of the license. Where the label or instructions have only minor flaws that neither affect food safety nor mislead consumers, the order is to correct first; a fine of up to 2,000 yuan comes only on refusal to correct. The new edition of the general standard for prepackaged food labels carries the number GB 7718-2025. It was issued on March 16, 2025, and takes effect from March 16, 2027. Two years of transition are given in between. It requires setting aside a separate area on the packaging showing at the same time the production date and the expiry date of the shelf life. The dates must use high-contrast, easy-to-read fonts and colors, and be laid out in year-month-day order (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A

When the boss lends his own money to an employee, that is a loan contract. Except where two natural persons agree otherwise, it must be in writing, stating how much was borrowed, how interest is computed, when repayment is due, and how it is made. A bonus promised verbally without anything on paper: in a real dispute, the side without evidence loses (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 劳动合同法（第十七、三十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第四百六十九、六百六十八条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: It is not about refusing to help people; it is about helping within the system too. A favor outside the system, once it becomes routine, turns into a dispute the moment you try to stop it. Mixing private lending with the company's books means that when the company loses, the bad debts land on your own head. The statutory parts have been verified; the recommendation itself is experience, hence grade C.

### 18. Sell before you build: use pre-sales and small orders to verify that someone will buy, then put money into production
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: A few dozen to a few hundred yuan for a sample or a single page. Then find a few dozen real customers and have them prepay or place small orders. This is a few weeks slower than starting production right away. The hard part is holding back from making the goods first.
- In plain terms: Someone followed 116 new Italian companies for about a year: founders who first stated a hypothesis and then tested it against market feedback ran better businesses, were more willing to change course in time, and were not more likely to give up early. So if nobody will prepay and nobody will place a small order, do not believe that once it is made, someone will naturally buy it.
- Benefit: A randomized controlled trial followed 116 Italian startups for about a year. A randomized controlled trial splits people randomly into two groups, gives the training to only one group, and then compares the results of the two. The result: founders who were trained and made decisions by first stating a hypothesis and then testing it strictly against market feedback performed better, were more likely to turn around in time to a different plan, and did not give up early at a higher rate. The authors' explanation is that this approach reduced the probability of continuing to pour money into projects that look promising but cannot actually be done (false positive projects) (Italy, published 2020).
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Camuffo A, Cordova A, Gambardella A, Spina C (2020). A Scientific Approach to Entrepreneurial Decision Making: Evidence from a Randomized Control Trial. Management Science 66(2):564-586. <https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2018.3249>
- Notes: This is only one study, with a small sample, and it was done in Italy, hence grade B. The title, source, and abstract were checked against Crossref and Semantic Scholar; the effect numbers in the full text were not verified for this section. On the practical side: if nobody will prepay and nobody will place a small order, do not believe that once it is made, someone will buy it.

### 19. Once the sample is made, run it through a mass-production checklist before talking about starting production
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: A few hundred yuan for one small-batch trial run. Then a few more hours to work out these items: yield (out of 100 pieces, how many are sellable), suppliers' minimum order quantities and payment terms, how much money inventory will tie up, packaging and logistics, certification and licenses, trademarks. The hard part is holding back from skipping this step and starting production straight away.
- In plain terms: The difference between one sample and a thousand pieces lies in where the money is pinned down. Yield determines the real cost. Minimum order quantities and payment terms determine how much money you must put up front. Inventory determines when the money comes back. If the product is in the compulsory certification catalog, it cannot leave the factory or go on sale without certification and the certification mark.
- Benefit: The difference between one sample and a thousand pieces lies in where the money is pinned down. Yield determines the real cost; yield means what share of a produced batch is compliant and sellable. The minimum a supplier makes you order at one time (minimum order quantity) and how long after shipment you get paid (payment terms) — these two determine how much money you must put up front. Inventory determines when the money comes back. Products listed in the compulsory product certification catalog must pass certification and carry the certification mark on the product before they may leave the factory and be sold. Leaving the factory and selling without certification is punished under the regulation on certification and accreditation. Running catering and selling food requires a food operation license (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 质检总局 (2009). 强制性产品认证管理规定（质检总局令第 117 号，第二、四十九条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2010/content_1533513.htm>；市场监管总局 (2023). 食品经营许可和备案管理办法（市场监管总局令第 78 号，第四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10606/202307/content_6894763.html>
- Notes: The checklist itself is an experienced method; the statutory parts have been verified against the original text. Before starting production, first search the State Intellectual Property Office's trademark query system for similar registrations, then apply for your own trademark. The red lines on using other people's trademarks and designs, see items 20 and 21 (keep receipts when buying stock, make the designs yourself or buy a license). For the compulsory certification catalog, go by the latest version published by the State Administration for Market Regulation.

### 20. For every batch of stock keep the receipts and the supplier's details, and never buy goods clearly below market price: when an employee sources fakes, it is the boss who gets sentenced
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. For every batch of goods keep these: the purchase contract, invoices, payment records, and the supplier's business license and authorization documents. Also set one internal rule: whoever sources the goods signs for them, and anything clearly priced below market is never bought. The hard part is passing up those cheap goods that look so profitable.
- In plain terms: Selling fakes: earn 30,000 yuan or sell 50,000 yuan, and it is enough for a sentence of up to 3 years. Goods sitting unsold in the warehouse count too. I did not know it was fake is not yours to decide: if the purchase price is clearly below market and you cannot explain why, you will be found to have known. Keep the purchase contract, invoices, payment records, and the supplier's details, and only then is there the path to escaping compensation.
- Benefit: Knowingly selling goods bearing counterfeit registered trademarks: once the money earned (illegal gains) reaches 30,000 yuan, or the amount sold reaches 50,000 yuan, the threshold for criminal punishment is crossed. Up to 3 years, with a fine, or a fine alone. Ten times this standard brings 3 to 10 years plus a fine. Goods still sitting unsold in the warehouse count too: where the value of goods reaches three times the sales-amount standard or more, it likewise constitutes a crime. Knowledge does not require your own confession. The 2025 judicial interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate lists five situations in which your knowledge may be found directly, unless you can produce evidence proving you truly did not know. First, knowing the registered trademark on the goods was altered, swapped, or covered over. Second, forging or altering the trademark holder's authorization documents, or knowing those documents were forged or altered. Third, having received criminal or administrative punishment for selling counterfeit goods and selling the same kind again. Fourth, without justified reason, buying or selling at a price clearly below the market price. Fifth, after being discovered by administrative or judicial authorities, moving or destroying infringing goods, accounting vouchers, and other evidence, or providing false proof. Where a company violates this clause, the unit is fined, and the directly responsible managers and other directly responsible personnel are punished under the same clause. That is, the company is fined, and the person who made the call and the person who handled it are sentenced under the same clause. So even if an employee sourced the goods, it is still the one who made the call who is fined and sentenced. The other way round, the Trademark Law leaves one way out: if at the time of selling you genuinely did not know the goods infringed someone's registered trademark, and you can prove the goods were lawfully obtained by you and name the provider, you bear no liability to compensate. The full toolkit for not paying is the receipts and the supplier's details; without them the way out cannot be used (nationwide; the judicial interpretation applies from April 26, 2025).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 刑法修正案（十一）（第十七、二十四项，即刑法第二百一十四、二百二十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/202012/t20201227_503682.shtml>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2025). 关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2025〕5 号，2025 年 4 月 26 日起施行）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202504/t20250424_693977.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2019). 商标法（2019 年修正，第五十七、六十四条；2026 年 6 月 26 日修订后为第七十二、七十八条，自 2027 年 1 月 1 日起施行）. <https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2019/7/30/art_95_28179.html>、<https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2026/6/26/art_95_206942.html>
- Notes: Cosmetics, liquor, mother-and-baby goods, digital products, and clothing are the high-frequency categories in such cases. The common point is that the same item has a huge price spread. Claims like channel goods, leftover stock, or overseas duty-free cannot replace receipts. Beyond the criminal sentence there is an administrative penalty: where infringement is established, the infringing goods and tools are confiscated, and with an illegal business turnover of 50,000 yuan or more a fine of up to 5 times can be added. If you have already taken in goods and then find something wrong, stop selling and seal them at once, then contact the brand owner or the market regulation department. Do not keep selling, and above all do not destroy the goods or the books. Destroying evidence falls exactly into the fifth situation above, presuming you knew it was fake. On using other people's designs, see item 21 (make the design yourself or buy a license). One scheme targets exactly this clause: someone comes to your door offering pens or other small goods for free, to be sold in your shop with the profit split later. Days later someone comes, buys one, gathers evidence on the spot, and then claims for infringement. Goods given for free miss all three conditions needed for escaping compensation in one stroke: no purchase contract or payment record, no real provider to name, and a price clearly out of line. None of lawfully obtained and naming the provider can be used. So anything delivered unsolicited, without receipts, leaving only a chat account, never goes on the shelf. If you really want to sell on consignment, sign a written consignment contract, keep a copy of the other side's ID card and business license, and write into it who owns the goods and who bears infringement liability.

### 21. Images on goods, packaging, hang tags, and promotional art: either make them yourself or buy a license; changing the color or adding an icon does not count as changed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Licenses from a legitimate image library start at a few dozen yuan per image. Original design by a hired designer runs a few hundred to a few thousand yuan. For every image used on a product, keep a copy of the license certificate and the purchase record. The hard part is not grabbing images found in a quick search online.
- In plain terms: Changing the color on someone's image, swapping the background, or adding your own icon is still using someone's work without permission. Reproducing someone's artwork for profit: earn 30,000 yuan, do 50,000 yuan in business, or print 500 copies, and it constitutes the crime of copyright infringement, punishable by up to 3 years. On the trademark side, being so alike as to cause confusion is itself infringement.
- Benefit: The Copyright Law lists using a work through adaptation, translation, annotation, and other means without the copyright holder's permission as infringement alongside direct copying. So changing the color on the original image, swapping the background, or adding your own icon is still using someone's work without permission. The threshold for criminal punishment is here: for profit, printing and selling (reproducing and distributing) an artwork without the permission of its copyright holder. Three numbers cross into crime. Money earned (illegal gains) reaching 30,000 yuan. Or the business reaching 50,000 yuan (illegal business turnover). Or the printed pieces adding up to 500 copies. Reaching any one of these numbers constitutes the crime of copyright infringement. Up to 3 years, with a fine, or a fine alone. Reaching ten times the standard brings 3 to 10 years. Only selling infringing copies printed by someone else, earning 50,000 yuan or more, or selling 100,000 yuan or more, is sentenced as the crime of selling infringing copies, up to 5 years. The same logic on the trademark side: using on the same kind of goods a trademark similar to a registered one, or using on similar goods an identical or similar trademark, is within the scope of infringement whenever it is likely to cause confusion. Similarity is an infringing situation written expressly in the law; changing a bit does not carve out a safe zone (nationwide; the judicial interpretation applies from April 26, 2025).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 著作权法（2020 年修正，第五十二条第六项、第五十三条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308796.html>；全国人大常委会 (2020). 刑法修正案（十一）（第二十、二十一项，即刑法第二百一十七、二百一十八条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/202012/t20201227_503682.shtml>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2025). 关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2025〕5 号）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202504/t20250424_693977.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2019). 商标法（2019 年修正，第五十七条第二项；2026 年修订后为第七十二条第二项）. <https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2019/7/30/art_95_28179.html>
- Notes: Three spots get stepped on most often. First, printing cartoon characters and celebrity photos found online onto clothes, cups, and phone cases. Second, fonts: most commercial fonts require a separate license; free preview does not mean free commercial use. Third, design drafts made for a client with unlicensed material tucked inside; the liability still sits with the one selling the goods. To keep it simple, use image libraries with commercial licenses and save the license page. Or have the designer state in the contract where the material comes from and the scope of the license, so the designer bears responsibility if trouble arises. Beyond criminal punishment there are civil compensation and administrative fines: where the public interest is harmed, illegal gains can be confiscated and a fine of 1 to 5 times the illegal business turnover imposed. Checking trademarks before production, see item 19 (run the sample through the mass-production checklist first). The other way round, when your own image is used by someone else, enforce your rights under these same rules.

### 22. Within your post, act by the procedures; for favors beyond the procedures, first work out who bears the responsibility
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. At work, act by the rules, by the physician's orders, by the process. Do not make decisions for others beyond your own authority. Before helping, ask yourself one question: if something goes wrong, who is responsible. The hard part is holding your ground on the spot against requests to just make an exception.
- In plain terms: Take a nurse as the example: the regulation states that on finding a patient in critical condition, the physician must be notified immediately; on finding a physician's order that violates the rules, the physician who issued it must be raised with. Other posts are the same: first see clearly where your own procedures draw the line of must report upward. The law does not tell people to walk past someone dying either: voluntary emergency rescue that injures the person being rescued requires no compensation.
- Benefit: Taking a nurse as the example, the duty boundaries written in the regulation are these. On finding a patient in critical condition, notify the physician immediately. In an emergency, to save a patient in mortal danger, necessary emergency care may be given first. On finding a physician's order that violates laws or regulations, or violates the technical norms of diagnosis and treatment, raise it with the physician, and when necessary report to the head of the department. The other side must also be stated clearly: the Civil Code provides that when a person voluntarily gives emergency aid and injures the person being rescued as a result, the rescuer does not pay compensation (bears no civil liability). What the law protects is rescue out of goodwill, not help someone and pay for it (nationwide).

### 23. When it fails, exit by the procedure: deregister through the simplified route if you can, go bankrupt when debts exceed assets, and do not just leave it alone
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Simplified deregistration can be done yourself: post it on the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System for 20 days. Where debts are still unpaid, first sort out the accounts, collect what is owed to you, pay what you owe; this step is called liquidation. Going bankrupt requires applying to the court and takes a few months. The hard part is not wanting to face the failure and dragging it out forever.
- In plain terms: With no debts owed and no unpaid wages, taxes, or social insurance, all investors write a commitment; if nobody objects during the 20 days of public notice, simplified deregistration goes through. An individually-owned business needs no public notice: 10 days without objection and it is deregistered directly. Unable to repay debts as they fall due, apply to the court for bankruptcy. Leaving it alone: the legal representative may not serve as legal representative of any other enterprise for 3 years.
- Benefit: Nothing you owe others and nothing owed to you, or everything already settled. No unpaid wages, no unpaid social insurance, no unpaid taxes. All investors have signed a written commitment. Meeting these conditions, post it for 20 days of public notice and with no objection raised, you can go through simplified deregistration. An individually-owned business needs no public notice: within 10 days with no objection from the departments concerned, it is deregistered directly. Where a company cannot repay debts as they fall due and what it owes exceeds what it has (insolvent), it may apply to the court for reorganization, composition, or bankruptcy liquidation. Where it has been dissolved without liquidation and owes more than it has, the person responsible for liquidation should apply for bankruptcy liquidation. The consequences of leaving it alone are these: failing to publish the annual report on schedule brings listing in the directory of abnormal operations. Three full years without publishing brings listing on the list of seriously unlawful enterprises. The legal representative and the person in charge may not serve as legal representative or person in charge of any other enterprise for 3 years. Those already listed in the directory of abnormal operations cannot even go through simplified deregistration (nationwide).
