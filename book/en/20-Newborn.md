# 20. How to care for a newborn

Scope: infant mortality and money. This section only covers what affects whether the child survives or how much you spend, and only where the gap is large enough and the evidence is solid. Soothing and day-to-day parenting tips are not covered here. For tasks before birth and on the day of discharge, see Section 27.
### 1. During infancy, put the baby to sleep on their back on a firm surface; share a room but not a bed; and do not place any soft items in the crib or bed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 人群=婴幼儿,怀孕 -->
- Cost: This costs nothing upfront. However, you’ll need to replace the mattress with a firm one, which may cost several hundred yuan. The real challenge is resisting pressure from older relatives to deviate from this rule.
- In plain terms: While the child is still an infant, lay the child face up on a firm surface that does not incline. An inclined cradle or an infant seat cannot be used as a bed. The adult and the child sleep in the same room, but not in the same bed. Do not put soft things on the bed, such as pillows, quilts, bed bumpers, or stuffed toys. In the United States alone, about 3500 infants die each year from causes related to sleep.
- Benefit: Each year, nearly 3,500 infants in the U.S. lose their lives to sleep-related issues, including sudden infant death syndrome, deaths of unknown cause, and accidental suffocation in bedding. The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends a safe sleep environment consisting of: infants sleeping on their backs; using a solid, non-inclined sleeping surface; sharing a room without sharing a bed; and avoiding soft bedding and overheating. Additional protective measures include breastfeeding, keeping infants away from nicotine and alcohol, ensuring all vaccinations are up to date, and offering a pacifier during sleep.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:Moon RY, Carlin RF, Hand I, Task Force on Sudden Infant Death Syndrome and the Committee on Fetus and Newborn (2022). Sleep-Related Infant Deaths: Updated 2022 Recommendations for Reducing Infant Deaths in the Sleep Environment. Pediatrics, 150(1), e2022057990. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2022-057990>
- Notes: “Do not place any soft items in the bed” means absolutely no pillows, blankets, bed rails, stuffed toys, or shaped pillows — even nursing pillows that appear perfectly safe are prohibited. Inclined cribs and baby chairs must never be used as sleeping areas. This requirement represents the biggest departure from common practice in this section, and it often requires the strongest resistance to family pressure.

### 2. Give the 1st dose of hepatitis B vaccine within 24 hours after birth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 人群=婴幼儿,怀孕 -->
- Cost: No money. The hepatitis B vaccine is on the immunization schedule (免疫规划) and is free to begin with. The child gets it at the hospital on the day of birth, so there is no extra trip.
- In plain terms: The 1st dose of hepatitis B vaccine has to be given within 24 hours after the child is born. Among people infected in infancy or early childhood, about 95% go on to chronic hepatitis. People infected only after they are grown mostly get better on their own, so this dose is more useful the earlier it is given. If the mother's hepatitis B surface antigen is positive, the child also needs hepatitis B immune globulin within 12 hours of birth, at the same time.
- Benefit: The World Health Organization says: "All infants should receive the hepatitis B vaccine as soon as possible after birth (within 24 hours)." The reason is that "about 95% of infections in infancy and early childhood develop into chronic hepatitis." The great majority of people infected as adults clear it on their own (they get better without treatment). The WHO's own words are that "under 5%" of infections after adulthood become chronic. The National Health Commission (国家卫生健康委员会) protocol for interrupting mother-to-child transmission requires that a child born to a mother who is positive for hepatitis B surface antigen "receive the 1st dose of hepatitis B vaccine as early as possible within 12 hours after birth, and at the same time an injection of 100 international units of hepatitis B immune globulin."
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:World Health Organization. Hepatitis B fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hepatitis-b>；国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2020). 预防艾滋病、梅毒和乙肝母婴传播工作规范（2020 年版）. <https://wjw.xinjiang.gov.cn/hfpc/zhgl6/202012/08b7de496f0b40e586d4b99d00cf79c4.shtml>（新疆维吾尔自治区卫生健康委员会转载）
- Notes: If the mother's hepatitis B surface antigen is positive, the child also needs 1 shot of hepatitis B immune globulin at the same time after birth. This has to be checked at prenatal care. Do not wait until after the birth to ask. The screening the mother herself needs is in Section 1.

### 3. Finish the vaccines on the national immunization schedule; they are free the whole way, and a missed series means catching up only the doses not yet given
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 人群=婴幼儿,孩子,怀孕 -->
- Cost: No money. The law states that "a vaccination provider may not charge any fee for giving a vaccine on the immunization schedule." You will make several trips to the vaccination clinic as the child ages. Keep the vaccination certificate (预防接种证). Do not lose it.
- In plain terms: Vaccines on the national immunization schedule are free for the whole series, and the vaccination provider cannot charge. Daycare and school both check the vaccination certificate, and a missed dose is generally found and made up. If you missed some, you only catch up the doses not yet finished. You do not start the whole series over, and you can continue even if the manufacturer changes.
- Benefit: When a child enters daycare or school, the daycare or the school checks the vaccination certificate. If the child was not vaccinated on the immunization schedule as required, they report it to the vaccination provider and help press the guardian to catch up. So a missed vaccine is generally found when the certificate is checked at daycare or school entry. The schedule now in force is the 2026 edition. Human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccine is already on the national immunization schedule. The starting age for the diphtheria-tetanus-pertussis vaccine (百白破) changed from 3 months to 2 months. There are 5 doses in all, 1 dose at each of 2 months, 4 months, 6 months, 18 months, and 6 years. From March (3) 2026, children of the right age in Tibet, Qinghai, Xinjiang, and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps (新疆生产建设兵团) also receive Japanese encephalitis vaccine (乙脑) as a routine vaccination. Several vaccines can be given on the same day: at this stage, national immunization-schedule vaccines can be given at the same time, following the schedule or the catch-up rules. 2 or more injected vaccines must go into different sites. "It is strictly forbidden to draw 2 or more vaccines into the same syringe and inject them." If they are not given on the same day, 2 or more injected live attenuated vaccines need an interval of not less than 28 days. There is no interval limit between inactivated vaccines, oral live attenuated vaccines, and other vaccines. The catch-up rule for a child under 18 years of age is to catch up as soon as possible: "only the doses not yet finished need to be given; there is no need to start the whole series over." The same vaccine can be continued if the manufacturer changes (nationwide, 2026 edition of the schedule).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2019). 疫苗管理法（第四十八、四十九条）. <http://ynswsjkw.yn.gov.cn/html/2019/faguiguizhangxin_0731/6509.html>（云南省卫生健康委员会转载）；国家疾病预防控制局、国家卫生健康委 (2026). 《国家免疫规划疫苗儿童免疫程序及说明（2026 年版）》解读问答. <https://www.ndcpa.gov.cn/jbkzzx/c100013/common/content/content_2073002930292035584.html>
- Notes: The most common misunderstanding is "the child is not strong, so wait." The official line is that a so-called "allergic constitution," allergies in the family, or a past allergy to a food or a drug is not a reason to skip the vaccine. Atopic dermatitis (eczema), hives, allergic rhinitis, and bronchial asthma can all be vaccinated as usual if the condition is stable. A child who is taking an allergy medicine, or using an inhaled steroid, can still be vaccinated. Physiological jaundice and breast-milk jaundice in a newborn can also be vaccinated as usual. There are only 2 true reasons not to vaccinate: an acute severe allergic reaction to the same vaccine before, or a severe allergy to a known component of the vaccine. A child with an immune deficiency, a child on chemotherapy or radiotherapy, or a child on an immunosuppressant needs a separate assessment. Explain the situation at the vaccination clinic and decide there. The first hepatitis B dose is in item 2 of this section. A non-schedule vaccine you pay for yourself is charged a vaccine fee and a vaccination service fee, and has nothing to do with the free vaccines this item is about.

### 4. For the first 6 months, feed only breast milk, not even water; from 6 months add complementary foods and keep breastfeeding
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=死亡率 人群=婴幼儿,怀孕 -->
- Cost: No money, and it saves the cost of formula. What it takes is time. Feeding takes a large part of every day. The hard part is keeping it up, and not stopping in the first 6 months.
- In plain terms: Start feeding in the 1st hour after the child is born. For the first 6 months, feed only breast milk, not even water. From a full 6 months, add complementary foods, with iron-rich animal foods every day, and keep breastfeeding to 2 years or longer. If there is not enough breast milk, or breastfeeding is not possible, use formula.
- Benefit: The World Health Organization recommends "starting breastfeeding within the 1st hour after birth." For the first 6 months, "exclusive breastfeeding, meaning no other food or liquid, including water." Then "from 6 months of age, start safe and adequate complementary foods, and continue breastfeeding to 2 years or beyond." The National Health Commission service guide also promotes exclusive breastfeeding from 0 to 6 months. The same guide requires that from a full 6 months "the day's complementary foods must include iron-rich animal foods."
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:World Health Organization. Breastfeeding. <https://www.who.int/health-topics/breastfeeding>；国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2024). 婴幼儿营养喂养评估服务指南（试行）（国卫办妇幼函〔2024〕452 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202502/content_7002872.htm>
- Notes: If breast milk is not enough, or the body does not allow breastfeeding, use formula. There is no need to feel guilty. The gap between breast milk and formula is far smaller than the safe-sleep item. That is the author's judgment. How often to add complementary foods, from that Health Commission service guide: at 6 to 8 months, 1 to 2 times a day; at 9 to 12 months, 2 to 3 times a day. Each day's complementary foods are chosen from 7 food groups, and not fewer than 4. If the child has severe eczema or an egg allergy, whether to avoid peanuts is in item 12 of this section (do not avoid peanuts). If the mother eats vegetarian, or wants the child to eat vegetarian, see item 14 of this section (on a vegetarian diet, supplement vitamin B12 every day).

### 5. Mix formula with water above 70 °C, let it cool before feeding, and throw away what is left
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 人群=婴幼儿,怀孕 -->
- Cost: No money. Each time, wait a few extra minutes for the milk to cool. The hard part is that when the child is hungry and crying, you still wait for the water to cool before you mix.
- In plain terms: Formula is not sterile. It can carry a bacterium that kills 4 in 10 to 8 in 10 of the newborns who catch it. Pour the hot water in first, wait until it falls to about 73.8 °C, then add the powder. In most cases that can kill this bacterium down to below 1/100000 of what it was. Hot water leaves a bit less vitamin C, but the formulas that were measured still met the standard. Let it cool after mixing, then feed. Throw away what is left.
- Benefit: Formula is not sterile. It can carry Cronobacter (阪崎克罗诺杆菌). The reported death rate after a newborn is infected is 40% to 80%. Experimental work shows: pour the hot water into the bottle first, wait until the water falls to about 73.8 °C, then add the powder, and in the great majority of cases the milk is still above 70 °C. That can cut this bacterium by 5 or more orders of magnitude, down to below 1/100000 of what it was. The price of hot water shows up in vitamin C. The World Health Organization and the UN Food and Agriculture Organization held an expert meeting in 2006. The figures seen at that meeting were: when formula is mixed with boiling water, only vitamin C falls clearly, by 5.6% to 65.6% across 4 formulas. Formula leaves the factory with extra vitamin C already added. The formula that lost the most still had 9.0 milligrams per 100 kilocalories after mixing, above the Codex Alimentarius floor of 8 milligrams. The meeting saw only this 1 study, so it drew no conclusion on it.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:Beary MA, Daly SE, Baker J, Snyder AB (2025). Assessing Hot Water Reconstitution Instructions and Labeling of Powdered Infant Formula to Ensure Cronobacter spp. Reduction. Journal of Food Protection, 88(9), 100571. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfp.2025.100571>；Sima WG, Legesse T, Girma S, et al. (2025). Emerging microbial risks: Cronobacter sakazakii in powdered infant formula for infants under six months of age in Ethiopia. BMC Microbiology, 26, 307. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s12866-025-04380-y>；World Health Organization & FAO (2007). Safe preparation, storage and handling of powdered infant formula: guidelines. <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241595414>；Bundesinstitut für Risikobewertung (2022). Recommendations for the hygienic preparation of powdered infant formula. Updated BfR Opinion No. 009/2022. <https://doi.org/10.17590/20220510-104706>；Agostoni C, Axelsson I, Goulet O, et al. (2004). Preparation and handling of powdered infant formula: a commentary by the ESPGHAN Committee on Nutrition. Journal of Pediatric Gastroenterology and Nutrition, 39(4), 320-322. <https://doi.org/10.1097/00005176-200410000-00002>；Silano M, Paganin P, Davanzo R (2016). Time for the 70 °C water precautionary option in the home dilution of powdered infant formula. Italian Journal of Pediatrics, 42, 17. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s13052-016-0228-9>；Losio MN, Pavoni E, Finazzi G, et al. (2018). Preparation of Powdered Infant Formula: Could Product's Safety Be Improved? Journal of Pediatric Gastroenterology and Nutrition, 67(4), 543-546. <https://doi.org/10.1097/MPG.0000000000002100>
- Notes: Disputed. The 2022 opinion of the German Federal Institute for Risk Assessment is that a healthy full-term baby can be mixed with water at 20 to 50 °C. It notes that most manufacturers also print 40 to 50 °C on the tin. Its reason is that 70 °C water cools quickly after it is in the bottle, so the kill is not as large as the calculation. Hot water also loses some nutrients, and feeding before it has cooled can burn the child. The European Society for Paediatric Gastroenterology, Hepatology and Nutrition, in 2004, also did not favor mixing with boiling water, worrying that it would damage vitamins and the probiotics added to the formula. The World Health Organization and the UN Food and Agriculture Organization still recommend above 70 °C. The highest risk is for babies born early, babies with a birth weight under 2500 grams, babies under 2 months, and babies with weak immunity. Mix those at 70 °C, or ask a doctor. For a healthy full-term baby mixed at the temperature on the tin, this German opinion considers the infection risk extremely low. The 70 °C threshold comes from public-health guidance. Beary and colleagues' own words were: "public-health guidance for caregivers of high-risk infants recommends reconstituting formula with water heated to at least 70 °C (158 °F) to inactivate microorganisms." Do not mix with water that has sat in a thermos for 1 day. Do not leave mixed milk at room temperature for the next feed.

### 6. Do not feed honey to a child under 1 year of age
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 人群=婴幼儿,怀孕 -->
- Cost: No money.
- In plain terms: If the child is under 1 year of age, do not feed honey. Honey water, honey mixed into complementary foods, and pastries that contain honey all count. Honey can carry the bacterium that causes botulism. The bacteria in an adult's gut can hold it down. An infant's cannot.
- Benefit: The US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention says "do not feed honey to children under 1 year of age," because "honey may contain the bacteria that cause botulism." An adult's gut bacteria can hold down these spores (the dormant form of this bacterium). An infant's cannot.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:US CDC. Preventing Botulism. <https://www.cdc.gov/botulism/prevention/index.html>
- Notes: What must not be fed includes honey water, complementary foods mixed with honey, and pastries that contain honey.

### 7. The vitamin K shot at birth has to be given
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 人群=婴幼儿,怀孕 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan to a few dozen yuan. This sum is usually already inside the hospital bill for the birth, so you do not pay it separately.
- In plain terms: A newborn has very little vitamin K stored. Without a supplement, bleeding can occur, and the kind that shows up late is often bleeding in the brain. In Tashkent, where this prevention was not generally done, 478 out of every 100000 newborns had this bleeding. With prevention, the estimate is that more than 78% can be cut. For a home birth, or a birth at an irregular facility, ask whether it was given.
- Benefit: A newborn has very little vitamin K stored. Without a supplement, vitamin K deficiency bleeding can occur, and the kind that shows up late often presents as intracranial hemorrhage (bleeding in the brain). The Belgian consensus recommendation is "1 intramuscular injection of 1 or 2 milligrams of vitamin K at birth." Where prevention is not provided systematically, the rate of vitamin K deficiency bleeding is 478 cases per 100000 people. That figure comes from 42000 newborns in Tashkent. The authors estimate that prevention can bring the rate down by "more than 78.5%."
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:Fiesack S, Smits A, Rayyan M, et al. (2021). Nutrients, 13(11), 4109. <https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13114109>；Tursunov D, Yoshida Y, Yrysov K, et al. (2018). Nagoya Journal of Medical Science, 80(1), 11. <https://doi.org/10.18999/nagjms.80.1.11>
- Notes: At a regular hospital in China, the hospital generally gives this shot as routine. That is the author's experience. No single nationwide official rule was found. Before discharge, look at the discharge summary, or ask. For a home birth, or a birth at an irregular facility, confirm it on your own initiative. The injection meant here goes into the muscle. That Belgian consensus calls the oral route "a slightly worse substitute," used only when the parents refuse the injection: 2 milligrams by mouth at birth, then, for a breastfed child, 1 to 2 milligrams a week for 3 months.

### 8. If an infant under 3 months reaches 38 °C, go to the hospital directly and do not watch at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 人群=婴幼儿,怀孕 -->
- Cost: Registration plus tests, a few hundred yuan.
- In plain terms: If the child is under 3 months and the temperature reaches 38 °C, go to the hospital directly. Do not watch at home, and do not give a fever medicine first and then wait. The American Academy of Pediatrics set a specific plan for febrile infants who still look all right, so "looking all right" is not a reason to wait at home. The UK guidance also counts a child under 3 months with a fever of 38 °C as high risk of serious illness.
- Benefit: The American Academy of Pediatrics wrote a specific guideline for full-term infants from 8 to 60 days of age who still look all right. That guideline defines fever as "a temperature of 38.0 °C or above." It splits them into 3 bands, 8 to 21 days, 22 to 28 days, and 29 to 60 days, and gives each band its own path. Infants under 8 days of age are outside that guideline. The UK NICE fever guideline covers children under 5 years of age. It states that a child under 3 months with a temperature of 38 °C or above is in the "high-risk group for serious illness."
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:Pantell RH, Roberts KB, Adams WG, et al. (2021). Evaluation and Management of Well-Appearing Febrile Infants 8 to 60 Days Old. Pediatrics, 148(2), e2021052228. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2021-052228>；NICE (2019, 2021 更新). Fever in under 5s: assessment and initial management (NG143), 1.2.12. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng143/chapter/Recommendations>
- Notes: At this age, a serious bacterial infection can show up as fever alone. So looking "all right" is not a reason to watch at home. Do not give a fever medicine yourself and then wait and see.

### 9. No matter how tired or angry you are, never shake a baby
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 人群=婴幼儿,怀孕 -->
- Cost: There is no monetary cost. The real difficulty lies in resisting the urge to shake a baby when you’re exhausted or upset.
- In plain terms: A baby’s head makes up a large proportion of their body size, yet their neck muscles are still very weak. Shaking them forcefully can cause bleeding inside the brain and eyes, potentially leading to death or lifelong disabilities. Gentle rocking while trying to soothe them is not dangerous; the real risk comes from uncontrolled, violent shaking. When you feel yourself losing control, place the baby back in their crib and step away for a few minutes. Letting them cry for a short while is far safer than holding them and shaking them.
- Benefit: Because a baby’s head is proportionally large and their neck muscles are underdeveloped, violent shaking can result in intracranial and retinal hemorrhage — bleeding in both the brain and eyes. This can be fatal or leave lasting impairments. Such harm is distinct from any gentle rocking used to calm them. Medically, injuries caused by shaking or impact are termed “abusive head trauma.” This condition is the leading cause of death from head injuries among children under 2 years old. In the United States between 1999 and 2014, it accounted for nearly 2,250 deaths of children under 5.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:Spies EL, Klevens J (2016). Fatal Abusive Head Trauma Among Children Aged <5 Years — United States, 1999–2014. MMWR 65(20):505-509. <https://doi.org/10.15585/mmwr.mm6520a1>；Choudhary AK 等 (2018). Consensus statement on abusive head trauma in infants and young children. Pediatric Radiology 48(8):1048-1065. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00247-018-4149-1>
- Notes: When you truly feel you’re about to lose control, the safest action is to return the baby to their crib and leave the room for a few minutes. Allowing them to cry briefly is much safer than holding them and shaking them. This advice is especially vital for parents who care for their children alone and often suffer from sleep deprivation. For guidance on managing your own emotions, please refer to Section 3.

### 10. For diapers, ignore the brand and check 3 things: the fit, how often you change it, and whether a sampling notice has named it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 人群=婴幼儿,怀孕 -->
- Cost: From 200 or 300 yuan a month up to more than 1000 yuan, depending on the grade you use.
- In plain terms: No official body has ranked "which brand is best." Only 2 things can be checked: product-quality sampling notices from the State Administration for Market Regulation (市场监管总局), and regulator investigations of a specific event. Expensive does not mean safe, and imported does not mean up to standard. Buy a small pack and try it for 1 week. Look for a red bottom and marks from tightness, and stock up only if it fits.
- Benefit: No official body has published a ranking of "which brand is best," so this book does not recommend a brand. Only 2 kinds of information can be checked. The 1st is product-quality sampling notices from the State Administration for Market Regulation. The 2nd is a regulator's investigation of a specific event. After the "infant diaper formamide problem" drew attention in June (6) 2026, the State Administration for Market Regulation led a joint investigation team to look into it.
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources:市场监管总局 (2026-06-22). 市场监管总局牵头成立联合调查组核查「婴幼儿纸尿裤甲酰胺问题」：「针对媒体反映的"婴幼儿纸尿裤甲酰胺问题"，市场监管总局、工业和信息化部、国家卫生健康委、国家疾控局高度重视，成立联合调查组，核查婴幼儿纸尿裤甲酰胺有关问题，并依法依规处理。有关情况将及时公布。」. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/xw/zj/art/2026/art_78c0425db7604b27bd7a3f36ba319975.html>；中国政府网转载. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202606/content_7072931.htm>；NHS. Nappy rash（2026-08-28 审阅）. <https://www.nhs.uk/baby/caring-for-a-newborn/nappy-rash/>
- Notes: When the joint investigation team was set up, it only said "the relevant findings will be announced in due course." TODO (to be checked: the investigation's conclusion. As of September (9) 30, 2026, no conclusion on diapers was found on the State Administration for Market Regulation website or in the national supervision sampling notices of recent years). How to buy in practice: expensive does not equal safe, and imported does not equal up to standard. Buy a small pack and try it for 1 week. Look for a red bottom and marks from tightness, and stock up only if it fits. Those sentences are the author's experience. The UK NHS says common reasons for a red bottom are urine and stool left on the skin too long, not changing often enough, and the diaper rubbing the skin. Some diapers that do not absorb, or do not breathe, can also cause it. The NHS also says the diaper should fit, and should not be wrapped too tight. How to look up sampling notices is in Section 5.

### 11. For big items, consider them in the order borrow, buy used, buy new, and do not kit everything out all at 1 time
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 人群=婴幼儿,怀孕 -->
- Cost: No extra money. What you save is usually counted by the 1000 yuan. The hard part is holding back from kitting everything out all at 1 time.
- In plain terms: A stroller, a swaddle, and toys are used for a short time. Borrow first. If you cannot borrow, buy used. Buy new last. For a used car seat, confirm it was not in a heavier crash, was not recalled, and has all its parts. If you cannot confirm that, do not use it. In a Scottish study, usually sleeping on a mattress another child had used carried about 3 times the sudden-death risk, but it is not clear whether the mattress caused it. A mattress is best bought new.
- Benefit: Strollers, cribs, swaddles, and toys are all used for a short time, and the used market has far more supply than demand. Used goods have 3 safety lines. The 1st is the car seat. The US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration says a seat that has been in a moderate or severe crash must be replaced and never used again. All 5 conditions must be met before a crash counts as minor and the seat can stay in use. The 5 are: the car could be driven away on its own, the door nearest the seat was not damaged, no one in the car was injured, the airbag did not deploy, and no damage can be seen on the seat. A used seat can be used only after you confirm 5 things. The 5 are: it has not been in a crash worse than minor, the label shows the date of manufacture and the model, it has not been recalled, the parts are complete, and the instructions are still there. The date of manufacture and the model are how you check recalls and whether the seat is too old. The 2nd is the mattress. A Scottish study compared infants who died suddenly with healthy infants of the same age, and looked back at which mattress each slept on. Usually sleeping on a mattress another child had used carried a higher risk of sudden infant death syndrome (OR 3.07, about 3 times, 95% CI 1.51–6.22). When the mattress came from another home the figure was 4.78. When it came from the same home it was 1.64, and the latter does not count as a difference. ### 12. If the child has severe eczema or an egg allergy, do not avoid peanuts; add them early under a doctor's guidance, but never feed a whole peanut

### 13. Watch these newborn jaundice signals: yellow within 24 hours of birth, or cannot be woken and will not feed, go to the hospital that day; getting yellower, or pale stools, go and get it checked soon

- Cost: Registration plus a bilirubin test, within a few hundred yuan. If phototherapy is needed, it is usually about 2 days in hospital. The hard part is making the trip even when the child still looks all right.
- In plain terms: More than 8 in 10 newborns are a bit yellow. Yellow before 24 hours of age, or yellow together with being sleepier than usual and hard to wake, not feeding, or a body that is floppy or stiff, means go to the hospital immediately. After 24 hours, getting yellower, or stool that turns pale, also means get it checked soon. Pale stool can be biliary atresia (胆道闭锁). Bilirubin that is too high can injure the brain and leave a disability for life.
- Benefit: The American Academy of Pediatrics 2022 guideline says "more than 80% of newborns will have some degree of jaundice." Bilirubin that is too high can cause acute bilirubin encephalopathy and kernicterus (核黄疸). The guideline calls kernicterus "a permanent disabling neurological disease." Its features include cerebral palsy with limbs that twist on their own, an inability to look upward, poor enamel on the baby teeth, and hearing damage. The guideline lists "jaundice within 24 hours after birth" as a risk factor for going on to severe jaundice. The UK National Health Service (NHS) splits what it tells parents into 2 tiers. Tier 1 is to call emergency services or go straight to emergency care. That tier is a child who has jaundice and, at the same time, is sleepier than usual or hard to wake, is not feeding, is floppy or stiff, or has jerking movements. A child who is jaundiced before 24 hours of age is also in this tier. Tier 2 is to see a doctor as soon as possible. That tier is age past 24 hours, jaundice that is not getting better or is getting worse, urine that is dark yellow or brown, or stool that is pale cream. A global estimate is that in 2010 about 480000 newborns had Rh hemolytic disease (caused by a blood-type mismatch between mother and child) or extreme hyperbilirubinemia. Of those children, 24% died, and 13% developed kernicterus. Of the children who survived kernicterus, more than 83% were left with 1 or more impairments. Pale stool can be biliary atresia. That disease needs a Kasai operation (葛西手术) within 60 days before the result is clearly better. From 2004, Taiwan printed a stool color card in every newborn's child-health handbook. The share who had the operation within 60 days rose from 60% in 2004 to 74.3% in 2005. In another Taiwan-wide study, after the color card was introduced, mortality among children with biliary atresia fell from 26.2% to 15.9%. That is a before-and-after comparison, not a trial that split children into groups.

The benefit is set at medium by judgment, because how common infant rickets is in China was not found. The advice for formula-fed infants is not the same everywhere. The American Academy of Pediatrics says that if the child drinks more than about 800 milliliters (27 ounces) a day of formula with vitamin D added, no extra supplement is needed. The domestic core messages say every infant is supplemented, and the exact amount follows what the doctor says. Do not raise the dose yourself. In a Canadian trial, the group on 1600 IU a day had vitamin D in the blood that was too high, and that arm was stopped early. Growth and bone did not differ across the groups from 400 to 1200 IU. An adult who is not short of vitamin D should not supplement it in order to live longer. See Section 6, item 3 (people who are not short of vitamin D).

### 17. When bathing an infant, put cold water in first and then mix in hot, and test it with the inside of the wrist or the elbow before putting the child in; if the water heater can set the outlet temperature, set it below 50 °C
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 人群=婴幼儿,怀孕 -->
- Cost: No money. Each bath takes 1 or 2 extra minutes to test the water. If you want it steadier, you can buy a water thermometer. The hard part is still testing before you put the child in, even when the child is crying and the adult is in a hurry.
- In plain terms: Hot water at 65 °C, in contact for 2 seconds, gives most adults a 3rd-degree burn, and an infant's skin is thinner. Put cold water in the basin first, then mix in hot. Stir it even, test it with the inside of the wrist or the elbow, and only then put the child in. Do not wash the child under a running tap. After 1 US state lowered the factory outlet temperature of water heaters to 49 °C, hospital admissions of children for hot-water burns fell by more than 1/2.
- Benefit: The US Consumer Product Safety Commission says most adults get a 3rd-degree burn from 2 seconds in water at 150 °F (about 65 °C). Water at 140 °F (60 °C) for 6 seconds, or water at 130 °F (about 54 °C) for 30 seconds, also scalds. Even at 120 °F (49 °C), a soak of 5 minutes can still cause a 3rd-degree burn. A World Health Organization report says the skin of infants and young children is thinner than an adult's, so a lower temperature can scald, and the scald goes deeper. The American Academy of Pediatrics teaches parents to test the water in the basin with the inside of the wrist or the elbow, and that it should be "warm, not hot." "Never bathe a baby under running water," because water moving through the pipes swings hot and cold, and a small change can scald. In 1983 the US state of Washington passed a law that new water heaters leave the factory preset at 49 °C. By 1988, the average home hot-water temperature had fallen from 61 °C in 1977 to 50 °C. Hospital admissions of children for tap-water burns fell from 5.5 a year in the 70s to 2.4 a year. Burned area, skin grafts, deaths, and days in hospital all fell too. That is a before-and-after comparison, not a trial that split people into groups. Australia sets a maximum of 50 °C for bathroom hot-water taps. In New South Wales from 1989 to 1996, the rate of hospital admission for burns among children aged 0 to 4 fell by 13%. Severe burns, those with a hospital stay of 10 days or more, fell by 30% in the 2 years after the 2nd stage. In 3 Chinese hospital series of childhood burns, scalds from hot liquid accounted for 87.6% to 90.6%, and children under 3 years were the largest share.
- Evidence grade: B
