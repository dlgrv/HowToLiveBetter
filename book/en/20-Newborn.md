# 20. How to care for a newborn

Scope: infant mortality and money. This section only covers a few measures with large effect sizes and hard evidence; day-to-day childrearing details are outside the scope of this book. For what to handle before the birth and on the day of discharge from the hospital, see Section 27.
### 1. Have the baby sleep on their back, on a firm surface, in the same room but not the same bed, with no soft objects in the bed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; a firm mattress costs a few hundred yuan. The hard part is standing up to what the older relatives say and sticking with it
- In plain terms: Put the baby down to sleep face up, on a firm surface. Adults and baby share the same room but not the same bed. No pillows, duvets, bed bumpers, stuffed toys or head-shaping pillows in the bed. An inclined cradle or infant seat is for sitting only, not for sleeping. In the US alone, about 3,500 babies die in their sleep every year.
- Benefit: In the US, about 3,500 babies die each year from sleep-related causes. That includes sudden infant death syndrome, unexplained death, and accidental suffocation in bed. The American Academy of Pediatrics' safe sleep environment is "back sleeping position; use a firm, non-inclined sleep surface; room-sharing without bed-sharing; avoid soft bedding and overheating". Several other things lower the risk too: breastfeeding, keeping the baby away from nicotine and alcohol, timely vaccination, and pacifier use
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Moon RY, Carlin RF, Hand I, Task Force on Sudden Infant Death Syndrome and the Committee on Fetus and Newborn (2022). Sleep-Related Infant Deaths: Updated 2022 Recommendations for Reducing Infant Deaths in the Sleep Environment. Pediatrics, 150(1), e2022057990. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2022-057990>
- Notes: "no soft objects in the bed" means no pillows, duvets, bumpers, stuffed toys or head-shaping pillows at all — not even nursing pillows, which look perfectly safe. An inclined cradle or infant seat must not be used as a place to sleep. This is the measure with the biggest difference in this section, and the one where you most need to stand up to pressure from older relatives

### 2. Give the first hepatitis B vaccine dose within 24 hours of birth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; the hepatitis B vaccine belongs to the national immunization program and is free to begin with. The baby gets it at the hospital on the day of birth, so no extra trip needed
- In plain terms: The baby needs the first hepatitis B vaccine dose within 24 hours of birth. About 95% of people infected with hepatitis B in infancy or early childhood go on to chronic hepatitis, while most people infected as adults clear it on their own — so the earlier this shot is given, the more it does. If the mother is hepatitis B surface antigen positive, the baby also gets hepatitis B immunoglobulin at the same time.
- Benefit: The World Health Organization: "all infants should receive their first dose of hepatitis B vaccine as soon as possible after birth, preferably within 24 hours." The reason: "approximately 95% of infections in infancy and early childhood develop into chronic hepatitis," while most infections acquired in adulthood clear on their own — that is, they get better without treatment
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: World Health Organization. Hepatitis B fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hepatitis-b>
- Notes: If the mother is hepatitis B surface antigen positive, the newborn also gets a shot of hepatitis B immunoglobulin at the same time. Sort this out during prenatal checkups — do not wait until after the birth to ask. Screening of the mother herself is covered in Section 1

### 3. Complete all the vaccines in the national immunization program — the full course is free, and if you fall behind you only catch up on the missed doses
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; the law states that "vaccination units may not charge any fee for vaccines in the immunization program". It takes several trips to the vaccination clinic by month of age. Keep the vaccination certificate safe and do not lose it
- In plain terms: All vaccines in the national immunization program are free, and vaccination units cannot charge a single cent. Nurseries and schools check the vaccination certificate at enrollment, so any missed doses will definitely be dug up and caught up then. If you fall behind, you only make up the doses that were not finished — you do not start the whole course over. You can continue the same vaccine from a different manufacturer. Eczema, hives, allergic rhinitis and asthma are all fine to vaccinate as long as the condition is stable, and the same goes for newborn jaundice.
- Benefit: When children enter nursery or school, childcare institutions and schools must check the vaccination certificate; if a child has not received the immunization program vaccines as required, they must report it to the vaccination unit and cooperate in urging the guardian to complete catch-up vaccination. So a missed dose is not "deal with it later" — it will definitely surface at school enrollment. The schedule currently in force is the 2026 edition. Human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccine has been added to the national immunization program. The DTaP vaccine start age has been moved from 3 months to 2 months, with 5 doses total — 1 dose each at 2 months, 4 months, 6 months, 18 months, and 6 years of age. Children of the appropriate age in Tibet, Qinghai, Xinjiang and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps will also routinely receive Japanese encephalitis vaccine from March 2026. Several vaccines can be given on the same day: at present, all national immunization program vaccines may be given at the same visit according to the immunization schedule or catch-up principles. Two or more injectable vaccines should go into different sites, and it is "strictly prohibited to mix two or more vaccines in the same syringe for injection". If not given on the same day, two or more injectable live attenuated vaccines should be at least 28 days apart; there is no interval restriction between inactivated vaccines, oral live attenuated vaccines, and other vaccines. For catch-up under 18 years old, the principle is to catch up as early as possible: "only the incomplete doses need to be made up, there is no need to restart the full course", and the same vaccine from a different manufacturer can be continued (nationwide, 2026 edition schedule)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2019). 疫苗管理法（第四十八、四十九条）. <http://ynswsjkw.yn.gov.cn/html/2019/faguiguizhangxin_0731/6509.html>（云南省卫生健康委员会转载）；国家疾病预防控制局、国家卫生健康委 (2026). 《国家免疫规划疫苗儿童免疫程序及说明（2026 年版）》解读问答. <https://www.ndcpa.gov.cn/jbkzzx/c100013/common/content/content_2073002930292035584.html>
- Notes: The most common misconception is "the child's constitution is weak, hold off on the shots." The official position: a so-called "allergic constitution", allergic disease in the family, or a past food or drug allergy in the child are not reasons to skip vaccination. Atopic dermatitis (that is, eczema), hives, allergic rhinitis and bronchial asthma can all be vaccinated normally as long as the condition is stable. A child taking anti-allergy medication, or using inhaled corticosteroids, can also be vaccinated. Physiological newborn jaundice and breast-milk jaundice are also fine for normal vaccination. The only real contraindications are two: a past acute severe allergic reaction to the same vaccine, or a severe allergy to a known component of the vaccine. Children with immunodeficiency, and those on chemotherapy or radiotherapy or taking immunosuppressants, need to be assessed one by one — explain the situation at the vaccination clinic before deciding. The first hepatitis B dose is item 2. Self-paid non-program vaccines are billed separately for the vaccine and the vaccination service fee, which has nothing to do with the free vaccination this item covers.

### 4. Feed only breast milk for the first 6 months — not even water — then start complementary foods at 6 months while continuing to breastfeed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan, and it saves the cost of formula. What it costs is time — feeding takes up a good chunk of every day. The hard part is keeping it going without stopping in the first 6 months
- In plain terms: Start breastfeeding within the first hour after birth. Feed only breast milk for the first 6 months, not even water. From 6 months of age, start complementary foods and keep breastfeeding until age 2 or beyond. The complementary foods must include iron-rich animal foods every day. If breast milk is not enough or breastfeeding is not possible, use formula and do not blame yourself. What you feed matters far less to whether the child survives than the back-sleeping item above.
- Benefit: The World Health Organization recommends "beginning breastfeeding within the first hour after birth". For the first 6 months, "exclusive breastfeeding, meaning no other food or liquids are provided, including water". Then "starting safe and adequate complementary foods from 6 months of age, while continuing breastfeeding up to 2 years of age or beyond". The National Health Commission's service guidelines likewise promote exclusive breastfeeding from 0 to 6 months. Those guidelines also require that from 6 months of age onward, "daily complementary foods must include iron-rich animal foods"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: World Health Organization. Breastfeeding. <https://www.who.int/health-topics/breastfeeding>；国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2024). 婴幼儿营养喂养评估服务指南（试行）（国卫办妇幼函〔2024〕452 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202502/content_7002872.htm>
- Notes: If breast milk is not enough, or your body does not allow breastfeeding, use formula and do not feel guilty. The gap between breastfeeding and formula is far smaller than the gap in the safe-sleep item. How often to add complementary foods: 1 to 2 times a day from 6 to 8 months, 2 to 3 times a day from 9 to 12 months. The foods eaten each day should cover at least 4 of the 7 food categories. Whether to keep peanuts away from a child with severe eczema or egg allergy is covered in item 12 of this section (do not keep peanuts away).

### 5. Make formula with water at 70 ℃ or hotter, let it cool before feeding, and throw out any leftovers
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; a few extra minutes each time to let the formula cool. The hard part is waiting for the water to cool before mixing, even when the baby is hungry and crying
- In plain terms: Formula is not sterile and may carry a bacterium called Cronobacter sakazakii. Once a newborn is infected, the reported case fatality rate is between 40% and 80%. To prepare it, first pour the hot water into the bottle, and wait until the water has cooled to about 73.8 ℃ before adding the powder. The formula will still be above 70 ℃ at that point, which kills this bacterium down to less than one hundred-thousandth of its original amount. Let the prepared formula cool before feeding, and throw out whatever the baby does not finish.
- Benefit: Formula is not sterile and may carry Cronobacter sakazakii. The reported case fatality rate after infection in newborns is 40% to 80%. Experimental research shows: if you first pour hot water into the bottle and wait until the water has cooled to about 73.8 ℃ before adding the powder, the formula temperature is still above 70 ℃ in the vast majority of cases. That reduces this bacterium by more than 5 log levels, meaning down to less than one hundred-thousandth of its original amount
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Beary MA, Daly SE, Baker J, Snyder AB (2025). Assessing Hot Water Reconstitution Instructions and Labeling of Powdered Infant Formula to Ensure Cronobacter spp. Reduction. Journal of Food Protection, 88(9), 100571. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfp.2025.100571>；Sima WG, Legesse T, Girma S, et al. (2025). Emerging microbial risks: Cronobacter sakazakii in powdered infant formula for infants under six months of age in Ethiopia. BMC Microbiology, 26, 307. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s12866-025-04380-y>；World Health Organization & FAO (2007). Safe preparation, storage and handling of powdered infant formula: guidelines. <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241595414>
- Notes: The 70 ℃ threshold comes from public health guidance. Beary et al.'s original wording is "public health guidelines targeting caregivers of high-risk infants recommend preparing powdered infant formula with water heated to at least 70 ℃ (158 ℉) to inactivate microorganisms". Preterm babies, low-birth-weight babies and babies under 2 months of age are at the highest risk. Do not make formula with water that has sat in a thermos all day. And do not leave prepared formula standing at room temperature until the next feed

### 6. Do not feed honey to babies under 1 year old
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: Do not feed honey to a child under 1 year old. Honey water, honey mixed into complementary foods, and pastries containing honey all count. Honey may carry the bacteria that cause botulism. The gut flora of an adult can hold it down; an infant's cannot. Home-made pickles and fermented foods follow the same logic.
- Benefit: The US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention: "do not feed honey to children under 1 year of age," because "honey can contain the bacteria that cause botulism." The adult gut flora can hold down these spores — the dormant state of this bacterium — an infant's cannot
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: US CDC. Preventing Botulism. <https://www.cdc.gov/botulism/prevention/index.html>
- Notes: What must not be fed includes honey water, complementary foods mixed with honey, and pastries containing honey. By the same logic, do not give home-made pickles or fermented foods to infants either

### 7. Make sure the baby gets the vitamin K shot at birth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan to a few dozen yuan. This is usually already included in the cost of the hospital delivery, with nothing extra to pay
- In plain terms: Newborns naturally have very little vitamin K stored in their bodies. Without supplementation they can bleed, and the late-onset kind is often bleeding in the brain. In places where this shot is not given universally, the incidence of this bleeding is 478 cases per 100,000 newborns. Getting the shot is estimated to cut that by more than 78%. Legitimate hospitals in China give it routinely. If the birth was at home, or in an unregulated facility, ask yourself whether it was given.
- Benefit: Newborns have very little vitamin K stored in their bodies. Without supplementation, vitamin K deficiency bleeding can occur, with late-onset cases often presenting as intracranial hemorrhage — bleeding in the brain. The Belgian consensus recommendation is "a single intramuscular dose of 1 or 2 mg of vitamin K at birth". In a region without systematic provision, the incidence of vitamin K deficiency bleeding was 478 cases per 100,000 population, a figure from 42,000 newborns in Tashkent. The authors estimated that prevention could reduce the incidence by "more than 78.5%"
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Fiesack S, Smits A, Rayyan M, et al. (2021). Nutrients, 13(11), 4109. <https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13114109>；Tursunov D, Yoshida Y, Yrysov K, et al. (2018). Nagoya Journal of Medical Science, 80(1), 11. <https://doi.org/10.18999/nagjms.80.1.11>
- Notes: For a birth in a legitimate Chinese hospital, the hospital gives this shot routinely and you do not need to worry about it. If the birth was at home, or in an unregulated facility, go and confirm it yourself. Oral dosing works less well than the shot; the shot here means an injection into the muscle

### 8. Take any baby under 3 months with a temperature of 38 ℃ straight to the hospital — do not observe at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A few hundred yuan for registration and tests
- In plain terms: If a baby under 3 months reaches 38 ℃, go straight to the hospital. Do not observe at home, and do not give fever medicine first and wait and see. At this age, even a serious bacterial infection may show up as nothing but a fever. So "seems in decent spirits" cannot be taken as a reason to wait at home. For a baby less than 8 days old, even the guidelines do not cover it — all the more reason to go right away.
- Benefit: The American Academy of Pediatrics has a dedicated guideline for well-appearing full-term infants aged 8 to 60 days. That guideline defines fever as "temperature ≥38.0 ℃". It splits the age into three bands — 8 to 21 days, 22 to 28 days, and 29 to 60 days — and gives a management pathway for each. Babies under 8 days of age are not even within the guideline's scope, which means this stage calls for immediate medical care all the more
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Pantell RH, Roberts KB, Adams WG, et al. (2021). Evaluation and Management of Well-Appearing Febrile Infants 8 to 60 Days Old. Pediatrics, 148(2), e2021052228. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2021-052228>
- Notes: At this age, a serious bacterial infection may present with nothing but a fever. So looking "in decent spirits" is not grounds for observing at home. Do not give fever medicine yourself first and then wait and see either

### 9. Never shake a baby, no matter how tired or angry you are
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan. The hard part is stopping your hands when you are too tired or too angry yourself
- In plain terms: A baby's head is large relative to the body, and the neck muscles have no strength yet. Violent shaking makes him bleed in the brain and in the eyes; it can shake a baby to death, or leave a lifelong disability. The gentle rocking used to soothe a baby to sleep does not count — what is dangerous is the out-of-control kind of violent shaking. When you are about to lose your temper, put the baby back in the crib and walk away for a few minutes. Letting him cry for a while is far safer than holding and shaking him.
- Benefit: A baby's head is disproportionately large and the neck muscles are weak. Violent shaking causes intracranial hemorrhage and retinal hemorrhage — bleeding in the brain and in the eyes. It can be fatal, and it can leave lifelong disability. Gentle rocking to soothe a baby to sleep is not the same thing. What is dangerous is uncontrolled violent shaking. Medicine calls this class of injuries from shaking and impact "abusive head trauma". It is the leading cause of death from head trauma in children under 2. In the US from 1999 to 2014, it killed nearly 2,250 children under 5
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Spies EL, Klevens J (2016). Fatal Abusive Head Trauma Among Children Aged <5 Years — United States, 1999–2014. MMWR 65(20):505-509. <https://doi.org/10.15585/mmwr.mm6520a1>；Choudhary AK 等 (2018). Consensus statement on abusive head trauma in infants and young children. Pediatric Radiology 48(8):1048-1065. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00247-018-4149-1>
- Notes: When you truly reach the moment of nearly losing control, put the baby back in the crib first and walk away for a few minutes. Letting him cry for a while is far safer than shaking him in your arms. This item matters most for people caring for a baby alone and chronically short of sleep. How to handle your own emotions is covered in Section 3

### 10. Judge diapers not by brand but by three things: fit, how often they are changed, and whether they have ever been flagged in a spot-check bulletin
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Anywhere from two to three hundred yuan to over a thousand yuan per month, depending on the tier you buy
- In plain terms: No official body has ever ranked "which brand is best". There are only two things you can check: the market regulator's product quality spot-check bulletins, and the regulators' investigations of specific incidents. Expensive does not mean safe, and imported does not mean compliant. Buy a small pack first and try it for a week — watch for diaper rash and elastic marks — and stock up only if it fits.
- Benefit: No official body has ever published a ranking of "which brand is best", so this book gives no brand recommendations. There are only two kinds of verifiable information. One is the product quality spot-check bulletins of the State Administration for Market Regulation. The other is the regulators' investigations of specific incidents. After the "formamide in infant diapers" issue drew attention in June 2026, the State Administration for Market Regulation led the formation of a joint investigation team to verify it
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 中国政府网 (2026-06-22). 市场监管总局牵头成立联合调查组核查「婴幼儿纸尿裤甲酰胺问题」. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202606/content_7072931.htm>（该页仅有标题，正文未展示，故本条只引用调查这一事实，不引用任何结论）
- Notes: TODO (to be verified: the conclusions of that investigation, and the original text of the market regulator's official bulletin). How to buy in practice: expensive does not mean safe, and imported does not mean compliant. Buy a small pack first and try it for a week — watch for diaper rash and elastic marks — and stock up only if it fits. Diaper rash comes mainly from changing too infrequently and poor breathability, and its link to brand is smaller than you would think. How to look up spot-check bulletins is covered in Section 5

### 11. Consider big-ticket baby items in the order "borrow, buy second-hand, buy new" — do not buy everything at once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan extra; the savings usually run into the thousands of yuan. The hard part is resisting the urge to kit out everything in one go
- In plain terms: Strollers, cribs, swaddles and toys all get used for a very short time, and the second-hand market has plenty of sellers and few buyers. Ask to borrow first; if you cannot borrow, buy second-hand; only buy new as the last resort — that usually saves money in the thousands of yuan. A car seat and a mattress should not be bought second-hand: structural damage to a seat after a crash cannot be seen with the eye. Postpartum care centers and early-education classes are big money you can spend or skip; apply a 24-hour cooling-off period first.
- Benefit: Strollers, cribs, swaddles and toys all have very short usable lives, and second-hand supply far exceeds demand. The two things that genuinely cannot be skipped, and should not be bought second-hand, are the car seat and the mattress. Structural damage to a car seat after a crash is invisible to the naked eye
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 作者经验，无直接文献；安全座椅的证据见第 1 节，冲动消费见第 5 节
- Notes: Postpartum care centers, early-education classes and baby swimming pools — this book neither recommends nor rules them out. But they are all a large sum you can spend or skip, and the 24-hour cooling-off period described in Section 5 applies

### 12. If your child has severe eczema or egg allergy, do not keep peanuts away — add them early under a doctor's guidance, but never feed whole peanuts
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A jar of peanut butter costs a few dozen yuan. Fed a few times a week, it takes no extra effort. Before adding it, take the child to see a doctor once for an assessment.
- In plain terms: A randomized trial of 640 high-risk infants. The group that ate peanuts regularly from 4 to 11 months of age had a peanut allergy rate of 1.9% by age 5; the group that avoided them completely was at 13.7%. Among the children whose skin test was already weakly positive at enrollment, it was 10.6% versus 35.3%. Avoiding peanuts actually makes allergy more likely. But never feed whole peanuts — they can block the windpipe.
- Benefit: The UK LEAP randomized trial. 640 infants were enrolled on the condition of severe eczema, egg allergy, or both, all aged 4 to 11 months at enrollment. They were randomly split into two groups — one eating peanut products regularly, one avoiding them completely — and the peanut allergy rate was checked at 60 months of age. Among the 530 children with a negative peanut skin test at enrollment, the avoidance group had 13.7% allergic and the eating group 1.9% (P<0.001). Among the 98 children whose skin test was already weakly positive at enrollment, the avoidance group was 35.3% and the eating group 10.6% (P=0.004). The two groups showed no difference in serious adverse events.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Du Toit G, Roberts G, Sayre PH, 等 (2015). Randomized trial of peanut consumption in infants at risk for peanut allergy. New England Journal of Medicine, 372(9), 803-813. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1414850>；国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2020). 婴幼儿喂养健康教育核心信息. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2020-08/01/content_5531915.htm>；Perkin MR 等 (2016). Randomized Trial of Introduction of Allergenic Foods in Breast-Fed Infants. New England Journal of Medicine, 374(18), 1733-1743. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1514210>（备注里那项一般婴儿试验 EAT）
- Notes: **the only acceptable forms are peanut butter thinned down or peanut powder mixed into complementary food — never whole peanuts.** The health authority's core feeding information states it plainly: "whole peanuts, nuts, jelly and similar foods easily enter the windpipe and cause choking; infants and young children should avoid eating them". What to do when someone is choking is covered in Section 13, item 26 (someone choking and unable to speak). This item applies only to high-risk children — those with severe eczema or an existing egg allergy. **Before adding peanuts, you must first see a doctor for an assessment — do not try it at home on your own**: before the LEAP trial enrolled anyone, every child was given a skin test, and children with a skin reaction larger than 4 mm were excluded from the trial and not allowed to eat. The evidence for ordinary children is far weaker. In another trial of 1,162 ordinary breastfed infants, analyzed by assigned group, the early-introduction group had food allergy in 5.6% and the regular-care group 7.1%, a difference that was not significant (P=0.32); it became significant only among the children who actually followed through (2.4% versus 7.3%). This way of analyzing tends to overstate the effect, and the authors themselves said the by-group analysis failed to prove efficacy — but it does show that early introduction is safe. Chinese official feeding documents currently take no position on whether allergenic foods should be introduced early or avoided, so this item is written on international trial evidence. Timing matters too: LEAP started from 4 months of age, while in China complementary foods begin only at 6 months — see item 4 of this section (only breast milk for the first 6 months). When and how to start for a high-risk child, follow the doctor.
