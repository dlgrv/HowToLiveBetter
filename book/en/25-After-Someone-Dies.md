# 25. What needs to be done after a person dies

Scope: money. This section covers only procedures and money: what to do first at the moment a person dies, which costs have a published fee schedule to go by, which money in accounts can be recovered, and which rights are exercised by close relatives. The first five items are ordered by when things happen — the scene, the death certificate, the body and cremation, disputing the cause of death, canceling the household registration — and the items after that are ordered by cost-effectiveness. Arrangements an older person should make while still alive (guardianship by agreement, wills, accounts) are covered in Section 17; the three payments for a death from a work injury are covered in Section 19.

The funeral material follows the newly revised funeral management regulations (殡葬管理条例) in force from March 30, 2026. The standards for the funeral grant and the survivor's pension under the old-age insurance system are set separately by the human resources and social security departments; no original text that could be checked word for word was obtained for this edition, so the body text gives no specific amounts.
### 1. Tell first whether a death at home or elsewhere is normal or unnatural: for an accident, an unknown cause of death, or a death discovered late in someone living alone, call the police immediately and do not move the body or the scene
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan, one phone call
- In plain terms: for an accident, an unknown cause of death, or a death discovered only after someone had been living alone for a long time, only the public security organs can issue the death certificate; once the scene has been disturbed, the case is both hard to explain and hard to certify. Without that one sheet of paper, everything downstream — cremation, canceling the household registration, the insurance claim — gets stuck. If you cannot tell which situation this is, call 110 first; after the police arrive and finish their investigation they can still issue the certificate.
- Benefit: Article 8, paragraph 2 of the household registration regulation (户口登记条例): "Where a citizen dies in an accident or the cause of death is unknown, the head of the household or the person who discovers the death shall immediately report it to the local public security police station or the township or town people's committee"; Article 23 of the funeral management regulations (殡葬管理条例): "Where a natural person dies a normal death, the medical and health institution shall issue a death certificate without delay. For a deceased person in an unnatural-death case (incident) that the public security organs handle according to law and who received no treatment from a medical and health institution, the death certificate is issued by the public security organs." For an unnatural death only the public security organs can issue the certificate; once the scene has been disturbed, the case is both hard to explain and hard to certify, and everything downstream — cremation, canceling the household registration, the insurance claim — gets stuck on that one sheet of paper
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (1958). 中华人民共和国户口登记条例（第八条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html>（广东省人民政府门户网站刊登）；国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，自 2026 年 3 月 30 日起施行，第二十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm>
- Notes: for a normal death such as someone long bedridden or dying at home under care, contact the institution that treated them in life, or the community health service center or township health center for the district, to issue the certificate (see the next item). If you cannot tell which situation this is, treat it as unnatural and call 110 first — after the police arrive and finish their investigation they can still issue the certificate; on the other hand, if you tidy up the scene before reporting, no one can help you. Do not move the body yourself, do not wash or dress it, and do not take away pill bottles or written notes.

### 2. The death certificate is the key to everything that follows: whoever provided treatment issues it, for a normal death at home go to the community health institution, issued within one day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: for a death in a medical institution, the institution responsible for treatment issues the certificate; for a normal death at home or elsewhere, go to the community health institution or township health center for the district; where the cause of death is clear it is issued within one day, and paper and electronic versions carry equal legal effect. Canceling the household registration, cremation, withdrawing the housing provident fund, claiming social insurance benefits, the insurance claim, handling inheritance — every one of these requires it, so as soon as you get it, make several photocopies; if it is lost it can be reissued only once, and if the information on it is wrong it can be re-issued only once.
- Benefit: the standard for registration and management of population death information (trial) (人口死亡信息登记管理规范（试行）): for a death inside a medical and health institution, the certificate is "issued by the medical and health institution responsible for treatment"; for a normal death at home or in another location, it is "issued by the community health institution or township (street) health center for the district"; where the public security or judicial organs determine the death to be unnatural, the public security and judicial departments handle it under current rules and procedures. The 2026 notice from five departments: the death certificate's "paper version and electronic version have the same legal effect," and a normal death with a clear cause should be certified within one day of the death occurring; if the certificate is lost, a reissue may be requested once from the original issuing institution, and where the basic information is wrong, a new issue may be requested once with supporting materials
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家卫生计生委办公厅 (2014). 人口死亡信息登记管理规范（试行）（国卫办规划发〔2014〕68 号，第九条）. <https://mzj.sz.gov.cn/szmz/pc/zwgk/jcxxgk/zcfg/byfw/content/post_2952215.html>（深圳市民政局转发）；国家卫生健康委、公安部、民政部、国家中医药局、国家疾控局 (2026). 关于加强居民死亡医学证明信息登记和电子证照管理工作的通知（国卫规划发〔2026〕5 号）. <https://wjw.fujian.gov.cn/xxgk/fgwj/gjwj/202602/t20260228_7102810.htm>（福建省卫生健康委员会转发）
- Notes: canceling the household registration, cremation, withdrawing the housing provident fund, claiming social insurance benefits, the insurance claim, handling inheritance — every one of these requires it. As soon as you have it, make several photocopies, and apply for the electronic version too where the institution can issue one. There is only one chance at a reissue, so do not lose the original; if the name or ID number is wrong, have it corrected on the spot — if it is only discovered when handling the inheritance, every window will have to be visited all over again.

### 3. Transport, storage, and cremation of the body can only go through the funeral parlor: a hospital morgue does not handle funeral services, and storage generally does not exceed 3 days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: transport, storage, preservation, cosmetic preparation, and cremation can only be done by the funeral parlor; a hospital morgue may not be outsourced and may not hold funeral services, at most it provides temporary placement of the body; funeral parlor storage generally does not exceed 3 days, and an extension requires going through the procedures. Cremation requires the death certificate and the cremation confirmation form, and the cremation certificate issued afterward is still needed for the inheritance, the insurance claim, and moving the ashes. The "one-stop service" people who approach you at the hospital are in most cases not from the funeral parlor.
- Benefit: Article 25 of the funeral management regulations (殡葬管理条例): "transport, storage, preservation, cosmetic preparation, and cremation services for the body are provided exclusively by the funeral parlor"; Article 24: "the morgue of a medical and health institution may not be outsourced and may not conduct funeral services; it may provide convenience services for temporary placement of the body"; Article 27: "a funeral parlor stores a body generally for no more than 3 days; where extended storage is needed, the family members or the party handing over the body shall complete extension procedures at the funeral parlor"; Article 28: "a funeral parlor shall cremate the body upon presentation of the death certificate and the cremation confirmation form, and shall issue a cremation certificate"; Article 29: "in areas where cremation is practiced, the body shall be cremated at or near the place of death, at a funeral parlor"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，自 2026 年 3 月 30 日起施行，第八、九、二十四、二十五、二十七、二十八、二十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm>
- Notes: the "one-stop service" people who approach you after a death in hospital are in most cases not from the funeral parlor; transport itself is a basic service item with a set price (see item 6). Keep the cremation certificate safe — it is needed for the inheritance, for insurance, and for moving the ashes. Cremation areas and burial areas are designated by provincial-level governments, and the funeral customs of ethnic minorities are protected under Article 9 of the regulations; burying instead of cremating where cremation is required, or building a grave for burial outside cemeteries and ecological burial areas, will draw an order from the civil affairs department to correct it within a time limit.

### 4. If you dispute the cause of death, request an autopsy within 48 hours and have the medical records sealed at the same time
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; the hard part is remembering to make the request at that moment
- In plain terms: if you dispute the cause of death, you must request an autopsy within 48 hours of the death; where cold-storage conditions exist it can be extended to 7 days, and the signed consent of close relatives is required. If relatives refuse, or the deadline passes and the determination of the cause of death is affected, the responsibility falls on the party that refused or delayed. Once the body is cremated there is no longer any possibility of an autopsy, so settle this before contacting the funeral parlor.
- Benefit: Article 26 of the regulation on the prevention and handling of medical disputes (医疗纠纷预防和处理条例): "where a patient dies and the medical and the patient's side dispute the cause of death, an autopsy shall be performed within 48 hours of the patient's death; where the body can be kept in frozen storage, this may be extended to 7 days"; "an autopsy requires the consent and signature of the deceased's close relatives; where they refuse to sign, this is treated as the close relatives not consenting to the autopsy"; "where a party refuses or delays the autopsy and, past the prescribed time limit, the determination of the cause of death is affected, the party that refused or delayed bears responsibility"; Article 24: sealing and unsealing of medical records "shall be done with both the medical side and the patient's side present"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2018). 医疗纠纷预防和处理条例（国务院令第 701 号，第二十四、二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>
- Notes: after this window has passed, saying "I felt something was wrong at the time" backfires — the responsibility falls on the party that delayed. Once the body is cremated there is no longer any possibility of an autopsy, so this must be settled before contacting the funeral parlor. How to have the medical records sealed is covered in Section 24.

### 5. Canceling the household registration has a statutory deadline: before burial in cities, within one month in the countryside
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one trip to the police station with the death certificate and the household registration booklet
- In plain terms: before burial in cities, within one month in the countryside, you must take the death certificate and the household registration booklet to the police station to cancel the registration. Putting it off stalls the transfer of inherited property, the claiming of benefits, and the surrender of insurance on the household registration status; if the pension keeps being paid, that counts as fraudulent claiming and will have to be paid back sooner or later.
- Benefit: Article 8, paragraph 1 of the household registration regulation (户口登记条例): "when a citizen dies, the head of the household, a relative, the person providing support, or a neighbor shall report the death to the household registration authority and cancel the registration — before burial in cities, within one month in the countryside. If a citizen dies at their temporary place of residence, the household registration authority there notifies the household registration authority of the place of permanent residence to cancel the registration"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (1958). 中华人民共和国户口登记条例（第八条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html>（广东省人民政府门户网站刊登）
- Notes: putting off the cancellation stalls the transfer of inherited property, the claiming of benefits, and the surrender of insurance on the household registration status; if the pension keeps being paid it constitutes fraudulent claiming and will have to be repaid sooner or later. While at the police station, also ask how to stop the pension and medical insurance benefits and how to apply for the funeral grant — those payments are covered in item 9.

### 6. Funeral services are split into basic and non-basic items; basic items have a published schedule and fees set by law
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; a few minutes to look over the schedule before setting out
- In plain terms: transport, storage of the body, the farewell ceremony, cremation, ash deposit, ecological burial, and niche burial at publicly run institutions are all on the national list of basic items, their fees are set by law, and no extra items may be charged outside the list. Before setting out, ask which items count as basic and what the local standards are, then discuss everything else.
- Benefit: transport of the body, storage of the body, the farewell ceremony, cremation, ash deposit, ecological burial, and niche burial provided by government-run funeral service institutions are all on the national list of basic items, with fee standards set by law; the list of non-basic items is determined by civil affairs departments at the level of cities divided into districts or above
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第五条：「殡葬服务分为基础项目和非基础项目，收费政策由国务院发展改革部门、财政部门会同国务院民政等部门制定。」「国家制定殡葬服务基础项目清单，将遗体接运、遗体存放、遗体告别、遗体火化、骨灰寄存、生态安葬以及政府举办的殡葬服务机构提供的骨灰格位安葬等纳入清单范围」；第五十条「殡葬服务实行清单化管理并动态调整，禁止在清单之外设立项目、收取费用。」
- Notes: provinces, autonomous regions, and directly administered municipalities may add items on top of the national list. First find out which items are basic and what the local fee standards are, then discuss everything else.

### 7. If you run into forced bundling, charges beyond the listed price, or one service split into several billed items, write it down and complain
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; the hard part is being willing to push back at that moment
- In plain terms: not marking prices clearly, adding charges beyond the listed price, tying funeral goods to a purchase or forcing sales through unreasonable conditions, and pushing you to accept paid third-party services are price violations named in the regulations; splitting one item into several billed items or charging beyond the proper scope also counts as a disguised price increase. On the spot, first ask for the price list and keep the receipts and chat records — complaining afterward is still in time. For price issues go to market regulation, for funeral services go to civil affairs.
- Benefit: the regulations list five types of price violations, including not marking prices clearly, charging beyond the listed price, forcing sales through bundling or attaching unreasonable conditions, and forcing acceptance of paid third-party services; in addition, they prohibit "disguised increases in fee standards through forms such as splitting items and charging beyond the proper scope"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第四十二条：「殡葬服务机构不得违反有关规定擅自设立收费项目，或者采取分解项目、扩大范围收费等形式变相提高收费标准。」第四十三条列举的价格违法行为含「（一）不按规定明码标价，或者在标价之外加价或者收取未标明的费用」「（三）采取捆绑、附加不合理条件或者不合理限制等方式，强制或者变相强制销售丧葬用品、提供殡葬相关服务并收取费用」「（四）强制或者变相强制丧属接受第三方的有偿服务，或者利用相关服务网点重复收取费用」；第五十一条要求民政部门与政务服务便民热线建立信息共享监督机制，「对丧属反映的殡葬服务问题，应当会同有关部门及时予以处理并将处理结果告知丧属」
- Notes: investigating and punishing price violations falls to the market regulation department; funeral services themselves fall to the civil affairs department. On the spot, first ask for the price list and keep the receipts and chat records — complaining afterward is still in time.

### 8. Intermediaries such as funeral agents, goods purchasing services, and ceremony planners must register with county-level civil affairs
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one question about whether they are registered
- In plain terms: intermediaries such as funeral agents, goods purchasing services, ceremony planning and officiating, and information consulting must register with the county-level civil affairs department; those that have not registered are not in the funeral service information system. Those who overcharge through deception, push families into lavish ceremonies, force services on people, or traffic in deceased persons' information will be dealt with according to law, flagged in the system, and publicly named. If a stream of sales calls arrives right after you finished the paperwork, in most cases your information was sold.
- Benefit: unregistered intermediaries are not in the funeral service information system; those who charge improperly by deception, induce lavish ceremonies, force services on people, or traffic in deceased persons' information will be dealt with according to law, flagged in the system, and made public
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第四十一条：「除殡葬服务机构外，从事殡葬服务代理、用品代购、策划主持、信息咨询等殡葬相关服务活动的组织和个人，应当向县级人民政府民政部门备案。」「对存在以欺骗等手段违规收费、诱导大操大办、强迫接受服务、倒卖逝者信息等损害丧属权益行为的依法处置，并在殡葬服务信息系统中进行标注，向社会公开。」
- Notes: "trafficking in deceased persons' information" is the most common trap in this trade: a stream of sales calls right after you finished the paperwork usually means the information was resold. Online memorial platforms are covered by the same constraints; Article 45 of the regulations prohibits inducing consumption and unlawful handling of deceased persons' information.

### 9. Money scattered in different places must be claimed one by one: housing provident fund balance, social insurance benefits, work injury benefits
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: trips to several windows, with the death certificate and proof of kinship prepared
- In plain terms: the balance in a housing provident fund account can be withdrawn by heirs and legatees — this money is not in a bank account and does not automatically enter the division of the estate, so it is the easiest to miss. A death from a work injury carries three separate payments: the funeral grant, the dependents' pension, and the one-time death benefit. For the balance of the old-age insurance individual account and survivor benefits, ask the social insurance agency where the person was insured; the required documents differ from place to place.
- Benefit: the balance in a housing provident fund account may be withdrawn by heirs and legatees; a death from a work injury carries three payments — the funeral grant, the dependents' pension, and the one-time death benefit
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 住房公积金管理条例（国务院令第 844 号修改，2026 年 9 月 20 日起施行）第二十四条末款. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12946/202608/content_7079363.html>：「职工死亡或者被宣告死亡的，职工的继承人、受遗赠人可以提取职工住房公积金账户内的存储余额；无继承人也无受遗赠人的，职工住房公积金账户内的存储余额纳入住房公积金的增值收益。」；工伤保险条例第三十九条的三笔待遇见第 19 节. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>；TODO（待核实：基本养老保险个人账户余额的继承规则，以及丧葬补助金与遗属抚恤金的具体标准，人社部相关文件本次未取得）
- Notes: the housing provident fund money is often forgotten, because it is not in a bank account and does not automatically enter the division of the estate. For the balance of the old-age insurance individual account and survivor benefits, ask the social insurance agency where the person was insured; document lists differ from place to place. The formal requirements for wills and inheritance are covered in Section 17.

### 10. For the deceased's accounts and personal information, close relatives may demand to inspect, copy, correct, and delete
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; a complaint or a lawsuit is needed only if the platform does not cooperate
- In plain terms: after a person dies, close relatives may, for their own lawful and legitimate interests, demand that platforms let them inspect, copy, correct, and delete the deceased's personal information; if the platform refuses it must give reasons, and the relatives can also sue in court. The exception is where the deceased made other arrangements in life — if an account heir was designated or clear wishes were left, those take precedence.
- Benefit: the law states expressly: where a natural person dies, close relatives may, for their own lawful and legitimate interests, exercise rights such as inspecting, copying, correcting, and deleting the deceased's relevant personal information
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 个人信息保护法. 中国人大网. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202108/t20210820_313088.html> 第四十九条：「自然人死亡的，其近亲属为了自身的合法、正当利益，可以对死者的相关个人信息行使本章规定的查阅、复制、更正、删除等权利；死者生前另有安排的除外。」；第五十条「个人信息处理者应当建立便捷的个人行使权利的申请受理和处理机制。拒绝个人行使权利的请求的，应当说明理由」「个人可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼」
- Notes: "except where the deceased made other arrangements in life" — if an account heir was designated or clear wishes were recorded, those take precedence. This is also why it is worth writing down, while still of sound mind, where the accounts and passwords are kept; see Section 17. Closing an account and deleting personal information are two different matters; see Section 14.
