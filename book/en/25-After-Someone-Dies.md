# 25. What to do after someone dies

Scope: money. This section only covers how to get the procedures done and how to collect the money. That includes what to do first right after the person dies, and which fees have a list you can check. It also covers which money sitting in which accounts can be taken back, and which matters close relatives have the right to handle in person. The first 5 items follow the order in which things happen: the scene, the death certificate, the body and cremation, what to do if you disagree with the cause of death, and canceling the hukou (户口 — household registration). Arrangements an older person should make while still alive, such as intended guardianship (意定监护 — a guardian named by agreement in advance), a will, and accounts, are in Section 17. The 3 benefits for a work-related death are in Section 19.

The funeral part follows the new Funeral Management Regulations, in force from March (3) 30, 2026. How the funeral allowance and the survivor allowance under the pension are calculated is in item 9 (housing provident fund balance and social insurance benefits).
### 1. If someone dies at home or outside, first tell a natural death from an unnatural one: for an accident, an unknown cause, or a death found only after the person lived alone, call the police at once and do not move the body or the scene
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs nothing. 1 phone call is enough.
- In plain terms: For a death by accident, a death nobody can explain, or a death found only after a long time living alone, if there was no treatment at a hospital, the police issue the death certificate. If the scene has been moved, what happened cannot be explained, and the certificate is hard to get. Without that piece of paper, cremation, canceling the hukou (户口 — household registration), and the insurance claim all get stuck. If you cannot tell which kind of death it is, call 110 first. After the police finish checking, they still issue the certificate.
- Benefit: Article 8, paragraph 2 of the Regulations on Household Registration: "Where a citizen dies in an accident or the cause of death is unknown, the head of the household or the person who discovers it shall immediately report to the local police station or the people's committee of the township or town." That means: if there was an accident, or nobody knows how the person died, the head of the household and the person who found them must report at once to the police station or the township or town government. Article 23 of the Funeral Management Regulations: "Where a natural person dies a natural death, the medical institution shall issue a death certificate promptly. For a deceased person in an unnatural-death case handled by the public security organs according to law, who was not treated by a medical institution, the public security organs issue the death certificate." That means: a natural death from illness is certified by the medical institution that treated him. A death with no hospital treatment, which the police are handling, is certified by the police. If the scene has been disturbed, you can neither explain it nor get the certificate easily. Cremation, canceling the hukou, and the insurance claim all wait on this one piece of paper.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (1958). 中华人民共和国户口登记条例（第八条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html>（广东省人民政府门户网站刊登）；国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，自 2026 年 3 月 30 日起施行，第二十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm>
- Notes: A death after a long illness, or dying at home at the end of life, counts as a natural death. You can ask the institution that treated him while he was alive to issue the certificate, or the community health service center or township health center for the area where he lived. Who issues the death certificate, the one that provided the treatment, is in item 2 (death certificate). If you cannot tell which kind it is, treat it as unnatural and call 110 first. After the police arrive and finish the investigation, they can still issue the certificate. If you tidy the scene before calling the police, no one can help you. Do not move the body yourself, wash him, or change his clothes, and do not take away medicine bottles or notes.

### 2. The death certificate is the key to everything that follows: whoever provided treatment issues it; for natural deaths at home, contact the local community health service center. It must be issued within one day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->

- Cost: No cost at all.  
- In plain terms: If the person died in a hospital, get the death certificate from the place that handled the treatment. For a natural death at home, get it from the community health service for that area, or from the township health center. When the death is natural and the cause is clear, it is issued within one day. Canceling the hukou (户口 — household registration), cremation, withdrawing the housing provident fund, collecting social insurance benefits, an insurance claim, and handling the inheritance all use it. As soon as you have it, make several copies.  
- Benefit: According to the “Interim Regulations on Registration and Management of Population Death Information,” death certificates for patients who die in medical institutions are issued by those institutions; for deaths occurring at home or elsewhere, they are issued by the local community health service center or township health clinic. Cases deemed non‑natural by police or judicial authorities are handled under separate procedures. In 2026, five government agencies confirmed that electronic death certificates carry the same legal weight as paper versions; both are equally valid. Certificates for natural deaths with known causes must be issued within one day. If lost, a single replacement can be requested from the original issuer. Errors in basic information may be corrected by submitting supporting documents.  
- Evidence grade: A  
- Sources:国家卫生计生委办公厅 (2014). 人口死亡信息登记管理规范（试行）（国卫办规划发〔2014〕68 号，第九条）. <https://mzj.sz.gov.cn/szmz/pc/zwgk/jcxxgk/zcfg/byfw/content/post_2952215.html>（深圳市民政局转发）；国家卫生健康委、公安部、民政部、国家中医药局、国家疾控局 (2026). 关于加强居民死亡医学证明信息登记和电子证照管理工作的通知（国卫规划发〔2026〕5 号）. <https://wjw.fujian.gov.cn/xxgk/fgwj/gjwj/202602/t20260228_7102810.htm>（福建省卫生健康委员会转发）
- Notes: This certificate is indispensable for deregistration, cremation, housing provident fund withdrawals, social insurance benefits, insurance claims, and estate settlement — so make plenty of copies right away. If electronic issuance is available, request it as well. Only one replacement is permitted, so keep the original safe. Any mistakes in name or ID number should be corrected on the spot; otherwise you’ll have to repeat the process at every office later on.

### 3. Transport, storage, and cremation of remains must be handled by funeral homes; hospital morgues cannot provide such services, and storage is generally limited to 3 days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost involved.
- In plain terms: Transport, storage, embalming, cosmetic preparation, and cremation can only be done by a funeral home. A hospital morgue does not handle the funeral. At most, it lets the body stay there for a short while. A funeral home generally keeps the body for no more than 3 days. If you need more days, you have to apply for an extension. Cremation takes a death certificate and a cremation confirmation document. The cremation certificate they give you afterward is still needed for the inheritance and for an insurance claim.
- Benefit: Article 25 of the Funeral Management Regulations states: “Services such as transport, storage, embalming, body preparation, and cremation must be provided exclusively by funeral homes.” No other entities are permitted to offer these services. Article 24 adds: “Morgues operated by medical institutions may not subcontract or provide funeral services; they may only offer temporary storage for remains.” Hospital morgues are thus restricted to short-term storage only. Article 27 specifies: “Funeral homes may store remains for no more than 3 days; extensions must be formally requested by the family or body transferor.” Article 28 requires cremation to be performed only upon presentation of a death certificate and cremation authorization document, after which a cremation certificate is issued. Article 29 further mandates that in regions where cremation is mandatory, remains must be cremated at a nearby funeral home.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，自 2026 年 3 月 30 日起施行，第八、九、二十四、二十五、二十七、二十八、二十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm>
- Notes: Most “one-stop” services offered at hospitals are not affiliated with licensed funeral homes. Transport itself is a standard service with fixed pricing; see Section 6 for details. Keep the cremation certificate safe — it is required for inheritance, insurance, and other legal procedures. Whether cremation or burial is mandated locally is determined by provincial authorities. Article 9 safeguards the funeral customs of ethnic minorities. Any instance of cremation being required yet burial performed, or burial outside designated cemeteries or ecological burial zones, will prompt civil affairs bureaus to order immediate correction.

### 4. Disagreement over cause of death: request an autopsy within 48 hours and have medical records sealed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost involved. The hard part is remembering to make this request at such a time.
- In plain terms: If you doubt the cause of death, the autopsy has to be done within 48 hours after the person dies. If the hospital can freeze the body, that can be extended to 7 days. The autopsy needs a close relative's signed consent. If they do not consent, or the time is missed, the responsibility for a cause of death that cannot be sorted out falls on that side. When you ask for the autopsy, also ask to have the medical records sealed. Once the body is cremated, an autopsy can no longer be done.
- Benefit: The time limit for autopsies is stipulated in Article 26 of the Regulations on Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes. The original text reads: “In cases where a patient dies and both the patient and medical institution dispute the cause of death, an autopsy must be performed within 48 hours after death; if the hospital has facilities to freeze the body, this period may be extended to 7 days.” In other words, to determine the true cause of death, an autopsy must be carried out within 48 hours of death, or up to 7 days if the hospital has freezing capabilities. The same article further states: “An autopsy must be approved and signed off by the deceased’s close relatives; failure to sign is regarded as refusal of consent.” If family members do not sign, it is interpreted as a refusal. It also specifies: “If consent is denied or the deadline is missed, leading to uncertainty over the cause of death, the party responsible for the refusal or delay bears all related liability.” Article 24 states that sealing and unsealing of medical records “must be conducted in the presence of both the patient and medical institution.” Both you and the hospital must be present during both the sealing and later unsealing of records.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2018). 医疗纠纷预防和处理条例（国务院令第 701 号，第二十四、二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>
- Notes: After this deadline passes, any later claims of “I always suspected something was wrong” will instead place liability on the party responsible for the delay. Once a body is cremated, no autopsy can ever be performed. Therefore, this decision must be made before contacting any funeral home. Details on how to seal medical records can be found in Section 24.

### 5. Legal deadlines for canceling hukou registration for the deceased: in cities, before burial; in rural areas, within one month
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No expense involved. Just bring the death certificate and household register to your local police station.
- In plain terms: In cities, you must cancel hukou registration before burial; in rural areas, you must do it within one month. Bring the death certificate and household register to the police station to complete the process. Failing to do so will block any inheritance transfers, benefit claims, or insurance cancellations due to the unresolved hukou status.
- Benefit: Article 8, Paragraph 1 of the Hukou Registration Regulations states: “When a citizen dies, in cities the household head, relatives, guardians, or neighbors must report the death to the hukou registration authority and cancel the hukou registration before burial; in rural areas, this must be done within one month. If a citizen dies while temporarily residing elsewhere, the local hukou registration authority must notify the permanent residence’s authority to cancel the registration.” In short, urban residents must act before burial, while rural residents have one month to do so. Any household head, relative, guardian, or neighbor may initiate the process at the police station, which serves as the hukou registration authority. If the deceased was living temporarily elsewhere, the local police station there must inform the permanent residence’s station to cancel the hukou registration.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (1958). 中华人民共和国户口登记条例（第八条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html>（广东省人民政府门户网站刊登）
- Notes: Leaving the hukou registration active will prevent any inheritance transfers, benefit claims, or insurance cancellations, as all these procedures rely on a cleared hukou status. If pension payments continue despite the death, they constitute fraudulent collection and must eventually be repaid. While at the police station, also ask how to suspend social insurance and yibao payments, as well as how to apply for funeral subsidies. Details on these financial matters are covered in Item 9 (housing provident fund balances and related social insurance benefits).

### 6. Funeral services are divided into basic and non-basic categories; a list of basic services exists, with their fees set by law
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: There is no cost involved. Just spend a few minutes before departure to review the official list.
- In plain terms: Transporting the body, storing it, the farewell, cremation, storing the ashes, and ecological burial are all on the national list of basic items. Burial in an ash niche provided by a public institution is on that list too. The prices of these items are set by the rules. Outside the list, they are not allowed to invent extra items and charge for them. Before you set out, first ask which items count as basic and what the local standard is, then talk about the rest.
- Benefit: All essential funeral services — including body transport, storage, cremation, memorial services, and eco-friendly burial — are included in the official list. Government-run funeral centers also offer designated burial plots under this same list. Their pricing follows legal standards, meaning funeral homes cannot set prices arbitrarily. Non-basic services are defined separately by civil affairs bureaus at or above the prefecture level.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第五条：「殡葬服务分为基础项目和非基础项目，收费政策由国务院发展改革部门、财政部门会同国务院民政等部门制定。」「国家制定殡葬服务基础项目清单，将遗体接运、遗体存放、遗体告别、遗体火化、骨灰寄存、生态安葬以及政府举办的殡葬服务机构提供的骨灰格位安葬等纳入清单范围」；第五十条「殡葬服务实行清单化管理并动态调整，禁止在清单之外设立项目、收取费用。」
- Notes: Provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities may add extra items to the national list. Always verify which services are classified as basic and what their local pricing is before proceeding. It is perfectly acceptable to want a dignified funeral for a loved one; this guideline does not discourage that. Its sole purpose is to clarify which services are fixed-price basics and which are optional extras. Once this distinction is clear, you can decide where to allocate funds — and avoid unnecessary overcharges during an already difficult time.

### 7. If you encounter forced bundling, extra charges beyond the listed price, or a single service being split into multiple line items for billing, make a note of it and file a complaint
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: No cost involved. The real challenge is staying diligent in the moment.

- In plain terms: The price violations the regulations name are: not posting a price list, adding money on top of the marked price, bundling funeral goods and forcing you to buy them, and forcing you to accept another provider's paid service. Splitting one item into several charges also counts as a disguised price rise. On the spot, ask for the price list first, and keep the proof of the charges and the chat records. Complaining afterward is still in time. For the price, go to the market supervision administration. For funeral services, go to the civil affairs bureau.

- Benefit: Regulations identify five categories of price violations. Four of them are: not displaying prices clearly or adding extra charges beyond the listed price — that is, no posted price list or charging more than advertised; bundling services or imposing unreasonable conditions to force a purchase — meaning you can’t obtain a service unless you buy another one; forcing or indirectly forcing you to use paid third‑party services — essentially pushing another provider’s services on you; and indirectly raising prices by re‑labeling charges — such as charging more under a different name. Additionally, it is prohibited to “split services into multiple items or expand the scope of charges to indirectly raise prices.” This means breaking one service into several line items for billing or extending the range of charges beyond what was originally stated.

- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第四十二条：「殡葬服务机构不得违反有关规定擅自设立收费项目，或者采取分解项目、扩大范围收费等形式变相提高收费标准。」第四十三条列举的价格违法行为含「（一）不按规定明码标价，或者在标价之外加价或者收取未标明的费用」「（三）采取捆绑、附加不合理条件或者不合理限制等方式，强制或者变相强制销售丧葬用品、提供殡葬相关服务并收取费用」「（四）强制或者变相强制丧属接受第三方的有偿服务，或者利用相关服务网点重复收取费用」；第五十一条要求民政部门与政务服务便民热线建立信息共享监督机制，「对丧属反映的殡葬服务问题，应当会同有关部门及时予以处理并将处理结果告知丧属」

- Notes: Price‑violation investigations fall under the market regulation authority; funeral services themselves are under the civil affairs bureau. Ask for a price list right away, and be sure to keep all receipts, invoices, and chat records. Filing a complaint later is still possible.

### 8. Funeral agents, buyers of funeral goods, and planners and hosts must register with the county-level civil affairs bureau
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs nothing. Ask whether they are registered.
- In plain terms: Funeral agents, buyers of funeral goods, planners and hosts, and information consultants have to register with the county-level civil affairs bureau. Those that are not registered are not in the funeral-service information system run by the civil affairs bureau. Whether you can look this up yourself differs from place to place. The surest way is to call the county-level civil affairs bureau and ask. Those who overcharge by trickery, or who resell information about the deceased, will be dealt with according to law.
- Benefit: An intermediary that is not registered is not in the funeral-service information system run by the civil affairs bureau. Those who charge in violation of the rules by deception, push a family into a large showy funeral, force people to accept a service, or resell information about the deceased will be dealt with according to law. They are also marked in the system and made public, and anyone can see it.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第四十一条：「除殡葬服务机构外，从事殡葬服务代理、用品代购、策划主持、信息咨询等殡葬相关服务活动的组织和个人，应当向县级人民政府民政部门备案。」「对存在以欺骗等手段违规收费、诱导大操大办、强迫接受服务、倒卖逝者信息等损害丧属权益行为的依法处置，并在殡葬服务信息系统中进行标注，向社会公开。」
- Notes: Online tomb-sweeping platforms are regulated the same way. Article 45 of the regulations does not allow them to induce spending, and does not allow them to handle the deceased's information unlawfully.

### 9. Collect the money held in different places, one place at a time: the housing provident fund balance, social insurance benefits, and work-injury benefits
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs nothing, but you have to go to several windows. Before you go, get the death certificate and proof of kinship ready.
- In plain terms: Heirs can withdraw the housing provident fund (公积金 — mandatory housing savings) balance. It is not in a bank account, so it is easy to miss. Money left in the individual account of the basic pension can also be inherited. If a person covered by the enterprise employees' pension dies of illness, the family also receives a funeral allowance and a survivor allowance for 3 to 24 months. If the death was caused by work, there are 3 more work-injury payments.
- Benefit: The balance of the housing provident fund account (the money still left in his provident-fund account) can be withdrawn by heirs and by legatees. A legatee is the person named in a will to receive something. If the death was caused by work, there are 3 more payments: a funeral allowance, a dependents' pension, and a one-time work-death benefit. Money left in the individual account of the basic pension can be inherited; the Social Insurance Law says so in writing. If a person covered by the employees' pension dies of illness, or dies from a cause that is not work, the family can also receive a funeral allowance and a survivor allowance, together called survivor benefits. The standard for enterprise employees is calculated on a nationwide formula. The funeral allowance is 2 times the previous year's monthly per capita disposable income of urban residents in that province. The survivor allowance uses that same monthly per capita income as the base, and is paid for 3 to 24 months according to the years of contributions. Under 5 years, 3 months are paid. For 5 to 10 years, 6 months are paid. For 10 to 15 years, 9 months are paid. For more than 15 years, each extra 1 year adds 1 month, up to 24 months. If the person had already retired, each 1 year of pension already received subtracts 1 month, and the minimum is 9 months.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2026). 住房公积金管理条例（国务院令第 844 号修改，2026 年 9 月 20 日起施行）第二十四条末款. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12946/202608/content_7079363.html>：「职工死亡或者被宣告死亡的，职工的继承人、受遗赠人可以提取职工住房公积金账户内的存储余额；无继承人也无受遗赠人的，职工住房公积金账户内的存储余额纳入住房公积金的增值收益。」；工伤保险条例第三十九条的三笔待遇见第 19 节. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2018 修正). 社会保险法（第十四、十七条）：「个人死亡的，个人账户余额可以继承」「参加基本养老保险的个人，因病或者非因工死亡的，其遗属可以领取丧葬补助金和抚恤金」. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8080816f135f46016f210989b9179a>（国家法律法规数据库）；人力资源社会保障部、财政部 (2021). 企业职工基本养老保险遗属待遇暂行办法（人社部发〔2021〕18 号，2021 年 9 月 1 日起施行，第四、五条），原件未公开挂网，条文按广州市花都区人民政府行政复议决定书（花都府行复〔2022〕226 号）的逐字引用核对：「丧葬补助金的标准，按照参保人员死亡时本省……上一年度城镇居民月人均可支配收入的2倍计算」「缴费年限不满5年的，发放月数为3个月……缴费年限30年以上的，按照30年计算，发放月数最高为24个月」. <https://www.huadu.gov.cn/xxgk/xzfy/content/post_8672730.html>；福建省人力资源和社会保障厅. 常见问题：「每领取1年基本养老金减少1个月，发放月数最低为9个月」「累计缴费年限不足5年的，其遗属待遇（含丧葬补助金和抚恤金）标准不得超过其个人缴费之和」. <http://rst.fujian.gov.cn/wz/cjwt/czzgly/202311/t20231113_6294929.htm>
- Notes: The provident-fund sum is the one most often forgotten, because it is not in a bank account and it does not go into the division of the estate on its own. Money left in the individual pension account and the survivor benefits are calculated separately and paid separately. Handle them together at the social insurance window where he was insured. The documents each place asks for are different. If contributions were under 5 years, the survivor benefits together do not exceed the money he himself paid in. This formula comes from the rules for the enterprise employees' pension. For survivor benefits under the pension for government bodies and public institutions, and under the urban and rural residents' pension, ask at the window. If the funeral expenses and the survivor allowance were paid into his bank account, how to withdraw them is in item 12 (withdraw the deposit directly at the bank). The formal requirements for a will and for inheritance are in Section 17. If you plan to renounce the inheritance to stay clear of the debt, do not withdraw this money first. See item 11 (a written statement renouncing the inheritance).

### 10. Close relatives can ask to access, copy, correct, and delete the deceased's account and personal information
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs nothing. Only if the platform will not cooperate do you need to complain or to sue.
- In plain terms: After the person has died, close relatives, for their own lawful and proper interests, can ask the platform to access, copy, correct, or delete the deceased's personal information. If the platform refuses, it has to give a reason. You can also sue in court. There is one exception. If he made other arrangements while alive, for example by naming someone to inherit an account or by leaving a clear wish, follow his arrangements.
- Benefit: The law states it in so many words: when a natural person has died, close relatives, for their own lawful and proper interests, may exercise the rights to access, copy, correct, delete, and otherwise deal with the deceased's relevant personal information. Close relatives can ask the platform to bring his information out to be looked at, to make 1 copy, to correct an error, and to delete it. Article 50 requires the platform to set up a convenient mechanism for accepting and handling applications. "If it refuses a person's request to exercise a right, it shall state the reasons." If the platform refuses, "the individual may bring a lawsuit in a people's court according to law."

So while he is still clear-headed, it is worth writing down where the accounts and the passwords are to go. See Section 17. Closing an account and deleting personal information are 2 different things. See Section 14.

### 11. Repay debts a relative left only with the estate you inherit; if you do not want the debt, renounce the inheritance in writing before the estate is divided
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Writing 1 written statement costs nothing. The real price is that the house, the deposits, and the rest of the estate are given up along with it. If you accept the inheritance, a creditor may sue you. You have to answer the suit, and you have to produce materials showing what the estate is worth.
- In plain terms: When a person dies, the debts they owed do not disappear with them. But heirs such as the children and the spouse repay only with the share of the estate they inherited. They do not pay the shortfall out of their own pocket. If you do not want to take on this debt, write a statement renouncing the inheritance before the estate is divided, and you give up the estate as well. If you say nothing, that counts as accepting the inheritance.
- Benefit: Article 1161 of the Civil Code: "An heir clears the taxes and debts the decedent legally owed, limited to the actual value of the estate obtained. This limit does not cover any part beyond the actual value of the estate that the heir repays voluntarily." That means: for the taxes and debts he owed, you pay only with the share of the estate you inherited. You do not pay the part beyond that, unless you yourself are willing to pay. Paragraph 2 of the same article: "An heir who renounces the inheritance may bear no duty to clear the taxes and debts the decedent legally owed." Article 1159 of the Civil Code says that, when the estate is divided, the taxes and debts he owed are paid first. Article 1124 of the Civil Code: a person who renounces an inheritance must "make a statement of renunciation in writing before the estate is handled; if no statement is made, the heir is deemed to accept the inheritance." Article 33 of the Supreme People's Court's Interpretation (1) on the Inheritance Book: the written statement is given to the estate administrator or to the other heirs. Article 35 of that interpretation: if the renunciation is made only after the estate has been divided, what is renounced is not the right of inheritance but property already in hand. Article 1145 of the Civil Code: the heirs may choose an estate administrator, and if none is chosen they serve together. Article 1147 of the Civil Code: the estate administrator must take stock of the estate, make an estate list, and deal with the decedent's claims and debts. In a case in Yuzhou, Henan, the borrower died of illness in March (3) 2023, and the children had not renounced the inheritance in writing. In May (5) 2023 the court ordered the children to repay 95000 yuan of principal and interest, within the actual value of the estate they inherited. The children said the value of the company they inherited could not be calculated, and the judgment could not be enforced. Accounts and a car in the children's names were frozen and seized, and they were placed on the list that restricts high consumption. Only in February (2) 2025 was there a settlement, and they paid 90000 yuan. In a Chongqing case, the borrower died of illness before the principal and interest on a loan of 400000 yuan were paid off, and every heir stated that they renounced the inheritance. But his spouse and daughter had already withdrawn the basic pension and the occupational annuity that counted as his estate. The procuratorate found that the 2 of them had not expressly renounced, so they were treated as having accepted the inheritance. In November (11) 2025 the spouse settled with the creditor and paid the debt in full. Debts of this kind run from 10000 yuan to several hundred thousand yuan, so the benefit is rated large.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大 (2020). 中华人民共和国民法典（第一千零六十四、一千一百二十四、一千一百四十五、一千一百四十七、一千一百五十三、一千一百五十九、一千一百六十一条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》继承编的解释（一）（法释〔2020〕23 号，第三十二、三十三、三十五条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282091.html>；全国人大常委会 (2023 修正). 民事诉讼法（第一百五十二、一百六十四条）. <https://fgw.sh.gov.cn/ys-syjf-zc-2.4.1-h5/20240408/073febdb2b04464390546232d4089f51.html>（上海市发展改革委转载）；国务院 (2006). 诉讼费用交纳办法（国务院令第 481 号，第六、二十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6929.htm>；宋文涛（河南省禹州市人民检察院）(2025-05-23). 典亮生活 守护美好｜借款人突然去世，欠下的债不是「无头账」. 检察日报，最高人民检察院网站刊登. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202505/t20250523_696425.shtml>；重庆市人民检察院 (2026-05-23). 【案说民法典】邓川：继承遗产，也须以所得遗产为限清偿债务. <http://www.cq.jcy.gov.cn/jcdt/202605/t20260523_7740396.shtml>
- Notes: The people this mainly benefits are you yourself, and your spouse and children. The spouse is counted separately. A loan the 2 of them signed together, or a loan taken for ordinary household spending, counts as a joint debt of the couple. The spouse is then personally the debtor, and renouncing the inheritance does not get out of it. When the estate is divided, half of the couple's joint property goes to the spouse first, and only what remains is the estate. If you plan to renounce, do not withdraw money from his accounts first. The housing provident fund balance and the balance of the individual basic-pension account named in item 9 of this section (housing provident fund balance and social insurance benefits) are money only an heir can withdraw. The Chongqing case was a withdrawal followed by a claim of renunciation. If renouncing the inheritance would leave you unable to meet a legal duty such as supporting a parent or raising a child, the renunciation is void. If collectors come to the door, first ask for the IOU, the contract, and the other proof, and have them go to the estate administrator or sue. Do not sign a promise to repay the part that is beyond the estate, because a repayment you are willing to make yourself is also recognized by the law. If you are actually sued, an ordinary first-instance procedure has to be finished within 6 months after the case is filed, and it can be extended. Summary procedure is 3 months. Lawyer fees are not among the litigation costs borne by the losing side. If you hire a lawyer, you pay. Keep the estate list and the materials showing what the house and the deposits are worth. Enforcement uses them to calculate how much you must repay. Why paying a debt by dying does not work is in Section 29, item 13 (do not treat death as a way to pay a debt).

### 12. After a family member dies, do not start the inheritance at a notary by default: withdraw a deposit of 50000 yuan or less at 1 bank directly from the bank, and handle a house at the real-estate registration office with all the heirs together
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs nothing. Go to each bank that holds a deposit, one bank after another. For a house, every heir has to be at the registration window on the same day. If someone will not come, or someone does not agree on the division, this path does not work, and the only routes left are a notary or a lawsuit.
- In plain terms: If the deceased's deposits at 1 bank add up to no more than 50000 yuan, the spouse, the children, and the parents can withdraw them with the documents, and they do not need a notary. A house does not have to go through a notary either. All the heirs go together to the real-estate registration office and can get it done there. A notary costs money. In Fuzhou and Xiamen, for example, a house is charged by floor area, and 1 unit is in principle charged at most 10000 yuan.
- Benefit: On the deposit side, Document No. 18 of 2021 from the China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission and the People's Bank of China provides that a small deposit that meets the conditions no longer requires notarization. There are 3 conditions. Condition 1: the balances of the accounts at the same bank add up to no more than 10000 yuan. A bank may raise that on its own, up to 50000 yuan. Condition 2: the applicant is the spouse, a child, or a parent, or an heir or a legatee named in a notarized will. Condition 3: the money is taken out all at once, and the account is closed afterward. The spouse, the children, and the parents submit 4 kinds of documents: a death certificate; kinship papers such as the household register (户口簿 — the hukou booklet), a marriage certificate, or a birth certificate; their own identity document; and a commitment letter signed in their own hand. Jin Gui [2024] No. 6 (金规〔2024〕6号) provides that, from June (6) 1, 2024, the limit is raised across the board to 50000 yuan. Small and medium-sized rural banks may wait until December (12) 31, 2025 to put it in place. Accumulated-gold products, and treasury bonds and wealth-management products sold through the bank, can be withdrawn the same way once they mature or are redeemed, and the amounts count together toward the 50000 yuan. Funeral expenses and a survivor allowance paid into his account do not count toward the 50000 yuan, and the spouse, the children, and the parents can withdraw them with this same set of documents. On the house side, Article 14 of the detailed implementing rules for the Interim Regulations on Real Estate Registration says that an application to register an inheritance or a bequest "shall submit materials on the death, a will or an agreement of all statutory heirs on how the real estate is divided, and materials on kinship with the decedent, and may also submit notarized materials or a legal document that has taken effect." Notarization is only one option. In 2016 the Ministry of Justice issued a document abolishing the 1991 joint notice that had required stronger notarization for real-estate registration. The Fuzhou Real Estate Registration and Transaction Center has explained what to do when notarized materials are not submitted. All the statutory heirs, or all the legatees, must go together to the registration office to have the materials checked, and sign an undertaking (a written statement that the materials are true). The fee follows the Fujian standard. For an inheritance notarization that does not involve real estate, the part within 200000 yuan is charged at 0.8%, and the minimum is 300 yuan. Real estate is charged by floor area. In Fuzhou and Xiamen the charge is no more than 70 yuan per square meter, and 1 unit is in principle no more than 10000 yuan. On that standard, the notarization fee for a deposit of 50000 yuan is 400 yuan, and a house of 90 square meters in Fuzhou is at most 6300 yuan. The saving is several hundred to several thousand yuan, so the benefit is rated medium.
