# 22. How to relax: entertainment venues and stress relief

The first half of this chapter covers the money pits and safe exits of entertainment venues, measured in money and personal freedom; the second half covers stress-relief methods, measured in energy and all-cause mortality. Numbers in the two halves are not converted against each other. Section 3 already covered which few things to do first when you feel low; what follows here is a more specific prescription.
### 1. Check where the emergency exits are before entering KTV, bars, or escape rooms; switch venues if they are locked or blocked
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; 10 seconds of looking up when you walk in
- In plain terms: By regulation, entertainment venues must keep evacuation routes and emergency exits clear while open — no sealing or locking them, no obstacles, no covering the signs. Internet cafes likewise may not seal doors or lock windows. If a venue fails to do this it is breaking the law and can be reported. If a private room is underground, has only one exit, or the corridors are piled with clutter, pick another place — in a fire these venues fill with thick smoke, narrow passages, crowded people, most of them drunk.
- Benefit: This is a legal obligation of the operator, which means failing to comply is illegal and reportable: "While in operation, entertainment venues shall keep evacuation routes and emergency exits clear, shall not seal off or lock evacuation routes and emergency exits, and shall not set up fences or other obstacles impeding evacuation on evacuation routes and at emergency exits," and "shall set up clearly visible direction signs on evacuation routes and at emergency exits and shall not obscure or cover the signs." Internet cafes are likewise required to "prohibit sealing or locking doors and windows, evacuation routes and emergency exits while in operation" and to "prohibit open-flame lighting and smoking"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2006). 娱乐场所管理条例（国令第 458 号，第二十、二十一条）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6297.htm>；国务院 (2002). 互联网上网服务营业场所管理条例（国令第 363 号，第二十一条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2002/content_61788.htm>
- Notes: Underground private room, single exit, cluttered corridors, unlit exit signs — if a venue has any one of these, go elsewhere. Fires in such places involve thick smoke, narrow corridors, crowds, and alcohol, with escape time measured in seconds. Under the regulations the report hotline should be posted on the warning signs inside the venue

### 2. Ask for the price list before ordering; entertainment venues must post clear prices and "play first, settle later" is not acceptable
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one request for the list
- In plain terms: By regulation, entertainment venues selling services and goods must post clear prices and show the price list on request; forcing or deceiving customers into spending is not allowed. Almost all "sky-high bar tab" disputes happen where nobody saw the price list and the bill is settled afterwards on verbal quotes. Ask for the list and photograph it when you enter the room, and get an itemized confirmation after ordering; if there is a dispute at checkout, call 12315 on the spot — recovering your money after paying is far harder.
- Benefit: "When providing entertainment services and selling goods, entertainment venues shall clearly mark prices and present a price list to consumers; they shall not force or deceive consumers into accepting services or purchasing goods." Violations of price laws and regulations are punished by the relevant departments according to law. The vast majority of "sky-high bar tab" disputes occur where no price list was shown and the bill was settled afterwards on verbal quotes
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2006). 娱乐场所管理条例（国令第 458 号，第二十九、五十三条）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6297.htm>
- Notes: How to apply it: ask for a paper or electronic price list when you enter the room and photograph it, get a confirmed itemized list after ordering, and ask the price of any "special cocktail" or "fruit platter" the server recommends before ordering it. If checkout is disputed, call 12315 on the spot — do not pay first and seek redress afterwards; once you have paid, the burden of proof becomes much harder

### 3. Leave immediately if anyone offers you "something" in a venue; hosting and supplying are not "helping a friend"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; walking out
- In plain terms: Entertainment venues are banned from selling or providing drugs and from hosting others' drug use; violations bring confiscation of illegal gains and suspension of business for 3 to 6 months, with license revocation in serious cases. The personal side is heavier: using drugs yourself brings a public-security penalty, and letting someone "have a go" in a room you booked or at your place is the criminal offense of harboring another person's drug use. If someone takes out unknown powder, pills, or a vape cartridge and asks you to try, walk out on the spot.
- Benefit: Entertainment venues are explicitly prohibited from "selling or providing drugs, or organizing, coercing, instigating, inducing, deceiving, or harboring others to smoke or inject drugs"; staff likewise may not take drugs or provide conditions for customers to do the above. Violations are punished by the public security authorities with confiscation of illegal gains and property, an order to suspend business for rectification for 3 to 6 months, and revocation of the entertainment business license in serious cases. The regulations also require venues to hang warning signs against drugs, gambling, and prostitution in prominent positions in the lobby, private rooms, and booths, with the report hotline noted
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2006). 娱乐场所管理条例（国令第 458 号，第十四、三十、四十二条）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6297.htm>
- Notes: The risk on the personal side outweighs the venue's: drug use itself brings a public-security penalty, and "letting a friend use it here" in a room you booked or at your home constitutes harboring another person's drug use, a criminal offense. The judgment is simple — as soon as someone produces unknown powder, pills, or an e-cigarette cartridge and asks you to "try it," leave on the spot; do not stay to watch

### 4. Do not eat candy or snacks from strangers, do not drink a beverage that left your sight, do not accept a vape cartridge handed to you
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; refuse anything handed to you, and swap for a fresh drink if yours ever left your sight
- In plain terms: Drugs today do not look like drugs — "head-spinning" e-cigarettes laced with synthetic cannabinoids can be made in all colors and flavors and look basically identical to ordinary e-cigarettes, handed around to try in bars, KTVs, and hotels. Luring someone into taking drugs means 10 to 15 days of detention and a fine of 1,000 to 5,000 yuan; a person tricked into taking them is also detained 10 to 15 days and can be fined up to 3,000 yuan, and addiction brings 3 years of community-based rehabilitation. If a drink has left your sight, swap it for a new one.
- Benefit: Drugs today do not look like drugs. Synthetic cannabinoid substances have been controlled as an entire class since July 1, 2021, mixed into vape oil to make "head-spinning" e-cigarettes. In a case reported by the Supreme People's Procuratorate, investigators described the vape oil as able to "become various colors and various flavors with added food coloring" and as "basically identical in appearance to ordinary e-cigarettes"; sellers promoted it to friends and acquaintances in bars, KTVs, and hotels and induced others to use it, and in that locality's drug-use cases handled that year minors made up nearly forty percent of users. Etomidate was added to the Schedule II psychotropic substances list on October 1, 2023. Deceiving someone into taking drugs is itself an offense: inducing, instigating, deceiving, or coercing another person to smoke or inject drugs means 10 to 15 days of detention and a fine of 1,000 to 5,000 yuan; where it constitutes a crime the sentence is up to 3 years, 3 to 7 years in serious circumstances, 3 to 10 years for coercion, and heavier for offenses against minors. The consequences land on the person deceived: smoking or injecting drugs means 10 to 15 days of detention and a fine of up to 3,000 yuan (in minor circumstances, up to 5 days of detention or a fine of up to 1,000 yuan), and possibly an order barring entry to entertainment venues for 6 months to 1 year; those addicted are ordered into 3 years of community-based rehabilitation, and those who refuse it or use again during it face 2 years of compulsory isolation rehabilitation, extendable by at most 1 more year (nationwide, Public Security Administration Punishments Law, from January 2026)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 最高人民检察院 (2021). 江苏苏州吴江区：办案检察官揭秘披着潮流外衣的新型毒品. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202110/t20211011_531831.shtml>；国家药监局、公安部、国家卫生健康委 (2023). 关于调整麻醉药品和精神药品目录的公告（2023 年第 120 号）. <https://yjj.sh.gov.cn/qtgzwj/20230911/3b612c94e35b4b63abba314c972a2593.html>（上海市药监局转载，原文页 nmpa.gov.cn 在本机返回 412）；全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第八十四、八十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第三百五十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2007). 禁毒法（第三十三、三十八、四十七条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/post_2520706.html>（广东省人民政府转载）
- Notes: This is not only for entertainment venues: the same logic applies at school gates, at friends' gatherings, and on long-distance buses, whether the item handed over is candy, a drink, or a vape cartridge. Drugging is often followed by robbery, sexual assault, and extortion; there are no published official statistics on that part — it is experiential judgment. Swapping a drink that has left your sight costs far less than pursuing it afterwards. For what to do when someone produces unknown powder, pills, or a cartridge in a venue, see item 3 of this section; for carrying things for other people, see item 27 of section 8.

### 5. Use your own ID card at internet cafes; never lend yours or use someone else's
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: By regulation, internet cafes must verify and register the ID card of each customer going online and record their internet activity, keeping it for no less than 60 days; the public security and cultural authorities can pull the records at any time, and nothing can be altered or deleted in that period. Whoever's card was used to log in is the person on whose account everything done on that machine during that time is recorded.
- Benefit: "Business operators of internet service venues shall verify and register valid identification such as the ID cards of customers going online, and shall record relevant information about their online activity. The registered content and record backups shall be kept for no less than 60 days," and shall be provided when the cultural administration department or public security organ makes a lawful inquiry; within the retention period they may not be modified or deleted. In other words, whoever's card was used to log in is the person on whose account what is done on that machine during that time is recorded
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2002). 互联网上网服务营业场所管理条例（国令第 363 号，第二十三条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2002/content_61788.htm>
- Notes: This is the concrete scenario of the same red line as section 9's "never lend your ID card." Operators may not admit minors and must hang a minors-prohibited sign in a prominent position at the entrance; song-and-dance entertainment venues likewise may not admit minors. If you "swipe your card to get a minor into an internet cafe," it is the cafe that gets fined, but the record stays on your ID

### 6. Skip script-murder and escape-room venues located in residential buildings or below the second underground floor
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one question about the floor before booking
- In plain terms: The 2022 notice from five departments states that script-based entertainment venues may not open inside residential buildings or below the first basement level. Such venues are dark, full of partitions and flammable props, and doors often only open after solving a puzzle, so a fire is far harder to escape than in an ordinary shop. Check the address and floor on the review page before booking, and switch venues if it is non-compliant.
- Benefit: The dedicated 2022 notice from five departments is very specific: "Script-based entertainment business venues shall not be located inside residential buildings, below the first basement level of a building (excluding the first basement level), or similar places," and requires venues to implement the requirements of the Work Safety Law and the Fire Protection Law, "routinely conducting self-awareness, self-inspection, and self-correction of fire risks, and improving their ability to organize evacuation and escape in emergencies and to fight incipient fires." The same notice also requires age-appropriateness notices for scripts; minors may not be admitted to scenes unsuitable for them, and script-based entertainment activities may not be offered to minors except on statutory holidays, rest days, and winter and summer vacations
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 文化和旅游部、公安部、住房和城乡建设部、应急管理部、市场监管总局 (2022). 关于加强剧本娱乐经营场所管理的通知. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-06/27/content_5698021.htm>
- Notes: The characteristics of these venues are darkness, many partitions, flammable props, and doors that often need a "puzzle solved" to open, so once a fire starts escape is harder than from an ordinary shop. The floor and whether it is a residential building can be seen on the review page before booking; if non-compliant, just pick another venue

### 7. Walk or run when your mood is low; the antidepressant effect size scales with intensity
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; about half an hour per session
- In plain terms: 218 randomized trials and more than 14,000 people meeting the criteria for major depression: compared with usual care and placebo pills, the clearest improvement came from brisk walking or jogging, followed by yoga, strength training, mixed aerobic exercise, and tai chi/qigong, with the gaps between them not large; the higher the intensity, the better the effect, and strength training and yoga are the easiest to keep up. The authors themselves say the evidence quality is low, so the usable takeaway is that moving beats not moving, and you should pick whichever one you can sustain.
- Benefit: A Bayesian network meta-analysis of 218 randomized trials, 495 arms, and 14,170 participants meeting clinical criteria for major depression, versus active controls (usual care, placebo pills): brisk walking or jogging Hedges' g −0.62 (95% credible interval −0.80 to −0.45), yoga −0.55 (−0.73 to −0.36), strength training −0.49 (−0.69 to −0.29), mixed aerobic exercise −0.43 (−0.61 to −0.24), tai chi or qigong −0.42 (−0.65 to −0.21). "The effects of exercise scale with the intensity prescribed," and strength training and yoga had the best acceptability
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Noetel M, Sanders T, Gallardo-Gómez D, et al. (2024). Effect of exercise for depression: systematic review and network meta-analysis of randomised controlled trials. BMJ, 384, e075847. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-075847>
- Notes: Contested. The authors themselves state that of the 218 studies only 1 met Cochrane's low risk-of-bias standard, and under CINeMA grading confidence was "low" for brisk walking/jogging and "very low" for the rest; exercise trials cannot blind participants, so expectation effects are hard to rule out. The direction of the conclusion still holds: moving beats not moving, do not set the intensity too low, and pick the kind you can sustain. This expands on section 3's "start moving when your mood is low" and cannot replace proper treatment after diagnosis

### 8. When anxiety disrupts your life, a mindfulness-based stress reduction course works no worse than the commonly prescribed anxiety medication
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: An 8-week course, one session per week plus daily practice at home
- In plain terms: 276 adults diagnosed with an anxiety disorder were randomized into two groups, one taking an 8-week mindfulness-based stress reduction course and one taking escitalopram; at the end, symptom improvement was nearly identical. The difference was in side effects: nearly eighty percent of the medication group had at least one adverse reaction and 10 people dropped out because of it; in the course group it was about fifteen percent, with no dropouts from adverse effects. This used the standardized 8-week curriculum, which is not the same as just any meditation app.
- Benefit: A non-inferiority randomized controlled trial at 3 urban academic medical centers in the United States, in which 276 adults diagnosed with an anxiety disorder were randomized 1:1 to an 8-week mindfulness-based stress reduction course or escitalopram (flexible dose 10 to 20 mg), with 208 completing. On the Clinical Global Impression of Severity scale at the endpoint, the mindfulness group dropped 1.35 and the medication group 1.43, a between-group difference of −0.07 (95% CI −0.38 to 0.23, P=0.65), with the lower bound of the credible interval inside the pre-set non-inferiority margin of −0.495. Adverse events differed sharply: 78.6% of the medication group had at least one study-related adverse event and 10 withdrew because of them; 15.4% of the mindfulness group, with no withdrawals from adverse events
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Hoge EA, Bui E, Mete M, Dutton MA, Baker AW, Simon NM (2023). Mindfulness-based stress reduction vs escitalopram for the treatment of adults with anxiety disorders: a randomized clinical trial. JAMA Psychiatry, 80(1), 13-21. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2022.3679>
- Notes: The trial used the standardized 8-week mindfulness-based stress reduction (MBSR) curriculum, not just any meditation app, and the two cannot be equated. This item concerns people who already meet the diagnostic criteria for an anxiety disorder, and seeing a doctor is still needed where indicated; its practical use is that if you have been putting off treatment out of concern about medication side effects, there is an option of comparable evidence strength

### 9. To calm down on the spot, use 5 minutes of "cyclic sighing": inhale in two beats, exhale long
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; 5 minutes a day
- In plain terms: In a controlled trial of 5 minutes daily for a month, all three breathing exercises improved mood and lowered breathing rate more than mindfulness meditation of the same duration, with "cyclic sighing," which lengthens the exhale, the best of the three. The method: breathe in through the nose, take a second short sip of air to fill the lungs fully, then slowly breathe it all out through the mouth. It is a single trial with self-reported results, but the cost is zero.
- Benefit: A remote randomized controlled trial comparing three 5-minute daily breathing exercises with mindfulness meditation of equal duration, over 1 month. The three breathing methods were cyclic sighing (emphasizing a prolonged exhale), box breathing (equal-length inhale, hold, and exhale), and cyclic hyperventilation with holds (long inhale, short exhale). Mixed-effects models showed the breathing exercises, especially the exhale-focused cyclic sighing, outperformed mindfulness meditation on mood improvement (P<0.05) and reduction in respiratory rate (P<0.05)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Balban MY, Neri E, Kogon MM, et al. (2023). Brief structured respiration practices enhance mood and reduce physiological arousal. Cell Reports Medicine, 4(1), 100895. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100895>
- Notes: A single trial, remote self-reported outcomes, a modest sample size, and the effect is "slightly better than meditation" rather than "solves the problem." But the cost is zero, it takes 5 minutes, and it can be done anywhere, so the value for effort is high. Method: breathe in through the nose, take a brief second sip of air to fill the lungs, then slowly and long breathe it all out through the mouth; repeat for 5 minutes

### 10. Treat "regularly seeing people" as a health expense; do not reach out only when your mood is low
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Time to meet and a little money
- In plain terms: 70 studies, 3.4 million people, an average follow-up of 7 years: socially isolated people were about thirty percent more likely to die over the same period than non-isolated people, those who felt lonely about a quarter more, and those living alone about thirty percent more — all three of similar magnitude, and the effect was more pronounced in people under 65. These are observational data, so the reverse explanation that "people in worse health to begin with are more likely to live alone" cannot be removed.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis of 70 prospective studies, 3.4 million participants, and an average follow-up of 7 years: social isolation, loneliness, and living alone were all associated with higher mortality, with adjusted weighted average odds ratios of 1.29 for social isolation, 1.26 for loneliness, and 1.32 for living alone after controlling for confounders; the three effect sizes were comparable, and larger in populations under 65 than in older populations
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Holt-Lunstad J, Smith TB, Baker M, Harris T, Stephenson D (2015). Loneliness and social isolation as risk factors for mortality: a meta-analytic review. Perspectives on Psychological Science, 10(2), 227-237. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691614568352>
- Notes: Contested. All the data are observational and reverse causation cannot be removed — people in already poorer health are more likely to live alone and socialize less. The authors' interpretation is to treat social relationships as a public-health factor on the same scale as smoking and obesity, but no randomized trial has shown that "adding social contact lowers mortality." This item does not conflict with section 3's "cut down on relationships that drain you": that one is about cutting off relationships that consume you, this one is about keeping the few that restore you

### 11. Use nearby green space if you live close to it; walking, strolling, and sitting all count
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; on your way anyway
- In plain terms: 9 studies, 7 countries, 8.32 million people: for every 0.1 increase in the vegetation index of greenery within 500 meters of your home, the probability of dying over the same period was about 4% lower. What was measured is the greenery around the home, not how often you visit a park, and people living in greener areas also tend to be wealthier and enjoy better air. So do not read this as "going to the park extends your life"; the usable part is to factor nearby green space into where you choose to live.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis of 9 cohort studies, 7 countries, and 8.32 million people: for every 0.1 increase in normalized difference vegetation index (NDVI) of surrounding greenery within 500 meters of the residence, the pooled hazard ratio for all-cause mortality was 0.96 (95% CI 0.94–0.97). Seven of the 9 studies found a significant negative association, 2 found none
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Rojas-Rueda D, Nieuwenhuijsen MJ, Gascon M, Perez-Leon D, Mudu P (2019). Green spaces and mortality: a systematic review and meta-analysis of cohort studies. The Lancet Planetary Health, 3(11), e469-e477. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2542-5196(19)30215-3>
- Notes: Contested. Between-study heterogeneity is extremely high (I²=95%), and what was measured is the greenery around the residence, not "how often you go to the park" — people living in greener areas also tend to be richer, have better air, and exercise more, so confounding is hard to rule out. Do not read this as "going to the park extends your life"; the usable part is: factor nearby green space into choosing where to live, at zero cost
