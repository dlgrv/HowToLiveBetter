# 19. Employment, resignation, and work injury

This section is about money: how amounts are calculated, what you sign, and how deadlines run. The first three items cover pay you should receive while still employed; the next five cover leaving a job; the following seven cover occupational disease and work injury. Work-injury payouts are an order of magnitude larger than layoff compensation, and the deadlines are tighter. Damage from occupational disease cannot be undone. Nine more items close the section. The first four cover what to do if you are bullied at work, how to obtain a separation certificate, how personal income tax on severance is calculated, and how to transfer social insurance when you change cities. The middle three cover double wages when there is no written contract, when the employer fails to pay social insurance, and liquidated damages demanded on exit. The last two cover how to respond when the employer wants to reassign or cut pay, and who pays wages and damages for dispatched workers.

Items in this section are grouped by theme below; numbers in parentheses are item numbers.

**Contracts and pay while employed**: overtime pay (Item 1), annual leave (Item 2), probation (Item 3), double wages without a written labor contract (Item 20), unpaid or underpaid social insurance (Item 21), put disagreement with reassignment or pay cuts in writing (Item 23), equal pay for equal work when dispatched (Item 24), long-term bullying at work (Item 16).

**Leaving and layoffs**: calculate N first when laid off (Item 4), no 30-day prior notice (Item 5), damages for unlawful termination (Item 6), do not sign “resignation for personal reasons” (Item 7), save evidence before leaving (Item 8), separation certificate (Item 17), individual income tax on severance (Item 18), liquidated damages demanded on exit (Item 22), transfer social insurance to the new place of enrollment (Item 19).

**Occupational disease and work injury**: three occupational health exams (Item 9), employer must supply protective gear (Item 10), apply for work-injury recognition (Item 11), call 120 first if you feel suddenly unwell (Item 12), no work-injury insurance enrollment (Item 13), work-capacity assessment (Item 14), three payments for work-related death (Item 15).
### 1. Calculate overtime pay at 1.5×, 2×, and 3× rates; report unpaid wages to labor inspection, with an extra 50% to 100% penalty for overdue payment
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Make a habit of keeping copies of clock-in records, shift schedules, and overtime approval forms, and screenshot messages where supervisors assign tasks in work chats. The hard part is remembering to save them every month.
- In plain terms: Overtime on regular workdays pays 1.5 times your wage, while working on a rest day without compensatory time off pays 2 times. Working on statutory public holidays like New Year's Day, Spring Festival, Labor Day, or National Day pays 3 times, and the law does not allow swapping holiday pay for compensatory time off. If your company refuses to pay, file a complaint with labor inspection authorities. If they still refuse after being ordered to pay within a set deadline, the employer must pay an additional 50% to 100% of the owed amount as compensation.
- Benefit: The Labor Law provides for no more than 8 hours per day and an average of no more than 44 hours per week. State Council regulations on working hours further clarify that under standard working hours, daily work is 8 hours and weekly work is 40 hours. Where other working hour systems are approved, relevant regulations apply. Overtime on normal workdays generally must not exceed 1 hour per day. If longer hours are needed for special reasons, it must not exceed 3 hours per day, and total monthly overtime must not exceed 36 hours. Overtime pay has three tiers: regular overtime pays no less than 150% of the regular wage; working on rest days without compensatory time off pays no less than 200%; working on statutory holidays pays no less than 300%. For statutory holidays, the law does not offer the option of substituting pay with compensatory time off. If an employer arranges overtime without paying overtime compensation, the labor administrative department will order payment within a specified time limit. If it fails to pay after the deadline, it will be ordered to pay additional compensation to the worker of between 50% and 100% of the amount owed (nationwide). To see which days are statutory holidays, see Article 2 of the State Council's Holiday Measures: New Year's Day (1 day), Spring Festival (4 days), Tomb Sweeping Day (1 day), Labor Day (2 days), Dragon Boat Festival (1 day), Mid-Autumn Festival (1 day), and National Day (3 days). Article 7 of the Holiday Measures allows unified holiday rescheduling to combine these days with weekends into longer consecutive breaks.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (1995 修订). 国务院关于职工工作时间的规定（第三、五条）；劳动部贯彻《国务院关于职工工作时间的规定》的实施办法（第三、五条）. <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/201710/t20171009_780941.html>；全国人大常委会 (2018). 劳动法（2018 年第二次修正，第三十六、四十一、四十三、四十四条）. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2023/art_d9aa750028b14b99a776cb93726a360d.html>（国家市场监督管理总局刊登）；全国人大常委会 (2007). 劳动合同法（第八十五条第三项）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>；国务院 (2024). 全国年节及纪念日放假办法（国务院令第 795 号修订，2025 年 1 月 1 日起施行，第二条、第七条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11726/202411/content_6989774.html>
- Notes: Rest days and statutory holidays must be calculated separately. Statutory holidays throughout the year only include the official festival days for New Year's Day, Spring Festival, Labor Day, National Day, and other designated holidays. In the extended breaks created by shifted working days, days that are not the actual festival dates listed in the Holiday Measures count as rest days: compensatory time off can be arranged, and if not possible, 200% must be paid. This is derived from Article 2 of the Holiday Measures and Article 44 of the Labor Law. Claims like "company policy requires pre-approval for overtime" or "the employee stayed voluntarily" do not give the company the right to withhold pay, but they make proving your case harder, so preserve work orders and approval records. An employer wishing to use special hour systems—such as comprehensive working hours or non-fixed working hours—must obtain approval from the labor administrative department beforehand rather than deciding on its own. For the arbitration statute of limitations regarding disputes, see Section 8, Item 19.

### 2. Calculate 5, 10, or 15 days of annual leave by cumulative career years, and cash out unused days at 300% daily wage
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Add up all the years you have worked across every employer; your social insurance contribution records serve as proof. The hard part is refusing to sign when your employer hands you a "voluntary waiver of annual leave" form.
- In plain terms: After working continuously for 12 months, you earn annual leave, and your working years are counted across all employers combined. You get 5 days a year for 1 to 10 years of service, 10 days for 10 to 20 years, and 15 days once you reach 20 years. If your employer cannot arrange the leave and you agree not to take it, they must pay you 300% of your daily wage for each unused day. But once you sign a form saying you give it up voluntarily, you only get your normal pay.
- Benefit: Annual leave is unlocked after working continuously for 12 months. The number of days is determined by cumulative total working years across all previous employers combined: 5 days for 1 to less than 10 years, 10 days for 10 to less than 20 years, and 15 days for 20 years or more. Statutory national holidays and regular rest days do not count toward annual leave days. If an employer genuinely cannot arrange leave due to work requirements and obtains the employee's consent not to take it, they must "pay annual leave wages at 300% of the employee's daily wage income" for each untaken day. Because this 300% already includes the normal pay for working that day, the actual extra payout received is 200%. Daily wage = employee's monthly wage ÷ 21.75. The monthly wage is calculated as the monthly average over the 12 months prior to payment, excluding overtime pay. Employees who join mid-year have their leave prorated based on the remaining calendar days in that year with the employer. Upon resignation, leave is prorated and settled based on time worked that year; days taken in excess cannot be deducted back. Employers that neither arrange leave nor pay compensation are ordered to rectify within a specified time limit; failing to comply within that limit incurs an additional penalty payment of equal amount on top of the annual leave compensation (Nationwide, implemented on January 1, 2008)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2007). 职工带薪年休假条例（国务院令第 514 号，第二、三、四、五、七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2008/content_859865.htm>；人力资源和社会保障部 (2008). 企业职工带薪年休假实施办法（人社部令第 1 号，第三、四、五、十、十一、十二、十五条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2009/content_1265995.htm>
- Notes: Cumulative working time must be tallied across all employers; do not count only your tenure at your current company if you switched jobs. **Only when an employee requests in writing not to take leave "for personal reasons" may the employer pay regular wages only**, so never sign a "voluntary waiver of annual leave" form. Employees who take a total of 20 or more days of personal leave in a year without pay deductions forfeit annual leave for that year. Sick leave exceeding specified thresholds also disqualifies annual leave: 2 months for under 10 years of total service, 3 months for 10 to 20 years, and 4 months for 20 years or more. Home leave, marriage or funeral leave, maternity leave, and work-related injury suspension with pay do not count toward annual leave.

### 3. Check statutory probation limits, ensure pay is at least 80%, and allow only one probation period
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Take a few minutes before signing to check the contract duration against the probation length.
- In plain terms: If a contract runs for at least 3 months but under 1 year, probation can last at most 1 month. For 1 year to under 3 years, it can be at most 2 months; for 3 years or more, at most 6 months. The same company can put you on probation only once; setting another probation period after you become permanent just because you changed roles is illegal. Probation pay cannot be lower than 80% of the contract wage or the lowest grade for the same position, and it cannot fall below the local minimum wage.
- Benefit: For labor contracts of at least 3 months but less than 1 year, probation cannot exceed 1 month. For contracts of at least 1 year but less than 3 years, it cannot exceed 2 months. For contracts of 3 years or more, as well as open-ended contracts without an expiration date, it cannot exceed 6 months. Contracts based on completing a specific project or contracts lasting under 3 months cannot include any probation period. "The same employer and the same employee may agree on a probation period only once"; an employer can test the same employee only one time. If a contract specifies only a probation period, that probation does not stand, and the stated duration becomes the actual contract term. Probation wages "must not be lower than the lowest wage grade for the same position at the employer, or 80% of the wage agreed upon in the labor contract, and must not be lower than the local minimum wage standard." If an employer sets a probation period longer than the statutory limit and the employee works it, the employer must pay additional compensation based on the employee's monthly wage (月工资, yuè gōngzī) after probation for the excess time worked. An employer wishing to terminate a contract during probation may do so only under circumstances specified by law and must state the grounds to the employee (national).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2007). 劳动合同法（第十九、二十、二十一、八十三条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: The three most common illegal practices: 1) Signing a 1-year contract but setting a 3-month probation period. 2) Reassigning an employee after regular status and imposing another probation period. 3) "Not paying social insurance (社保, shèbǎo) during probation." The obligation to pay social insurance begins on your very first day of work, regardless of probation status; see Section 12, Item 16 for employer obligations. Being dismissed during probation does not mean you leave empty-handed; it depends on whether the employer's reason is among the legally permitted grounds. Unlawful dismissal is compensated at 2N under Item 6.

### 4. If you are laid off, calculate N first: one month’s wage per full year, half a month if under six months
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. Run the numbers once with a calculator; about ten minutes.
- In plain terms: When you are laid off, statutory economic compensation is calculated like this: one month’s wage for each full year worked; at least six months but less than a year counts as a year; less than six months gets half a month. Monthly wage here is the average over the 12 months before you leave, including bonuses, allowances, and subsidies. If wages exceed three times the local average monthly wage for the prior year, pay is capped at three times that average, and years of service count for at most 12 years.
- Benefit: Statutory economic compensation under the Labor Contract Law is what the employer owes when you are laid off—commonly called N. The formula: “one month’s wage for each full year of service … six months or more but less than one year counts as one year; less than six months, half a month’s wage to the worker.” If monthly wage exceeds three times the local average monthly wage of employees for the prior year, use triple that average as the cap, and count at most twelve years of service.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第四十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: Monthly wage means average wage over the twelve months before the labor contract is terminated. Bonuses, allowances, and subsidies all count—not base pay alone. That is what employers most often understate. Whether this sum is subject to personal income tax is in Item 18 of this section (tax on compensation).

### 5. If the employer dismisses you citing post-illness inability to work, incompetence, or major change of circumstances, and did not give 30 days’ prior notice, it must also pay one extra month’s wage
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost.
- In plain terms: If the employer dismisses you because you cannot work after illness, cannot perform the job, or circumstances changed substantially, it must give 30 days’ written notice. Without notice, it must pay one month’s wage on top of N. That is the common N+1. In negotiation, list that month separately; do not let the employer fold it into N.
- Benefit: The Labor Contract Law gives employers two options when terminating under this article. One is “notify the worker in writing thirty days in advance.” The other is “may terminate the labor contract after paying the worker one additional month’s wage.” If they want you out immediately, they pay an extra month. That extra month is commonly called payment in lieu of notice (代通知金), counted separately from N. That month is based on your prior month’s wage; N uses average wage over the 12 months before termination—the bases differ
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第三十六、三十九、四十、四十一、四十六、四十七、八十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>；国务院 (2008). 劳动合同法实施条例（国务院令第 535 号）第二十条：「用人单位依照劳动合同法第四十条的规定，选择额外支付劳动者一个月工资解除劳动合同的，其额外支付的工资应当按照该劳动者上一个月的工资标准确定」. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2008/content_1107604.htm>
- Notes: The extra month appears only in the three situations under Article 40 of the Labor Contract Law. Illness or non-work injury and, after the medical period, inability to do the original or reassigned work. Inability to perform after training or reassignment. Major change in objective circumstances at signing and failed negotiation. Other situations have no extra month. Economic layoffs (Article 41) require only 30 days’ notice to the union or all employees. Mutual termination proposed by the employer is N. Serious misconduct termination by the employer has no compensation. Unlawful termination is 2N. For the Article 40 three cases, keep N and the extra 1 separate in negotiation; do not let the employer count the extra month as part of N

### 6. For unlawful termination, damages are double the economic compensation standard
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. Arbitration takes several months. The hard part is staying with the process the whole time and not dropping out midway.
- In plain terms: If termination is unlawful, damages are double the economic compensation standard—commonly called 2N. Whether termination is unlawful depends on the facts. Ask legal aid or a lawyer first, then decide whether to negotiate with the company or go to arbitration.
- Benefit: The Labor Contract Law provides that where an employer unlawfully terminates or ends a labor contract, it “shall pay damages to the worker at twice the economic compensation standard under Article 47 of this Law.” The calculation is to compute one economic compensation amount under that standard, then pay double
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第八十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: This is the common 2N. What counts as unlawful termination depends on the facts. Consult legal aid or a lawyer first, then choose negotiation or arbitration; routes are in Section 7

### 7. Do not sign “resignation for personal reasons”—once signed, N usually disappears
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. The hard part is resisting pressure to sign on the spot.
- In plain terms: Signing that you resigned for personal reasons usually wipes out both economic compensation and unemployment insurance. Voluntary resignation is not among the situations that trigger compensation, except when you leave because of employer faults such as unpaid wages or failure to pay social insurance. You do not have to sign the same day; sleep on it.
- Benefit: The Labor Contract Law limits economic compensation to the legally listed situations. A worker who initiates departure is not included unless the reason is statutory employer fault such as unpaid wages or failure to pay social insurance. Signing “voluntary resignation” usually voids both economic compensation and unemployment insurance.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第三十八条、第四十六条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: A common employer line is “sign first to process exit; compensation comes separately.” If they mean it, put it in the agreement on the spot; otherwise do not sign. Not signing that day has no legal downside; sleep on it.

### 8. Before leaving, save pay slips, attendance records, the labor contract, social insurance records, and chat logs
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. About half an hour to archive materials. The hard part is remembering before you walk out.
- In plain terms: In dismissal or wage-deduction disputes the employer must prove its case, but for overtime you must prove first, and employers only have to keep wage records for two years. Once you hand back the computer and accounts, you cannot get these materials back. With pay slips, attendance, social insurance records, and original chat logs in hand, you have something to use when the employer denies liability.
- Benefit: Once you return the computer and accounts, these materials are gone for good. Labor disputes follow “who asserts must prove”: what you claim, you must prove. Where evidence is in the employer’s hands, the employer must produce it; failure to do so carries adverse consequences for the employer. In arbitration, if you cannot obtain employer-held materials, the tribunal may order the employer to submit them within a set time. For dismissal, termination, contract cancellation, wage reduction, and calculation of length of service, the employer bears the burden of proof. Overtime is different: you must first prove you worked overtime. If you show overtime records are with the employer and it will not produce them, adverse consequences shift to the employer. The employer is only required to keep wage payment records for at least two years. When paying wages, the employer must give you a personal wage breakdown. Without a written contract, wage payment records, social insurance payment records, and a work ID can all prove an employment relationship. Employment registration forms, attendance records, and testimony of other employees can also be used. Chat logs count as electronic data and can be evidence. Submit originals; printouts from original records still count as originals. In one Shanghai procuratorate case, a worker sought ten years of minimum-wage shortfalls. First- and second-instance courts recognized only the last two years before resignation. The courts held the employer need keep only two years of records, so the worker had to prove the prior eight years. He had ten years of pay slips and bank statements, but the employer disputed authenticity of the slips. In August 2024 he applied for procuratorial supervision. After the procuratorate protested, the employer paid 19000 yuan (1.9万) in a mediated settlement. He sued when the company cleared staff in 2021; by the supervision application about three years had passed (nationwide)

On exit, also obtain a separation certificate from the employer—see Item 17 of this section (separation certificate)

### 9. Before dusty, noisy, or chemical jobs, check the contract for hazards; the employer arranges and pays for three occupational health exams
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No cost. When signing, read the “occupational disease hazard disclosure” section line by line. Keep a copy of each exam report. The hard part is asking in person and getting the documents in hand.
- In plain terms: When signing the contract, the employer must truthfully state in writing what occupational disease hazards the post has, how to protect yourself, and what benefits apply. If it concealed them, you may refuse that work and the employer cannot fire you for it. Three occupational health exams—before starting, while on the job, and before leaving—are arranged and paid by the employer. Without an exit exam, the employer cannot terminate the contract with you.
- Benefit: The Law on Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases requires that when signing a labor contract, the employer explain four things. Those four are: what occupational disease hazards the work may cause, what consequences may follow, how to protect yourself, and what benefits apply. The statutory wording is to “truthfully inform the worker and specify it in the labor contract; concealment or deception is forbidden.” If you are later assigned hazardous work not disclosed in the contract, the employer must inform you again truthfully and renegotiate contract terms with you. If the employer fails, “the worker has the right to refuse work involving occupational disease hazards, and the employer may not terminate the labor contract for that reason.” There are three exams: pre-placement, during employment, and pre-departure. “Occupational health examination costs shall be borne by the employer”; the employer pays and must inform you of results in writing. Without a pre-placement exam, the employer must not assign work involving occupational disease hazards. Workers with occupational contraindications must not be assigned prohibited tasks. If health damage related to the job is found, the worker must be transferred from the original post and properly placed. **“An employer must not terminate or end the labor contract with a worker who has not undergone the pre-departure occupational health examination.”** On leaving, you “have the right to obtain a copy of your occupational health surveillance file; the employer shall provide it truthfully and free of charge, and affix its seal on the copy provided.” That occupational health surveillance file must be copied truthfully, given free, and stamped on the copy (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2018). 职业病防治法（2018 年修正，第三十三、三十五、三十六条）. <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/201711/t20171104_779851.html>（北京市人民政府转载）
- Notes: Three questions at interview roughly show whether the employer is serious: whether the post has occupational disease hazards, whether the contract states them, and who pays for the pre-placement exam. If they cannot answer or say “start first and we’ll see,” weigh the risk yourself. Exit exams are most often skipped: without a pre-departure exam, the employer cannot terminate or end the contract. That exam is also key material for a later occupational disease diagnosis; likewise get a stamped copy of the surveillance file before you go. Occupational disease itself follows the work-injury route; benefits start at Item 11.

### 10. Dust, noise, and chemical toxins often cause irreversible harm (for example pneumoconiosis and noise-induced deafness): the employer must supply protective gear, and you can refuse work with no protections
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No cost. The hard part is wearing particulate respirators, earplugs, and safety goggles consistently, and refusing unprotected tasks—coworkers may call you picky.
- In plain terms: Pneumoconiosis, noise-induced deafness, and chemical eye burns are all statutory occupational diseases. Once lungs or hearing are damaged, they cannot be restored. You have the right to know what hazards the job carries and to demand protective facilities and personal protective equipment from the employer. You may refuse work without protections. Pay cuts or dismissal for using these rights do not count.
- Benefit: The statutory list of occupational diseases, effective 1 August 2025, has 12 major categories and 135 conditions, including 4 open-ended clauses. The first category is occupational pneumoconiosis. It covers silicosis, coal workers' pneumoconiosis, graphite pneumoconiosis, carbon black pneumoconiosis, asbestosis, and talc pneumoconiosis. Also cement pneumoconiosis, mica pneumoconiosis, potter's pneumoconiosis, aluminum pneumoconiosis, welder's pneumoconiosis, and foundry worker's pneumoconiosis, plus one open-ended clause. Occupational ear, nose, throat, and oral diseases include noise-induced deafness and blast-induced deafness. Occupational eye diseases include chemical eye burns and welder's flash keratitis. Harm from pneumoconiosis and noise-induced deafness is permanent. The US National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (NIOSH) states that silicosis is permanent lung damage that progresses over time, with no known cure. A Lancet review states that after patients stop dust exposure, lung function can still worsen as the disease progresses. The US Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) states that noise-induced hearing loss is permanent and cannot be corrected by surgery or drugs. The law gives four rights you can cite on the spot. First, to know what occupational disease hazards exist in the workplace, what consequences they can cause, and what protective measures are required. Second, to demand qualified protective facilities and personal protective equipment and improved working conditions. Third, to criticize, report, and complain about violations of occupational disease prevention law and practices that endanger life and health. Fourth, “refuse unlawful orders and forced work without occupational disease protections”—you may refuse noncompliant orders and unprotected tasks. ### 12. Don't believe "toughing it out to your desk counts as a workplace injury": call an ambulance first, don't rush to clock in
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free.
- In plain terms: Rushing to the office when a sudden medical emergency strikes will not necessarily get you extra compensation. Being deemed a workplace injury requires falling ill during work hours and at your workstation; falling ill on your commute before arriving does not qualify anyway. If emergency resuscitation takes more than 48 hours, it does not qualify either. Delaying medical care just to meet these conditions shrinks your odds of surviving.
- Benefit: Being deemed a workplace injury requires dying of a sudden illness "during working hours and at the workstation," or dying within 48 hours despite emergency resuscitation. Delaying medical treatment just to meet these two criteria costs you your chance of survival, and the compensation may still not be awarded.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:工伤保险条例第十五条第一款第（一）项：「在工作时间和工作岗位，突发疾病死亡或者在48小时之内经抢救无效死亡的」视同工伤；第十四条第（六）项只把上下班途中「受到非本人主要责任的交通事故或者城市轨道交通、客运轮渡、火车事故伤害的」列为工伤，途中突发疾病不在其中. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>
- Notes: Both conditions must be met at the same time: during working hours, and at the workstation. Falling ill on the commute before arriving at work does not qualify. If someone falls ill while at work and is then sent home or to the hospital, dying within 48 hours despite resuscitation efforts may still qualify. The law looks at where you were when the illness started, not where you ultimately passed away. Short videos often frame this as "drag yourself to the office to get a payout," which gets things completely backward. What changes the outcome is whether you reach a hospital in the first few dozen minutes after onset. In addition, 48 hours is a hard cutoff—if resuscitation exceeds 48 hours, it does not count.

### 17. Request a termination certificate when leaving a job, stating contract term, departure date, position, and years of service
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->

- In plain terms: Whether you are fired, your contract expires, or you resign, your employer must issue a termination certificate when you leave and transfer your personnel file and social insurance within 15 days. The certificate must state your contract term, departure date, job position, and years of service at the company. You need it to register for unemployment benefits. If the employer refuses to provide it, labor authorities will order them to correct it, and they must compensate you for any losses caused.
- Benefit: The Labor Contract Law stipulates that the employer must issue proof of termination or dissolution when ending an employment contract. The law makes no distinction based on who initiated the departure: contract non-renewal, voluntary resignation, and dismissal are all covered. The employer must also complete the transfer procedures for your personnel file and social insurance relations within 15 days. The implementing regulations require the certificate to specify four items: employment contract term, date of termination or dissolution, job position, and length of service at this employer. The Regulations on Unemployment Insurance stipulate that unemployed workers must promptly register for unemployment at the social insurance agency using this certificate issued by the employer. Unemployment benefits are calculated starting from the date of unemployment registration. If an employer fails to issue a written certificate, the labor administrative department will order rectification. If this causes damage to you, the employer must pay compensation. Furthermore, the law provides that if a new employer hires a worker whose contract with their former employer has not yet been terminated, and this causes losses to the former employer, the new employer bears joint and several liability. ### 18. Before spending severance, check individual income tax: within 3× the local prior-year average employee wage is tax-free; the excess is taxed separately, not merged into that year's salary
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. Look up the local prior-year average employee wage, then spend a few minutes checking how the employer withheld tax. The individual income tax app shows line-by-line filing for each payment.
- In plain terms: One-time compensation from ending employment is tax-free up to 3 times the local prior-year average employee wage. Amounts above that are taxed on their own, not added to that year's wages. If the employer lumped the whole payment into the month's wages and withheld tax that way, ask the employer first, then call 12366 for the tax bureau.
- Benefit: A Ministry of Finance and State Taxation Administration circular covers one-time compensation income from terminating a labor relationship with an employer. That income includes economic compensation, living allowances, and other subsidies. The portion within 3 times the local prior-year average employee wage is exempt from individual income tax. The portion above 3 times is not merged into annual comprehensive income; it is taxed separately under the comprehensive income rate table. On that table, the portion within 36000 yuan for the year is taxed at 3%; the portion from 36000 yuan to 144000 yuan at 10%. Higher brackets go up to 45%. Example: if the local prior-year average employee wage is 100000 yuan (10万), 3 times is 300000 yuan (30万). On 360000 yuan (36万) compensation, only the 60000 yuan (6万) excess is taxed—3480 yuan. ### 24. If dispatched to work at another company, your pay must follow the same rules as direct hires in similar posts, and both firms share joint liability for harm suffered on the job
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Finding your employment contract and checking which company stamped it takes a few minutes. Then ask the dispatch agency about the pay and social insurance terms in the labor dispatch agreement—it is legally obligated to inform you. If a formal dispute arises, labor arbitration is free of charge, with cases concluded within 45 days of acceptance, extendable by up to 15 days. If dissatisfied with the award, you can file a lawsuit; first-instance ordinary civil proceedings are legally required to conclude within 6 months, which may be extended, and you cover your own attorney fees.
- In plain terms: As an agency-dispatched worker, your employment contract is signed with the dispatch agency, but the host company where you actually work each day also carries statutory obligations. Your wages must follow the exact same distribution method as direct hires in comparable positions, and the host firm must pay your overtime and performance bonuses. If you suffer harm or injury at the host company, both companies bear joint liability, meaning you can demand full compensation from either one. During any gap months when you are returned by the host and have no work assigned, the dispatch agency must still pay you at least the local minimum wage.
- Benefit: Under the Labor Contract Law, the labor dispatch agency is your legal employer and must sign a fixed-term contract of at least 2 years with you, paying wages on a monthly basis. During periods when you are not placed on an assignment, it must pay you monthly at or above the local minimum wage. The dispatch agency must inform you of the contents of the dispatch agreement and cannot withhold any remuneration paid by the host employer (legally termed the "client enterprise"). Neither entity may charge you any placement or service fees. Dispatched workers have the right to equal pay for equal work alongside the client enterprise's regular employees. The client enterprise must apply an identical remuneration distribution system to similar roles. Where no identical role exists, pay is benchmarked against comparable or similar local positions. The client enterprise must pay overtime and performance bonuses, provide post-related welfare benefits, and must not discriminate against dispatched personnel. For continuous placement, wages must be adjusted normally. Labor dispatch may only be used for temporary, auxiliary, or substitutable positions; a temporary position is defined as one lasting no longer than 6 months. The total number of dispatched workers at a client enterprise must not exceed 10% of its total workforce. If the client enterprise causes harm or loss to a dispatched worker, both the dispatch agency and the client enterprise bear joint and several liability. Joint liability means you can demand full compensation from either firm or pursue both simultaneously. If a labor dispute arises with either party, both must be joined as co-parties in arbitration. If an occupational injury occurs at the client enterprise, the dispatch agency must apply for the official work-related injury determination and bear work-related injury insurance liability, while the client enterprise must assist in the investigation. The client enterprise may return a worker only under strictly limited statutory conditions. Arrangements disguised as contracting or outsourcing that in substance function as labor dispatch are handled under labor dispatch rules (national, Interim Provisions effective March 1, 2014).

### 25. Keep your own copy of your employment contract; if you already signed a blank or incomplete one, message your employer that same day to fill it in and give you a copy, creating a paper trail showing it was blank when signed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Read the contract from start to finish before signing; if fields like the employer's name, contract term, job post, or salary are blank, do not sign yet. If you have already signed, send a WeChat message or email to the person handling it that very day. Specify which fields were blank when you signed, and ask the company to fill them in and give you a copy. If the company refuses, call 12333 or file a complaint with the labor inspection division of the human resources and social security bureau. Once a case is formally filed, the investigation concludes within 60 working days, with a 30-working-day extension for complex cases. If things escalate to seeking compensation, file for labor arbitration within 1 year; it is free of charge, with cases concluded within 45 days of acceptance, extendable by at most 15 days. If dissatisfied with the award, take it to court; a first-instance ordinary civil procedure is required by law to conclude within 6 months, which can be extended, and you pay your own lawyer fees. The hard part is working up the nerve to ask for the contract right after starting a new job.
- In plain terms: You and your employer must each keep an original copy of the employment contract; if mandatory items like salary or contract term are missing, the employer will be ordered to fix it. If you sign a blank contract and have no proof, the court may simply enforce whatever the employer filled in later. Once you sign, ask the employer to fill it out and give you a copy, and make sure you create a written record showing it was blank at the time of signing.
- Benefit: The Labor Contract Law stipulates that a contract takes effect only after both parties sign or seal the text. Both the employer and the employee must each hold one copy. The contract must explicitly include nine mandatory clauses, including the employer's name, contract duration, job description and work location, working hours, remuneration, social insurance, and labor protection. If the text provided by the employer fails to include all mandatory terms, or if the employer fails to deliver a copy to the employee, the labor administrative department will order it to be corrected. If this causes harm to the employee, compensation must be paid. The conclusion of employment contracts between employers and employees inherently falls under the supervision of labor security inspection. If terms regarding salary or working conditions are unclear, they may be renegotiated. If negotiations fail, collective bargaining agreements apply. If there is no collective agreement or it does not specify wages, equal pay for equal work applies. Contracts signed against a party's true intention through fraud, coercion, or taking advantage of someone's distress are void or partially void, as determined by an arbitration tribunal or court. The Workers' Daily reported a case from Korla, Xinjiang: a hotel sales manager claimed the salary written in his contract was lower than what had been negotiated during the interview. He said he only signed his name when signing the contract, and the remaining content was filled in later by the hotel. The court ordered an appraisal to determine whether the filled-in content and the signature were created around the same time. He abandoned the appraisal and therefore had to bear the adverse legal consequences. The court held that as an adult capable of making his own decisions (legally, a person with full capacity for civil conduct), he had to take responsibility for his own signature. The court ruled that the contract did not meet the conditions for invalidity, and his wage claims were not fully supported. The Daxing Court in Beijing heard a case with the opposite outcome: 20 shuttle bus drivers said they had signed blank contracts and held no copy. When signing, the company had them sign a stack of documents without letting them read the contents. In the contracts produced by the company, all contract terms were handwritten. Even though the drivers had different hire dates and signing frequencies, their termination dates were all written as the exact same day. This date happened to coincide with the day the company's contract with its client ended. The court found it more credible that the drivers had signed contracts with blank term fields. Since the company could not prove that this termination date reflected the genuine agreement at the time, the court ruled it an illegal dismissal, requiring the payment of illegal termination compensation. The second-instance court upheld the original judgment. In disputes arising from decisions such as firing, dismissal, contract termination, or wage reductions, the burden of proof rests with the employer (nationwide; the two cases represent only local judicial determinations).
