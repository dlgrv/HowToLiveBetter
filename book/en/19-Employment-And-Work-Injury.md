# 19. Employment, resignation, and work injury

This section is about money: how amounts are calculated, what you sign, and how deadlines run. The first three items cover pay you should receive while still employed; the next five cover leaving a job; the following seven cover occupational disease and work injury. Work-injury payouts are an order of magnitude larger than layoff compensation, and the deadlines are tighter. Damage from occupational disease cannot be undone. Nine more items close the section. The first four cover what to do if you are bullied at work, how to obtain a separation certificate, how personal income tax on severance is calculated, and how to transfer social insurance when you change cities. The middle three cover double wages when there is no written contract, when the employer fails to pay social insurance, and liquidated damages demanded on exit. The last two cover how to respond when the employer wants to reassign or cut pay, and who pays wages and damages for dispatched workers.

Items in this section are grouped by theme below; numbers in parentheses are item numbers.

**Contracts and pay while employed**: overtime pay (Item 1), annual leave (Item 2), probation (Item 3), double wages without a written labor contract (Item 20), unpaid or underpaid social insurance (Item 21), put disagreement with reassignment or pay cuts in writing (Item 23), equal pay for equal work when dispatched (Item 24), long-term bullying at work (Item 16).

**Leaving and layoffs**: calculate N first when laid off (Item 4), no 30-day prior notice (Item 5), damages for unlawful termination (Item 6), do not sign “resignation for personal reasons” (Item 7), save evidence before leaving (Item 8), separation certificate (Item 17), individual income tax on severance (Item 18), liquidated damages demanded on exit (Item 22), transfer social insurance to the new place of enrollment (Item 19).

**Occupational disease and work injury**: three occupational health exams (Item 9), employer must supply protective gear (Item 10), apply for work-injury recognition (Item 11), call 120 first if you feel suddenly unwell (Item 12), no work-injury insurance enrollment (Item 13), work-capacity assessment (Item 14), three payments for work-related death (Item 15).
### 1. Overtime pay runs at 1.5×, 2×, and 3×; if unpaid, complain to labor inspection, and late payment triggers an extra 50% to 100%
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. Routinely keep copies of clock-in records, shift schedules, and overtime approval forms, and screenshot work-group messages where managers assign extra work. The hard part is remembering to save them every month.
- In plain terms: Weekday overtime is paid at 1.5 times your wage. Rest-day overtime without compensatory time off is paid at 2 times. Work on statutory public holidays such as New Year's Day, Spring Festival, Labor Day, and National Day is paid at 3 times, and the law does not allow compensatory time off to replace that pay. If the employer refuses, file a complaint with labor inspection. After a payment deadline is set and the employer still does not pay, the employer must also pay an additional half to the full amount owed on top of the arrears.
- Benefit: Normal working hours under the law are no more than 8 hours per day and no more than 44 hours per week on average. Weekday overtime is generally capped at 1 hour per day. Where special reasons require more, it must not exceed 3 hours per day, and the total in one month must not exceed 36 hours. Overtime pay has three tiers. Extending working hours on ordinary days: at least 150% of wages. Working on a rest day when compensatory rest cannot be arranged: at least 200%. Working on a statutory public holiday: at least 300%. For statutory public holidays, the law does not offer a “substitute with compensatory rest” option. If the employer arranges overtime but does not pay overtime wages, the labor administration department orders it to pay within a set period. If it still does not pay after that period, it must pay the worker an additional sum of 50% to 100% of the amount due on top of the arrears (nationwide). Which days are statutory public holidays is in Article 2 of the State Council holiday measures: New Year's Day 1 day, Spring Festival 4 days, Qingming Festival 1 day, Labor Day 2 days, Dragon Boat Festival 1 day, Mid-Autumn Festival 1 day, National Day 3 days. Article 7 of the holiday measures allows “unified holiday bridging,” linking these days with weekends into longer breaks
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2018). 劳动法（2018 年第二次修正，第三十六、四十一、四十三、四十四条）. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2023/art_d9aa750028b14b99a776cb93726a360d.html>（国家市场监督管理总局刊登）；全国人大常委会 (2007). 劳动合同法（第八十五条第三项）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>；国务院 (2024). 全国年节及纪念日放假办法（国务院令第 795 号修订，2025 年 1 月 1 日起施行，第二条、第七条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11726/202411/content_6989774.html>
- Notes: Rest days and statutory public holidays are calculated separately. Statutory public holidays are only the legally designated days each year, such as New Year's Day, Spring Festival, Labor Day, and National Day. In long breaks created by bridging rest days, days that are not the festival days listed in the holiday measures count as rest days: compensatory rest may be arranged where possible; otherwise pay is 200%. That follows from Article 2 of the holiday measures and Article 44 of the Labor Law. “Company policy requires overtime approval” and “I stayed voluntarily” do not mean the employer may withhold pay, but they make evidence harder, so keep assignment and approval records. If the employer wants a comprehensive working-hours system or a flexible working-hours system, it must obtain approval from the labor administration department first; it cannot decide on its own. For dispute arbitration time limits, see Section 8, Item 19.

### 2. Annual leave is 5, 10, or 15 days by cumulative service years; unused days are paid at 300% of daily wage
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. Add up years worked at all employers once; social insurance payment records are proof. The hard part is refusing to sign a “voluntary waiver of annual leave” form if the company pushes one.
- In plain terms: With cumulative service of at least 1 year but less than 10 years across all employers, you get 5 days of annual leave per year. At 10 years it is 10 days; at 20 years, 15 days. Job changes still count toward the total. If the employer cannot schedule leave and you agree not to take it, those days are paid at 3 times daily wage. Sign a “voluntary waiver of annual leave” form and you only get normal wages.
- Benefit: After 12 consecutive months of work you are entitled to annual leave. The number of days follows **cumulative** working time—years at all employers added together. At least 1 year but less than 10 years: 5 days; at least 10 years but less than 20 years: 10 days; 20 years or more: 15 days. Statutory public holidays and rest days do not count toward annual leave days. Where the employer truly cannot arrange leave because of work needs and you agree in writing not to take it, it must “pay annual leave wages at 300% of the employee’s daily wage income” for the days not taken. That 300% already includes the normal wage for the day you would have worked, so the extra cash in hand is 200%. Daily wage = your monthly wage ÷ 21.75. Monthly wage is the average over the 12 months before payment, excluding overtime pay. If you joined the employer mid-year, days are prorated by remaining calendar days at that employer. On resignation, accrue and settle for the portion of the year already worked; excess leave already taken is not clawed back. If the employer neither arranges leave nor pays, it is ordered to correct within a deadline. If it still does not after the deadline, besides the annual leave wage payment, it must pay an additional sum equal to that amount as compensation (nationwide; effective 1 January 2008).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2007). 职工带薪年休假条例（国务院令第 514 号，第二、三、四、五、七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2008/content_859865.htm>；人力资源和社会保障部 (2008). 企业职工带薪年休假实施办法（人社部令第 1 号，第三、四、五、十、十一、十二、十五条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2009/content_1265995.htm>
- Notes: Cumulative working time must be summed across employers; if you changed jobs, do not count only tenure at the current employer. **Only when the employee “does not take leave for personal reasons and states so in writing” may the employer pay only normal wages**, so do not sign a “voluntary waiver of annual leave” form. If personal leave totals more than 20 days in a year and the employer does not deduct wages, you get no annual leave that year. Excessive sick leave also disqualifies you: less than 10 years of service—2 months; 10 to 20 years—3 months; more than 20 years—4 months. Home leave, marriage and bereavement leave, maternity leave, and work-injury wage continuation periods do not count toward annual leave.

### 3. Probation has a legal cap, wages must be at least 80%, and it can only be agreed once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. Before signing, spend a few minutes checking the contract term against the probation length.
- In plain terms: For a contract of 3 months to 1 year, probation is at most 1 month. For 1 to 3 years, at most 2 months. For 3 years or more, at most 6 months. The same employer may only put you on probation once; a second probation after transfer after regularization is unlawful. Probation wages must not be below the lowest grade for the same post or 80% of the contract wage, and must not be below the local minimum wage.
- Benefit: For a contract of more than 3 months but less than 1 year, probation must not exceed 1 month. For more than 1 year but less than 3 years, not more than 2 months. For 3 years or more, and for open-ended contracts with no end date, not more than 6 months. For a contract for a defined piece of work, or a contract of less than 3 months in total, probation may not be agreed. “The same employer and the same employee may agree on probation only once”—one probation round per person at that company. If the contract lists only a probation period, that probation is void and the period counts as the labor contract term. Probation wages “must not be lower than 80% of the lowest grade wage for the same post at the employer or 80% of the wage agreed in the labor contract, and must not be lower than the minimum wage standard at the employer’s location.” If probation exceeds the legal limit and you actually worked, the employer must pay compensation for the excess period at the monthly wage standard after probation ends. During probation the employer may terminate only for the legally listed grounds and must explain the reason to the worker (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2007). 劳动合同法（第十九、二十、二十一、八十三条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: Three common violations: a 1-year contract with 3 months of probation; a second probation after transfer following regularization; and “no social insurance during probation”—social insurance obligations start from day one of work regardless of probation; employer duties are in Section 12, Item 16. Being dismissed in probation does not mean zero pay; it depends on whether the employer’s stated reason is one of the lawful grounds for termination. Unlawful termination is compensated at 2N per Item 6.

### 4. If you are laid off, calculate N first: one month’s wage per full year, half a month if under six months
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. Run the numbers once with a calculator; about ten minutes.
- In plain terms: When you are laid off, statutory economic compensation is calculated like this: one month’s wage for each full year worked; at least six months but less than a year counts as a year; less than six months gets half a month. Monthly wage here is the average over the 12 months before you leave, including bonuses, allowances, and subsidies. If wages exceed three times the local average monthly wage for the prior year, pay is capped at three times that average, and years of service count for at most 12 years.
- Benefit: Statutory economic compensation under the Labor Contract Law is what the employer owes when you are laid off—commonly called N. The formula: “one month’s wage for each full year of service … six months or more but less than one year counts as one year; less than six months, half a month’s wage to the worker.” If monthly wage exceeds three times the local average monthly wage of employees for the prior year, use triple that average as the cap, and count at most twelve years of service.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第四十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: Monthly wage means average wage over the twelve months before the labor contract is terminated. Bonuses, allowances, and subsidies all count—not base pay alone. That is what employers most often understate. Whether this sum is subject to personal income tax is in Item 18 of this section (tax on compensation).

### 5. If the employer dismisses you citing post-illness inability to work, incompetence, or major change of circumstances, and did not give 30 days’ prior notice, it must also pay one extra month’s wage
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost.
- In plain terms: If the employer dismisses you because you cannot work after illness, cannot perform the job, or circumstances changed substantially, it must give 30 days’ written notice. Without notice, it must pay one month’s wage on top of N. That is the common N+1. In negotiation, list that month separately; do not let the employer fold it into N.
- Benefit: The Labor Contract Law gives employers two options when terminating under this article. One is “notify the worker in writing thirty days in advance.” The other is “may terminate the labor contract after paying the worker one additional month’s wage.” If they want you out immediately, they pay an extra month. That extra month is commonly called payment in lieu of notice (代通知金), counted separately from N. That month is based on your prior month’s wage; N uses average wage over the 12 months before termination—the bases differ
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第三十六、三十九、四十、四十一、四十六、四十七、八十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>；国务院 (2008). 劳动合同法实施条例（国务院令第 535 号）第二十条：「用人单位依照劳动合同法第四十条的规定，选择额外支付劳动者一个月工资解除劳动合同的，其额外支付的工资应当按照该劳动者上一个月的工资标准确定」. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2008/content_1107604.htm>
- Notes: The extra month appears only in the three situations under Article 40 of the Labor Contract Law. Illness or non-work injury and, after the medical period, inability to do the original or reassigned work. Inability to perform after training or reassignment. Major change in objective circumstances at signing and failed negotiation. Other situations have no extra month. Economic layoffs (Article 41) require only 30 days’ notice to the union or all employees. Mutual termination proposed by the employer is N. Serious misconduct termination by the employer has no compensation. Unlawful termination is 2N. For the Article 40 three cases, keep N and the extra 1 separate in negotiation; do not let the employer count the extra month as part of N

### 6. For unlawful termination, damages are double the economic compensation standard
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. Arbitration takes several months. The hard part is staying with the process the whole time and not dropping out midway.
- In plain terms: If termination is unlawful, damages are double the economic compensation standard—commonly called 2N. Whether termination is unlawful depends on the facts. Ask legal aid or a lawyer first, then decide whether to negotiate with the company or go to arbitration.
- Benefit: The Labor Contract Law provides that where an employer unlawfully terminates or ends a labor contract, it “shall pay damages to the worker at twice the economic compensation standard under Article 47 of this Law.” The calculation is to compute one economic compensation amount under that standard, then pay double
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第八十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: This is the common 2N. What counts as unlawful termination depends on the facts. Consult legal aid or a lawyer first, then choose negotiation or arbitration; routes are in Section 7

### 7. Do not sign “resignation for personal reasons”—once signed, N usually disappears
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. The hard part is resisting pressure to sign on the spot.
- In plain terms: Signing that you resigned for personal reasons usually wipes out both economic compensation and unemployment insurance. Voluntary resignation is not among the situations that trigger compensation, except when you leave because of employer faults such as unpaid wages or failure to pay social insurance. You do not have to sign the same day; sleep on it.
- Benefit: The Labor Contract Law limits economic compensation to the legally listed situations. A worker who initiates departure is not included unless the reason is statutory employer fault such as unpaid wages or failure to pay social insurance. Signing “voluntary resignation” usually voids both economic compensation and unemployment insurance.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第三十八条、第四十六条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: A common employer line is “sign first to process exit; compensation comes separately.” If they mean it, put it in the agreement on the spot; otherwise do not sign. Not signing that day has no legal downside; sleep on it.

### 8. Before leaving, save pay slips, attendance records, the labor contract, social insurance records, and chat logs
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. About half an hour to archive materials. The hard part is remembering before you walk out.
- In plain terms: In dismissal or wage-deduction disputes the employer must prove its case, but for overtime you must prove first, and employers only have to keep wage records for two years. Once you hand back the computer and accounts, you cannot get these materials back. With pay slips, attendance, social insurance records, and original chat logs in hand, you have something to use when the employer denies liability.
- Benefit: Once you return the computer and accounts, these materials are gone for good. Labor disputes follow “who asserts must prove”: what you claim, you must prove. Where evidence is in the employer’s hands, the employer must produce it; failure to do so carries adverse consequences for the employer. In arbitration, if you cannot obtain employer-held materials, the tribunal may order the employer to submit them within a set time. For dismissal, termination, contract cancellation, wage reduction, and calculation of length of service, the employer bears the burden of proof. Overtime is different: you must first prove you worked overtime. If you show overtime records are with the employer and it will not produce them, adverse consequences shift to the employer. The employer is only required to keep wage payment records for at least two years. When paying wages, the employer must give you a personal wage breakdown. Without a written contract, wage payment records, social insurance payment records, and a work ID can all prove an employment relationship. Employment registration forms, attendance records, and testimony of other employees can also be used. Chat logs count as electronic data and can be evidence. Submit originals; printouts from original records still count as originals. In one Shanghai procuratorate case, a worker sought ten years of minimum-wage shortfalls. First- and second-instance courts recognized only the last two years before resignation. The courts held the employer need keep only two years of records, so the worker had to prove the prior eight years. He had ten years of pay slips and bank statements, but the employer disputed authenticity of the slips. In August 2024 he applied for procuratorial supervision. After the procuratorate protested, the employer paid 19000 yuan (1.9万) in a mediated settlement. He sued when the company cleared staff in 2021; by the supervision application about three years had passed (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2007). 劳动争议调解仲裁法（第六、三十九条）. <https://rsj.ankang.gov.cn/Content-2150407.html>（安康市人力资源和社会保障局转载）；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）（法释〔2020〕26 号，第四十二、四十四条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282121.html>；最高人民法院 (2019). 关于民事诉讼证据的若干规定（法释〔2019〕19 号，第十四、十五条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/212721.html>；劳动部 (1994). 工资支付暂行规定（第六条），黑龙江省人民政府「政策帮你问」引述. <https://www.hlj.gov.cn/hlj/c116413/202402/c00_31712524.shtml>；劳动和社会保障部 (2005). 关于确立劳动关系有关事项的通知（劳社部发〔2005〕12 号），黑龙江省人民政府「政策帮你问」引述. <https://hlj.gov.cn/hlj/c116413/202408/c00_31757488.shtml>；郭勇辉, 边琬茹 (2025). 两年之外的工资支付情况举证责任应由谁承担？检察日报. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/llyj/202508/t20250806_703164.shtml>（最高人民检察院网站刊载）；维权路径见第 7 节
- Notes: Save only your own employment-related materials. Do not take company source code, client lists, or technical documents—risks of taking them are in Section 11. For chat logs, do not rely on screenshots alone; keep originals on the phone and do not delete. Best to save pay slips and bank statements every month; if you sue years after leaving, the employer is not required to keep records from more than two years ago. That Shanghai case turned around because of a procuratorate protest; most people never reach that step. On exit, also obtain a separation certificate from the employer—see Item 17 of this section (separation certificate)

### 9. Before dusty, noisy, or chemical jobs, check the contract for hazards; the employer arranges and pays for three occupational health exams
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No cost. When signing, read the “occupational disease hazard disclosure” section line by line. Keep a copy of each exam report. The hard part is asking in person and getting the documents in hand.
- In plain terms: When signing the contract, the employer must truthfully state in writing what occupational disease hazards the post has, how to protect yourself, and what benefits apply. If it concealed them, you may refuse that work and the employer cannot fire you for it. Three occupational health exams—before starting, while on the job, and before leaving—are arranged and paid by the employer. Without an exit exam, the employer cannot terminate the contract with you.
- Benefit: The Law on Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases requires that when signing a labor contract, the employer explain four things. Those four are: what occupational disease hazards the work may cause, what consequences may follow, how to protect yourself, and what benefits apply. The statutory wording is to “truthfully inform the worker and specify it in the labor contract; concealment or deception is forbidden.” If you are later assigned hazardous work not disclosed in the contract, the employer must inform you again truthfully and renegotiate contract terms with you. If the employer fails, “the worker has the right to refuse work involving occupational disease hazards, and the employer may not terminate the labor contract for that reason.” There are three exams: pre-placement, during employment, and pre-departure. “Occupational health examination costs shall be borne by the employer”; the employer pays and must inform you of results in writing. Without a pre-placement exam, the employer must not assign work involving occupational disease hazards. Workers with occupational contraindications must not be assigned prohibited tasks. If health damage related to the job is found, the worker must be transferred from the original post and properly placed. **“An employer must not terminate or end the labor contract with a worker who has not undergone the pre-departure occupational health examination.”** On leaving, you “have the right to obtain a copy of your occupational health surveillance file; the employer shall provide it truthfully and free of charge, and affix its seal on the copy provided.” That occupational health surveillance file must be copied truthfully, given free, and stamped on the copy (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2018). 职业病防治法（2018 年修正，第三十三、三十五、三十六条）. <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/201711/t20171104_779851.html>（北京市人民政府转载）
- Notes: Three questions at interview roughly show whether the employer is serious: whether the post has occupational disease hazards, whether the contract states them, and who pays for the pre-placement exam. If they cannot answer or say “start first and we’ll see,” weigh the risk yourself. Exit exams are most often skipped: without a pre-departure exam, the employer cannot terminate or end the contract. That exam is also key material for a later occupational disease diagnosis; likewise get a stamped copy of the surveillance file before you go. Occupational disease itself follows the work-injury route; benefits start at Item 11.

### 10. Dust, noise, and chemical toxins often cause irreversible harm (for example pneumoconiosis and noise-induced deafness): the employer must supply protective gear, and you can refuse work with no protections
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No cost. The hard part is wearing particulate respirators, earplugs, and safety goggles consistently, and refusing unprotected tasks—coworkers may call you picky.
- In plain terms: Pneumoconiosis, noise-induced deafness, and chemical eye burns are all statutory occupational diseases. Once lungs or hearing are damaged, they cannot be restored. You have the right to know what hazards the job carries and to demand protective facilities and personal protective equipment from the employer. You may refuse work without protections. Pay cuts or dismissal for using these rights do not count.
- Benefit: The statutory list of occupational diseases, effective 1 August 2025, has 12 major categories and 135 conditions, including 4 open-ended clauses. The first category is occupational pneumoconiosis. It covers silicosis, coal workers' pneumoconiosis, graphite pneumoconiosis, carbon black pneumoconiosis, asbestosis, and talc pneumoconiosis. Also cement pneumoconiosis, mica pneumoconiosis, potter's pneumoconiosis, aluminum pneumoconiosis, welder's pneumoconiosis, and foundry worker's pneumoconiosis, plus one open-ended clause. Occupational ear, nose, throat, and oral diseases include noise-induced deafness and blast-induced deafness. Occupational eye diseases include chemical eye burns and welder's flash keratitis. Harm from pneumoconiosis and noise-induced deafness is permanent. The US National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (NIOSH) states that silicosis is permanent lung damage that progresses over time, with no known cure. A Lancet review states that after patients stop dust exposure, lung function can still worsen as the disease progresses. The US Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) states that noise-induced hearing loss is permanent and cannot be corrected by surgery or drugs. The law gives four rights you can cite on the spot. First, to know what occupational disease hazards exist in the workplace, what consequences they can cause, and what protective measures are required. Second, to demand qualified protective facilities and personal protective equipment and improved working conditions. Third, to criticize, report, and complain about violations of occupational disease prevention law and practices that endanger life and health. Fourth, “refuse unlawful orders and forced work without occupational disease protections”—you may refuse noncompliant orders and unprotected tasks. And “any reduction of wages, benefits, or other treatment, or termination or ending of the labor contract, because the worker lawfully exercised proper rights is void.” Pay cuts, benefit cuts, or dismissal for exercising these rights do not count (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
