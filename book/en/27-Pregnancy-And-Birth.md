# 27. Pregnancy and childbirth: from finding out you are pregnant to discharge and paperwork

The units mix two different yardsticks: the first half is about mortality (maternal and fetal), the second half is about money and time spent on paperwork, with no conversion between the two. This section only covers "points in time" and "what to do"; it does not cover prenatal nutrition, prenatal education, and similar topics that lack hard evidence or have tiny effect sizes. For maternity benefits, maternity leave, and the money side of raising a child, see Section 18; for care after the baby is born, see Section 20; for emergencies, see Section 13.

The World Health Organization's figures: in 2023, about 260,000 women worldwide died from pregnancy and childbirth, of which about 75% died from five categories — postpartum hemorrhage, infections, hypertensive disorders of pregnancy (preeclampsia and eclampsia), childbirth complications, and unsafe abortion — and "most maternal deaths are preventable, because the health-care solutions to prevent and manage complications are well known." The items with the largest effect sizes in this section are the ones that intercept these problems in advance.
### 1. Start taking 0.4 mg of folic acid daily when preparing for pregnancy, and keep taking it until the end of the first 3 months

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: a few dozen yuan per bottle; rural women can get it free through the national folic acid supplementation program. One tablet a day, and you have to remember to take it
- In plain terms: taking one folic acid tablet a day before and around conception cuts the chance of the baby having a neural tube defect (the anencephaly and spina bifida family) by roughly seventy percent; for those who have already had an affected child, the risk of it happening again drops to about a third of what it was.
- Benefit: Cochrane systematic review (5 trials, 6708 births, high-quality evidence): daily periconceptional folic acid supplementation, fetal neural tube defect risk RR 0.31 (95% CI 0.17 to 0.58), i.e. a reduction of about seventy percent; for those who have previously had a child with a neural tube defect, recurrence risk RR 0.34 (95% CI 0.18 to 0.64). Subgroup analyses showed no difference in effect between 0.4 mg and higher doses, nor between folic acid alone and a multivitamin
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: De-Regil LM, Peña-Rosas JP, Fernández-Gaxiola AC, Rayco-Solon P (2015). Effects and safety of periconceptional oral folate supplementation for preventing birth defects. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (12), CD007950. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007950.pub3>
- Notes: the key is "starting before pregnancy" — the neural tube closes within 28 days of conception, so by the time a test shows you are pregnant, most of the window has already passed. That is why you start 3 months ahead if you plan to conceive. In the same review, folic acid showed no clear effect on cleft lip and palate, congenital heart disease, or miscarriage — do not expect it to fix anything else

### 2. Set up the Maternal and Child Health Handbook at the community health service center before week 13, and use up the free prenatal check quota

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan, this is part of the national basic public health service program; it takes several trips according to gestational week
- In plain terms: at least five prenatal visits during pregnancy, plus a postpartum home visit and a 42-day postpartum check, are paid for by the state. Register at the community clinic before week 13 and get the Maternal and Child Health Handbook to unlock them; skip it and you lose them for nothing.
- Benefit: the maternal health management services under the national basic public health service specification (third edition) give permanently resident expectant mothers in the district 1 first-trimester health check, 2 second-trimester health checks, 2 third-trimester health checks, 1 postpartum home visit, and 1 postpartum health check at 42 days. Register and have the first prenatal check before week 13; the two second-trimester visits fall at weeks 16 to 20 and 21 to 24, and the two third-trimester visits at weeks 28 to 36 and 37 to 40. Locally published service lists also include a full set of initial lab tests and postpartum depression screening
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家卫生计生委 (2017). 国家基本公共卫生服务规范（第三版）. <https://www.nhc.gov.cn/ewebeditor/uploadfile/2017/04/20170417104506514.pdf>；靖江市人民政府. 国家基本公共卫生服务项目——孕产妇健康管理服务（地方服务清单公开）. <https://www.jingjiang.gov.cn/xxgk/zdlyxxgk/wsjk/art/2023/art_383017a2e7794892bc772bfbe042f379.html>
- Notes: these 5 visits are the free floor, not the whole of prenatal care; the hospital will add ultrasound, blood glucose, fetal heart monitoring, and other self-paid items as needed. The earlier you register, the earlier the pregnancy risk grading gets done, and only then can high-risk women be referred to a hospital equipped to handle them. If you live across district lines, register at your current address — there is no need to go back to your place of household registration

### 3. Have HIV, syphilis, and hepatitis B tested at the very first prenatal visit; free blocking treatment exists even if you test positive

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan, the program for preventing mother-to-child transmission provides free testing
- In plain terms: get HIV, syphilis, and hepatitis B tested at the first prenatal visit — the tests are free; even if something comes back positive it is not a dead end, the state provides the blocking drugs and protocols free of charge, which can push down the chance of passing it to the child.
- Benefit: the national work specification for preventing mother-to-child transmission of HIV, syphilis, and hepatitis B (2020 edition) requires that relevant information be explained at the first trimester or the first prenatal visit and that "appropriate, standardized free testing" be provided, with post-test counselling or referral according to the result. A positive result is not a dead end either: Article 44 of the State Council regulation on HIV/AIDS prevention and treatment stipulates that pregnant and postpartum women living with HIV be "provided free of charge with treatment and counselling for preventing mother-to-child transmission of HIV"; Article 43 requires the provision of blocking, treatment, postpartum home visits, infant follow-up, and testing services. If the mother is hepatitis B surface antigen positive, the newborn should receive both the hepatitis B vaccine and hepatitis B immunoglobulin at birth (see Section 20, item 2)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2020). 预防艾滋病、梅毒和乙肝母婴传播工作规范（2020 年版）. <https://wjw.xinjiang.gov.cn/hfpc/zhgl6/202012/08b7de496f0b40e586d4b99d00cf79c4.shtml>（新疆维吾尔自治区卫生健康委员会转载）；国务院 (2006). 艾滋病防治条例（第四十三、四十四条）. <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk_20040/zcwj2022/flfg/202304/t20230408_2992986.html>（北京市卫生健康委员会转载）
- Notes: the earlier these three are tested, the more the tests matter, because every intervention needs time. Article 3 of the same regulation also states that "no organization or individual may discriminate against people living with HIV, AIDS patients, and their family members", whose lawful rights in marriage, employment, medical care, schooling, and so on are protected by law. For everyday prevention and testing see Section 1, for post-exposure prophylaxis see Section 13

### 4. Avoid every cigarette and every drop of alcohol throughout pregnancy, and keep household members from smoking indoors

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan, it actually saves money; the hard part is getting everyone in the household to quit together
- In plain terms: smoking during pregnancy raises the chances of preterm birth, of the baby being born too small, and of cleft lip and palate, doubles the risk of abnormal bleeding during pregnancy and delivery, and raises the risk of sudden infant death as well. A family member smoking indoors counts the same. Whenever you quit, it still pays off.
- Benefit: the position of the United States Centers for Disease Control and Prevention: smoking during pregnancy means the baby "may be born too small even at full term" and "may be born too early", it "doubles the risk of abnormal bleeding during pregnancy and delivery", it raises the risk of birth defects such as cleft lip and cleft palate, and it "damages the tissues of the unborn baby's developing lungs and brain, and the damage can last through childhood"; infants born to mothers who smoked during pregnancy, and infants exposed to smoke after birth, face a higher risk of sudden infant death syndrome. The official advice is "the best time to quit smoking is before you get pregnant. But quitting at any time during pregnancy can still help" and "it is never too late to quit"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Smoking During Pregnancy. <https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/campaign/tips/diseases/pregnancy.html>
- Notes: secondhand smoke counts against the fetus too, so this item concerns the whole household, not just the pregnant woman herself. There is no known safe amount of alcohol; the simplest course during pregnancy is not to drink at all. For the toll tobacco and alcohol take on the adults themselves, see Section 2

### 5. If you have high-risk factors for preeclampsia, start one low-dose aspirin a day after week 12

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: a few dozen yuan; one tablet a day, taken until delivery
- In plain terms: if you fall into one of the high-risk groups, one low-dose aspirin a day from week 12 cuts the chance of preeclampsia by about fifteen percent, preterm birth by about twenty percent, and the chance of the baby dying in the perinatal period by about twenty percent. If you are not in these groups, do not take it on your own.
- Benefit: a Grade B recommendation of the United States Preventive Services Task Force: for women at high risk of preeclampsia, begin low-dose aspirin (81 mg/day) after week 12 of pregnancy as prophylaxis. Pooled effects: preeclampsia RR 0.85 (95% CI 0.75 to 0.95, 16 studies), preterm birth RR 0.80 (95% CI 0.67 to 0.95, 13 studies), perinatal death RR 0.79 (95% CI 0.66 to 0.96, 11 studies), small for gestational age / intrauterine growth restriction RR 0.82 (95% CI 0.68 to 0.99, 16 studies)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Aspirin Use to Prevent Preeclampsia and Related Morbidity and Mortality: Preventive Medication. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/low-dose-aspirin-use-for-the-prevention-of-morbidity-and-mortality-from-preeclampsia-preventive-medication>
- Notes: the high-risk list is a history of preeclampsia in a previous pregnancy, multiple pregnancy, chronic hypertension, pre-existing type 1 or type 2 diabetes, kidney disease, and autoimmune disease (systemic lupus erythematosus, antiphospholipid syndrome). If you do not belong to these groups, do not take it on your own — this is a prescription decision; lay out your history clearly at the prenatal visit and let the doctor judge. Domestic tablets are mostly 100 mg enteric-coated, so follow the doctor's instructions

### 6. Get screened for gestational diabetes at week 24 and after; do not skip it because the sugar drink is a nuisance

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: a few dozen to over a hundred yuan; several fasting blood draws, taking up half a morning
- In plain terms: after week 24, half a morning spent drinking a sugar solution and giving blood finds gestational diabetes. Leave it unfound and unmanaged, and the risks of a very large baby, difficult labor, ending up with a cesarean, preeclampsia, and newborn low blood sugar all go up.
- Benefit: a Grade B recommendation of the United States Preventive Services Task Force: screen asymptomatic pregnant women for gestational diabetes at week 24 and after, and a one-time screen should be done at week 24 or later. On screening before week 24, the Task Force concluded that the current evidence is insufficient to assess the balance of benefits and harms (Grade I). Unmanaged gestational diabetes pushes up the risks of macrosomia, difficult labor, cesarean delivery, preeclampsia, and neonatal hypoglycemia
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Gestational Diabetes: Screening. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/gestational-diabetes-screening>
- Notes: once found, most cases are controlled with diet and exercise alone; the minority need insulin. Do not skip this screen out of fear that a positive result means injections. Blood glucose should be rechecked after delivery — women who have had gestational diabetes face a markedly higher risk of type 2 diabetes later on

### 7. Memorize this "go to the hospital right now" list; it applies through pregnancy and for a year after delivery

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; five minutes to memorize, and it is best if everyone in the household knows it too
- In plain terms: if any item on this list shows up, go to the hospital the same day; do not wait at home through the night. Do not let your guard down after the birth either — the same items still count for a year after delivery.
- Benefit: the pregnancy and postpartum emergency warning signs listed by the United States Centers for Disease Control and Prevention: a headache that will not go away or keeps getting worse; dizziness or fainting; changes in vision; a fever of 38 °C or higher; severe swelling of the hands or face; thoughts of harming yourself or the baby; trouble breathing; chest pain or a racing heart; severe nausea and vomiting; severe abdominal pain that will not go away; the baby's movements stopping or clearly decreasing during pregnancy; vaginal bleeding or fluid leaking during pregnancy; vaginal bleeding or abnormal discharge after delivery; severe swelling, redness, or pain in one arm or leg. These problems can occur within a year after delivery — they are not something that "ends once the confinement month is over"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Hear Her — Urgent Maternal Warning Signs. <https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html>；World Health Organization. Maternal mortality fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/maternal-mortality>
- Notes: the value of this list is that it separates "endure it" from "must go now". A headache plus blurred vision plus abdominal pain points to preeclampsia; one-sided calf swelling and pain points to deep vein thrombosis (see Section 13); and by the World Health Organization's account, postpartum hemorrhage can kill a healthy woman within hours. If any item appears, go straight to the hospital — do not first wait at home through the night

### 8. If your water breaks, lie flat where you are, raise the hips, and call 120; do not walk around and do not bathe

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: your water breaking is not a moment to tidy up and set out at leisure — it means lie down on the spot, prop up your hips, and call 120. Standing and walking drains the amniotic fluid faster, and the umbilical cord can be swept down along with it; that is an emergency that can cost the baby's life.
- Benefit: the handling guidance of the Beijing Municipal Health Commission: once the water breaks, "immediately find somewhere to lie down flat, to keep the amniotic fluid from flowing out", "do not walk around at will", and "immediately dial the 120 emergency number"; while waiting for the ambulance, notify family members to bring the bags and meet at the hospital. Public health communication from medical institutions explains why further: in an upright position the amniotic fluid is lost faster, and the umbilical cord can slip down with it, causing umbilical cord prolapse — an emergency that endangers the fetus — which is why you should stay in bed with the hips raised, lying on the left side if possible
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 北京市卫生健康委员会. 每周急救话题：准妈妈羊水早破怎样办？ <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/bmfw_20143/jkzs/jzjj/202001/t20200115_1575632.html>；北京市顺义区妇幼保健院. 孕晚期在家破水怎么办？ <https://www.bch-syfy.cn/Html/News/Articles/9453.html>
- Notes: of the three signs of impending labor, regular contractions and bloody show allow time to prepare; a broken water is the only one of them that means "lie down on the spot and call for a ride". After the water breaks, bathing, squatting over the toilet, and driving yourself are all classic mistakes. Note the time the water broke and the color of the fluid and tell the doctor — amniotic fluid that is yellow-green or contains meconium particles suggests fetal distress. If it comes with regular contractions, a marked drop in fetal movement, or fever and chills, stress all the more that it is an emergency

### 9. Ask for an epidural outright if you want one; it does not raise the risk of cesarean

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: a few hundred to over a thousand yuan, prices vary by region; confirm with the hospital and the anesthesiology department in advance that it is available
- In plain terms: an epidural works better than other pain relief options, and it does not make a cesarean more likely; the claim that "an epidural leaves your back aching for life" has no basis either. It now has its own separate billing item, and hospitals are more willing to provide it.
- Benefit: the conclusion of a Cochrane systematic review is that epidural analgesia is more effective than non-epidural methods in relieving labor pain and improving maternal satisfaction with pain relief, and that "epidural analgesia had no effect on the risk of caesarean section or long-term backache"; the increase in assisted vaginal deliveries seen earlier is no longer visible in studies after 2005. From 2024, the pricing project establishment guide for obstetric medical service items of the National Healthcare Security Administration made "labor analgesia", "doula delivery", and "family-accompanied birth" separately priced items, precisely because the absence of an independent price item had suppressed hospitals' willingness to provide labor analgesia
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Anim-Somuah M, Smyth RMD, Cyna AM, Cuthbert A (2018). Epidural versus non-epidural or no analgesia for pain management in labour. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (5), CD000331. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD000331.pub4>；国家医疗保障局 (2024). 重构产科服务价格项目 助力构建生育友好型社会. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202406/content_6957553.htm>
- Notes: of the two most widely circulated claims — "an epidural leaves you with lifelong back pain" and "an epidural causes difficult labor and a cesarean" — neither finds support in the review above. The real side effects are hypotension, fever, leg weakness, urinary retention, longer first and second stages of labor, and a greater likelihood of oxytocin use. Whether to have one is your call, but do not give it up because of rumors. Whether it is available also depends on the hospital having anesthesiologists on site around the clock, so ask clearly at the prenatal visit

### 10. Do not request a cesarean without a medical indication, and do not schedule surgery to pick an auspicious date

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; the hard part is standing up to what the family says
- In plain terms: looking at whole populations, once the cesarean share goes above about ten percent, maternal and newborn deaths stop falling. A cesarean that is needed should absolutely be done; having one to pick a date or out of fear of pain just means going through a major abdominal surgery for nothing, and it affects the next pregnancy too.
- Benefit: the 2015 statement of the World Health Organization: at the population level, maternal and newborn deaths fall as the cesarean rate rises to about 10%; beyond 10%, there is no evidence that mortality improves further. The World Health Organization also stresses that every effort should be made to provide cesarean sections to women who need them — not to hit any particular rate
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: World Health Organization (2015). WHO Statement on Caesarean Section Rates（WHO/RHR/15.02）. <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-RHR-15.02>
- Notes: this item is not an argument against cesarean — with a proper indication it is life-saving surgery, so have it when it is needed and do not tough it out. What it argues against is cesarean without a medical indication: fear of pain, auspicious timing, calculating school-entry dates. The costs are on record: a cesarean is major abdominal surgery with a slower recovery, and in a subsequent pregnancy the risks of placenta previa, placenta accreta, and uterine rupture are higher — which directly affects whether you would want a second child

### 11. Confirm your maternity insurance enrollment status before pregnancy; an unemployed spouse can also claim childbirth medical expenses

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one call to the social insurance or medical insurance hotline
- In plain terms: when the employer pays into maternity insurance, the medical costs of childbirth and the allowance during maternity leave both come out of the fund; if the spouse has no job, the medical-expense side can still be claimed. Whether enough months have been paid in, and whether giving birth out of town requires filing in advance, must be asked ahead of time — afterwards it cannot be fixed.
- Benefit: Article 54 of the Social Insurance Law: where an employer has paid maternity insurance premiums, its employees are entitled to maternity insurance benefits; an employee's unemployed spouse receives childbirth medical expense benefits under state regulations, with the funds paid from the maternity insurance fund. Maternity insurance benefits consist of two parts: childbirth medical expenses and the maternity allowance. Article 56 lists the three situations qualifying for the maternity allowance (maternity leave taken by a female employee, leave for family-planning surgery, and other circumstances provided by laws and regulations), with the maternity allowance "paid according to the average monthly wage of employees of the employing unit in the previous year"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2010). 中华人民共和国社会保险法（第五十四、五十六条）. <https://guangdong.chinatax.gov.cn/gdsw/qysw_gkwj/2020-02/12/content_8e8c485d0db34b989531f3ba57cec5a9.shtml>（国家税务总局广东省税务局转载）
- Notes: two things must be asked in advance, because they cannot be remedied afterwards: first, whether the months of continuous enrollment meet the local eligibility threshold; second, whether giving birth outside the enrollment location requires prior filing for out-of-town delivery — without the filing you may have to pay out of pocket first and claim reimbursement manually afterwards, or be unable to claim at all. For the number of maternity leave days and how the maternity allowance is calculated, see Section 18, item 2

### 12. Get the Medical Certificate of Birth done before discharge; decide the name in advance and do not get the characters wrong

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; done in passing with the discharge paperwork
- In plain terms: this certificate is the prerequisite for household registration, insurance enrollment, and schooling, and only the delivering institution can issue it the first time. Going back for it after discharge means a separate process and extra paperwork. Check in advance that the characters of the name can be typed into the household registration system.
- Benefit: Article 23 of the Maternal and Infant Health Care Law: medical and health care institutions and personnel engaged in home delivery issue the uniformly printed Medical Certificate of Birth for newborns in accordance with the regulations of the health administrative department under the State Council. This certificate is the precondition for everything that follows — household registration, insurance enrollment, the medical insurance card, kindergarten and school enrollment all require it — and the first issue can only be handled by the delivering institution; applying again after discharge means going through a separate process and providing additional supporting documents
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (1994, 2017 修正). 中华人民共和国母婴保健法（第二十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647619.htm>
- Notes: before filling it in, check the parents' names, ID card numbers, and the child's name word by word. The characters used in the name must be typeable in the household registration system — rare characters cause endless trouble; changing the name or the certificate both require reissue or replacement, far more costly than checking on the spot. Once you have the certificate, store it separately and carefully; it ranks in the same tier as the household registration booklet and the ID card

### 13. Do not refuse either the newborn heel-prick screening or the hearing screening

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: free or a few dozen yuan in most regions, some extended items self-paid; one heel prick after birth
- In plain terms: one heel-prick blood sample and one hearing test can catch hypothyroidism, phenylketonuria, and hearing loss while the baby looks completely normal. By the time symptoms show, the intellectual damage can no longer be repaired.
- Benefit: Article 3 of the administrative measures for newborn disease screening: "The nationally screened conditions for newborns include congenital hypothyroidism, phenylketonuria, and other hereditary metabolic diseases of newborns, as well as hearing impairment." What these conditions share is that early on there is no visible abnormality at all; by the time symptoms become obvious the intellectual damage is irreversible, while early detection and early intervention (thyroid hormone supplementation, special formula, hearing aids or cochlear implants) work very well. Article 10 provides that when a screen is positive, the institution must promptly notify the guardian to come for diagnostic testing
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 卫生部 (2009). 新生儿疾病筛查管理办法（卫生部令第 64 号，第三、十、十一条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2009/content_1371363.htm>
- Notes: Article 11 of the measures requires that before screening, the items, conditions, methods, sensitivity, and costs be truthfully explained to the guardian and signed consent obtained — so you will receive an informed consent form; read it carefully before signing to see which items are the national ones and which are self-paid add-ons. A positive initial screen is not a diagnosis; the great majority are ruled out on retesting, so do not fall apart at this step. If the initial hearing screen is not passed, go for the rescreen as notified — do not decide for yourself that "the child can hear, so never mind"

### 14. Enroll the newborn in resident medical insurance as soon as you have the birth certificate; do not wait for household registration

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: one year of resident medical insurance premiums, several hundred yuan
- In plain terms: with the birth certificate in hand you can enroll the child online, without waiting for household registration. Once enrolled, hospitalization costs from the delivery stay can be reimbursed on the spot — one hospital stay for a newborn easily runs to five figures in yuan.
- Benefit: the position published on the Chinese government website in 2024: "a newborn can enroll online with just the Medical Certificate of Birth", without household registration first; "after the newborn is discharged, medical expenses can be reimbursed directly". The earlier practice was to enroll only after household registration, with hospitalization costs paid in full out of pocket first and claimed back later; the average processing time for enrollment and payment was also cut from 28.7 working days at the start of the year to 6.4 working days
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 中国政府网 (2024). 健全基本医疗保险参保长效机制国务院政策例行吹风会. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202409/content_6973567.htm>
- Notes: preterm birth, jaundice treated with blue light, and newborn pneumonia are common causes of newborn hospitalization, easily costing tens of thousands of yuan, so the difference between being enrolled and not is large. Local rules on "within how many days after birth one must enroll for coverage to start from the date of birth" are not entirely uniform; for the local implementation details defer to the local medical insurance department, and the easiest thing is to call 12393 before discharge

### 15. Register the birth at the local police station within one month of the child's birth

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one trip to the household registration window
- In plain terms: the law gives you one month, not "whenever I get around to it". Household registration gates the later vaccinations, kindergarten, schooling, and getting an ID card, and the longer you drag it out, the more paperwork you have to make up.
- Benefit: Article 7 of the household registration regulations: "Within one month after an infant's birth, the head of the household, a relative, the person supporting the infant, or a neighbor shall declare the birth registration to the household registration authority at the infant's place of permanent residence." This is a statutory deadline, not a suggestion. Household registration is the precondition for later vaccinations, kindergarten, schooling, and applying for an ID card, and the longer it is delayed, the more documents have to be supplied afterwards
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (1958). 中华人民共和国户口登记条例（第七条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/post_2531969.html>（广东省人民政府转载）
- Notes: bring the Medical Certificate of Birth, both parents' ID cards and household registration booklet, and the marriage certificate. Registration under either the father or the mother is allowed, but the policies of the two places differ (school catchment and insurance enrollment location are both affected), so think it through before going — changing it later is a hassle. Children born outside marriage can equally have birth registration declared

### 16. Do not skip the 42-day postpartum check; it doubles as the postpartum depression screening

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan, part of basic public health services; half a day of time
- In plain terms: this check-up is free, and besides looking at the wound, the uterus's recovery, and the pelvic floor, it includes a postpartum depression screening. Serious problems can appear at any point within a year after delivery — do not assume you are safe once the confinement month is over.
- Benefit: under the maternal health management services of the national basic public health service specification (third edition), the postpartum period includes 1 postpartum home visit and 1 postpartum health check at 42 days; in locally published service lists, this visit includes lab tests (complete blood count, routine vaginal discharge test) and 1 postpartum depression screening. Serious complications can occur within a year after delivery — "thoughts of harming yourself or the baby", "vaginal bleeding or abnormal discharge after delivery", and "severe swelling and pain in one limb" on the list in item 7 all belong to the postpartum period
