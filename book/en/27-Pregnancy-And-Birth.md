# 27. Pregnancy and childbirth: From confirming pregnancy to discharge and paperwork

The first half of this section measures death risk for the pregnant woman and the fetus. The second half measures money and the time spent running errands. The two accounts are not converted into each other. The content only covers “what to do when.” Things without hard evidence, or with only a small effect — prenatal nutrition, fetal education, and the like — are not written here. Maternity allowances, maternity leave, and the money side of raising a child are in Section 18; how to care for the child after birth is in Section 20; emergencies are in Section 13.

Figures from the World Health Organization: in 2023, about 260000 women worldwide died from pregnancy and childbirth. About 75% of those deaths came from five causes: postpartum hemorrhage, infection, hypertensive disorders of pregnancy (blood pressure that rises after pregnancy, including preeclampsia and eclampsia), complications during delivery, and unsafe abortion. The WHO also says that “most maternal deaths are preventable, because the health-care solutions to prevent or manage complications are well known.” The items in this section with the largest effect are exactly those that catch these problems early.

Items in this section are grouped by theme below; numbers in parentheses are the item numbers.

**What you can do yourself before and during pregnancy:** start folic acid when you are preparing to conceive (Item 1); no cigarette and no drink of alcohol (Item 4); you do not need to rehome the cat (Item 17); inactivated influenza vaccine (Item 18); from 28 weeks onward, lie on your side when you fall asleep (Item 19).

**Prenatal care and screening:** register for the Maternal and Child Health Handbook (Item 2); HIV, syphilis, and hepatitis B (Item 3); low-dose aspirin (Item 5); gestational diabetes screening (Item 6).

**Go to the hospital at once when something is wrong:** the “go to the hospital immediately” list (Item 7); if your water breaks, lie flat where you are (Item 8).

**During childbirth:** if you want labor analgesia, say so directly (Item 9); do not ask for a cesarean without a medical indication (Item 10).

**After birth:** the 42-day postpartum checkup (Item 16); breastfeeding is not the same as contraception (Item 20).

**Money and documents:** confirm maternity insurance (Item 11); the Medical Certificate of Birth (Item 12); heel-prick screening and hearing screening (Item 13); enroll the child in resident yibao (医保 — basic medical insurance) (Item 14); file the birth registration (Item 15).
### 1. Start taking 0.4 mg of folic acid daily before pregnancy and continue through the first 3 months
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 人群=怀孕 -->
- Cost: A few tens of yuan per bottle. Rural women pay nothing, as the national free folic acid supplementation program provides it. One pill a day; the real challenge is remembering to take it daily.
- In plain terms: Taking one folic acid pill daily around the time of conception reduces the chance of neural tube defects in the baby by about 70%. The national program recommends taking it from 3 months before conception through the first 3 months of pregnancy. Neural tube defects are birth defects such as anencephaly and spina bifida. For parents who have already had a child with this condition, the risk of recurrence drops to about one-third.
- Benefit: A Cochrane systematic review combining 5 trials and 6,708 births rated the evidence quality as high. Taking daily folic acid around conception reduced the relative risk of fetal neural tube defects to RR 0.31 (95% CI 0.17 to 0.58), a reduction of about 70%. For those who had already had a child with a neural tube defect, the relative risk of recurrence was RR 0.34 (95% CI 0.18 to 0.64), falling to roughly one-third. Subgroup analysis showed no difference in effect between 0.4 mg and higher doses. Taking folic acid alone versus multivitamins also showed no difference. The 2010 Ministry of Health management protocol for the folic acid supplementation program specifies free distribution to rural women planning pregnancy, to be taken "from 3 months before pregnancy through the first 3 months of pregnancy," providing 0.4 mg tablets. The US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention notes that neural tube defects occur in the first few weeks of pregnancy, often before you know you are pregnant.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:De-Regil LM, Peña-Rosas JP, Fernández-Gaxiola AC, Rayco-Solon P (2015). Effects and safety of periconceptional oral folate supplementation for preventing birth defects. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (12), CD007950. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007950.pub3>；卫生部办公厅 (2010). 2010 年增补叶酸预防神经管缺陷项目管理方案. <https://www.nhc.gov.cn/wjw/gfxwj/201006/22a0b4d4651e4b6d896453d443d9f79d/files/1733384846754_72260.doc>；Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. About Folic Acid. <https://www.cdc.gov/folic-acid/about/index.html>
- Notes: The key is to "start taking it before pregnancy." Neural tube defects develop during the first few weeks of pregnancy; starting only after a positive pregnancy test may already be too late. Therefore, if you are planning to have a child, start 3 months in advance as done in national programs. In the same pooled review, folic acid showed no clear protective effect against cleft lip and palate, congenital heart disease, or miscarriage—do not expect it to prevent other conditions. Pregnant women on a strict vegan diet should also supplement with vitamin B12 daily; see Section 20, Item 14 (Take vitamin B12 daily if you are vegan). If you are still deciding when to give birth, see Section 18, Item 7 (Having children a few years later) for the age trade-offs.

### 2. Register for the Maternal and Child Health Handbook at a community health center before 13 weeks of pregnancy to claim free prenatal checkups
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 人群=怀孕 -->
- Cost: No cost at all — this is part of the national basic public health service program. You’ll need to make several visits based on your gestational age.
- In plain terms: You are entitled to at least five free prenatal checkups during pregnancy, plus home visits after delivery and a 42-day postpartum checkup, all paid for by the state. To access these services, you must register for the Maternal and Child Health Handbook at a community health center before 13 weeks of gestation. Failing to do so means you lose out on all these free services.
- Benefit: The third edition of the National Basic Public Health Service Standards outlines specific maternal health management services available to all permanent residents who are pregnant. These include one health checkup in early pregnancy, two in mid-pregnancy, two in late pregnancy, one postpartum home visit, and one 42-day postpartum checkup. Registration for the handbook must be completed before 13 weeks of pregnancy, at which point your first prenatal checkup will also be scheduled. The two mid-pregnancy checkups occur between 16–20 weeks and 21–24 weeks respectively, while the two late-pregnancy checkups are scheduled for 28–36 weeks and 37–40 weeks. Local service lists also include a full set of initial lab tests and postpartum depression screening as part of these free services.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国家卫生计生委 (2017). 国家基本公共卫生服务规范（第三版）. <https://www.nhc.gov.cn/ewebeditor/uploadfile/2017/04/20170417104506514.pdf>；靖江市人民政府. 国家基本公共卫生服务项目——孕产妇健康管理服务（地方服务清单公开）. <https://www.jingjiang.gov.cn/xxgk/zdlyxxgk/wsjk/art/2023/art_383017a2e7794892bc772bfbe042f379.html>
- Notes: These five free checkups represent the minimum number of services provided; they do not cover all possible prenatal tests. Depending on your individual health needs, your doctor may recommend additional ultrasounds, blood glucose tests, or fetal heart rate monitoring, all of which will incur extra costs. Registering for the handbook as early as possible also allows for earlier assessment of your pregnancy risk level. This risk grading system categorizes pregnant women based on their individual risk factors, ensuring those identified as high-risk receive timely care at specialized medical facilities. Even if you live in a different district than your registered household location, you can still register at your current place of residence — there is no requirement to return to your place of household registration.

### 3. At the first prenatal visit, get the three tests for HIV, syphilis, and hepatitis B; if HIV is found, free prevention of mother-to-child transmission is available
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 人群=怀孕 -->
- Cost: HIV counseling and initial screening are free under the regulations. For syphilis and hepatitis B, national documents do not say they are free; ask the prenatal hospital whether they charge
- In plain terms: At the first prenatal visit, get the three tests for HIV, syphilis, and hepatitis B done. The initial HIV screen does not come out of your pocket. A positive result is not a dead end. If HIV is found, the state provides free treatment and counseling. If syphilis is found, treat early with penicillin. If hepatitis B is found, the newborn gets vaccine and immunoglobulin at the same time at birth, which blocks transmission from mother to child.
- Benefit: Mother-to-child transmission means the disease passing from mother to child. The Work Specifications for Preventing Mother-to-Child Transmission of HIV, Syphilis, and Hepatitis B (2020 Edition) set requirements. At the first prenatal checkup, the hospital must give relevant information, “provide appropriate, standardized testing, and provide follow-up counseling based on the results.” If the status is still unclear before delivery, treat the person as infected in principle. The specifications require that pregnant women found to have syphilis “receive standardized penicillin treatment as early as possible,” and that the newborn get preventive penicillin right after birth. For pregnant women positive for hepatitis B surface antigen, those who meet the criteria may receive antiviral treatment after informed consent. A positive result is not a dead end. Article 23 of the Regulations on the Prevention and Control of HIV/AIDS states that people who voluntarily accept HIV counseling and testing are “provided free counseling and initial screening.” Article 44 requires free “treatment and counseling to prevent mother-to-child transmission of HIV” for pregnant women infected with HIV. Article 43 requires services such as blocking transmission (keeping the disease from the child), treatment, postpartum visits, and infant follow-up and testing. When the mother is positive for hepatitis B surface antigen, the newborn must receive hepatitis B vaccine and hepatitis B immunoglobulin at the same time at birth (see Section 20, Item 2)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2020). 预防艾滋病、梅毒和乙肝母婴传播工作规范（2020 年版）. <https://wjw.xinjiang.gov.cn/hfpc/zhgl6/202012/08b7de496f0b40e586d4b99d00cf79c4.shtml>（新疆维吾尔自治区卫生健康委员会转载）；国务院 (2006). 艾滋病防治条例（第二十三、四十三、四十四条）. <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk_20040/zcwj2022/flfg/202304/t20230408_2992986.html>（北京市卫生健康委员会转载）
- Notes: The earlier these three are checked, the more useful they are, because blocking and treatment both take time. Article 3 of the HIV/AIDS regulations also states that “no unit or individual may discriminate against people living with HIV, AIDS patients, or their family members”; lawful rights in marriage, work, medical care, and schooling are protected. Everyday prevention and testing are in Section 1. Post-exposure prophylaxis after high-risk behavior is in Section 13

### 4. Do not smoke or drink at all during pregnancy, and keep smoking out of the house
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 人群=怀孕 -->
- Cost: Free; it even saves money on cigarettes and alcohol. The hard part is getting household members to quit too.
- In plain terms: Smoking during pregnancy raises the odds of premature birth, low birth weight, and cleft lip or palate, and it doubles the risk of abnormal bleeding during pregnancy and delivery. If the mother smokes, or if the baby breathes secondhand smoke after birth, the risk of sudden infant death is higher. Quitting helps at any point. There is also no known safe amount of alcohol during pregnancy, so do not drink at all.
- Benefit: According to the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), smoking during pregnancy can cause babies to be born "too early" or "too small, even if born full term." It "doubles the risk of abnormal bleeding during pregnancy and delivery" and increases the risk of birth defects such as cleft lip and cleft palate. It also "damages your baby's developing lungs and brain," damage that can persist into childhood. Babies born to mothers who smoke during pregnancy, as well as babies exposed to secondhand smoke after birth, face a higher risk of sudden infant death syndrome (SIDS). Official guidance notes that "it is best to quit smoking before you get pregnant, but quitting at any time during pregnancy can still help," and "it is never too late to quit." Regarding alcohol, the CDC states that there is "no known safe amount of alcohol use during pregnancy," and no safe time during pregnancy to drink. A baby's brain develops throughout the entire pregnancy, and exposure to alcohol can cause harm at any point.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Smoking During Pregnancy. <https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/campaign/tips/diseases/pregnancy.html>；Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. About Alcohol Use During Pregnancy. <https://www.cdc.gov/alcohol-pregnancy/about/index.html>
- Notes: Secondhand smoke inhaled by a pregnant woman affects the fetus just the same, so household members should not smoke indoors either. The same applies to alcohol: because no intake level is known to be safe, the simplest approach during pregnancy is not drinking at all. For the health balance sheet of smoking and alcohol on adults themselves, see Section 2.

### 5. Take daily low-dose aspirin after 12 weeks of pregnancy if at high risk for preeclampsia, as directed by a doctor
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 人群=怀孕 -->
- Cost: A few dozen yuan. Take the medication daily as directed by your doctor until delivery. The hardest part is remembering to take it every day.
- In plain terms: Preeclampsia is a serious high blood pressure condition during pregnancy. You are considered high-risk if you had it in a previous pregnancy, are carrying twins or multiples, have chronic high blood pressure, had diabetes before pregnancy, or have kidney disease or an autoimmune disease. For high-risk pregnant women taking daily low-dose aspirin from 12 weeks onward as prescribed, the risk of preeclampsia drops by about 15%, while preterm birth and infant death around the time of delivery each drop by about 20%. Anyone not at high risk should not take it on their own.
- Benefit: The U.S. Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF) issues a B-grade recommendation: start low-dose aspirin (81 mg/day) after 12 weeks of pregnancy as preventive treatment for individuals at high risk for preeclampsia. Preeclampsia is a hypertensive disorder of pregnancy. Pooled findings across studies show the following (95% CI represents the confidence interval): preeclampsia RR 0.85 (95% CI 0.75 to 0.95, 16 studies), a reduction of about 15%; preterm birth RR 0.80 (95% CI 0.67 to 0.95, 13 studies), a reduction of about 20%; perinatal mortality RR 0.79 (95% CI 0.66 to 0.96, 11 studies), a reduction of about 21% (perinatal mortality refers to death in the period immediately before or after birth); small for gestational age / intrauterine growth restriction RR 0.82 (95% CI 0.68 to 0.99, 16 studies), a reduction of about 18% (referring to fetuses smaller than expected for gestational age).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Aspirin Use to Prevent Preeclampsia and Related Morbidity and Mortality: Preventive Medication. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/low-dose-aspirin-use-for-the-prevention-of-morbidity-and-mortality-from-preeclampsia-preventive-medication>
- Notes: High-risk criteria include: a history of preeclampsia in a prior pregnancy, a multifetal gestation (twins or higher-order multiples), and chronic hypertension. Other high-risk factors include preexisting type 1 or type 2 diabetes before pregnancy, renal disease, and autoimmune conditions such as systemic lupus erythematosus or antiphospholipid syndrome. Do not self-medicate if you do not fall into these categories. This medication requires a prescription; disclose your complete medical history during prenatal visits so the doctor can make a proper clinical judgment. In China, it is predominantly sold as 100 mg enteric-coated tablets; take the exact dosage prescribed by your physician.

### 6. At 24 weeks of pregnancy or later, get one gestational diabetes screen; do not skip it because drinking the sugar water is a bother
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 人群=怀孕 -->
- Cost: A few dozen to a little over 100 yuan. You go fasting, several blood draws, and it takes half a morning
- In plain terms: Gestational diabetes is high blood sugar that appears only after pregnancy begins. After 24 weeks, half a morning of drinking sugar water and several blood draws can find it. If you neither screen nor manage it, the risks of a fetus that grows too large, difficult birth, a cesarean, preeclampsia, and newborn low blood sugar all go up.
- Benefit: The US Preventive Services Task Force gives a Grade B recommendation: screen pregnant people without symptoms (no felt discomfort) for gestational diabetes at 24 weeks or later. Gestational diabetes is high blood sugar that appears only after pregnancy begins. When only one screen is done, that one should be at 24 weeks or later. On screening before 24 weeks, the Task Force concludes that current evidence is insufficient to judge benefits and harms (Grade I). Untreated gestational diabetes raises the risks of macrosomia (a fetus that grows too large), difficult birth, cesarean, preeclampsia, and newborn hypoglycemia
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Gestational Diabetes: Screening. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/gestational-diabetes-screening>；Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Gestational Diabetes. <https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/about/gestational-diabetes.html>
- Notes: Per the US Preventive Services Task Force, after a diagnosis people usually start with exercise, diet changes, and blood sugar checks; insulin or oral drugs come only if sugar stays uncontrolled. Do not skip this screen because you fear needing injections. Recheck blood sugar after birth: the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention says about half of people who had gestational diabetes later develop type 2 diabetes

### 7. Memorize the "go to the hospital immediately" red flags—they apply during pregnancy and up to a year postpartum
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 人群=怀孕 -->
- Cost: Free. Takes five minutes to memorize, and anyone living with you should know them too.
- In plain terms: If any of these warning signs appear, go to the hospital right away—do not wait overnight at home, and remember this still applies for up to a full year after childbirth. Watch out for a headache that will not go away or keeps getting worse, changes in your vision, a fever reaching 38 °C, or severe swelling in your hands or face. Seek immediate care for trouble breathing, chest pain, a racing heart, severe belly pain that does not ease up, or if fetal movement stops or noticeably drops. Go straight in for vaginal bleeding or leaking fluid, one arm or leg that is swollen and painful, or thoughts of hurting yourself or your baby.
- Benefit: The US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) lists the following urgent maternal warning signs: a headache that will not go away or worsens over time, dizziness or fainting, changes in vision, and a fever of 38 °C or higher; severe swelling of the hands or face, thoughts of harming yourself or your baby, trouble breathing, and chest pain or a fast-beating heart; severe nausea and vomiting, severe belly pain that does not go away, and baby's movement stopping or slowing down during pregnancy; vaginal bleeding or fluid leaking during pregnancy, and abnormal vaginal bleeding or discharge after delivery; swelling, redness, or pain in one arm or leg. These complications can occur anytime up to a year postpartum—danger does not end simply because the initial month of recovery is over.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Hear Her — Urgent Maternal Warning Signs. <https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html>；World Health Organization. Maternal mortality fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/maternal-mortality>
- Notes: The purpose of this list is to clearly separate "wait and see" from "must go to the emergency room right now." A persistent headache paired with blurred vision and upper abdominal pain points toward preeclampsia (a pregnancy-related hypertension disorder). A single calf that is swollen and painful points toward deep vein thrombosis (see Section 13). According to the World Health Organization (WHO), postpartum hemorrhage can kill a healthy woman within hours. If any of these signs appear, go straight to the hospital—never try to wait it out overnight at home.

### 8. If your water breaks, lie flat where you are, raise your hips, and call 120; do not walk around and do not bathe
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 人群=怀孕 -->
- Cost: No money spent
- In plain terms: When your water breaks, do not pack slowly and then leave. The right move is to lie down where you are, raise your hips, and call 120. Walking while standing makes the fluid drain faster, and the cord may come out with it. That is an emergency that can kill the baby.
- Benefit: The Beijing Municipal Health Commission’s handling advice: after the water breaks, “immediately find a place to lie flat to stop the amniotic fluid from flowing out,” “do not walk around casually,” and “call 120 emergency services at once.” While waiting for the vehicle, tell family to bring things and meet at the hospital. Medical explainers add why: when a person stands, amniotic fluid is lost faster, and the baby’s umbilical cord may come out with it — cord prolapse, an emergency that threatens the fetus. So lie down, raise the hips; a left lateral position is fine
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:北京市卫生健康委员会. 每周急救话题：准妈妈羊水早破怎样办？ <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/bmfw_20143/jkzs/jzjj/202001/t20200115_1575632.html>；北京市顺义区妇幼保健院. 孕晚期在家破水怎么办？ <https://www.bch-syfy.cn/Html/News/Articles/9453.html>
- Notes: The three signs that birth is near are regular contractions, show (bloody mucus), and water breaking; water breaking is the one that means “lie down on the spot and call a car.” This is the author’s summary of the two explainers above. An explainer from Shunyi District Maternal and Child Health Hospital says getting out of bed to stand, walk, sit, or squat all make fluid drain faster and raise the risk of cord prolapse. So after the water breaks, do not bathe, do not squat on the toilet, and do not drive yourself. Note the time the water broke and the color of the fluid, and tell the doctor. Fluid that is light green, yellow-green, or even brownish yellow may mean meconium (the baby’s stool) is mixed in. If you feel a cord-like object hanging in the vagina, it may be cord prolapse — call 120 at once, lie flat, and raise the hips. If fetal movement is clearly different from usual, tell the doctor as well

### 9. Ask for labor analgesia — it does not raise the risk of C-section  
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 人群=怀孕 -->
- Cost: Several hundred to over 1,000 yuan; prices vary by region. Be sure to ask the hospital and anesthesiology department in advance whether this service is available. The main hurdle is simply bringing it up yourself.  
- In plain terms: Labor analgesia is a shot of anesthetic in the lower back that takes the edge off labor pain. It works better than other pain relief and does not make a cesarean more likely. The claim that “it leaves you with lifelong back pain” has no basis either. It now has its own billing item, so hospitals are more willing to offer it.  
- Benefit: A Cochrane systematic review found that epidural analgesia is more effective than non‑epidural methods at reducing labor pain and improving maternal satisfaction. Labor analgesia uses exactly this technique. The same review states that epidural analgesia has no effect on the risk of C-section or long‑term back pain. Instrumental delivery — using tools to assist birth — shows no increased incidence in studies published after 2005. Starting in 2024, the National Healthcare Security Administration introduced separate pricing categories for “labor analgesia,” “doula‑assisted childbirth,” and “family‑member labor support,” precisely because previously there were no dedicated rates to motivate hospitals to offer these services.  
- Evidence grade: A  
- Sources:Anim-Somuah M, Smyth RMD, Cyna AM, Cuthbert A (2018). Epidural versus non-epidural or no analgesia for pain management in labour. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (5), CD000331. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD000331.pub4>；国家医疗保障局 (2024). 重构产科服务价格项目 助力构建生育友好型社会. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202406/content_6957553.htm>
- Notes: Both popular myths — “epidural analgesia causes lifelong back pain” and “it makes a C-section more likely” — lack any scientific basis. Cochrane’s comparison of epidural analgesia with other pain‑relief methods confirms it has no impact on C‑section risk or chronic back problems. Real side effects may include low blood pressure, fever, weak legs, difficulty urinating, longer first‑ and second‑stage labor, and a higher need for oxytocin. The decision to use it is yours; don’t let unfounded rumors deter you. Availability also depends on whether the hospital has an anesthesiologist on duty 24/7 — be sure to ask during prenatal visits.

### 10. Do not request a C-section without medical need or just to pick an auspicious date
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 人群=怀孕 -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is standing up to family opinions.
- In plain terms: According to the World Health Organization (WHO), once a region's C-section rate exceeds 10%, there is no evidence that maternal or newborn deaths continue to drop. If surgery is needed, have it done without hesitation. But for women who have had a C-section, serious placental complications are more common in future pregnancies. Getting surgery just to dodge labor pain or pick a lucky calendar date is not worth it.
- Benefit: A 2015 WHO statement noted that as a population's cesarean section rate rises up to around 10%, maternal and neonatal deaths decrease. Beyond 10%, there is no evidence that mortality rates improve any further. The WHO also stressed that every effort should be made to provide C-sections to women in need, rather than striving to achieve a specific target rate. Another meta-analysis comparing cesarean delivery to vaginal birth found that in subsequent pregnancies, placenta previa, placenta accreta, placental abruption, and stillbirth were all more common after a C-section. These findings come from observational follow-up studies, and the authors noted that they cannot definitively prove the C-section caused these outcomes.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:World Health Organization (2015). WHO Statement on Caesarean Section Rates（WHO/RHR/15.02）. <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-RHR-15.02>；Keag OE, Norman JE, Stock SJ (2018). Long-term risks and benefits associated with cesarean delivery for mother, baby, and subsequent pregnancies: Systematic review and meta-analysis. PLoS Medicine, 15(1), e1002494. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1002494>
- Notes: When medically indicated, a C-section is a life-saving procedure—get it done without hesitation. What should be avoided is undergoing surgery without medical need, such as out of fear of pain, picking an auspicious hour, or timing a child's school enrollment cutoff. A cesarean section is major abdominal surgery. A meta-analysis comparing cesarean section with vaginal delivery evaluated what happens in subsequent pregnancies: placenta previa had an odds ratio (OR) of 1.74, placenta accreta had an OR of 2.95, placental abruption had an OR of 1.38, and stillbirth had an OR of 1.27. This directly matters if you plan to have another child. On the flip side, women who had a C-section experienced less urinary incontinence and pelvic organ prolapse later on. These findings all stem from observational follow-up studies, and the authors noted that they cannot definitively prove causality.

### 11. Before pregnancy, confirm your maternity insurance enrollment; an unemployed spouse can also claim maternity medical expenses
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 人群=怀孕 -->
- Cost: No money spent. One call to the social insurance or yibao (医保 — basic medical insurance) advice line
- In plain terms: When the employer pays maternity insurance, medical costs of childbirth and the allowance during maternity leave both come from that fund. If the spouse has no job, the medical-cost part can still be claimed. Whether you have enough months of enrollment, and whether birth outside the local area needs advance filing, must be asked early.
- Benefit: Article 54 of the Social Insurance Law: when the employer has paid maternity insurance premiums, the employee enjoys maternity insurance benefits. An unemployed spouse of the employee enjoys maternity medical expense benefits under national rules, paid from the maternity insurance fund. When the unit pays maternity insurance, the employee gets benefits, and medical costs when that unemployed spouse gives birth also come from the same fund. Maternity insurance benefits have two parts: maternity medical expenses — the medical costs of childbirth itself — and the maternity allowance — the money paid to you during maternity leave. Article 56 lists three situations for the allowance: female employees on maternity leave after birth, leave for family-planning procedures, and other situations set by laws and regulations. The maternity allowance is “paid according to the employer’s average monthly wage of employees in the previous year” — the unit’s prior-year average, not your own wage
- Evidence grade: A

### 17. Do not give away your cat during pregnancy: have someone else scoop the litter daily, cook meat thoroughly, and wear gloves in soil

Having a pet cat at home does not make infection more likely, so there is no need to give the cat away. The bigger risks are eating undercooked meat and touching soil without washing your hands. ### 18. Get an inactivated flu shot during pregnancy if flu season hits; get it at any stage, and avoid nasal sprays

- Cost: Out of pocket, tens to over a hundred yuan per dose. One trip to a community vaccination clinic. The tricky part is picking a brand whose package insert does not list pregnancy as a contraindication.
- In plain terms: Pregnant women who catch the flu are much more likely to be hospitalized than women who are not pregnant. Getting a flu shot during pregnancy cuts your own risk of flu hospitalization by about 40%. In the baby's first 6 months of life, confirmed flu cases drop by about half, and hospitalizations for flu drop by about 70%. Evidence shows no increase in miscarriages, stillbirths, or birth defects.
- Benefit: A 2022 World Health Organization (WHO) position paper synthesized available evidence. Pregnant women with influenza are more likely to be hospitalized than non-pregnant individuals, with an odds ratio of OR 6.8 (95% CI 6–7.7). A trial in Bangladesh randomized 340 pregnant women into two groups: one received the inactivated influenza vaccine and the other received a pneumococcal vaccine as a control, followed up to 24 weeks after birth. Among those receiving the flu vaccine, there were 6 laboratory-confirmed infant influenza cases versus 16 in the control group, giving a vaccine efficacy of 63% (95% CI 5–85). Maternal febrile respiratory illness was reduced by 36% (95% CI 4–57). Two placebo-controlled, double-blind trials in South Africa found that among HIV-uninfected pregnant women, confirmed influenza dropped from 3.6% to 1.8%, an efficacy of 50.4% (95% CI 14.5–71.2). In their infants, confirmed flu dropped from 3.6% to 1.9%, an efficacy of 48.8% (95% CI 11.6–70.4). Pooling 4 trials and 3 observational studies showed confirmed influenza in infants under 6 months was reduced by about 48% (95% CI 33–59). Four observational studies evaluating infant hospitalization for confirmed influenza found a reduction of about 72% (95% CI 39–87). Another meta-analysis pooling only two trials found a 34% reduction in confirmed influenza (95% CI 15–50), though there was no difference in "influenza-like illness," which refers to symptom-based cases without laboratory confirmation. For maternal hospitalization, records from four countries between 2010 and 2016 showed that vaccination was associated with an approximately 40% reduction in hospitalizations for confirmed influenza (95% CI 12–59). Regarding safety, a meta-analysis of 40 studies found an adjusted odds ratio for stillbirth of aOR 0.84 (0.65–1.08) and for congenital malformations of 1.03 (0.99–1.07), both within the margin of error; preterm birth was slightly lower at 0.87 (0.78–0.96). A US study evaluating three influenza seasons from 2012 to 2015 found an aOR of 0.9 (0.6–1.5) for vaccination within 28 days before miscarriage, showing no association. The Chinese CDC 2026–2027 technical guidelines designate pregnant women as a priority group, noting that vaccination at any gestational stage protects infants under 6 months of age, with greater benefits seen in late pregnancy. ### 19. Sleep on your side after 28 weeks of pregnancy — either side works, avoid sleeping on your back
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 人群=怀孕 -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is changing sleep habits if you are used to sleeping on your back.
- In plain terms: Falling asleep flat on your back after 28 weeks of pregnancy carries a notably higher risk of stillbirth than sleeping on your side. The left side and the right side are equally safe, so choose whichever feels comfortable. If you wake up in the middle of the night on your back, do not panic — just roll onto your side and go back to sleep. This link is based on mothers recalling their sleep positions after the fact, so it remains controversial.
- Benefit: Stillbirth here refers to fetal death after 28 weeks of pregnancy. A pooled analysis combined raw data from five studies: four in New Zealand, Australia, and the UK, and one international online survey. It included 851 pregnant women who experienced a stillbirth and 2,257 control women with healthy outcomes. The studies asked participants retrospectively how they fell asleep in recent weeks. Compared with falling asleep on the left side, falling asleep on the back had an adjusted odds ratio (aOR) for stillbirth of 2.63 (95% CI 1.72–4.04). Because stillbirth is rare, the odds ratio approximates relative risk, meaning the risk was about 2.6 times as high. Falling asleep on the right side had an aOR of 1.04 (0.83–1.31), showing no difference from the left side. The link remained similar among women with small-for-gestational-age infants, maternal obesity, or smoking. The authors estimated that if all pregnant women fell asleep on their side after 28 weeks of pregnancy, late stillbirths could drop by about 5.8% (3.2%–9.2%). The UK National Health Service (NHS) lists avoiding sleeping on your back after 28 weeks as one measure to reduce stillbirth risk. It also advises that if you wake up on your back, there is no need to worry — simply roll back onto your side to sleep.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:Cronin RS, Li M, Thompson JMD, et al. (2019). - Notes: Controversial. The numbers above rely entirely on recall after delivery. Mothers who lost a baby may remember things differently, as the authors acknowledge. There are also no randomized trials. A US study prospectively asked 8,706 first-time pregnant women about their sleep positions and followed their outcomes. Up to 30 weeks of pregnancy, sleeping on the back or on the right side was not associated with stillbirth, small babies, or pregnancy-related hypertension (aOR 0.99, 0.89–1.11). However, it only tracked sleep positions up to week 30, leaving the final weeks unrecorded. Because sleeping on your side costs nothing and causes no harm, the UK continues to recommend it. - In plain terms: You can get pregnant again as early as 3 weeks after childbirth, even if your period has not returned and you are breastfeeding. Breastfeeding only protects against pregnancy if all three conditions hold: exclusive breastfeeding, no period yet, and a baby under 6 months old. Conceiving less than 6 months after delivery significantly raises the risk of premature birth for the next child. The World Health Organization (WHO) recommends waiting at least 2 years before trying to conceive again.
- Benefit: According to the UK National Health Service (NHS), you can get pregnant as early as 3 weeks after childbirth, even while breastfeeding and before your period returns. Unless you plan to conceive immediately, use contraception every time you have sex. Relying on breastfeeding for contraception is known as the lactational amenorrhea method (LAM), which requires meeting three conditions simultaneously: exclusively breastfeeding, no return of menses, and an infant under 6 months of age. When followed strictly, it is over 99% effective. With typical use, about 2 in 100 women become pregnant within 6 months. You must switch to another method if any of the following occur: the baby reaches 6 months, you introduce formula, solid foods, or a pacifier, your period returns (even slight spotting counts), nighttime feedings stop, or feeding frequency decreases. A meta-analysis pooling 67 studies controlled for maternal age and socioeconomic status, using an interpregnancy interval of 18 to 23 months as the reference. For intervals under 6 months, the odds ratio (OR) for preterm birth was 1.40 (95% CI 1.24–1.58). The OR for low birthweight was 1.61 (1.39–1.86), and for small for gestational age, 1.26 (1.18–1.33). Intervals of 6 to 17 months and over 59 months also showed elevated risks across all three measures. A Canadian study of over 148,000 pregnancies compared a 6-month interval to an 18-month interval. For mothers aged 35 and older, maternal mortality or severe morbidity was 0.62% versus 0.26%, with an adjusted relative risk (aRR) of 2.39 (2.03–2.80)—roughly 2.4 times higher. For mothers aged 20 to 34, there was no significant difference (0.92, 0.83–1.02). For infants of mothers aged 20 to 34, the combined rate of stillbirth, infant death, extreme prematurity, or extreme smallness for gestational age was 2.0% versus 1.4%, with an aRR of 1.42 (1.36–1.47). A 2005 WHO expert consultation recommended waiting at least 24 months after a live birth before attempting another pregnancy. The experts unanimously agreed that intervals under 18 months should be avoided. After a miscarriage or induced abortion, wait at least 6 months.

- Notes: Disputed. Recommended spacing intervals vary. Among WHO experts, one group advocated 18 months while another favored 27 months; 24 months was chosen as a compromise. The NHS advises waiting at least 12 months. An Australian study using within-mother comparisons across 40,441 mothers reached a different conclusion: for intervals of 0 to 5 months, the OR for preterm birth was 1.07 (0.86–1.34) and for low birthweight was 1.03 (0.79–1.34), showing no significant difference. In contrast, between-mother comparisons showed an OR of 1.41 for preterm birth. This suggests part of the risk associated with short intervals may stem from underlying maternal characteristics. Contraception itself is uncontroversial; only the ideal interval is debated. According to the NHS, condoms and contraceptive implants can be used immediately after birth, including while breastfeeding. Combined hormonal contraceptives generally should be delayed until at least 6 weeks postpartum if breastfeeding.
