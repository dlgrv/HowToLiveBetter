# 33. How to live after disability

> Translator's note: The hotlines and institutions mentioned in this chapter are Chinese. 120 is the medical emergency number, 110 the police number, 12315 the market regulation and consumer complaint hotline, 12356 the mental health support hotline, and 12366 the tax service hotline. 残联 (残疾人联合会) refers to the China Disabled Persons' Federation, the organization representing persons with disabilities; its county-level branches handle disability certificates, subsidies, and rehabilitation referrals.

This section is about life after a disability has already happened. How to prevent disability is covered in Section 1 and Section 13, and it is not repeated here.

Four kinds of measures are kept separate and are not converted into one another. The emergency and complication entries are counted in mortality terms. The subsidies, benefits, and court-case entries are counted in money terms. The schooling and paperwork entries are counted in time terms. The psychiatric hospitalization, legal capacity, and guardianship entries are counted in personal freedom terms.

The World Health Organization's fact sheet gives three numbers. About 1.3 billion people worldwide live with significant disability — 16% of the world's population, or one person in six. People with disabilities die up to 20 years earlier than people without disabilities. People with disabilities are twice as likely to develop depression, asthma, diabetes, stroke, obesity, or poor oral health. A large part of these gaps is not caused by disability itself, but by being unable to see a doctor, go to school, find a job, or leave the house. So half of the entries in this section are about the body, and half are about paperwork and rights.

Five items already covered elsewhere are listed here by pointer only, without repetition. How to get a disability certificate, the seven categories, and grade 1–4 assessment: Section 24, item 11 (disability certificate). When to have a disability assessment done: Section 24, item 10 (disability assessment). How to claim the two disability subsidies: Section 7, item 8 (two subsidies). How work-injury capacity assessment and disability grade convert into money: Section 19, item 15 (work capacity assessment). How to apply for long-term care insurance for severe loss of self-care ability: Section 17, item 7 (long-term care insurance) — that entry sits in the "elderly at home" section, but this benefit has no age threshold, and younger people can claim it just the same.

For most entries in this section the beneficiary is you and your family, which falls into the two highest grades. Cases that involve other people are noted separately in the notes.
### 1. Sudden severe headache and sweating after a spinal cord injury: first sit the person upright, loosen clothing, and call 120 at the same time
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Three actions, done in a few dozen seconds.
- In plain terms: In a person with a spinal cord injury, part of the body's alarm wiring is cut off. A full bladder or tight clothing may go unnoticed by the person, while blood pressure shoots sharply upward. The signal is sudden severe headache and sweating. At that point first sit the person upright, loosen tight clothing, check whether the bladder is full, and call 120 at the same time.
- Benefit: This kind of episode is called autonomic dysreflexia. A systematic review searched four databases — Medline, CINAHL, EMBASE, and PsycINFO — and included 31 studies, of which 6 were randomized controlled trials. The review states that the non-drug management of an episode as it happens is three steps: place the patient in an upright position, loosen tight clothing, and remove the triggering stimulus. These three steps rest on clinical consensus and physiological data, which is level 5 evidence. Antihypertensive drugs are used only when blood pressure stays elevated; among them prazosin has level 1 evidence, and nifedipine and prostaglandin E2 have level 2 evidence. The review also states that strategies to prevent episodes mainly target the two common groups of triggers — the genitourinary system and surgery — and most of them are supported only by level 4 and level 5 evidence
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Krassioukov A, Warburton DE, Teasell R, Eng JJ; Spinal Cord Injury Rehabilitation Evidence Research Team (2009). A systematic review of the management of autonomic dysreflexia after spinal cord injury. Archives of Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation, 90(4):682-695. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apmr.2008.10.017>
- Notes: It is graded B because those three on-the-spot actions have no randomized controlled trials behind them, only consensus and physiological reasoning. The review itself says most of these measures are supported by uncontrolled studies only. The medication part is a doctor's business; do not give antihypertensive drugs at home on your own. Lying the person flat raises blood pressure higher, so sit them upright instead. Family members and caregivers should also know these three steps. Do not ride it out as if it were an ordinary headache.

### 2. The first ten years after becoming disabled are the high-risk window for suicide; do not treat it as something a "change of mindset" fixes
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. One phone call, or one doctor's appointment.
- In plain terms: In the first years after becoming disabled, an emotional collapse — in yourself or in family members — is common and is not a matter of willpower. Long-term follow-up at two British spinal cord injury centers found that the suicide rate in this group is five times that of the general population. More than nine in ten suicides happen in the first ten years after the injury. For people whose injury was itself caused by a suicide attempt, the risk is even higher.
- Benefit: The study followed 2,304 patients with traumatic spinal cord injury admitted between 1991 and 2010 who survived the first year after injury and had neurological deficits at discharge, with follow-up through the end of 2014. Of these, 63 cases (2.7%) were injured by a suicide attempt. By the end of follow-up 533 people had died; 4.2% of the deaths were suicides, and 91% of the suicides happened in the first ten years after injury. The age-standardized suicide mortality rate was 62.5 per 100,000 per year (95% CI 36.4–88.6, credible range), five times the 2014 suicide rate in the general population of England and Wales (12.2 per 100,000). For the subgroup injured by a suicide attempt, compared with other causes of injury, the odds ratio was 4.32 for death (P<0.001) and 9.46 for suicide (P<0.001)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Savic G, DeVivo MJ, Frankel HL, Jamous MA, Soni BM, Charlifue S (2018). Suicide and traumatic spinal cord injury - a cohort study. Spinal Cord, 56(1):2-6. <https://doi.org/10.1038/sc.2017.98>
- Notes: This study looked only at spinal cord injury; extending it to blindness, amputation, burns, and other causes of disability is extrapolation. What it does establish is the time window: the risk does not end at discharge — keep watch for ten years. For a person to talk to, call 12356 — see Section 29, item 11 (12356). Do not stockpile sleeping pills or pesticides at home — see Section 1, item 25 (suicidal thoughts). If grief or emotional paralysis is still stuck in place after six months, book an appointment in psychiatry or clinical psychology — see Section 29, item 8 (grief lasting more than half a year).

### 3. Psychiatric hospitalization is based on the principle of voluntariness; only two situations allow admission without the person's consent
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Remember the two situations so you can state them out loud when needed.
- In plain terms: A relative's objection or an employer's objection cannot get a person locked into a psychiatric hospital long-term. The law recognizes only two situations: the person has harmed themselves or poses that danger, or has endangered the safety of others or poses that danger. The first situation requires a guardian's consent before admission; a hospital may not admit when the guardian objects. If the person disputes the diagnosis, they can demand a second diagnosis within three days, and afterwards can commission an independent assessment themselves.
- Benefit: Article 30 of the Mental Health Law states that "inpatient treatment for mental disorders follows the principle of voluntariness." Inpatient treatment may be imposed only when the diagnostic conclusion and condition assessment show the patient is a person with a severe mental disorder and one of the following situations applies. First, "the person has already harmed themselves or poses a danger of harming themselves." Second, "the person has already endangered the safety of others or poses a danger of endangering the safety of others." Article 31: in the first situation, a medical institution may carry out inpatient treatment only with the guardian's consent; if the guardian does not consent, it must not be carried out. Article 32: in the second situation, if the person or the guardian objects, they may demand a second diagnosis, which must be requested within three days of receiving the diagnostic conclusion and issued by two licensed psychiatrists other than those who made the initial diagnosis; if they still dispute the second diagnosis, they may independently commission a medical assessment of a mental disorder from an assessment body with lawful professional credentials. Article 35: if the second diagnostic conclusion or assessment report shows the person cannot be confirmed as having a severe mental disorder, or the patient does not need inpatient treatment, the medical institution must not hospitalize them. Article 78: deliberately sending a person without a mental disorder to a medical institution for treatment as if they had one carries liability for compensation under the law (nationwide, in force from May 1, 2013, amended 2018)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 中华人民共和国精神卫生法（2018 年修正，第三十条、第三十一条、第三十二条、第三十五条、第七十八条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf7448a066d>
- Notes: The other side must also be stated clearly, otherwise this entry reads as "nobody can be taken to hospital." Article 28 provides that when a person suspected of having a mental disorder has already harmed themselves or endangered the safety of others, or poses that danger, their close relatives, employer, or local public security organs must immediately take measures to stop it and take the person to a medical institution for a psychiatric diagnosis. A medical institution that receives such a person must not refuse to make a diagnosis. So "being taken in for a diagnosis" and "being kept in for hospitalization" are two separate doors — the first one is low, the second is high. Article 75 lists the responsibilities of medical institutions: imposing restraint or isolation in violation of the rules, or diagnosing a person without a mental disorder as having one, leads to disciplinary action up to revocation of the practice license. What to do when someone nearby has urges to hurt others — see Section 8.

### 4. The person who cares for a disabled family member long-term needs someone watching their own health
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Hand part of the caregiving work to others and get a check-up yourself.
- In plain terms: A caregiver breaking down is not an act. An American study followed more than eight hundred older adults for four years. Among those who cared for a spouse with loss of self-care ability while reporting mental strain, the risk of death was about sixty percent higher than among people who did not provide care. Those who provided care but did not feel strained showed no such increase. So what needs watching is not the caregiving itself but the long-term strain that comes with it.
- Benefit: The study was a prospective population cohort, 1993 to 1998, average follow-up 4.5 years, conducted in four US communities. There were 392 caregivers and 427 non-caregivers, aged 66 to 96, all living with their spouse. Over four years 103 people (12.6%) died. After adjusting for sociodemographic factors, pre-existing disease, and subclinical cardiovascular disease, those who provided care while reporting mental or emotional strain had a 63% higher risk of death than non-caregiving controls (RR 1.63, 95% CI 1.00–2.65, credible range). For those who provided care without reporting strain (RR 1.08, 95% CI 0.61–1.90) and for those whose spouse was disabled but who did not provide care themselves (RR 1.37, 95% CI 0.73–2.58), adjusted mortality showed no increase
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Schulz R, Beach SR (1999). Caregiving as a risk factor for mortality: the Caregiver Health Effects Study. JAMA, 282(23):2215-2219. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.282.23.2215>
- Notes: Controversy: the lower bound of the study's credible range sits exactly at 1.00, which means the possibility of "no difference" is not fully excluded. The population is older Americans living with their spouse, and the care recipients were disabled elderly spouses; extending this to people caring for a young disabled family member is extrapolation. What it does establish is direction: the strained subgroup carries the higher risk. Delegate as much of the caregiving work as you can; ask the local China Disabled Persons' Federation (残联) and the civil affairs bureau about residential care and day-care services. Caregivers should not stop medication for their own chronic conditions — see Section 16, item 1 (take medication as prescribed, full course). The beneficiary of this entry is you yourself.

### 5. For long-term wheelchair users: switch to a pressure-relieving cushion and check the ischial and tailbone areas once every day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A pressure-relieving cushion runs from a few hundred yuan to over a thousand. One check a day, one minute.
- In plain terms: An ordinary foam cushion, pressed on for long enough, breaks the skin. Once broken, it heals slowly and gets infected easily. In a trial conducted in nursing homes, the group using pressure-relieving cushions had clearly fewer pressure sores in the ischial area. The wheelchair itself also has to be fitted to the person's measurements; a one-size-fits-all model is not enough.
- Benefit: This randomized clinical trial was conducted in 12 nursing homes and enrolled, between 2004 and 2008, 232 residents over 65 years old who spent more than 6 hours a day in a wheelchair and scored 18 or below on the Braden scale. Everyone was first fitted with a properly fitted wheelchair, then randomized into two groups. One group used skin-protecting cushions (air, viscous fluid plus foam, or gel plus foam); the other used 7.6 cm segmented foam cushions. Follow-up lasted 6 months or until a pressure sore appeared. For pressure sores over the ischial tuberosities: 8 cases (6.7%) in the segmented foam group versus 1 case (0.9%) in the skin-protecting cushion group, P=0.04. Counting pressure sores over the ischium and sacrococcygeal area together: 21 cases (17.6%) in the segmented foam group versus 12 cases (10.6%) in the skin-protecting cushion group, P=0.14 — no difference was demonstrated
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Brienza D, Kelsey S, Karg P, et al. (2010). A randomized clinical trial on preventing pressure ulcers with wheelchair seat cushions. Journal of the American Geriatrics Society, 58(12):2308-2314. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1532-5415.2010.03168.x>
- Notes: Two reasons for grading B. First, the sample is only 232 people, the primary endpoint's P value of 0.04 just cleared the bar, and the secondary endpoint showed no difference. Second, the population is very old nursing home residents; extending this to younger wheelchair users is extrapolation. In the trial everyone was first fitted with a properly fitted wheelchair, so "fit" and "cushion" work together — do not swap the cushion alone. For pressure sore prevention on the side of long-term bed rest, air mattresses and regular turning — see Section 17, item 8 (pressure sores). How to apply for the subsidy for basic assistive devices — see item 7 of this section (ask at the county China Disabled Persons' Federation about everything).

### 6. Do not buy "cures" and devices claiming to reverse paralysis, blindness, or deafness
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing — it saves money. The hard part is a family member who clings to hope and cannot be talked out of it.
- In plain terms: The Advertising Law says it outright. Advertisements for medical services, drugs, and medical devices may not guarantee therapeutic effect, may not cite cure rates or response rates, and may not have people give testimonials. So phrases like "stand up after three treatment courses" or "hundreds have already recovered" are themselves illegal advertising — there is no need to examine the theory behind them. Products that are not medical devices are not even allowed to mention treating disease.
- Benefit: Article 16 of the Advertising Law provides that advertisements for medical services, drugs, and medical devices must not contain the following: "(1) assertions or guarantees of efficacy or safety; (2) statements of cure rates or response rates; (3) comparisons with the efficacy and safety of other drugs, medical devices, or other medical institutions; (4) use of advertising spokespersons to make recommendations or provide endorsements." Article 17 provides that outside advertisements for medical services, drugs, and medical devices, "any other advertisement involving disease treatment functions is prohibited, and medical terminology, or wording that could easily confuse the promoted goods with drugs or medical devices, must not be used." Article 4 provides that advertisements must not contain false or misleading content, must not deceive or mislead consumers, and the advertiser is responsible for the truthfulness of the advertising content (nationwide, amended 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 中华人民共和国广告法（2021 年修正，第四条、第十六条、第十七条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8081817ab231eb017abd6bd860052d>
- Notes: There is only one test: see whether it dares to promise everything. The rehabilitation department of a proper hospital will not promise you will stand up after a set number of treatment courses. When you encounter this kind of promotion, report it to 12315 or the local market regulation authorities. Health supplements run on the same playbook — see Section 6, item 10 (health supplements). If you already bought something and want your money back, use the online-shopping and prepaid-consumption route — see Section 5, item 29 (online shopping follows the platform's rules). The beneficiaries of this entry are you and your family.

### 7. Once the disability certificate is issued, go to the county China Disabled Persons' Federation and ask about everything you can apply for in one visit
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. One trip to the county China Disabled Persons' Federation (残联), one morning of asking. More than one department will have to be visited later.
- In plain terms: The disability certificate itself pays nothing; it is a key. Attached behind it are several benefits that are independent of one another, and every one of them has to be applied for in person — none arrives automatically. On your first visit to the Disabled Persons' Federation, go through the whole list item by item; that beats making trip after trip.
- Benefit: At least six benefits attach to the certificate, each with its own legal basis. First, the living subsidy for poor persons with disabilities and the nursing subsidy for persons with severe disabilities — see Section 7, item 8 (two subsidies). Second, rehabilitation assistance for children with disabilities — see item 8 of this section (rehabilitation assistance). Third, the subsidy for basic assistive devices, based on Article 26 of the Regulation on Disability Prevention and Rehabilitation of Persons with Disabilities. Fourth, the subsidy for home accessibility modifications — see item 9 of this section (ramps, handrails, and bathroom modifications). Fifth, quota employment and employment services — see item 10 of this section (disclose the certificate proactively). Sixth, the personal income tax reduction — see item 11 of this section (personal income tax reduction). Article 26 of the Regulation on Disability Prevention and Rehabilitation of Persons with Disabilities further provides that the state, through key rehabilitation programs, provides basic rehabilitation services to poor persons with disabilities in urban and rural areas and to persons with severe disabilities, and "provides subsidies for basic assistive devices in accordance with relevant state regulations." Article 20 provides that relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level and the China Disabled Persons' Federation shall use community resources to set up rehabilitation facilities or purchase services, and carry out community rehabilitation work including rehabilitation guidance, daily living skills training, rehabilitation nursing, assistive device fitting, information consultation, and referral (nationwide, in force from July 1, 2017)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2017). 残疾预防和残疾人康复条例（第二十条、第二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-02/27/content_5171308.htm>
- Notes: For the certificate application process itself, see Section 24, item 11 (disability certificate). Subsidy standards and specific rules are set mostly at the provincial level; the same benefit can differ several-fold between two provinces. When asking, write down the exact name of each item, then at home look up the original document by that name on the local Disabled Persons' Federation and government websites — do not rely only on what the counter staff say. Do not expect one visit to cover everything; go back and ask again a year later, policies change.

### 8. If your child is under 7 and has a disability or autism, apply for rehabilitation assistance at the county China Disabled Persons' Federation
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Submit one application at the county China Disabled Persons' Federation (残联), then choose an institution from the designated list.
- In plain terms: The state has built a rehabilitation assistance system for children with disabilities and children with autism aged 0 to 6, covering surgery, assistive devices, and rehabilitation training. Assistance targets poor families first; where local finances allow, the age limits and family income conditions can be loosened. These years are the window when rehabilitation works best — do not wait until school age. Choose the institution from the Disabled Persons' Federation's designated list.
- Benefit: The State Council's Opinion on Establishing a Rehabilitation Assistance System for Disabled Children provides that assistance covers eligible children aged 0–6 with visual, hearing, speech, physical, or intellectual disabilities, and children with autism. It includes children with disabilities from families receiving the urban and rural minimum living guarantee (低保, dibao) or registered as impoverished households, children with disabilities taken in and raised by child welfare institutions, disabled orphans, children with disabilities covered by support for persons in extreme poverty, and children with disabilities from other families in financial difficulty. The specific determination rules for "other families in financial difficulty" are set by local people's governments at or above the county level. The document also states that "regions in a position to do so may extend the age range of rehabilitation assistance for disabled children and may loosen the restrictions on the financial condition of the assisted family." Funding standards are set by category by local people's governments at or above the county level based on local financial capacity, the number of people covered, disability category, and other factors, with a dynamic adjustment mechanism. The superior legal basis is Article 26 of the Regulation on Disability Prevention and Rehabilitation of Persons with Disabilities: the state establishes a rehabilitation assistance system for disabled children, "gradually ensuring that children aged 0–6 with visual, hearing, speech, physical, or intellectual disabilities and children with autism receive surgery, assistive device fitting, and rehabilitation training free of charge" (nationwide, from 2018)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2018). 关于建立残疾儿童康复救助制度的意见（国发〔2018〕20 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-07/10/content_5305296.htm>；国务院 (2017). 残疾预防和残疾人康复条例（第二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-02/27/content_5171308.htm>
- Notes: Assistance standards are set by each province; first look up the "implementation measures for rehabilitation assistance for disabled children" on the local Disabled Persons' Federation website. The list of designated institutions is also held by the Disabled Persons' Federation. The document requires the Disabled Persons' Federation, together with relevant departments, to strengthen oversight of designated rehabilitation institutions' entry and exit, establish regular inspection and comprehensive evaluation mechanisms, and set up a blacklist system. Institutions not on the list must be paid for entirely out of pocket, and when something goes wrong they are hard to hold to account. The beneficiary of this entry is your child, which falls into the second grade. Institutions that promise a "guaranteed cure" are handled per item 6 of this section (cures for paralysis).

### 9. For ramps, handrails, and bathroom modifications at home, you can apply for a subsidy from the government at or above the county level
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Applying is free of charge. The modifications themselves are subsidized in most cases, though you may have to cover part of the cost. You will need to visit the China Disabled Persons' Federation (残联) and the subdistrict office.
- In plain terms: The Law on Building an Accessible Environment contains a provision: governments at or above the county level shall support and guide home accessibility modifications and shall give appropriate subsidies to eligible households of persons with disabilities and older people. These programs are generally led by the Disabled Persons' Federation and run on annual quotas, so register early. How much is paid and which items are covered is decided locally.
- Benefit: Article 19 of the Law on Building an Accessible Environment: "People's governments at or above the county level shall support and guide home accessibility modifications. Appropriate subsidies shall be given to eligible households of persons with disabilities and older people." Article 18 provides that for existing residential buildings, residential areas, public buildings, public places, transport facilities, urban and rural roads, and other structures that fail to meet accessibility engineering standards, people's governments at or above the county level shall formulate targeted accessibility modification plans based on actual conditions and organize their implementation. Article 62 provides that any organization or individual has the right to submit opinions and suggestions on strengthening and improving the accessible environment to the relevant government departments, and to file complaints and reports about violations of this law; departments receiving complaints and reports "shall handle them promptly and give a reply." Article 63 provides that for acts violating this law that harm the public interest, the people's procuratorate may issue procuratorial recommendations or bring a public interest lawsuit (nationwide, in force from September 1, 2023)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 中华人民共和国无障碍环境建设法（第十八条、第十九条、第六十二条、第六十三条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff80818188d7430b0189018493370940>
- Notes: Graded B because the law only says "appropriate subsidies shall be given" — how much and who counts as eligible are left entirely to local authorities, with no unified national figure. First search for "accessibility modifications for households of poor persons with severe disabilities" on the local Disabled Persons' Federation website. Quotas are mostly allocated annually, so get in the queue early. Installing an elevator in an apartment block or modifying stairwells is a separate process that goes through the housing and construction department. If accessibility facilities in public places fall short of the standard, file a complaint with the competent department under Article 62 — it owes you a reply.

### 10. When job hunting, disclose proactively that you hold a disability certificate: hiring you lets an employer offset a payment
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. One extra sentence in the interview. The hard part is saying it.
- In plain terms: An employer's share of employees with disabilities must not be lower than 1.5% of its total workforce; the exact ratio is set by the province. Falling short means paying the disability employment security fund for the shortfall in headcount, with each person valued at the employer's average annual wage per employee from the previous year. So hiring you is not charity — it saves real money. State the certificate clearly in the interview; the other side can do the math themselves.
- Benefit: Article 8 of the Regulation on Employment of Persons with Disabilities: "The proportion of employees with disabilities arranged by an employer shall not be lower than 1.5% of the employer's total workforce. The specific proportion is set by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or directly administered municipality based on local conditions." Article 9: an employer that fails to reach the proportion set by the provincial people's government where it is located shall pay the disability employment security fund. Article 8 of the Measures for the Collection and Use of the Disability Employment Security Fund gives the formula: "Annual payment = (number of employees on the payroll in the previous year × the employment ratio for persons with disabilities set by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or directly administered municipality where the employer is located − the number of employees with disabilities actually employed in the previous year) × the employer's average annual wage per employee in the previous year." Article 16: for 3 years from the date of business registration, small and micro enterprises with a total workforce of 20 or fewer that have not reached the required proportion are exempt from the fund. Article 36 of the Law on the Protection of Persons with Disabilities provides that the state grants tax preferences under the law to employers that meet or exceed the required proportion of employees with disabilities, or that employ persons with disabilities in concentrated arrangements (nationwide, Regulation on Employment of Persons with Disabilities in force from May 1, 2007)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2007). 残疾人就业条例（第八条、第九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6646.htm>；财政部、国家税务总局、中国残联 (2015). 残疾人就业保障金征收使用管理办法（财税〔2015〕72 号，第六条、第八条、第十六条）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2015-09/15/content_5650063.htm>；中华人民共和国残疾人保障法（第三十六条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8080816f135f46016f1d134c88132b>
- Notes: For a small or micro enterprise registered within the past three years with 20 or fewer staff, this argument does not hold — do not count on it. Exemption and reduction rules for the security fund have been adjusted repeatedly in recent years; follow the Ministry of Finance's announcements for the current year. Also, quota employment requires genuine employment. Being on the payroll in name only while letting the employer use you to offset the fund is fraud, and you would be implicated too. For the other side — recognition as a person with employment difficulties and social insurance subsidies — see Section 7, item 12 (persons with employment difficulties). For vocational training subsidies, see Section 7, item 13 (vocational training subsidies). The beneficiary of this entry is you yourself.

### 11. Personal income tax can be reduced for persons with disabilities; call the provincial tax bureau to find out by how much
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Tick one box when filing, or submit filing materials once.
- In plain terms: The Individual Income Tax Law states that the income of persons with disabilities can be granted a tax reduction. But how much and for how long the law leaves to provincial governments, so it differs from province to province. Some provinces set an annual reduction cap, others reduce by a percentage. The way to find out is to call 12366 and ask the local tax bureau, or look for the reduction items in the Individual Income Tax app.

### 16. Do rehabilitation at a proper institution with a rehabilitation medicine department; let the therapist set the intensity — harder is not better
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Inpatient rehabilitation runs a few hundred yuan a day; the self-paid share varies by location. Time is counted in months. Practice is needed every day, and it is tedious.
- In plain terms: Taking the person home to lie flat and wait for natural recovery is the most common mistake at this stage. Organized multidisciplinary team care lets more people still be alive and still living at home a year later. But do not over-correct by adding intensity yourself. In a trial with more than two thousand participants, earlier, more frequent, and higher-dose activity within 24 hours after a stroke produced worse outcomes at three months. How much to practice and when — follow the rehabilitation department and the therapist.
- Benefit: A Cochrane systematic review included 29 trials with 5,902 participants, comparing organized stroke unit inpatient care with other forms of care. At the final follow-up (median 1 year), the odds ratio for poor outcome (death, disability, or need for institutional care) was 0.77 (95% CI 0.69–0.87, credible range), the odds ratio for death was 0.76 (95% CI 0.66–0.88), and the odds ratio for death or dependence on others was 0.75 (95% CI 0.66–0.85); the quality of the evidence was moderate. Converted to absolute numbers, for every 100 people receiving stroke unit care, 2 more survive, 6 more live at home, and 6 more are self-caring. On the other side, the AVERT trial randomized 2,104 patients across 56 acute stroke units in 5 countries, comparing "usual care" with "usual care plus very early, high-dose activity." At three months, good outcome (modified Rankin Scale 0–2) was 480 people (46%) in the very early group versus 525 (50%) in the usual care group; adjusted odds ratio 0.73 (95% CI 0.59–0.90, P=0.004). Deaths were 88 versus 72, with no difference demonstrated (odds ratio 1.34, 95% CI 0.93–1.93, P=0.113)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Langhorne P, Ramachandra S; Stroke Unit Trialists' Collaboration (2020). Organised inpatient (stroke unit) care for stroke: network meta-analysis. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 4:CD000197. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD000197.pub4>；AVERT Trial Collaboration group (2015). Efficacy and safety of very early mobilisation within 24 h of stroke onset (AVERT): a randomised controlled trial. Lancet, 386(9988):46-55. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(15)60690-0>
- Notes: Controversy: both pieces of evidence come from stroke; extending them to spinal cord injury, amputation, burns, and other causes of disability is extrapolation. What AVERT shows is that "the earlier and the harder, the better" does not hold — not that "early activity is useless." In the trial, the overwhelming majority in both groups were out of bed within 24 hours; what differed was frequency and total dose. When choosing an institution, check whether it has a rehabilitation medicine department and full-time rehabilitation therapists and occupational therapists — do not go by the word "rehabilitation" on the sign. For how referrals between primary-level and tertiary hospitals work and how the deductible is calculated — see Section 24, item 1 (stepwise referral). For the community rehabilitation and assistive device side, see item 7 of this section (ask at the county China Disabled Persons' Federation about everything).

### 17. When hearing declines, get fitted for hearing aids; the reason is to hear, not to prevent dementia
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Hearing aids run from a few thousand to tens of thousands of yuan, mostly out of pocket. Fitting is required, plus several adjustment visits.
- In plain terms: The claim "wearing hearing aids prevents dementia" has not held up so far. A three-year trial with nearly a thousand participants showed no difference overall. A difference was seen only in the subgroup already at higher risk of cognitive decline. So the reason to get hearing aids is to hear now — to talk with people and hear cars when crossing the street — not to protect your brain decades from now.
- Benefit: The ACHIEVE trial was conducted in 4 US communities and enrolled 977 people aged 70 to 84 with untreated hearing loss and no obvious cognitive impairment. They were randomized into two groups: one received a hearing intervention (hearing counseling and hearing aids), the other received health education as control, with follow-up every 6 months. The primary endpoint was the change in overall cognitive score over 3 years. In the primary analysis pooling the two source populations there was no significant difference between the groups: hearing intervention −0.200 (95% CI −0.256 to −0.144), control −0.202 (95% CI −0.258 to −0.145), difference 0.002 (95% CI −0.077 to 0.081), P=0.96. A prespecified sensitivity analysis showed the intervention effect differed between the two source populations (interaction P=0.010). Participants from the long-running observational Cardiovascular Health Study were older, had more risk factors for cognitive decline, and had lower baseline cognitive scores. The researchers' conclusion was that hearing intervention may reduce 3-year cognitive change in populations at higher risk of cognitive decline, but not in lower-risk populations. The trial reported no serious adverse events attributed to the intervention
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Lin FR, Pike JR, Albert MS, et al.; ACHIEVE Collaborative Research Group (2023). Hearing intervention versus health education control to reduce cognitive decline in older adults with hearing loss in the USA (ACHIEVE): a multicentre, randomised controlled trial. Lancet, 402(10404):786-797. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(23)01406-X>
- Notes: Controversy: the widespread online claim that "hearing aids cut cognitive decline by nearly half" comes from a subgroup of this trial, not its primary result — the primary result was no difference between the groups. This entry is here to separate "get hearing aids" from "prevent dementia": the former is worth doing, the latter should not be the reason. Candidates with hearing disabilities can be exempted from the foreign-language listening section of the college entrance exam — see item 13 of this section (reasonable accommodations). People with hearing impairment must wear hearing devices while driving — see item 15 of this section (C5).

### 18. Declaring an adult "unable to manage their own affairs" requires a court application; the family's word does not settle it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: A court application requires a litigation fee, generally a few dozen to one hundred yuan. The assessment fee is separate, usually a few thousand yuan, advanced first by the applicant. The whole process takes several months.

### 19. An adult's guardian is chosen by statutory order; when the ward injures someone, the guardian pays
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. If there is a dispute, it must be resolved at the residents' committee, villagers' committee, the civil affairs department, or the court.
- In plain terms: After an adult loses capacity, the guardian follows this order: spouse; parents and children; other close relatives; and finally other individuals or organizations willing to take the role. If a dispute breaks out over who serves, the residents' committee, villagers' committee, or civil affairs department of the person's place of residence designates the guardian, and the court can be asked to decide if the designation is disputed. Conversely, while a person is still of sound mind they can designate their future guardian in writing. When the ward injures someone, the guardian pays.
- Benefit: Article 28 of the Civil Code: adults without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity have the following persons with capacity for guardianship serve as guardian in this order: "(1) the spouse; (2) parents and children; (3) other close relatives; (4) other individuals or organizations willing to serve as guardian, provided they obtain the consent of the residents' committee, villagers' committee, or civil affairs department of the ward's place of residence." Article 31: where there is a dispute over who the guardian is, the residents' committee, villagers' committee, or civil affairs department of the ward's place of residence makes the designation; if a party disputes the designation they may apply to the people's court for designation, or apply to the people's court directly. In making a designation, "the ward's true wishes shall be respected, following the principle of what is most favorable to the ward." Where the ward's rights and interests are unprotected before a designation is made, the residents' committee, villagers' committee, a relevant organization designated by law, or the civil affairs department serves as temporary guardian. Article 33: "An adult with full civil capacity may, through prior consultation with close relatives or other individuals or organizations willing to serve as guardian, designate their own guardian in writing." Article 1188: "Where a person without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity causes harm to others, the guardian bears tort liability. If the guardian has fulfilled their guardianship duties, their tort liability may be mitigated." If the ward has property, the compensation is paid from that property, and the guardian covers any shortfall. Article 1189: where a guardian entrusts guardianship duties to another person, the guardian still bears tort liability, and the entrusted party bears corresponding liability if at fault (nationwide, in force from January 1, 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 中华人民共和国民法典（第二十八条、第三十一条、第三十三条、第一千一百八十八条、第一千一百八十九条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>
- Notes: The provision in Article 33 is voluntary (designated) guardianship. Choosing the person in writing while the person is still of sound mind avoids most later disputes; how to draft it is in Section 17, item 1 (voluntary guardianship). Be careful with the compensation part: handing the person to a care institution or a relative does not transfer the duty — liability remains with the guardian, and the entrusted party is liable only for their own fault. So buying guardian liability insurance for the ward makes sense. - Notes: The process costs must be counted first. First-instance ordinary procedure starts at 6 months and can be extended; the simplified procedure is 3 months. Lawyer fees are not among the litigation costs listed in Article 6 of the Measures for Paying Litigation Costs, and the "losing party bears the costs" rule in Article 29 does not include lawyer fees. So first take the two low-cost routes: complain to the human resources and social security department or the China Disabled Persons' Federation (残联), so the competent department orders a correction; and apply for legal aid at the same time — Article 42 waives the financial-hardship check for persons with disabilities. Getting evidence is the hardest hurdle. When you are rejected after an interview, the other side rarely leaves a written reason; record audio and keep chat records wherever you can. If you are already employed, follow the labor dispute arbitration route; on preserving evidence and limitation periods see Section 19, item 8 (preserve evidence before leaving the job). The beneficiary of this entry is you yourself.
