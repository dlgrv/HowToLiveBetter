# 26. Building a Website or Platform: Licenses, Filing, and Servers

This section answers one question: what kind of business, in legal terms, the website or app you want to build actually is, which license it needs, where the servers should be hosted, and who is responsible for what users post on it and the money it collects. The yardstick throughout is personal freedom and money: first the things that can run into criminal red lines, then those that require administrative licenses, then the platform's day-to-day obligations, and finally experience-based items — such as choosing a server — where only judgment, not rules, can be offered. The red lines for salaried technical staff are covered in Section 11, and how to register a company and file taxes in Section 12; this section does not repeat them. For a comparison table of the various licenses and a decision table for choosing a server, see [which licenses a platform needs](../../docs/en/做平台要办哪些证.md).
### 1. Don't collect money yourself and pass it on to sellers; route payments through a licensed payment institution that settles directly

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; connect to a licensed payment institution's split-settlement or escrow-transaction product, so that the buyer's money is settled directly to the seller and never lands in the platform's own account
- In plain terms: if the buyer's money first enters the platform's own account before being passed to the seller, what you are running is a payment business: this requires approval from the People's Bank of China, with registered capital of at least 100 million yuan, fully paid in. Doing it without approval means being shut down, confiscation of illegal gains, and fines from several hundred thousand to several million yuan, with the legal representative and directly responsible persons fined another 100,000 to 500,000 yuan; where it is treated as illegal business operation, serious cases get up to five years and especially serious ones more than five years. There is exactly one safe approach: the money does not enter the platform account.
- Benefit: the payment business is defined as "transferring monetary funds according to electronic payment instructions submitted by the payee or the payer"; setting up a payment institution "requires approval from the People's Bank of China and a payment business permit", with minimum registered capital of 100 million yuan in fully paid-in money capital; "whoever, without legal approval, establishes a non-bank payment institution without authorization, or engages in or engages in disguised payment business, shall be banned by the People's Bank of China according to law, and illegal gains confiscated"; where illegal gains reach 500,000 yuan or more, a fine of 1 to 5 times that amount is imposed concurrently; where there are no illegal gains or they are under 500,000 yuan, a fine of 500,000 to 2 million yuan applies; the legal representative and directly responsible persons are separately fined 100,000 to 500,000 yuan; the criminal law lists "illegally engaging in fund payment and settlement business" as illegal business operation, punished with up to 5 years for serious cases and more than 5 years for especially serious ones; under the interpretation jointly issued by the two Supreme bodies, the "serious circumstances" threshold is an illegal business volume of 5 million yuan or more, or illegal gains of 100,000 yuan or more (nationwide, from May 2024)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2023). 非银行支付机构监督管理条例（国务院令第 768 号，第二、六、八、二十七、四十七条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11086/202401/content_6924970.html> ; 中国人民银行 (2024). 非银行支付机构监督管理条例实施细则（中国人民银行令〔2024〕第 4 号，第六十八条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11546/202408/content_6970979.html> ; 全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百二十五条第三项）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml> ; 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2019). 关于办理非法从事资金支付结算业务、非法买卖外汇刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（第一、三、四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/201901/t20190131_407161.shtml>
- Notes: the term "erqing" (二清 — "second clearing", unofficial slang for a platform holding and re-paying out transaction funds without a payment license) does not appear in official documents; the implementation rules phrase it as "engaging in payment business in disguised form", meaning transferring monetary funds based on electronic payment instructions submitted by users without approval. The three situations enumerated in the two Supreme bodies' interpretation are cashing out via fabricated transactions, cashing out via corporate accounts, and cheque cashing out; platform collection-and-payment on behalf of others is not named, so this item is written strictly to the letter of the statute and does not claim it "definitely constitutes a crime". The safe approach is still only one: the money does not enter the platform account

### 2. Paid websites need a license, transaction-matching needs a different class of license, and free ones only need filing

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=多 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: filing is free; applying for a value-added telecom business operation license requires a company, with registered capital of no less than 1 million yuan for operations within one province and no less than 10 million yuan for cross-province operations; the review period is 60 days and the license is valid for 5 years
- In plain terms: a website that charges users counts as commercial and needs a value-added telecom business operation license: the applicant must be a company — an individual cannot get one — registered capital of no less than 1 million yuan for operating within one province and no less than 10 million yuan for cross-province operation, a 60-day review, and the license is good for 5 years. Free websites only need filing, and filing costs nothing. Operating without a license or beyond its scope means confiscation of illegal gains plus a fine of 3 to 5 times that amount; with no illegal gains, a fine of 100,000 to 1 million yuan; in serious cases the site is shut down.
- Benefit: "the State applies a licensing system to commercial internet information services and a filing system to non-commercial internet information services; no one may engage in internet information services without obtaining a license or completing filing"; commercial means "providing information or webpage creation and other such services to internet users for compensation over the internet"; online data processing and transaction processing business means "using various data and transaction/affairs processing application platforms connected to public telecommunications networks or the internet to provide users with online data processing and transaction/affairs processing via public telecom networks or the internet", and it "includes transaction processing business, electronic data interchange business, and network/electronic equipment data processing business"; operating without a license or beyond its scope brings confiscation of illegal gains plus a fine of 3 to 5 times that amount, or a fine of 100,000 to 1 million yuan where there are no illegal gains or they are under 50,000 yuan, with the website ordered closed in serious cases (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2000). 互联网信息服务管理办法（国务院令第 292 号，2024 年 12 月 6 日第二次修订，第三、四、七、十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1860864.htm> ; 国务院 (2024). 关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定（国务院令第 797 号，第十八项）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2025/issue_11786/202501/content_6997034.html> ; 国务院 (2000). 电信条例（2016 年第二次修订，第七、九、十三、六十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2016/content_5139478.htm> ; 工业和信息化部 (2017). 电信业务经营许可管理办法（工信部令第 42 号，第六、九、十一、十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2017/content_5240090.htm>
- Notes: no official sentence states outright that "an e-commerce platform with matching and funds processing must obtain an EDI license"; the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology's service guide only says to "apply for the corresponding telecom business operation license according to the business classification", and the basis for judgment is the business definition itself in the classification catalogue, so this item states only the definition and does not draw the conclusion for you. An individual cannot obtain this license: the applicant must be a lawfully established company

### 3. Livestreaming needs a network culture business license; the audiovisual program license is essentially out of reach for private companies

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=多 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: the network culture business license is approved by the provincial-level culture administrative department, with a decision within 20 days of acceptance; it is valid for 3 years and must be renewed 30 days before expiry; the audiovisual program license requires the applicant to be a solely state-owned or state-controlled entity
- In plain terms: running livestreaming, game commentary and the like — online performance — requires a network culture business license issued by the provincial culture department, with online performance written explicitly in the business scope; the result comes within 20 days of acceptance, the license lasts 3 years, and performance videos must be kept for at least 60 days. Doing it without approval brings an order to stop, a warning and a fine of up to 30,000 yuan; refusing to stop lands you on the cultural market blacklist. The audiovisual program license requires the applicant to be a solely state-owned or state-controlled entity, so private companies essentially cannot get it.
- Benefit: online performance business operators "shall, under the Interim Provisions on the Administration of Internet Culture, apply to the provincial-level culture administrative department for a Network Culture Business License, whose business scope must explicitly include online performance"; game skill demonstrations or commentary "shall be governed by reference to these measures"; all online performance video material "must be retained for no less than 60 days"; engaging in commercial internet cultural activities without approval brings an order to stop, a warning and a fine of up to 30,000 yuan, and inclusion on the cultural market blacklist for those who refuse to stop; providing internet audiovisual program services requires the Permit for Dissemination of Audiovisual Programs through Information Networks, for which the applicant must "have legal-person status and be a solely state-owned or state-controlled entity"; doing so without authorization brings a warning, an order to correct, and a fine of up to 30,000 yuan, with penalties under the Broadcasting and Television Administration Regulations in serious cases (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 文化部 (2016). 网络表演经营活动管理办法（文市发〔2016〕33 号，第二、四、六、十三、二十条）. <https://zwgk.mct.gov.cn/zfxxgkml/scgl/202012/t20201206_918194.html> ; 文化部 (2011). 互联网文化管理暂行规定（文化部令第 51 号，2017 年经文化部令第 57 号修订，第七、八、九、十六、二十一、二十八条）. <https://zwgk.mct.gov.cn/zfxxgkml/zcfg/bmgz/202012/t20201204_905340.html> ; 广电总局、信息产业部 (2007). 互联网视听节目服务管理规定（第 56 号令，2015 年修订，第二、七、八、二十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2022-08/23/content_5722664.htm> ; 国家网信办等七部门 (2021). 关于加强网络直播规范管理工作的指导意见（国信办发文〔2021〕3 号，第 9 项）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-02/10/content_5586472.htm>
- Notes: the seven-department document states the correspondence most clearly: platforms running commercial online performance need the network culture license plus ICP filing; online audiovisual requires the audiovisual program permit or completed registration; internet news information requires a news information service license. The text of the Interim Provisions on the Administration of Internet Culture reposted on the central government website in 2025 is the unrevised 2011 original; cite the current version published by the Ministry of Culture and Tourism

### 4. Servers inside mainland China require filing, and the access provider must itself hold a value-added telecom license

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: filing is free, submitted with the server at the provider, usually taking a few days to a few weeks; checking a provider's credentials takes ten minutes
- In plain terms: a server inside mainland China means filing, which is free, submitted through the provider along with the server, and usually takes a few days to a few weeks. Operating without filing brings an order to correct within a time limit plus a 10,000 yuan fine, and site closure if you don't; not displaying the filing number on the homepage means another fine of 5,000 to 50,000 yuan. Data-center hosting, cloud hosting, and broadband access are themselves all licensed businesses, so check whether the provider holds a license in the MIIT system before discussing price. Moving the machines abroad sidesteps filing, but the users and the money are still inside mainland China, so every obligation in the items that follow still applies in full — plus an extra layer of data-export cost.
- Benefit: "without filing, no one may provide non-commercial internet information services within the People's Republic of China"; operating without filing brings an order to correct within a time limit plus a 10,000 yuan fine, and closure of the website for refusal to correct; "internet access service providers may not provide internet access services to organizations or individuals that have not filed and provide non-commercial internet information services", and value-added telecom operators providing access services "may not provide access, collection on behalf of others, or similar services to entities or individuals that have not lawfully obtained an operating license or completed filing for non-commercial internet information services"; data-center hosting and cloud hosting (internet data center business) and broadband access (internet access service business) are themselves value-added telecom businesses requiring licenses; a website homepage that does not display its filing number brings an order to correct plus a fine of 5,000 to 50,000 yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 信息产业部 (2005). 非经营性互联网信息服务备案管理办法（信产部令第 33 号，2024 年经工信部令第 68 号修订，第五、十三、十八、二十二条）. <https://www.miit.gov.cn/gyhxxhb/jgsj/cyzcyfgs/bmgz/xxtxl/art/2024/art_84a0cfa0ebd049bbbe751dca9a008e56.html> ; 工业和信息化部 (2017). 电信业务经营许可管理办法（工信部令第 42 号，第二十、二十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2017/content_5240090.htm> ; 工业和信息化部 (2015). 电信业务分类目录（2015 年版，B11、B14、B21、B25）. <https://www.miit.gov.cn/zwgk/zcwj/wjfb/tg/art/2020/art_e98406cd89844f7e92ea1bcf3b5301e0.html>
- Notes: to check whether a provider holds a license, use the MIIT's comprehensive telecom business market management information system at tsm.miit.gov.cn, searching by the company's full name or license number; for website filing checks use beian.miit.gov.cn. Verify the license before talking price — if an unlicensed "data center" has an incident, you won't even have anyone to appeal to. Putting machines abroad sidesteps filing, but with users in mainland China and money in mainland China, none of the obligations in items 5 through 10 of this section goes away, and there is an added layer of data-export cost

### 5. Once users sell things on your platform, you must verify and register them, report information, and keep records for three years

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; the back end must handle credential verification and registration files, report tax-related information quarterly, and keep transaction data for three years
- In plain terms: letting others come and sell on your platform means verifying and registering each seller's identity, address, contact details and administrative licenses, building files that are re-checked and updated at least once every six months, reporting identity information to market regulation authorities and tax-related information to the tax authorities quarterly, and keeping product and transaction information for three years. Missing any one of these brings an order to correct first, then a fine of 20,000 to 100,000 yuan if the deadline passes, and in serious cases suspension of business for rectification plus a fine of 100,000 to 500,000 yuan.
- Benefit: platforms "shall require operators applying to sell goods or provide services on the platform to submit true information on their identity, address, contact details, administrative licenses and so on, verify and register it, establish registration files, and verify and update them regularly", which departmental rules refine as "at least once every six months"; the platform must report the identity information of on-platform operators to the market regulation department and "report the identity information and tax-related information of on-platform operators to the tax authorities in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations on tax collection and administration"; product and service information and transaction information "must be kept for no less than three years from the date the transaction is completed"; violating any of the above brings an order to correct within a time limit, a fine of 20,000 to 100,000 yuan for missing the deadline, and in serious cases an order to suspend business for rectification plus a fine of 100,000 to 500,000 yuan; tax-related information must be reported "within the month following the end of each quarter", with the same penalties for late reporting (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 电子商务法（第十、二十七、二十八、三十一、八十条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf8af050b81> ; 市场监管总局 (2021). 网络交易监督管理办法（市场监管总局令第 37 号，第八、二十四、二十五、三十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5602020.htm> ; 国务院 (2025). 互联网平台企业涉税信息报送规定（国务院令第 810 号，第二、三、四、十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2025/issue_12146/202507/content_7030983.html> ; 国家税务总局 (2025). 关于互联网平台企业报送涉税信息有关事项的公告（税务总局公告 2025 年第 15 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202506/content_7029727.htm>
- Notes: sellers also carry registration obligations; the statutory exceptions are "individuals selling self-produced agricultural and sideline products or household handicrafts, individuals using their own skills to provide convenience services that lawfully require no license, and occasional small-value transactions"; the quantitative threshold for occasional small-value transactions is in Article 8 of the network transaction supervision and administration measures — annual transaction volume of 100,000 yuan. The platform must also distinguish and label registered and unregistered operators, and must not delete consumer reviews

### 6. You are responsible for what users post: moderation mechanisms, a complaint entry point, immediate suspension and reporting on discovering illegal content

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: moderation staffing and mechanisms are the platform's largest long-term expense; the complaint entry point and disposition records must all leave an audit trail
- In plain terms: content posted by users is the platform's responsibility to manage: you must build the whole set of mechanisms — registration, review before publishing, comment review, real-time patrols, emergency response — put a complaint entry point in a prominent position, and upon discovering illegal information immediately stop its transmission, keep records, and report to the competent authorities. Failing to do this brings an order to correct, a public warning and a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 yuan; refusing to correct or serious cases mean 500,000 to 2 million yuan, plus possible suspension for rectification, site closure, or license revocation, with the directly responsible person fined another 50,000 to 200,000 yuan. Moderation staffing is the largest ongoing expense.
- Benefit: platforms "shall bear primary responsibility for information content management", "shall improve the systems for user registration, account management, review of published information, review of comments and replies, management of page and layout ecology, real-time patrols, emergency response, and handling of internet rumors and black-industry-chain information", and "shall set up a convenient complaint and reporting entry point in a prominent position"; those discovering illegal information "shall immediately take disposition measures according to law, preserve relevant records, and report to the relevant competent authorities"; the Cybersecurity Law requires that on "discovering information prohibited from publication or transmission by laws or administrative regulations, [an operator] shall immediately stop transmitting that information, take elimination and other disposition measures to prevent its spread, preserve relevant records, and report to the relevant competent authorities"; failure brings an order to correct, a warning, public notification, and a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 yuan; refusal to correct or serious circumstances bring 500,000 to 2 million yuan, plus possible orders to suspend business for rectification, close the website or app, or revoke the license or business registration, with the directly responsible person fined 50,000 to 200,000 yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家互联网信息办公室 (2019). 网络信息内容生态治理规定（网信办令第 5 号，第八、九、十、十六、三十七条）. <http://www.cac.gov.cn/2019-12/20/c_1578375159509309.htm> ; 全国人大常委会 (2025). 网络安全法（2025 年修正，2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第四十九、六十九条）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-12/29/c_1768735112911946.htm> ; 国务院 (2000). 互联网信息服务管理办法（第十五、十六、二十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1860864.htm>
- Notes: the nine-item list of prohibited content is in Article 15 of the internet information service management measures; write your moderation rules against it. Livestreaming platforms must also "establish a livestream content review platform" and have "the technical capability to block internet livestreams in real time"; livestream content and logs are kept for 60 days, and online performance video material likewise for no less than 60 days

### 7. Services that publish information or carry instant messaging must require users to provide real identity information

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: connecting to a real-name verification channel charges per check; design the product on the principle of "real names in the back end, free choice in the front end"
- In plain terms: if you offer information publishing, instant messaging and the like, require users to provide real identity information when signing the agreement or activating the service, and don't serve those who won't. Design the product with real names in the back end and free choice on the front end, verifying by mobile phone number or similar means. Skipping real-name checks — or serving unverified users anyway — brings an order to correct first; refusing, or serious cases, mean a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 yuan, possible suspension of the relevant business, suspension for rectification, site closure or license revocation, and the directly responsible person fined another 10,000 to 100,000 yuan.
- Benefit: "when a network operator handles network access or domain name registration for users, handles subscription procedures for landline or mobile telephone services, or provides users with services such as information publishing or instant messaging, it shall require users to provide real identity information when signing the agreement or confirming provision of the service. Where a user does not provide real identity information, the network operator shall not provide the relevant services"; failing to require real-name verification, or still providing services to users who do not provide real identity information, brings an order to correct, and a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 yuan for refusal to correct or serious circumstances, plus possible orders to suspend the relevant business, suspend operations for rectification, close the website or app, or revoke the license or business registration, with the directly responsible person fined 10,000 to 100,000 yuan; livestreaming services verify "real identity information based on mobile phone numbers and other such means" under the principle of "real names in the back end, free choice in the front end"; on the livestream e-commerce side the verification standard is "real identity information authentication based on ID card information, unified social credit code, and the like" (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025). 网络安全法（2025 年修正，2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第二十六、六十四条；2016 年文本为第二十四、六十一条）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-12/29/c_1768735112911946.htm> ; 国家互联网信息办公室 (2016). 互联网直播服务管理规定（第十二、十六条）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2016-11/04/c_1119847629.htm> ; 国家网信办等七部门 (2021). 网络直播营销管理办法（试行）（国信办发文〔2021〕5 号，第八条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-04/23/content_5601682.htm>
- Notes: the Cybersecurity Law was amended in 2025 and takes effect on January 1, 2026; the real-name article moved from Article 24 to Article 26 and the penalty provision from Article 61 to Article 64, with the wording unchanged — update materials citing the old article numbers

### 8. Do not open livestream accounts to anyone under 16, and handle tipping in age-based tiers

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; add an age check and guardian-consent flow at registration and payment
- In plain terms: people under 16 cannot register as livestream publisher accounts; those 16 and older need identity verification and consent from a parent or other guardian. Tipping is now split into three tiers by age: no tipping under 8, guardian consent from 8 to 16, and guardian consent or verified proof of income above 16. If a child with limited civil capacity tips without guardian consent and the guardian asks for the money back, the courts will support the claim.
- Benefit: "providers of internet livestreaming services shall not provide livestream publisher account registration services to minors under the age of sixteen; when providing such registration to minors aged sixteen or above, they shall verify the minor's identity information and obtain the consent of the minor's parents or other guardians"; the regulations require "establishing a dynamic verification mechanism for the real identity information of livestream publishers" and "reasonably limiting the per-transaction and per-day cumulative spending amounts of minors of different age groups when using their services"; the current tipping tiers are "tipping services shall not be provided to minors under the age of eight; tipping services may be provided to minors aged eight to sixteen with the consent of their guardian; tipping services may be provided to minors aged sixteen or above with the consent of their guardian or after verifying their proof-of-income documents"; where a person with limited civil capacity tips without guardian consent and the guardian requests restitution, the courts shall support the request (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 未成年人保护法（第七十四、七十六条）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2020-10/22/c_1604928959588622.htm> ; 国务院 (2023). 未成年人网络保护条例（国务院令第 766 号，第三十一、四十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10806/202311/content_6913813.html> ; 中央网信办秘书局 (2026). 关于加强网络直播打赏规范管理的通知（第 7 项）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2026-04/13/c_1777815804150225.htm> ; 最高人民法院 (2020). 关于依法妥善审理涉新冠肺炎疫情民事案件若干问题的指导意见（二）（法发〔2020〕17 号，第九条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/230181.html>
- Notes: the standard has changed. The 2022 four-department opinion and the 2020 notice from the National Radio and Television Administration said minors were barred from tipping outright; the April 2026 notice changed this to tiers at ages 8 and 16, without stating that the two earlier documents were repealed, and no official transition explanation was given — so the statement "tipping by minors is banned outright" is no longer accurate. Refund disputes are handled under the principle of the best interests of the minor

### 9. Handle infringement notices promptly; if nothing follows within 15 days of forwarding the counter-notice, restore the listing

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; build a notice, forwarding, counter-notice, and publication workflow, with all records archived
- In plain terms: when a rights holder sends an infringement notice with preliminary evidence, the platform must promptly delete, block, or disconnect the link and forward the notice to the seller; failing to act promptly means joint liability with the seller for the portion of losses caused by the delay. After the seller's counter-notice is forwarded to the rights holder, if no complaint or lawsuit notice arrives within 15 days, restore the listing. Knowing about an infringement and not acting brings joint liability in damages, plus an order to correct and a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 yuan, or 500,000 to 2 million yuan in serious cases; sending a bad-faith false notice that causes a seller losses doubles the damages.
- Benefit: the rights holder "has the right to notify the e-commerce platform operator to take necessary measures such as deletion, blocking, disconnection of links, and termination of transactions and services. The notice shall include preliminary evidence of the infringement"; upon receiving the notice the platform "shall promptly take the necessary measures and forward the notice to the on-platform operator; if it fails to take the necessary measures promptly, it bears joint liability with the on-platform operator for the enlarged portion of the harm"; the platform "shall promptly terminate the measures it has taken if, within fifteen days after the counter-notice reaches the intellectual property rights holder, it receives no notice that the rights holder has complained or filed suit"; where the platform knows or should know of an infringement and fails to take necessary measures, it bears joint liability with the infringer, and the intellectual property authority may order correction within a time limit, with a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 yuan for missing the deadline and 500,000 to 2 million yuan in serious cases; "anyone who sends an erroneous notice in bad faith, causing losses to an on-platform operator, bears doubled liability in damages" (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 电子商务法（第四十二、四十三、四十四、四十五、八十四条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf8af050b81> ; 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一千一百九十五、一千一百九十六、一千一百九十七条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/233181.html>
- Notes: outside e-commerce law, general network services follow the Civil Code's notice-and-takedown rules; the difference is that the counter-notice waiting period there is a "reasonable period" rather than 15 days. Publishing the notices, counter-notices, and outcomes received is an explicit requirement of Article 44 of the e-commerce law

### 10. Don't casually send user information abroad; cross-border transfer has statutory conditions and headcount thresholds

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; keeping user data inside mainland China is the simplest option; where transfer abroad is genuinely needed, use the standard contract or certification
- In plain terms: sending mainland users' information to a machine outside mainland China counts as an outbound transfer — a server located abroad doesn't mean the data hasn't left. You must go down one of four routes: a security assessment organized by the cyberspace authority, certification by a professional body, signing the standard contract, or another route provided by law; you must also tell users who the overseas recipient is and how the data will be handled, and obtain their separate consent. The tiers are set by headcount accumulated since January 1 of the current year: under 100,000 people with no sensitive information exempts you from all three; 100,000 to 1 million requires the standard contract or certification; over 1 million, or 10,000 or more people whose sensitive information is involved, requires declaring a security assessment.
- Benefit: providing personal information abroad requires meeting one of four conditions: passing a security assessment organized by the cyberspace authority, obtaining personal information protection certification from a professional body, concluding a contract with the overseas recipient under the standard contract drawn up by the cyberspace authority, or another condition provided by laws and administrative regulations; and the platform must inform the individual of the overseas recipient's name, contact details, processing purpose, processing methods, categories of personal information, and how to exercise their rights, "and obtain the individual's separate consent"; the thresholds are tiered by cumulative headcount: for data processors other than critical information infrastructure operators, "having provided abroad, cumulatively since January 1 of the current year, the personal information of fewer than 100,000 people (not including sensitive personal information)" exempts them from declaring an assessment, concluding the standard contract, and certification; 100,000 or more but fewer than 1 million people requires concluding the standard contract or passing certification; 1 million or more people, or sensitive personal information of 10,000 or more people, requires declaring a security assessment (nationwide, from March 2024)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 个人信息保护法（第三十八、三十九、四十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202108/t20210820_527244.shtml> ; 国家互联网信息办公室 (2024). 促进和规范数据跨境流动规定（网信办令第 16 号，第三、四、五、七、八条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11366/202405/content_6954192.html> ; 国家互联网信息办公室 (2022). 数据出境安全评估办法（网信办令第 11 号，第四条）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2022-07/07/c_1658811536396503.htm>
- Notes: a server abroad does not mean "the data hasn't left the country" — users registering inside mainland China while you transmit their information to a machine abroad is an outbound transfer. Where provision abroad is genuinely needed "for concluding or performing a contract to which the individual is a party" — cross-border shopping, cross-border delivery, flight and hotel bookings — the three routes are waived. The headcount is counted "cumulatively from January 1 of the current year", not over any rolling twelve months

### 11. Choose a server by first asking whether you can tolerate downtime, then whether anyone will maintain it, and only then comparing prices

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: a starter VPS runs tens of yuan per month, a cloud host that can carry real traffic runs hundreds to thousands of yuan per month, and dedicated server hosting starts at several thousand yuan per year with hardware you buy yourself
- In plain terms: what separates the three tiers is not price but what you have in hand on the day something breaks: mainstream cloud providers have a filing channel, public credentials, incident announcements and compensation clauses; small providers can be half the price, but when overselling, a disappearing operator, or an upstream ban hits, you have no one to appeal to; dedicated server hosting only makes sense when bandwidth and hardware are clearly cheaper and you have your own people to maintain it. A static site with no user data is fine on a VPS costing tens of yuan a month; anything with registration and transactions should go on a cloud host that scales elastically and takes snapshot backups, with the backups kept in two places.
- Benefit: what separates the three tiers is not price but what you have when things break. Mainstream cloud providers have a filing channel, public credentials, incident announcements and compensation clauses, and someone to turn to when problems arise; small providers can be half the price, but when overselling, a disappearing operator, or an upstream ban hits, you have no one to appeal to — check whether they hold a value-added telecom business operation license in the MIIT system before discussing price; dedicated server hosting only pays off when bandwidth or hardware costs are clearly cheaper and you have your own staff to maintain it. A static site with no user data is fine on the cheapest VPS; anything with user registration and transactions should go on a cloud host that scales elastically and takes snapshot backups, with backups kept in at least two places
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 作者经验，无直接文献；服务商资质与备案的法定要求见本节第 4 条，日志留存 6 个月和等级保护义务见第 11 节第 14 条
- Notes: this item is judgment, not evidence. The only thing you can verify is the provider's license. The right place to save money is configuration and bandwidth, not credentials
