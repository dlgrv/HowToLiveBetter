# 5. Don't Waste Money

This section's unit of account is money: savings percentages, annualized return differences, and expected value — not converted into lifespan or time. Entries involving investments discuss only the algorithms and verified historical data; nothing here constitutes investment advice.
### 1. Turn off all auto-renewals and switch to manual renewal at expiry
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: a one-time 10 to 20 minutes going through the "automatic deductions" lists in Alipay/WeChat/Apple/Android; after that, one extra tap per renewal
- In plain terms: what you save is those subscriptions you stopped using long ago but that keep charging you every month. Going through the auto-deduction lists in Alipay, WeChat, Apple and Android takes well under 20 minutes; regulations require merchants to prominently notify you before charging and to give you a simple entry point for cancelling at any time.
- Benefit: the savings equal the total of subscriptions "no longer used but still being charged," which varies from person to person; regulators already require operators to prominently remind you before renewal and provide a "simple, cancel-anytime" option, and cancellation costs nothing extra (China, in force since 2021)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 国家市场监督管理总局 (2021, 2025 修正). 网络交易监督管理办法（总局令第 37 号，第十八条）. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/fgs/art/2025/art_4b47c79b8d994a42bba4835997688faa.html>
- Notes: for services you genuinely use often, annual payment is usually cheaper than monthly, and keeping auto-renewal can actually be the better deal; this item targets the part you "forgot is still being charged."

### 2. Do your annual individual income tax reconciliation between March and June, and fill in every special additional deduction you qualify for
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; a dozen or so minutes in the Individual Income Tax app
- In plain terms: if you worked only a few months in the year, changed jobs mid-year, or never filled in deductions like children's education, mortgage, rent or supporting elderly parents, the reconciliation usually gets you a refund, and it takes a dozen or so minutes on your phone. If your annual comprehensive income is under 120,000, or the tax you owe is under 400 yuan, you can skip it.
- Benefit: wages and salaries, labor remuneration, author's remuneration and royalties are combined, and you either get a refund or pay the difference. A 2023 announcement by the State Taxation Administration extended the exemption from filing through December 31, 2027: if annual comprehensive income does not exceed 120,000 yuan, or the tax due does not exceed 400 yuan, you may skip it. Conversely, if you had income in only a few months of the year, changed jobs mid-year, or left deductions like children's education, mortgage, rent or supporting elderly parents unfilled, you can usually get money back
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家税务总局 (2025). 个人所得税综合所得汇算清缴管理办法（第六条）及其解读. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202502/content_7006465.htm>；财政部、税务总局 (2023). 关于延续实施个人所得税综合所得汇算清缴有关政策的公告. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202308/content_6900594.htm>
- Notes: if you owe tax, you must still file — not doing so is illegal. Special additional deductions cover children's education, continuing education, major medical expenses, housing loan interest, housing rent, supporting elderly parents, and care of infants under 3; you can choose only one of housing loan interest and housing rent

### 3. Use your housing provident fund for more than buying a home: rent, renovation and property fees are all valid withdrawals, and from September 20, 2026 the revised regulation applies
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: one withdrawal application
- In plain terms: the housing provident fund is not locked to home purchases only. The revised regulation lists nine situations where you can withdraw, and renting, renovating your own home and paying property fees all count; once the fund center accepts your application, it must tell you yes or no within 3 days.
- Benefit: the revised regulation lists nine permitted withdrawal situations, including paying rent, renovating an owner-occupied home, and paying property fees for an owner-occupied home; the fund management center must decide whether to approve the withdrawal within 3 days of accepting the application
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 国务院关于修改《住房公积金管理条例》的决定（国务院令第 844 号，2026 年 7 月 31 日国务院第 93 次常务会议通过，「自2026年9月20日起施行」）：修改后第二十四条「职工有下列情形之一的，可以提取职工住房公积金账户内的存储余额：（一）支付房租的；（二）购买、建造、翻建、大修自住住房的；（三）偿还购房贷款本息的；（四）装修自住住房的；（五）支付自住住房物业费的；（六）离休、退休的；（七）完全丧失劳动能力，并与单位终止劳动关系的；（八）出境定居的；（九）国务院批准的其他住房消费情形。」；第二十五条「住房公积金管理中心应当自受理申请之日起3日内作出准予提取或者不准提取的决定」；第十八条缴存比例「均不得低于职工上一年度月平均工资的5％」. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12946/202608/content_7079363.html>
- Notes: the money in the account is your own contributions plus the matching portion from your employer, and it only accrues interest at a very low rate while it sits there. The regulation also states that if a contributor dies or is declared dead, heirs and legatees may withdraw the balance. Required documents and local details are set by each local management center — check your local center's service guide before making the trip.

### 4. Recalculate your mobile and broadband plans every year, downgrade the ones you don't use, and complain if the carrier won't let you switch
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; a dozen or so minutes checking one bill
- In plain terms: old plans are often tens of yuan more expensive than what's on sale now — hundreds of yuan a year thrown away for nothing. Carriers may not refuse, block or delay number portability without a valid reason, and may not set special pricing just for porting users; if they hold your number hostage, complain.
- Benefit: Ministry of Industry and Information Technology rules explicitly state that carriers may not "refuse, obstruct or delay providing number portability services to users without a valid reason," may not "use technical means such as interception or restriction to affect the service quality of number-portability users," and may not "set special pricing and marketing schemes for number-portability users." Old plans are often tens of yuan more expensive than current plans, and leaving them untouched means throwing away several hundred yuan a year
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 工业和信息化部 (2019). 携号转网服务管理规定（工信部信管〔2019〕242 号）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-11/13/content_5451483.htm>
- Notes: first check your actual usage over the past six months in the carrier's app, then choose a plan based on that usage. When customer service says "downgrading requires a store visit" or "you can't change during the contract period," demand written justification, and if that fails, file a complaint via 12300

### 5. Don't buy lottery tickets
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: by regulation, the minimum share of sales that lottery-number games must pay out as prizes is 50%, and for the Double Color Ball it's 51% — every 1 yuan you bet returns 0.51 yuan on long-term average, a steady 49% loss. In 2024, 623.486 billion yuan was sold nationwide, of which 161.031 billion yuan went straight into the public welfare fund and never came back as prizes.
- Benefit: under Ministry of Finance rules, the minimum prize payout ratio is 50% of sales for lottery/number games, 65% for instant tickets, and 73% for sports betting; the Double Color Ball accrues prizes at 51% of sales, so every 1 yuan bet returns only 0.51 yuan in long-term expectation — an expected loss of 49%. In 2024, national lottery sales were 623.486 billion yuan, of which 161.031 billion yuan went into the public welfare fund and never reached buyers (China, 2024)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 财政部 (2015). 关于规范和加强彩票资金构成比例管理的通知（财综〔2015〕94 号）. <http://m.mof.gov.cn/czxw/201511/t20151113_1560814.htm>；财政部 (2025). 财政部公告 2025 年第 8 号（2024 年彩票公益金筹集分配情况）. <http://zhs.mof.gov.cn/zhengcefabu/202508/t20250828_3970977.htm>；中国福利彩票双色球游戏规则（第十四条）. <https://mzj.nc.gov.cn/ncsmzj/flcp/202111/5dccc1a5da89434e94a62f8ea0e5887e.shtml>
- Notes: the negative expected value is a result of how lotteries are designed, not a matter of luck; treating it as 2 yuan of entertainment per ticket rather than a financial decision is the only sensible framing.

### 6. Walk away the moment you hear "high yield", "guaranteed principal" or "sure profit"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: zero; you only need to stop when you hear those words
- In plain terms: in the regulators' own words: treat yields above 6% with a question mark, above 8% as very dangerous, and above 10% as ready to lose your entire principal. Legitimate wealth-management products are no longer allowed to promise principal protection or guaranteed returns; losses from illegal fundraising are borne by the participants themselves, and if things go wrong nobody bails you out.
- Benefit: the words of the then-chairman of the China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission at the 2018 Lujiazui Forum: "yields above 6% deserve a question mark, above 8% are very dangerous, and above 10% you should be prepared to lose all your principal"; the new asset-management rules require that financial institutions "must not promise principal protection or guaranteed returns" and "must not advance funds to honor payments when redemption difficulties arise" — that is, legitimate wealth-management products no longer come with principal protection; losses from participating in illegal fundraising are "borne by the fundraising participants themselves," and after the fact nobody covers for you (China, 2018 and 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 郭树清在第十届陆家嘴论坛的讲话（2018 年 6 月），天津市地方金融管理局转载. <https://jrgz.tj.gov.cn/ztlm/ztzl/jrjfwphjr/202006/t20200625_2744709.html>；中国人民银行等 (2018). 关于规范金融机构资产管理业务的指导意见（银发〔2018〕106 号，第二条、第六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2018/content_5323101.htm>；国务院 (2021). 防范和处置非法集资条例（国务院令第 737 号，第二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5588815.htm>
- Notes: the 6%/8%/10% figures are rule-of-thumb thresholds given by the regulator, not legal provisions; TODO (to be verified: the original speech page on the former CBIRC website loads dynamically and could not be scraped; the quote follows the version republished by the Tianjin Local Financial Administration). Not investment advice.

### 7. Don't pay only the credit card minimum, and don't take installment plans or consumer loans for spending
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: zero; you need to keep enough cash on hand to pay the bill in full before the due date
- In plain terms: the minimum payment is not a discount — it's a loan accruing interest daily, equivalent to roughly 18% annualized, and your interest-free grace period is voided on the spot, with interest charged on the full remaining principal. Judge rates by their annualized figure — the central bank already requires card issuers to display the annualized rate prominently and forbids showing only a "daily rate."
- Benefit: credit card overdraft rates were capped at a "daily rate of 0.05 per mille" before 2020, with a floor of 0.7 times that cap — equivalent to 18.25% annualized simple interest (from 2021, pricing became a matter negotiated between issuer and cardholder); after a minimum payment, interest accrues daily on the full remaining principal and the interest-free period lapses at the same time. The central bank requires issuers to display the annualized rate prominently and "not to display only the daily rate or daily repayment amount" — decide based on the annualized figure (China, 2020 notice)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 中国人民银行 (2020). 关于推进信用卡透支利率市场化改革的通知（银发〔2020〕327 号）. <https://xining.pbc.gov.cn/zhengwugongkai/4081330/4406346/4693549/4159909/index.html>
- Notes: 18.25% comes from "0.05 per mille × 365" and is a simple-interest figure; compounding monthly would make it higher. Some banks offer discounts — go by the annualized rate published on your own statement. If you genuinely can't cover it, first look for a legitimate channel with a lower rate rather than letting minimum payments roll interest over.

### 8. Don't tip streamers, don't top up games, don't impulse-buy
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: zero; a few minutes once to turn off password-free payments, set per-transaction and daily limits, and delete the payment methods linked in the platforms
- In plain terms: in 2024 domestic game revenue was 325.783 billion yuan with 674 million users, about 480 yuan per person. Money spent is essentially unrecoverable: the law only supports refunds for what minors spent without a parent's consent — adults have no such clause. The lowest-effort defense is turning off password-free payment, setting per-transaction and daily limits, and unlinking your payment methods.
- Benefit: this is a large category of spending: in 2024 the domestic game market's actual sales revenue was 325.783 billion yuan with 674 million users, about 480 yuan per person (derived by dividing the two figures). Money spent is essentially unrecoverable: only for tipping and top-ups by persons with limited civil capacity (minors) made without a guardian's consent does the rule that "the people's court shall support" a guardian's request for return apply; no such provision exists for adults' tipping and top-ups. Minors were already limited to 1 hour of online gaming, 8 to 9 p.m., on Fridays, Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays (China, 2020 and 2021)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 国家新闻出版署 (2024). 今年中国游戏市场收入超 3257 亿元创新高（中国音数协游戏工委《2024 年中国游戏产业报告》）. <https://www.nppa.gov.cn/xxfb/ywdt/202412/t20241216_877445.html>；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于依法妥善审理涉新冠肺炎疫情民事案件若干问题的指导意见（二）（法发〔2020〕17 号，第 9 条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/230181.html>；国家新闻出版署 (2021). 关于进一步严格管理 切实防止未成年人沉迷网络游戏的通知（国新出发〔2021〕14 号）. <https://www.nppa.gov.cn/xxfb/tzgs/202108/t20210830_666285.html>
- Notes: grade B because there is no direct measurement of "how much not tipping saves," only aggregate figures; turning off password-free payment and setting limits is an experiential practice (C). The original text of Articles 19 and 145 of the Civil Code is quoted verbatim in the next entry. Not investment advice.

### 9. For children's top-ups and tipping on phones: for large sums spent by those over eight without parental ratification, you can claim a refund
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; it does take time to gather evidence and negotiate — platforms typically refuse first and refund later; if negotiation fails, only a lawsuit remains, which starts at months of time with attorney fees on you, so having the materials ready and going through the platform and 12315 first is the better deal
- In plain terms: money spent by a child over eight is void if the amount is clearly out of proportion to their age and intelligence and the parents neither consent nor ratify it — you can claim a refund; children under eight cannot make such decisions on their own in the first place. If the parents never take a position, the law treats it as refusal to ratify.
- Benefit: minors over eight years of age are persons with limited civil capacity, and civil juristic acts not suited to their age and intelligence are valid only with the consent or ratification of their statutory agent; those under eight are persons without civil capacity, and their acts are performed on their behalf by the statutory agent. If the statutory agent says nothing, this is deemed a refusal to ratify
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典. 最高人民检察院转载全文. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml> 第十九条：「八周岁以上的未成年人为限制民事行为能力人，实施民事法律行为由其法定代理人代理或者经其法定代理人同意、追认；但是，可以独立实施纯获利益的民事法律行为或者与其年龄、智力相适应的民事法律行为。」第二十条：「不满八周岁的未成年人为无民事行为能力人，由其法定代理人代理实施民事法律行为。」第一百四十五条：「限制民事行为能力人实施的纯获利益的民事法律行为或者与其年龄、智力、精神健康状况相适应的民事法律行为有效；实施的其他民事法律行为经法定代理人同意或者追认后有效。」「法定代理人未作表示的，视为拒绝追认。」
- Notes: the key is evidence: proving the operator was the child, not you. Useful materials include top-ups concentrated during school hours or when parents are at work, device usage records, the child's chat and game records, and the child's own account. There is no uniform monetary threshold for "suited to age and intelligence" — a skin costing a few dozen yuan and tens of thousands of yuan in tipping are not the same thing. Prevention is cheaper: set a password-free payment cap, enable youth mode, and never share your payment password with the child. For the side of this where you yourself tip and top up impulsively, see the entry "Don't tip streamers, don't top up games, don't impulse-buy" in this section.

### 10. Teach your child one sentence: anyone who asks you to operate a parent's phone or read out a verification code is a scammer
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; explain the six categories below to your child once and agree on a phrase — "if you get scammed, tell me first, I won't scold you"; meanwhile set a password-free payment cap, enable youth mode, and keep the payment password from the child
- In plain terms: these six scam formats differ in every detail except the final step, which is always the same action — getting the child to transfer money on a parent's phone or read out a verification code. So teaching one sentence is enough: whoever tells you to touch a parent's phone or read out a code is a scammer. Add "if you get scammed, tell me first, I won't scold you," and only then will the child dare to say it the same day, and only then is there a chance of getting the money back.
- Benefit: six categories of scams targeting minors, broken down by the Quanzhou anti-fraud center together with the Quanzhou education bureau — different setups but the same final step of making the child transfer money on a parent's phone: cheap off-platform private trades of game accounts and gear (after Xiao Wang privately transferred 260 yuan he was told "transfers by minors violate the rules, the account will be frozen"; using his father's phone he clicked a "verification link" and more than 30,000 yuan was taken from the account); "removing the anti-addiction limit" (threatening "transfer or it's cancelled and your parents go to jail"; 7,000 yuan was transferred on his mother's phone; the source states the anti-addiction system is an official setting and "there is no such thing as a 'private unlock' channel"); tipping refunds (someone posing as "official customer service" says minors' tips are refundable and demands "sending gifts to a designated livestream room from a parent's Alipay to process the refund"; 73,000 yuan was tipped from a mother's account and she was then blocked; the source: "if someone adds you asking you to 'send gifts for a refund', it is 100% a scam"); impersonating classmates or teachers (after stealing an account, sending a voice message saying their name and asking to borrow money; at the start of the school term, infiltrating class group chats posing as the teacher to demand "500 yuan in school fees" with the same avatar and nickname, and planting shills saying "already paid"); celebrity fandom (pulling the child into a "star's fan group, claim an 888 yuan red packet," first "verifying" with a 48.88 yuan transfer, then frightening them: "a minor participating is a violation, the platform will freeze 80,000 and your parents go to jail," scamming 16,000 yuan in total; the source: "real police and lawyers will never contact a minor online for an investigation, let alone ask to operate a parent's phone"); second-hand platforms luring buyers with underpriced goods into leaving the platform for private transfers. The same material lists three red lines for children themselves: never lend, sell or rent out bank cards or SIM cards (a card used to move stolen money may constitute the crime of assisting information network criminal activity, leaving a record that affects graduate school and civil service exams), "shoujikou" phone-relay side jobs are illegal, and overseas "high-paying jobs" may mean being controlled on arrival (nationwide, October 2025)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 泉州市反诈骗中心 (2025). 开学第一课｜反诈是青少年的必修课，这 6 大陷阱一定要避开！<http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202510/t20251028_7026836.htm>（福建省公安厅转载）
- Notes: grade B because the authorities provide script breakdowns and individual cases, with no statistics on incidence or prevention effectiveness. Keep this distinct from the previous entry: money a child tops up or tips themselves can be claimed back under the Civil Code, but money a child is scammed into transferring does not fall under that rule — you can only report to the police for payment interception as in Section 8, item 2, and recovery depends on whether the money is still in the account. The scammer's core line for frightening children is "you broke the rules and your parents go to jail"; explaining in advance that this very phrase is a hallmark of scams is more useful than questioning after the fact. The promise "tell me first, no scolding" must actually be kept — children hiding out of fear of being scolded is the common reason losses grew in these cases. For the adult version of impersonating acquaintances and for AI face-swapping, see Section 8, items 3 and 4.

### 11. Decline extended warranties on electronics
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: zero; you bear the small-probability expense of a self-paid repair outside the warranty period
- In plain terms: extended warranties typically sell for 10% to 50% of the product's original price, while their gross margin is more than ten times that of ordinary goods — in 2003 Best Buy earned more than half its profits from extended warranties that made up only 3% to 4% of revenue. That gap is the extra money you're paying.
- Benefit: extended warranties are usually priced at 10% to 50% of the product's original price; industry estimates cited by research put the average gross margin on extended warranties at 50% to 60%, roughly 18 times that of ordinary goods, and in 2003 extended warranties accounted for only 3% to 4% of Best Buy's revenue yet more than 50% of its profit (United States, 2000s retail data)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Chen T, Kalra A, Sun B (2009). Why Do Consumers Buy Extended Service Contracts? Journal of Consumer Research 36(4):611-623. <https://doi.org/10.1086/605298>
- Notes: the gross margin figures are industry estimates cited second-hand in the paper (Business Week 2004), not the paper's own calculations; this applies to ordinary people who can absorb one repair bill — those who live off a single device with no backup are a different case. Screen-crack insurance for phones needs its own expected-value calculation based on your actual drop frequency.

### 12. Prefer generics that passed consistency evaluation and drugs selected in centralized procurement
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: zero; one extra question at prescribing or purchase — "is there a centralized-procurement or generic-name version?"
- In plain terms: the second centralized-procurement round cut average prices by 53%, with a maximum cut of 93%; by April 2026, 11 rounds had been run covering 490 drugs. When a drug is prescribed or bought, one extra question — "is there a centralized-procurement one, a generic-name one?" — and the same ingredient costs far less.
- Benefit: the second round of nationally organized drug centralized procurement cut average prices by 53% and up to 93%; as of April 2026, 11 rounds had been conducted covering 490 drugs, with 1,695 drugs having passed or been deemed to have passed consistency evaluation — about 2/3 of commonly used chemical drugs in clinical practice (China, official data 2020 to 2026)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家医疗保障局 (2020). 第二批国家组织药品集中采购和使用工作答记者问. <https://www.nhsa.gov.cn/art/2020/1/17/art_38_2264.html>；国家医疗保障局 (2026). 国务院政策例行吹风会：健全全周期全渠道药品价格形成机制. <http://www.nhsa.gov.cn/art/2026/4/15/art_14_20221.html>
- Notes: the controversy: entry into centralized procurement uses consistency evaluation as the quality threshold, and the official position is therapeutic equivalence; some clinicians question actual efficacy differences in individual products, but there is no citable systematic evidence. Ask the doctor before switching drugs — especially for long-term chronic-disease medication.

### 13. Set up family mutual-aid linking once in the medical insurance app, and the money in your employee medical insurance individual account can pay for your spouse's, parents' and children's care and prescriptions
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one family mutual-aid binding at the medical insurance app or service office of the place where you're insured
- In plain terms: do the binding once and the money in your employee medical insurance individual account can pay for your spouse's, parents' and children's medical visits and prescriptions; without it, the money is only for your own use and just sits in the account.

### 14. Drink boiled tap water instead of buying bottled water year-round

- Cost: time to boil water plus a small gas/electricity charge; a kettle
- In plain terms: Beijing's first-tier water price works out to 0.005 yuan per liter; bottled water at 1 yuan per 500 ml comes to 2 yuan per liter — a 400-fold difference. Tap water is governed by a mandatory national standard, so boiling it before drinking is enough day to day; if a water source really fails and the water turns smelly or cloudy, that isn't solved by boiling a few more minutes — stop using it and report it.
- Benefit: Beijing's first-tier residential water price is 5 yuan/cubic meter, i.e. 0.005 yuan/liter; bottled water at 1 yuan/500 ml is 2 yuan per liter, 400 times that of tap water. ### 15. Don't trade stocks frequently
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: zero; it takes restraint against the urge to act the moment you see news
- In plain terms: in data on 66,000 US accounts, the most actively trading group earned only 11.4% annualized while the market did 17.9%. The six-plus points of difference were almost entirely eaten by trading costs — the two groups' gross returns were actually similar; every time the hand moves, the money shrinks.
- Benefit: a study of 66,465 US discount-brokerage household accounts (1991 to 1996): the most actively trading households earned 11.4% annualized against 17.9% for the market and 16.4% for the average household; the average household's annual turnover was 75%. The difference in gross returns between frequent and infrequent traders was small — the gap came mainly from trading costs (United States, 1991 to 1996)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Barber BM, Odean T (2000). Trading Is Hazardous to Your Wealth: The Common Stock Investment Performance of Individual Investors. The Journal of Finance 55(2):773-806. <https://doi.org/10.1111/0022-1082.00226>
- Notes: the data is US, from the 1990s; A-share commission and stamp-duty structures differ, but the mechanism — the higher the turnover, the lower the net return — is the same. Not investment advice.

### 16. Don't invest with borrowed money, don't use leverage, don't buy what you don't understand
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: zero; it takes resisting the "borrow a bit and earn more" impulse when markets are good
- In plain terms: if you buy stocks with borrowed money and the price falls through the line and you can't top up the collateral, you're force-liquidated — paper losses become real losses on the spot, and even the chance to "wait for it to come back" is gone. Regulators bar people with under six months of trading experience or daily average assets below 500,000 yuan from opening margin accounts — the threshold itself says this tool isn't meant for ordinary people.
- Benefit: leverage magnifies losses: after a margin purchase, if the collateral's market value falls below the maintenance guarantee ratio line and no additional collateral is posted, the position is force-liquidated — paper losses become real losses on the spot, with no chance to "wait for it to come back." Regulators stipulate that clients with "less than half a year of securities trading experience, insufficient risk-bearing capacity, or average daily securities assets below 500,000 yuan over the most recent 20 trading days" may not open credit accounts — the threshold itself shows this is not a tool for ordinary people (China, 2015)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 中国证监会 (2015). 证券公司融资融券业务管理办法（证监会令第 117 号，第十二条）. <https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c106256/c1654005/content.shtml>；上海证券交易所. 《上海证券交易所融资融券交易实施细则》解读（投资者教育材料）. <https://www.sse.com.cn/services/tradingservice/margin/edu/c/10074042/files/a1f1c4833302451fb9130dbb94116c56.pdf>
- Notes: "don't buy what you don't understand" has no direct literature and rests on consensus (C); investing with consumer loans or credit-card cash advances is leverage in disguise — for the rates see item 7 of this section. Not investment advice.

### 17. Use broad-based index funds instead of actively managed funds as the long-term core holding
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: giving up the possibility of "picking the star fund manager"; you must accept drawdowns identical to the index
- In plain terms: actively managed funds in aggregate already hold positions very close to the whole market, but the extra management fee they charge comes straight out of your net return; simulations show very few funds earn back their own cost.

### 18. Among funds in the same category, prefer the one with lower fees
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: zero; one extra glance at "management fee + custody fee + sales service fee" before subscribing
- In plain terms: every extra percentage point of annual fee means roughly 18% less in hand after 20 years and about 26% less after 30 years. Picking the lower-fee fund among similar ones is the only variable here you can pin down in advance.
- Benefit: fee differences erode through compounding: 1 extra percentage point of annual fee means the terminal value is 0.99^20 ≈ 18.2% lower after 20 years and about 26% lower after 30 years. China began public-fund fee reform in 2023, lowering the caps on management and custody fees for active equity funds (exact cap figures to be verified)
- Evidence grade: C

- Notes: the compounding formula is pure arithmetic and assumes both funds have identical pre-fee returns; in practice you must also count subscription fees, redemption fees, and the sales service fee on Class C shares. Not investment advice.

### 19. Don't stake your money on one stock, one platform or one property
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: zero; accept that you "won't happen to hit the single best-performing one"
- In plain terms: as long as several assets don't rise and fall in perfect lockstep, spreading money across them lowers volatility without changing expected returns. Conversely, if all your money sits in one stock, one platform or one property, whatever happens to it happens to all of you.
- Benefit: as long as several assets are not perfectly correlated, spreading the money across them keeps expected returns unchanged while reducing volatility — the basic conclusion of portfolio selection proposed by Markowitz in 1952 and used ever since; conversely, with all your money in one stock or platform, its variance is your entire variance, and when it fails there is no hedge at all
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Markowitz, H. (1952). Portfolio Selection. The Journal of Finance, 7(1), 77–91. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1952.tb01525.x>
- Notes: grade B because the classic literature is a theoretical derivation and gives no empirical figure for "how much less you lose by diversifying"; diversification only reduces volatility and guarantees nothing about losses. Buying a broad-based index fund (item 17 of this section) is itself the easiest form of diversification. Not investment advice.

### 20. If you pay individual income tax, open a personal pension account — 12,000 yuan a year is deductible pre-tax; if you don't pay income tax, opening one doesn't pay
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->

### 21. Pay per visit or short-term for the gym, unless you have a steady track record of over a year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: the per-visit price is higher than the amortized annual-card price; it requires honestly assessing how often you actually go
- In plain terms: in that three-year US dataset, members on monthly contracts above 70 dollars averaged only 4.3 visits a month — over 17 dollars per visit — while a 10-visit pass cost just 10 dollars a visit; over the life of their membership they paid 600 dollars more on average. Unless you already have a steady record of going for over a year, per-visit or short-term is cheaper.
- Benefit: a study of 3 years of data on 7,752 members of 3 US health clubs: members choosing monthly contracts above 70 dollars averaged only 4.3 visits a month, over 17 dollars per visit, while a 10-visit pass cost only 10 dollars per visit; these users paid on average 600 dollars more over their membership. Monthly-paying members who still hadn't cancelled after more than a year ran 17% higher than annual-card members (United States, three-year panel data)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: DellaVigna S, Malmendier U (2006). Paying Not to Go to the Gym. American Economic Review 96(3):694-719. <https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.96.3.694>
- Notes: a single study with US data, but the mechanism (overestimating one's future self-control) is general. For someone with a full year of going at least twice a week, an annual card really is cheaper.

### 22. Sign a written contract when paying in advance; if the merchant becomes risky, stop prepaying — and before they run off you have the right to recover the unconsumed balance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; the hard part is insisting on the contract on the spot and not accepting "just top up first"
- In plain terms: anyone taking prepayments must sign a written contract stating how refunds work. - Benefit: the regulation stipulates that merchants collecting prepayments must conclude a written contract and agree on a refund method; upon major business risk they shall stop collecting prepayments; before deciding to close or relocate they must notify consumers in advance, and consumers have the right to demand continued performance or a refund of the unconsumed balance. ### 24. Don't stockpile over "strikethrough prices" and big promotions
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: giving up some genuinely good discounts; it requires remembering the everyday prices of what you regularly buy
- In plain terms: people judge whether something is expensive by the first number they see, and later adjustments never fully compensate — so "was 999, now 499" systematically makes you overestimate how cheap it is. And by regulation, the baseline for a discount is only the store's lowest actual transaction price within the seven days before the promotion — the strikethrough price wasn't necessarily ever really charged.
- Benefit: judgment anchors on the first number seen, and later adjustments are typically insufficient, so "was 999, now 499" systematically inflates your estimate of how much you're saving; regulators stipulate that the discount baseline must be the same store's lowest transaction price within the seven days before the promotion — meaning the strikethrough price is not necessarily a price ever actually charged (China, in force since 2020)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: Tversky A, Kahneman D (1974). Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and Biases. Science 185(4157):1124-1131. <https://doi.org/10.1126/science.185.4157.1124>；国家市场监督管理总局 (2020). 规范促销行为暂行规定（总局令第 32 号，第二十一条）. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/fgs/art/2023/art_cae53a080be2401e8f91c6d6291539f8.html>
- Notes: the anchoring effect is a laboratory finding with no direct monetary quantification of stockpiling losses; the hidden costs of stockpiling also include expiry, space taken, and "well, we have it anyway, use more." Only stockpile things with a long shelf life that you will certainly use up and whose unit price really is below the historical price.

### 25. Prefer pure-protection (term) insurance; treat "returned premiums" and "dividends" as the non-guaranteed part
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- In plain terms: the dividends and returned-premium portions are not guaranteed — regulators require the brochure to state in bold that "future policy dividends are non-guaranteed benefits." The extra premium you pay is essentially having the insurance company hold money in savings for you: the returns aren't guaranteed, and it's hard to get out.
- Benefit: regulatory rules require participating-product brochures to state in bold type that "future policy dividends are non-guaranteed benefits, and their distribution is uncertain," with the interest-spread cap for dividend demonstrations being 4.5% minus the product's assumed interest rate; the extra premium of return-of-premium/participating products is essentially savings entrusted to the insurance company — returns not guaranteed and liquidity poor (China, in force since 2023)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 中国银保监会 (2022). 一年期以上人身保险产品信息披露规则（银保监规〔2022〕24 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2023-01/04/content_5735014.htm>
- Notes: the controversy: for people who genuinely cannot save at all, the forced-savings function of return-of-premium products has real value; this entry targets ordinary people with self-discipline. ### 26. Buy enough third-party liability coverage: compulsory insurance limits are uniform nationwide and not high, and anything beyond comes out of your own household
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- Benefit: compulsory insurance applies uniform liability limits nationwide, calculated separately across four categories: death and disability, medical expenses, property damage, and no-fault; any amount beyond the limits is borne by the tortfeasor personally. Drivers without compulsory insurance have the vehicle detained and face a fine of 2 times the premium due
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2006, 2012 修订). 机动车交通事故责任强制保险条例（国务院令第 462 号，第 618 号令修改）第二十三条：「机动车交通事故责任强制保险在全国范围内实行统一的责任限额。责任限额分为死亡伤残赔偿限额、医疗费用赔偿限额、财产损失赔偿限额以及被保险人在道路交通事故中无责任的赔偿限额。」「责任限额由保监会会同国务院公安部门、国务院卫生主管部门、国务院农业主管部门规定。」第二十一条限额内赔偿；第三十九条未投保的「由公安机关交通管理部门扣留机动车……处依照规定投保最低责任限额应缴纳的保险费的2倍罚款」. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2012/content_2131962.htm>
- Notes: TODO (to be verified: the specific amounts of the current compulsory insurance liability limits are set by the financial regulator; no original text suitable for verbatim verification was obtained this time). The point is the structure, not the numbers: compulsory insurance pays by category, with uniform and not-generous limits, and compensation for injuring someone can far exceed it — the difference has no backstop at all. ### 27. First save up an emergency fund of 3 to 6 months of living expenses, kept somewhere you can withdraw anytime
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: no money, but this sum gives up higher returns and must sit only in demand deposits, money market funds or instantly withdrawable savings
- In plain terms: with 3 to 6 months of living expenses kept somewhere instantly accessible, you won't need to borrow at an 18% annualized rate when you lose your job or fall ill, and you won't have to sell investments at a loss. Bank deposits carry deposit insurance — up to 500,000 yuan paid out per bank.
- Benefit: with an emergency fund you don't have to borrow when unemployed or ill (for the annualized rates on consumer loans and minimum payments see item 7 of this section) or sell investments at a loss; bank deposits are protected by deposit insurance, with a maximum payout of 500,000 yuan per bank (China, 2015)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 国务院 (2015). 存款保险条例（国务院令第 660 号，第五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2015-03/31/content_9562.htm>
- Notes: "3 to 6 months" is a common rule of thumb with no official or academic original source found, hence grade C; size it by your own fixed expenses, not by income. Not investment advice.

### 28. Before prepaying your mortgage, run one comparison first — don't go on feel
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: half an hour of arithmetic; it requires an honest estimate of the stable after-tax return you can actually get
- In plain terms: prepaying earns you a certain return, and its value equals your mortgage rate. So you only need to answer one question: is the after-tax return you can reliably achieve over the long term higher than your mortgage rate? If yes, don't prepay; if no, or you're unsure, prepay. With equal installment payments, interest takes up a large share early on, so the earlier you repay the more you save — use your bank app's prepayment calculator and just look at the numbers.
- Benefit: the algorithm: let the mortgage's annual rate be r (per the contract; the 5-year-plus LPR was 3.5% on August 20, 2026, and the actual rate is the LPR plus or minus points), and the stable after-tax annualized return of the alternative investment be g. Prepayment's certain rate of return is r; if g is stably greater than r, keeping the money invested is the better deal; if g is less than r or uncertain, prepaying amounts to locking in a risk-free r. With equal installments, interest dominates early on and the earlier you repay the more interest you save — see the numbers directly in your bank app's "prepayment calculation" (China, 2026 rates)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 全国银行间同业拆借中心受中国人民银行授权 (2026). 2026 年 8 月 20 日贷款市场报价利率（LPR）公告. <https://www.chinamoney.com.cn/chinese/rdgz/20260820/3399885.html>
- Notes: only the algorithm is given, no conclusion: that g rarely stays above r for long is the norm, but keeping enough emergency cash takes priority over either side. Watch for prepayment penalty clauses in the contract. Not investment advice.

### 29. Online, trust platform rules and statutes, not streamers and "good reviews"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: no money; one extra look at after-sales terms and deposit rules before ordering, and keep the order records, livestream replay screenshots and chat logs after delivery
- In plain terms: merchant fraud is compensated at three times the price paid, or 500 yuan when that falls short; platforms that knowingly let sellers infringe and do nothing are jointly liable. Fake orders, fake reviews and false information in livestreams all carry liability, and organizing fake orders draws fines up to 2 million yuan with possible license revocation. A deposit can be at most 20% of the contract amount — forfeited if you breach, doubled if the seller breaches. Big-data price discrimination against existing customers is expressly prohibited.
- Benefit: operators engaging in fraud compensate at three times the price paid, or 500 yuan when the price is below that; platforms that know or should know a seller is infringing and fail to act bear joint liability; livestream rooms and hosts must not fabricate transactions, follows, views, likes or other traffic, and must not publish false information — where damage results they bear civil liability; organizing fake transactions or fake reviews to help others promote draws fines up to 1 million yuan, or 1 million to 2 million yuan plus possible license revocation in serious cases; a deposit may not exceed 20% of the contract's subject amount — it is not returned if you fail to perform and doubled by the seller if they fail to perform; automated decision-making on personal information must not impose unreasonable differential treatment on transaction prices and other terms (big-data price discrimination against existing customers) (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2013 修正). 消费者权益保护法（第四十四、五十五条）. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zfjcj/tzgg/art/2023/art_615af9ed6bcd4974bf853dd2e02bc663.html>（市场监管总局转载）；国家网信办等七部门 (2021). 网络直播营销管理办法（试行）（第十八、二十八条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-04/23/content_5601682.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2025 修订). 反不正当竞争法（第九、二十五条）. <https://www.ssf.gov.cn/portal/rootfiles/2025/09/01/1758444945285569-1758444945303119.pdf>（全国社保基金理事会转载）；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第五百八十六、五百八十七条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/233181.html>；全国人大常委会 (2021). 个人信息保护法（第二十四条）. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/gk/tjfg/xgfxfg/202503/t20250310_1958923.html>（国家统计局转载）；山东省市场监管局 (2025). 六起网络不正当竞争典型案例. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/jjj/fbzdjz/art/2025/art_cbaed1c006584baeb6823a6b01325ba4.html>
- Notes: for "which platform has the best value and is least likely to sell you shoddy goods" there is no citable official comparison, and this book does not rank platforms — what can be found are aggregate spot checks (see the next entry). After the 2025 revision of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law, false advertising moved from old Article 8 to Article 9 and the penalty provisions from Article 20 to Article 25, in force from October 15, 2025 — don't copy the old article numbers. In the Shandong case, a company organized "paid order-fakers" to conduct 75 fake transactions with principal of over 680,000 yuan and was investigated; the amount of the fine was not stated in the bulletin. For the 7-day no-questions-asked return see item 23 of this section.

### 30. When something bought in a livestream goes wrong, first demand the seller's and host's information from the platform — the platform must provide it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; simply make the demand to the platform
- In plain terms: the most common dead end in livestream disputes is not knowing whom to sue. Once a dispute arises, you can directly require the platform to provide the livestream operator's and the host's information and business records, and the platform must comply.
- Benefit: when a consumer dispute arises, the operator of the livestream marketing platform shall, at the consumer's request, provide relevant information about the livestream room operator and the livestream marketing personnel as well as records of the related business activities — this resolves the most common dead end in livestream rights protection: not knowing whom to hold accountable
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2024). 中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法实施条例（国务院令第 778 号，2024 年 7 月 1 日施行）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202403/content_6940159.htm>：「经营者通过网络直播等方式提供商品或者服务的，应当依法履行消费者权益保护相关义务。直播营销平台经营者应当建立健全消费者权益保护制度，明确消费争议解决机制。发生消费争议的，直播营销平台经营者应当根据消费者的要求提供直播间运营者、直播营销人员相关信息以及相关经营活动记录等必要信息。」「直播间运营者、直播营销人员发布的直播内容构成商业广告的，应当依照《中华人民共和国广告法》的有关规定履行广告发布者、广告经营者或者广告代言人的义务。」
- Notes: record the livestream screen and the promises made before ordering; when a problem appears, first demand the entity information from the platform, then decide between complaint and lawsuit. The same regulation also provides: the scope of goods excluded from 7-day no-questions-asked returns must not be expanded without authorization, "not eligible for no-questions-asked return" must not be set as the default option, and returns must not be refused without your confirmation.

### 31. Before buying big-ticket items, check national spot-check bulletins, CCC certification and energy-efficiency labels
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: no money; a few minutes checking the State Administration for Market Regulation and Certification and Accreditation Administration websites
- In plain terms: in 2024 national spot checks covered 25,250 product batches, with 14.3% failing; e-commerce channels were worse — 23.5% of 7,297 batches failed, versus 15.1% in physical stores. Spend a few minutes before a big purchase checking the spot-check bulletins and verifying the CCC certificate, and make sure the energy-efficiency label is there where it's required.
- Benefit: 2024 national supervision spot checks covered 25,250 product batches with a failure rate of 14.3%; e-commerce channels accounted for 7,297 batches with a 23.5% failure rate, up 1.8 percentage points from the previous year, while physical stores had 8,055 batches with a 15.1% failure rate; the authenticity of Compulsory Product Certification (CCC) certificates can be checked in the "Certification Results" section of the Certification and Accreditation Administration website; energy-efficiency labels are mandatorily managed by product catalog, and labels must include the efficiency grade, efficiency indicators and an efficiency information code — forging, misusing or omitting a required label is punished under the Energy Conservation Law (nationwide)
