# 1. Don't Die Early

This section covers three things. First, death from accidents and injuries, such as car crashes, fires, and poisoning. Second, the few vaccines and screenings backed by the hardest evidence. Third, which signals from your body mean it is time to get checked. The numbers in the Benefit column come from randomized trials, from studies that followed large groups of people for many years, or from official statistics. The numbers in the Cost column are the author's rough estimates based on market prices; they are meant only for comparing options with one another, so do not cite them anywhere else.
### 1. Wear a seat belt, front and back seats alike
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Nothing. Two extra seconds each time you get in the car.
- In plain terms: Fasten your seat belt in the front seat, and your chance of being killed in a car crash drops by about half. In the front seat of a light truck (pickup, van), it drops by 60%. Among people in the US who died in a car in 2022 and whose seat belt use could be established, half were not wearing one.
- Benefit: The US traffic safety authority (NHTSA) estimates that seat belts cut the risk of fatal injury for front-seat car occupants by 45%. For front seats of light trucks, by 60%. Among people who died in passenger vehicles (cars carrying people) in the US in 2022 and whose belt use could be established, 50% were unbelted. The World Health Organization (WHO) puts it this way: seat belts reduce the risk of death for people inside the vehicle by up to 50%. WHO estimates that 248,099 people died in road traffic crashes in China in 2021, which works out to 17.4 deaths per 100,000 people
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: NHTSA (2024). Occupant Protection in Passenger Vehicles: 2022 Data (DOT HS 813 573). <https://crashstats.nhtsa.dot.gov/Api/Public/ViewPublication/813573> ; WHO (2025). Road traffic injuries fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries> ; WHO GHO RS_196/RS_198 (China, 2021). <https://ghoapi.azureedge.net/api/RS_196?$filter=SpatialDim%20eq%20%27CHN%27>
- Notes: Buckle up in the back seat too. In the same report, 60% of people killed in the second row were not wearing a belt. The 45% and 60% figures are estimates the US traffic safety authority (NHTSA) computed from crash databases (citing Kahane 2015), not randomized trials. The WHO figure for deaths in China is a modeled estimate, several times higher than what the traffic police registry records. The two counts cover different scopes; only the WHO figure is used here.

### 2. Wear a helmet on a motorcycle or e-bike, and buckle it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Buying one runs 100 to 300 yuan. Five seconds to put it on each time.
- In plain terms: Wear a helmet and buckle the strap, and a motorcyclist's chance of dying in a crash drops by about 40%. Head injuries drop by about 70%. The strap has to be fastened tight; a helmet sitting loose on your head does not count as wearing one.
- Benefit: Cochrane pooled several studies and calculated them together. For motorcycle riders wearing a helmet, the risk of death drops 42%. Raw value OR 0.58, confidence range 95% CI 0.50 to 0.68. Head injuries drop 69% (OR 0.31, 95% CI 0.25 to 0.38)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Liu BC 等 (2008). Helmets for preventing injury in motorcycle riders. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD004333.pub3>
- Notes: A helmet must be buckled. Hanging on the handlebar does not count as wearing it. The figures above come from motorcycles. E-bikes are an extrapolation from them; there is no direct study, but the way a head gets hurt in a collision is the same thing.

### 3. Install smoke alarms; if you burn coal or use gas heating indoors in winter, add a carbon monoxide alarm
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A smoke alarm runs 30 to 100 yuan, a carbon monoxide alarm 50 to 150 yuan. After installation, replace the batteries once a year.
- In plain terms: A working smoke alarm cuts your chance of dying in a house fire by about 60%. At the moment a fire starts, 40% of victims are asleep; your nose alone will not wake you. If you burn coal or use gas in the house in winter, add a carbon monoxide alarm. In China in 2018, 11,500 people died of carbon monoxide poisoning; in the three winter months, 70% of those deaths happened at home.
- Benefit: A study in North Carolina, USA examined a set of house fires and looked back at households that did and did not have an incident. With a working smoke alarm in the home, the risk of death drops by about 60%. Raw value OR 0.39, confidence range 95% CI 0.18 to 0.83. In the US from 2018 to 2020, in fatal fires in occupied homes, 24% of households had no smoke alarm, and in 41% someone was asleep when the fire started. China reported 11,523 deaths from carbon monoxide poisoning in 2018. Of those deaths, 72.59% in December, 67.42% in January, and 66.48% in February happened at home
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Marshall SW 等 (1998). Fatal residential fires: who dies and who survives? JAMA. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.279.20.1633> ; USFA (2022). Fatal Fires in Residential Buildings (2018-2020), Topical Fire Report Series 22(2). <https://www.usfa.fema.gov/downloads/pdf/statistics/v22i2.pdf> ; You J 等 (2020). Number of Deaths due to Carbon Monoxide Poisoning by Month and by Place of Death — China, 2018. China CDC Weekly. <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2020.008>
- Notes: Why B: for smoke alarms there is only one study, done by looking back at households with and without incidents, not a randomized trial. For carbon monoxide alarms there is no mortality study at all. The benefit here is inferred from the fact that poisoning deaths cluster in winter and at home.

### 4. Replace gas hoses and stoves when they expire, do not modify piping yourself, and feel free to turn away door-to-door gas company sales
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A code-compliant gas hose costs a few dozen yuan. A stove gets replaced every few years. When you replace it, take a look at whether it is past its date.
- In plain terms: A compliant hose costs a few dozen yuan. Replace it when it expires — that is a duty the Urban Gas Management Regulation explicitly places on users. Modifying piping or removing the gas meter yourself is expressly forbidden by the regulation. The same regulation also says the gas company may not force you to buy products or services it designates. A sales pitch at your door claiming you "must replace" something can simply be refused.
- Benefit: The regulation makes "timely replacement of gas-burning appliances and connecting hoses that the state has explicitly phased out or that have reached the end of their service life" a user obligation. In other words, when your stove or hose is past its years of use, or has been phased out nationally, you replace it. The regulation also lists "installing, altering, or dismantling indoor gas facilities and gas metering devices without authorization" as prohibited conduct — modifying piping or removing the gas meter yourself falls under it. The regulation further states that a gas operator may not require users to buy products it designates, nor require them to accept services it provides
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2010). 城镇燃气管理条例（国务院令第 583 号）第二十七条：「燃气用户应当遵守安全用气规则，使用合格的燃气燃烧器具和气瓶，及时更换国家明令淘汰或者使用年限已届满的燃气燃烧器具、连接管等」；第二十八条禁止行为含「（一）擅自操作公用燃气阀门」「（二）将燃气管道作为负重支架或者接地引线」「（四）擅自安装、改装、拆除户内燃气设施和燃气计量装置」；第二十条燃气经营者不得「（六）要求燃气用户购买其指定的产品或者接受其提供的服务」；第二十九条用户可就收费、服务向燃气管理等部门投诉，「有关部门应当自收到投诉之日起15个工作日内予以处理」. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2010/content_1758214.htm>
- Notes: Some people come to the door in the name of a "safety inspection" and, once the check is done, push shutoff valves and alarms. Article 20, item 6 of the regulation shuts down that path directly: you can refuse and you can file a complaint. ### 5. Do not pick, buy, or eat wild mushrooms; no "folk method of identification" holds up
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Nothing. The hard part is giving up a seasonal wild delicacy.
- In plain terms: In 2025 alone, there were 828 mushroom poisonings nationwide, 2,165 people affected, and 13 deaths. That same year, 34 mushroom species previously never recorded as toxic to humans were newly identified. Silver-needle testing, "if insects ate it, it's safe," "only brightly colored ones are poisonous" — none of these claims holds up.
- Benefit: The Chinese Center for Disease Control and Prevention (China CDC) investigated 828 mushroom poisoning incidents in 2025, involving 2,165 people, with 13 deaths. The case fatality rate was 0.6%, meaning 0.6% of those poisoned died. From 2019 to 2024 there were 276 to 676 incidents per year, with case fatality rates between 0.87% and 2.86%. In 2025 alone, 138 toxic mushroom species were identified, 34 of them recorded in the country as causing poisoning for the first time
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Mushroom Poisoning Outbreaks — China, 2025. China CDC Weekly (2026)：「In 2025, China CDC investigated 828 mushroom poisoning incidents across 27 provincial-level administrative divisions (PLADs), affecting 2,165 individuals and causing 13 deaths - a case fatality rate of 0.6%, the lowest of the past six years. In total, 138 poisonous mushroom species were identified, including 34 newly recorded in poisoning incidents in China.」「From 2019 to 2024, the annual number of incidents ranged from 276 to 676, and the case fatality rate ranged from 0.87% to 2.86%.」<https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2026.120>；同刊 2024 年度报告 <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2025.106>
- Notes: If you eat a wrong one, induce vomiting immediately, then bring the leftover mushrooms or photos of them to the hospital. Mushroom poisoning comes in several distinct types, and identifying which mushroom it was directly determines how the doctor treats you. Every year new species are recorded as capable of poisoning people, so the very premise that "locals can tell them apart" does not stand. Silver-needle testing, boiling with garlic, "if insects ate it, it's safe," "only brightly colored ones are poisonous" — none of these claims holds up.

### 6. Do not push an e-bike into the stairwell, into the elevator, or charge it at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Nothing. The hard part is parking at the shared charging point downstairs every time, a few dozen meters of extra walking.
- In plain terms: Pushing an e-bike into the stairwell or the elevator, or charging it at home, is expressly forbidden by the Ministry of Emergency Management's fire safety rules for high-rise residential buildings. Those who fail to correct it are fined 500 to 1,000 yuan as individuals, or 2,000 to 10,000 yuan if they run a business. The fine is the smaller matter; what matters is that this kind of fire burns right on your only escape route.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Emergency Management's rules state in writing: "It is prohibited to park electric bicycles or charge them in the public lobbies, evacuation corridors, stairwells, or emergency exits of high-rise civil buildings." In other words, in the building lobby, corridors, stairwells, and emergency exits, you may neither park nor charge. Those who refuse to correct it are fined 500 to 1,000 yuan if they are individuals or units not running a business, and 2,000 to 10,000 yuan if they run a business
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 应急管理部 (2021). 高层民用建筑消防安全管理规定（应急管理部令第 5 号，第三十七条、第四十七条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-08/01/content_5628815.htm>
- Notes: Do not buy modified batteries, do not use off-brand chargers, do not leave it charging unattended overnight. Once a lithium battery catches fire, it burns over an entire area within tens of seconds while giving off highly toxic smoke. A bike blocking the stairwell amounts to setting fire to your only escape route. TODO (to verify: official annual national figures on e-bike fires and deaths)

### 7. Measure your blood pressure; if it is high, take medication and bring it to target
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A digital blood pressure monitor runs 100 to 200 yuan. One measurement takes a minute. Most blood pressure drugs cost a few yuan to a few dozen yuan a month. The hard part is staying on the medication even without symptoms.
- In plain terms: For every 10 mmHg your blood pressure goes down, your chance of a major cardiovascular problem drops by 20%, stroke by 27%, heart failure by 28%, and death by 13%. Among people in China aged 35 to 75, 44.7% have hypertension, and only 7.2% actually have it under control.
- Benefit: Pooling 123 trials with more than 610,000 people and calculating them together. For every 10 mmHg drop in systolic pressure, major cardiovascular events fall by about 20%. Raw value RR 0.80, confidence range 95% CI 0.77 to 0.83. Systolic pressure is the higher of the two numbers in a blood pressure reading. Stroke falls by about 27% (RR 0.73). Heart failure falls by about 28% (RR 0.72). Overall mortality drops 13% (RR 0.87, 95% CI 0.84 to 0.91). China screened 1.7 million people aged 35 to 75: 44.7% had hypertension. Among them, 44.7% knew they had it, 30.1% were on treatment, and only 7.2% had it controlled
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ettehad D 等 (2016). Blood pressure lowering for prevention of cardiovascular disease and death: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(15)01225-8> ; Lu J 等 (2017). Prevalence, awareness, treatment, and control of hypertension in China (China PEACE Million Persons Project). Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(17)32478-9>
- Notes: Start by measuring your blood pressure once. In China, fewer than 1 in 10 people with hypertension has it under control, so this single measurement is one of the most cost-effective actions in the book. What counts as target (130 or 140) is still debated, but "knowing you have high blood pressure and bringing it down" is not in dispute.

### 8. After age 35, if you are overweight get your fasting glucose checked once; repeat every three years even if normal
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A fasting glucose test costs a dozen or so yuan, glycated hemoglobin a few dozen yuan. One blood draw at the hospital is all it takes.
- In plain terms: For a few dozen yuan and one tube of blood, prediabetes can be caught at a stage when you have no symptoms at all. The official US screening recommendation: people aged 35 to 70 who are overweight or obese should get tested. If the result is normal, get tested again every three years.
- Benefit: The US Preventive Services Task Force is the official US body that issues screening recommendations. Its current recommendation: screen adults aged 35 to 70 who are overweight or obese for prediabetes and type 2 diabetes, even if they have no symptoms at all. Overweight means a body mass index ≥25, obesity ≥30. This recommendation carries a grade of B. It also states: "For adults with normal blood glucose, screening every 3 years is a reasonable practice"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Screening for Prediabetes and Type 2 Diabetes. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/screening-for-prediabetes-and-type-2-diabetes>
- Notes: Testing positive for prediabetes does not mean you must start medication. A considerable share of people can turn it around with lifestyle changes alone. Among Chinese adults, only a minority know they have diabetes; many find out only after complications appear

### 9. Do not speed and do not drink and drive
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Nothing. Drive a bit slower and each trip takes a few extra minutes. The hard part is drinking less at dinner parties.
- In plain terms: For every 1% increase in average speed, the chance of killing someone in a crash rises by about 4%. With alcohol there is no "a little is fine" safe line. Risk starts climbing when the alcohol concentration in the blood is still very low.
- Benefit: According to the World Health Organization (WHO): for every 1% increase in average speed, the risk of a fatal crash rises 4%. The risk from drunk driving starts rising from very low blood alcohol concentrations
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: WHO (2025). Road traffic injuries fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries>
- Notes: The 1% / 4% pairing for speed and death is derived from a crash model, not a randomized trial. For drunk driving, WHO gave only the direction, not numbers. The question of "how much you drink, how much risk rises" was not verified against the primary literature in this section.

### 10. Use a child safety seat for children under 4; do not hold them in your arms
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One runs 300 to 2,000 yuan and lasts several years. One extra minute each trip to buckle the child in.
- In plain terms: For infants under one year old, riding in a safety seat cuts the chance of fatal injury in a car crash by about 70%. For children aged 1 to 4, by about half. Holding the child in your arms gives none of this protection — in a hard braking you cannot hold on either.
- Benefit: The US traffic safety authority (NHTSA) estimates that using a safety seat cuts the risk of fatal injury for infants under 1 year old in a car by 71%. For toddlers aged 1 to 4, by 54%. The World Health Organization (WHO) puts infant mortality reduction at 71%
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: NHTSA (2024). Occupant Protection in Passenger Vehicles: 2022 Data (DOT HS 813 573). <https://crashstats.nhtsa.dot.gov/Api/Public/ViewPublication/813573> ; WHO (2025). Road traffic injuries fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries>
- Notes: Fix the seat in place according to the manual; installing it wrong is the same as not installing it. Under age 1 it must face rearward, that is, with the child looking at the back of the car. The figures above are US official estimates from crash data.

### 11. If there are small children at home, fit window and balcony limiters; a window screen is not protection
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A set of window limiters or child safety locks costs a few dozen yuan. Ten minutes to install.
- In plain terms: A set of limiters costs a few dozen yuan. New York City installed window guards free of charge for high-risk households and did in-person outreach, and reported child falls in one borough dropped by half in three years. New York later went as far as passing a law requiring landlords to install guards in homes where small children live. A screen pops open with one push; it does not count as protection.
- Benefit: Starting in 1972, New York City ran a program called "Children Can't Fly," distributing window guards free in high-risk neighborhoods and doing door-to-door education. Reported falls in the Bronx fell 50% from 1973 to 1975. Afterwards, New York City amended its sanitary code to require landlords to install window guards in apartments where children aged 10 or younger live
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Spiegel CN, Lindaman FC (1977). Children can't fly: a program to prevent childhood morbidity and mortality from window falls. American Journal of Public Health：「Significant reduction in falls resulted, particularly in the Bronx, where reported falls declined 50 percent from 1973 to 1975」，1976 年纽约市卫生法典修订「to require that landlords provide window guards in apartments where children ten years old and younger reside」. <https://doi.org/10.2105/AJPH.67.12.1143>；(2021). Unintentional Window Falls in Children and Adolescents. Academic Pediatrics：2007 年 1 月至 2017 年 8 月全国电子伤害监测系统 38,840 例急诊就诊，「The majority of falls occurred in children under the age of 6 and were related to falls from a second story or below」. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.acap.2020.07.008>
- Notes: Do not place beds, sofas, boxes, or anything else a child can climb by the window or balcony edge. A screen only keeps mosquitoes out; it comes off with one push, so never treat it as a guard. It is not only high floors that need this either: in that US data, most falls happened from the second floor or lower.

### 12. Keep children in sight near water; wear a life jacket when boating or swimming in open water
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A life jacket runs 50 to 200 yuan. On top of that comes the attention it takes to watch the child.
- In plain terms: When you fall into the water wearing a life jacket, your chance of drowning is only about half of what it would be without one. Drowning is still the number one cause of death for children aged 1 to 14 in China. The drowning death rate in rural areas is about twice that of cities.
- Benefit: A study using US Coast Guard data matched people with similar circumstances into pairs for comparison. For people who fell out of recreational boats, wearing a life jacket cut the risk of drowning by about half. The raw value is an adjusted RR 0.51, confidence range 95% CI 0.35 to 0.74. In China, the drowning death rate for those under 20 fell from 6.60 per 100,000 people in 2013 to 3.28 per 100,000 people in 2021. Rural areas run about 2 times the urban rate. Drowning remains the leading cause of death for children aged 1 to 14. Among injury deaths of those aged 0 to 19 in 2021, drowning accounted for 31.1% and road traffic for 27.9%
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Cummings P 等 (2011). Association between wearing a personal floatation device and death by drowning among recreational boaters. Injury Prevention. <https://doi.org/10.1136/ip.2010.028688> ; Li Z 等 (2023). Unintentional Drowning Mortality Among Individuals Under Age 20 — China, 2013–2021. China CDC Weekly. <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2023.198> ; Zhou J 等 (2024). Injury Mortality of Children and Adolescents Aged 0–19 Years — China, 2010–2021. China CDC Weekly. <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2024.057>
- Notes: Grade A applies only to the life jacket half. "Never out of sight" is common wisdom; on its own it is only grade C — no randomized trial exists, and none ever will. Drowning deaths in China cluster in rural areas, in summer, and among males aged 15 to 19.

### 13. After 60, train balance and leg strength; adapt the bathroom and stairs at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Train 2 to 3 times a week, 30 minutes of tai chi or balance exercises each time. Non-slip mats, grab bars, and night lights for the bathroom run a few dozen to a few hundred yuan. The hard part is keeping the training up long-term.
- In plain terms: Practicing balance two or three times a week, for example tai chi, cuts the number of falls among older people by about 23% and the number of people who fall by 15%. Making the home non-slip and installing grab bars cuts it by another 20% or so. Falls are the number one cause of injury death among people over 65 in China, and more than half of them happen in the person's own home.
- Benefit: Cochrane pooled 108 trials with 23,407 people and calculated them together. Exercise cuts the number of falls among older people by 23%. The raw value is a rate ratio for falls RaR 0.77, confidence range 95% CI 0.71 to 0.83. The number of people who fall drops 15% (RR 0.85). Having someone come to the home for a safety assessment and modifications lowers the fall rate by about 20% (RR 0.81, 95% CI 0.68 to 0.97). Practicing tai chi lowers fall risk by about 29% (RR 0.71). In China, falls are the leading cause of injury death for people over 65. Of falls in older people recorded by surveillance in 2018, 55.97% happened at home
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Sherrington C 等 (2019). Exercise for preventing falls in older people living in the community. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD012424.pub2> ; Gillespie LD 等 (2012). Interventions for preventing falls in older people living in the community. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007146.pub3> ; Lu Z 等 (2021). Characteristics of Falls Among Older People — China, 2018. China CDC Weekly. <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2021.013>
- Notes: This applies to people over 60 living in their own homes, not in care facilities. The studies above counted the number of falls, not the number of deaths. The road from a fall to a hip fracture and on to death is short for an older person.

### 14. Get tested for hepatitis B (the five-panel test); if you have no antibodies, get vaccinated
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One test costs a few dozen yuan. Three doses of adult hepatitis B vaccine come to 100 to 300 yuan in total, given within six months.
- In plain terms: One test costs a few dozen yuan. In the Qidong trial, which followed people for 30 years, those vaccinated against hepatitis B at birth later had an 84% lower chance of primary liver cancer and were 72% less likely to carry the hepatitis B virus. An adult who tests negative for antibodies gets three catch-up doses within half a year, for one to two hundred yuan.
- Benefit: The Qidong trial was randomized by group rather than by individual and followed participants for 30 years. Vaccinating newborns against hepatitis B cut primary liver cancer by 84%. The confidence range is 95% CI 23% to 97%. The hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg) positive rate fell 72% (95% CI 68% to 75%); that positivity rate is the share of people found to be carrying the virus. Across China's whole population, the share of HBsAg-positive people fell 52% from 1992 to 2014, and by 97% among children under 5
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Qu C 等 (2014). Efficacy of neonatal HBV vaccination on liver cancer and other liver diseases over 30-year follow-up of the Qidong hepatitis B intervention study. PLoS Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1001774> ; Cui F 等 (2017). Prevention of Chronic Hepatitis B after 3 Decades of Escalating Vaccination Policy, China. Emerging Infectious Diseases. <https://doi.org/10.3201/eid2305.161477>
- Notes: This applies to adults born before 1992 who were never vaccinated or whose antibodies have faded. The 84% liver cancer figure comes from people vaccinated at birth. In the same trial, those who only got catch-up doses as adolescents had an efficacy of just 21% against becoming a virus carrier. For adults, the point of vaccination is mainly to avoid new infection, so the 84% figure cannot be applied to them directly. Vaccination does nothing for people already infected; they need regular follow-up at a hepatology department. How to tell you are already infected: the hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg) line on your lab report is positive.

### 15. If punctured by a nail or wood splinter or if a wound gets dirt in it, get it treated the same day and ask whether you need a tetanus shot
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Registration plus wound treatment costs a few dozen yuan. If the doctor says a shot is needed, the vaccine or immunoglobulin runs a few dozen to a few hundred yuan.
- In plain terms: Once tetanus develops, roughly 1 in every 10 people dies. Deep wounds and wounds contaminated with soil carry the highest risk. Getting it treated the same day costs no more than a few dozen yuan; do not wait until the wound swells up.
- Benefit: Among tetanus cases in the US, roughly 1 in every 10 is fatal. Deep wounds and wounds contaminated with soil carry higher risk
- Evidence grade: B

### 16. Women: get the HPV vaccine, the earlier the better

- Cost: The domestic bivalent vaccine runs about 300-plus yuan a dose, the imported nine-valent about 1,300 yuan a dose. 2 to 3 doses in total. Half a day for each visit.

### 18. Women over 30: get screened for cervical cancer, HPV testing first

- Cost: An HPV test runs 100 to 300 yuan a time. If the result is negative, once every 5 years is enough. The sample takes a few minutes.
- In plain terms: In that trial in rural India, women who had an HPV test just once in their lives were about half as likely to die of cervical cancer, and about 50% less likely to be found with late-stage cervical cancer. - Benefit: The trial in rural India was randomized by group rather than by individual. Its subjects were women aged 30 to 59. ### 19. From 45 to 50, start colorectal cancer screening: a fecal immunochemical test for occult blood in stool, or a colonoscopy

### 22. After 65, get the pneumococcal vaccine
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A few hundred yuan. Free for older adults in some regions.
- In plain terms: In a trial with 84,500 older people, the 13-valent pneumococcal vaccine blocked about 46% of the pneumonia types it covers and 75% of the most dangerous invasive infections. But it showed no effect against "pneumonia from all causes" — do not expect it to cover all pneumonia.
- Benefit: The Netherlands randomly split 84,500 people over 65 into two groups for comparison. The vaccine used was the 13-valent pneumococcal conjugate vaccine. Its efficacy against vaccine-type pneumonia was 45.6%. The confidence range is 95.2% CI 21.8–62.5. Vaccine types are the few strains this vaccine can handle. Its efficacy against invasive pneumococcal disease was 75.0% (95% CI 41.4–90.8), the most dangerous category of infection. Against pneumonia from all causes combined, it had no effect

- Sources: Bonten MJ, Huijts SM, Bolkenbaas M, et al. (2015). Polysaccharide conjugate vaccine against pneumococcal pneumonia in adults. New England Journal of Medicine, 372(12), 1114-1125. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1408544>

### 23. Test for Helicobacter pylori; if positive, eradicate it

- In plain terms: In the Linqu, Shandong trial, which followed people for 22 years, those who had H. pylori eradicated later had about half the chance of stomach cancer and about 40% lower chance of dying from it. One test costs one to two hundred yuan; eradication takes two weeks of medication.
- Benefit: The randomized trial in Linqu, Shandong followed participants for 22 years. People who received H. pylori eradication treatment had a risk of stomach cancer about half as high. ### 24. Heavy smokers: get a low-dose chest CT once a year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One scan runs 200 to 400 yuan and takes 10 minutes. Also count the follow-up testing after a false alarm, and the dread during that stretch of time.
- In plain terms: Heavy smokers who qualify and get a low-dose CT every year are 20% less likely to die of lung cancer than those who only get a chest X-ray, and 6.7% less likely to die overall. The price is follow-up testing after a suspicious result and the dread that comes with it.
- Benefit: The US NLST randomized trial enrolled 53,454 people, all aged 55 to 74 with a smoking history of 30 pack-years or more who had quit no more than 15 years ago. A pack-year is the unit for counting smoking: one pack a day for one year equals one pack-year. The results: compared with people who only got chest X-rays, those who got low-dose CT had a 20.0% relative reduction in lung cancer death. Confidence range 95% CI 6.8 to 26.7. Overall mortality fell relatively by 6.7%, confidence range 95% CI 1.2 to 13.6

- Sources: National Lung Screening Trial Research Team (2011). Reduced Lung-Cancer Mortality with Low-Dose Computed Tomographic Screening. New England Journal of Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1102873>

### 27. If blood is visible in your urine, get checked once — even if it does not hurt, even if it is gone the next day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->

- In plain terms: For people over 60, a single episode of visibly bloody urine ends in a bladder cancer diagnosis about 2.8% of the time; at ages 40 to 59, about 1.2%. ### 28. When erectile function falters, get your cardiovascular system checked first; do not treat it as purely a matter "down there"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Registration costs a few dozen yuan. Blood pressure measurement plus blood tests for glucose and lipids run one to two hundred yuan. One hospital visit, a few days to wait for results.
- In plain terms: Men with erectile dysfunction go on to develop cardiovascular disease about 40% more often than others, and heart attacks about 60% more often. The younger the man, the bigger the gap. This problem often shows up before chest pain does. So get your blood pressure, blood glucose, and blood lipids checked first; do not just buy pills to paper over it.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis combined 14 follow-up studies totaling 92,757 men, followed on average 6.1 years. Compared with men without erectile dysfunction, those with it faced the following risks afterwards. Overall cardiovascular events about 44% higher (RR 1.44, 95% CI 1.27 to 1.63; this is the confidence range). Myocardial infarction about 62% higher (RR 1.62, 1.34 to 1.96). Cerebrovascular events about 39% higher (RR 1.39, 1.23 to 1.57). Death from all causes about 25% higher (RR 1.25, 1.12 to 1.39). Cardiovascular death did not separate from the comparison group (RR 1.19, 0.97 to 1.46). The younger the age, the higher the multiple. Among men whose baseline cardiovascular risk was moderate, the multiple was higher than in both the high-risk and low-risk groups. Studies that judged the condition with a questionnaire yielded a multiple of 1.61 (1.38 to 1.86). Studies asking a single question: 1.27 (1.18 to 1.37)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Vlachopoulos CV, Terentes-Printzios DG, Ioakeimidis NK, Aznaouridis KA, Stefanadis CI (2013). Prediction of cardiovascular events and all-cause mortality with erectile dysfunction: a systematic review and meta-analysis of cohort studies. Circulation: Cardiovascular Quality and Outcomes, 6(1), 99-109. <https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCOUTCOMES.112.966903>；DailyMed（美国国立医学图书馆的药品说明书库）. VIAGRA (sildenafil citrate) tablet, CONTRAINDICATIONS. <https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=d905dc8d-917f-4ea3-a4ee-a1ecf6967d4e>
- Notes: All 14 are studies that followed a group of people and looked at outcomes, not randomized trials. So the "40% more" is an association and cannot be read as causation. An occasional one-off does not count; only when it persists beyond three months is it treated as a problem. ### 29. Losing weight, quitting smoking, and keeping blood pressure and glucose under control also improve erectile function
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Nothing — it may even save money. The hard part is sticking with it long-term: control what you eat, get moving, quit smoking.
- In plain terms: Bring cardiovascular risk factors under control and erectile function itself improves. Pooling randomized trials, the questionnaire score rose by 2 to 3 points on average. The questionnaire's maximum is 25 points, with 22 or above counted as normal.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis combined 6 randomized controlled trials with 740 men. The interventions were lifestyle changes, or medication to control cardiovascular risk factors, with follow-up of at least 6 weeks. Result: scores on the erectile function questionnaire (IIEF-5) rose by 2.66 points on average (95% CI 1.86 to 3.47; this is the confidence range). Dropping the 2 trials that used statins (143 people), the remaining 4 trials relying purely on lifestyle change (597 people) still improved by 2.40 points (1.19 to 3.61). IIEF-5 is a 5-question questionnaire, scored 5 to 25. The original text sorts scores into five tiers: 22 to 25 counts as no dysfunction, 5 to 7 as severe
- Evidence grade: A

### 30. Use a condom for the entire act of sex, and never share needles
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One to two yuan apiece. The hard part is not the money; it is using one every single time, and putting it on from the very start.
- In plain terms: Worn every time, for the entire act, condoms cut the probability of heterosexual HIV transmission by more than 70%. "Most of the time" barely differs from not wearing one at all. Syphilis and gonorrhea are blocked by the same barrier.
- Benefit: One study combined 25 studies with 10,676 heterosexual couples. In each couple one partner had HIV and the other did not — formally called serodiscordant. Compared with people who never use them, those who used a condom every time had an HIV transmission risk about 71% lower. Raw value RR 0.29, confidence range 95% CI 0.20 to 0.43. Compared with inconsistent users, about 77% lower (RR 0.23, 95% CI 0.13 to 0.40). The authors' conclusion: condoms are imperfect, but "consistent use can reduce HIV transmission by over 70%." The same layer of protection covers syphilis, gonorrhea, chlamydia, and other sexually transmitted infections

### 31. After a high-risk encounter, get tested for HIV once; it is free at the CDC and the result is confidential

### 32. The moment a suicidal thought appears, tell one person nearby; hand over those tens of minutes
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Nothing. The hard part is speaking up in the moment, and admitting you cannot be alone for these few hours.
- In plain terms: Of 82 people who attempted suicide, 47.6% said no more than 10 minutes passed from the first thought to the act. What has to be survived is not an unbearable lifetime but tens of minutes. In the same group, 76.8% had some contact with another person during that window — they just did not say it. Looking further out: of people who reach hospital after self-harm, about 2% die by suicide within a year and about 7% over 9 or more years; the great majority do not die this way.
- Benefit: 82 suicide attempt survivors were interviewed within 3 days of admission: 47.6% (39 people) said no more than 10 minutes elapsed from the first suicidal thought of that episode to the act itself. People whose process took longer scored notably higher on suicide intent. Scores on an impulsivity scale had no relation to how long the process lasted. 76.8% (63 people) said they had some contact with a partner, family member, or friend during that time. The authors' conclusion: the window for intervention is usually very short, and professionals have limited room to step in at that stage — which is why recognizing the signals and the methods of intervention must be spread among ordinary people. Separately, a systematic review pooled 90 follow-up studies. Among people treated after self-harm, the median share who self-harmed again without dying within a year was 16%. Deaths by suicide within 1 year were 2%. Over 9 or more years of follow-up, about 7% died by suicide
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Deisenhammer EA, Ing CM, Strauss R, Kemmler G, Hinterhuber H, Weiss EM (2009). - Notes: Why B: the Deisenhammer study had only 82 people, came from a single hospital, and relied on retrospective recall. ### 33. Do not count on being "brought back" as a safety net: after drinking pesticide or inhaling gas, the ER can save your life but not your lungs or brain

- Cost: Nothing. Just knowing this in advance.
- In plain terms: Paraquat has no antidote; gastric lavage should not be done, and hemoperfusion or dialysis does not change the course of the disease. The body actively pumps it into the lungs, causing pneumonia and pulmonary fibrosis, while also damaging the kidneys and liver. In 257 cases across 10 hospitals in Bangladesh the case fatality rate was 43.2%. Carbon monoxide is the other extreme: six weeks later, 46.1% of the normal-pressure oxygen group had cognitive impairment, versus 25.0% in the hyperbaric oxygen group. ### 35. Do not trade "one kidney is no big deal" for money: the remaining one has to work for two, and 86% of kidney sellers later said their health got worse

- Cost: Nothing. Just see the ledger clearly first.
- In plain terms: First look at the best case: properly screened donors with postoperative follow-up. In that US dataset of 96,000 people, 15 years after donating a kidney, 31 in 10,000 had reached kidney failure, versus 4 in 10,000 among people equally healthy and equally eligible to donate who did not. Now the selling side: of 305 people in India who had sold a kidney, surveyed on average 6 years later, 86% said their health had worsened and three-quarters were still in debt. ### 36. Do not pick up, dismantle, or sell metal parts of unknown origin; keep away from anything bearing the trefoil warning symbol
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Nothing. Seeing an unfamiliar metal cylinder, lead pot, or a component removed from equipment, walk around it: do not pick it up, do not dismantle it, do not put it in your pocket.
- In plain terms: Radiation has no smell, color, or temperature; you cannot sense it, so "looks harmless" cannot be used to judge. Most radioactive sources are just a piece of ordinary metal that neither glows nor heats. The chance of an ordinary person running into one is extremely low: about 178,000 radioactive sources are in use nationwide, and the accident rate holds steady below 1 incident per 10,000 sources. But that one encounter is irreversible: in Brazil in 1987, people dismantled a radiotherapy machine from an abandoned clinic as scrap metal; about 112,000 people were monitored and 4 died.
- Benefit: As of 2025, there are about 178,000 radioactive sources in use nationwide and 355,000 units (sets) of radiation-emitting devices. The annual incidence of radiation accidents involving radioactive sources holds steady below 1 per 10,000 sources. The classification works like this. Loss, theft, or loss of control of a Category Ⅰ or Category Ⅱ source constitutes a major radiation accident. Category Ⅲ is a larger accident. Loss or theft of Category Ⅳ or Ⅴ sources, or exposure of people above the annual dose limit, is an ordinary radiation accident. Official case: in June 2020, the ironmaking plant of Ningxia Iron and Steel Group was performing gamma-ray flaw detection on a blast furnace pipeline retrofit project. A Category Ⅱ iridium-192 source irradiated three workers. Chen suffered acute radiation skin injury, grade Ⅲ, on the left hand. Liu suffered grade Ⅳ injury to the left thumb and index finger, grade Ⅲ to the middle, ring, and little fingers. Fan suffered grade Ⅰ–Ⅱ injury to the left hand. All three received doses far exceeding the national dose limits, and the two responsible units had their radiation safety licenses revoked and were fined 70,000 yuan. International case: in Goiânia, Brazil, in 1987, a teletherapy machine in an abandoned clinic was dismantled, the 50.8 TBq cesium-137 source inside removed, and the sealed capsule holding the source broken open. About 112,000 people were monitored; 249 were contaminated internally or externally. Of those 249, 129 were moderately to severely contaminated and 50 needed close medical supervision. In the end 4 died (national / international)

- Notes: If you actually encounter one: keep your distance, remember the location, do not move it, call 110.
