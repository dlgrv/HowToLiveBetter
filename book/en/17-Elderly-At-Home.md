# 17. Elderly at home

Scope: money and personal freedom. When you have elderly family at home, most trouble is not running out of money—it is failing to talk things through and get papers signed in advance. The last three entries cover pressure ulcers with long-term bed rest, cataract surgery, and fractures from an elder walking a dog; those are counted in mortality terms and are not compared with other entries in this section.
### 1. While the elder is still lucid, name a future guardian in writing
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: A few hundred CNY at a notary office (公证处) for one document. One conversation with the elder can settle it. The hard part is bringing it up: you have to talk face to face about the day they will no longer be able to manage for themselves.
- In plain terms: While the elder’s mind is still clear, spend a few hundred yuan at a notary office on a written document that names who will be their guardian later. Without it, once they cannot decide for themselves, a court decides who signs for them and who handles their money. If family disagree, that takes even longer.
- Benefit: The Civil Code (民法典) lets lucid adults who can decide for themselves (legally, adults with full capacity for civil conduct (具有完全民事行为能力的成年人)) agree in advance who will be their guardian. That person can be a close relative or any willing individual or organization. The agreement must determine the guardian in writing (以书面形式确定自己的监护人). When they later cannot decide at all, or can decide only in part (lose or partially lose capacity for civil conduct (丧失或者部分丧失民事行为能力)), the named person takes charge. Without that document, who signs and who manages money goes through court appointment.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第三十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: This is voluntary guardianship (意定监护)—someone you choose yourself in advance. That person ranks ahead of the default statutory guardianship order. Elders living alone, with children far away, or in complicated family situations need this more. Having it notarized at a notary office is recommended.

### 2. Make a will; later ones override earlier ones, and notarized wills no longer rank first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Handwriting a will yourself costs nothing. Notarization at a notary office (公证处) runs from a few hundred to over a thousand CNY. The hard part is bringing it up: you must discuss end-of-life matters with family.
- In plain terms: The law recognizes six types of will, but one formal mistake voids the whole document—that is the most common failure. For a fully handwritten will, the testator must write every word, sign, and date it. If several wills conflict, the last one controls. Since 2021, a notarized will no longer automatically ranks above the others.
- Benefit: The Civil Code (民法典) accepts six will forms: wholly handwritten, written by another person, printed, audio/video, oral, and notarized. Each has required formal steps (形式要件). If multiple wills conflict, the last one prevails. Since 2021, a notarized will no longer automatically ranks first.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一千一百三十四条至第一千一百四十二条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: For a wholly handwritten will, the testator must write every word, sign, and date it. A printed will needs at least two witnesses. Testator and witnesses must sign every page and date them. Heirs, people named to receive property, and their close relatives cannot serve as witnesses. Wrong form voids the whole will—that is the most common failure.

### 3. Keep the elder’s money in a separate account; set a two-person rule for large spending
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. Talk once with family and pick a dollar threshold. The hard part is how to bring it up so the elder does not feel you are guarding against them.
- In plain terms: Scammers often give small gifts and warm attention first, build trust, then take a large sum at once. They often say “do not tell anyone.” Agree in advance: spending above a set amount must be cleared with a specific person first. That way the family knows, and paying is delayed.
- Benefit: In 2022 elder-fraud case releases, the Supreme People’s Procuratorate (最高检) said these crimes use small favors and feigned kinship to gain trust, then false claims and promised high returns to take large sums. In one case, a fake collectibles firm sent staff to clean and chat with elders and even take them as “godparents.” Goods were sealed in black bags with orders not to open them so family would not see. The firm took more than 1.92 million CNY; the boss received 7 years 6 months for fund-raising fraud. Beijing’s local financial regulator warned in 2023 not to believe “do not tell anyone.” When unsure, consult family, children, and trusted neighbors before investing. U.S. Federal Trade Commission totals reported by people aged 60+ rose from about USD 600 million in 2020 to about USD 2.4 billion in 2024, driven mainly by single losses over USD 100,000. Agreeing that “spending above X must be cleared with someone first” lets the family know before money moves and pushes the decision back.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:最高人民检察院 (2022). 最高检发布打击整治养老诈骗犯罪典型案例（许某燕集资诈骗案及答记者问）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202206/t20220617_560010.shtml>；北京市地方金融监督管理局 (2023). 揭秘专骗老年人新套路 提高防骗意识 守护好「钱袋子」. <https://jrj.beijing.gov.cn/jrxcjy/jraq/ffffjzxkt/202311/t20231127_3310210.html>；Federal Trade Commission (2025). FTC Issues Annual Report to Congress on Agency's Actions to Protect Older Adults（Protecting Older Consumers, 2024-2025）. <https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/12/ftc-issues-annual-report-congress-agencys-actions-protect-older-adults>
- Notes: No study has measured how much less fraud families see with this rule. Grade B because the scam pattern and “consult family more” come from procuratorial and financial regulators, same nature as entry 6 (housing-for-pension). Excitement makes people easier to persuade—see entry 4 (“I need to go home and talk with my family”). General anti-fraud rules are in Section 8 and Section 13. Do not frame it as “afraid you’ll be scammed”; “we track household money in one place” is easier for elders to accept. Do not take all of an elder’s money into a child’s custody—that triggers a different kind of dispute.

### 4. Give the elder a line they can always use: “I need to go home and talk with my family”
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. One conversation, a few minutes.
- In plain terms: Sales pitches and fraud rely on on-the-spot pressure so you pay before you can think. When people are excited, exaggerated ads land harder; elders are more swayed than younger adults. Agree on “I need to go home and talk with my family” in advance. They can say it anytime, save face on the spot, and decide after they cool down at home.
- Benefit: Pitches and fraud both lean on live pressure. The U.S. Federal Trade Commission anti-scam guide says scammers want action before you can think. On phone calls they tell you not to hang up so you cannot verify. The guide says tell someone—a friend, family member, or neighbor—before you act; saying it aloud may expose the scam. The former China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission (银保监会) risk notice in 2021 also told elders to consult family before buying wealth products. One experiment enrolled 116 people aged 65–85 and 115 aged 30–40. Researchers used a win/loss game to stir some people’s emotions, then showed exaggerated ads. Those whose emotions were stirred wanted to buy more. Regardless of arousal, elders wanted to buy more than younger adults. Authors advise waiting until emotions settle before spending. Another experiment enrolled people previously cheated by mail fraud, mostly older. Without prior warning, nearly half agreed outright to a simulated telemarketing scam call. With a reminder call two to four weeks ahead, far fewer agreed outright. Agreeing on “I need to go home and talk with my family” gives the elder an exit without deciding on the spot.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:Federal Trade Commission (2023). How To Avoid a Scam. <https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-avoid-scam>；中国银保监会消费者权益保护局 (2021). 关于警惕「投资养老」「以房养老」金融诈骗的风险提示（2021 年第三期）. <https://dfjrjgj.hunan.gov.cn/dfjrjgj/yhlj/202106/t20210608_19450856.html>（湖南省地方金融监督管理局转载）；Kircanski K, Notthoff N, DeLiema M, et al. (2018). Emotional arousal may increase susceptibility to fraud in older and younger adults. Psychology and Aging, 33(2), 325-337. <https://doi.org/10.1037/pag0000228>；Scheibe S, Notthoff N, Menkin J, et al. (2014). Forewarning reduces fraud susceptibility in vulnerable consumers. Basic and Applied Social Psychology, 36(3), 272-279. <https://doi.org/10.1080/01973533.2014.903844>
- Notes: Grade B because neither experiment tested this exact sentence. One tested buying urge when emotional; one tested whether advance warning helps—no study compared fraud rates for elders who had this line ready. Use together with entry 3 (separate account and two-person rule). Do fall-prevention fixes at home too—see Section 1. The line is a face-saving exit, not a way to control them. When surrounded by sales staff, speaking up to refuse in front of a room is often harder than judging whether the pitch is true.

### 5. Avoid any “invest in elder care” scheme that makes the elder pay first: cards, beds, elder apartments, travel elder care, and conference sales are the same illegal fund-raising pattern
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. Read the list below to the elder once—minutes. Then refuse outright: no lectures, no site visits, no leaving a phone number. Hard if they already went several times and know the promoters.
- In plain terms: Any elder-care label that takes money upfront and promises returns is the same scam: membership cards, bed reservations, elder apartments, travel elder care, conference sales of elder products. Under the rules, losses from this kind of fund-raising are borne by participants themselves. One case raised CNY 136 million by “pre-booking elder apartment beds,” harming more than 2,100 people. The only step you can block is paying.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Civil Affairs (民政部) lists five patterns—recognize the label, not the person. (1) Fake elder-service agencies: no real entity, rented venue, claiming a cooperation deal with a licensed home to “piggyback.” (2) “Elder services”: VIP/membership/prepaid cards, prepaid “service fees,” promises beyond bed capacity. (3) “Elder investment”: stakes in wellness bases, fake elder apartments, long-term bed leases, “time bank” mutual aid, with buyback, leaseback, or repurchase promises. (4) “Elder products”: buyback, consignment, consumption rebates, free exams, gifts, conference marketing, wellness talks, “expert” clinics. (5) “Travel elder care”: cheap or free tours, stored-value rebates, points-based elder care. Recovery is ruled out in the regulations. Article 25 of the Regulations on Prevention and Disposal of Illegal Fund-raising (防范和处置非法集资条例): “Losses suffered from participating in illegal fund-raising shall be borne by the participants themselves.” Reported cases: Dai et al. raised CNY 136 million via pre-booked elder apartment beds, more than 2,100 victims; Dai received 15 years and a CNY 400,000 fine for fund-raising fraud. Cao raised CNY 13.207 billion in the name of elder-industry investment, involving more than 110,000 people—life imprisonment, deprivation of political rights for life, and confiscation of all personal property. Health-product conference sales were prosecuted as fraud: Ni used free meals, gifts, and chauffeured rides to pull elders into lectures; speakers used fake personas, fake cases, and inflated “discounts” to claim anti-cancer effects. Elders paid dozens of times cost for “dihydroquercetin”; sales exceeded CNY 620,000. Ni received 10 years 3 months and a CNY 120,000 fine (nationwide; regulations effective 2021-05-01).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:民政部 (2023). 关于养老服务领域非法集资的风险提示. <https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202306/content_6886556.htm>（中国政府网发布）；民政部、中国银保监会 (2021). 关于养老领域非法集资的风险提示. <https://www.mca.gov.cn/n152/n164/c36171/content.html>；国务院 (2021). 防范和处置非法集资条例（国务院令第 737 号，第二、二十五、三十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-02/10/content_5586632.htm>；最高人民检察院 (2022). 检察机关打击整治养老诈骗犯罪典型案例. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202206/t20220617_560010.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2025). 依法惩治涉民生领域诈骗犯罪典型案例（案例五 倪某荣诈骗案）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/482861.html>
- Notes: Judge by “pay first” and “promised returns,” not “looks legitimate.” Licensed homes sell services; once the pitch adds investment, rebates, or payback, do not trust it. Lectures, tours, and free clinics are not illegal and are hard to stop—the step you can stop is payment, together with entry 3’s two-person rule. If money was already paid, immediately follow Section 8 entry 2: call 110 or 96110 for payment stop, and report to the local lead agency for illegal fund-raising. “Housing-for-pension” is a separate path—see entry 6 (housing reverse mortgage insurance).

### 6. Except insurers’ housing reverse mortgage insurance, avoid all other “housing-for-pension” pitches; never mortgage the home to buy wealth products
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost. Keep the property deed at home; do not hand it to anyone pitching “housing-for-pension” (以房养老). Show mortgage, guarantee, and loan contracts to your children before signing. Hard when door-to-door promoters are warm and keep coming back—the elder feels rude saying no.
- In plain terms: The legitimate form is only housing reverse mortgage insurance (住房反向抵押养老保险) from insurers, still in pilot. Door-to-door pitches are unrelated: they trick elders into mortgaging the house, then using the loan to buy recommended wealth products. Some did not know the home was mortgaged and ended with no house plus debt.
- Benefit: The former CBIRC Consumer Protection Bureau (银保监会消保局) separated real from fake. The real product is “housing reverse mortgage insurance for older persons.” Elders with full legal title mortgage the home to the insurer; they keep living in it, using it, and collecting rent. Sale or disposal needs the mortgagee (insurer) to agree. They receive pension payments under the contract until death. The business remains pilot-scale with few participants. Fraudsters’ “housing-for-pension” is “completely unrelated” to the state pilot and is “using national policy as cover to promote illegal fund-raising.” The method is tricking elders into mortgage, then buying recommended products—“often a Ponzi scheme of paying old debts with new money.” The notice quotes consequences: “Some participants did not even know their property was mortgaged; they ultimately lost the house and still owed the loan.” It also gives yield lines: “If a wealth product promises returns above 6%, question it; above 8% is very dangerous; above 10%, prepare to lose all principal; ‘guaranteed high yield’ is financial fraud.” The same notice requires careful contract signing and not signing blank contracts (nationwide, June 2021).
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:中国银保监会消费者权益保护局 (2021). 关于警惕「投资养老」「以房养老」金融诈骗的风险提示（2021 年第三期）. <https://dfjrjgj.hunan.gov.cn/dfjrjgj/yhlj/202106/t20210608_19450856.html>（湖南省地方金融监督管理局转载）；民政部、中国银保监会 (2021). 关于养老领域非法集资的风险提示（第四种表现形式：以宣称「以房养老」为名非法集资）. <https://www.mca.gov.cn/n152/n164/c36171/content.html>
- Notes: Grade B because these lines come from regulator risk notices and judgment, not research. The 6%, 8%, and 10% lines are consumer reference points, not statutory thresholds. CBIRC was merged into the National Administration of Financial Regulation (国家金融监督管理总局); the original page may be unavailable—provincial financial regulator reposts are cited. Wealth loss is secondary; after the home is mortgaged the elder may still owe a large loan—loss exceeds principal. General rules on signing and blank contracts: Section 8 entry 17. After fraud: payment stop, Section 8 entry 2.

### 7. If an elder is bedbound long term or severely disabled, apply for long-term care insurance (长期护理保险) at the local basic medical insurance (医保) office—it is not only for the elderly
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: One trip to the local medical insurance office where you are enrolled, plus one disability-level assessment. If you pass the first assessment, the fund pays the assessment fee—no out-of-pocket cost.
- In plain terms: Long-term care insurance helps pay for hired care. If someone at home is severely disabled for at least about six months, apply at the local medical insurance office. Eligible care fees need not be paid upfront first; roughly 70% is reimbursed for employee enrollees and about 50% for urban/rural resident enrollees; bed and meal charges are not covered. There is no age floor—young people with severe disability can apply too.
- Benefit: Covered long-term care service fees have no deductible (起付线)—you do not need to pay a threshold amount before reimbursement starts. For those enrolled under the employee policy, the fund reimburses about 70%. For those enrolled under the non-employed urban/rural resident policy, about 50%. The annual cap (年度最高支付限额) is at most 50% of the prior year’s per-capita disposable income of urban/rural residents in the local pooling area.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国家医保局等八部门 (2026). 关于印发《加快建立长期护理保险制度实施方案》的通知. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202603/content_7063915.htm>：保障对象为「按规定参保缴费且失能状态长期持续（一般为6个月以上），经申请通过评估认定的失能人员」，「制度起步阶段保障重度失能人员」；「待遇享受不设起付标准。符合规定的长期护理服务费用，按未就业城乡居民参保政策参保的，基金支付比例为50%左右；按单位职工参保政策参保的，基金支付比例为70%左右，退休人员享受单位职工参保待遇」；「参保人员基金年度最高支付限额不超过统筹地区上年度城乡居民人均可支配收入的50%」；「待遇享受固定等待期原则上按照6个月设置」；另见中共中央办公厅、国务院办公厅《关于加快建立长期护理保险制度的意见》. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12666/202604/content_7065119.html>
- Notes: Disability must be “long-term and ongoing (generally 6 months or more)”—a few weeks in hospital does not qualify. There is also a 6-month waiting period before benefits start. Bed fees, meals, and similar non-care costs are not paid. Medical costs that basic medical insurance should cover are not paid by long-term care insurance. Those already receiving work-injury living-care benefits do not double-dip. This is separate paperwork and money from the disability care subsidy in the two disability subsidies (两项补贴)—no conflict; see Section 7 entry 8. Home and community care often get higher reimbursement rates; institutional care is not required. The program rolls out by locality with different assessment standards. Call 12393 or the local medical insurance handler first to ask whether it is live locally and how to book assessment. If assessment fails, reapply if disability worsens.

### 8. If someone is bedbound long term, treat pressure ulcers as enemy number one: electric alternating-pressure mattress, scheduled turning, daily check of bony prominences
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Home electric alternating-pressure air mattresses start at a few hundred CNY. One turn takes minutes. The hard part is turning every two to three hours, including at night—one caregiver cannot sustain that alone.
- In plain terms: Among elders who already had pressure ulcers before hip-fracture surgery, the chance of dying within 30 days was about one-fifth higher. An electric air mattress cut new ulcers by about 60%. Turning on schedule, done for real, cut risk by about 70%. Do not leave someone prone for long stretches. Check sacrum, heels, hips, and shoulder/back daily; if pressure leaves red that does not blanch, contact a nurse or doctor the same day.
- Benefit: 19,520 hip-fracture surgery patients (U.S. NSQIP 2016–2019). Those with preoperative pressure ulcers had higher 30-day mortality. After adjusting for other factors the association held (OR 1.2, 21% higher odds, P=0.004). They also had more complications: deep vein thrombosis (OR 1.59), pneumonia (OR 1.39), unplanned readmission (OR 1.43), and 0.4 more hospital days on average. 324 hospitalized elders (mean age 86), median follow-up 18 months. Pressure ulcers independently predicted long-term death (HR 1.500, 95% CI 1.059 to 2.126, P=0.020)—meaning after other factors were removed, ulcers alone predicted outcome. Network meta-analysis of 65 randomized trials compared active air support surfaces to standard hospital mattresses. Active air surfaces (electric air mattresses) had lower pressure-ulcer incidence (RR 0.42, 95% CI 0.29 to 0.63, moderate certainty). Mixed active air: RR 0.22 (0.07 to 0.66). Cochrane review (2026 update), 11 trials, 4,462 people: 2-hour vs 4-hour turning intervals—unclear difference (RR 1.05, 95% CI 0.79 to 1.39, very low certainty); other interval comparisons equally uncertain. Trial of 1,226 ICU patients: wearable sensors with real-time alerts helped enforce 2-hour turns; pressure-ulcer incidence below usual care (RR 0.28, 95% CI 0.10 to 0.75, moderate certainty). Trial of 116 patients: prone (face-down) positioning had higher pressure-ulcer incidence than supine (face-up) (RR 4.55, 95% CI 2.31 to 8.98).
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:Porter SB, Pla R, Chow JH, et al. (2022). Preoperative Pressure Ulcers, Mortality, and Complications in Older Hip Fracture Surgery Patients. JAAOS Global Research & Reviews, 6(11). <https://doi.org/10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-22-00117>；Ottaviani S, Rondanina E, Longo E, 等 (2026). Impact of pressure ulcers and frailty on long-term mortality: a prospective cohort study of hospitalized older adults. European Geriatric Medicine, 17(2), 665-672. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s41999-025-01331-8>；Shi C, Dumville JC, Cullum N (2018). Support surfaces for pressure ulcer prevention: A network meta-analysis. PLoS ONE, 13(2), e0192707. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0192707>；Latimer SL, Chaboyer WP, Probst S, 等 (2026). Repositioning for pressure injury prevention in adults. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 6, CD009958. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD009958.pub4>
- Notes: Grade B for three reasons. (1) The two mortality studies only show worse outcomes among people who already had ulcers—not proof that preventing ulcers lowers mortality. (2) Randomized trials on turn intervals are weak. (3) Mattress evidence is stronger but compared hospital mattresses—extrapolation to home mattresses. Daily sacrum/heel/hip/shoulder checks for non-blanching red are standard nursing practice without a dedicated citation. Care services long-term care insurance may cover: this section entry 7. Sudden leg swelling: treat as deep vein thrombosis, Section 13 entry 11. Long bed rest after falls or major injury: Section 1 entry 34.

### 9. If cataracts already affect walking, get an ophthalmology workup for surgery; if both eyes are affected, do not delay the second eye too long
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Accompany the elder to ophthalmology once; the doctor decides whether to operate. One eye per surgery; many hospitals do day surgery and discharge the same day. If both eyes have cataracts, operate in two separate procedures. Out-of-pocket cost depends on intraocular lens (IOL) choice and local basic medical insurance (医保) reimbursement—ask before surgery.
- In plain terms: In women aged 70+, having first-eye cataract surgery sooner cut falls over the next year by about 34%; fewer people fractured bones. The number who fell at least once did not drop. Second-eye surgery showed no clear fall difference. Operated patients had about 16% lower hip fracture over one year.
- Benefit: Randomized trial of 306 women aged 70+ with cataracts. Half had first-eye surgery within about 4 weeks; half waited 12 months per usual care. Over one year, early surgery cut fall counts by about 34%. Rate ratio 0.66, 95% CI 0.45 to 0.96. At least one fall: surgery group 49%, wait group 45%—no reduction. Two or more falls: surgery 18%, wait 25%. Fractures: surgery group 4 (3%), wait group 12 (8%). Cochrane included this trial: first-eye surgery in women reduced fall counts; second-eye surgery did not. Second-eye randomized trial, 239 women aged 70+: surgery group fall rate 32% lower but difference may be chance (rate ratio 0.68, 95% CI 0.39 to 1.19). U.S. Medicare 5% sample: 1,113,640 cataract patients aged 65+ (2002–2009), observational only. Surgery associated with about 16% lower one-year hip fracture odds (OR 0.84, 95% CI 0.81 to 0.87). Absolute difference 0.20 percentage points—about 2 fewer cases per 1,000 people.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:Harwood RH, Foss AJ, Osborn F, Gregson RM, Zaman A, Masud T (2005). Falls and health status in elderly women following first eye cataract surgery: a randomised controlled trial. British Journal of Ophthalmology, 89(1), 53-59. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bjo.2004.049478> ; Foss AJ, Harwood RH, Osborn F, Gregson RM, Zaman A, Masud T (2006). Falls and health status in elderly women following second eye cataract surgery: a randomised controlled trial. Age and Ageing, 35(1), 66-71. <https://doi.org/10.1093/ageing/afj005> ; Gillespie LD, Robertson MC, Gillespie WJ, et al. (2012). Interventions for preventing falls in older people living in the community. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 9, CD007146. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007146.pub3> ; Tseng VL, Yu F, Lum F, Coleman AL (2012). Risk of fractures following cataract surgery in Medicare beneficiaries. JAMA, 308(5), 493-501. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2012.9014> ; Desapriya E, Subzwari S, Scime-Beltrano G, et al. (2010). ### 10. When an elder walks the dog, hold the leash in the palm—not wrapped on the hand—keep it short, and train the dog not to lunge forward
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Holding and shortening the leash costs nothing; walk with the elder once and show them. Training the dog not to lunge takes time; self-training or a dog trainer—trainers cost money and prices vary widely by region. Hard because elders often use retractable leashes and wrap the leash around the wrist—they must change the habit.
- In plain terms: In the U.S., fractures while walking a leashed dog among people aged 65+ rose from about 1,700 to about 4,400 per year; about eight in ten were women. Hip was the most common fracture site; nearly three in ten needed hospital admission. About three-quarters of hand injuries from dog leashes came from the leash wrapped on the hand and the dog pulling. So hold the leash in the palm, keep it short, and train the dog not to lunge.
- Benefit: U.S. emergency surveillance extrapolated nationally: leashed-dog walking fractures among people aged 65+, about 1,671 in 2004 and about 4,396 in 2017, a statistically significant rise. Among fracture patients 78.6% were female, 28.7% hospitalized. By single site, hip most common at 17.3%; by region, upper limb most at 52.1%. Another national emergency dataset (2001–2018): dog-leash-related injuries extrapolated to about 360,000 (exact 356,746). Of these, 193,483 were dog-pull injuries and 136,767 trips or entanglement. Injuries per million population rose from 25.4 to 105.5. One hand-surgery clinic over 5 years: 443 dog-leash hand injuries; about three-quarters were hand or finger entanglement with a pull (abstract: 337, 75.9%). About one-fifth needed surgery (96, 21.6%). U.S. CDC: 2001–2006, about 86,629 fall injuries per year associated with cats and dogs, 88.0% with dogs. Among dog-related falls, 26% happened while walking the dog and 21.2% from the dog knocking or pulling the person down.
- Evidence grade: B
