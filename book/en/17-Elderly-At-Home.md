# 17. Elderly at home

Scope: money and personal freedom. When you have elderly family at home, most trouble is not running out of money. It is failing to talk things through and get papers signed in advance. Pressure ulcers from long-term bed rest, cataract surgery, fractures from an elder walking a dog, and a hospital check when memory gets worse: these four are counted in mortality terms and are not compared with the other entries in this section.
### 1. While the elder is still lucid, name a future guardian in writing
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: A few hundred yuan at a notary office (公证处) for the document. A conversation with the elder can settle it. The hard part is bringing it up: you have to say, face to face, that a day will come when they can no longer manage for themselves.
- In plain terms: While the elder's mind is still clear, spend a few hundred yuan at a notary office on a written document that names who will be their guardian later. Without that document, once they cannot decide for themselves, the order set by law applies: spouse first, then parents and children, then other close relatives. If the family fights over it, the residents' committee (居委会), the villagers' committee (村委会), the civil affairs bureau (民政), or a court names the guardian.
- Benefit: The Civil Code (民法典) says an adult who is clear-headed and can decide for themselves (in law, an adult with full capacity for civil conduct (具有完全民事行为能力的成年人)) may agree in advance who will be their guardian. That person can be a close relative, or another willing individual or organization. The agreement must name the guardian in writing (以书面形式确定自己的监护人). When they later cannot decide, or can decide only in part (lose or partly lose capacity for civil conduct (丧失或者部分丧失民事行为能力)), the person named in the document takes charge. Without that document, Article 28 of the Civil Code sets the guardian in this order: spouse; parents and children; other close relatives; then another willing individual or organization with the consent of the residents' committee, the villagers' committee, or the civil affairs bureau. Article 31 says that if people dispute who the guardian should be, the residents' committee, the villagers' committee, or the civil affairs bureau designates one. Anyone who disagrees may ask a court to designate the guardian, and they may also apply to a court directly.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第二十八条、第三十一条、第三十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: This document is agreed guardianship (意定监护), meaning a person the elder names in advance. That person ranks ahead of the default legal order. Elders who live alone, whose children live far away, or whose family situation is complicated need this more. Notarizing it at a notary office is recommended.

### 2. Make a will, and remember that a later will replaces an earlier one; a notarized will no longer ranks ahead
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Writing a will in your own hand costs nothing. Notarizing one at a notary office (公证处) costs a few hundred yuan to more than a thousand yuan. The hard part is bringing it up: you have to talk with the family about what happens after death.
- In plain terms: The law recognizes 6 kinds of will, and each kind has steps that must be completed. The kind you write yourself must be written by the person from start to finish, signed, and dated with the year, month, and day. If several wills conflict, the last one controls. From 2021, a notarized will no longer automatically ranks ahead of the others.
- Benefit: The Civil Code (民法典) recognizes 6 forms of will: written by the person, written by someone else at their request, printed, audio or video, oral, and notarized. Each has its own formal requirements (形式要件). For the kind written by the person, Article 1134 of the Civil Code requires that it be "written by the testator in their own hand, signed, and dated with the year, month, and day." If several wills are made and they conflict, the last one controls. From 2021, a notarized will no longer automatically ranks ahead.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一千一百三十四条至第一千一百四十二条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》继承编的解释（一）（法释〔2020〕23 号，第二十四条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282091.html>
- Notes: A handwritten will must be written by the person from start to finish, signed, and dated with the year, month, and day. A printed will needs 2 or more witnesses. The testator and the witnesses must sign every page and write the year, month, and day. An heir, anyone the will gives property to, and anyone with an interest tied to those people cannot be a witness. The judicial interpretation names the interested persons it means: their creditors, their debtors, and people who are in business with them as partners. Whether a spouse, a child, or another close relative of those people counts, the statute and the interpretation do not say directly. The author suggests not asking them to witness, so the will is not fought over later. If the form is wrong, the whole will is void. That is the most common way these fail. After the person dies, whether a small deposit or a house must be notarized for inheritance is covered in section 25, item 12 (do not default to notarization when handling an inheritance).

### 3. Keep the elder's money in a separate account, and set a two-person rule for large spending
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 人群=老人,60岁 -->
- Cost: No cost. Talk it over with the family once and set an amount. The hard part is how to bring it up so the elder does not feel you are guarding against them.
- In plain terms: People who scam elders often start with small gifts and warm concern, build up trust, then take a large sum. They also often say "do not tell anyone." Agree on a rule in advance: spending above a set amount has to be mentioned to a specific person first. Then the family knows before the money goes out, and the decision to pay is pushed back.
- Benefit: In 2022 the Supreme People's Procuratorate (最高检) released typical elder-fraud cases. It said these crimes first use small favors and a show of family concern to win trust, then false claims and promises of high returns to take large sums. In one case, a company selling fake collectibles sent staff to clean for elders and sit and talk with them, and even took them as "godparents." When they sold the goods they sealed them in a black bag and told the elder not to open it, so the elder's family would not find out. The company took in more than 1920000 yuan. The boss was sentenced to 7 years and 6 months for fund-raising fraud. In 2023 the Beijing Local Financial Regulatory Bureau (北京市地方金融管理局) warned elders not to believe a scammer who says "do not tell anyone." When you are unsure, talk with family, children, and neighbors and friends you trust, and ask the family before you invest. The U.S. Federal Trade Commission totaled the reported losses of people aged 60 and over. In 2020 the total was about 600000000 US dollars. In 2024 it had risen to about 2400000000 US dollars. The rise was mainly cases in which a single loss exceeded 100000 US dollars. Agreeing in advance that "spending above a set amount has to be mentioned to a specific person first" lets the family know before the money goes out, and pushes that decision back.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:最高人民检察院 (2022). 最高检发布打击整治养老诈骗犯罪典型案例（许某燕集资诈骗案及答记者问）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202206/t20220617_560010.shtml>；北京市地方金融监督管理局 (2023). 揭秘专骗老年人新套路 提高防骗意识 守护好「钱袋子」. <https://jrj.beijing.gov.cn/jrxcjy/jraq/ffffjzxkt/202311/t20231127_3310210.html>；Federal Trade Commission (2025). FTC Issues Annual Report to Congress on Agency's Actions to Protect Older Adults（Protecting Older Consumers, 2024-2025）. <https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/12/ftc-issues-annual-report-congress-agencys-actions-protect-older-adults>
- Notes: No study has compared how much less fraud families see after they set this rule. The grade is B because the scam pattern and the advice to "talk with family more" come from the procuratorate and from financial regulators. The basis is the same kind as item 6 (housing-for-pension). A person who is worked up is easier to talk into something: see item 4 ("I need to go home and talk with my family"). General rules for avoiding fraud are in section 8 and section 13. Do not describe this as "we are afraid you will be scammed." Describe it as "the household keeps one set of accounts," which an elder accepts more easily. Do not hand all of an elder's money to the children to hold. That leads to a different kind of dispute.

### 4. Give the elder a line they can always use: “I need to go home and talk with my family”
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 人群=老人,60岁 -->
- Cost: No cost. One conversation, a few minutes.
- In plain terms: Sales pitches and fraud rely on pressure on the spot, so you pay before you have time to think. Once emotions are stirred up, people are more eager to buy what exaggerated ads are selling, and elders are more eager to buy than younger people. Agree with the elder in advance on the line “I need to go home and talk with my family.” They can say it at any time, have a way to step back on the spot, and decide after they cool down at home.
- Benefit: Pitches and fraud both lean on live pressure. The U.S. Federal Trade Commission anti-scam guide says scammers want action before you can think. On phone calls they tell you not to hang up so you cannot verify. The guide says tell someone—a friend, family member, or neighbor—before you act; saying it aloud may expose the scam. The former China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission (银保监会) risk notice in 2021 also told elders to consult family before buying wealth products. One experiment enrolled 116 people aged 65–85 and 115 aged 30–40. Researchers used a win/loss game to stir some people’s emotions, then showed exaggerated ads. Those whose emotions were stirred wanted to buy more. Regardless of arousal, elders wanted to buy more than younger adults. Authors advise waiting until emotions settle before spending. Another experiment enrolled people previously cheated by mail fraud, mostly older. Without prior warning, nearly half agreed outright to a simulated telemarketing scam call. With a reminder call two to four weeks ahead, far fewer agreed outright. Agreeing on “I need to go home and talk with my family” gives the elder an exit without deciding on the spot.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:Federal Trade Commission (2023). How To Avoid a Scam. <https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-avoid-scam>；中国银保监会消费者权益保护局 (2021). 关于警惕「投资养老」「以房养老」金融诈骗的风险提示（2021 年第三期）. <https://dfjrjgj.hunan.gov.cn/dfjrjgj/yhlj/202106/t20210608_19450856.html>（湖南省地方金融监督管理局转载）；Kircanski K, Notthoff N, DeLiema M, et al. (2018). Emotional arousal may increase susceptibility to fraud in older and younger adults. Psychology and Aging, 33(2), 325-337. <https://doi.org/10.1037/pag0000228>；Scheibe S, Notthoff N, Menkin J, et al. (2014). Forewarning reduces fraud susceptibility in vulnerable consumers. Basic and Applied Social Psychology, 36(3), 272-279. <https://doi.org/10.1080/01973533.2014.903844>
- Notes: Grade B because neither experiment tested this exact sentence. One tested buying urge when emotional; one tested whether advance warning helps—no study compared fraud rates for elders who had this line ready. Use together with entry 3 (separate account and two-person rule). Do fall-prevention fixes at home too—see Section 1. The line is a face-saving exit, not a way to control them. When surrounded by sales staff, speaking up to refuse in front of a room is often harder than judging whether the pitch is true.

### 5. Do not touch any "invest in elder care" pitch that makes the elder pay first: a card, a bed, an elder apartment, travel elder care, and selling elder products are the same illegal fund-raising
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 人群=老人,60岁 -->
- Cost: No cost. Read the labels below to the elder once. It takes a few minutes. After that, refuse on the spot: do not go to the lecture, do not go on the tour of the site, and do not leave a phone number. The hard part is that if the elder has already gone several times and gotten to know the people, they are harder to talk back out of it.
- In plain terms: Elder-care labels that make the elder pay first and promise a return are all the same scam. Common ones are buying a card, buying a bed, buying an elder apartment, travel elder care, and selling elder products at a meeting. Under the rules, if this money is lost, you can only bear it yourself. A reported case took 136000000 yuan by pre-booking beds in an elder apartment and harmed more than 2100 people.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Civil Affairs (民政部) lists five forms. Recognizing the label is more reliable than recognizing the person. The first is borrowing the name of an elder-care service agency: there is no real operation, they rent a site for a short time, and they claim a cooperation agreement with an elder-care home so they can trade on its name. The second is in the name of providing "elder-care services": they sell a "VIP card," a "membership card," or a "prepaid card," and have people prepay "elder-care service fees." The service they promise clearly exceeds the beds they can supply. The third is in the name of investing in an "elder-care project." Common labels are buying a stake in a wellness and elder-care base, and selling a fictional elder apartment. There is also long-term rental of elder-care beds, and "time bank" mutual-aid elder care. The cover is sales that promise to return the principal, leaseback after the sale, and an agreed repurchase. The fourth is in the name of selling "elder products": repurchase of goods, consignment sale, consumption rebates, free checkups, gifts, conference marketing, wellness lectures, and expert free clinics. The fifth is in the name of enjoying "travel elder care": cheap or free travel, stored-value rebates, and points-based elder care. The money cannot be got back. The regulation says so. Article 25 of the Regulations on Prevention and Disposal of Illegal Fund-raising (防范和处置非法集资条例) says, "Losses suffered from taking part in illegal fund-raising shall be borne by the participants themselves." Lost money can only be borne by the person who put it in. In officially reported cases, Dai and others illegally raised 136000000 yuan in the name of pre-booking beds in an elder apartment, with more than 2100 victims. Dai was sentenced to 15 years for fund-raising fraud and fined 400000 yuan. Cao, in the name of investing in the elder-care industry, committed fund-raising fraud of 13207000000 yuan, involving more than 110000 people. Cao was sentenced to life imprisonment, deprivation of political rights for life, and confiscation of all personal property. Conference sales of health products were prosecuted as fraud. Ni used free banquets, gifts, and a car to pick people up and drop them off, and pulled elders in to hear lectures. The lecturers used "a fake persona, fake cases, and an inflated price cut" to invent cancer-prevention and cancer-fighting effects. Elders were induced to buy "dihydroquercetin" (二氢槲皮素) at tens of times the cost price. Sales were more than 620000 yuan. Ni was sentenced to 10 years and 3 months and fined 120000 yuan (nationwide; the regulations took effect on 2021-05-01).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:民政部 (2023). 关于养老服务领域非法集资的风险提示. <https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202306/content_6886556.htm>（中国政府网发布）；民政部、中国银保监会 (2021). 关于养老领域非法集资的风险提示. <https://www.mca.gov.cn/n152/n164/c36171/content.html>；国务院 (2021). 防范和处置非法集资条例（国务院令第 737 号，第二、二十五、三十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-02/10/content_5586632.htm>；最高人民检察院 (2022). 检察机关打击整治养老诈骗犯罪典型案例. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202206/t20220617_560010.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2025). 依法惩治涉民生领域诈骗犯罪典型案例（案例五 倪某荣诈骗案）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/482861.html>
- Notes: The judgment does not rest on "this place looks legitimate." It rests on "did they make you pay first, and did they promise a return." An elder-care home sells services. Once the talk adds investment, a rebate, or getting the principal back, do not trust it. An elder being taken to a lecture, a trip, or a free clinic is not itself illegal, and it is hard to stop. The step you can stop is the payment, used together with the two-person confirmation rule in item 3. If money has already been paid, immediately follow section 8, item 2 and call 110 or 96110 to ask for a payment stop. At the same time, report it to the local lead department for handling illegal fund-raising. Housing-for-pension is a different path: see item 6 (housing reverse mortgage insurance). The cap on deposits and membership fees at a legitimate nursing home, and the account that receives them, are in item 11 (a deposit of at most 12 months of bed fees). If a cheap tour took people into a shop and they bought something, see section 5, item 50 (a tour group taken into a shop).

### 6. Except insurers’ housing reverse mortgage insurance, avoid all other “housing-for-pension” pitches; never mortgage the home to buy wealth products
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 人群=老人,60岁 -->
- Cost: No cost. Keep the property deed at home; do not hand it to anyone pitching “housing-for-pension” (以房养老). Show mortgage, guarantee, and loan contracts to your children before signing. Hard when door-to-door promoters are warm and keep coming back—the elder feels rude saying no.
- In plain terms: The only legitimate housing-for-pension arrangement is insurers’ housing reverse mortgage insurance (住房反向抵押养老保险), and it is still a pilot. The “housing-for-pension” that scammers describe is unrelated to it: they trick the elder into mortgaging the house, then using the borrowed money to buy the wealth products they recommend. Some people did not even know the house had been mortgaged. In the end the house was gone and they still owed the loan.
- Benefit: The former CBIRC Consumer Protection Bureau (银保监会消保局) separated real from fake. The real product is “housing reverse mortgage insurance for older persons.” Elders with full legal title mortgage the home to the insurer; they keep living in it, using it, and collecting rent. Sale or disposal needs the mortgagee (insurer) to agree. They receive pension payments under the contract until death. The business remains pilot-scale with few participants. Fraudsters’ “housing-for-pension” is “completely unrelated” to the state pilot and is “using national policy as cover to promote illegal fund-raising.” The method is tricking elders into mortgage, then buying recommended products—“often a Ponzi scheme of paying old debts with new money.” The notice quotes consequences: “Some participants did not even know their property was mortgaged; they ultimately lost the house and still owed the loan.” It also gives yield lines: “If a wealth product promises returns above 6%, question it; above 8% is very dangerous; above 10%, prepare to lose all principal; ‘guaranteed high yield’ is financial fraud.” The same notice requires careful contract signing and not signing blank contracts (nationwide, June 2021).
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources:中国银保监会消费者权益保护局 (2021). 关于警惕「投资养老」「以房养老」金融诈骗的风险提示（2021 年第三期）. <https://dfjrjgj.hunan.gov.cn/dfjrjgj/yhlj/202106/t20210608_19450856.html>（湖南省地方金融监督管理局转载）；民政部、中国银保监会 (2021). 关于养老领域非法集资的风险提示（第四种表现形式：以宣称「以房养老」为名非法集资）. <https://www.mca.gov.cn/n152/n164/c36171/content.html>
- Notes: Grade B because these lines come from regulator risk notices and judgment, not research. The 6%, 8%, and 10% lines are consumer reference points, not statutory thresholds. CBIRC was merged into the National Administration of Financial Regulation (国家金融监督管理总局); the original page may be unavailable—provincial financial regulator reposts are cited. Wealth loss is secondary; after the home is mortgaged the elder may still owe a large loan—loss exceeds principal. General rules on signing and blank contracts: Section 8 entry 17. After fraud: payment stop, Section 8 entry 2.

### 7. If an elder at home is bedbound long term or severely disabled, apply for long-term care insurance at the yibao office where they are enrolled; it is not paid only to the elderly
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Submit an application at the yibao office where the person is enrolled (医保 — basic medical insurance). Then have a disability-level assessment. If they pass on the first assessment, the fund pays the assessment fee. You do not pay it yourself.
- In plain terms: Long-term care insurance pays care-service fees and generally does not pay cash directly. If someone at home is severely disabled and has been so for half a year or more, apply at the yibao office where they are enrolled. Care fees have no deductible (起付线), so reimbursement is not held back until a set amount has been paid first. Employee enrollees get about 70% back. Urban and rural resident enrollees get about 50% back. Bed fees and meal fees are not covered. The document sets no age floor. A young person with severe disability can apply too.
- Benefit: Long-term care service fees that meet the rules have no deductible (起付线). A deductible is a sum you pay yourself before reimbursement starts. Here you do not pay that sum first. People enrolled under the employee policy are reimbursed by the fund at about 70%. People enrolled under the policy for urban and rural residents who are not employed are reimbursed at about 50%. The most that can be paid in a year (the annual payment cap (年度最高支付限额)) is no more than 50% of the previous year's per-capita disposable income of urban and rural residents in the local pooling area. The people who can receive benefits are those who are enrolled and paying as required, whose disability has generally lasted more than 6 months, and who pass an assessment after they apply. At the start, severe disability is covered first. The document sets no age floor. A person under 18 who is not working is enrolled with their parents and, if eligible, receives benefits at the urban and rural resident rate. The fund mainly pays care institutions and care workers. In principle it does not pay cash directly to the disabled person. Institutional bed fees, meal fees, and other charges that are not care are not paid by the fund.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国家医保局等八部门 (2026). 关于印发《加快建立长期护理保险制度实施方案》的通知. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202603/content_7063915.htm>：保障对象为「按规定参保缴费且失能状态长期持续（一般为6个月以上），经申请通过评估认定的失能人员」，「制度起步阶段保障重度失能人员」；「待遇享受不设起付标准。符合规定的长期护理服务费用，按未就业城乡居民参保政策参保的，基金支付比例为50%左右；按单位职工参保政策参保的，基金支付比例为70%左右，退休人员享受单位职工参保待遇」；「参保人员基金年度最高支付限额不超过统筹地区上年度城乡居民人均可支配收入的50%」；「除新生儿等特殊群体外，对未在当地长期护理保险制度启动时初次参保，以及中断缴费后再次参保的情形，应制定待遇享受等待期……待遇享受固定等待期原则上按照6个月设置」；「对机构床位费、膳食费等非护理服务费用以及应由医疗保险支付的医疗费用，基金不予支付」；「原则上不直接向失能人员发放现金」；另见中共中央办公厅、国务院办公厅《关于加快建立长期护理保险制度的意见》. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12666/202604/content_7065119.html>
- Notes: Disability must be "long-term and continuous (generally 6 months or more)." A few weeks in hospital is not enough to apply. If the person was not enrolled when the local program started and enrolled later, or enrolled again after a gap in payment, there is a waiting period before benefits, in principle 6 months. Bed fees, meal fees, and other charges that are not care are not paid by long-term care insurance. Medical costs that yibao should pay are not paid by long-term care insurance either. Someone already receiving a work-injury living-care benefit does not receive this on top. This money and the care subsidy inside the two disability subsidies (两项补贴) are two procedures and two sums of money. They do not conflict. See section 7, item 8 (the two subsidies). Care at home and in the community gets a more favorable reimbursement rate. The person does not have to move into an institution. The program is rolling out place by place, and the disability assessment standards differ. Call 12393 first, or the medical insurance handler where the person is enrolled, and ask whether it has started locally and how to book an assessment. If the assessment fails, do not give up. Apply again after the disability gets worse.

### 8. If someone at home is bedbound long term, treat pressure ulcers as the first enemy: an electric air mattress, turning on a schedule, and a daily look at the places where bone sticks out
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A home electric alternating-pressure air mattress starts at a few hundred yuan. A turn takes only a few minutes. The hard part is turning every 2 to 3 hours, including at night. One caregiver cannot keep that up alone.
- In plain terms: For elders who already had a pressure ulcer before hip-fracture surgery, the chance of dying within 30 days was about 20% higher. Switching to an electric air mattress cut new pressure ulcers by about 60%. In an intensive care unit (ICU), using a sensor reminder so that turning on time actually happened cut them by about 70%, while laying the person face down produced far more pressure ulcers. Look once a day at the sacrum, heels, hips, and the shoulders and back. If you find red that does not fade when pressed, find a nurse or a doctor that same day.
- Benefit: 19520 patients who had hip-fracture surgery (U.S. NSQIP, National Surgical Quality Improvement Program, 2016 to 2019). People who already had a pressure ulcer before surgery had a higher share of deaths within 30 days. After other factors were taken into account, the link still held (OR 1.2, a 21% increase in the odds, P=0.004). They were also more likely to have other problems: deep-vein thrombosis (OR 1.59), pneumonia (OR 1.39), and unplanned readmission (OR 1.43). The average hospital stay was also 0.4 days longer. 324 hospitalized elders (mean age 86) were followed for a median of 18 months. A pressure ulcer on its own predicted long-term death (HR 1.500, 95% CI 1.059 to 2.126, P=0.020). "On its own" means that after other factors are set aside, it still predicts. 65 randomized trials were pooled (a network meta-analysis). People using one had a lower rate of pressure ulcers (RR 0.42, 95% CI 0.29 to 0.63, moderate certainty). A powered hybrid air support surface was RR 0.22 (0.07 to 0.66). A Cochrane review of 11 trials and 4462 people, updated in 2026, compared turning every 2 hours with turning every 4 hours. The difference was unclear (RR 1.05, 95% CI 0.79 to 1.39, very low certainty). Comparisons among other turning intervals were equally uncertain. In a trial of 1226 intensive-care patients, wearable sensors that reminded staff in real time and gave a visual alert made turning every 2 hours actually happen. The pressure-ulcer rate was lower than usual care (RR 0.28, 95% CI 0.10 to 0.75, moderate certainty). In a trial of 116 people, the prone group (lying face down) had a higher pressure-ulcer rate than the supine group (lying flat) (RR 4.55, 95% CI 2.31 to 8.98).

### 10. When an elder walks the dog, hold the leash in the palm, do not wrap it around the hand, keep the leash short, and train the dog not to lunge forward on the leash

- In plain terms: In the United States, fractures among people aged 65 and over while walking a dog were about 1700 in 2004 and about 4400 in 2017. - Benefit: U.S. emergency surveillance data, scaled up to the whole country, counted fractures in people aged 65 and over who fell while walking a dog on a leash. A hand-surgery clinic saw 443 patients with hand injuries from a dog leash over 5 years. ### 13. When an elder's memory keeps getting worse and daily life starts to slip, take them to a memory clinic or neurology for one check, and first rule out depression, medicines, and poor hearing or vision, causes that can be changed

- Cost: A visit to a memory clinic or neurology, with a cognitive test, a blood draw, and a urine test, takes half a day to a day. Fees follow the local schedule. Yibao (医保 — basic medical insurance) reimburses according to the rules. The hard part is that the elder does not admit they are confused and does not want to go.
- In plain terms: Memory that keeps getting worse is not necessarily just muddled old age. First check for depression, medicines that do not suit them, and poor hearing or vision, causes that can be changed. Very few cases can actually be cured. Among people with dementia, those who truly improve are under 1%. Checking early has another use: while the elder can still decide for themselves, settle who will manage their money and their medical care later.
- Benefit: The UK NICE dementia guideline (NG97) requires that, when dementia is suspected, the history be taken from the person and from family who know them. It also requires a physical exam, blood and urine tests to rule out causes that can be reversed, and a cognitive test. The guideline names four things to check first: delirium (sudden confusion that comes in spells), depression, poor vision or hearing, and medicines with a heavy anticholinergic load. If those are ruled out and dementia is still suspected, refer the person to a specialist service such as a memory clinic for a diagnosis. Causes that might be reversed accounted for 9%. Those who truly improved were only 0.6%: partial improvement 0.29%, full improvement 0.31%. The same NICE guideline requires an early discussion with the patient and the family about arrangements made in advance. That includes authorizing someone else to manage health and money, and the person's wishes for future care. A 2024 action plan by 15 departments, including the National Health Commission (国家卫健委), calls for pushing a first cognitive screen for people aged 65 and over, and encourages hospitals to set up memory clinics.
