# 17. When There Are Elderly People at Home

Scope: money and personal freedom. Most of the trouble comes not from a lack of money, but from not having spelled things out and signed the papers in advance.
### 1. Designate a future guardian in writing while the elderly person is still of sound mind
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: a few hundred yuan for notarization; one conversation
- In plain terms: while the elderly person is still clear-headed, spend a few hundred yuan to notarize a written document naming who will be their guardian in the future. Without this document, once the person can no longer make their own decisions, who signs for them and who manages their money has to be decided by a court appointment — and if the family disagrees, that drags on even longer.
- Benefit: The Civil Code provides that an adult with full civil capacity may, through prior consultation with close relatives or other individuals or organizations willing to serve as guardian, "designate their own guardian in writing," and that this guardian performs the duties when the person loses or partially loses civil capacity. Without this document, who signs and who manages the money in the future has to go through a court appointment.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第三十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: This is called yiding jianhu (意定监护 — voluntary guardianship by prior agreement), and it takes precedence over the statutory order of guardianship. It is all the more necessary for those living alone, with children in another city, or in families with complicated relationships. Notarization is recommended.

### 2. Make a will, and remember that the later one overrides the earlier one — notarized wills no longer take priority
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan for a handwritten will; several hundred to over a thousand yuan for a notarized one
- In plain terms: the law recognizes six forms of will, but if the form is wrong the whole will is void — and that is the most common way people get tripped up. A self-written will must be handwritten in full by the testator, signed, and dated with year, month, and day. A printed will requires two or more witnesses, with the testator and witnesses signing and dating every page; heirs, legatees, and their close relatives cannot act as witnesses. If several conflicting wills exist, the last one prevails, and since 2021 a notarized will no longer takes priority automatically.
- Benefit: The Civil Code recognizes six forms of will — self-written, written on behalf, printed, audio-video, oral, and notarized — each with its own formal requirements. Where several wills with conflicting contents have been made, the last one prevails, and since 2021 a notarized will no longer has overriding effect.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一千一百三十四条至第一千一百四十二条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: A self-written will must be handwritten in full by the testator and signed and dated with year, month, and day. A printed will requires two or more witnesses, and the testator and witnesses sign and date every page. Heirs, legatees, and their close relatives cannot serve as witnesses. If the form is wrong, the whole will is void — this is the most common pitfall.

### 3. Keep the elderly person's money in a separate account, and set a rule that large expenditures require confirmation by two people
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: health-product, collectible, and investment scams aimed at the elderly all follow the same pattern — months of trust-building first, then a one-time cash grab. Agreeing in advance that "any spending above a certain amount gets mentioned to one named person first" effectively pushes the moment of paying out past the impulse window. Framing this as "keeping unified records of household money" goes down more easily than "we're afraid you'll be scammed."
- Benefit: Health-product, collectible, and investment scams aimed at the elderly all work by dragging out the process to build trust and then harvesting everything at once. Agreeing in advance that "any spending above a certain amount must first be discussed with a named person" pushes the decision past the impulse window.
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 作者经验，无直接文献；防骗通则见第 8 节和第 13 节
- Notes: Do not present this as "we're afraid you'll be scammed" — framing it as "unified bookkeeping for household money" is easier to accept. Do not put all of the elderly person's money into the children's hands either; that invites a different kind of dispute.

### 4. Give the elderly person a ready-made excuse they can say out loud at any time
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: sales pitches and scams feed on in-the-moment pressure. Agreeing in advance on one phrase — "I need to go back and discuss this with my family" — gives the elderly person a graceful way out on the spot, which works better than reasoning with them after the fact.
- Benefit: Both sales pressure and scams rely on pressure applied in the moment. Agreeing in advance on one phrase — "I need to go back and discuss this with my family" — gives the elderly person a face-saving exit, which is more effective than reasoning after the fact.
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 作者经验，无直接文献
- Notes: Use this together with the previous item. At the same time, do the fall-prevention modifications at home — see Section 1.

### 5. Avoid every "invest in retirement" scheme that asks the elderly person to pay first: membership cards, bed purchases, senior apartments, sojourn retirement, senior products — all the same illegal fundraising
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; read the list of schemes below to the elderly person once, refuse outright when they come up, and do not attend lectures, do not go on site visits, do not leave a phone number
- In plain terms: every retirement scheme that asks the elderly person to pay money first and promises returns in exchange — VIP and membership cards, prepaid care-service fees, buying beds, buying shares in wellness and retirement bases, buying senior apartments, sojourn retirement, senior products sold through sales meetings — is the same playbook. Under the rules, the money cannot be recovered: losses from illegal fundraising are borne by the participants themselves. Look at the scale in official notices: one operator collected 136 million yuan under the pretext of pre-booking senior apartment beds, with more than 2,100 victims; another, flying the banner of investing in the retirement industry, took in 13.207 billion yuan involving more than 110,000 people. The only step that can be blocked is the moment of paying.
- Benefit: The five patterns listed by the Ministry of Civil Affairs — recognizing the scheme's name is more reliable than judging the person: in the name of elderly-care service institutions (no real facility, temporarily renting premises, or falsely claiming a cooperation agreement with a care institution to piggyback on its credibility); in the name of providing "care services" ("VIP cards," "membership cards," "prepaid cards," prepaying "care service fees," promising services obviously exceeding bed capacity); in the name of investing in "retirement projects" (buying shares in wellness and retirement bases, selling fictitious senior apartments, long-term leasing of care beds, "time bank" mutual-aid retirement, using sale-with-return-of-principal, sale-and-leaseback, and agreed buybacks as cover); in the name of selling "senior products" (product buybacks, consignment sales, consumption rebates, free medical checkups, free gifts, sales meetings, wellness lectures, free expert consultations); in the name of enjoying "sojourn retirement" (cheap or free travel, stored-value rebates, points-based retirement). That the money cannot be recovered is written into the rules: Article 25 of the Regulation on the Prevention and Disposal of Illegal Fundraising states that "losses suffered from participating in illegal fundraising shall be borne by the fundraising participants themselves." For scale, see officially announced cases: Dai Mouping and others illegally raised 136 million yuan under the pretext of pre-booking senior apartment beds, with more than 2,100 victims; Dai Mouping was sentenced to 15 years for fundraising fraud and fined 400,000 yuan; Cao Mouming committed fundraising fraud of 13.207 billion yuan under the name of investing in the retirement industry, involving more than 110,000 people, and was sentenced to life imprisonment, deprivation of political rights for life, and confiscation of all personal property; the health-product sales-meeting route is prosecuted as fraud — Ni Mourong used free banquets, gifts, and chauffeured transport to bring elderly people to lectures, where instructors fabricated anti-cancer and cancer-fighting effects through "fake personas, fake cases, inflated bargaining," inducing them to buy "dihydroquercetin" at dozens of times the cost price, with sales of more than 620,000 yuan; he was sentenced to 10 years and 3 months and fined 120,000 yuan (nationwide; the Regulation took effect May 1, 2021).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 民政部 (2023). 关于养老服务领域非法集资的风险提示. <https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202306/content_6886556.htm>（中国政府网发布）；民政部、中国银保监会 (2021). 关于养老领域非法集资的风险提示. <https://www.mca.gov.cn/n152/n164/c36171/content.html>；国务院 (2021). 防范和处置非法集资条例（国务院令第 737 号，第二、二十五、三十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-02/10/content_5586632.htm>；最高人民检察院 (2022). 检察机关打击整治养老诈骗犯罪典型案例. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202206/t20220617_560010.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2025). 依法惩治涉民生领域诈骗犯罪典型案例（案例五 倪某荣诈骗案）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/482861.html>
- Notes: The test is not "does this outfit look legitimate" but "does it ask you to pay first, does it promise returns" — the essence of a care institution is providing services, and any promise carrying investment or rebate properties disqualifies it. Being taken to lectures, trips, or free checkups is not itself illegal and is hard to block; what can be blocked is the step of paying out. Use this together with the two-person confirmation rule in Item 3. If money has already been paid, immediately call 110 or 96110 to request a payment freeze per Section 8, Item 2, and report to the local lead department for handling illegal fundraising. Reverse-mortgage retirement is a separate track — see the next item.

### 6. Apart from the insurer's reverse-mortgage endowment insurance for housing, touch no other "house-for-pension" scheme, and never mortgage the house to buy wealth-management products
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; never hand the property ownership certificate to anyone who comes to the door in the name of "house-for-pension," and before signing any mortgage, guarantee, or loan agreement, show it to the children first
- In plain terms: the only legitimate form of house-for-pension is the insurer's reverse-mortgage endowment insurance for housing, which is still in a pilot phase and fairly niche. The "house-for-pension" sold door to door has nothing at all to do with the national pilot: the elderly person is tricked into mortgaging the house, and the borrowed money is used to buy the recommended wealth-management product — in essence, new money paying off old money. The regulator's own words: some people did not even know their property had been mortgaged, and in the end lost the house while still carrying the loan. Be suspicious of any wealth-management product promising returns above 6%, anything above 8% is dangerous, and above 10% be prepared to lose the entire principal.
- Benefit: The Consumer Protection Bureau of the China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission spelled out the distinction between the genuine and the fake: there is only one legitimate form of house-for-pension, namely "reverse-mortgage endowment insurance for elderly people's housing," in which an elderly person with full legal ownership of the home mortgages the property to an insurance company while retaining possession, use, and income from it plus the right of disposal with the mortgagee's consent, and draws a pension under the agreed terms until death; it remains in a pilot stage and fairly niche. The "house-for-pension" promoted by criminals is "completely unrelated" to the nationally piloted reverse-mortgage endowment insurance; it is "a means of exploiting national policy to build momentum for illegal fundraising activities." The mechanics are to trick elderly people into mortgaging their property and then use the borrowed money to buy the recommended wealth-management products — "often a 'new money for old money' Ponzi scheme." On the consequences, the original text states: "some participants did not even know their property had been mortgaged, and in the end lost their homes while still carrying the loans." The return-rate thresholds it gives: "if a wealth-management product promises returns above 6%, be suspicious; above 8% it is very dangerous; above 10% be prepared to lose the entire principal — 'guaranteed principal with high returns' is financial fraud." The same advisory also urges "caution at the contract-signing stage: never sign a blank contract" (nationwide, June 2021).
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 中国银保监会消费者权益保护局 (2021). 关于警惕「投资养老」「以房养老」金融诈骗的风险提示（2021 年第三期）. <https://dfjrjgj.hunan.gov.cn/dfjrjgj/yhlj/202106/t20210608_19450856.html>（湖南省地方金融监督管理局转载）；民政部、中国银保监会 (2021). 关于养老领域非法集资的风险提示（第四种表现形式：以宣称「以房养老」为名非法集资）. <https://www.mca.gov.cn/n152/n164/c36171/content.html>
- Notes: The reason for grade B is that this is a regulator's risk-advisory framing and rules of thumb, not research data; the 6%, 8%, and 10% thresholds are the regulator's consumer guidance, not a statutory standard. The Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission has since been merged into the National Financial Regulatory Administration, and the original publication page is unreachable; the citation is a repost by a provincial financial regulatory bureau. The crux of this item is not "the wealth-management product lost money" — it is that once the house is mortgaged, the elderly person still owes the loan, a loss far greater than the principal. For the general rules on signing and blank contracts, see Section 8, Item 15; for payment freezes after being scammed, see Section 8, Item 2.

### 7. If an elderly person at home is bedridden long-term or severely disabled, apply for long-term care insurance at the medical-insurance department of the insured place
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: one application plus one disability-level assessment; if the first assessment is passed, the assessment fee is paid by the fund
- In plain terms: if the elderly person is severely disabled and this state has lasted more than half a year, go to the medical-insurance department of the insured place and apply for long-term care insurance: qualifying care costs have no deductible — those insured under the employee policy are reimbursed about 70%, those insured under the urban and rural resident policy about 50%, and the annual cap is at most half of the local residents' per-capita disposable income of the previous year. If the first assessment is passed, the assessment fee is covered by the fund; bed fees, meal fees, and the like are not in the reimbursement scope.
- Benefit: Qualifying long-term care service costs have no deductible; for those insured under the employee policy the fund pays about 70%, and for those insured under the non-employed urban and rural resident policy about 50%; the annual payment cap does not exceed 50% of the pooled region's per-capita disposable income of urban and rural residents for the previous year.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家医保局等八部门 (2026). 关于印发《加快建立长期护理保险制度实施方案》的通知. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202603/content_7063915.htm>：保障对象为「按规定参保缴费且失能状态长期持续（一般为6个月以上），经申请通过评估认定的失能人员」，「制度起步阶段保障重度失能人员」；「待遇享受不设起付标准。符合规定的长期护理服务费用，按未就业城乡居民参保政策参保的，基金支付比例为50%左右；按单位职工参保政策参保的，基金支付比例为70%左右，退休人员享受单位职工参保待遇」；「参保人员基金年度最高支付限额不超过统筹地区上年度城乡居民人均可支配收入的50%」；「待遇享受固定等待期原则上按照6个月设置」；另见中共中央办公厅、国务院办公厅《关于加快建立长期护理保险制度的意见》. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12666/202604/content_7065119.html>
- Notes: The disability must be "long-lasting (generally more than 6 months)" — a few weeks in hospital does not qualify. Non-care costs such as bed fees and meal fees, and medical expenses that fall to medical insurance, are not paid by long-term care insurance; those already receiving work-injury living-care benefits do not receive both. Home-based and community care get preferential payment rates, so entering an institution is not necessarily required. The program has rolled out in stages across localities — first call the medical-insurance handling agency of the insured place to ask whether it has started locally and how to book an assessment.
