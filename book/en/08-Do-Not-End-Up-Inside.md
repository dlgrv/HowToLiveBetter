# 8. Don't Get Yourself Locked Up: Law and Property Safety

The scope of this chapter is money and personal freedom: what liability the law actually imposes, how much loss can be avoided, and what procedural rights you have — it is not converted into life expectancy. Article numbers follow verified official source texts. Items are ordered by "largest loss in a single incident, easiest to act on", from high to low; items that require advance arrangement sit in the middle, and experience-based items go last.
### 1. Stop, rescue, and call the police after a traffic accident — never flee
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Stopping, turning on the hazard lights, rescuing people, and calling the police costs at most an hour or two of waiting for traffic police. For a minor accident where no one is hurt and both sides agree on the facts, photograph the scene first. Then write down the other party's name, phone number, license plate number, driver's license number, insurance certificate number, and the damaged parts. After both parties sign, you may leave the scene first and settle it between yourselves.
- In plain terms: Flee, and the accident is presumed to be entirely your fault by default. Your license is revoked, and you can never take the test again. There is a more practical layer too: property damage insurance and third-party liability insurance pay nothing for hit-and-run, so hundreds of thousands in compensation come entirely out of your own pocket. Staying at the scene to rescue people and call the police costs at most an hour or two.
- Benefit: The party that flees bears full responsibility for the accident. Only if you can prove the other party was also at fault can your share be reduced somewhat. Fleeing also revokes your driver's license, and you can never take the test again. If the accident constitutes the crime of causing a traffic accident, fleeing means 3 to 7 years in prison; if fleeing leads to someone's death, more than 7 years. Insurance will not cover it either: in commercial auto insurance, both property damage insurance and third-party liability insurance pay nothing for "hit-and-run after a traffic accident" or "deliberately destroying or faking the scene, destroying evidence," so hundreds of thousands in compensation come entirely out of your own pocket (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 道路交通安全法（2021 年修订，第七十、一百零一条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/205308/index.html>（北京市公安交通管理局转载）；国务院 (2017). 道路交通安全法实施条例（国务院令第 405 号，2017 年修订，第八十六、九十二条）. <http://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=75>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第一百三十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；中国保险行业协会 (2020). 机动车商业保险示范条款（2020 版）（第九、二十二条）. <https://www.iachina.cn/art/2020/9/4/art_24_104621.html>
- Notes: If someone is injured, rescue first, then call the police. If you must move the scene to rescue people, first mark the original positions of the vehicles and the people. "It bumped me and I drove off without noticing" can also be counted as fleeing afterward; if you are not sure, call the police. The insurance clause cited above comes from the model clauses, which are the insurance industry's unified template; what you actually get paid is governed by the policy in your own hands.

### 2. The moment you realize you have been scammed, call 110 or 96110 right away to demand a payment stop — do not investigate on your own first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. One phone call: state clearly the time and amount of the transfer and the other party's account number. Keep the transfer screenshots and chat records.
- In plain terms: The short stretch right after the money leaves your account is the only window. In that window the police can urgently stop the payment and freeze it fast, and the bank must cooperate. Once the money has been passed along and scattered, it is basically unrecoverable. So call 110 or 96110 first; do not go checking or asking around on your own first. When 96110 calls you, it is the police trying to talk you out of a scam — pick up.
- Benefit: Public security organs have the authority to immediately query, urgently stop payments on, and rapidly freeze case-involved funds. Banks and payment institutions must cooperate. Frozen funds are returned to the victim through the proper procedure. Once the money has been transferred away and scattered, the odds of getting it back drop sharply, so the earlier the payment stop, the more effective it is. 96110 is the nationwide unified anti-fraud warning hotline; when it calls, it is the police intervening to stop you, so always answer (nationwide, from December 2022).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2022). 反电信网络诈骗法（第二十、三十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>；福建省公安厅 (2023). 96110 来电，请务必接听. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm>（省级公安机关页面）
- Notes: Anyone claiming to be the police, prosecutors, or a court and telling you to move your money to a "safe account" is running a scam. The law only says "establish a payment-stop and freeze system," meaning banks are to hold the money. How much can be recovered depends on whether the money is still in the account when you report. The description of the "National Anti-Fraud Center" app and 96110 is taken from a Fujian Provincial Public Security Department page; the original page on the Ministry of Public Security website would not open.

### 3. Memorize the hard anti-fraud rules: do not trust unexpected callers, do not disclose information, do not click links, verify extra before any transfer — the seven most common scams all have this shape
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Memorize "three don'ts and one more," read through the seven categories below, install the "National Anti-Fraud Center" app, and always answer when 96110 calls. Anyone asking you to transfer money, share your screen, download an unfamiliar app, click a link, or read out a verification code — hang up first, then call the official number to verify. The hard part is that the caller often pushes urgently; you have to withstand that push and hang up first.
- In plain terms: Whenever someone asks you to transfer money, share your screen, download an unfamiliar app, click a link, or read out a verification code, hang up first and call back on the official number to verify. The seven high-frequency types are order-brushing rebates, fake investment schemes, online loans, fake customer service, impersonating the police or courts, pig-butchering romance scams, and sex-lure order-brushing. They share exactly one common feature: they get you to transfer money first, pay fees first, or share your screen first. Recognizing it beforehand is far cheaper than chasing the money afterward.
- Benefit: The law requires governments and relevant departments to explain the methods of all kinds of telecom and online fraud to the public. Public security organs, together with the finance, telecom, and cyberspace authorities, build a warning-and-intervention system and dissuade in time anyone flagged as a possible fraud victim. 96110 is exactly this nationwide unified warning-and-intervention hotline; answer when it calls. The Fujian Provincial Public Security Department breaks down the tell-tale signs type by type. Order-brushing rebates: "the person inviting you to make money together most likely wants to make money off you," and if you cannot finish the task list, your principal is not returned. Fake investment schemes: paid shills in the group post their earnings, small rebates come first to draw you into investing more, and withdrawals demand "tax payments." Online loans: "a real loan does not require a security deposit or a fabricated bank statement; once you run into that, it is certainly fake." Fake customer service: they say your membership will auto-charge and hurt your credit record and push you to transfer money to cancel. Impersonating the police or courts: "police, prosecutors, and courts do not have so-called safe accounts; any police-prosecutor-court agency that handles cases or takes statements by phone, QQ, WeChat, or the internet is fraud." Pig-butchering: "do not lightly trust a lover you have never met and have only seen in photos or videos" — they never agree to a video call, never meet in person, yet always have urgent need of money. Sex-lure order-brushing: before meeting up you must first "do tasks," and the money you hand over keeps growing. Recognizing it beforehand is far cheaper than stopping payments afterward. How much a payment stop can recover depends on whether the money is still in the account when you report; how to stop a payment, see item 2 (call 110 or 96110 to demand a payment stop) (nationwide, from December 2022).
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2022). 反电信网络诈骗法（第八、二十、三十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>；福建省公安厅 (2023). 96110 来电，请务必接听. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm>（省级公安机关页面，含「来电不轻信，信息不透露，链接不点击，转账多核实」口诀及「警惕 192 开头的诈骗电话」提示）；福建省公安厅 (2023 到 2024). 识骗技巧专栏：刷单 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202307/t20230703_6196881.htm>；投资理财 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202312/t20231220_6362145.htm>；贷款 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202312/t20231207_6326741.htm>；冒充客服 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202310/t20231020_6279505.htm>；冒充公检法 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202309/t20230913_6255863.htm>；杀猪盘 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202308/t20230818_6232030.htm>；色诱刷单 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202403/t20240307_6410612.htm>
- Notes: Anyone calling themselves the police, prosecutors, or courts, customer service, your boss, or platform risk control, and asking you to "cooperate with an investigation," "cancel a membership," "brush orders for rebates," or use "insider investment tips," is running the same playbook with a new skin. A real loan needs no security deposit, and the police, prosecutors, and courts have no "safe account" — remembering these two sentences alone blocks more than half of it. Graded B because the maxim is only official publicity language, with no figure for how much less you get scammed after memorizing it. ### 4. Seeing a face on video or hearing a voice on the phone is not verification — when money is involved, hang up first and call back the old number stored in your own contacts
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. One extra phone call, a few extra minutes of waiting. In everyday life, post fewer front-facing videos and long voice clips on public accounts. The hard part is that the caller often pushes urgently; hold back and do not transfer on the spot.
- In plain terms: Seeing a face or hearing a voice does not count as verification. The moment money is involved, hang up first, then call back the old number stored in your own contacts, or ask someone you both know. If the other side says "bad signal" or "in a meeting" and refuses a live video call, treat it as a scam outright.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Public Security's cybersecurity bureau gives three methods. The first is motion verification: ask the other person to make one continuous head-turn, or cover their face completely with a palm and then move it away quickly. The original text says that "with current AI technology, simulating these complex facial occlusions and lighting changes tends to produce stuttering, blurring, or distortion." The second is listening for voice details: "AI-generated speech sometimes lacks natural breathing pauses and emotional rises and falls, or carries a slight mechanical quality or abnormal background noise." The third is multi-channel verification, which the original text calls "the most core move": "after hanging up, directly dial the other party's regular number that you know well (do not call back the suspicious incoming number), or verify indirectly through a third person you both know." The maxim is "do not lightly trust video or voice; verify more with motion checks." On the legal side: face-swapping and voice-cloning have long been banned for fraud. Platforms offering face generation, face replacement, synthetic voices, and voice imitation must apply a conspicuous label, and no organization or individual may maliciously delete, alter, forge, or conceal that label. But it is the compliant platforms that follow the rules; scammers will not add the label, so "no AI label seen" cannot be turned around into evidence that the other side is a real person. The Ministry of Public Security's criminal investigation bureau published one case: a 14-person ring was caught with more than 50,000 synthesized dynamic face videos and more than 500,000 items of citizens' personal information, and the police also seized 60 cloud servers (nationwide; the labeling obligation applies from September 1, 2025).
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 公安部网安局 (2025). 眼见≠为实，警惕 AI 新型诈骗！<http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202509/t20250922_7013008.htm>（福建省公安厅转载）；国家互联网信息办公室、工业和信息化部、公安部 (2022). 互联网信息服务深度合成管理规定（第六、十六、十七条，2023 年 1 月 10 日施行）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-12/12/content_5731431.htm>；国家互联网信息办公室、工业和信息化部、公安部、国家广播电视总局 (2025). 人工智能生成合成内容标识办法（第十条，2025 年 9 月 1 日施行）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654684782215.htm>；公安部刑侦局、央视新闻 (2026). 警方查获 5 万多条合成动态人脸视频 谁在「复制」你的脸？<http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202603/t20260330_7117146.htm>（福建省公安厅转载）
- Notes: Call back on the old number stored in your own contacts. Do not call back the number shown on the incoming call, and do not use a new number the other side sent in chat. If they refuse a live video call or the requested action citing "bad signal" or "in a meeting," treat it as a scam outright. Motion verification is only an aid; AI keeps getting better, and the only stable method is the third one — verification. If money has already been transferred, see item 2 of this chapter (call 110 at once to demand a payment stop). To keep your own face and voice from being harvested, see section 14. Graded B because the official material gives identification methods and cases, with no figure for how much the scam rate drops after verifying this way. ### 5. If you are accused or summoned, get a lawyer first — no private settlements, no deleting records
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Legal fees start from several thousand yuan. If you are in financial hardship, you can apply to a legal aid institution for a free lawyer. While being questioned, answer only questions related to the case and leave the rest to the lawyer. The hard part is holding steady on the spot and not rushing to explain yourself.
- In plain terms: From the very first time you are called in for questioning, you can already have a lawyer. When a lawyer requests to see you, the detention center arranges it within 48 hours at the latest, and the conversation is not listened in on. You do not have to answer questions unrelated to this case.
- Benefit: From the first interrogation onward, or from the moment coercive measures are taken against you (your personal freedom restricted), you have the right to retain a lawyer. While the case is still in the investigation stage with the public security organs, only a lawyer can be retained; no one other than a lawyer may act as your defender. When a lawyer requests a meeting, the detention center arranges it within 48 hours at the latest, and the meeting is not monitored. No one may be forced to prove their own guilt. You have the right to refuse to answer questions unrelated to this case. One summons or compulsory summons lasts at most 12 hours, at most 24 hours for especially major and complex cases. A summons is a notice to come in for questioning; a compulsory summons is being forcibly brought in for questioning. Successive summonses may not be used to hold a person in disguised detention. Fabricating facts to falsely accuse someone and have them criminally pursued carries up to 3 years; with serious consequences, 3 to 10 years. If after detention or arrest the case is withdrawn, not prosecuted, or ends in acquittal, you can apply for state compensation, calculated by the day at the previous year's national average daily wage of employees (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 刑事诉讼法（2018 年修正，第三十四、三十五、三十九、五十二、一百一十九、一百二十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百四十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2012). 国家赔偿法（2012 年修正，第十七、三十三条）. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/gk/tjfg/xgfxfg/202503/t20250306_1958899.html>（国家统计局转载）
- Notes: Do not go privately to the other party, and do not go to their family either. Any "private settlement" can be read as you admitting guilt. Chat records, call logs, and travel records are the material you use to prove your innocence; deleting them is deleting your own evidence too. An honest mistake, or a report that later turns out to be unfounded, is not the crime of false accusation. This item applies to accused persons regardless of sex. For three expanded items see item 33 of this chapter (pursuing the party that fabricated facts), item 34 (insufficient evidence must lead to acquittal, plus appeals and retrials), and item 35 (state compensation after custody is counted by the day).

### 6. When something happens, come forward and tell it straight: turning yourself in cuts your sentence by up to 40 percent, and "outlasting the limitation period" is a dead end
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Go to a public security organ and surrender yourself, or stay where you are, call the police, and then tell the whole story as it happened. The hard part is withstanding the thought "let's wait and see — maybe nobody will look into it."
- In plain terms: Turning yourself in can cut your sentence by up to 40 percent, and for lighter offenses the punishment can be waived altogether. If you tell the truth only after the authorities come to you, the reduction is still up to 20 percent. Running away and refusing to admit anything buys you none of this. Counting on hiding for a few years and letting the case expire does not work either: once a case has been filed, the time you spend hiding does not count toward the limitation period.
- Benefit: Article 67 of the Criminal Law: voluntarily surrendering after committing a crime and truthfully confessing your own crime constitutes voluntary surrender. For those who surrender, punishment may be lighter or mitigated, and for lighter crimes punishment may be waived. Those who do not surrender but truthfully confess their own crime (a frank confession) may receive lighter punishment; those whose truthful confession prevents especially serious consequences may receive mitigated punishment. The sentencing guidance issued by the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate gives explicit ranges. The baseline sentence is the sentence first calculated from the facts of the case, and every percentage below subtracts from it. Voluntary surrender reduces the baseline sentence by up to 40%; for lighter crimes, by more than 40%, or punishment may be waived according to law. For frank confessions: truthfully confessing your own crime reduces the baseline by up to 20%; truthfully confessing a same-type, more serious crime the judicial organs do not yet know about reduces it by 10% to 30%; a truthful confession that prevents especially serious consequences reduces it by 30% to 50%. Pleading guilty and accepting punishment reduces the baseline by up to 30%; where surrender, major frank confession, returning stolen goods with restitution, compensation and forgiveness all appear together, by up to 60%; for lighter crimes, by more than 60% or punishment waived according to law. On the other side, the limitation period has four tiers by the statutory maximum penalty: 5, 10, 15, and 20 years (Article 87 of the Criminal Law). But Article 88 states plainly: once the procuratorate, the public security organs, or the state security organs have filed a case for investigation, or the court has accepted the case, anyone who evades investigation or trial is not shielded by the limitation period (nationwide; the sentencing ranges apply from July 1, 2021).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大. 中华人民共和国刑法（经刑法修正案十一修正的整合文本）第六十七、八十七、八十八条. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808181796a636a0179822a19640c92>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2021). 关于常见犯罪的量刑指导意见（试行）（法发〔2021〕21 号，2021 年 7 月 1 日施行）三（六）（七）（十四）. 甘肃省张掖市人民检察院转载全文. <http://www.zhangye.jcy.gov.cn/info/1039/2086.htm>；最高人民法院 (2010). 关于处理自首和立功若干具体问题的意见（法发〔2010〕60 号）一. 最高人民法院公报. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/f3089058d37e11cd16d801530934ae.html>
- Notes: The beneficiary of this item is you yourself. Voluntary surrender is a factor for leniency, not a get-out-of-jail card; how much is reduced is decided by the court, and the sentencing guidance requires the leniency ranges to be applied strictly for serious violent crimes and drug crimes. Traffic accidents follow a separate rule: staying at the scene, rescuing people, and reporting to the public security organs counts as voluntary surrender, but because these are already legal duties the leniency is applied strictly; surrendering after fleeing still counts as voluntary surrender, only the baseline is set by the heavier statutory tier, and then the court decides whether and how much to reduce — how to do it, see item 1 of this chapter (stop, rescue, and call the police after a traffic accident). Getting a lawyer first does not conflict with confessing truthfully — see item 5 of this chapter (get a lawyer first when accused): what the lawyer checks for you is whether the written record is accurate, not a story invented on your behalf.

### 7. Never touch the steering wheel after drinking — including e-bikes and "just moving the car a bit"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: A designated driver or a taxi costs tens of yuan. Agree before the gathering on who is not drinking today. The hard part is pushing back when people urge you to drink.
- In plain terms: Driving after drinking means a 6-month license suspension and a fine of one to two thousand yuan. A second offense means detention plus revocation. Drunk driving means immediate revocation, no retest for 5 years, and criminal liability for the crime of dangerous driving, which leaves a criminal record that affects political vetting and some professions. Insurance will not cover it either: driving after drinking, and auto insurance pays nothing, period. "Just moving the car a bit" still counts as driving.
- Benefit: Driving after drinking means the driver's license is suspended for 6 months and the fine is 1,000 to 2,000 yuan. A repeat offense means detention of up to 10 days plus license revocation. Drunk driving means the license is revoked outright, no retest for 5 years, and criminal liability for the crime of dangerous driving, with short-term detention plus a fine — detention means being locked up for a short term. It is heavier for commercial drivers: driving after drinking means 15 days of detention, a 5,000-yuan fine, and a 5-year driving ban; drunk driving means a 10-year ban and never being allowed to drive commercially again in this lifetime. Causing a major accident while impaired or drunk, if it constitutes a crime, means a lifetime driving ban. Commercial auto insurance pays nothing for driving after drinking or taking drugs (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 道路交通安全法（2021 年修订，第九十一条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/205308/index.html>（北京市公安交通管理局转载）；全国人大常委会 (2015). 刑法修正案（九）（第八条，即刑法第一百三十三条之一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180205_364562.shtml>；中国保险行业协会 (2020). 机动车商业保险示范条款（2020 版）（第九、二十二条）. <https://www.iachina.cn/art/2020/9/4/art_24_104621.html>
- Notes: Drunk driving is a criminal offense and leaves a criminal record, which affects the person's political vetting and some professional qualifications. "Drinking" and "drunkenness" are separated by the alcohol content in the blood; the specific threshold is set by national standards. This section has not verified that value, so do not estimate by feel which tier you are in.

### 8. Never lend your bank card, SIM card, or payment accounts to anyone — "paofen" is not a side job
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. When someone says "just let your card run a payment through, I'll give you a few hundred yuan," refuse.
- In plain terms: Renting out, lending, or selling your bank card, SIM card, or payment accounts to others is illegal in itself. The penalty is a fine of 1 to 10 times the illegal gains, up to 200,000 yuan if there are no gains, detention of 15 days in serious cases, plus entries in your credit record and restrictions on account functions. If you knowingly help collect and move money for someone else's crime, that is the crime of assisting information network criminal activities, punishable by up to 3 years.
- Benefit: Renting out, lending, or buying and selling phone cards, bank accounts, payment accounts, or internet accounts is illegal in itself. Illegal gains are confiscated, plus a fine of 1 to 10 times those gains. Where there are no illegal gains, the fine is up to 200,000 yuan. In serious cases, detention of up to 15 days. You can also be entered into the credit record, have account functions restricted, and have non-counter services suspended — meaning that apart from walking up to the counter, no other channel works for you. Knowingly providing payment settlement and similar help to someone using the internet to commit a crime, if serious, constitutes the crime of assisting information network criminal activities (paofen's legal cousin, "bangxin zui" for short), punishable by up to 3 years plus a fine (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2022). 反电信网络诈骗法（第三十一、四十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2015). 刑法修正案（九）（第二十九条，即刑法第二百八十七条之二）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180205_364562.shtml>
- Notes: Saying "I didn't know what he wanted the card for" does not shield you. Investigators will infer whether you knew from outward circumstances: having taken a cut of the proceeds, or transaction volumes clearly out of the ordinary, can both count as you knowing. If your card was used to receive stolen money, even if no crime is ultimately established, the account will be frozen and you personally will be disciplined.

### 11. Against an attack you cannot escape you may fight back, but only at the person actually attacking, and stop when they stop

The other: for online defamation where you genuinely cannot produce evidence yourself, the court may require the public security organ to help investigate (same article, paragraph 3, added by Criminal Law Amendment IX). Reposting carries the same liability; spreading what you know is false also counts as "fabricating facts." For what to do after being cyberbullied, see item 37 of this chapter (preserve evidence first, then choose your route). The article numbers for insult and defamation moved from Article 42 of the old law to Article 50 of the new one.

### 17. Read the paper through before signing; never sign for someone else; never sign a blank sheet
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Spend a few extra minutes reading contracts, IOUs, guarantee letters, and onboarding documents from start to finish. Ask on the spot about anything you do not understand, and photograph it for your records. The hard part is reading it slowly to the end with the other party watching you.
- In plain terms: Once signed, it counts. "I didn't read it carefully" or "I thought it said something else" basically will not stand up in court. So spend a few extra minutes reading it from start to finish, ask on the spot about anything unclear, and photograph it for your records. Do not sign for other people, do not sign a blank sheet, and write the stated purpose on copies of your ID card. An electronic signature and a facial-recognition confirmation count as signing all the same.
- Benefit: A lawfully formed contract is protected by law and binding on both parties who signed it. Once signed, it must be performed in full as agreed. "I didn't read it carefully" or "I thought it said something else" is very hard to establish in court. Not signing means you will not carry debts and responsibilities that are not yours (nationwide).

### 18. Write a full IOU when lending money; before guaranteeing for someone, decide whether you are willing to repay in their place
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. One sheet of paper stating the lender, the borrower, the amount, the interest rate, the term, and the repayment method, signed by both parties. Let the money move by transfer, leaving a trail; never hand over cash.
- In plain terms: The IOU must state the lender, the borrower, the amount, the interest rate, the term, and the repayment method, with both signatures. Let the money move by transfer, never cash; a complete document is what makes a lawsuit winnable. When guaranteeing for someone, look hard at whether the two characters meaning "joint and several" are there. A general guarantee means the creditor must first sue the borrower and fail to recover through enforcement before it is your turn. Sign a joint and several liability guarantee, and the creditor can come straight to you.
- Benefit: A loan contract should be in written form, and its content generally includes the type of loan, currency, purpose, amount, interest rate, term, and repayment method. A complete document is what makes a lawsuit winnable. Where a guarantee contract does not stipulate the type of guarantee, or stipulates it unclearly, liability is borne as a general guarantee. A general guarantor can first wait: the creditor must first sue or arbitrate, then apply for enforcement against the borrower's property, and only if enforcement still comes up short is it your turn to repay in their place. Conversely, if you signed a "joint and several liability guarantee," that layer of protection is gone, and the creditor can come straight to you (nationwide).

Whether the two characters meaning "joint and several" appear on the guarantee letter decides whether you stand behind the borrower or beside him when the debt is chased; read it carefully before signing.

### 19. Rights claims have deadlines: 3 years for civil litigation, 1 year for labor arbitration — once past, the other side saying "the statute of limitations has run" is enough
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Do not chase payment only in your head; leave a trail with WeChat, SMS, email, or a registered letter.
- In plain terms: The civil litigation limitation period is 3 years, counted from the day you knew your rights were harmed. Past the deadline the court will not step in on its own, but the other side saying "the limitation period has run" in court is enough — the money still exists, you just cannot get it back. And one payment-demand message that leaves a trace restarts the 3 years from the beginning. Labor disputes run on a separate track: the arbitration limitation is 1 year.
- Benefit: The Civil Code provides that "the limitation period for requesting the people's court to protect civil rights is three years." The starting point is "counted from the date the rights holder knew or should have known that their rights were harmed and who the obligor is" — that is, from the day you knew you had taken a loss and also knew whom to turn to. It does not exceed 20 years from the date the rights were harmed. Once the period expires, "the obligor may raise the defense of non-performance." The court will not dig into the limitation on its own, but the moment the other side raises it in court it becomes effective; the money still exists, it just cannot be recovered anymore. Four kinds of things interrupt the limitation, and once interrupted the 3 years are recalculated from the start. First, you demand performance from the person who owes you. Second, the other party agrees to perform. Third, you file suit or apply for arbitration. Fourth, other circumstances of equivalent effect. Where repayment is agreed in installments, it is counted from the day the final installment falls due. Four categories of claims are not subject to the limitation: demands to stop infringement, remove obstruction, or eliminate danger; recovery of your own real property and of registered movable property; child support, support for parents, and support between family members; and other circumstances provided by law. Labor disputes run on a separate track: the arbitration limitation is 1 year, counted from the day you knew or should have known of the infringement of your rights. But "for disputes over wage arrears during the existence of the labor relationship, a worker's application for arbitration is not subject to the arbitration limitation period in the first paragraph of this article; however, where the labor relationship has terminated, the application must be filed within one year from the date of termination" (nationwide).

- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一百八十八、一百八十九、一百九十二、一百九十三、一百九十五、一百九十六、一百九十七条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2007). 劳动争议调解仲裁法（第二十七条）. <https://rsj.ankang.gov.cn/Content-2150407.html>（安康市人力资源和社会保障局转载）

### 20. If sued and subject to enforcement, report your assets truthfully and repay what you can — do not move your house or money to relatives, friends, or a company
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Report your assets as the court requires: what you have now, and what you had in the year before the day you received the enforcement notice. Repay what you can first. If you cannot repay, negotiate a settlement or installments with the applicant for enforcement — the applicant for enforcement is the party chasing the debt.
- In plain terms: Putting the house under a company's name or transferring money to relatives and friends is not some clever "move" — it falls exactly within the enumerated circumstances of the crime of refusing to comply with judgments. That crime carries up to 3 years, and 3 to 7 years for especially serious circumstances. The relatives, friends, and companies who help hide money or transfer titles are treated as accomplices, and the assets are recovered all the same. Refusing to report assets or falsely reporting them can mean a fine of up to 100,000 yuan for an individual plus 15 days of detention. Genuinely having no money is not this crime.
- Benefit: Transferring assets is not a "move" — it is itself a circumstance for criminal liability. The NPC Standing Committee issued an interpretation of Article 313 of the Criminal Law. The interpretation directly lists "the person subject to enforcement hiding, transferring, or deliberately destroying assets, or transferring assets gratuitously or at a plainly unreasonable low price, making the judgment or ruling impossible to enforce" as "having capacity to perform but refusing, with serious circumstances." Plainly put: hiding money, moving money, deliberately smashing your own things, giving them away, or selling them off cheap, turning the judgment into a blank sheet of paper. The 2024 interpretation by the two Supreme bodies (the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate) added ten more items. Among them: disposing of your own property rights through sham settlements or sham transfers. Also: buying another person's assets at a plainly unreasonable high price, and providing guarantees for another person's debts. All of these deliberately hollow out the property you could have used to repay your debts. The crime of refusing to comply carries up to 3 years. Especially serious circumstances carry 3 to 7 years plus a fine. "Especially serious" means these situations: obstructing enforcement through sham litigation, sham arbitration, or sham notarization. Mobbing the enforcement site in a crowd. Besieging, detaining, or beating enforcement personnel. Driving the applicant for enforcement to suicide or self-harm through refusal to comply. An outsider who is not a party to the case, knowing the situation, conspiring with the debtor and helping hide money or transfer titles is treated as an accomplice to the crime of refusing to comply. Property disposed of unlawfully shall be recovered according to law or returned by order. Transfers made after the defendant receives the notice of response but before the judgment takes effect count the same. On the civil side: for gratuitous transfers and transfers at a plainly unreasonable low price, the creditor can ask the court to revoke the transfer. Refusing to report assets or falsely reporting them: the court can impose a fine, up to 100,000 yuan for an individual, and detention of up to 15 days (nationwide; the interpretation by the two Supreme bodies from December 2024).

### 25. Premarital property is safe — but put house title additions and parental contributions in writing first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Writing a written property agreement yourself costs nothing; going to a notary costs extra. When parents contribute money, ask them to write one written line of explanation. The whole thing takes a few hours at once. The hard part is opening the money conversation with your family.
- In plain terms: Premarital property is yours personally and does not become community property just because the marriage lasts long. Spouses can agree in writing on how premarital and marital property is owned. One thing to watch: if you agree to add the other party's name to the house, you can still back out before the ownership registration is changed; after registration it is theirs. In divorce, the side that hides money, transfers it, squanders it, or fakes debts can receive a smaller share or none.
- Benefit: Premarital property is personal property and does not become community property with the passage of the marriage. Spouses can agree in writing: premarital property and marital acquisitions belong to each separately, jointly, or partly jointly, and this agreement binds both sides. ### 34. Insufficient evidence means acquittal by design; extracted confessions must be excluded; after conviction there are still petitions and retrials
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->

### 36. As a victim seeking compensation, go through 12315, the courts, or a lawyer — do not meet the other party alone, and never bundle "pay me" and "I won't go public" into one sentence
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Raise the claim through 12315, by suing in court, or through a lawyer. Record the entire conversation when you meet. The hard part is holding back from negotiating with the other party one-on-one.
- In plain terms: You are the victim seeking compensation from the infringing party; this is itself legally grounded and does not automatically become extortion just because "the demand is high." But the cost can be enormous: Guo Li was first sentenced to five years for extortion and only later acquitted on retrial. So use channels that leave a record, never go alone to a place the other party designates, and never say "pay me" and "then I won't go public" as one bundled condition.
- Benefit: Official case: in 2008, after the authorities announced that some batches of "Shien" brand milk powder contained melamine, the examination result for Guo Li's daughter was "several punctate strong echoes visible in the central collecting systems of both kidneys." Guo Li repeatedly sought compensation from the seller and Shien and exposed the matter to media. In June 2009 the two sides reached an agreement: Shien paid 400,000 yuan in compensation, and Guo Li issued a written statement that he would no longer pursue the matter and waived his compensation claims. The company then reported to the police, and the Chao'an County People's Court convicted Guo Li of extortion and sentenced him to five years of fixed-term imprisonment. The Guangdong Provincial People's Procuratorate issued a retrial recommendation. The recommendation held that "the existing evidence is insufficient to establish that Guo Li subjectively had the purpose of unlawful possession." As his daughter's statutory guardian, Guo Li "has the right to claim compensation from the milk powder manufacturer, and his claim is legally grounded." The recommendation also stated that "consumers have the right to expose and criticize, through mass media, acts that harm their lawful rights and interests." On April 7, 2017, the Guangdong Higher People's Court acquitted Guo Li on retrial. The reasoning: "the original trial's finding that Guo Li, with the purpose of unlawful possession, used threats and coercion to forcibly demand property is unclear in fact and insufficient in evidence, and the nature of Guo Li's conduct did not exceed the scope of a civil dispute." Guo Li was detained from July 23, 2009 to July 22, 2014 — 1,826 days (Guangdong; acquittal on retrial in 2017).
- Evidence grade: B

### 37. When you are mobbed online: turn on protections and secure evidence first, then choose among the three routes — platform, injunction, police report
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. A dozen or so minutes on the spot for protection and evidence. If it reaches applying for an injunction or a private prosecution, you have to make trips to court and possibly hire a lawyer. The hard part is securing evidence while being attacked instead of trading insults.
- In plain terms: Do three things on the spot first: turn on all of the platform's protective settings, use the platform's quick evidence-capture function to fix the content in place, then report it through the shortcut entry. Then choose among three routes. One: apply to the court for a personality-rights infringement injunction to make them stop first. Two: sue in court yourself; if gathering evidence is difficult, you can ask the public security organs to assist. Three: where the consequences are especially serious and the impact vile, it converts to public prosecution, and you do not have to carry the burden of proof yourself.
- Benefit: The SPC's Guideline Fa Fa [2023] No. 14, article 15, is about the injunction. Where a rights holder has evidence that a person is committing or is about to commit an unlawful act infringing their personality rights, and failure to stop it promptly would cause damage to their lawful rights and interests that is hard to make up, and the rights holder applies under article 997 of the Civil Code, the people's court may issue a personality-rights infringement injunction. A personality-rights infringement injunction is a court order directing the other party to stop first. Article 11 of that guideline is about evidence. In private prosecution cases, "where the victim has genuine difficulty providing evidence, they may request assistance from the public security organs." The public security organs shall promptly identify the perpetrator and collect evidence materials on the spread, diffusion, and impact. Network service providers shall provide the necessary technical support. "Where evidence has been gathered with the assistance of the public security organs and the case meets the conditions for accepting a private prosecution, the people's court shall decide to docket it." Where it cannot be collected, the public security organs shall explain in writing. A private prosecution case is one where the victim goes to court themselves. Article 12 of that guideline lists five situations to be recognized as "seriously disrupting social order" under article 246(2) of the Criminal Law. One: causing the victim or their close relatives mental breakdown, suicide, or other grave consequences, with vile social impact. Two: arbitrarily targeting ordinary members of the public, with information spreading on a large scale and drawing masses of vulgar malicious comments, and vile social impact. Three: insulting or defaming multiple people or repeatedly spreading such content, with vile social impact. Four: organizing or directing people to spread it in volume across multiple platforms, with vile social impact. Five: others. Article 4 of the guideline: organizing "human-flesh searches" (doxxing), unlawfully collecting and publishing citizens' personal information to an unspecified majority, is convicted and punished as the crime of infringing citizens' personal information where the circumstances are serious. The Provisions on the Governance of Online Violence Information, article 23, requires platforms to provide these protective options. You can block strangers or specific users. You can limit the visibility of your own posts. You can forbid reposts or comments. You can accept private messages only from friends, or refuse all private messages. Article 25 requires a quick evidence-capture function. Article 26 requires a prominent quick complaint and report entry. Article 15 requires platforms, on discovering suspected crimes, to report to the public security organs promptly.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部 (2023). 关于依法惩治网络暴力违法犯罪的指导意见（法发〔2023〕14 号，第 4、11、12、15 条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/412992.html>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第九百九十七条）. 最高人民检察院转载全文 <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；国家互联网信息办公室、公安部、文化和旅游部、国家广播电视总局 (2024). 网络暴力信息治理规定（第十五、二十三、二十五、二十六条，2024 年 8 月 1 日施行）. 国务院公报 <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11526/202408/content_6969181.html>
- Notes: The order follows "fast and cheap": the platform is fastest and cheapest — secure evidence and report first. The court is slowest and most expensive — decide last whether to go that far. The process costs of all three routes you carry yourself. The injunction requires applying to court yourself. A private prosecution means docketing at court yourself and producing the evidence yourself. To have the procuratorate take over and convert to public prosecution, you have to hit one of the five situations listed in article 12 of Guideline No. 14 of 2023; falling short, you can only sue yourself. Two things not to do. One: do not trade insults — cursing back turns you into the person punished under item 16 of this chapter (no insulting, no rumors online). Two: do not delete the content in your own account — that is evidence. When it gets unbearable, call 12356 — see section 1, item 25. Demanding a platform delete your personal information — see section 14, item 8. For the path of being filmed and spread online after helping a stranger — see [docs/Should-You-Stop-To-Help-A-Stranger.md](../../docs/en/Should-You-Stop-To-Help-A-Stranger.md).

### 38. The "buy insurance for someone first, then act" route is legally blocked at every step: not a cent of the money is collectible, and the crime is intentional homicide plus insurance fraud punished concurrently
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free.
- In plain terms: All four doors are shut. Buying insurance on a family member's life with death as the payment condition behind their back makes the contract void. Where the policyholder deliberately causes the insured person's death, the insurer pays nothing. Deliberately killing the decedent forfeits the right of inheritance. The Criminal Law also lists this kind of fraud as a separate item, punished concurrently with intentional homicide, up to the death penalty. Not a cent of the money is collectible.
- Benefit: Insurance Law article 34: a contract with death as the condition for paying insurance benefits is void without the insured person's consent and approval of the insured amount. The insured person is the one being covered. Parents insuring minor children are not subject to this restriction. Article 39: where the policyholder designates a beneficiary, it requires the insured person's consent. The policyholder is the person paying for the insurance; the beneficiary is the person who collects the money after something happens. Article 43 governs deliberate harm. Where the policyholder deliberately causes the insured person's death, disability, or illness, the insurer bears no obligation to pay insurance benefits. Where the policyholder has paid premiums for two full years or more, the cash value is returned to other rights holders. Where the beneficiary deliberately causes the insured person's death, disability, or illness, or deliberately attempts to kill the insured person, that beneficiary loses the right to benefit. Article 27, paragraph 2: where the policyholder or insured person deliberately creates the insured event, the insurer has the right to rescind the contract and bears no liability to pay compensation or benefits, and premiums are not refunded except as provided in article 43. Article 1125 of the Civil Code: one who deliberately kills the decedent forfeits the right of inheritance. Article 198, paragraph 1, item 5 of the Criminal Law lists "the policyholder or beneficiary deliberately causing the insured person's death, disability, or illness to defraud insurance money" as insurance fraud. Where the amount is relatively large, up to 5 years plus a fine of 10,000 to 100,000 yuan. Where the amount is huge, 5 to 10 years. Where especially huge, more than 10 years. Paragraph 2: where such conduct also constitutes another crime, punishment follows the rules on concurrent punishment for multiple crimes — concurrent punishment means the sentences for several crimes are counted together. Article 232: intentional homicide carries the death penalty, life imprisonment, or not less than 10 years of fixed-term imprisonment. The case is the Zhang Mousong case in Xuancheng, Anhui. In the early hours of July 18, 2016, he drove a car into a pond and his wife drowned. Less than half a month before the incident, he had taken out 4 policies on her of 1 million yuan each. On July 25 he was caught. The court sentenced him to death with reprieve plus a fine of 50,000 yuan for intentional homicide and insurance fraud punished concurrently.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2015 修正). 中华人民共和国保险法第二十七、三十四、三十九、四十三条. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf7c4060811>；全国人大 (2020). 中华人民共和国民法典第一千一百二十五条. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>；全国人大常委会. 中华人民共和国刑法（经刑法修正案十一修正的整合文本）第一百九十八条、第二百三十二条. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808181796a636a0179822a19640c92>；安徽省人民检察院转载《法制日报》(2020-01-13). 杀妻骗保牵出案中案（安徽省人民检察院民事虚假诉讼监督典型案例）. <http://www.ah.jcy.gov.cn/jczt/jjslhyqkjc/mtjj/202001/t20200113_2758841.shtml>
- Notes: The beneficiary of this item is yourself; what you avoid is the death penalty or life imprisonment. The casualties and verdict in the case are taken from the original text of the typical case published by the procuratorate; the main text does not include details beyond the investigative means. Faking accidents or exaggerating losses — insurance fraud that does not involve loss of life — is likewise a crime, counting the people who helped testify too; see section 9, item 21 (faking accidents, exaggerating losses to cheat claims). Buying insurance for family is normal in itself; this item targets the thought of "buy insurance first, then act." Impulses to harm family members should be treated as an acute condition — see items 14 and 15 of this chapter (the thought arising in yourself, harsh words from someone close to you).

### 39. Ask for the case-acceptance receipt on the spot when reporting to the police, and a written notice if no case is opened: within 7 days you may apply for reconsideration, then within 7 more for review, and the procuratorate can order the police to open a case
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Ask one extra question on the spot and get the case-acceptance receipt into your hand. Afterward, follow up on progress by the dates above. The hard part is speaking up to ask, over and over.
- In plain terms: Reporting to the police and having a case opened are two different things. When the public security organ accepts a case it should give you a case-acceptance receipt; that slip of paper is your only proof that "you reported it," so ask for it on the spot. Where the decision is not to open a case, they should give you a written notice of non-acceptance. If you disagree, within 7 days you can apply for reconsideration; if you still disagree with the reconsideration result, within another 7 days you can apply for review. You can also go to the procuratorate and ask it to supervise the opening of a case.
- Benefit: The Measures for Handling Criminal Cases by Public Security Organs, as amended by Ministry of Public Security Order No. 159, article 171, governs the case-acceptance receipt. The original text: "when accepting a case, the public security organ shall draw up a case-acceptance register and a case-acceptance receipt, and hand the receipt to the person who seized and delivered the suspect, the person reporting, the complainant, or the informant." Article 174: "cases accepted, or criminal leads discovered, shall be reviewed promptly by the public security organ." Article 178 governs whether a case is opened. After acceptance, where review finds facts of a crime requiring criminal liability that falls within its own jurisdiction, the case is opened with approval of the responsible person of the public security organ at or above the county level. Where a case is not opened, you still get a piece of paper: "for a case with a complainant where the decision is not to open a case, the public security organ shall draw up a notice of non-acceptance and deliver it to the complainant within three days." Article 179 governs what to do if you disagree. The original text: "if the complainant disagrees with the decision not to open a case, they may within seven days of receiving the notice of non-acceptance apply to the deciding public security organ for reconsideration; the public security organ shall decide within thirty days of receiving the reconsideration application." It continues: "if the complainant disagrees with the reconsideration decision, they may within seven days of receiving the reconsideration decision apply to the next higher public security organ for review; the next higher organ shall decide within thirty days of receiving the review application. Where a higher public security organ revokes a decision not to open a case, the lower organ shall implement it." For major and complex cases the reconsideration and review time limits can be extended, but "by no more than thirty days, with written notice to the applicant." Article 112 of the Criminal Procedure Law: where there are facts of a crime requiring criminal liability the case shall be opened; where not opened, "the reasons for not opening the case shall be notified to the complainant. If the complainant disagrees, they may apply for reconsideration." Article 113: "where the victim believes the public security organ has failed to open a case for investigation in a case that should be investigated, and raises it with the people's procuratorate, the procuratorate shall require the public security organ to explain the reasons for not opening the case; where the procuratorate finds the reasons untenable, it shall notify the public security organ to open a case, and the public security organ shall open one upon receiving the notice." The Measures for Handling Administrative Cases by Public Security Organs, article 61, has the same requirement. Cases within the unit's jurisdiction "shall be investigated and handled immediately, a case-acceptance register and a case-acceptance receipt shall be drawn up, and the receipt handed to the person reporting, the complainant, the informant, and the person who seized and delivered the suspect." The period for handling public-order cases "shall not exceed 30 days from acceptance; for major and complex cases it may be extended by 30 days with approval of the public security organ at the next higher level," and the appraisal period does not count toward it. The Regulations on Supervision and Inspection by Public Security Organs, article 9, governs complaints about the police. The original text: "for ongoing violations and misconduct by public security organs and their people's police reported by the public, the inspection body shall dispatch officers promptly, handle it on the spot per the rules, and promptly feed the outcome back to the complainant" (nationwide).

- Sources: 全国人大 (2018). 刑事诉讼法（2018 年修正，第一百一十二、一百一十三条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8080816f135f46016f1d1b81b01351>；公安部 (2020). 关于修改《公安机关办理刑事案件程序规定》的决定（公安部令第 159 号）及修正后全文. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5549884.htm>；公安部 (2013/2019 修正). 公安机关办理行政案件程序规定（公安部令第 125 号，第 149 号修改决定）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2013/content_2321117.htm>、<http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-12/31/content_5428593.htm>；国务院 (2011). 公安机关督察条例（国务院令第 603 号，第九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2011-09/08/content_6138.htm>
- Notes: The beneficiary is yourself. What this item solves is "I assumed that once I reported it, it would definitely be handled." Whether it is handled, and how fast, are governed by explicit standards and deadlines, not by the mood of the officer taking the call that day. Remember the three moves in order. One: get the case-acceptance receipt on the spot, and write down the case number and the accepting unit. Two: when the deadline passes with no result, ask about progress within the limits above — ask "which stage is the case at," not "why is nobody dealing with me." Three: when the notice of non-acceptance arrives, read the reasons first, apply for reconsideration within 7 days and for review within another 7, and at the same time you can raise case-opening supervision with the people's procuratorate — the three routes do not conflict. A public-order case means fights, petty theft, and the like. The handling period runs from the day of acceptance, no more than 30 days. For major and complex cases, with approval of the public security organ at the next higher level it can be extended by another 30 days. Time spent on forensic appraisal does not count. ### 40. Do not hand money or gift cards to the people handling your case or enforcing the law: bribery gets you sentenced too, and bribing supervisory, law-enforcement, or judicial personnel is punished more heavily
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. It is simply not doing it.
- In plain terms: "Spend a little money to smooth things over" is a separate crime in the Criminal Law, and the one caught is not only the person taking the money. Offering bribes carries up to 3 years. Serious circumstances carry 3 to 10 years, especially serious carries more than 10 years or life imprisonment. Bribing supervisory, administrative law-enforcement, or judicial personnel is squarely in the expressly aggravated tier. Giving money because you were extorted, without obtaining an improper benefit, does not count as bribery.
- Benefit: Criminal Law article 389: "giving property to a state functionary to obtain improper benefits is the crime of offering bribes"; "in economic exchanges, giving property to a state functionary in violation of state regulations, where the amount is relatively large, or giving kickbacks or handling fees to a state functionary under various names in violation of state regulations, is punished as offering bribes"; "giving property to a state functionary because of extortion, without obtaining an improper benefit, is not bribery." Criminal Law article 390 (as amended by Criminal Law Amendment XII, in force from March 1, 2024): "whoever commits the crime of offering bribes is sentenced to up to three years of fixed-term imprisonment or criminal detention, plus a fine; where the bribery to obtain improper benefits is serious in circumstances, or causes major losses to state interests, 3 to 10 years of fixed-term imprisonment plus a fine; where the circumstances are especially serious, or the losses to state interests especially major, 10 years or more of fixed-term imprisonment or life imprisonment, plus a fine or confiscation of property." There are seven aggravating circumstances calling for heavier punishment: "(1) bribing repeatedly or bribing multiple people; (2) a state functionary offering bribes; (3) offering bribes in national key projects or major projects; (4) offering bribes to obtain promotion or adjustment of position or rank; (5) bribing supervisory, administrative law-enforcement, or judicial personnel; (6) offering bribes in fields such as the eco-environment, finance, work safety, food and drugs, disaster prevention and relief, social security, education, and healthcare while carrying out unlawful or criminal activity; (7) using proceeds of unlawful activity to offer bribes." The final paragraph leaves the opening of voluntary confession: "where the bribe-giver voluntarily confesses the bribery before being prosecuted, a lighter or mitigated punishment may be given; among these, where the crime is minor, or where they played a key role in breaking open or solving a major case, or performed a major meritorious service, a mitigated or exempted punishment may be given" (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会. 中华人民共和国刑法（经刑法修正案十一修正的整合文本）第三百八十九条. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808181796a636a0179822a19640c92>；全国人大常委会 (2023). 中华人民共和国刑法修正案（十二）（第五项，修改刑法第三百九十条；自 2024 年 3 月 1 日起施行）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8081818c3ce31f018cb6a6bc412f55>
- Notes: The beneficiary is yourself. What this item aims at is the thought that "a little bribery can buy exemption from punishment." The person opposite you has colleagues, case ledgers, surveillance, and inspection bodies; what you hand over is evidence against him when he is investigated, and evidence of your own crime — and this category of bribery is squarely in the expressly aggravated tier. If the other side really does demand favors, the correct move is the channels from item 39 (ask for the case-acceptance receipt on the spot): keep the case-acceptance receipt and paperwork, and report to the inspection body or the discipline inspection and supervisory organs. Cash gifts in hospitals fall under a different set of rules; see section 24, item 12.
