# 8. Don't Get Yourself Locked Up: Law and Property Safety

The scope of this chapter is money and personal freedom: what liability the law actually imposes, how much loss can be avoided, and what procedural rights you have — it is not converted into life expectancy. Article numbers follow verified official source texts. Items are ordered by "largest loss in a single incident, easiest to act on", from high to low; items that require advance arrangement sit in the middle, and experience-based items go last.
### 1. Stop, rescue, and call the police after a traffic accident — never flee

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; stopping, turning on hazard lights, rescuing people, and phoning the police costs at most an hour or two waiting for traffic police. For a minor accident with no injuries and no dispute over the facts, photograph the scene, record the other party's name, phone number, license plate, driver's license number, insurance certificate number, and the damaged parts, and once both parties sign you may leave the scene and settle privately.
- In plain terms: if you flee, you are presumed fully at fault by default, your license is revoked and you may never reapply for one. If it constitutes the crime of traffic accident, fleeing means 3 to 7 years, and fleeing causing death means more than 7 years. There is a more practical layer too: property damage and third-party liability insurance pays nothing for hit-and-run, so hundreds of thousands in compensation come entirely out of your own pocket. Staying to rescue and call the police costs at most an hour or two.
- Benefit: the fleeing party bears full responsibility for the accident, reduced only if the other party is proven also at fault; fleeing revokes the driver's license with lifetime ban on reobtaining one; if it constitutes the crime of traffic accident, fleeing means 3 to 7 years, and fleeing causing death means more than 7 years; commercial auto insurance — both property damage and third-party liability — pays nothing for "hit-and-run" or "deliberately destroying or faking the scene, destroying evidence", so hundreds of thousands in compensation are paid entirely out of pocket (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 道路交通安全法（2021 年修订，第七十、一百零一条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/205308/index.html>（北京市公安交通管理局转载）；国务院 (2017). 道路交通安全法实施条例（国务院令第 405 号，2017 年修订，第八十六、九十二条）. <http://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=75>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第一百三十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；中国保险行业协会 (2020). 机动车商业保险示范条款（2020 版）（第九、二十二条）. <https://www.iachina.cn/art/2020/9/4/art_24_104621.html>
- Notes: When someone is injured, rescue first, then call the police; if you move the scene to rescue people, mark the original positions. "Hit and drove off without noticing" can also be deemed fleeing in a later determination — if unsure, call the police. The model clauses are an industry model text; each insurer's terms are governed by its own policy.

### 2. The moment you realize you've been scammed, call 110 or 96110 to demand a payment freeze — don't investigate on your own first

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one phone call, clearly stating the transfer time, amount, and the other party's account number, and keep the transfer screenshots and chat records.
- In plain terms: the few dozen minutes right after the money leaves your account is the only window: the police can urgently stop payments and freeze quickly, and banks must cooperate. Once the money has been passed along and split up, it is basically unrecoverable. So call 110 or 96110 first — don't go checking or asking around on your own first. When 96110 calls you, it is the police trying to talk you out of a scam; always pick up.
- Benefit: public security organs have the authority to conduct immediate queries, emergency payment stops, and rapid freezes on case-involved funds; banks and payment institutions must cooperate, and frozen funds are returned to victims through the proper procedure. Once money is transferred away and dispersed, the recovery probability drops sharply — the earlier the payment stop, the more effective. 96110 is the nationwide unified anti-fraud warning hotline; a call from it is the police intervening to stop you, so always answer (nationwide, from December 2022).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2022). 反电信网络诈骗法（第二十、三十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>；福建省公安厅 (2023). 96110 来电，请务必接听. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm>（省级公安机关页面）
- Notes: The law only stipulates "establishing a payment-stop and freeze system"; how much can be recovered depends on whether the money is still in the account when you report. Descriptions of the "National Anti-Fraud Center" app and 96110 are drawn from a Fujian Provincial Public Security Department page; the original page on the Ministry of Public Security website could not be opened. Anyone claiming to be police, prosecutors, or a court and asking you to transfer money to a "safe account" is a scammer.

### 3. Memorize the hard anti-fraud rules: don't trust unexpected callers, don't disclose information, don't click links, verify before transferring

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; memorize "three don'ts and one more" (三不一多). Anyone asking you to transfer money, share your screen, download an unfamiliar app, click a link, or read out a verification code — hang up first, then call back on the official number to verify. Install the "National Anti-Fraud Center" app.
- In plain terms: whenever someone asks you to transfer money, share your screen, download an unfamiliar app, click a link, or read out a verification code, hang up first and call back on the official number to verify. Callers posing as police, prosecutors, or courts, customer service, your boss, or platform risk control — asking you to "cooperate with an investigation", "cancel a membership", "place orders for rebates", or use "insider investments" — are all the same playbook in different clothes. Spotting it beforehand is far cheaper than chasing the money afterward.
- Benefit: the law requires governments and relevant departments to publicize the various forms of telecom and online fraud, and public security organs — together with finance, telecom, and cyberspace authorities — must build a warning and intervention system to dissuade potential victims identified by alerts. 96110 is exactly this nationwide unified warning-and-intervention hotline, so answer when it calls. Recognizing fraud beforehand is far cheaper than stopping payments afterward, and how much a payment stop recovers depends on whether the money is still in the account when you report (see item 2) (nationwide, from December 2022).
- Evidence grade: B

- Notes: Graded B because the maxim is official publicity language with no figure for "how much less you get scammed after memorizing it". ### 4. Seeing a face on video or hearing a voice on the phone is not verification — hang up and call back on the old number stored in your own contacts

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one extra phone call, a few extra minutes; post fewer front-facing videos and long voice clips on public accounts.
- In plain terms: seeing a face or hearing a voice is not verification. The method from the Ministry of Public Security's cybersecurity bureau: ask the person to make one continuous head-turn, or cover their face completely with a palm and then quickly move it away — current face-swapping stumbles and distorts at these occlusions and lighting changes. But the most reliable move is to hang up and call back the old number stored in your own contacts, or quietly ask someone you both know. If they refuse a live video call or the requested action citing "bad signal" or "in a meeting", treat it as a scam outright. Also: compliant platforms must label AI content, scammers won't — so "no AI label" cannot be taken in reverse as proof the other side is a real person.
- Benefit: three methods from the Ministry of Public Security's cybersecurity bureau. Motion verification (ask the person to make one continuous head-turn, or cover their face completely with a palm and quickly move it away; "with current AI technology, simulating these complex facial occlusions and lighting changes tends to produce stuttering, blurring, or distortion"). Voice details ("AI-generated speech sometimes lacks natural breathing pauses and emotional inflection, or carries a slight mechanical quality or abnormal background noise"). Multi-channel verification is "the most core method": "after hanging up, directly dial the other party's regular number that you know well (do not call back the suspicious incoming number), or verify indirectly through a third person you both know"; the maxim is "don't lightly trust video or voice, verify with motion checks". Face-swapping and voice-cloning are long banned by law for fraud. Platforms providing face generation, face replacement, synthetic voices, and voice imitation must apply conspicuous labels, and no organization or individual may maliciously delete, alter, forge, or conceal labels — but platforms follow the rules while scammers don't add labels, so "no AI label" cannot be taken in reverse as proof the other side is a real person. In one case published by the Ministry of Public Security's criminal investigation bureau, a 14-person ring was caught with more than 50,000 synthesized dynamic facial videos and more than 500,000 items of citizens' personal information, with 60 cloud servers seized (nationwide; the labeling obligation applies from September 1, 2025).
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 公安部网安局 (2025). 眼见≠为实，警惕 AI 新型诈骗！<http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202509/t20250922_7013008.htm>（福建省公安厅转载）；国家互联网信息办公室、工业和信息化部、公安部 (2022). 互联网信息服务深度合成管理规定（第六、十六、十七条，2023 年 1 月 10 日施行）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-12/12/content_5731431.htm>；国家互联网信息办公室、工业和信息化部、公安部、国家广播电视总局 (2025). 人工智能生成合成内容标识办法（第十条，2025 年 9 月 1 日施行）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654684782215.htm>；公安部刑侦局、央视新闻 (2026). 警方查获 5 万多条合成动态人脸视频 谁在「复制」你的脸？<http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202603/t20260330_7117146.htm>（福建省公安厅转载）
- Notes: Graded B because the official material is identification guidance and cases, with no figure for "how much the scam rate drops after verifying this way". Call back using the old number stored in your own contacts — not the number on the caller display, and not a new number the other party sent in chat; if they refuse a live video call or the requested action citing "bad signal" or "in a meeting", treat it as a scam outright. Motion verification is only an aid; models keep improving, and the only stable method is the third one — callback verification. If money has already been transferred, see item 2 of this chapter; to prevent your face and voice from being harvested, see section 14. ### 5. If accused or summoned, get a lawyer first — no private settlements, no deleting records

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Legal fees start from several thousand yuan; if in financial hardship you can apply for a free lawyer through a legal aid institution. During interrogation, answer only questions related to the case and leave the rest to your lawyer.
- In plain terms: from the first interrogation or from the moment coercive measures are taken against you, you have the right to a lawyer; when a lawyer requests a meeting, the detention center must arrange it within 48 hours at the latest, and the meeting is not monitored. You have the right not to answer questions unrelated to the case, and no one can force you to prove your own guilt. A single summons lasts at most 12 hours (24 for especially major and complex cases), and successive summonses cannot be used as disguised detention. Don't make private contact with the other party — any "private settlement" may be treated as an admission of guilt. Chat logs, calls, and travel records are your materials for proving your innocence; deleting them only robs you of evidence.
- Benefit: from the first interrogation or from the moment coercive measures are taken, you have the right to retain a lawyer; at the investigation stage only a lawyer may be retained. When a lawyer requests a meeting, the detention center arranges it within 48 hours at the latest, and the meeting is not monitored. No one may be forced to prove their own guilt, and you have the right to refuse questions unrelated to the case. A summons or compulsory summons lasts at most 12 hours per instance, at most 24 for especially major and complex cases, and successive summonses may not amount to disguised detention. Fabricating facts to falsely accuse another person and subject them to criminal pursuit carries up to 3 years; with serious consequences, 3 to 10 years. If a case is withdrawn, not prosecuted, or ends in acquittal after detention or arrest, you may apply for state compensation, calculated daily at the previous year's national average daily wage of employees (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 刑事诉讼法（2018 年修正，第三十四、三十五、三十九、五十二、一百一十九、一百二十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百四十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2012). 国家赔偿法（2012 年修正，第十七、三十三条）. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/gk/tjfg/xgfxfg/202503/t20250306_1958899.html>（国家统计局转载）
- Notes: Do not make private contact with the other party or their family; any "private settlement" may be read as an admission. Chat records, call logs, and travel records are your materials for self-exoneration — deleting them only loses you evidence. The crime of false accusation does not apply to mistaken reporting or inaccurate whistleblowing. This item makes no distinction by the accused person's sex. For three expanded items see item 31 of this chapter (pursuing the party that fabricated facts), item 32 (insufficient evidence must lead to acquittal, plus appeals and retrials), and item 33 (state compensation after custody is counted by the day).

### 6. Never touch the steering wheel after drinking — including e-bikes and "just moving the car a bit"

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: A designated driver or taxi costs tens of yuan; agree before a gathering with friends on who will not drink.
- In plain terms: driving after drinking means a 6-month license suspension plus a fine of one to two thousand yuan; a second offense means detention and revocation. Drunk driving means immediate revocation, no reapplication for 5 years, criminal liability for dangerous driving on top, and a criminal record that affects political vetting and some professions. For commercial vehicle operators it is heavier: drunk driving means a 10-year driving ban and a lifetime ban from commercial driving. Commercial auto insurance pays nothing for driving after drinking or taking drugs. "Just moving the car a bit" still counts as driving.
- Benefit: driving after drinking brings a 6-month license suspension and a fine of 1,000 to 2,000 yuan; a repeat offense brings detention of up to 10 days and revocation. Drunk driving brings immediate license revocation, no reapplication for 5 years, and criminal liability for dangerous driving (short-term detention plus a fine). For commercial operators, driving after drinking brings 15 days of detention, a 5,000-yuan fine, and a 5-year driving ban; drunk driving brings a 10-year ban and a lifetime ban from commercial driving. Causing a major accident while impaired or drunk, if it constitutes a crime, brings a lifetime driving ban. Commercial auto insurance pays nothing for driving after drinking or drug use (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 道路交通安全法（2021 年修订，第九十一条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/205308/index.html>（北京市公安交通管理局转载）；全国人大常委会 (2015). 刑法修正案（九）（第八条，即刑法第一百三十三条之一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180205_364562.shtml>；中国保险行业协会 (2020). 机动车商业保险示范条款（2020 版）（第九、二十二条）. <https://www.iachina.cn/art/2020/9/4/art_24_104621.html>
- Notes: Drunk driving is a criminal offense and leaves a criminal record, affecting the person's political vetting and some professional qualifications. The blood alcohol thresholds for "drinking" and "drunkenness" are set by national standards; this chapter has not verified the specific values — do not estimate by feel.

### 7. Never lend your bank card, SIM card, or payment accounts to anyone — "paofen" is not a side job

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; refuse requests like "just let your card run a payment through, I'll give you a few hundred".
- In plain terms: renting out or selling bank cards, SIM cards, or payment accounts to others is illegal in itself: a fine of 1 to 10 times the illegal gains, up to 200,000 yuan if there are no gains, detention of 15 days in serious cases, plus entries in your credit record and restrictions on account functions. If you knowingly provide payment settlement help for someone else's crime, that is the crime of assisting information network criminal activities, punishable by up to 3 years. In practice "knowingly" is often inferred from objective circumstances — taking a cut of the proceeds or clearly abnormal transaction volumes can both count.
- Benefit: renting out, lending, or buying and selling phone cards, bank accounts, payment accounts, or internet accounts is itself illegal: confiscation of illegal gains plus a fine of 1 to 10 times the gains, up to 200,000 yuan where there are no gains, detention of up to 15 days in serious cases, plus possible credit-record entries, account function restrictions, and suspension of non-counter banking services. Knowingly providing payment settlement or similar help for another person's online crime, if serious, constitutes the crime of assisting information network criminal activities, punishable by up to 3 years plus a fine (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2022). 反电信网络诈骗法（第三十一、四十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2015). 刑法修正案（九）（第二十九条，即刑法第二百八十七条之二）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180205_364562.shtml>
- Notes: "Knowingly" is in practice often inferred from objective circumstances; taking a cut of proceeds or transaction volumes clearly contrary to common sense can both be deemed knowing. If your card is used by others to receive stolen money, even if no crime is established, the account will be frozen and you will be disciplined.

### 8. Check your credit report twice a year for free, looking for loans and cards you never opened

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; ten minutes online.
- In plain terms: the regulations give you the right to two free checks of your own credit report each year — ten minutes online. When your ID is misused for online loans or card applications, you usually only find out when your mortgage is rejected, by which time years have already passed. Negative records stay for 5 years from the end of the underlying conduct and must be deleted after that; you can raise a dispute and must receive a written reply within 20 days. Check through the People's Bank of China Credit Reference Center website or your mobile banking app — not third-party "credit check" apps, which are themselves collecting your information.
- Benefit: administrative regulations state that "a personal information subject has the right to obtain their own credit report free of charge twice each year"; negative information "is retained for 5 years from the date the negative act or event ends; beyond 5 years it must be deleted"; if you dispute the report, the credit reference institution or information provider must reply in writing within 20 days of receiving the dispute.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2013). 征信业管理条例（国令第 631 号，第十六条、第十七条、第二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2013-01/29/content_2303.htm>
- Notes: When an ID is misused for online loans or cards, the person usually discovers it only when a mortgage application is rejected — by then several years have passed. ### 9. In a conflict, call the police before doing anything else — whoever strikes first almost always loses out

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; hold back from talking back or shoving, retreat to where there are people and call the police, and let the other party's actions stay on camera.
- In plain terms: hitting someone means 5 to 10 days of detention plus a fine of 500 to 1,000 yuan; beating someone as a group, beating the elderly, pregnant women, children, or people with disabilities, repeat beatings, or beating several people at once means 10 to 15 days of detention plus a fine of one to two thousand yuan. Injuring someone to the level of "minor injury" makes it a criminal case with up to 3 years; serious injury, 3 to 10 years. Mutual fighting does not count as legitimate self-defense — so whoever strikes first almost always loses out. Retreating to a crowded place, calling the police, and leaving the other party's moves on camera works better than fighting back. If they are already down or have run away and you keep hitting, it is no longer defense.
- Benefit: beating a person or intentionally injuring them means 5 to 10 days of detention plus a fine of 500 to 1,000 yuan; acting as a group, beating the elderly, pregnant women, children, or people with disabilities, repeat beatings, or beating several people at once means 10 to 15 days of detention plus a fine of 1,000 to 2,000 yuan; group brawls under the provision on picking quarrels and provoking trouble mean up to 15 days of detention plus a 2,000-yuan fine. Intentional injury constituting a crime carries up to 3 years; serious injury, 3 to 10 years. Mutual fighting does not count as legitimate self-defense, but a counterstrike is generally recognized as defense when the other party struck first with clearly excessive means, or when you tried hard to avoid the conflict and they persisted in the attack. Excessive defense requires both "clearly exceeding the necessary limits" and "causing major harm", and even excessive defense should receive mitigated or waived punishment (nationwide; the Public Security Administration Punishments Law takes effect January 1, 2026).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第三十、五十一条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二十、二百三十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部 (2020). 关于依法适用正当防卫制度的指导意见（法发〔2020〕31 号，第 6、9、11、14 条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/251611.html>
- Notes: The Public Security Administration Punishments Law was revised in 2025 with all article numbers changed — beating others moved from Article 43 of the old law to Article 51 — so materials citing old article numbers are outdated. The injury standard behind "minor injury means criminal liability" is set by forensic assessment standards; this chapter has not verified them. Striking someone who is already down or has fled means the unlawful attack has ended and it is no longer defense.

### 10. Whoever you hold a grudge against — unpaid wages, fired, cheated of money — go through complaints, arbitration, or the courts; don't go after them yourself

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; complaints and arbitration are both free of charge. It takes one or two trips and a few months of waiting — the hard part is holding back from violence during those months.
- In plain terms: the worst outcome of the legal route is that the money isn't recovered; the outcome of revenge is that the money still isn't recovered, plus you pay with your own life. Take the Wuxi case: Xu Moujin, denied his diploma and unhappy with his internship pay, went on a knife attack that killed 8 and wounded 17; he was sentenced to death, and the sentence has been carried out — he was angry at his school and over that money, he stabbed his classmates, and he never got a cent of the money. For unpaid wages, go through complaints and arbitration; in 2024, nationwide, this route recovered 8.65 billion yuan for 654,000 people.
- Benefit: the legal route has official numbers: in 2024, labor inspection nationwide handled 137,000 cases, recovering 8.65 billion yuan in wages for 654,000 people, and 998 people were sentenced for the crime of refusing to pay labor remuneration; for the procedure see item 2 of section 7. The price list of the revenge route is written in Article 232 of the Criminal Law: "whoever intentionally kills shall be sentenced to death, life imprisonment, or fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 10 years; if the circumstances are relatively minor, to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 3 years but not more than 10 years". ### 18. If sued or subject to enforcement, report your assets truthfully and repay what you can — don't move your house or money to relatives, friends, or a company

- Notes: Graded B because the statute contains only the words "other means"; "a person who is drunk or in a state of semi-consciousness cannot effectively consent" is prevailing judicial doctrine, and this chapter obtained no currently operative public interpretation that could be verified verbatim. The age-side standard is strict — see item 18 of section 9: under 12 is always deemed "should have known". This item makes no distinction by sex; men can equally be victims. When you yourself drink to the point of blacking out, both risks — being accused and being blackmailed — exist at once; see item 30 of this chapter.

### 30. If the other person demands money with threats to report you, send your photos, or tell your employer — pay nothing and report to the police directly

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0; screenshot for evidence and bring your phone to the police station.
- In plain terms: demanding property by threatening "I'll go to the police" or "I'll send the photos to your employer" is extortion, not a private matter. A value of 2,000 to 5,000 yuan or more already counts as "relatively large", with up to 3 years; 30,000 to 100,000 or more is "huge", 3 to 10 years; 300,000 to 500,000 or more is "especially huge", more than 10 years. Three or more extortions within two years count as "multiple" even if each sum is small. The approach is exactly the same as against nude-chat blackmail: pay not a cent and delete not a single record.
- Benefit: Article 274 of the Criminal Law: extortion of public or private property, in a relatively large amount or committed repeatedly, carries up to 3 years of fixed-term imprisonment, short-term detention, or public surveillance, with a fine imposed concurrently or separately; a huge amount or other serious circumstances carry 3 to 10 years of fixed-term imprisonment plus a fine; an especially huge amount or other especially serious circumstances carry more than 10 years of fixed-term imprisonment plus a fine. The interpretation of the two Supreme bodies on several issues concerning the application of law in extortion criminal cases (Fa Shi [2013] No. 10, effective April 27, 2013), Article 1: extortion of public or private property valued at 2,000 to 5,000 yuan or more, 30,000 to 100,000 yuan or more, or 300,000 to 500,000 yuan or more shall be recognized as "relatively large", "huge", and "especially huge" respectively; Article 3: three or more extortions within two years shall be recognized as "repeated extortion" (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020 年修正). 刑法（第二百七十四条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>（北京市公安交管局转载整合文本）；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2013). 关于办理敲诈勒索刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2013〕10 号）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/5310.html>
- Notes: The three tiers are set by each province within the above ranges; the local standard governs. Pay the first sum and a second will follow — the same as the nude-chat blackmail in section 13. Don't delete chat records, close your account, or block them and call it done — that deletes your own evidence too; see item 5 of this chapter. If a case is really opened against you, those records are also your materials for self-exoneration. This item concerns the other party demanding money with leverage over you; conversely, you as the victim seeking compensation from the infringing party is a different matter — a high amount does not make it extortion; see item 34 of this chapter.

### 31. If someone reports you on fabricated facts, you can demand they be pursued: public security detention from 5 days, and up to 3 years where it is a crime

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0; report to the public security organ and explain that the other party fabricated the facts.
- In plain terms: fabricating facts to falsely accuse and frame another person in an attempt to have them criminally pursued or administratively punished is itself a public security violation: up to 5 days of detention or a fine of up to 1,000 yuan, or 5 to 10 days of detention with a possible fine of up to 1,000 yuan in more serious cases. Where it reaches the criminal level, it is the crime of false accusation: up to 3 years where circumstances are serious, 3 to 10 years where serious consequences follow, with heavier punishment for state functionaries. But the law draws its own line: where it was not deliberate framing but mistaken reporting or an inaccurate tip, neither paragraph applies — that is why many cases end without pursuing the reporter, not because there is no provision.
- Benefit: the Public Security Administration Punishments Law (revised June 27, 2025, effective January 1, 2026), Article 50: fabricating facts to falsely accuse and frame another person in an attempt to subject them to criminal pursuit or public security administration punishment carries up to 5 days of detention or a fine of up to 1,000 yuan; in more serious cases, 5 to 10 days of detention with a possible fine of up to 1,000 yuan. Article 243 of the Criminal Law: fabricating facts to falsely accuse another person with intent to subject them to criminal pursuit, where circumstances are serious, carries up to 3 years of fixed-term imprisonment, short-term detention, or public surveillance; where serious consequences follow, 3 to 10 years; state functionaries committing the crime are punished more heavily; where it was not deliberate framing but mistaken reporting or an inaccurate tip, the preceding two paragraphs do not apply (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第五十条）. <https://gaj.beijing.gov.cn/wsgs/2024zcwj/zcfg/202506/t20250630_4134266.html>（北京市公安局转载全文）；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百四十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>
- Notes: To pursue the other party, what must be proven is "fabricating facts" plus "intent to frame", not "I didn't do it" — these two differ greatly in evidentiary difficulty, so don't treat pursuit as a guaranteed outcome. There are no nationwide published statistics on false-accusation case filings and dispositions; this book could not find any, so neither an incidence rate nor a sex distribution is given. For the evidence and remedies on your own side, see items 32 and 33 of this chapter.

### 32. Insufficient evidence means acquittal by design; extracted confessions must be excluded; after conviction there are still petitions and retrials

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0; petitions and retrials take time and usually require a lawyer — fees as in item 5 of this chapter.
- In plain terms: the threshold for conviction is written in the Criminal Procedure Law: the evidence deciding the case must be verified as true through statutory procedures, and all the evidence taken together must exclude reasonable doubt; failing that, it falls under "insufficient evidence, the alleged crime cannot be established", and acquittal is required. Confessions obtained by torture or other illegal methods, and witness testimony or victim statements obtained by violence or threats, must be excluded — at whatever stage discovered, investigation, review before prosecution, or trial — and may not serve as the basis for prosecution opinions, prosecution decisions, or judgments. After a judgment takes effect, the party, their legal representative, and close relatives may still petition the court or the procuratorate, and where statutory circumstances are met the court must retry the case.
- Benefit: Article 55 of the Criminal Procedure Law: for evidence to be reliable and sufficient it must meet conditions including that all evidence on which the case is decided is verified as true through statutory procedures, and that all the evidence taken together excludes reasonable doubt on the facts found; Article 56: confessions of suspects or defendants collected by illegal methods such as torture, and witness testimony or victim statements collected by violence, threats, or other illegal methods, must be excluded; where evidence that should be excluded is discovered during investigation, review before prosecution, or trial, it shall be excluded in accordance with law and may not serve as the basis for prosecution opinions, prosecution decisions, and judgments; Article 200, item (3): where the evidence is insufficient to find the defendant guilty, an acquittal shall be pronounced on the ground that the evidence is insufficient and the alleged crime cannot be established; Article 252: the party, their legal representative, and close relatives may petition the people's court or the people's procuratorate against a judgment or ruling that has taken legal effect, but such petition does not stay the execution of the judgment or ruling; Article 253 lists the circumstances in which the people's court shall retry the case (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 刑事诉讼法（2018 年修正，第五十五、五十六、二百、二百五十二、二百五十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml>
- Notes: These are the bottom lines written into the system, not guarantees of outcome. This book has no verifiable official data on acquittal rates or petition-reversal rates in Chinese criminal cases, so no proportions are given and no individual cases are assessed. Three things you can do yourself: retain a lawyer right after the first interrogation (item 5 of this chapter), read the interrogation transcript word for word before signing, and never admit to something you didn't do in order to "get out sooner" — pleading guilty is a leniency factor, not an exemption.

### 33. If charges are dropped, not pursued, or you are acquitted after being detained, apply for state compensation — counted by the day

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0; submit the compensation application to the organ that made the detention, arrest, prosecution, or judgment.
- In plain terms: if the case is withdrawn, not prosecuted, or you are acquitted after detention or arrest, you can apply for state compensation. The personal freedom item is counted by the day: the Supreme People's Procuratorate's notice sets it at 495.94 yuan per day from May 18, 2026 (the previous year was 475.52 yuan), so a year in custody is roughly 180,000 yuan. Beyond that money, you may demand that the influence be eliminated, your reputation restored, and an apology made within the scope affected by the infringement, with mental-distress compensation for serious consequences. One situation where the state does not pay: where you deliberately made false confessions or fabricated evidence of guilt, leading to your detention.
- Benefit: Article 17 of the State Compensation Law: the victim has the right to compensation where, after detention measures were taken against a citizen in violation of the Criminal Procedure Law, or detention was lawful but exceeded the statutory time limit, the case is subsequently withdrawn, prosecution is declined, or acquittal is pronounced terminating criminal liability; where arrest was carried out and the case is subsequently withdrawn, prosecution declined, or acquittal pronounced terminating criminal liability; or where a retrial under the trial supervision procedure overturns the verdict to acquittal and the original sentence has been executed. Article 33: compensation for infringement of a citizen's personal freedom is calculated daily at the state's previous-year average daily wage of employees. Article 35: where mental harm is caused, the influence shall be eliminated, reputation restored, and an apology made within the scope affected by the infringement, and where serious consequences follow, corresponding mental-distress compensation shall be paid. Article 19: the state bears no liability where a citizen was detained or sentenced because they deliberately made false confessions or fabricated other evidence of guilt. Notice of the Supreme People's Procuratorate, May 2026: the National Bureau of Statistics announced on May 15, 2026 that the 2025 average annual wage of employed persons in urban non-private units nationwide was 129,441 yuan, giving an average daily wage of 495.94 yuan; from May 18, 2026, procuratorates at all levels shall calculate compensation for infringement of personal freedom at 495.94 yuan per day when making state compensation decisions (the standard from May 2025 was 475.52 yuan per day) (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2012 年修正). 国家赔偿法（第十七、十九、三十三、三十五条）. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/gk/tjfg/xgfxfg/202503/t20250306_1958899.html>（国家统计局转载）；最高人民检察院 (2026). 最高人民检察院公布最新侵犯公民人身自由的赔偿金标准. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202605/t20260519_727675.shtml>
- Notes: This daily standard updates each May with the previous year's average annual wage of urban non-private unit employees published by the National Bureau of Statistics — check the current year's figure when using it; don't apply this item's number directly. Mental-distress compensation is set by the handling organ according to the consequences, with no unified formula, and this item gives no amount. Also know what is not compensated: that decision letter deemed lost wages, compensation for bodily and life rights, property losses, attorney fees, and detention center living-cost subsidies alike outside the statutory scope of state compensation.

### 34. As a victim seeking compensation, go through 12315, the courts, or a lawyer — don't meet the other party alone, and never bundle "pay me" and "I won't go public" into one sentence

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0; raise the claim through 12315, by suing in court, or through a lawyer; record the entire negotiation if you meet.
- In plain terms: as a victim seeking compensation from the infringing party — including asking for a very high amount, including going to the media — is in itself legally grounded and does not automatically become extortion just because "the demand is high". The procuratorate's opinion in the Guo Li case states it plainly: the existing evidence was insufficient to establish that he had the purpose of unlawful possession; as his daughter's statutory guardian he had the right to claim compensation from the milk powder manufacturer, and consumers also have the right to expose and criticize through the media. But the case also shows how large the cost can be: he was first sentenced to five years for extortion and detained for 1,826 days, and it was not until April 7, 2017 that the Guangdong Higher People's Court acquitted him on retrial. So do three things: use channels that leave a record; never go alone to a place the other party designates; and never say "pay me" and "then I won't go public, won't report" as one bundled condition.
- Benefit: official case: in 2008, after the authorities announced that some batches of "Shien" brand milk powder contained melamine, the examination result for Guo Li's daughter was "several punctate strong echoes visible in the central collecting systems of both kidneys"; Guo Li repeatedly sought compensation from the seller and Shien and exposed the matter to media. In June 2009 the two sides reached an agreement: Shien paid 400,000 yuan in compensation and Guo Li issued a written statement that he would no longer pursue the matter and waived his compensation claims. The company then reported to the police, and the Chao'an County People's Court convicted Guo Li of extortion and sentenced him to five years of fixed-term imprisonment. The Guangdong Provincial People's Procuratorate's retrial recommendation held that "the existing evidence is insufficient to establish that Guo Li subjectively had the purpose of unlawful possession", that Guo Li as statutory guardian "has the right to claim compensation from the milk powder manufacturer, and his claim is legally grounded", and that "consumers have the right to expose and criticize, through mass media, acts that harm their lawful rights and interests". On April 7, 2017, the Guangdong Higher People's Court acquitted Guo Li on retrial, reasoning that "the original trial's finding that Guo Li, with the purpose of unlawful possession, used threats and coercion to forcibly demand property is unclear in fact and insufficient in evidence, and the nature of Guo Li's conduct did not exceed the scope of a civil dispute". Guo Li was detained from July 23, 2009 to July 22, 2014 — 1,826 days (Guangdong; acquittal on retrial in 2017).
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 广东省人民检察院 (2017). 广东省检察院再审建议对郭利案改判无罪. <https://jcy.heyuan.gov.cn/node/142>（河源市人民检察院转载）；广东省高级人民法院赔偿委员会 (2018). 郭利以再审无罪为由申请、广东省潮州市中级人民法院赔偿的国家赔偿案件（（2018）粤委赔 11 号）. <https://www.gdcourts.gov.cn/caipanwenshuxuandeng/caipanwenshuxuandeng/content/post_1045709.html>
- Notes: Graded B because it is a single case, not a generalizable proportion — and it proves two things at once: that claiming compensation is itself lawful, and that a case can still be opened against you. The lowest-risk path is to hand the claim to a lawyer or sue directly, with no one-on-one contact with the other party; be especially careful when they invite you to "talk it out in person" in their own locality — reports and case filings usually happen there. Once a settlement is signed, follow the agreement; making a fresh high demand after signing is the easiest thing to read as "coercion". For what to do if a case is opened against you, see items 5, 32, and 33 of this chapter; the reverse situation — the other party demanding money with leverage over you — is covered in item 30 of this chapter.

### 35. When you are mobbed online: turn on protections and secure evidence first, then choose among the three routes — platform, injunction, police report
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; a dozen or so minutes on the spot for protection and evidence; going as far as applying for an injunction or a private prosecution means visits to court and possibly a lawyer
- In plain terms: do three things on the spot first — turn on all of the platform's protective settings (block strangers or specific users, limit who can see what you post, forbid reposts or comments, accept private messages only from friends or none at all), use the platform's quick evidence-capture function to fix the content in place, then report it through the prominent shortcut entry. Then there are three routes: if the attack is ongoing and not stopping it promptly would cause damage hard to make up, you can ask the court for a personality-rights infringement injunction to make them stop first; insults and defamation in principle require you to sue in court yourself, and where gathering evidence is genuinely difficult the court can ask the public security organs to help identify who is behind it and collect evidence of the spreading and the impact — once they have assisted and the case meets the conditions, the court must docket it; at the "seriously disrupting social order" tier the case converts to public prosecution and you do not have to carry the burden of proof. Doxxing — putting your personal information out there — is handled as the crime of infringing citizens' personal information when the circumstances are serious.
- Benefit: (法发〔2023〕14 号 — the SPC's Guideline No. 14 of 2023 on personality-rights protection), article 15: where a rights holder has evidence that a person is committing or is about to commit an unlawful act infringing their personality rights, and failure to stop it promptly would cause irreparable damage to their lawful rights and interests, the people's court may issue a personality-rights infringement injunction upon application under article 997 of the Civil Code; article 11: in private prosecution cases, "where the victim has genuine difficulty providing evidence, they may request assistance from the public security organs", which shall promptly identify the perpetrator and collect evidence materials on the spread, diffusion, and impact; network service providers shall provide the necessary technical support, "where evidence has been gathered with the assistance of the public security organs and the case meets the conditions for accepting a private prosecution, the people's court shall decide to docket it", and where it cannot be collected they must explain in writing; article 12 lists five situations to be recognized as "seriously disrupting social order" under article 246(2) of the Criminal Law: causing the victim or their close relatives mental breakdown, suicide, or other grave consequences with vile social impact; arbitrarily targeting ordinary members of the public with information spreading on a large scale and drawing masses of vulgar malicious comments with vile social impact; insulting or defaming multiple people or repeatedly spreading such content with vile social impact; organizing or directing people to spread it in volume across multiple platforms with vile social impact; and others. Article 4: organizing "human-flesh searches" (doxxing), unlawfully collecting and publishing citizens' personal information to an unspecified majority, is convicted and punished as the crime of infringing citizens' personal information where the circumstances are serious. The Provisions on the Governance of Online Violence Information, article 23, requires platforms to offer protective options such as blocking strangers or specific users, limiting the visibility of one's own posts, forbidding reposts or comments, and accepting private messages only from friends or none at all; article 25 requires a quick evidence-capture function; article 26 requires a prominent quick complaint and report entry; article 15 requires platforms to report suspected crimes to the public security organs promptly
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部 (2023). 关于依法惩治网络暴力违法犯罪的指导意见（法发〔2023〕14 号，第 4、11、12、15 条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/412992.html>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第九百九十七条）. 最高人民检察院转载全文 <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；国家互联网信息办公室、公安部、文化和旅游部、国家广播电视总局 (2024). 网络暴力信息治理规定（第十五、二十三、二十五、二十六条，2024 年 8 月 1 日施行）. 国务院公报 <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11526/202408/content_6969181.html>

### 36. The "buy insurance for someone first, then act" route is legally blocked at every step: not a fen of the money is collectible, and the crime is intentional homicide plus insurance fraud punished concurrently
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: all four doors are shut. The first: buying insurance on a family member's life with death as the payment condition, without that person's consent and approval of the insured amount, makes the contract void; the policyholder designating a beneficiary also requires the insured person's consent (parents insuring minor children are not subject to this). The second: if the policyholder deliberately causes the insured person's death, the insurer bears no payment obligation; a beneficiary who deliberately causes the insured's death — even attempted deliberate killing — loses the right to benefit outright. The third: the route of the insurance money falling back into the estate does not exist either: deliberately killing the decedent forfeits the right of inheritance. The fourth: the Criminal Law lists "policyholder or beneficiary deliberately causing the insured person's death, disability, or illness to defraud insurance money" as a separate item, and states that where it also constitutes another crime, punishments are imposed concurrently — intentional homicide is counted separately, with the death penalty at the top. As for "it can't be traced": in the Xuancheng, Anhui case of a staged vehicle-into-water death, less than half a month before the incident the man bought 4 policies on his wife of 1 million yuan each, two days before it he searched online for "how to escape a car sinking into water" and "how long it takes to die from drowning", and the day after reporting it he searched "statutory beneficiaries' distribution ratios"; combined with no braking marks before the car entered the water and tire tracks across the grass running dead straight, he was arrested 7 days later and ultimately sentenced to death with reprieve plus a 50,000-yuan fine, for intentional homicide and insurance fraud punished concurrently.
- Benefit: Insurance Law article 34: a contract with death as the condition for paying insurance benefits is void without the insured person's consent and approval of the insured amount (parents insuring minor children are not restricted); article 39: designating a beneficiary requires the insured person's consent; article 43: where the policyholder deliberately causes the insured person's death, disability, or illness, the insurer bears no obligation to pay insurance benefits, and where the policyholder has paid premiums for two full years or more the cash value is returned to other rights holders; a beneficiary who deliberately causes the insured's death, disability, or illness, or attempts to kill the insured deliberately, loses the right to benefit; article 27(2): where the policyholder or insured person deliberately creates the insured event, the insurer may rescind the contract and bears no liability to pay compensation or benefits, and premiums are not refunded except as provided in article 43. Civil Code article 1125: one who deliberately kills the decedent forfeits the right of inheritance. Criminal Law article 198(1)(v) lists "policyholder or beneficiary deliberately causing the insured person's death, disability, or illness to defraud insurance money" as insurance fraud: where the amount is relatively large, up to 5 years plus a fine of 10,000 to 100,000 yuan; where large, 5 to 10 years; where especially large, over 10 years; article 198(2): where such conduct also constitutes another crime, punishment is under the rules on concurrent punishment for multiple crimes; article 232: intentional homicide is punishable by death, life imprisonment, or not less than 10 years of fixed-term imprisonment. Case: the Zhang Mousong case in Xuancheng, Anhui — in the early hours of July 18, 2016 he drove into a pond, drowning his wife; less than half a month earlier he had taken out 4 policies on her of 1 million yuan each; he was arrested on July 25, and was sentenced to death with reprieve plus a fine of 50,000 yuan for intentional homicide and insurance fraud punished concurrently

### 37. Ask for the case-acceptance receipt on the spot when reporting to the police, and a written notice if no case is opened: within 7 days you may apply for reconsideration, then within 7 more for review, and the procuratorate can order the police to open a case
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one extra question on the spot for the receipt, then follow up by the dates
- In plain terms: reporting to the police and having a case opened are two different things. When the public security organ accepts a case it must draw up a case-acceptance register and a case-acceptance receipt and hand the receipt to the person reporting, complaining, or informing — that slip of paper is your only proof that "you reported it", so ask for it on the spot; it is worth the trouble. For public-order cases (fights, petty theft and the like) the handling period is at most 30 days from acceptance, extendable by another 30 days with approval of the public security organ at the next higher level when the case is major and complex, and time spent on forensic appraisal does not count toward it. On the criminal track the case must be reviewed promptly after acceptance, and a case is opened only if there are facts of a crime requiring criminal liability; where the decision is not to open a case and there is a complainant, a notice of non-acceptance must be drawn up and delivered within 3 days. Disagreement does not mean just putting up with it: within 7 days of receiving the notice you may apply to the deciding public security organ for reconsideration, which must decide within 30 days; if you still disagree with the reconsideration result, within 7 days you may apply to the next higher public security organ for review, which must decide within 30 days — for major and complex matters it may be extended, but by no more than 30 days and with written notice to you. There is also an independent route: if the victim believes the public security organ should open a case but has not, they can go to the people's procuratorate, which shall require the public security organ to explain its reasons and, finding the reasons untenable, notify it to open a case, and the public security organ must then open one. Violations and misconduct by officers in handling the case go to the inspection (dubao) body as a complaint, on a separate track from the case itself.

### 38. Do not hand money or gift cards to the people handling your case or enforcing the law: bribery gets you sentenced too, and bribing supervisory, law-enforcement, or judicial personnel is punished more heavily
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; it is simply not doing it
- In plain terms: "spend a little money to smooth things over" is a separate crime in the Criminal Law, and the one caught is not only the person taking the money. The crime of offering bribes is defined as giving property to state functionaries to obtain improper benefits; Criminal Law Amendment XII, in force from March 1, 2024, reworked the sentencing into three tiers: up to 3 years plus a fine; 3 to 10 years where the bribery to obtain improper benefits is serious in circumstances or causes major losses to state interests; over 10 years or life imprisonment where especially serious, plus a fine or confiscation of property. It also lists seven aggravating circumstances, two of which aim straight at this kind of scene: "bribing supervisory, administrative law-enforcement, or judicial personnel", and bribing in fields such as education, healthcare, social security, food and drugs, and work safety while carrying out unlawful or criminal activity. The law leaves two openings: giving property because you were extorted, without obtaining an improper benefit, is not bribery; and voluntarily confessing the bribery before being prosecuted can bring a lighter or mitigated punishment, with a mitigated or exempted punishment where the crime is minor and you played a key role in cracking a major case.
- Benefit: Criminal Law article 389: "giving property to a state functionary to obtain improper benefits is the crime of offering bribes"; "in economic exchanges, giving property to a state functionary in violation of state regulations, where the amount is relatively large, or giving kickbacks or handling fees to a state functionary under various names in violation of state regulations, is punished as offering bribes"; "giving property to a state functionary because of extortion, without obtaining an improper benefit, is not bribery". Criminal Law article 390 (as amended by Criminal Law Amendment XII, in force from March 1, 2024): "whoever commits the crime of offering bribes is sentenced to up to three years of fixed-term imprisonment or criminal detention, plus a fine; where the bribery to obtain improper benefits is serious in circumstances, or causes major losses to state interests, 3 to 10 years of fixed-term imprisonment plus a fine; where the circumstances are especially serious, or the losses to state interests especially major, 10 years or more of fixed-term imprisonment or life imprisonment, plus a fine or confiscation of property"; the seven aggravating circumstances are "(1) bribing repeatedly or bribing multiple people; (2) a state functionary offering bribes; (3) offering bribes in national key projects or major projects; (4) offering bribes to obtain promotion or adjustment of position or rank; (5) bribing supervisory, administrative law-enforcement, or judicial personnel; (6) offering bribes in fields such as the eco-environment, finance, work safety, food and drugs, disaster prevention and relief, social security, education, and healthcare while carrying out unlawful or criminal activity; (7) using proceeds of unlawful activity to offer bribes"; final paragraph: "where the bribe-giver voluntarily confesses the bribery before being prosecuted, a lighter or mitigated punishment may be given; among these, where the crime is minor, or where they played a key role in breaking open or solving a major case, or performed a major meritorious service, a mitigated or exempted punishment may be given" (nationwide)
