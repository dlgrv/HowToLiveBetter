# 7. How to get by when you have no money

This section is about money and social safeguards. How much you can receive, what fees you can avoid, what services you can get, and what conditions you must meet are all written here — not converted into years of life. Policies change every year and differ by place, so amounts and thresholds follow what your locality publishes for the current year.

Items in this section are grouped by topic below, with item numbers in parentheses.

**Losing a job**: Apply online for unemployment insurance benefits (item 1); get recognized as someone facing employment difficulties (item 12); claim vocational training subsidies (item 13).

**Unpaid wages**: File a complaint with labor inspection first (item 2); apply for legal aid (item 3); when wages are owed, first figure out who hired you (item 22).

**Emergency relief**: Go to a shelter (item 4); apply for temporary assistance (item 6); apply for dibao (item 7); the two disability subsidies (item 8); replace a lost ID card at once (item 11); when you truly have nowhere to sleep (item 21); when you already cannot repay credit cards or online loans (item 23).

**Medical and social insurance**: Do not let resident medical insurance lapse (item 9); for serious illness, use medical insurance and medical assistance first (item 10); do not panic if social insurance contributions are interrupted (item 18); add a medical or critical-illness policy (item 20).

**Finding work and starting over**: Public employment services and gig markets (item 5); use a systematic approach when job hunting (item 14); pay no deposit and surrender no ID (item 15); even after prison or bankruptcy there is a path to start over (item 19).

**Housing and food**: Queue for public rental housing (item 16); cut housing and food to the minimum (item 17).
### 1. After losing a job, apply online for unemployment insurance benefits first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money spent. You can submit the application on a phone through any of three portals: the "Zhangshang 12333" (掌上12333) App, the electronic social security card mini-program in WeChat or Alipay, or the National Social Insurance Public Service Platform. A social security card or ID card is enough; in most regions you need not go in person. The prerequisite is completing unemployment registration first.
- In plain terms: If your employer and you have paid for a full 1 year, and you did not resign on your own, then after unemployment registration and if you are willing to look for work, you can receive money. With under 5 years of contributions after that full 1 year, you can receive for at most 12 months; with a full 5 years but under 10 years, at most 18 months; with 10 years or more, at most 24 months. While you receive benefits, employee medical insurance is paid for you by unemployment insurance.
- Benefit: All three conditions must be met. First, your employer and you must have paid unemployment insurance for a cumulative full 1 year. Second, you did not resign on your own (the legal term is “employment interrupted not due to the person’s own will”). Third, you have completed unemployment registration and are willing to seek work. How long you can receive depends on contribution years. Cumulative full 1 year but under 5 years: at most 12 months. Full 5 years but under 10 years: at most 18 months. 10 years or more: at most 24 months. The monthly amount is set by the provincial government and differs by province. It is below the local minimum wage and above urban dibao. The national goal is to raise it gradually to 90% of the minimum wage. While receiving unemployment benefits, you still participate in employee basic medical insurance and enjoy its benefits. The basic medical insurance premiums due are paid from the unemployment insurance fund; you pay nothing personally (nationwide; the regulations have been in force since 1999)

- Notes: Resigning on your own does not count as “not due to the person’s own will,” so you cannot receive benefits. When leaving a job, do not sign “voluntary resignation for personal reasons” — see Section 19, item 7. Unemployment benefits are counted from the day you complete unemployment registration; register late and you get money late. The first thing after leaving is to register. Registration needs the termination certificate issued by the employer — see Section 19, item 17 (termination certificate). Whether to keep a non-compete and what to do if the company pays no compensation — see Section 11, item 12 (signed a non-compete). While you receive benefits, employee medical insurance is paid for you by unemployment insurance. Amounts differ a lot by province; follow what the place where you are insured publishes. The “90% of minimum wage” wording comes from a 2017 guidance opinion of the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and the Ministry of Finance (人社部发〔2017〕71 号); the original text says “gradually raise the unemployment insurance benefit standard to 90% of the minimum wage standard.” That is a goal, not the amount paid today. The original page on the ministry site could not be opened locally; this sentence was checked against a citation in a Liaoning provincial human resources and social security department document.

### 2. When wages are owed, complain to labor inspection first, then apply for labor arbitration; both routes are free, and most cases have a result within a few months
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money spent; complaints and arbitration both charge nothing. Gather the labor contract, pay stubs, attendance records, transfer records, and chat logs, and visit the human resources and social security bureau or the arbitration commission 1 to 3 times. Arbitration has a 1-year deadline (the legal term is the arbitration limitation period). If you are still working at the unit and wages are being delayed, that 1-year limit does not apply. If you have already left, you must file within 1 year from the day you left. The hard part is the repeated trips and hunting for evidence piece by piece; it is easy to lose heart in the middle.
- In plain terms: First call 12333 or complain to labor inspection at the human resources and social security bureau; after a case is filed, the investigation finishes within 60 working days. If confirmed, the company can be ordered to pay by a deadline; if it still does not pay, it must add another 50% to 100% as compensation. If that fails, apply for labor arbitration; the case is closed within 45 days. But “case closed” is not “money in hand”: if the company has no assets or the boss has fled, you may win and still get nothing.
- Benefit: The first step is to complain. Call 12333, or complain to labor inspection at the local human resources and social security bureau. By law the investigation finishes within 60 working days after filing; complex cases may be extended by another 30 working days. If confirmed, the company can be ordered to pay by a deadline. If it still does not pay after the deadline, it must add compensation of 50% to 100% of the amount due. In 2024, inspection agencies nationwide handled 137000 cases and recovered 8650000000 yuan in wages for 654000 people. If you have an IOU or a clear wage sheet, you may also apply directly to a grassroots court for a payment order. A payment order is a court order telling the company to pay; the court issues it within 15 days of accepting the case. The second step is to apply for arbitration. By law the case is closed within 45 days of acceptance, extendable by another 15 days. For unpaid wages you may apply for advance enforcement: part of the award can be enforced before the whole case is finished. If the amount owed does not exceed 12 times the local monthly minimum wage, it is final in one arbitration: after the award the company cannot sue again. In 2024, 4257000 cases were handled nationwide and 4156000 were closed. The closed amount was 93470000000 yuan. The mediation success rate was 79.6%, the arbitration closure rate 98.2%, and the arbitration termination rate 73.7%. Some people say this takes “half a year to a year”; that refers to cases that go on to court after arbitration. Those cases take 6 months at first instance (3 months under the summary procedure), plus 3 months for a second instance. In 2024 courts closed 88000 cases seeking labor remuneration and recovered 28540000000 yuan; 998 people were sentenced for the crime of refusing to pay labor remuneration (nationwide)

If the company has no assets or the boss has fled, you may still get nothing after winning. The Supreme People’s Court only publishes the total amount enforced (2300000000000 yuan in 2024) and that the “enforcement completion rate stayed high”; there is no official figure for how much of wage cases actually reached the workers’ pockets. The term “arbitration termination rate” is not defined in the bulletin; this item treats it as “no further court case after the award.” Unpaid wages at certain amounts are enough for a criminal case. One type is owing one person for more than 3 months, with an amount of 5000 to 20000 yuan or more. Another is owing 10 or more people, totaling 30000 to 100000 yuan or more. In either case, if payment is still refused after an order to pay, that is enough to open a case. Provincial high courts and procuratorates set the exact amount within that range and publish it; check your province before complaining, and raise it face to face when you complain. Migrant workers on construction sites also have dedicated wage accounts and a rule that the general contractor pays first; going after the general contractor works better than going after the labor boss. Both routes assume there is an employing unit. An unlicensed workshop or shop still counts; you can complain and arbitrate. Private work for a household or individual, such as being a nanny, is not a labor relationship — go to court; see item 22 (first figure out who hired you). Rural migrant workers applying for legal aid to recover wages are not checked for financial hardship. ### 3. Apply for legal aid if you cannot afford a lawsuit—unpaid wages, support payments, and workplace injuries are already covered
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Legal aid does not charge recipients a single cent.
- In plain terms: You can apply for free legal aid to recover unpaid wages, claim child or elder support, seek social insurance or dibao (minimum subsistence allowance) benefits, claim compensation for workplace injuries, or seek relief after acting as a Good Samaritan. Call 12348 or visit your local legal aid center. You no longer need to travel back to your hometown to get an official certificate of financial hardship; a truthful personal statement of your finances is enough. In criminal cases, a lawyer is automatically assigned if the defendant has not hired one and is a minor, has a visual, hearing, or speech disability, or faces life imprisonment or the death penalty.
- Benefit: State Council regulations originally specified six categories of civil legal aid: claiming state compensation according to law; requesting social insurance or dibao (minimum subsistence allowance) benefits; requesting pensions or relief funds; claiming elder support, child support, or spousal maintenance; demanding payment of labor remuneration; and asserting civil rights and interests arising from Good Samaritan acts. The Legal Aid Law effective January 1, 2022 expanded this scope to nine categories. New additions include confirming labor relations; declaring a person to have no civil capacity or limited civil capacity; personal injury compensation arising from workplace injuries, traffic accidents, food and drug safety incidents, or medical malpractice; and compensation for environmental pollution and ecological damage. Migrant workers claiming wages or workplace injury compensation, as well as social assistance recipients, are exempt from financial hardship reviews. Good Samaritans asserting their rights are also exempt from financial hardship restrictions. In criminal cases, Article 25 of the Legal Aid Law lists specific groups—such as minors, persons with visual, hearing, or speech disabilities, adults unable to fully recognize their own conduct, and defendants who may face life imprisonment or the death penalty—where public security organs, procuratorates, and courts must notify a legal aid institution to assign a defense lawyer if none has been retained.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2003). 法律援助条例（国令第 385 号，第十条、第十一条、第十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6944.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2021). 法律援助法（第二十五、三十一、三十二、四十二条）. <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/202504/t20250402_4053713.html>；司法部有关负责人 (2021). 就贯彻实施法律援助法答记者问. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2021-12/29/content_5665114.htm>
- Notes: Under the Legal Aid Law effective since January 1, 2022, another key change took effect: previously, applicants had to "submit proof of financial hardship." Now, applicants only need to provide a truthful personal statement of their financial circumstances, eliminating the need to return to their registered hometown to obtain certification. Access points include local legal aid centers, public legal service centers, or calling the hotline 12348.

### 4. Turning to a shelter when you have nowhere else to go – they provide food, lodging, and even a ticket home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: There is no cost. You can go to your local civil affairs bureau or shelter on your own, or ask police or civil affairs officials to take you there. Upon arrival, you must truthfully provide your name and list any personal belongings. The hard part is simply admitting that you have no money and nowhere to stay.
- In plain terms: Shelters cover meals and lodging, send you to a hospital if you fall ill, help you contact family, and issue a ticket home if you cannot afford one. Stays generally do not exceed 10 days. Coming and leaving are voluntary — this is not detention, and no cash is handed out. Tickets are issued for the hukou place or the place of residence, so decide where you want to go before you arrive.
- Benefit: Shelters supply food and lodging that meet basic hygiene standards. Should you suffer a medical emergency while there, they arrange prompt hospital treatment. They also assist in reaching out to relatives or employers. For those unable to pay for travel back to their registered home or current residence, a free transport voucher – essentially a train or bus ticket – is provided. “Household registration” refers to your hometown, while “current residence” is wherever you currently live. Generally, stays are capped at 10 days; extensions are granted if family members are on the way, medical care is needed, or other arrangements must be made. Police and other government officials encountering homeless individuals in the course of their duties must inform them of this assistance option. Special care is given to disabled persons, minors, the elderly, and anyone with mobility issues – they are guided and escorted to shelters (nationwide, since 2003).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2003). 城市生活无着的流浪乞讨人员救助管理办法（国务院令第 381 号，第五、六、七条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62246.htm>；民政部 (2003). 城市生活无着的流浪乞讨人员救助管理办法实施细则（民政部令第 24 号，第十一、十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62510.htm>
- Notes: Entry and departure are entirely voluntary; this is not a detention facility. Basic food and lodging are provided, but no cash is given. Transport vouchers are issued based on household registration or current residence – be sure to decide where you wish to go before seeking help. For shelter arrangements at night or during winter, see Section 21 (when there truly is nowhere else to sleep).

### 5. Use free public employment services and gig platforms instead of paid agencies
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost at all. You can register at any public employment service agency at the county level or higher — such as employment service centers or human resources markets. Local gig platforms also allow free registration. If you don’t know the exact location, call 12333 for help.
- In plain terms: Public agencies like employment service centers and human resources markets provide job referrals, career guidance, policy advice, and both employment and unemployment registration — all completely free of charge. In fact, the law explicitly forbids them from charging for these services. Registration and posting job listings on gig platforms is likewise free. It’s a good idea to complete your unemployment registration at the same time, since it’s required to receive unemployment benefits and qualify for assistance as someone facing employment difficulties.
- Benefit: The law guarantees that the following services must be provided at no cost: advice on employment policies; information about current job openings and labor shortages; career guidance and job matching; processing of both employment and unemployment registration; and targeted employment support for individuals facing employment difficulties. These agencies are prohibited from engaging in any commercial activities or profiting off these services. Gig platform job postings and registrations are also offered to the public free of charge, as part of the broader public employment information system. Special support is further extended to long-term unemployed workers and low-income gig workers facing financial hardship (nationwide).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大常委会 (2007, 2015 修正). 就业促进法（第三十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647636.htm>；人力资源社会保障部等五部门 (2022). 关于加强零工市场建设 完善求职招聘服务的意见（人社部发〔2022〕38 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-07/09/content_5700177.htm>
- Notes: Unemployment registration is a prerequisite for receiving unemployment benefits and qualifying as someone facing employment difficulties — so it’s worth completing at the same time. For gig workers paid on a daily basis, always verify your earnings on the spot, and be sure to collect the employer’s contact details and a record of the work performed.

### 6. Apply for temporary assistance when emergencies strike
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Apply to the township government or subdistrict office where the emergency occurred, explaining what happened to your family—such as a fire, traffic accident, sudden critical illness, or a sudden surge in essential daily expenses. Sudden crises follow a "fast-track small-sum relief" route: no household financial checks, no public announcements, usually processed within 3 days. Sudden surges in medical or educational expenses are limited to dibao (subsistence allowance), low-income (dibao-edge), and rigid-expenditure hardship households, and require public announcement before approval. Prepare documents according to the local checklist; it typically takes 1 to 2 trips.
- In plain terms: If an unexpected emergency hits your home—like a fire, car crash, or sudden severe illness—and you temporarily cannot afford basic living expenses, you can apply for temporary assistance at the local township government or subdistrict office where the incident happened. You do not need to already be enrolled in dibao. These urgent claims are usually processed within 3 days, providing up to 1 month of the local dibao standard. For families already in dibao or near-dibao hardship facing sudden surges in healthcare or schooling costs, assistance can cover up to 6 months of the dibao standard.
- Benefit: Two categories of households can receive temporary assistance. The first consists of families who temporarily cannot afford basic living expenses such as food, shelter, and medical care due to accidents, sudden major illnesses, or similar crises. The second consists of dibao households whose non-discretionary expenses suddenly surge beyond their means. Eligible situations and payout amounts are determined and published by local governments at or above the county level (national system, local amounts, since 2014). Under the Social Assistance Law taking effect on July 1, 2026, eligible recipients are defined as "individuals whose basic living conditions face temporary severe hardship due to unexpected, urgent, or catastrophic events," receiving either cash assistance or goods in kind. Applications are submitted to the township government or subdistrict office where the crisis occurred. In urgent, small-sum cases, relief must be granted first, with paperwork completed afterward. Guidelines issued by the Ministry of Civil Affairs and Ministry of Finance in late 2025 specify implementation rules: sudden crises, accidental injuries, and critical illnesses follow a "fast-track small-sum relief" channel without household financial verification or public announcements, with processing usually capped at 3 days and payouts capped at 1 month of the local dibao standard. For households on the verge of dibao or facing rigid-expenditure hardships due to sudden increases in medical or schooling costs, assistance is granted after public announcement, in principle under a "single approval, phased disbursement" model. For temporary assistance outside fast-track small-sum relief, the highest tier cannot exceed 6 times the local monthly dibao standard (nationwide, starting 2026, specific standards set locally).

Where inconsistencies arise, the formal law prevails.

### 7. If income is below the local dibao line, apply for dibao; if income is a bit above the line or medical and schooling costs are high, apply for medical, education, housing, and employment assistance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money spent. Household members who live together submit a written application to the township government or subdistrict office of the hukou place. If that is hard to do yourself, you may ask the villagers’ committee or residents’ committee to apply on your behalf. You must accept checks of household income and assets. Usually 2 to 3 trips, with ID cards, hukou booklets, and proof of income and assets. The hard part is laying household finances open for inspection and waiting through public notice. If you do not qualify for dibao, applying for medical, housing, or employment assistance is also done at the hukou township government or subdistrict office. Education assistance is applied for at the school the child attends.
- In plain terms: If household per-person income is below the local dibao line and assets also meet the rules, you can apply. At the end of 2024, the national urban dibao line averaged 798.1 yuan per person per month, and the rural line 593.9 yuan. Money is paid as the gap, or in fixed tiers. If income is a bit above the dibao line, or medical and schooling costs are too high, you may still apply for medical, schooling, and housing assistance even without dibao.
- Benefit: Divide household income by the number of people; if that is below the local dibao standard and household assets are within the rules, you can receive dibao. At the end of 2024, the national average urban dibao standard was 798.1 yuan per person per month, and the rural standard 593.9 yuan per person per month. Urban dibao covered 6250000 people, rural 33615000. Dibao money is paid monthly as the gap: local dibao standard minus household per-person income. For example, if the local standard is 798.1 yuan and household income is 500 yuan per person per month, each person receives 298.1 yuan per month, not 798.1 yuan. There are two further tiers for those who do not qualify for dibao. The first is dibao-edge households: assets meet the rules, and household per-person income is below 1.5 times the local dibao standard (2023 national document). From July (7) 2026 the Social Assistance Law (社会救助法) leaves the uplift ratio above the dibao standard to the province. The second is rigid-expenditure hardship households. The condition is that household per-person income is below local prior-year per-person disposable income, and assets meet the rules. Unavoidable costs such as medical care and schooling must also exceed a local share of household income. Both tiers can receive medical assistance, education assistance, and housing assistance. Education assistance means fee reductions, grants and living subsidies, and student loans. Housing assistance in cities means priority public rental housing or rental subsidies. Unemployed people with work capacity in dibao-edge and rigid-expenditure hardship households can also receive employment assistance. ### 14. Stop just sending out resumes—use a systematic approach: learn skills, set goals, and ask for help

### 15. No deposits, no ID retention, no “training loans”, no pyramid schemes, no usury
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Zero expense. If an employer or agency suggests “pay first, then get a job”, “pay for training before placement”, or “recruit others to earn money”, refuse immediately. Keep all chat logs and proof of any payments. The difficulty lies in the fact that when money is scarce, people may be tempted to take risks — saying “no” on the spot is not easy.
- In plain terms: Employers cannot keep your ID or demand a deposit, nor can they charge you under any other pretext. If a job placement agency fails to find you a job, it must refund the placement fee. “Training loans” that require upfront fees, as well as pyramid schemes that pay out based on recruitment, are all officially recognized scams. Any interest on a loan exceeding four times the one-year LPR is not legally enforceable by courts, though the principal amount must still be repaid.
- Benefit: Employers are prohibited from withholding IDs or demanding guarantees or deposits. Violations result in mandatory refunds and fines ranging from 500 to 2,000 RMB per worker. Job placement agencies likewise cannot retain IDs or collect deposits; if they fail to place you, they must return all fees. Schemes that demand training fees or loans under the guise of employment are flagged by multiple government agencies as “training loan” scams. Pyramid schemes, where earnings depend on recruiting others, are illegal; participants face fines up to 2,000 RMB. ### 23. Call the bank or platform yourself to apply for personalized installments or extensions when you cannot repay credit cards or online loans; do not borrow new debt to pay old debt or pay agencies for "suspending interest and freezing balances," "credit repair," or "anti-collection"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Call the official customer service number on the back of your card or in the app, stating clearly that you cannot repay right now but are willing to do so. Prepare proof of income, unemployment, or illness; this often takes multiple phone calls. Once an agreement is reached, repay monthly according to the new plan; you cannot apply for new credit cards until the balance is fully paid off. The difficult part is proactively answering the bank's calls and explaining your difficulties over and over again.
- In plain terms: If you cannot repay your credit card balance but are willing to pay, you can negotiate a personalized installment plan with the bank for up to 5 years; once an agreement is signed, the bank must stop collection calls. However, the bank has the right to refuse, and existing overdue records will still remain on your credit report. Never use paid agencies that promise to "freeze interest" or "clean your credit record"—regulatory authorities have specifically issued warnings against them.
- Benefit: Credit cards are covered by clear regulations. If your outstanding balance exceeds your repayment ability and you are still willing to repay, the bank "may" negotiate with you on an equal footing to sign a personalized installment repayment agreement. The agreement can last for up to 5 years. It must clearly state the total amount owed, the amount per installment, and whether annual fees and interest are charged during the repayment period. Once both parties sign the agreement, the bank and its entrusted debt collection agencies must stop collection efforts. If you fail to repay according to the agreement, this protection no longer applies. If a verbal agreement is reached, the bank must keep the audio recording at least until the debt is fully paid off. Debts that have already been placed on an installment plan cannot be split into installments again; personalized installments are the sole exception (2022 regulatory notice). When the People's Bank of China repealed old bank card measures in 2025, it specified that credit cards continue to be governed by these rules. Online loans do not have unified rules like personalized installments; whether you can extend or reschedule installments depends on your contract and the lending institution. If the lender is a consumer finance company, regulations prohibit it from using violence, threats, intimidation, or harassment for debt collection. Neither banks nor consumer finance companies are permitted to collect debts from uninvolved third parties. Regarding fee-charging intermediaries, five government departments including the National Financial Regulatory Administration jointly issued a risk alert in 2026. The alert stated that claims such as "loans/credit cards do not need to be repaid," "debt negotiation," "debt restructuring," and "credit report whitewashing" are false information. Such "agent rights defense" operations charge high service fees, and if you want to back out midway, you may be sued for breach of contract. They collect your bank cards, SIM cards, home address, and your children's schools. Following their instructions to submit fabricated documents, deliberately evade debt, or make malicious complaints can constitute illegal or criminal behavior in serious cases. In 2022, the Beijing Banking and Insurance Regulatory Bureau also singled out advertisements for "interest suspension and balance freezing," "stopping debt collection," and "full interest waivers." It warned that trusting these operators, delaying repayment, and avoiding contact with banks is very likely to leave overdue negative marks on your credit report. In 2022, the National Development and Reform Commission launched a special campaign targeting "credit repair." The notice stated that these entities deliberately confuse credit disputes with credit restoration, running scams under the guise of "credit repair, whitewashing, or deleting records." Negative information on credit reports is retained for 5 years starting from the date the negative behavior or event ends, and is deleted after 5 years. During the retention period, you may add an explanatory statement to the record, which credit reporting agencies must record (National).
