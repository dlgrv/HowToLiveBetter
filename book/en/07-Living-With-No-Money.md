# 7. How to Live When You Have No Money

This chapter covers money and coverage: how much you can claim, how much can be waived, what services you can get, and what the conditions are — it does not convert anything into lifespan. Policies change year by year and differ by locality, so all amounts and thresholds follow what is published locally for the current year. Items are ordered by "costs nothing, least paperwork, fastest effect" first; items requiring long-term eligibility or with large regional differences come later, and experience-based entries come last.
### 1. If You Lose Your Job, First Claim Unemployment Insurance Benefits Online
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; submit via the "Zhangshang 12333" app, the electronic social security card mini-program (WeChat/Alipay), or the National Social Insurance Public Service Platform, using just your social security card or ID card — in most areas no in-person visit is needed; the prerequisite is that unemployment registration has been completed
- In plain terms: If your employer and you paid in for a full year and you did not quit voluntarily, once unemployment registration is done you can claim: a full 1 year but less than 5 years of contributions gets at most 12 months, 5 to 10 years at most 18 months, over 10 years at most 24 months. Each province sets its own amount — lower than the local minimum wage, higher than dibao (低保 — means-tested subsistence allowance). While you collect, employee medical insurance is paid for you by the unemployment insurance fund. Quitting voluntarily does not count.
- Benefit: Conditions are cumulative unemployment insurance contributions of at least 1 year by employer and employee, interruption of employment not of one's own will, completed unemployment registration, and a willingness to seek work; at least 1 year but under 5 years of contributions gets at most 12 months, at least 5 but under 10 years at most 18 months, over 10 years at most 24 months; the amount is set by provincial governments, below the local minimum wage and above urban dibao, with the national goal of gradually raising it to 90% of the minimum wage standard (nationwide, regulation in force since 1999)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (1999). 失业保险条例（国务院令第 258 号，第十四、十七、十八条）. <https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=517>；人力资源社会保障部. 全国人社政务服务平台·失业保险待遇申领办事指南. <https://www.12333.gov.cn/portal/common/bszn/sydysl?pfaId=202105281700000004>；国家发展改革委就业司 (2022). 失业保险金标准，将逐步提高至最低工资的 90%. <https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/fggz/jyysr/jysrsbxf/202206/t20220627_1328819.html>
- Notes: Voluntary resignation does not count as "not of one's own will"; during the benefit period the unemployment insurance fund pays employee medical insurance on your behalf. Amounts vary widely by province — go by what is published where you are insured. The "90% of minimum wage" figure comes from a 2017 guidance opinion of the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security; neither the MOHRSS original page nor the government website's could be opened, document number pending verification.

### 2. For Unpaid Wages, Complain to Labor Inspection First, Then Apply for Labor Arbitration — Both Routes Are Free, and Most Cases Get a Result Within a Few Months
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; neither the complaint nor arbitration costs anything; have your labor contract, pay slips, attendance records, transfer records, and chat logs ready, and make 1 to 3 trips to the human resources and social security bureau or the arbitration commission; the statute of limitations is 1 year — wage arrears during employment are not subject to the 1-year limit, but you must file within 1 year of leaving the job
- In plain terms: Neither route costs money. First call 12333 or go to the labor inspection office of the human resources and social security bureau to complain; after a case is filed they must finish checking within 60 working days and can order payment within a set deadline, with an extra 50% to 100% in compensation if the deadline is missed — in 2024 this route recovered 8.65 billion yuan nationwide for 654,000 people. If that fails, apply for labor arbitration: a decision within 45 days, and awards up to 12 times the local monthly minimum wage are final in one ruling — the company cannot sue. But distinguish "case closed" from "money in your account": if the company has no assets or the boss has run off, you can win and still not collect. The deadline is 1 year — you must file within a year of leaving.
- Benefit: Step one: call 12333 or complain to the local human resources and social security bureau's labor inspection office; by law the check must be completed within 60 working days of filing (complex cases may be extended 30 working days), payment can be ordered by a set deadline with 50% to 100% of the amount due added as compensation if the deadline passes, and in 2024 inspection bodies nationwide handled 137,000 cases and recovered 8.65 billion yuan in wages for 654,000 people; where there is an IOU or wage table and the arrears are clear, you can apply directly to a basic-level court for a payment order, issued within 15 days of acceptance; step two: apply for arbitration, with a legally required decision within 45 days of acceptance (extendable by 15 days), advance enforcement available for wage claims, and awards up to 12 times the local monthly minimum wage final in one ruling with no right of the company to sue — in 2024, 4.257 million cases were handled nationwide, 4.156 million concluded, with 93.47 billion yuan in concluded amounts, a mediation success rate of 79.6%, an arbitration closure rate of 98.2%, and an arbitration finality rate of 73.7%; the "six months to a year" comes from the subset of cases that go from arbitration to court: first instance 6 months (simplified procedure 3 months) plus second instance 3 months — in 2024 courts concluded 88,000 wage-recovery cases recovering 28.54 billion yuan, and 998 people were sentenced for the crime of refusing to pay wages (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 人力资源社会保障部 (2025). 2024 年度人力资源和社会保障事业发展统计公报. <https://www.mohrss.gov.cn/SYrlzyhshbzb/zwgk/szrs/tjgb/202506/W020250616518526345602.pdf>；最高人民法院 (2025). 最高人民法院工作报告. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202503/content_7013680.htm>；国务院 (2004). 劳动保障监察条例（国务院令第 423 号，第九、十七、二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2004/content_63042.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2007). 劳动争议调解仲裁法（主席令第八十号，第二十七、四十三、四十四、四十七、五十、五十三条）. <https://chinajob.mohrss.gov.cn/h5/c/2022-07-15/356212.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2012 修正). 劳动合同法（第三十、八十五条）. <https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/c5193025/content.html>；全国人大常委会 (2023 修正). 民事诉讼法（第一百零九、一百五十二、一百六十四、一百八十三、二百二十五、二百二十七条）. <https://amr.guizhou.gov.cn/zwgk/xxgkml/jcxxgk/zcfg/fl/202401/t20240129_83645867.html>；全国人大常委会 (2011). 刑法修正案（八）第四十一条，即刑法第二百七十六条之一. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/cwhhdbdh/c6626/c14002/c14003/201905/t20190523_390477.html>；最高人民法院 (2013). 关于审理拒不支付劳动报酬刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2013〕3 号，第三条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/5041.html>；国务院 (2019). 保障农民工工资支付条例（国务院令第 724 号，第十、二十六、三十、三十一、三十二、四十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5469641.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2021). 法律援助法（第三十一、四十二条）. <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/202504/t20250402_4053713.html>
- Notes: Useful, but distinguish "case closed" from "money in your account": the 98.2% closure rate means the procedure is finished — if the company has no assets or the boss has run off, you can win the award and still fail to enforce it; the Supreme People's Court publishes only total enforced amounts (2.3 trillion yuan in 2024) and a claim that "the completion rate of enforcement remains high" — the actual collection rate for wage arrears cases is not published, so this link has no official data. The arbitration finality rate is not defined in the communiqué; this entry reads it as "no further court proceedings after the award". Owing one person 3 months or more of wages of 5,000 to 20,000 yuan or more, or owing 10 or more people a cumulative 30,000 to 100,000 yuan or more, and still not paying after being ordered to, meets the criminal filing threshold — raise it directly in your complaint. Rural construction workers have separate wage-specific accounts and general contractor first liability for payment; going after the general contractor works better than going after the crew boss. For migrant workers applying for legal aid for wage claims, means testing is waived. The Labor Contract Law and Civil Procedure Law texts are quoted from reposting pages of the State Taxation Administration and the Guizhou Provincial Market Regulation Bureau (both gov.cn); the NPC website page could not be opened; for Criminal Law Amendment (VIII) the NPC website WebFetch handshake failed, so curl was used to open and verify it.

### 3. If You Cannot Afford a Lawsuit, Apply for Legal Aid — Wage Recovery, Alimony, and Workplace Injury Cases Are Already Within Scope
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; legal aid charges the aid recipient nothing
- In plain terms: Recovering wages, claiming alimony or child support, claiming social insurance or dibao benefits, or compensation for injuries from a brave act — these are all already inside the free legal aid scope. Call 12348 or go to your local legal aid center. Since 2022 you no longer need to go back to your home region for a certificate of financial hardship; stating your situation truthfully yourself is enough. For defendants facing a possible death sentence, and for blind, deaf, or mute defendants and minors, a lawyer is appointed regardless of financial status.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation lists the civil aid scope as "claims for state compensation under law; claims for social insurance benefits or minimum subsistence allowance benefits; claims for pension payments and relief funds; claims for alimony, child support, or support payments; claims for payment of labor remuneration; and assertion of civil rights arising from brave-and-courageous acts"; in criminal cases, for those facing a possible death sentence, and for blind, deaf, or mute defendants and minors, a lawyer is appointed regardless of economic status
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2003). 法律援助条例（国令第 385 号，第十条、第十一条、第十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6944.htm>；司法部有关负责人 (2021). 就贯彻实施法律援助法答记者问. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2021-12/29/content_5665114.htm>
- Notes: The Legal Aid Law, in force from January 1, 2022, changed "submit a certificate of financial hardship" to a truthful self-statement of financial status — no more going back to your home region for paperwork. Entry points are local legal aid centers, public legal service centers, or the 12348 hotline.

### 4. When You Are Out of Options, Go to a Relief Station — Food, Lodging, and a Ticket Home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; go to the local relief and management station to ask for help (or tell a police officer or the civil affairs department), and register your name and belongings truthfully
- In plain terms: They provide food, a bed, hospital transport if you fall ill, help contacting family, and a ticket home if you have no money to travel. Generally no more than 10 days; you come voluntarily and leave voluntarily — it is not detention, and no cash is given. The ticket is issued to your household registration place or place of residence, so decide where you want to return to before you go.
- Benefit: Relief stations provide food meeting hygiene requirements, lodging, hospital transfer for sudden acute illness within the station, and help contacting relatives and employers; those without travel money to return to their household registration place or place of residence are issued travel (boat) vouchers; the relief period is generally no more than 10 days, extendable for those awaiting pickup, treatment, or placement; police and other administrative personnel who find vagrants and beggars in the course of duty must tell them how to seek help, and must guide and escort persons with disabilities, minors, the elderly, and those with limited mobility (nationwide, since 2003)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2003). 城市生活无着的流浪乞讨人员救助管理办法（国务院令第 381 号，第五、六、七条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62246.htm>；民政部 (2003). 城市生活无着的流浪乞讨人员救助管理办法实施细则（民政部令第 24 号，第十一、十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62510.htm>
- Notes: Help is voluntary and leaving is voluntary — this is not internment. What is provided is basic food and lodging, not cash. The travel voucher is issued to your household registration place or place of residence, so think through where you want to go before asking for help.

### 5. For Acute and Critical Emergencies, Treatment Comes Before Payment — Call 120 Even If You Have No Money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan for the call; treatment costs after the emergency response are separate
- In plain terms: Emergency centers and hospitals may not refuse, pass off, or delay treatment of acute, severe, or critical injuries over payment; violators are ordered to correct and responsible persons disciplined. Ambulance costs for unidentified persons or those truly unable to pay are covered by the disease emergency relief fund. So call 120 even with no money. The fund only covers the emergency stretch — subsequent inpatient treatment costs are separate.
- Benefit: Emergency centers (stations) and emergency network hospitals may not refuse or delay pre-hospital emergency care over payment; violators are ordered to correct and responsible persons are disciplined; medical institutions of all levels and types may not refuse, pass off, or delay treatment of acute, severe, or critical patients on any grounds; emergency costs for unidentified persons or those unable to pay are covered by the disease emergency relief fund (nationwide, since 2013 and 2014)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2013). 关于建立疾病应急救助制度的指导意见（国办发〔2013〕15 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2013-03/01/content_6069.htm>；国家卫生计生委 (2013). 院前医疗急救管理办法（国家卫生计生委令第 3 号，第二十五、三十七条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2580977.htm>
- Notes: The fund covers only the emergency stage; later hospitalization and treatment costs are still self-paid or go through medical insurance or medical assistance (see item 8). Do not delay an emergency out of fear of the bill.

### 6. Use Free Public Employment Services and Gig-Work Markets to Find Work, Not Fee-Charging Agencies
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; register at a county-level or above public employment service agency (employment service center, human resources market) or a local gig-work market, or call 12333 to ask for addresses
- In plain terms: Public employment service agencies (employment service centers, human resources markets) provide job referrals, career guidance, policy consultation, and employment/unemployment registration free of charge by law, and the law explicitly bars them from operating for profit; registration and posting of gig-work information is free too. While you are there, get your unemployment registration done — claiming unemployment benefits and getting certified as a person with employment difficulties both require it.
- Benefit: Free provision by law of employment policy consultation, job supply and demand information, career guidance and job referrals, employment and unemployment registration, and employment assistance for persons with employment difficulties; agencies may not engage in for-profit activities; registration and publishing of gig-work job-seeking and recruiting information is provided free to the public and falls within public employment information services, with strengthened support for gig workers in difficulty such as long waiting times and low-income households (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007, 2015 修正). 就业促进法（第三十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647636.htm>；人力资源社会保障部等五部门 (2022). 关于加强零工市场建设 完善求职招聘服务的意见（人社部发〔2022〕38 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-07/09/content_5700177.htm>
- Notes: Unemployment registration is the prerequisite procedure for claiming unemployment insurance benefits and being certified as a person with employment difficulties — get it done while you are there. For gig jobs paid daily, verify the pay on the spot and keep contact details and records.

### 7. After a Sudden Setback, Apply for Temporary Assistance First
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; apply to the township government or sub-district office, describe the setback (fire, traffic accident, a family member's sudden major illness, a sudden spike in essential living expenses); after review and public notice, approval is by the county-level civil affairs department; documents follow the local checklist, usually 1 to 2 trips
- In plain terms: If something suddenly happens to your household (fire, car accident, a family member's sudden major illness) causing temporary severe difficulty in basic living, you can apply to the township government or sub-district office for temporary assistance — you do not have to already be a dibao recipient. It is mostly paid as a one-time grant, and amounts vary a lot by locality.
- Benefit: Households facing temporary severe difficulty in basic living due to accidents, a family member's sudden major illness, and the like, and dibao households whose essential living expenses suddenly rise beyond what they can bear, can receive temporary assistance; specific items and standards are determined and published by local governments at the county level or above (national system, amounts set locally, since 2014)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 国务院 (2014, 2019 修订). 社会救助暂行办法（国务院令第 649 号，第四十七、四十八、四十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2019/content_5468952.htm>
- Notes: You do not need to already be a dibao recipient; it is mostly paid as a one-time grant, amounts vary widely by locality, and current-year local publication is authoritative.

### 8. If Household Income Is Below the Local Dibao Line, Apply for Dibao
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; members of the household living together submit a written application to the township government or sub-district office of the household registration place; if applying is difficult, the village or residents' committee can be entrusted to do it; you must accept household income and asset checks, usually 2 to 3 trips, and need an ID card, household register, and proof of income and assets
- In plain terms: Households whose per-capita income is below the local dibao line and whose assets meet the rules can apply. At the end of 2024 the national average was 798.1 yuan per person per month for urban dibao and 593.9 yuan for rural, covering 6.25 million and 33.615 million people respectively. You apply where your household is registered; owning a house, a car, savings, or having children able to support you may disqualify you. Dibao status is also linked to subsidized medical insurance enrollment, medical assistance, and means-test-free legal aid.
- Benefit: Households with per-capita income below the local dibao standard and assets meeting the rules can receive the guarantee; at the end of 2024 the national average urban dibao standard was 798.1 yuan/person·month and rural 593.9 yuan/person·month, covering 6.250 million and 33.615 million people respectively; standards are set as the previous year's local per-capita consumer spending of residents multiplied by a quantified ratio and adjusted as needed (national averages, varying by locality)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2014, 2019 修订). 社会救助暂行办法（国务院令第 649 号，第九、十、十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2019/content_5468952.htm>；国家统计局 (2025). 社会事业向好发展 民生福祉不断增进——「十四五」以来社会民生统计报告. <https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202509/content_7042627.htm>；民政部等四部门 (2024). 关于进一步做好最低生活保障标准确定调整工作的指导意见（民发〔2024〕16 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202403/content_7007237.htm>
- Notes: Dibao is the safety net of last resort; you apply at your household registration place, and household assets (housing, car, savings) above the limit or children with the ability to support you may disqualify you; dibao status also links to subsidized medical insurance enrollment, medical assistance, means-test-free legal aid, and more (see items 2 and 8).

### 9. Holders of a Disability Certificate Should Apply for the Two Disability Subsidies
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; take the certificate to the sub-district or township office and submit one application
- In plain terms: Disabled people in dibao households can receive the living subsidy; those graded level 1 or 2 who need long-term care can receive the nursing subsidy; both can be collected together. Provinces set their own standards, ranging from a few dozen to a few hundred yuan a month. Bring your second-generation disability certificate and it takes one visit to the sub-district or township office.
- Benefit: Two systems established by the State Council: the living subsidy for disabled persons in difficulty is "targeted at disabled persons in dibao households, with localities in a position to do so gradually expanding to low-income disabled persons and other disabled persons in difficulty"; the nursing subsidy for severely disabled persons is "targeted at severely disabled persons graded level 1 or level 2 who need long-term care". The two can be received together
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2015). 关于全面建立困难残疾人生活补贴和重度残疾人护理补贴制度的意见（国发〔2015〕52 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2015-09/25/content_10181.htm>
- Notes: Standards are set by provincial governments and vary greatly by locality, from a few dozen to a few hundred yuan a month. Applications require the second-generation disability certificate; if the person cannot go in person, a family member can be entrusted with the application.

### 10. Do Not Let the 400-Yuan-a-Year Urban-Rural Resident Medical Insurance Lapse; Those in Difficulty Can Get It Waived or Reduced
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Individual contribution of at least 400 yuan per person per year (national standard for 2024 and 2025; localities may exceed it), paid during the centralized enrollment period; persons in extreme difficulty are fully subsidized, dibao recipients get a fixed-amount subsidy
- In plain terms: Individuals pay from 400 yuan a year, with the government adding roughly 700 yuan per head; persons in extreme difficulty are fully subsidized and dibao recipients get a fixed-amount subsidy. The cost of lapsing is real: those who do not enroll during the centralized period or who have not enrolled continuously face a 3-month fixed waiting period after enrolling, with in principle 1 more month added for each additional year lapsed.
- Benefit: Government subsidy of at least 670 yuan per person per year (2024), 700 yuan (2025 — the national standard as relayed by a Hebei provincial document), with the 2026 government work report raising it another 24 yuan; for dibao recipients and persons in extreme difficulty, eligible medical expenses after medical insurance reimbursement can receive medical assistance at a rate of at least 70%. Cost of lapsing: those who did not enroll during the centralized period or did not enroll continuously have a fixed benefit waiting period of 3 months after enrollment, with in principle 1 month added for each additional year lapsed (from 2025, nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家医保局 (2024). 《关于做好 2024 年城乡居民基本医疗保障有关工作的通知》政策解读（医保发〔2024〕19 号）. <https://www.nhsa.gov.cn/art/2024/8/26/art_105_13634.html>；河北省医疗保障局等 (2025). 关于做好 2025 年城乡居民基本医疗保障有关工作的通知（冀医保发〔2025〕6 号，地方文件）. <https://www.renqiu.gov.cn/renqiu/ybjbmwj/202510/6d90754638a248cba655e94ea518a3bb.shtml>；国务院办公厅 (2021). 关于健全重特大疾病医疗保险和救助制度的意见（国办发〔2021〕42 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5659514.htm>；国务院办公厅 (2024). 关于健全基本医疗保险参保长效机制的指导意见（国办发〔2024〕38 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/202408/content_6965741.htm>；国家医保局 (2026). 2026 年政府工作报告提到这些医保工作. <https://www.nhsa.gov.cn/art/2026/3/5/art_14_19809.html>
- Notes: The original page of the National Healthcare Security Administration's 2025 notice (Yibao Fa 〔2025〕 No. 22) could not be opened; the 700 yuan figure is taken from a local document's relay and awaits verification. While receiving unemployment insurance benefits, employee medical insurance is paid on your behalf by the unemployment insurance fund, so do not enroll twice.

### 11. With a Serious Illness, Go Through Medical Insurance, Catastrophic Illness Insurance, Medical Assistance, and Cross-Region Filing First — Never Touch Online Microloans
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; the order is: before a cross-province hospitalization, file through the "National Cross-Region Medical Treatment Filing" mini-program inside the "National Healthcare Security Administration" WeChat official account → settle directly at discharge → if out-of-pocket costs after reimbursement are still too heavy, apply to the township government or sub-district office for medical assistance → if employed, take sick leave under the medical treatment period, and for workplace injuries go through work injury insurance → if you cannot pay your credit card, proactively negotiate installment with the bank → only if that is still not enough, turn to the individual assistance platforms designated by the Ministry of Civil Affairs; documents are mainly the diagnosis certificate, expense itemization, and settlement statements, 2 to 4 trips
- In plain terms: The order is fixed: before a cross-province hospitalization, file through the "National Healthcare Security Administration" official account, then settle directly at discharge without fronting the full amount; if out-of-pocket costs after reimbursement are still too heavy, apply to the township or sub-district for medical assistance (dibao recipients and persons in extreme difficulty get assistance at no less than 70%); if employed, you get a medical treatment period of 3 to 24 months by seniority, during which your contract cannot be terminated and sick-leave pay cannot be below 80% of the local minimum wage; for work injuries there is the suspension-of-work-with-pay period and your original wage keeps being paid; if you cannot pay your credit card, proactively negotiate with the bank — up to 5 years of installments is possible, and once the agreement is signed the bank must stop collections. Do not touch online microloans or high-interest private loans: courts do not support interest above 4 times the one-year LPR, but the principal is still owed, and from a sickbed you have no energy to fight that kind of lawsuit.
