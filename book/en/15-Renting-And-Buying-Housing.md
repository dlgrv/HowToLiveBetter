# 15. Renting and buying a home

Scope: money. When renting, the places money is most easily lost are the deposit and the agent. When buying, the expensive parts are the interest on the loan, and the things the seller knows that you do not. The items near the end of the section are about buying: which account the money should go into, when the earnest money and the purchase price can be asked back, and what to do if handover is late. Also how to stop a seller selling the same home twice, and how to talk the mortgage rate down. The last two items are about after you move in: the property manager must not cut water or power to collect fees, and for a new home that leaks, go to the developer for repairs within the warranty period.
### 1. Put the deposit amount, refund timeline, and deduction conditions into the lease
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Ten extra minutes when signing. The hard part is bringing it up to the landlord's face.
- In plain terms: Before signing, put three things in the lease: the deposit amount, when it will be returned, and what deductions are allowed. If a situation is not written into the contract, the landlord cannot withhold your deposit on move-out without a valid reason.
- Benefit: Administrative regulations are explicit: "Where a lessor collects a deposit, the amount of the deposit, the return timeline, and circumstances for deductions shall be agreed upon in the housing lease contract. Except for circumstances stipulated in the contract, the lessor may not deduct the deposit without legitimate reasons." Three things must be in the lease: the deposit amount, when it gets returned, and when deductions apply. For situations not listed in the contract, the landlord cannot keep your money without a valid reason.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: Take photos and videos together with the landlord on move-out day. Record the readings on the water, electricity, and gas meters, and photograph the walls and floors. If your deposit is withheld without cause, first file a complaint with the housing rental authority. For modest sums, consider small claims litigation; see Section 8, Item 22 (calculating whether suing is worth it). For whether a leased commercial space can operate or qualify for food and beverage permits, see Section 12, Item 27 (leasing commercial space to open a shop).

### 2. If your landlord cuts utilities, changes locks, or threatens you to force you out, call the police first to create a record: regulations prohibit using these tactics to evict you
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. It only takes a phone call to the police and can be done on the spot.
- In plain terms: Landlords cannot cut off your water or electricity, change the locks, or show up with threats to force you to break your lease or move out—regulations strictly forbid it. If this happens, call the police right away to get an official incident record on file. Do not physically confront them yourself.
- Benefit: Administrative regulations state clearly: "Lessors shall not use violence, threats, or other illegal means to force lessees to terminate residential leasing contracts or vacate leased housing." "Vacate" simply means making you move out. If this happens, call the police first to preserve evidence. Do not physically confront the landlord yourself.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: Calling the police creates an official police response record, which serves as evidence when claiming damages later. At the same time, notify the housing rental authority. If the landlord refuses to pay compensation, file a lawsuit in court. Under standard civil procedures of first instance, cases are generally concluded within 6 months by rule (subject to extension), and you cover your own attorney fees. For small dispute amounts, use small-claims procedures, which conclude within 2 months; see Section 8, Item 22 (calculating whether a lawsuit is worth the cost).

### 3. Agents must not collect or pay rent and deposits; payments go directly to landlords
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost at all. It takes just a few minutes to verify the recipient before paying. The hard part is resisting any pressure from agents to send the money to them instead.
- In plain terms: By rule, rent and deposits must not be collected or paid through an agent — pay the landlord directly. Every time the money passes through the agent, there is one more chance for them to take it and disappear. Before you pay, check that the payee’s name matches the name on the title certificate.
- Benefit: Administrative rules explicitly forbid real estate agencies from collecting or paying rent and deposits on behalf of landlords. This means agents have no legal authority to handle such payments. Keeping money out of their hands eliminates one major risk factor for fraud and financial loss.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: Before paying, double-check that the recipient’s name matches the one on the property title. If it does not, ask for a written authorization and keep a copy for your records. Agents are only permitted to charge a commission fee for their intermediary services.

### 4. Check for an escrow account before renting a managed apartment, and never pay a full year upfront for a discount
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Takes about 10 to 15 minutes to check.
- In plain terms: Paying a full year upfront saves very little money. If the rental company goes bust or runs off, both your deposit and your unused prepaid rent vanish with it. Signing up for a rent installment loan is even worse: the rental firm disappears, but you are still stuck paying off the loan every month. Before signing, always check whether the company has publicly listed an official rental escrow account.
- Benefit: Administrative regulations require subleasing housing rental enterprises to establish a designated housing rental fund escrow account and disclose it to the public. Subleasing enterprises operate by leasing properties in bulk from landlords and then subletting them to tenants. The small discount gained by paying a full year upfront does not balance out the risk of losing both the deposit and the remaining prepaid rent if the enterprise collapses. Tenants who also sign a rent-installment loan take on even greater risk.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: Check carefully whether a financing loan is bundled into the contract. If a financing app pays a full year of rent to the rental agency upfront while you repay the app monthly, you still have to keep paying the loan even if the agency goes bust. Never sign this type of contract.

### 5. If the Rental Property Is Sold During the Lease, the Lease Remains Valid and You Do Not Have to Move
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free
- In plain terms: If your rental home is sold during your lease, your tenancy agreement remains fully valid. The new landlord must honor the original contract and let you live there until the term expires. You do not have to move out, nor do you need to renegotiate the rent. The prerequisite is that you already lawfully occupy the property; keep your lease agreement, bank transfer records, and proof of your move-in date handy.
- Benefit: The Civil Code recognizes the rule of "sale does not break tenancy." The legal text states: where any change occurs to the ownership of the leased property during the period in which the lessee possesses it in accordance with the lease contract, the validity of the lease contract is not affected. If the property changes hands during the lease term, the existing signed contract remains valid. The new owner must allow you to stay until the lease term ends under the original contract terms.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第七百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: The prerequisite is that you have already lawfully moved in and occupied the premises as stipulated in the contract. Keep your lease, payment transfer records, and evidence of your move-in date safe. There are two exceptions: if the property was already mortgaged before being rented out and is later auctioned off to settle debts, or if it was seized by a court before the lease. See Item 6 (checking property ownership certificates and mortgage status).

### 6. Check property ownership and mortgages before renting, and pay by bank transfer with a memo
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Bring your ID card to the local real estate registry; a search takes about half an hour. The hard part is getting the landlord to show the title deed in person.
- In plain terms: Before signing, have the landlord show their ID and title deed. Then take your ID to the real estate registry to check whether the property is mortgaged or seized by a court—you can check this even before signing a lease. If a property was already mortgaged or seized before it was rented out and later gets auctioned off to a new owner, you cannot demand to keep living there. Pay everything by bank transfer with a clear memo to leave a paper trail.
- Benefit: Before signing, the landlord is required to present their identity documents and real estate title certificate. If there is no title deed, they must provide other documents proving their legal right to lease the property. The landlord must also cooperate with you in checking and verifying the property's information; this is a statutory obligation placed on lessors under administrative regulations. Even before signing a lease, you can bring your ID to the real estate registry in the property's city or county to check several things: the property's basic status, whether there are co-owners, whether any mortgage or right of habitation is registered, and whether it has been seized by a court. After signing, you can inspect the registry records using the rental contract. This step is crucial because the rule of "tenancy continues despite ownership transfer" has two exceptions: if the home was mortgaged to a bank before being rented out and is later auctioned off to satisfy debts, you cannot demand that the new owner let you stay under the original lease. The same applies if the property was seized by a court before leasing. When a court auctions the property, if the lease impedes a pre-existing mortgage, the court will extinguish the lease before auctioning. Conversely, if the home was leased to you and you moved in before the landlord mortgaged it, the mortgage will not affect your lease. If the property is seized or ownership disputes prevent you from living there, you can terminate the contract. If a third party claims rights and prevents you from living there, you can request a reduction in or exemption from rent. Paying all funds by bank transfer with a memo like "Rent for [Address] for [Month]" is author experience: if a dispute arises, it proves both that you paid and what you paid for.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第九条第一项：「向承租人出示身份证明材料、拟出租住房的不动产权属证书或者其他证明其具有合法出租权利的材料，并配合承租人依法查询、核实拟出租住房有关信息」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>；自然资源部 (2024 修正). 不动产登记资料查询暂行办法（第八、十九、二十、二十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11506/202408/content_6966482.html>；自然资源部 (2025). 关于修改《不动产登记资料查询暂行办法》的决定（自然资源部令第 18 号，第二十一条第三项改为「不动产是否存在抵押权登记、预告登记、异议登记或者居住权登记等情形」）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12546/202602/content_7057456.html>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第四百零五、七百二十三、七百二十四、七百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于修改《最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用〈中华人民共和国工会法〉若干问题的解释》等二十七件民事类司法解释的决定（法释〔2020〕17 号，第二十一项第 6 点修改城镇房屋租赁合同解释原第二十条，条文顺序调整后为第十四条：「（一）房屋在出租前已设立抵押权，因抵押权人实现抵押权发生所有权变动的；（二）房屋在出租前已被人民法院依法查封的」）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282621.html>；最高人民法院 (2004). 关于人民法院民事执行中拍卖、变卖财产的规定（法释〔2004〕16 号，第三十一条第二款；法释〔2020〕21 号删去第五至七条后为第二十八条第二款）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/shenpan/xiangqing/713.html>；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于修改《最高人民法院关于人民法院扣押铁路运输货物若干问题的规定》等十八件执行类司法解释的决定（法释〔2020〕21 号）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282661.html>；证据留存与转账备注同理见第 8 节关于彩礼和借条的两条
- Notes: The items you can check before signing do not include the owner's name. That is why you must verify that the name on the title deed matches the landlord's ID in person. Require the landlord to be present in person when signing; if they cannot attend, demand a power of attorney. If a sub-landlord sublets to you, obtain written consent from the original landlord. If the landlord refuses to honor rights like lease termination or rent reduction, you must file a lawsuit in court. Under standard first-instance civil procedure, cases are generally concluded within 6 months by rule (which can be extended), and legal fees are usually borne by yourself. Adding a memo to transfers is not explicitly required by regulations, but is author experience. You can also run these checks before signing to buy a pre-owned home; after signing, you should also file for advance notice registration—see Item 14 (Advance notice registration).

### 7. If an agent is to hold the price on a second-hand home, the money must go through the dedicated transaction-funds deposit account the agency opened at a bank
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. The hard part is insisting on the dedicated account; you may have to argue with the agent a few more times.
- In plain terms: When an agent collects the purchase price, the rules say it must go through the dedicated account the agency opened at a bank. That account is supervised; the money must not go into the agency’s own account, and must not be transferred to an individual broker.
- Benefit: The regulation is plain: where the parties agree that a real-estate brokerage will collect and pay the transaction funds, the funds shall be transferred through the dedicated client transaction-settlement deposit account that the brokerage has opened at a bank. The purchase price goes into a supervised dedicated account, not the agency’s own account, and not some broker’s private account.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:住房城乡建设部、国家发展改革委、人力资源社会保障部 (2011). 房地产经纪管理办法（令第 8 号，2016 年第 29 号令修改）第二十四条：「房地产交易当事人约定由房地产经纪机构代收代付交易资金的，应当通过房地产经纪机构在银行开设的客户交易结算资金专用存款账户划转交易资金。」第十八条「房地产经纪服务实行明码标价制度……在经营场所醒目位置标明房地产经纪服务项目、服务内容、收费标准」；第十九条「两家或者两家以上房地产经纪机构合作开展同一宗房地产经纪业务的，只能按照一宗业务收取佣金，不得向委托人增加收费」；第十七条代办贷款、代办房地产登记等其他服务「应当向委托人说明服务内容、收费标准等情况，经委托人同意后，另行签订合同」. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1918920.htm>
- Notes: The purchase price is from 100000 to several million yuan — the largest single sum in this book. Do not WeChat it to the broker. There are two other common ways of charging extra. One is two agencies jointly doing the same deal; that can be charged as only one commission. The other is handling a loan or a title transfer for you; those are further services, need a separate contract, and the fee must be stated in advance. Send every payment by transfer and note what it is for; see item 6 (check the title certificate and the mortgage before signing). Where the money for an off-plan home goes is in item 10 (the pre-sale funds supervision account).

### 8. Do not rent subdivided units: the minimum rental unit is the originally designed room; kitchens, bathrooms, and balconies must not be lived in
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. The trade-off is that on the same budget you live farther out or in a smaller place.
- In plain terms: By rule, the smallest unit you may rent is the room as originally designed. Kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, and basement storage must not be lived in. During the lease the landlord also cannot raise rent unilaterally on a whim.
- Benefit: The rules state clearly that the minimum rental unit is the originally designed room; one room must not be partitioned into several units rented separately. Per-person living floor area must not fall below the local minimum. Kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, and basement storage must not be rented for people to live in. During the lease the lessor may not unilaterally raise rent at will. For violations of Article 8, the competent authority shall order the lessor to correct within a time limit; if the lessor fails to correct on time, a fine of 5000 yuan to 30000 yuan may be imposed.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:住房和城乡建设部 (2010). 商品房屋租赁管理办法（令第 6 号）第八条：「出租住房的，应当以原设计的房间为最小出租单位，人均租住建筑面积不得低于当地人民政府规定的最低标准。厨房、卫生间、阳台和地下储藏室不得出租供人员居住。」第九条：「出租人应当按照合同约定履行房屋的维修义务并确保房屋和室内设施安全……房屋租赁合同期内，出租人不得单方面随意提高租金水平。」第二十二条：「违反本办法第八条规定的，由直辖市、市、县人民政府建设（房地产）主管部门责令限期改正，逾期不改正的，可处以5000元以上3万元以下罚款。」<http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1845070.htm>
- Notes: When an illegal subdivision is enforced, the fine falls on the lessor. But correction means tearing out the partitions, so tenants living there have to move — that follows from this rule. Deposit and rent already paid are often not recovered; that is the author’s experience. Safety is worse too: partition walls often block escape routes, and many people in one unit share one circuit. When viewing, check three things: whether walls were added later, whether the room has a window, and whether the meter can handle that many people.

### 9. Urban residents with hukou who want to live in the countryside can only rent rural houses — they must not buy homestead land or houses on it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. The trade-off is that the house is not yours; the maximum single lease term is 20 years, and when it ends you renegotiate with the landlord.
- In plain terms: People with urban hukou must not go to the countryside to buy homestead land (宅基地), farmers’ houses, or “small-property-rights houses” (小产权房) — national documents expressly forbid it. A villager saying “a written contract is enough” does not make it work either. To live long-term in the countryside, sign a lease, at most 20 years per term, and renew when it ends.
- Benefit: A State Council General Office document states clearly: “Urban residents must not purchase rural homestead land, farmer housing, or ‘small-property-rights houses.’” The same document also provides that after a farmer sells or leases out housing, a further application for homestead land will not be approved. A 2019 document from the Central Rural Work Leading Group Office and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs repeats it: “It is strictly forbidden for urban residents to purchase rural homestead land.” The same document gives the lawful path: urban residents may rent rural houses for residence or business. The lease term must not exceed 20 years; when it ends the parties may agree again.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院办公厅 (2007). 关于严格执行有关农村集体建设用地法律和政策的通知（国办发〔2007〕71 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_2395.htm>；中央农村工作领导小组办公室、农业农村部 (2019). 关于进一步加强农村宅基地管理的通知（中农发〔2019〕11 号）第五、六部分. <https://www.moa.gov.cn/govpublic/NCJJTZ/201909/t20190920_6328397.htm>
- Notes: Homestead land is land the village allocates to its own villagers to build housing; it can only go to people of that village. When renting a rural house, sign a written contract with the farming household that rents it out, stating the term, the rent, whether renovation or alteration is allowed, and how renovation costs are settled at the end. The documents cap the term at 20 years; do not sign “lease for 50 years” or “lease for 70 years,” and do not pay many years of rent in one lump sum. The documents also forbid going to the countryside to build villas, large compounds, and private clubs on homestead land; do not put up money for villagers to “co-build.”

### 10. When buying off-plan housing, check the pre-sale permit first, and only pay deposits, down payments, and loans into the regulatory escrow account specified in the contract
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Checking the pre-sale permit before signing and verifying the account before paying takes a few minutes. The hard part is refusing to transfer when the salesperson asks you to send money to another account.
- In plain terms: Off-plan housing means buying a home before it is built. Without a pre-sale permit, developers are not allowed to sell or collect deposits. By rule, all your down payment and mortgage loan money must go into a supervised escrow account and stays locked until the home is ready to hand over. Money paid into any other account loses this protection.
- Benefit: In official regulations, off-plan housing is called commercial housing pre-sale, where a developer sells a property before completion. In August 2026, the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, the Ministry of Natural Resources, and the National Financial Regulatory Administration issued a document stating: "All home-purchase funds paid by buyers, including down payments and personal housing loans, shall be deposited into the fund supervision account." The supervision is lifted only after the building passes completion acceptance inspection and water, electricity, gas, and heating meet handover conditions. Developers must specify in the purchase contract the rights and responsibilities regarding depositing purchase funds into the supervision account. If the developer defaults and fails to deliver on time, you can terminate the contract as agreed. The developer must refund the money per contract terms and bear liability for breach of contract. The People's Bank of China and the National Financial Regulatory Administration simultaneously stipulated that commercial banks must directly disburse personal housing loans for pre-sale homes into the project's pre-sale fund supervision account. A 2022 document from three ministries had already specified that deposits, down payments, and mortgage loans "shall all be directly deposited into the supervision account." For developments without a pre-sale permit, developers must not pre-sell homes or collect deposits under the guise of subscription, reservation, queue numbering, or issuing VIP cards. A pre-sale contract signed without a pre-sale permit is deemed invalid by the courts; however, if the permit is obtained before filing a lawsuit, it can be recognized as valid. Funds paid outside the supervision account are not covered by this oversight framework. The same 2026 document also stipulated that for properties sold as completed homes, developers may collect a "small deposit" after obtaining a construction permit. Such deposits are supervised by the housing fund regulatory agency and held in a "deposit supervision account" opened at the project's lead bank, and supervision is lifted only after the property is ready for handover.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:住房城乡建设部、自然资源部、金融监管总局 (2026). 关于完善商品住房销售制度的通知（建房规〔2026〕3 号，第一部分第（二）（三）项，第二部分第（三）项）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_13026/202610/content_7082773.html>；中国人民银行、金融监管总局 (2026). 关于改革完善房地产信贷管理推动加快构建房地产发展新模式的意见（银发〔2026〕171 号，第二十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_13026/202610/content_7082770.html>；银保监会办公厅、住房城乡建设部办公厅、人民银行办公厅 (2022). 关于商业银行出具保函置换预售监管资金有关工作的通知（银保监办发〔2022〕104 号，第二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-11/14/content_5726954.htm>；住房城乡建设部 (2010). 关于进一步加强房地产市场监管完善商品住房预售制度有关问题的通知（建房〔2010〕53 号，第一部分第（一）项，第二部分第（九）项）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2010/content_1629136.htm>；最高人民法院 (2020 修正). 关于审理商品房买卖合同纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2020〕17 号修正后文本，第二条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282621.html>
- Notes: For developments that obtained a construction engineering planning permit before August 28, 2026, pre-sale conditions and fund supervision follow local legacy rules. Depositing down payments and loans into supervision accounts was already required in 2010 and 2022 documents. For properties sold as completed homes where developers collect a small deposit during construction, new rules require deposits to enter a dedicated deposit supervision account. The account name and number must match what is written in the purchase contract; verify them before paying. If the contract is deemed invalid and the developer refuses to refund the money, you must sue in court. By law, standard first-instance civil proceedings conclude within 6 months, though extensions are possible, and legal fees are generally paid out of pocket. For how to pay for second-hand homes, see Item 7 (dedicated transaction fund deposit accounts).

### 11. Demand a refund from the developer if you paid a deposit or down payment on a new home, but the formal contract fell through or the mortgage was denied through no fault of your own (e.g., policy tightened or banks stopped lending after signing)
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Just read a couple of extra clauses carefully when signing the reservation agreement and formal contract. If the developer refuses to refund, you will need to sue in court.
- In plain terms: If you paid a reservation deposit but never signed the formal contract, or your mortgage was denied, check who is at fault first. If neither you nor the developer is to blame, the developer must refund your deposit, or return your purchase money with interest. Sudden policy tightening or banks suspending lending after signing usually counts as no-fault. But if the loan fails because of your own overdue credit history or insufficient income, you are at fault, and you likely will not get the deposit back.
- Benefit: The Supreme People's Court judicial interpretation states: when a developer collects a deposit through a subscription, booking, or reservation, and a formal sales contract is not concluded due to one party's fault, the deposit rules apply. Under the deposit rule: if the paying party defaults, the deposit is forfeited; if the receiving party defaults, it must refund double the deposit, as noted in Section 12, Item 15 (putting "deposit" in writing). If the contract fails to conclude for reasons not attributable to either party, "the seller shall return the deposit to the buyer." Another provision covers loans. If the sales contract stipulates mortgage financing, and the loan contract cannot be concluded due to one party's fault so the sales contract cannot be performed, the other party may terminate the contract and claim damages. If the loan contract fails due to reasons not attributable to either party, both parties may terminate the contract. The developer must refund the principal and interest of the purchase money received, or return the deposit. The law only states general principles for what counts as "not attributable to either party"; the author provides the following examples. Local mortgage policies tightening after signing or banks halting loans generally count as no-fault. Overdue marks on your credit report or inadequate income bank statements count as your fault. Incomplete developer permits or banks refusing to lend to the specific development count as the developer's fault. These two provisions only apply to newly built commercial housing purchased directly from developers, not to second-hand homes.

### 12. Check how area discrepancies are handled before signing for a new home, and photograph promises in brochures, ads, and scale models
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Just read two extra clauses carefully before signing, and snap photos or screenshots.
- In plain terms: Unless your purchase contract specifies otherwise, if the officially registered floor area differs from the contract by within 3%, you settle the difference based on the actual size. If the discrepancy exceeds 3%, you have the right to cancel the purchase, and the developer must refund your money with interest within 30 days. Specific advertising promises about the homes and community facilities that clearly influenced your buying decision also count as part of the deal, even if left out of the final contract.
- Benefit: Regulations from the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development specify that when pricing by area, the sales contract must define how area discrepancies are handled. If the contract does not specify, default statutory rules apply: when the absolute difference ratio is within 3% (inclusive), the price is settled based on the actual area. If the discrepancy exceeds 3%, the buyer has the right to terminate the contract. Upon termination, the developer must refund all payments made within 30 days from the date of the buyer's request, together with interest. If the buyer chooses not to terminate: when the registered area is larger, the buyer pays for the excess up to 3%, while the developer covers any portion exceeding 3% and the property rights belong to the buyer; when the registered area is smaller, the developer refunds the shortfall within 3%, and must double the refund for the portion exceeding 3%. Regarding promotional claims, the Supreme People's Court judicial interpretation states that sales advertisements and promotional materials are generally invitations to treat rather than contract terms. However, if the developer makes specific, definite statements regarding the properties and supporting facilities within the planning boundary that exert a major influence on the buyer's decision to sign or on pricing, those promises are considered binding contract terms: "even if such descriptions and promises are not incorporated into the commercial property sale contract, they shall still be regarded as contract terms," and breach of them entails legal liability.

### 13. Send a formal written notice first and claim contractual damages if a developer delays delivering your new home or property certificate
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Draft a written notice, send it by tracked courier, and keep a copy with the delivery receipt. If you actually cancel the purchase or claim compensation, you will most likely need to go to court.
- In plain terms: If a developer delays handing over your home, first send a formal written notice demanding delivery. If they still fail to deliver within 3 months, you can cancel the contract. If your contract lacks a penalty clause, compensation is based on local market rent for similar homes. If you cannot obtain the title deed due to the developer's fault, they must also compensate you, and you can cancel the purchase if the delay exceeds 1 year past the registration deadline. If the developer asks for your final decision, you must decide within 3 months; if they do not ask, you have 1 year from the date you learned you could cancel.
- Benefit: Judicial interpretations by the Supreme People's Court stipulate that if a developer delays delivery and still fails to hand over the property within 3 months after being formally notified, the buyer may terminate the contract, unless agreed otherwise. The right to terminate has a time limit: if the developer formally demands a decision, the buyer must exercise it within 3 months; if the developer does not, the right must be exercised within 1 year from the date the buyer knew or should have known of the right to terminate, after which it expires. If the contract specifies neither liquidated damages nor a calculation method, damages for delayed delivery are determined based on rental rates for comparable properties in the same neighborhood over the overdue period. If the buyer fails to obtain the property ownership certificate by the applicable deadline due to the developer's fault, the developer is liable for breach of contract. There are three applicable deadlines: the date agreed in the contract; 90 days from the handover date for off-plan housing; or 90 days from contract signing for completed housing. If liquidated damages are not specified, compensation is calculated on the total purchase price paid, referenced to the benchmark overdue loan interest rates set by the People's Bank of China. If property registration still cannot be completed more than 1 year after the registration deadline due to the developer's fault, the buyer may terminate the contract and claim damages. If contractually agreed liquidated damages are excessively high and the developer requests a reduction, courts adjust them using 30% above the actual loss as the benchmark.

### 14. Check for court freezes and mortgages before signing to buy a home, and file for pre-registration at the real estate registry after signing

- In plain terms: Before signing, take your ID to the real estate registry to verify whether the home has any mortgages, court freezes, or pre-existing registrations to someone else. Once registered, if the seller tries to sell or mortgage the property again without your consent, the registry will reject it. You must apply for the formal title transfer within 90 days once you are eligible to do so, or the pre-registration expires.
- Benefit: The Civil Code specifies: after signing a home purchase agreement, parties may apply for pre-registration at the registry as agreed to secure future property rights. ### 15. Negotiate a lower mortgage rate or refinance when your floating rate is significantly higher than new loans
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Call your bank's customer service or apply through mobile banking, taking a few dozen minutes each time. The bank may not agree.
- In plain terms: Mortgage interest rates are set by adding or subtracting points from the benchmark Loan Prime Rate (LPR). That margin used to be locked in for the life of the loan, but since 2024, rules allow you to negotiate changes with your bank. If your rate sits significantly higher than the national rate on newly issued mortgages, you can ask the bank to lower it or apply for a new loan to replace the old one. On a 30-year loan of 1 million yuan, lowering the rate by half a percentage point saves roughly 100,000 yuan in total interest.
- Benefit: A 2024 announcement from the People's Bank of China allows borrowers and lenders to negotiate adjustments to the margin added to or subtracted from the LPR. - Cost: Free. Spend an extra two to three hours on handover day inspecting walls, floors, doors, windows, plumbing, and wiring room by room, taking photos for records. Hiring a third-party home inspector costs extra.
- In plain terms: When delivering a newly built home, the developer must provide a Residential Quality Warranty. Statutory minimum warranty terms for contractors cover water leaks in bathrooms, roofs, and exterior walls for 5 years, and electrical wiring, plumbing, and interior finishings for 2 years, both counted from completion acceptance. The warranty period the developer gives you cannot be shorter than the remaining balance of the contractor's warranty. If the developer refuses or stalls repairs within this window, you can hire someone else to fix it, and the developer must foot the bill.
- Benefit: Regulations from the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development stipulate that when selling commercial residential properties, developers must provide buyers with a Residential Quality Warranty and a Residential User Manual. The developer bears quality warranty liability for sold homes, with the warranty period starting on the delivery date. The warranty period for commercial housing cannot be shorter than the remaining term of the quality warranty provided to the developer by the construction contractor. If quality defects falling under warranty scope occur during this period, the developer must perform repairs and compensate for resulting losses. The developer is not liable for damage caused by force majeure or improper use. Minimum warranty periods for construction contractors are set by State Council regulations, running from the date the project passes completion acceptance: 5 years for roof waterproofing, as well as anti-leakage for bathrooms, rooms, and exterior walls with waterproofing requirements; 2 heating and cooling periods for heating and cooling systems; 2 years for electrical conduits and wiring, water supply and drainage pipelines, equipment installation, and decoration or finishing works; and the design documents' reasonable lifespan for foundation infrastructure and main structural frames. Supreme People's Court judicial interpretations establish that developers bear repair liability during the warranty period: "Where the seller refuses to repair or delays repair within a reasonable period, the buyer may make repairs personally or commission others to do so. Repair costs and other losses caused during the repair period shall be borne by the seller." If housing quality issues seriously affect normal residential use, you may terminate the contract and claim compensation. If verification confirms the quality of the main structure is indeed substandard, you may also terminate the contract and claim compensation. Inspecting room by room on handover day, entering issues into the handover delivery sheet, and photographing them is based on author experience. Because damage from improper use is excluded from warranty coverage, logging problems upon handover makes it difficult for the developer to claim you broke them during renovations.
