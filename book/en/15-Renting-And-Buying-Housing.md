# 15. Renting and buying a home

Scope: money. Where renting most easily loses you money is the security deposit and agents. Where buying costs the most is mortgage interest, plus what the seller knows and you do not.
### 1. When renting, the deposit amount, refund timing, and grounds for deduction must be written into the contract
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. Spend an extra ten minutes when signing the contract. The hard part is raising it in front of the landlord.
- In plain terms: How much the deposit is, when it is returned, and when it may be deducted must go into the contract before you sign. Deduction grounds not written in the contract cannot be used to withhold your deposit when you move out.
- Benefit: Administrative regulations state clearly: “Where the lessor collects a deposit, the amount, time of return, and circumstances in which the deposit may be deducted shall be stipulated in the housing lease contract. Except as provided in the contract, the lessor shall not deduct the deposit without proper cause.” Three things must appear in the contract: how much is collected, when it is returned, and when deduction is allowed. Grounds not in the contract cannot be used to take your money.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: On move-out day, take photos and video together with the landlord. Record water, electricity, and gas meter readings, and photograph walls and floors. If the deposit is withheld without cause, complain first to the housing lease administration (房屋租赁管理部门). For smaller amounts, use small-claims litigation; see Section 8.

### 2. If water or power is cut, locks are changed, or someone threatens you at the door to force you out — call the police and preserve evidence: regulations forbid using these methods to pressure you to vacate
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. One call to the police; you can do it on the spot.
- In plain terms: A landlord cannot use cutting utilities, changing locks, or door-to-door threats to force you to terminate the lease or move out; regulations forbid it. If it happens, call the police first. The police response record is evidence if you later seek compensation for losses. Do not confront the other party yourself.
- Benefit: Administrative regulations state clearly: “The lessor shall not use violence, threats, or other illegal means to compel the lessee to terminate the housing lease contract or vacate the leased housing.” Vacating means being made to move out. In such cases call the police first and keep evidence. Do not confront them yourself.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: Calling the police is to obtain a response record; later it serves as evidence when you claim losses. Also notify the housing lease administration (房屋租赁管理部门).

### 3. Agents must not collect or pay rent and deposits on your behalf — pay the landlord directly
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. A few minutes to verify the payee before payment. The hard part is resisting the agent’s push to pay it to the agency.
- In plain terms: By rule, rent and deposits must not pass through an agent for collection or payment; pay the landlord directly. Every extra hop through an agent is one more chance it absconds with the money. If it runs, you lose the rent and the landlord can still push you out. Before paying, check that the payee matches the name on the real property ownership certificate (不动产权属证书).
- Benefit: Administrative regulations list “collecting or paying housing rent and deposits on behalf of others” as prohibited conduct for real estate brokerage firms; an agent cannot collect rent and deposits for the landlord. Each pass through an agent adds a chance of absconding with funds.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: Before paying, check that the payee matches the name on the ownership certificate. If not, clarify and keep written authorization. An agent may only charge a brokerage fee for matchmaking.

### 4. Before signing a long-term rental apartment lease, check its regulated escrow account — do not pay a full year upfront for a small discount
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. One check takes about ten minutes.
- In plain terms: Paying a full year upfront only buys a small discount. Once the company fails, your deposit and the remaining months of rent are gone together. It is worse with a rent loan: if the apartment company disappears, you still repay the loan month by month. Before signing, check whether it publicly lists a regulated housing-rent fund account.
- Benefit: Administrative regulations require housing rental enterprises that operate by subleasing to “establish a regulated housing-rent fund account and disclose it to the public.” Subleasing means the firm leases from the landlord in bulk and re-rents to you. The small discount from paying a year upfront does not offset the risk of losing deposit and remaining rent when the firm runs. Signing a rent loan at the same time raises the risk further.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: At signing, see whether a loan is bundled in. If an installment app pays the full year to the apartment and you repay monthly, you must keep repaying after the apartment disappears. Do not sign that kind of contract.

### 5. If the home is sold during the lease, the lease stays valid — you do not have to move
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost
- In plain terms: If the home is sold during the lease term, your lease remains valid. The new owner must let you stay through the original contract until it ends. You do not move out and do not renegotiate rent, provided you are already living there lawfully and you keep the contract, transfer records, and move-in timing.
- Benefit: The Civil Code has a rule called “sale does not break lease” (买卖不破租赁). The statute reads: a change of ownership of the leased thing during the lease term does not affect the lease contract’s validity. If the owner changes during the term, the signed lease still holds; the new owner must honor the original contract through expiry.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第七百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: This assumes you already occupy the unit lawfully — the statute calls that lawful possession and use. Keep the contract, transfer records, and evidence of when you moved in.

### 6. Before signing a lease, verify the ownership certificate and mortgage status; route all payments by transfer with a purpose note
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. Bring your ID to the real estate registration authority (不动产登记机构) once; the check takes about half an hour. The hard part is asking the landlord to produce the ownership certificate in person.
- In plain terms: Before signing, ask the landlord for ID and the ownership certificate. Then go to the registration authority with your ID — you can check even before a contract is signed — for whether the home is mortgaged or sealed. If it was mortgaged or sealed before lease, and it is auctioned to a new owner, you cannot demand to keep living there. Pay everything by transfer with a purpose note so you have a record.
- Benefit: Before signing, the landlord must show you identity proof and the real property ownership certificate. Without that certificate, other proof of lawful authority to lease must be provided. The landlord must also cooperate when you query and verify information on the unit — an administrative duty on lessors. Before any contract, you can go with your ID to the registration authority in the city or county where the property sits and check several items: basic facts about the home, whether there are co-owners, whether mortgage or habitation-right registration exists, and whether it is sealed. After a contract is signed, you can check registration results with the lease. This step matters because “sale does not break lease” has two exceptions. If the home was mortgaged to a bank before lease and later auctioned to repay debt, you cannot require the new owner to let you stay on the original contract. The same if it was sealed by a court before lease. When the court auctions, if the lease blocks an earlier mortgage, the court may remove the lease before sale. Conversely, if it was leased to you first and you moved in, and the landlord mortgaged later, the mortgage does not break your lease. If sealing, title dispute, or similar makes the unit unusable, you may terminate the contract. If a third party asserts rights and you cannot use the home, you may pay less rent or none. Routing all money by transfer with a note such as “rent for [address], [month]” is author experience: in a dispute it shows you paid and what you paid for.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第九条第一项：「向承租人出示身份证明材料、拟出租住房的不动产权属证书或者其他证明其具有合法出租权利的材料，并配合承租人依法查询、核实拟出租住房有关信息」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>；自然资源部 (2024 修正). 不动产登记资料查询暂行办法（第八、十九、二十、二十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11506/202408/content_6966482.html>；自然资源部 (2025). 关于修改《不动产登记资料查询暂行办法》的决定（自然资源部令第 18 号，第二十一条第三项改为「不动产是否存在抵押权登记、预告登记、异议登记或者居住权登记等情形」）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12546/202602/content_7057456.html>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第四百零五、七百二十三、七百二十四、七百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于修改《最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用〈中华人民共和国工会法〉若干问题的解释》等二十七件民事类司法解释的决定（法释〔2020〕17 号，第二十一项第 6 点修改城镇房屋租赁合同解释原第二十条，条文顺序调整后为第十四条：「（一）房屋在出租前已设立抵押权，因抵押权人实现抵押权发生所有权变动的；（二）房屋在出租前已被人民法院依法查封的」）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282621.html>；最高人民法院 (2004). 关于人民法院民事执行中拍卖、变卖财产的规定（法释〔2004〕16 号，第三十一条第二款；法释〔2020〕21 号删去第五至七条后为第二十八条第二款）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/shenpan/xiangqing/713.html>；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于修改《最高人民法院关于人民法院扣押铁路运输货物若干问题的规定》等十八件执行类司法解释的决定（法释〔2020〕21 号）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282661.html>；证据留存与转账备注同理见第 8 节关于彩礼和借条的两条
- Notes: Among what you can check before signing, owner identity is not included — so match the name on the certificate to the landlord’s ID in person. At signing the landlord should appear in person; if not, get a written power of attorney. If a sublessor rents to you, you need the original landlord’s written consent. Rights to terminate or pay reduced rent, if the landlord refuses, require a court suit. Ordinary first-instance procedure starts at 6 months; lawyer fees are usually yours. Transfer purpose notes are not directly required by regulation; that part is author experience.

### 7. If an agent collects payment on a resale, funds must go through the agency’s dedicated client transaction settlement account at a bank
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. The hard part is insisting on the dedicated account; you may need a few rounds with the agent.
- In plain terms: Purchase price runs from hundreds of thousands to millions — the largest single payment in this book. If an agent collects, rules require the bank’s dedicated account, not WeChat to an individual broker. Also, two agencies on one deal may charge only one commission; loan processing and transfer registration need separate contracts and fees stated upfront.
- Benefit: The rules state clearly: where a real estate brokerage is entrusted to collect or pay transaction funds, transfer must go through its dedicated client transaction settlement account opened at a bank. Purchase money enters a supervised dedicated account, not the agency’s own account or a broker’s private account.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:住房城乡建设部、国家发展改革委、人力资源社会保障部 (2011). 房地产经纪管理办法（令第 8 号，2016 年第 29 号令修改）第二十四条：「房地产交易当事人约定由房地产经纪机构代收代付交易资金的，应当通过房地产经纪机构在银行开设的客户交易结算资金专用存款账户划转交易资金。」第十八条「房地产经纪服务实行明码标价制度……在经营场所醒目位置标明房地产经纪服务项目、服务内容、收费标准」；第十九条「两家或者两家以上房地产经纪机构合作开展同一宗房地产经纪业务的，只能按照一宗业务收取佣金，不得向委托人增加收费」；第十七条代办贷款、代办房地产登记等其他服务「应当向委托人说明服务内容、收费标准等情况，经委托人同意后，另行签订合同」. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1918920.htm>
- Notes: Purchase price is hundreds of thousands to millions — the largest single payment in this book; do not WeChat an individual broker. Two common overcharges: two agencies jointly brokering one deal may take only one commission; loan processing and transfer registration are separate services — separate contracts and upfront fee disclosure. Route all payments by transfer with purpose notes; see item 6 (verify ownership certificate and mortgage before signing).

### 8. Do not rent subdivided units: the minimum rental unit is the originally designed room; kitchens, bathrooms, and balconies must not be lived in
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. The trade-off is that on the same budget you live farther out or in a smaller place.
- In plain terms: By rule, the smallest unit you may rent is the room as originally designed. Kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, and basement storage must not be rented for people to live in. After an illegal subdivision is caught, you may have to move. During the lease the landlord also cannot raise rent unilaterally on a whim.
- Benefit: The rules state clearly that the minimum rental unit is the originally designed room; one room must not be partitioned into several units rented separately. Per-person living area must meet the local minimum. Kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, and basement storage must not be rented for residence. During the lease the lessor may not unilaterally raise rent at will.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:住房和城乡建设部 (2010). 商品房屋租赁管理办法（令第 6 号）第八条：「出租住房的，应当以原设计的房间为最小出租单位，人均租住建筑面积不得低于当地人民政府规定的最低标准。厨房、卫生间、阳台和地下储藏室不得出租供人员居住。」第九条：「出租人应当按照合同约定履行房屋的维修义务并确保房屋和室内设施安全……房屋租赁合同期内，出租人不得单方面随意提高租金水平。」<http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1845070.htm>
- Notes: When an illegal subdivision is enforced, tenants move; deposit and rent already paid are often not recovered. Safety is worse too: partition walls often block escape routes, and many people on one circuit strain the wiring. When viewing, check three things: whether walls were added later, whether the room has a window, and whether the meter can handle that many people.

### 9. Urban household registration (城镇户口) holders who want to live in the countryside should only rent rural housing — do not buy homestead land (宅基地) or houses on it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. The trade-off is that the house is not yours; one lease term is at most 20 years, then you negotiate again with the landlord.
- In plain terms: Urban registrants may not go to villages to buy homestead land, farmers’ houses, or “small-property-rights housing” (小产权房); national documents forbid it. “A written contract is enough,” even from villagers, does not make the money buy ownership. To live in the countryside long term, sign a lease — at most 20 years per term — and renew when it ends.
- Benefit: A General Office of the State Council document states clearly: “Urban residents must not go to rural areas to purchase homestead land, farmers’ housing, or ‘small-property-rights housing.’” The same document provides that after a farmer sells or leases housing, a new homestead application will not be approved. A 2019 document from the central rural work office and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs repeats: “Urban residents are strictly forbidden to purchase homestead land in rural areas.” That document also gives the lawful path: urban residents may rent rural housing to live or operate a business; the lease term must not exceed twenty years, and the parties may agree anew when it expires.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院办公厅 (2007). 关于严格执行有关农村集体建设用地法律和政策的通知（国办发〔2007〕71 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_2395.htm>；中央农村工作领导小组办公室、农业农村部 (2019). 关于进一步加强农村宅基地管理的通知（中农发〔2019〕11 号）第五、六部分. <https://www.moa.gov.cn/govpublic/NCJJTZ/201909/t20190920_6328397.htm>
- Notes: Homestead land is village land allocated to registered villagers to build homes; only locals may receive it. When renting rural housing, sign a written contract with the farming household lessor stating term, rent, whether you may renovate or rebuild, and how renovation costs are settled at expiry. Leases over 20 years are invalid — do not sign “50 years” or “70 years,” and do not pay many years’ rent upfront. The documents also forbid using homestead land for villa compounds or private clubs in the countryside; do not pay villagers for “co-build” schemes.
