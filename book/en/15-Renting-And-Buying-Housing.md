# 15. Renting vs. buying a home

Renting can easily cost you extra through security deposits and realtor fees. Buying a home, on the other hand, mainly involves mortgage interest plus all those hidden details that sellers know but buyers don’t. Later sections cover buying specifics: which bank account to use, when you can get your deposit and purchase price back, what to do if the closing is delayed, how to guard against double‑selling, and how to negotiate a lower mortgage rate.
### 1. When renting, the deposit amount, refund timing, and grounds for deduction must be written into the contract
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. Spend an extra ten minutes when signing the contract. The hard part is raising it in front of the landlord.
- In plain terms: How much the deposit is, when it is returned, and when it may be deducted must go into the contract before you sign. Deduction grounds not written in the contract cannot be used to withhold your deposit when you move out.
- Benefit: Administrative regulations state clearly: “Where the lessor collects a deposit, the amount, time of return, and circumstances in which the deposit may be deducted shall be stipulated in the housing lease contract. Except as provided in the contract, the lessor shall not deduct the deposit without proper cause.” Three things must appear in the contract: how much is collected, when it is returned, and when deduction is allowed. Grounds not in the contract cannot be used to take your money.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: On move-out day, take photos and video together with the landlord. Record water, electricity, and gas meter readings, and photograph walls and floors. If the deposit is withheld without cause, complain first to the housing lease administration (房屋租赁管理部门). For smaller amounts, use small-claims litigation; see Section 8.

### 2. When utilities are cut off, locks are changed, or you’re threatened to leave — call the police first and keep records: laws forbid these tactics to force eviction
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost at all. Just make a phone call to the police — it can be done right away.
- In plain terms: Landlords are strictly prohibited by law from cutting off utilities, changing locks, or using threats to force tenants to terminate their lease or move out. If this happens to you, call the police immediately. The police report serves as proof if you later seek compensation for any losses. Do not take any action against the landlord on your own.
- Benefit: Administrative regulations make it clear: “Landlords must not use violence, threats, or any other illegal means to compel tenants to end their housing lease or vacate the rental property.” Eviction means being forced to move out. In such situations, call the police first to secure evidence — never confront the landlord yourself.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: Calling the police is essential to obtain an official report that can be used as proof when filing a compensation claim later. Also be sure to notify your local housing authority.

### 3. Agents must not collect or pay rent and deposits; payments go directly to landlords
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost at all. It takes just a few minutes to verify the recipient before paying. The hard part is resisting any pressure from agents to send the money to them instead.
- In plain terms: By regulation, agents are not allowed to collect or pay rent and deposits on behalf of landlords — all payments must go straight to the landlord. Any time money passes through an agent’s hands, it creates one extra chance for them to abscond with the funds. If that happens, you lose both the rent you paid and your right to stay in the property. Always confirm that the recipient’s name matches the one listed on the property title before making any payment.
- Benefit: Administrative rules explicitly forbid real estate agencies from collecting or paying rent and deposits on behalf of landlords. This means agents have no legal authority to handle such payments. Keeping money out of their hands eliminates one major risk factor for fraud and financial loss.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: Before paying, double-check that the recipient’s name matches the one on the property title. If it does not, ask for a written authorization and keep a copy for your records. Agents are only permitted to charge a commission fee for their intermediary services.

### 4. Check a long-term rental’s escrow account before paying a full year’s rent at once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost at all. It takes only about ten minutes to check.
- In plain terms: The discount you get from paying a full year’s rent up front is minimal. If the rental company goes bankrupt, you lose both your security deposit and the rent for the remaining months. It gets even worse if you take out a rent loan: even if the company disappears, you still have to repay the loan monthly. Always verify whether the company has a publicly listed escrow account before signing a contract.
- Benefit: Regulations require any housing rental business that sublets units to set up a dedicated escrow account for rental funds and make it publicly available. Subletting means a company rents entire properties from landlords and then re-rents them to tenants. The tiny discount from upfront annual payments simply does not outweigh the risk of losing both your deposit and all future rent payments if the company folds. For those who also take out rent loans, the risk is even greater.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: Carefully check whether any loan is tied to your contract. If a third-party app lets you pay a full year’s rent at once to the rental company, and you then repay that amount monthly via the app, you will still be obligated to make those payments even if the company disappears. Never sign such a contract.

### 5. If the home is sold during the lease, the lease stays valid — you do not have to move
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost
- In plain terms: If the home is sold during the lease term, your lease remains valid. The new owner must let you stay through the original contract until it ends. You do not move out and do not renegotiate rent, provided you are already living there lawfully and you keep the contract, transfer records, and move-in timing.
- Benefit: The Civil Code has a rule called “sale does not break lease” (买卖不破租赁). The statute reads: a change of ownership of the leased thing during the lease term does not affect the lease contract’s validity. If the owner changes during the term, the signed lease still holds; the new owner must honor the original contract through expiry.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第七百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: This assumes you already occupy the unit lawfully — the statute calls that lawful possession and use. Keep the contract, transfer records, and evidence of when you moved in.

### 6. Verify property title and mortgage status before signing a rental contract; use bank transfers with purpose notes for all payments
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: No cost involved. Bring your ID and visit the local land registry office; the check takes about half an hour. The main difficulty is getting the landlord to produce the property title certificate in person.
- In plain terms: Before signing, ask the landlord to show both their ID and the property title certificate. Then, using your own ID, go to the land registry office to check whether the property is mortgaged or under any legal seizure. This check can be done even without a signed contract. If a property is already mortgaged or seized before you rent it, a later auction could transfer ownership away from the landlord, leaving you unable to stay there. Always pay rent via bank transfer and include a note specifying the purpose — this creates a clear paper trail.
- Benefit: Prior to signing, the landlord must present their ID and the official property title certificate. If no title certificate exists, they must provide other documentation proving they have the legal right to rent out the property. Landlords are also legally obligated to cooperate with tenants by allowing them to verify the property’s status. Even before a contract is signed, a tenant can bring their ID to the local land registry office and obtain several key details: basic property information, whether there are co‑owners, any existing mortgages or registered right of habitation, and whether the property is under any legal seizure. After signing, the lease contract itself can be used to request the same information. This step is crucial because there are two notable exceptions to the general rule that “a lease survives a property sale.” If a property was mortgaged to a bank before being rented out and later auctioned to settle that debt, the new owner is not required to honor the original lease. The same applies when a property is seized by a court prior to the lease; in such cases the court may terminate the lease before the auction proceeds. Conversely, if you signed the lease and moved in before the landlord placed a mortgage on the property, that mortgage does not affect your tenancy rights. Should a property be seized or its ownership disputed, preventing you from living there, you may terminate the lease and request either a partial or full rent refund. Using bank transfers with purpose notes such as “rent for [address] in [month]” is a practical precaution recommended by the author: in any dispute this documentation can prove that payment was made and exactly what it covered.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第九条第一项：「向承租人出示身份证明材料、拟出租住房的不动产权属证书或者其他证明其具有合法出租权利的材料，并配合承租人依法查询、核实拟出租住房有关信息」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>；自然资源部 (2024 修正). 不动产登记资料查询暂行办法（第八、十九、二十、二十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11506/202408/content_6966482.html>；自然资源部 (2025). 关于修改《不动产登记资料查询暂行办法》的决定（自然资源部令第 18 号，第二十一条第三项改为「不动产是否存在抵押权登记、预告登记、异议登记或者居住权登记等情形」）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12546/202602/content_7057456.html>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第四百零五、七百二十三、七百二十四、七百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于修改《最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用〈中华人民共和国工会法〉若干问题的解释》等二十七件民事类司法解释的决定（法释〔2020〕17 号，第二十一项第 6 点修改城镇房屋租赁合同解释原第二十条，条文顺序调整后为第十四条：「（一）房屋在出租前已设立抵押权，因抵押权人实现抵押权发生所有权变动的；（二）房屋在出租前已被人民法院依法查封的」）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282621.html>；最高人民法院 (2004). 关于人民法院民事执行中拍卖、变卖财产的规定（法释〔2004〕16 号，第三十一条第二款；法释〔2020〕21 号删去第五至七条后为第二十八条第二款）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/shenpan/xiangqing/713.html>；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于修改《最高人民法院关于人民法院扣押铁路运输货物若干问题的规定》等十八件执行类司法解释的决定（法释〔2020〕21 号）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282661.html>；证据留存与转账备注同理见第 8 节关于彩礼和借条的两条
- Notes: The pre‑contract checks do not reveal the name of the property owner; therefore you must personally compare the name on the title certificate with the landlord’s ID. At the time of signing, the landlord should be present; if they cannot attend, a written power‑of‑attorney is required. If you are renting from a sub‑landlord, written consent from the original landlord is mandatory. Should a landlord refuse to acknowledge your rights to terminate the lease or receive a rent reduction, legal action in court may be necessary; a typical first‑instance civil trial takes at least six months, and legal fees are generally the tenant’s responsibility. Although there is no specific law mandating purpose notes on transfer records, this practice is strongly advised based on the author’s experience. The same verification steps can be applied before purchasing a resale property; after purchase, a preliminary registration must be completed as described in item 14 (Preliminary registration).

### 7. For second-hand home sales where agents collect payment on behalf of buyers, the funds must be deposited into a dedicated transaction account opened by the agency at a bank
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: No direct cost is involved. The challenge is ensuring compliance with the requirement to use the dedicated account — this may require some negotiation with the real estate agent.  
- In plain terms: The purchase price for a second-hand home can range from hundreds of thousands to millions of dollars, making it the largest single transaction discussed in this guide. By regulation, when an agent collects payment on behalf of a buyer, those funds must be transferred to a dedicated account opened by the agency at a bank, rather than being sent via WeChat to an individual agent. Additionally, when two agencies collaborate on a single sale, only one commission fee may be charged; any services such as loan processing or title transfer must be covered under separate contracts with clearly stated fees.  
- Benefit: The rules are quite explicit: any real estate agency entrusted with collecting and disbursing transaction funds must route them through a dedicated client settlement account at a bank. This ensures the money is held in a regulated account, not in the agency’s general account or an individual agent’s personal account.  
- Evidence grade: A  
- Sources:住房城乡建设部、国家发展改革委、人力资源社会保障部 (2011). 房地产经纪管理办法（令第 8 号，2016 年第 29 号令修改）第二十四条：「房地产交易当事人约定由房地产经纪机构代收代付交易资金的，应当通过房地产经纪机构在银行开设的客户交易结算资金专用存款账户划转交易资金。」第十八条「房地产经纪服务实行明码标价制度……在经营场所醒目位置标明房地产经纪服务项目、服务内容、收费标准」；第十九条「两家或者两家以上房地产经纪机构合作开展同一宗房地产经纪业务的，只能按照一宗业务收取佣金，不得向委托人增加收费」；第十七条代办贷款、代办房地产登记等其他服务「应当向委托人说明服务内容、收费标准等情况，经委托人同意后，另行签订合同」. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1918920.htm>
- Notes: Given the substantial sums involved — often reaching several million dollars — it is crucial to avoid sending payments via WeChat to individual agents. Two common pitfalls involve commission practices: first, when two agencies jointly handle a sale, only one commission fee is permitted; second, services such as loan processing and title transfer constitute separate offerings and must be detailed in separate contracts with clear pricing. All payments should be transferred via bank transfer with a purpose description, as outlined in Section 6 (verifying property titles and mortgage status prior to signing). For payments related to off-plan properties, refer to Section 10 (pre-sale fund regulation accounts).

### 8. Do not rent subdivided units: the minimum rental unit is the originally designed room; kitchens, bathrooms, and balconies must not be lived in
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. The trade-off is that on the same budget you live farther out or in a smaller place.
- In plain terms: By rule, the smallest unit you may rent is the room as originally designed. Kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, and basement storage must not be rented for people to live in. After an illegal subdivision is caught, you may have to move. During the lease the landlord also cannot raise rent unilaterally on a whim.
- Benefit: The rules state clearly that the minimum rental unit is the originally designed room; one room must not be partitioned into several units rented separately. Per-person living area must meet the local minimum. Kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, and basement storage must not be rented for residence. During the lease the lessor may not unilaterally raise rent at will.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:住房和城乡建设部 (2010). 商品房屋租赁管理办法（令第 6 号）第八条：「出租住房的，应当以原设计的房间为最小出租单位，人均租住建筑面积不得低于当地人民政府规定的最低标准。厨房、卫生间、阳台和地下储藏室不得出租供人员居住。」第九条：「出租人应当按照合同约定履行房屋的维修义务并确保房屋和室内设施安全……房屋租赁合同期内，出租人不得单方面随意提高租金水平。」<http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1845070.htm>
- Notes: When an illegal subdivision is enforced, tenants move; deposit and rent already paid are often not recovered. Safety is worse too: partition walls often block escape routes, and many people on one circuit strain the wiring. When viewing, check three things: whether walls were added later, whether the room has a window, and whether the meter can handle that many people.

### 9. Urban residents with hukou who want to live in the countryside can only rent rural houses — they must not buy land or houses on it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No upfront expense. The trade-off is that the house remains the landlord’s property; the maximum single lease term is 20 years, after which a new agreement must be negotiated.
- In plain terms: People with urban hukou are expressly prohibited by national regulations from purchasing rural land, houses built on it, or “small-property-rights houses.” Even if a villager promises a written contract, paying for such a property does not make it legally yours. To reside long-term in the countryside, you must sign a lease agreement limited to 20 years per term, which can be renewed afterward.
- Benefit: An official State Council document clearly states: “Urban residents must not purchase rural land, farmer housing, or ‘small-property-rights houses.’” It further specifies that once a farmer sells or leases out their home, they cannot apply for additional land allocation. A 2019 joint directive from the Central Rural Work Leading Group Office and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs reiterates this ban while allowing urban residents to rent rural houses for residence or business purposes, provided the lease does not exceed 20 years per agreement.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院办公厅 (2007). 关于严格执行有关农村集体建设用地法律和政策的通知（国办发〔2007〕71 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_2395.htm>；中央农村工作领导小组办公室、农业农村部 (2019). 关于进一步加强农村宅基地管理的通知（中农发〔2019〕11 号）第五、六部分. <https://www.moa.gov.cn/govpublic/NCJJTZ/201909/t20190920_6328397.htm>
- Notes: Rural land is allocated exclusively to local villagers for housing construction. When renting a rural house, always secure a written contract detailing the lease term, rent amount, permitted modifications, and how renovation costs are handled at lease end. Never sign agreements for 50‑ or 70‑year terms, nor pay rent for multiple years upfront. Additionally, it is illegal to use rural land for building villas, large estates, or private clubs; avoid any arrangement where you fund joint construction with villagers.

### 10. Before buying a pre-sale property, check for a pre-sale permit first; only deposit, down payment, and mortgage funds should be transferred to the designated regulatory account listed in the contract
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: This costs nothing. It only takes a few minutes to verify the pre-sale permit before signing and to confirm the account details before making any payments. The real challenge is staying firm when a sales agent tries to persuade you to send the money to a different account.

- In plain terms: A pre-sale property is one that is sold before construction is even finished. Without a valid pre-sale permit, developers are not allowed to sell such properties or accept any deposits. By law, all down payments and mortgage funds must be deposited into a government-regulated account; only after the building is ready for occupancy can this restriction be lifted. Any money sent to any other account falls outside this protective framework.

- Benefit: Under Chinese regulations, pre-sale properties are referred to as “commercial housing under pre-sale.” In August 2026, the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, the Ministry of Natural Resources, and the National Financial Regulatory Administration jointly issued a directive stating that “all funds paid by buyers — including down payments and personal mortgage loans — must be deposited into a designated regulatory account.” Only once the building passes final inspections and meets all utility requirements can the account be released. The contract must explicitly outline the developer’s obligations regarding these funds. Should the developer fail to deliver the property on schedule, buyers may terminate the contract per its terms, and the developer must refund all payments plus any penalties. The People’s Bank of China and the National Financial Regulatory Administration further stipulate that mortgage lenders must remit such loans directly to the pre-sale regulatory account. Earlier directives from 2010 and 2022 already required that deposits, down payments, and mortgages “must all be deposited directly into the regulatory account.” Developers lacking a pre-sale permit may not sell units or collect deposits via pre-booking, reservation lists, or VIP cards. Contracts signed without a valid permit are deemed legally void; however, if the permit is obtained prior to litigation, the contract may be upheld. Money sent to any account other than the designated regulatory one enjoys no such safeguards.

- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:住房城乡建设部、自然资源部、金融监管总局 (2026). 关于完善商品住房销售制度的通知（建房规〔2026〕3 号，第一部分第（二）（三）项，第二部分第（三）项）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_13026/202610/content_7082773.html>；中国人民银行、金融监管总局 (2026). 关于改革完善房地产信贷管理推动加快构建房地产发展新模式的意见（银发〔2026〕171 号，第二十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_13026/202610/content_7082770.html>；银保监会办公厅、住房城乡建设部办公厅、人民银行办公厅 (2022). 关于商业银行出具保函置换预售监管资金有关工作的通知（银保监办发〔2022〕104 号，第二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-11/14/content_5726954.htm>；住房城乡建设部 (2010). 关于进一步加强房地产市场监管完善商品住房预售制度有关问题的通知（建房〔2010〕53 号，第一部分第（一）项，第二部分第（九）项）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2010/content_1629136.htm>；最高人民法院 (2020 修正). 关于审理商品房买卖合同纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2020〕17 号修正后文本，第二条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282621.html>

- Notes: For properties that obtained a construction planning permit prior to 28 August 2026, pre-sale rules and regulatory requirements remain governed by local regulations. Both the 2010 and 2022 directives reaffirm that down payments and mortgage funds must be placed in a regulatory account. For already-completed properties where a deposit is paid in advance, the new rules mandate that such deposits be held in a separate deposit-specific account. The account name and number must match those stated in the contract; double-check them before any transfer. Should a contract be ruled invalid and the developer refuse a refund, legal action in civil court is required; the standard timeline for a first-instance trial is at least six months, and legal fees are typically borne by the plaintiff. Information on payment procedures for existing homes is provided in Section 7 (Special Deposit Accounts for Transaction Funds).

### 11. If you’ve paid a reservation deposit or down payment for a new home but no formal contract is signed and the loan isn’t approved (e.g., due to tighter regulations or a bank halting lending after the agreement), you’re not at fault, and the developer must refund the money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: No expense involved. Just read two specific clauses carefully when signing both the reservation agreement and the formal contract. If the developer refuses to refund, you’ll have to file a lawsuit.

- In plain terms: When you’ve paid a reservation deposit but no formal contract is signed, or when the contract stipulates that a loan will be used yet the loan isn’t approved, and neither you nor the developer is responsible — such as when regulations tighten or banks stop lending after the agreement is signed — the developer must return the deposit or down payment plus any accrued interest. However, if your own credit record shows delinquencies or your income falls short of requirements, the deposit won’t be refunded.

- Benefit: Supreme Court rulings make it clear: if a developer collects a deposit via a reservation, order, or booking agreement and no formal sales contract is signed due to reasons attributable to either party, the standard deposit rules apply. Under these rules, if the party paying the deposit breaches the agreement, the deposit is forfeited; if the developer breaches it, they must return double the amount, as detailed in Section 12, Article 15 (where deposits are explicitly defined). Since neither party is at fault here, “the seller must return the deposit to the buyer.” Another provision addresses loans: if a sales contract calls for loan financing but no loan agreement can be finalized due to reasons on either side, the other party may terminate the contract and seek compensation. When no fault lies with either party, both parties may cancel the contract, and the developer must refund the full purchase price plus interest, or return the deposit. The law outlines general principles for determining “no fault on either side”; the author provides examples: tighter local mortgage rules or a bank halting lending after the contract is signed generally qualify as such circumstances. Conversely, poor credit or insufficient income on your part would be deemed your own fault, while incomplete development permits or a bank’s refusal to fund the project would be the developer’s fault. These rules apply solely to new residential properties purchased from developers; they don’t cover second-hand homes.

- Evidence grade: A

### 13. If a developer delays delivery or title registration, send a written notice first to preserve evidence, then demand compensation per the contract

### 14. Before signing a home purchase agreement, check whether the property has any liens or mortgages; after signing, register a notice of pre-registration with the land registry

- Benefit: The Civil Code stipulates that after a home purchase agreement is signed, both parties may apply for a notice of pre-registration to protect future ownership rights. “Once such a notice is in place, any attempt to transfer ownership without the pre-registered owner’s consent carries no legal effect.” This notice has a limited validity period; it expires if the contract is terminated or if not filed within 90 days of when formal registration becomes possible. Implementing rules from the Ministry of Natural Resources confirm that this applies to both pre‑sale and resale properties. While the notice is active, the registry will reject any application submitted by the seller without your written permission. In the case of pre‑sale homes, if the developer fails to apply jointly, you may file the notice on your own. Before signing, you can use your ID to check for mortgages, pre‑registrations, objections, or liens; online filing records do not affect contractual validity. ### 15. For homeowners with variable-rate mortgages whose rates are significantly higher than newly issued mortgages, it’s possible to negotiate a rate cut with the bank or apply for a replacement loan
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: There’s no cost involved. Calling the bank’s customer service line or applying via mobile banking takes just a few dozen minutes. However, the bank isn’t obligated to agree to the request.

- In plain terms: Mortgage rates are calculated by adding a certain number of basis points to a base rate. Previously, this added amount remained fixed throughout the loan term; starting in 2024, borrowers are allowed to negotiate changes with their banks. If your mortgage rate is noticeably higher than current market rates, you can try negotiating a reduction or apply for a new loan to replace the existing one. For instance, taking out a new 1-million-yuan loan over 30 years at a rate 0.5 percentage points lower would save roughly 100,000 yuan in interest.

- Benefit: In 2024, the People’s Bank of China issued a notice permitting borrowers and lenders to negotiate changes to the basis-point addition applied to the LPR when calculating mortgage rates. The LPR is the benchmark rate used for mortgage calculations. Previously, this added amount stayed fixed for the entire loan term; this policy change reversed that rule. In August 2026, the People’s Bank of China and the National Financial Regulatory Administration further clarified that when the rate on an existing variable-rate mortgage deviates substantially from current market rates, borrowers may negotiate a rate adjustment or apply for a new variable-rate mortgage to replace the old one — at their own discretion. The amount of the new loan cannot exceed the remaining principal on the original loan. To estimate potential savings, use the equal principal and interest formula: for a 1-million-yuan loan over 30 years, a 4.0% annual rate results in a monthly payment of roughly 4,774 yuan, while a 3.5% rate lowers that figure to about 4,490 yuan. This translates to a monthly saving of roughly 284 yuan, and a total interest reduction of around 100,000 yuan over the full term.
