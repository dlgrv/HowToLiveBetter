# 15. Renting and buying a home

Scope: money. When renting, the places money is most easily lost are the deposit and the agent. When buying, the expensive parts are the interest on the loan, and the things the seller knows that you do not. The items near the end of the section are about buying: which account the money should go into, when the earnest money and the purchase price can be asked back, and what to do if handover is late. Also how to stop a seller selling the same home twice, and how to talk the mortgage rate down. The last two items are about after you move in: the property manager must not cut water or power to collect fees, and for a new home that leaks, go to the developer for repairs within the warranty period.
### 1. When renting, the deposit amount, refund timing, and grounds for deduction must be written into the contract
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. Spend an extra ten minutes when signing the contract. The hard part is raising it in front of the landlord.
- In plain terms: How much the deposit is, when it is returned, and when it may be deducted must go into the contract before you sign. Deduction grounds not written in the contract cannot be used to withhold your deposit when you move out.
- Benefit: Administrative regulations state clearly: “Where the lessor collects a deposit, the amount, time of return, and circumstances in which the deposit may be deducted shall be stipulated in the housing lease contract. Except as provided in the contract, the lessor shall not deduct the deposit without proper cause.” Three things must appear in the contract: how much is collected, when it is returned, and when deduction is allowed. Grounds not in the contract cannot be used to take your money.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: On move-out day, take photos and video together with the landlord. Record water, electricity, and gas meter readings, and photograph walls and floors. If the deposit is withheld without cause, complain first to the housing lease administration (房屋租赁管理部门). For smaller amounts, use small-claims litigation; see Section 8. For a shopfront rented to open a store, and whether catering can get permits, see Section 12, item 27 (renting a shopfront to open a store).

### 2. When utilities are cut off, locks are changed, or you’re threatened to leave — call the police first and keep records: laws forbid these tactics to force eviction
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost at all. Just make a phone call to the police — it can be done right away.
- In plain terms: Landlords must not cut water or power, change locks, or come to the door with threats to force you to end the lease or move out — the regulations forbid it. If it happens, call the police first and keep the evidence. Do not confront them yourself.
- Benefit: Administrative regulations make it clear: “Landlords must not use violence, threats, or any other illegal means to compel tenants to end their housing lease or vacate the rental property.” Eviction means being forced to move out. In such situations, call the police first to secure evidence — never confront the landlord yourself.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: Calling the police is essential to obtain an official report that can be used as proof when filing a compensation claim later. Also be sure to notify your local housing authority.

### 3. Agents must not collect or pay rent and deposits; payments go directly to landlords
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost at all. It takes just a few minutes to verify the recipient before paying. The hard part is resisting any pressure from agents to send the money to them instead.
- In plain terms: By rule, rent and deposits must not be collected or paid through an agent — pay the landlord directly. Every time the money passes through the agent, there is one more chance for them to take it and disappear. Before you pay, check that the payee’s name matches the name on the title certificate.
- Benefit: Administrative rules explicitly forbid real estate agencies from collecting or paying rent and deposits on behalf of landlords. This means agents have no legal authority to handle such payments. Keeping money out of their hands eliminates one major risk factor for fraud and financial loss.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: Before paying, double-check that the recipient’s name matches the one on the property title. If it does not, ask for a written authorization and keep a copy for your records. Agents are only permitted to charge a commission fee for their intermediary services.

### 4. Check a long-term rental’s escrow account before paying a full year’s rent at once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No cost at all. It takes only about ten minutes to check.
- In plain terms: Paying a full year at once gets you only a small discount. If the company absconds, your deposit and the remaining ten-odd months of rent may all be gone. A rent loan makes it worse: if the rental firm is gone, you still repay the loan monthly. Before signing, check whether it has a publicly disclosed funds supervision account.
- Benefit: Regulations require any housing rental business that sublets units to set up a dedicated escrow account for rental funds and make it publicly available. Subletting means a company rents entire properties from landlords and then re-rents them to tenants. The tiny discount from upfront annual payments simply does not outweigh the risk of losing both your deposit and all future rent payments if the company folds. For those who also take out rent loans, the risk is even greater.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: Carefully check whether any loan is tied to your contract. If a third-party app lets you pay a full year’s rent at once to the rental company, and you then repay that amount monthly via the app, you will still be obligated to make those payments even if the company disappears. Never sign such a contract.

### 5. If the home is sold during the lease, the lease stays valid — you do not have to move
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost
- In plain terms: If the home is sold during the lease term, your lease remains valid. The new landlord must let you stay under the original contract through expiry. You do not move out and do not renegotiate the rent. The premise is that you already live there lawfully, and you keep the contract, transfer records, and evidence of when you moved in.
- Benefit: The Civil Code has a rule called “sale does not break lease” (买卖不破租赁). The statute reads: a change of ownership of the leased thing during the lease term does not affect the lease contract’s validity. If the owner changes during the term, the signed lease still holds; the new landlord must honor the original contract through expiry.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第七百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: This assumes you already occupy the unit lawfully — the statute calls that lawful possession and use. Keep the contract, transfer records, and evidence of when you moved in.

### 6. Before you sign a rental contract, check the title certificate and whether the home is mortgaged. Send every payment by transfer and note what it is for
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Take your identity card to the real-estate registration office once. A check takes half an hour. The hard part is having the landlord produce the title certificate in front of you.
- In plain terms: Before you sign, have the landlord produce an identity card and the title certificate. Then take your identity card to the real-estate registration office and check whether the home is mortgaged or has been sealed. You can check before any contract is signed. A home that was already mortgaged or sealed before it was let, and is then auctioned and changes owner, cannot be one you demand to go on living in. Send the money by transfer and note what it is for, so you have a record.
- Benefit: Before you sign, the landlord must show you identity papers and the real-estate title certificate. If there is no title certificate, the landlord must produce other material that shows a right to let the home. The landlord must also cooperate while you inquire into and check the information on this home. That is a duty the administrative regulations put on the lessor. Before any contract is signed, take your identity card to the real-estate registration office of the city or county where the home is, and you can check several things. What you can see is: the basic facts of the home, whether there are co-owners, whether a mortgage or a right of residence is registered, and whether the home has been sealed. After the contract is signed, the lease lets you check the registration result. This step matters because "a sale does not break a lease" has two exceptions. If the home was mortgaged to a bank before it was let, and is later auctioned to pay the debt, you cannot require the new owner to let you stay on under the old contract. The same is true if the home had already been sealed by a court before it was let. When the court auctions, if the lease gets in the way of a mortgage that came first, the court removes the lease and then auctions. The other way around, if the home was let to you first and you had already moved in, and the landlord mortgaged it only later, the mortgage does not affect your lease. If the home is sealed, or the title is in dispute, so that you cannot live there, you can end the contract. If someone else asserts a right and you cannot stay, you can demand to pay less rent or none. Sending all the money by transfer, noted as "rent for such-and-such a home for such-and-such a month," is the author's experience: if there is a dispute, it can show that you paid, and what you paid for.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第九条第一项：「向承租人出示身份证明材料、拟出租住房的不动产权属证书或者其他证明其具有合法出租权利的材料，并配合承租人依法查询、核实拟出租住房有关信息」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>；自然资源部 (2024 修正). 不动产登记资料查询暂行办法（第八、十九、二十、二十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11506/202408/content_6966482.html>；自然资源部 (2025). 关于修改《不动产登记资料查询暂行办法》的决定（自然资源部令第 18 号，第二十一条第三项改为「不动产是否存在抵押权登记、预告登记、异议登记或者居住权登记等情形」）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12546/202602/content_7057456.html>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第四百零五、七百二十三、七百二十四、七百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于修改《最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用〈中华人民共和国工会法〉若干问题的解释》等二十七件民事类司法解释的决定（法释〔2020〕17 号，第二十一项第 6 点修改城镇房屋租赁合同解释原第二十条，条文顺序调整后为第十四条：「（一）房屋在出租前已设立抵押权，因抵押权人实现抵押权发生所有权变动的；（二）房屋在出租前已被人民法院依法查封的」）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282621.html>；最高人民法院 (2004). 关于人民法院民事执行中拍卖、变卖财产的规定（法释〔2004〕16 号，第三十一条第二款；法释〔2020〕21 号删去第五至七条后为第二十八条第二款）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/shenpan/xiangqing/713.html>；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于修改《最高人民法院关于人民法院扣押铁路运输货物若干问题的规定》等十八件执行类司法解释的决定（法释〔2020〕21 号）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282661.html>；证据留存与转账备注同理见第 8 节关于彩礼和借条的两条
- Notes: Among the things you can check before a contract is signed, who the owner is is not included. So the name on the title certificate has to be matched, in person, to the landlord's identity card. Have the landlord attend the signing in person. If the landlord cannot come, you need a power of attorney. If a sub-landlord is subletting to you, you need the original landlord's written consent. If the landlord will not accept the right to end the contract or to pay less rent, you have to sue. Ordinary first-instance procedure starts at 6 months. Lawyer fees are generally yours. Noting the purpose on a transfer is not laid down directly in any regulation. It is the author's experience. You can check the same way before signing to buy a second-hand home, and after signing you still file an advance-notice registration. See item 14 (advance-notice registration).

### 7. If an agent is to hold the price on a second-hand home, the money must go through the dedicated transaction-funds deposit account the agency opened at a bank
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. The hard part is insisting on the dedicated account; you may have to argue with the agent a few more times.
- In plain terms: When an agent collects the purchase price, the rules say it must go through the dedicated account the agency opened at a bank. That account is supervised; the money must not go into the agency’s own account, and must not be transferred to an individual broker.
- Benefit: The regulation is plain: where the parties agree that a real-estate brokerage will collect and pay the transaction funds, the funds shall be transferred through the dedicated client transaction-settlement deposit account that the brokerage has opened at a bank. The purchase price goes into a supervised dedicated account, not the agency’s own account, and not some broker’s private account.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:住房城乡建设部、国家发展改革委、人力资源社会保障部 (2011). 房地产经纪管理办法（令第 8 号，2016 年第 29 号令修改）第二十四条：「房地产交易当事人约定由房地产经纪机构代收代付交易资金的，应当通过房地产经纪机构在银行开设的客户交易结算资金专用存款账户划转交易资金。」第十八条「房地产经纪服务实行明码标价制度……在经营场所醒目位置标明房地产经纪服务项目、服务内容、收费标准」；第十九条「两家或者两家以上房地产经纪机构合作开展同一宗房地产经纪业务的，只能按照一宗业务收取佣金，不得向委托人增加收费」；第十七条代办贷款、代办房地产登记等其他服务「应当向委托人说明服务内容、收费标准等情况，经委托人同意后，另行签订合同」. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1918920.htm>
- Notes: The purchase price is from 100000 to several million yuan — the largest single sum in this book. Do not WeChat it to the broker. There are two other common ways of charging extra. One is two agencies jointly doing the same deal; that can be charged as only one commission. The other is handling a loan or a title transfer for you; those are further services, need a separate contract, and the fee must be stated in advance. Send every payment by transfer and note what it is for; see item 6 (check the title certificate and the mortgage before signing). Where the money for an off-plan home goes is in item 10 (the pre-sale funds supervision account).

### 8. Do not rent subdivided units: the minimum rental unit is the originally designed room; kitchens, bathrooms, and balconies must not be lived in
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. The trade-off is that on the same budget you live farther out or in a smaller place.
- In plain terms: By rule, the smallest unit you may rent is the room as originally designed. Kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, and basement storage must not be lived in. During the lease the landlord also cannot raise rent unilaterally on a whim.
- Benefit: The rules state clearly that the minimum rental unit is the originally designed room; one room must not be partitioned into several units rented separately. Per-person living floor area must not fall below the local minimum. Kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, and basement storage must not be rented for people to live in. During the lease the lessor may not unilaterally raise rent at will. For violations of Article 8, the competent authority shall order the lessor to correct within a time limit; if the lessor fails to correct on time, a fine of 5000 yuan to 30000 yuan may be imposed.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:住房和城乡建设部 (2010). 商品房屋租赁管理办法（令第 6 号）第八条：「出租住房的，应当以原设计的房间为最小出租单位，人均租住建筑面积不得低于当地人民政府规定的最低标准。厨房、卫生间、阳台和地下储藏室不得出租供人员居住。」第九条：「出租人应当按照合同约定履行房屋的维修义务并确保房屋和室内设施安全……房屋租赁合同期内，出租人不得单方面随意提高租金水平。」第二十二条：「违反本办法第八条规定的，由直辖市、市、县人民政府建设（房地产）主管部门责令限期改正，逾期不改正的，可处以5000元以上3万元以下罚款。」<http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1845070.htm>
- Notes: When an illegal subdivision is enforced, the fine falls on the lessor. But correction means tearing out the partitions, so tenants living there have to move — that follows from this rule. Deposit and rent already paid are often not recovered; that is the author’s experience. Safety is worse too: partition walls often block escape routes, and many people in one unit share one circuit. When viewing, check three things: whether walls were added later, whether the room has a window, and whether the meter can handle that many people.

### 9. Urban residents with hukou who want to live in the countryside can only rent rural houses — they must not buy homestead land or houses on it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. The trade-off is that the house is not yours; the maximum single lease term is 20 years, and when it ends you renegotiate with the landlord.
- In plain terms: People with urban hukou must not go to the countryside to buy homestead land (宅基地), farmers’ houses, or “small-property-rights houses” (小产权房) — national documents expressly forbid it. A villager saying “a written contract is enough” does not make it work either. To live long-term in the countryside, sign a lease, at most 20 years per term, and renew when it ends.
- Benefit: A State Council General Office document states clearly: “Urban residents must not purchase rural homestead land, farmer housing, or ‘small-property-rights houses.’” The same document also provides that after a farmer sells or leases out housing, a further application for homestead land will not be approved. A 2019 document from the Central Rural Work Leading Group Office and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs repeats it: “It is strictly forbidden for urban residents to purchase rural homestead land.” The same document gives the lawful path: urban residents may rent rural houses for residence or business. The lease term must not exceed 20 years; when it ends the parties may agree again.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:国务院办公厅 (2007). 关于严格执行有关农村集体建设用地法律和政策的通知（国办发〔2007〕71 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_2395.htm>；中央农村工作领导小组办公室、农业农村部 (2019). 关于进一步加强农村宅基地管理的通知（中农发〔2019〕11 号）第五、六部分. <https://www.moa.gov.cn/govpublic/NCJJTZ/201909/t20190920_6328397.htm>
- Notes: Homestead land is land the village allocates to its own villagers to build housing; it can only go to people of that village. When renting a rural house, sign a written contract with the farming household that rents it out, stating the term, the rent, whether renovation or alteration is allowed, and how renovation costs are settled at the end. The documents cap the term at 20 years; do not sign “lease for 50 years” or “lease for 70 years,” and do not pay many years of rent in one lump sum. The documents also forbid going to the countryside to build villas, large compounds, and private clubs on homestead land; do not put up money for villagers to “co-build.”

### 10. When you buy an off-plan home, look at the pre-sale permit first. Earnest money, the down payment, and the loan go only into the pre-sale funds supervision account named in the contract
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. Look at the pre-sale permit before you sign, and check the account before you pay — a few minutes. The hard part is refusing when the salesperson tells you to transfer to some other account.
- In plain terms: An off-plan home is a home put on sale before it is finished. Without a pre-sale permit, the developer cannot sell it and cannot take earnest money. The down payment and the loan you pay are, under the rules, to go in full into the supervision account; supervision is lifted only when the home can be handed over. Money sent to some other account is outside that protection.
- Benefit: An off-plan home is called a commercial-housing pre-sale in the regulations — the developer selling you a home that is still being built. In August (8) 2026 the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, the Ministry of Natural Resources, and the National Financial Regulatory Administration issued a document: “The down payment, the personal housing loan, and other purchase funds paid by the buyer shall all be deposited into the funds supervision account.” Supervision is lifted only after the home has passed completion inspection and water, electricity, gas, and heat are ready for handover. The developer must write into the purchase contract the rights and duties of depositing the purchase funds into the supervision account. If the developer is in breach and cannot hand the home over on time, you may end the contract as the contract provides. The developer must return the money as the contract says and bear liability for the breach. The People’s Bank of China and the National Financial Regulatory Administration at the same time provided that a personal housing loan for a pre-sale home is paid by the bank directly into the project’s pre-sale funds supervision account. A 2022 document from three departments already stated that earnest money, the down payment, and the mortgage loan “shall all be deposited directly into the supervision account.” A project that has not received a pre-sale permit must not be pre-sold, and the developer must not take earnest money by subscription, reservation, queue numbers, or VIP cards. A pre-sale contract signed without a pre-sale permit is held invalid by the court. If the permit is obtained before the suit is filed, it may be held valid. Money paid somewhere other than the supervision account is outside this supervision. The same 2026 document also provides that for a project sold as a finished home, after the construction permit is obtained the developer may first take a “small amount of earnest money.” That earnest money is supervised by the housing-funds supervision body, held in an “earnest-money supervision account” opened at the project’s lead bank, and supervision is lifted only when the home is ready for handover.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources:住房城乡建设部、自然资源部、金融监管总局 (2026). 关于完善商品住房销售制度的通知（建房规〔2026〕3 号，第一部分第（二）（三）项，第二部分第（三）项）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_13026/202610/content_7082773.html>；中国人民银行、金融监管总局 (2026). 关于改革完善房地产信贷管理推动加快构建房地产发展新模式的意见（银发〔2026〕171 号，第二十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_13026/202610/content_7082770.html>；银保监会办公厅、住房城乡建设部办公厅、人民银行办公厅 (2022). 关于商业银行出具保函置换预售监管资金有关工作的通知（银保监办发〔2022〕104 号，第二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-11/14/content_5726954.htm>；住房城乡建设部 (2010). 关于进一步加强房地产市场监管完善商品住房预售制度有关问题的通知（建房〔2010〕53 号，第一部分第（一）项，第二部分第（九）项）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2010/content_1629136.htm>；最高人民法院 (2020 修正). 关于审理商品房买卖合同纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2020〕17 号修正后文本，第二条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282621.html>
- Notes: For a project that had already received its construction-project planning permit before 28 August (8) 2026, the pre-sale conditions and the funds supervision follow each place’s old rules. That the down payment and the loan go into the supervision account was already required by the 2010 and 2022 documents. For a project sold as a finished home where the developer takes a small amount of earnest money while still building, the new rules require the earnest money to go into an earnest-money supervision account. The account name and number of the supervision account are whatever the purchase contract writes; check them once before you pay. If the contract is held invalid and the developer still does not return the money, you have to sue. Ordinary first-instance procedure starts at 6 months. Lawyer fees are generally yours. How the price of a second-hand home is paid is in item 7 (the dedicated transaction-funds deposit account).

### 11. If you paid reservation earnest money or a down payment on a new home, no formal contract was signed or the loan was not approved (for example policy tightened after signing, or the bank stopped lending), and it is not your fault, demand the money back from the developer
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. When you sign the reservation and the formal contract, read two more clauses. If the developer will not return the money, you have to sue.
- In plain terms: If you paid reservation earnest money but no formal contract was signed, or the loan was not approved, look first at whose fault it is. If the cause is neither yours nor the developer’s, the developer must return the earnest money, or return the purchase money plus interest. Policy tightening after signing or the bank stopping lending generally counts as neither side’s fault. Overdue items on your own credit report or income that is not enough count as your cause, and you may not get the earnest money back.
- Benefit: The Supreme People's Court's judicial interpretation states: if a developer takes earnest money through a subscription, an order, or a reservation, and no formal sale contract is signed because of one side's cause, the earnest-money rules apply. The earnest-money rules are: if the side that paid breaks the deal, the earnest money is not returned; if the side that received it breaks the deal, it is returned double. See item 15 of section 12 (write 定金 when you take money). If no contract is signed for a cause that cannot be laid on either side, "the seller shall return the earnest money to the buyer." Another article covers the loan. If the sale contract agrees payment by loan, and no loan contract is signed because of one side's cause, so that the sale contract cannot be performed, the other side can end the contract and demand compensation. If no loan contract is signed for a cause that cannot be laid on either side, either side can end the contract. The developer must return the principal and interest of the purchase money it received, or return the earnest money. The articles only state the principle of what counts as a cause that cannot be laid on either side. The examples below are the author's. Policy on housing loans tightening locally after you sign, or the bank stopping lending, is generally a cause that cannot be laid on either side. Overdue entries on your own credit report, or income records that are not enough, count as your cause. Incomplete papers on the project, so that the bank will not lend on it, counts as the developer's cause. These two articles cover only commercial housing bought from a developer. They do not apply to buying a second-hand home.

### 12. Before you sign for a new home, read how an area difference is handled, and photograph the promises on the brochure, the advertisements, and the model
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Read two more clauses before you sign, and take photographs and screenshots while you are at it.
- In plain terms: If the contract does not agree otherwise, and the area registered after handover differs from the contract by 3% or less, the price is settled on the actual area, with the extra returned or the shortfall paid. If the difference is over 3%, you can return the home, and the developer returns the money plus interest within 30 days. A promise in an advertisement that is specific about the homes and the facilities in the compound, and that affects whether you buy, counts even if it was not written into the contract.
- Benefit: The ministry regulation states that where the price is by area, the contract must say how an area difference is handled. If the contract does not say, the following applies. "Where the absolute value of the area-difference ratio is within 3% (including 3%), the price is settled on the actual area." Over 3%, you have the right to return the home. If you return it, the developer must, within 30 days of the day you ask, return the price already paid, and pay interest at the same time. If you do not return it and the registered area is larger than the contract, you pay for the part within 3%, the developer bears the part over 3%, and the property right is yours. If the registered area is smaller than the contract, the developer returns the part within 3%, and returns double the part over 3%. On advertisements, the Supreme People's Court's judicial interpretation states: sales advertisements and promotional material are generally only an invitation to negotiate. They are not a contract. But a statement by the developer about homes and supporting facilities inside the scope of the development plan, if it is specific and definite, and if it has a major effect on whether you sign and on the price, counts as contract content. "That statement and promise, even if not written into the commercial-housing sale contract, shall still be contract content," and breaking it means liability for breach.

### 13. If a developer is late handing over a new home, or late getting the title deed done, send a written demand first and keep the evidence, then claim the penalty in the contract
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Write a demand, send it by courier, and keep a copy. If you actually want to return the home or claim compensation, it will most likely mean a lawsuit.
- In plain terms: If the developer hands over late, send a written demand first. If it still has not handed over within three months, you can return the home. If the contract states no penalty, it pays you the rent of a similar home in the same area. If you cannot get the title deed because of the developer, it also pays; if the delay runs more than a year past the deed deadline, you can also return the home. The right to return the home has a time limit — do not keep putting off saying what you will do.
- Benefit: The Supreme People's Court's judicial interpretation states: if the developer is late handing the home over, and still has not handed it over within 3 months after you demand it, you can end the contract, unless the contract agrees otherwise. The right to end the contract has a time limit. If the developer demands that you say what you will do, you must decide within 3 months. If the developer does not demand that, you must use the right within one year of the day you knew you could end the contract. After that it is gone. If the contract agrees neither a penalty nor how compensation is calculated, late handover is counted as the rent, over the period of the delay, of a similar home in the same area. If, because of the developer, you still have no title deed by one of the following deadlines, the developer bears liability for breach. There are three deadlines: the deed deadline agreed in the contract; for an off-plan home, 90 days from the day of handover; for a finished home, 90 days from the day the contract was signed. If the agreed penalty is too high and the developer asks for it to be reduced, the court adjusts it on the standard of exceeding the actual loss by 30%.

### 14. Before you sign to buy, check for a seal and for mortgages. After you sign, file an advance-notice registration at the real-estate registration office

After it is filed, if the seller sells again or mortgages again without your consent, the registration office will not process it. ### 15. If you are carrying a floating-rate mortgage, and the rate is a clear step above newly issued mortgages, ask the bank to negotiate a lower rate or apply to replace the loan
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. Call the bank’s service line, or apply in the mobile bank — a few tens of minutes once. The bank does not have to agree.
- In plain terms: A mortgage rate is a benchmark rate plus a few points. The points added used to be locked for the life of the contract; from 2024 you are allowed to discuss changing them with the bank. If your rate is a clear step above newly issued mortgages nationwide, you can ask the bank to lower it, or apply for a new loan to replace the old one. On a loan of 1000000 yuan over 30 years, a rate half a percentage point lower means about 100000 yuan less interest.
- Benefit: A 2024 announcement of the People’s Bank of China allows the borrower and the lender to negotiate a change in the points added to, or taken off, the LPR in the mortgage rate. The LPR is the loan prime rate; a mortgage rate is that rate plus or minus points. In a press briefing, the responsible person at the People’s Bank of China said that the points written in the old contract were fixed for the whole term of the loan, and that this change alters that. ### 18. When taking handover of a new home, inspect on the spot and write problems into the handover record; for leaks and faults in water and power lines, go to the developer for repairs within the warranty period in the Residential Quality Warranty
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No monetary cost. Spend two or three extra hours on handover day checking walls, floors, doors, windows, and utilities room by room, and photograph for the record. Hiring someone to help inspect costs extra.
- In plain terms: When the developer hands over, it must give you a Residential Quality Warranty (《住宅质量保证书》). The contractor’s statutory minimum warranty periods are 5 years for leaks in bathrooms, roofs, and exterior walls, and 2 years for water and power lines and fit-out, all counted from completion acceptance. The warranty the developer gives you must not be shorter than what remains of the contractor’s warranty. If within the warranty period it will not repair or keeps delaying, you may hire someone yourself and the developer pays.
- Benefit: Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development rules state that when selling commercial housing, the developer must provide the buyer with a Residential Quality Warranty (《住宅质量保证书》) and Residential Use Instructions (《住宅使用说明书》). The developer bears quality warranty liability for homes it sells; the warranty period runs from the day of handover. The commercial-housing warranty must not be shorter than what remains under the quality warranty the contractor gave the developer. For quality problems within the warranty scope during the period, the developer must repair and also compensate for losses caused. Damage from force majeure or improper use is not the developer’s responsibility. The contractor’s minimum warranty periods are set by a State Council regulation, counted from the day of passing completion acceptance. Roof waterproofing, and seepage prevention for bathrooms, rooms, and exterior walls that require waterproofing, is 5 years. Heating and cooling systems are 2 heating seasons and cooling seasons. Electrical lines, water-supply and drainage pipes, equipment installation, and fit-out works are 2 years. Foundations and the main structure last for the reasonable service life stated in the design documents. A Supreme People’s Court judicial interpretation states that during the warranty period the developer bears the duty to repair. “If the seller refuses to repair or delays repair beyond a reasonable period, the buyer may repair personally or through others. The repair cost and other losses caused during the repair period are borne by the seller.” If a quality problem seriously affects normal residence and use, you may end the contract and demand compensation. If the main structure fails inspection as unqualified, you may also end the contract and demand compensation. Checking room by room on handover day, writing problems into the handover record, and photographing them is the author’s experience. Improper use is outside the warranty, so problems noted at handover are hard for the developer to blame on your later renovation.
